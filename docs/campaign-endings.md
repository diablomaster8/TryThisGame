# Hazel's Grove: the campaign and how to reach every ending

The campaign is a twelve-chapter story played in its own save (pick **Campaign** on the
title screen and choose your Keeper). The Grove, the care rules and the minigames are the
same as the Free Grove; the story runs on top of them.

The story is about treating your Nubbins well. Every Nubbin has a hidden **trust** value
(the stats sheet shows it). Petting, hand-feeding and play raise it; throws, drops, hunger
and crying unattended lower it. When trust collapses (below 15) a Nubbin **turns Shadow**:
ash-grey, dark emote ball, wary. It keeps its distance and stops greeting you. Shadow is
for life, but a Shadow Nubbin cared for patiently **bonds** again at trust 70 and loves as
fully as any other, still grey. Left at rock bottom (under 5) for ten minutes it **runs
away**; strays come back to the Grove edge twenty minutes later, hoping to be found.

## The eight endings

The ending is decided when chapter 12 closes, in this priority order.

1. **The Collector** (dark). In chapter 2 choose *"Two hundred? …Fine. Take it."* and never
   buy anyone back. Everything else is ignored.
2. **The Empty Lantern** (sad). Sable is gone when the story ends. Let her trust fall under
   5 (throw her, let her starve) and stay there for ten minutes so she runs away, and do not
   take her back in before chapter 12 closes. Or simply sell/lose her any other way.
3. **Eternal Dawn** (secret, golden). Raise one of your own Nubbins to Eternal during the
   campaign (two rebirths, then a Normal-type evolution with alignment above 0.8, below
   -0.8, or within 0.1 of 0) while Sable is bonded. Moss does not count.
4. **Wild Grove** (open). Chapter 9: *"Open every pen. Now."* Chapter 12: *"Open. No gate."*
5. **Hazel's Grove** (the true ending). Sable bonded (trust 70+), chapter 9 *"Tell him what
   Hazel wrote"*, Moss's affection 40 or more when chapter 10 begins (pet him often), never
   sold, chapter 12 *"A home."*
6. **Moss Sleeps** (bittersweet). Sable bonded, but Moss's affection under 40 when chapter
   10 begins.
7. **The Shadow's Keeper** (bittersweet). Sable bonded, Corvin not reconciled (chapter 9
   *"Buy Ash back"* or *"Open every pen"*), Moss loved.
8. **The Long Road** (default). Sable with you but not yet bonded.

Buying Ash back in chapter 9 also returns a Nubbin you sold in chapter 2, which lifts the
Collector ending.

## Tips

- Sable gains trust slowly while unbonded (60% of normal). Feeding by hand (+4) and petting
  (+3) are the fastest ways. She flees when you come within four units; wait for her to
  stop, then tap her and choose Feed or Pet. The Keeper walks faster than she runs.
- Lanterns only need tapping once each per round.
- The debug panel (backtick) has chapter buttons and a *Trust -40* button for testing.
- The Journal (📖) shows the current goal, a hint, friendship and the Paths page with these
  instructions.
