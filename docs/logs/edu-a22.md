Colombia on a map (mountains, two coasts, three Andes ranges, coffee country) is at
`a22-01-story` in both bands, with the coffee cherries at `a22-02-market` and the Magdalena river
at `a22-13-complete`. Arepas: `a22-02-kitchen`. Buñuelos: `a22-08-prep` (sprout) and

a think question in reader `a22-01-story-done`.

## Care notes kept

- No war is named. The riders become "trouble, far behind, small as a thread" (sprout) and stay a
  thin line on a far ridge (reader).
- Pedro's death is said plainly in both bands ("Pedro died"), in Abuela's own words, with comfort
  straight after (remembering him, the family together, the butterflies).
- Abuela is frightened, never a villain. Bruno's years in the walls are told as love. Nobody is
  hurt when the house falls.
- Antonio's animals are real-world animals (toucan, jaguar, capybara, coati); lessons treat them
  as such.
- The sprout version leaves out nothing: every choice on this page is kind and safe, so every
  original choice id (and the `a22-job` flags) is kept in both bands, with no `defaultChoice`.
- No quotes from the film. The two lifted lines are not used; Abuela's memory line and Mirabel's
  river line are rewritten in the page's own words. No songs. Hazel's margin note keeps the
  game's own S24 text.

## For Maor

- **Adviser sign-off before ship.** The script's own care notes ask for Colombian advisers. Three
  things in the Learning Grove version especially need their read: the sombrero vueltiao notice
  (made by the Zenú people of northern Colombia from caña flecha cane; "vueltiao" as "turned"),
  the word "minga" for neighbours working together (used widely in Colombia, with Indigenous and
  campesino roots), and the plain wording of the flight from home and Pedro's death.
- The Spanish glosses (abuela, tía, tío, primo, prima, bebé, buñuelo, caña flecha, vueltiao) are
  standard; a Colombian Spanish reader should confirm the glossary.
- Domains of several scenes were chosen to keep lessons from repeating in a row, so a few
  science lessons sit on flame and heat in several quests (a lamp runs through the page). They
  use different facts each time.
