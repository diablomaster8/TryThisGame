## Frame

You are Balla Fasséké's apprentice and your Nubbin learns the balafon. Balla gives the other tellings in his own
voice at 11 points (the rod, the stick or the branch; who the buffalo of Do was; how Sosso remembers
Soumaoro). In A5-6 Balla is sent to Sosso and asks you to be Sundiata's memory on the road; in A5-12 you give
that memory back. In A5-9 the atlas turns its own pages to show Balla in Soumaoro's chamber.
