## Where the writing stands

**Story.** All ten units can be followed, and the strongest are the warmest. S24 carries three generations through one object each: Hazel's list, Nova's ticks, Tilly's badges. S21's Clank turns from "best friend" to "just here is allowed" without a wasted scene. Most breaks are local and cheap to fix. Some scenes don't say who is here: Cinder is never introduced in the S19 reader, Tilly is only "Sparks's cousin" in S20, and the S19 sprout's Sable route never says Moss has died. Callbacks lean on skipped scenes (the White Year, Fossil's flower, S24's Atlas margin notes). Some numbers don't add up: Byte's eleven Nubbins against eight counted (the roster holds eight), and S20's blizzard dropping "suddenly" when its own lesson says hours. S24's collector routes hide the cost of the ending the player chose. One real contradiction crosses stories: S22's Nova says "I'm not going anywhere", and S24's is "always passing". Spouse love is still missing. Pixel and Byte share every S21 scene and are never shown as a couple.

**Humour.** The reader bands are reliably funny. S20 has Pixel's rulings and Gordon ("It's more like weather"). S21 has the announcements, "The four is silent!" and Byte's "Provisionally.". S24 has "law of carts", "Tea (refused)" and "Insufferable". The sprout bands keep the punchlines but lose the set-ups. "Children are not bricks" has no bricks, the main route's rhyme has no "Badly", and Pixel's "That's one" never says one what. The fixes are of one kind: put the set-up back, give a straight man one dry line (Gordon asking if the sky will hit him, Fairweather's backwards telescope, Fern's "It was a better tree"), and let a Nubbin do something wordless (counting four buns). S19's farewell stays grave. Its smiles go before it (the rhyme, a tummy growl in the wait) and after it ("It's the wind. Mostly.").

**Learning Grove.** Both bands keep their caps. No story line runs over 14 words in Sprout or 30 in Reader. Sprout averages 8.1 to 9.5 words a line, and Reader 13.5 to 15.3, though the S24 sprout has 215 lines of 11 to 14 words. What's left is placement and plainness. The reader units still put a lesson between a set-up and its payoff: S19's quizzes inside Cinder's last minutes (one offering "Funny."), S22's "It hurts less" / "It's quicker" and "You can SING?", and S24's net, whistle and sunrise. Sprout still says the deep things abstractly: "Not frozen still", "I always leave first. It hurts less", "That's why wild birds must stay free". It also keeps some irony a child takes literally ("You'll hate it", "She says it's the wind"). Death is mostly told plainly, with comfort after, but two openings never say it. Pronoun trouble is mild and local (S21's long look, S24's "She was right there").
