## Where the writing stands

**Story.** All ten units can be followed, and the emotional cores are strong: the knitted toy in S25, Linnet's ramp in S26, Spindle's last word in S27, Parcel at the cart stop in S28, Dusty's cold house in S29. Most breaks are a missing sentence, not a missing scene. Some pronouns point at people who haven't arrived yet: "He doesn't like people in there" (S27 reader) names Corvin two scenes early, and Dusty's "Everything in it is hers" (S29) comes a scene before Fossil is named. The S25 sprout never links the little light, the vanished toy and the hopping wild Nubbin, so its ending doesn't pay off. Two set-ups miss their payoff: Granny Brock asks for cake in S29 and never gets it, and Marina's rule six, "Come back." (canon 39), is missing from both S29 bands. Several reader recs leaned on "skipped" callbacks that are in fact required campaign or variant scenes: the Lantern Night tale, Hazel's notebook, S21's word for Clank and Bramble's youngest. Those were dropped. One reader fix would have broken canon, since Bramble ran off and was found; she never died. Spouse love appears once, at S28's party, and even there Pixel and Byte never speak to each other.

**Humour.** The reader bands are reliably funny: Moss's knees, Fern's formal apology to a toy, "a hole with ambitions", Byte's "WHY IS THE HALL WET?", Mr Grue's peace of mind "in coal", "practically a hug". The sprout bands keep the jokes that are pictures (Parcel turning both taps on, the Nubbin sitting on the old toy, "Never mind!" over the lost cheese). They lose the jokes that are irony: "I'm not here", "KEPT", "Frost does odd things", "a little, rude generator". The fixes are of two kinds. First, show the straight face: Moss on the ledge "you can see all of him", and Corvin "says it anyway". Second, give the wordless Nubbin a beat: hopping over the toy, tripping on Byte's word, licking a rosette, bowing to each flooded room. The grave moments stay grave (Spindle, Fossil, Hush, Gran's illness), with the smile placed before or after them.

**Learning Grove.** Both bands keep their caps: no story line runs over 14 words in Sprout (mean 8.3 to 9.6) or 30 in Reader (mean 14.6 to 16.1). The S28 sprout has the most long lines, with 158 lines of 11 to 14 words. Deaths are said plainly with comfort after (Spindle, Fossil, the still ones, Moss in S26's ending), but two Reader units still say "the rain on the hill" for Fossil. Lessons mostly grow from the story. The exceptions are placement: the S25 storm's two lessons sit after the lightning hook, the S28 bench lesson splits the reunion, the S28 kitchen opens on pectin, and S29's forces lesson answers Dusty's trowel joke before Marina can. A few lessons fight their own story. The S28 sprout makes the child say "Hazel" just before the choice to keep it secret, the S25 trust lesson praises a promise nobody made, and the S27 sprout cites a past wrong it never told. The S29 sprout's "not letting me go" is not broken. That scene plays only for saves that chose `held` elsewhere, as the band notes say.
