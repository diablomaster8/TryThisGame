## Where the writing stands

**Story.** Both mains can be followed and are among the strongest Atlas pages. A10 is warm and brave: Tala's knees, Tui's grief, Heihei's total lack of curiosity. A11 is reliably funny, and the farewell stays grave and light. The real breaks are payoffs. A11's ribbon route ties the Friend ribbon to Eeyore's door in A11-11, then has it come out of his pot in A11-19. That error runs through all three A11 units and breaks the signature choice. A10 has three set-ups it never planted: the stone loose on deck, Maui's "collects things" clue, and Gramma dying "in her sleep" a minute after giving orders. Spouse love is still the thinnest theme. Sina and Tui get one shared beat, and sprout cuts even that.

**Humour.** The mains are funny when a straight man sits beside a silly one: Maui and the sea, Eeyore beside everyone, Rabbit's lists. The flat stretches are the teaching ones: A10-8's wayfinding, A11-10's party, A11-17's search for Rabbit. Each fix adds a foil, and in A10-8 Heihei wobbles too. The bands lose jokes in two ways. First, cuts: sprout drops Stick, Heihei's plop, Sina's lunch, and A09's "I saw it with my feet". Second, reader lessons that explain the joke or sit between a line and its punchline. A cross-story rec bans both.

**Learning Grove.** All eight band files meet their caps. No story line is over 14 words in Sprout or over 30 in Reader. The biggest problem is lesson placement. Lessons cut into Gramma Tala's last words in both A10 bands, into the Rissolution and the knighting in both A11 bands, and into the comet's arrival and the octopus in A09. The fix is one rule with a content-check warning. The second problem is lessons that contradict their own scenes. A10 sprout counts Pua aboard when he stayed home. It also tells children to walk up to a fire giant two scenes after "stay safe and tell a grown-up". A11 teaches "indoors, away from trees" in a house a tree just knocked over. A09 scolds touching a stinging bush right after the heroes grab one. A08 reader runs a sand-timer sum on a timer the scene never describes. Its sum (3 × 2 = 6) and the bard's 84 minutes are both right. Sprout drops two key names: it never says the trickster is Maui, and never says the raft is called MAMMA. Reader bands lean on K-2 standard codes. Most of the flagged "new names" are recurring characters; only A08's opening square, A10's Maui and Te Fiti, and A11's Kanga and Roo need a fix.
