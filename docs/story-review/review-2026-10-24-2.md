## Where the writing stands

**Story.** All ten units can be followed, and the four pages are faithful and warm: the Oz bands keep every variant, the Prince's planets are tied to the plane at night, the Camphor Tree is strong on family, and Vasilisa's doll carries trust and a mother's love from the first scene to the last. Two breaks are real. A18's ending drops the merchant's promised thimble and his "who won" joke, and its doll hand-over reads as Vasilisa giving her doll away. Most other gaps are local. A15's Corvin echoes name nobody, A16-15 says "all four of you" when Sable came, and A17's "It's all downhill. Going." reads as a typo. The readers' flags on Granny's "bad boy", Kanta's mother and the Lamplighter's Grove lamp were checked and dropped: Granny is speaking to Kanta, and lamps placed through rewards are the normal channel.

**Humour.** A16 main is reliably funny (the King, the Rose, the pilot's muttering), and A18's Baba Yaga is the best straight woman in the Atlas. The thin stretches are A16-14, A18-4 and the middle of A17, where the Nubbin goes quiet. The fixes are of one kind: answer a set-up that is already on the page. Let Baba Yaga say "It was a small goose", let the Businessman lose his place to a squeaking Nubbin, let Oz squeak behind his screen. A suitor's goose in A18-4 sets up the supper gag. The cross-story rec carries A15's keepsake list ("a paper troll, a green bottle, an olive leaf...") on to the next three pages until your pocket gives up. No pill is taken on screen, as A16's notes require.

**Learning Grove.** Both bands meet their story-line caps (no story line over 14 in Sprout, none over 30 in Reader), but the joined replies don't. A17 Sprout has 23 lesson replies over 14 words, and Reader replies run to 43. One maths reply is wrong (A17 a17-10-plant teaches 3 × 4 = 12), which is the run's other Critical. Three systemic faults cover most of the rest. Reader bands carry 145 K-2 standard tags. About a dozen lessons use numbers the scene never says. Lessons sit on grave beats in all seven band files: the Witch melting, the telegram, the lost-child pond, Vasilisa's mother, Moss's oak. A15 Reader's secret lesson also contradicts its own Promise choice. Sprout still meets a skull it has never been shown and a few idioms ("half an eye", "a quarter of oats").
