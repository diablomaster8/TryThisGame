## Where the writing stands

**Story.** All four pages can be followed, and each is faithful to its source while keeping the Keeper a guest. A19 (the dragon) and A22 (the candle) are the most compelling, because their signature choices really change later scenes: how you met Toothless, and your job for Mirabel. A20 (the weeping) is the gravest page and the most careful one; Thökk's three outcomes pay off in A20-14, A20-16 and A20-17. A21 (the flower field) is the funniest and warmest of the four. Its one real break is that the Grove neighbour and the apprentice both show as "Fern", so the page's own title joke blurs. The other gaps are local and cheap to fix. Himmel's ring arrives with no set-up. Isabela's "How do you know that?" is never answered. A19's "Five dragons" should be four. Loki and Linie are named before they are introduced, and Bramble's "rail" assumes the player played S3 or S8. No Critical issues this run.

**Humour.** A21 is reliably funny: the mimic, Heiter's "small print", Serie's thousand-year ban. A22 is funny when the family is in the room (Bruno's rat voices, "I have to hug Isabela"), but its big set piece, the dinner, ends on a flat shared line. A19 is funny up to A19-10, and then the Gobber jokes stop just where the page needs relief. A20 is grave by design and its notes protect that, so the recs add comedy only around the grave beats: the Thing-field game before Baldr's death, the thimble, Sleipnir's legs, and Líf and Lífthrasir bickering over a leaf. The fixes are of one kind: pay off set-ups the pages already plant. The launcher that "will probably go off", Pepa's "What cloud?", the chairs, the bee, the window in the arena wall, the mirror-lotus. All of them stay inside a single page, so none assumes which other pages the player has finished. Spouse warmth is the thinnest theme; the recs give Félix and Pepa, Julieta and Agustín, and Líf and Lífthrasir one bicker each.

**Learning Grove.** All six bands stay under their story-line caps (sprout means 8.0 to 9.0 words a line, reader 13.6 to 15.4). Only four lesson replies run over the cap. The bands are faithful, but sprout often cuts the main page's jokes and leaves their payoffs orphaned: the eel, the knotted Nubbin in the net, Pepa's cloud, "mind the ceiling". The two systemic problems are placement and tags. Fourteen lessons sit inside grave beats (Stoick's apology, Abuela at the river, Luisa's "what am I for?"), and three split a punchline from its set-up. About a quarter of reader lessons carry K-2 codes, against EDU-STYLE's 3–5 rule for reader bands. Eleven reader sums use numbers the scene never says. A19 reader has a problem of its own: 23 opinion lessons where every answer is "That's a fair answer". Sprout's one-new-name rule breaks where whole groups arrive together (A19's trainees, A20's four sons).
