## Where the writing stands

**Story.** All ten units can be followed, and the frames do real work: the Prague household around the clay man, the K'iche' grandmother's kitchen with the granddaughter interrupting, and Pixel on the speaker as player two. The breaks are small and cheap to fix, but a few are real errors. PA3's grub count says thirty-three where the journal's own "1 of 12" makes it thirty-four. A31's rat sets a condition ("I'm riding on your Nubbin") that is false on the stay branch. PA3's sealed ending hands over Elderbug's mask without Elderbug, and PA3 still says "Ask Corvin", which leans on S9 though the cartridge only needs chapter 5. Callbacks mostly use scenes the player must have seen; the exceptions are A31's hearthstone, which never happened, and Cinder, named only at the end of PA1. Choices are strong. A31's stay/go corn and PA2's name both pay off over many quests. PA2's naming buttons give the answer away, though.

**Humour.** The best laughs are character comedy: A31's polite mosquito and louse ("Very kind. Bit damp."), "That's squash", Grubfather's "it's THEIR choice", Tails's "Most people just ask about the tails", and the A30 flood. What's missing is the payoff. Set-ups wait at the end of every cartridge: Pixel's biscuit (PA3), the Emerald lick and Eggman's parachute (PA1), the guest rule and the homework (A31), Hannah's burnt crust (A30), Ash's "Is it a Pokémon?" (PA2). Each fix is a callback with a straight man. Pixel and Byte's marriage (canon 38) is still almost absent outside PA2, so the Arcade's dry spouse joke is the one cross-story thread worth adding. The grave beats (Yossele's rest, the locked Chao, Quirrel, the Grubfather's lost children) stay grave; every joke proposed sits beside them.

**Learning Grove.** No line in any of the six band units is over its cap (sprout means 8.1 to 9.8 words, reader 13.8 to 15.8). What's left is placement and fit. Lessons keep landing inside the moment they should follow: a word game between "Dead" and Yossele closing his eyes, a fair-play ask between "That's cheating!" and "That's squash." Several maths asks bring numbers the scene never says, and PA1 Sprout asks a child to count five friends when four arrive. Standard tags are the biggest batch. Every reader unit uses D2.*.K-2 where EDU-STYLE asks for the 3-5 codes, many reader lessons use grade 1-2 codes, and Sprout tags grade 3-4 science. Sprout still keeps a handful of idioms ("slept like a stone", "ran rings round"), and drops some of the main page's best small jokes (the louse, the crust).
