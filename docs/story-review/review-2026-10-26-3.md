## Where the writing stands

**Story.** All ten units can be followed, and no Critical breaks were found. Book 3 keeps its canon intact: the Long Night order, Sable's one word, Moss heard by the Keeper alone until Byte's "I heard him", Corvin's return at the gate, and Hazel going up the hill. The real breaks are dead or unshown choices. S2's first two choices (the two piles, and who carries Wisp) set flags that nothing reads. On the Collector route the sold Nubbin stands in row two and is never mentioned again, yet the ending blames you for not going back. Smaller seams are a story told before its time: S3 gives away "same tea" in its first scene. Then lines that assume a scene the player may have missed: Sable's "which one it means", Marina's "feed yourself" on Fern's pie, and Moss's "this time" in S1. Several reader claims did not survive the check against source. Fossil's trowel is not a contradiction. Byte's "other ten of us" is the right count. Reader-1's c9-close maths already waits for the last line. The bands do play S2 and S8, so Wisp and Bramble are reminders, not missing introductions.

**Humour.** The run's funniest writing is character comedy with a straight man. Moss gets "Anyone didn't" and "a bird's nest with opinions". Byte has "Same tea" and his stopwatches, Fossil taps the rock, the steward says "Received", and Pixel's MAYBE becomes OBVIOUSLY. The flattest stretches sit just outside the grave beats: Shadow's Keeper, Wild Grove, the S4 sluice, S5's wait until dark and the one repeating chat line. The recs fill them with wordless Nubbin business (the garden-ornament freeze, the snoring heap, the second plum) and with callbacks the stories already set up: Nova's owl-speech ending, Hazel back in the gap, Fossil's fourth tap, Clover's Thursday and Briar's joke. Grave beats stay grave. The soup puck on the Moss-gone route becomes relief rather than a joke, and the told epilogue stops reusing Corvin's "say nothing, badly".

**Learning Grove.** Both band files meet their caps: no story line over 14 words in Sprout (mean 8.9) or 30 in Reader (mean 15.9). The joined choice-and-lesson replies that run long are uncapped and read fine. The real problems are placement and repetition. Ten lessons fire inside the gravest beats. Sprout drills "m-m-Moss" as Moss lifts the stone, and offers "Sweets" for Gran's flower. Reader explains crocodile jaws as Sable lifts the shard and gold ice as Wisp goes under. Sprout repeats its Corvin/cold sound, its M-for-Moss and its melting frost three times each, and reader explains frost at 0 °C three times. Sprout's notebook drops "Took two months. Worth it.", the line the child must read aloud in chapter 9. Byte arrives unintroduced in both bands, and neither says Pixel is his wife. Reader-1 still carries K-2 C3 codes.
