## Where the writing stands

**Story.** All ten units can be followed scene by scene. The frames carry them: Pixel as player two in Mercy and The Hundred-Year Sleep, the Larkins two houses along, and Siti and Wulan's shadow screen by the pond. Both cartridges stay faithful to their sources: the True Pacifist route with the dummy, the snails, the dogs and the True Lab, and the Plateau, the four Beasts, the twelve memories and the open question at the end. The real breaks are in the wiring, not the writing. PA4's 25 "play after" scenes have no host scene, so `variantFor` can never play them, and its signature candy and every friend line are dead. PA5's eight memories have no host, and its "hunt" scenes wait for a value no flag ever holds. LB's nine variants have no host either. LK's cucumber variant is named for the wrong teller. Several choice values (left, lie, low, told, kept, ramp) also match Grove flags that `variantFor` reads. Two more numbers are wrong: LK reader's "fourteen crocodiles" against a check of ten, and LB sprout's "five races" with four results. The Hebrew course stresses arba on the wrong syllable by the standard it uses everywhere else. Five choices still change nothing: PA4's "be", PA5's told/kept, LK's "sorry" and LB's boats are the ones worth paying off.

**Humour.** The funniest writing in the run is character comedy faithful to the sources: Sans's whoopee cushion and "dumpling with a crown", Undyne's burning kitchen, Papyrus's sock note, the Yiga's bananas and "Oh, come ON", Purah at the Nubbin's height, Siti and Wulan's "you may sulk" / "I never sulk. I wait", and the Larkins' pine-cone café. What's missing is payoff. Set-ups wait all through the cartridges and lines: Pixel's friends receipt, the dumpling, the teaspoon, Ned's backwards letters, the crocodile voices, Pixel's finger count and "Is it blossoming yet?". The recs pay each one off beside a grave or tender beat, never on it. The flattest stretches are PA4's Waterfall, PA5's four solemn road memories (road players get no laugh in any memory), LK5 and LK8, and the Hebrew fruit fetch. Spouse love shows only in LB (Ama and Sam, rarely together); the cross-story rec brings Pixel and Byte in to bicker.

**Learning Grove.** Both bands meet their caps in LB and LK: no line over 14 words in sprout (means 7.0 and 7.8) or over 30 in reader (means 12.7 and 13.3). The Hebrew sprout band's seven "over cap" lines are counting and colour lists, where each word is counted twice, in Hebrew and in transliteration; they are fine read aloud. Most flagged "new names" are the same family or the same two tellers returning each scene; only LB1's opening, Pixel in LK10 and Hazel in Hebrew LH5 need a fix. The real child-read problems are lessons whose answers aren't in the scene: the fourteen crocodiles, the five races, a glass of water nobody holds, "steady as a duck" on one branch only, a broken promise never made, and sails with no wind. Reader bands carry K-2 standards, seven of them C3 codes that EDU-STYLE rules out for readers. The readers' suggested replacements were partly off the approved list, so the cross-story rec uses approved codes only. Humour is steady in both bands (Sam as the crowd and the pond monster, "Nobody eats it!", the Wobbler falling over), but it often sits in the main line and drops out of sprout.
