## Where the writing stands

**Story.** All ten bands can be followed. Each story has one clear want and one clear change: Marina's dry pools, Fossil's three wishes, Ash's lost name, Null's clock, and Bramble found. The breaks are local. Some payoffs go unsaid in sprout. The band never says the three notes are Ash's first name, and "lanterns" (S7) and "lighter" (S6) arrive with no set-up. In reader, the key lines are knotted: Ember's "I'd rather have lost", Byte's "those make the same graph", and Bramble looking up the path "the way she looked the first night", which nothing set up. Pronouns blur wherever two women share a scene: Marina and Fossil, Fern quoting Hazel, the Old Girl and Nova. Most of the flagged "new names" are campaign regulars the child already knows (Ember, Gran Hazel, Moss, Corvin, Clover, Cinder, the Circuit), and none needs a fix. Canon holds: no Nubbin speaks, Dusty stays "Dusty", Pixel and Byte bicker as a married pair. One canon thread is missing: Marina's Rule five (canon 39) is dropped from both S4 bands.

**Humour.** Reader is reliably funny in all five stories. Dusty's shrinking trowel, Marina against the sleeping steward, the very confident duck, "That was a plum", and Fern's sat-on fruit all work. Sprout is warmer than it is funny, and its laughs come in set-pieces. The fixes are of one kind: turn a stated joke into a physical one. Fossil swims like a crab, the frozen Nubbin goes plop, the grey heads swish, Byte's nose itches, and the whistle tries escalate to a honk the duck answers. Two cross-story recs turn Hazel's lift-and-push gate and Nova's "Slower than that" into refrains a child can say aloud. Grave beats stay grave. Fossil's evening, Ember's kitchen and the reunion keep their quiet, and each gets its smile just before or after.

**Learning Grove.** Both bands meet their caps. Sprout means run 7.7 to 8.6 words a line and none is over 14. Reader means run 13.2 to 15.5 and none is over 30. That suits 7 to 10 year olds, though S4 and S5 reader lean on vocabulary (mortar, outflow, reservoir) more than sentence length. Lessons grow out of the stories. Three issues need care. Sprout's S5 says "won't be with us much longer", the euphemism EDU-STYLE forbids. Sprout's S4 offers a cold-water wade-in before any safety line. And lessons sometimes land on the best moments: a phonics quiz inside sprout's reunion, a guilt question between Fern's "sorry" and Bramble's answer, two stacked lessons on Fossil's sunset and on Null's turn. Each fix moves a lesson as late as it can go before the choices, or cuts one. No new lesson needs a new standard.
