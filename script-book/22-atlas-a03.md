## A3-1. The Fire

*Along with the Gods (Joo Ho-min’s webtoon; the films The Two Worlds, 2017, and The Last 49 Days, 2018): the fire, and the fall* · audience: teen · where: Nova’s atlas: the Forty-Nine Days page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time once Nova’s atlas is in the Grove and Mature content is set to 13+ or 18+; the first quest on the page.

**Cast:** Ja-hong (Kim Ja-hong, the firefighter, in Along with the Gods (webtoon by Joo Ho-min; films 2017 and 2018); original design and dialogue), The girl (The child Ja-hong carries out of the fire, in the films; original dialogue), Gang-rim (Gang-rim, leader of the three guardians, in Along with the Gods (webtoon by Joo Ho-min; films 2017 and 2018); original design and dialogue)

### Step 1: Step into the smoky page and find the fire.

Hint: Touch the page, then follow the sirens up the street. · check: `explore:a03-city:fire-street`

#### Scene `a03-01-page`

_Nova’s atlas falls open at a page washed in grey ink. A city street, a tall building, and smoke drawn in long soft strokes._  
_Under the street, as if the paper were thinner there, you can see a road of white sand going a long way down._  
_Somebody has written in the corner, in Nova’s hand: “Forty-nine days. Read it with somebody.”_  
_The smoke moves. Then it smells of smoke, and you and your Nubbin are standing on a wet pavement, with sirens coming._  
_Your Nubbin is glowing, very faintly, the way a coal does after the fire has gone down. It looks at its paws, surprised._  
_A fire engine pulls up. A man in a yellow helmet is out of it before it has stopped._  
**Ja-hong**: There’s a kid on the seventh floor. Somebody saw her at the window. I’m going up.  
_Nobody argues with him. It looks as if nobody ever has._  

**Choices**

- **Stay back, and keep your Nubbin close.**
    - You stand by the engine with your Nubbin in your arms. It is warm, warmer than usual, and it won’t stop looking up.
- **Call after him to be careful.**
    - **Ja-hong**: Always am.
    - He says it the way people say things they have said a thousand times, and then he is gone into the smoke.

#### Done `a03-01-page-done`

_Floor by floor, a torch moves up past the windows. At the seventh it stops._  

### Step 2: Watch the seventh-floor window.

Hint: Stay by the fire engine and look up. · check: `watch:a03-jahong:window`

#### Scene `a03-01-window`

_A small face appears at the seventh-floor window, and then his helmet beside it._  
_Behind them the stairwell glows the wrong colour. The way down is gone._  
_He wraps the girl in his coat, all of her, until she is just a bundle with feet._  
_He looks down at the street, at the people with the cushion, at the distance. He doesn’t look for very long._  
_Then he holds her tight against his chest, turns his back to the air, and steps out._  
_Your Nubbin covers its eyes with both paws. You don’t, and afterwards you wish you had._  

#### Done `a03-01-window-done`

_The bundle sits up on the cushion and starts to cry, which is the best sound in the street._  
**The girl** _(shock)_: Where’s the man? The man in the hat?  
_The man in the hat does not sit up._  

#### Complete `a03-01-complete`

_A little way off, Ja-hong stands up out of himself, the way you get up from a chair. He pats his chest, puzzled._  
**Ja-hong**: Is she all right? Somebody tell me she’s all right.  
_She is, and you nod, because you can’t say it yet. He breathes out, and then he looks down at where he was lying._  
**Ja-hong** _(soft)_: Oh.  
_By the fire engine three people in long black coats are waiting, as if they have been there all along. Nobody else can see them._  
_The tallest is reading from a list. He looks up at Ja-hong, then, for slightly longer, at your glowing Nubbin._  
**Gang-rim**: Kim Ja-hong. Firefighter. You did well today. Come with us, please.

## A3-2. The Three Guardians

*Along with the Gods (Joo Ho-min’s webtoon; the films The Two Worlds, 2017, and The Last 49 Days, 2018): the guardians meet the paragon* · audience: teen · where: Nova’s atlas: the Forty-Nine Days page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A3-1, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A3-1

**Cast:** Gang-rim (Gang-rim, leader of the three guardians, in Along with the Gods (webtoon by Joo Ho-min; films 2017 and 2018); original design and dialogue), Haewonmak (Haewonmak, the fierce guardian, in Along with the Gods (webtoon by Joo Ho-min; films 2017 and 2018); original design and dialogue), Deok-choon (Lee Deok-choon, the kind guardian, in Along with the Gods (webtoon by Joo Ho-min; films 2017 and 2018); original design and dialogue), Ja-hong (Kim Ja-hong, the firefighter, in Along with the Gods (webtoon by Joo Ho-min; films 2017 and 2018); original design and dialogue)

### Step 1: Meet the three guardians, and find out what they want with your Nubbin.

Hint: Talk to the tall one with the list. · check: `talk:a03-gangrim:list`

#### Scene `a03-02-coats`

_Up close the black coats are very plain, and very well kept. Their wide black hats throw a shade like a small roof._  
**Haewonmak** _(angry)_: The big one’s ours. What’s the little glowing one? A stray? I’ll take it.  
_He has a sword nearly as long as he is, and a face that looks ready to start an argument with weather._  
**Deok-choon** _(sad)_: You will not take it, you’ll frighten it. Look, it’s shaking. Come here, little one.  
_She is young, with a round kind face, and she is already holding out her sleeve for your Nubbin to climb into._  
**Gang-rim**: Haewonmak. Deok-choon. Let me check.  
_Gang-rim runs one finger down his list, then down the back of it, then down a second sheet from his coat._  
**Gang-rim**: Not on any list. It isn’t dead. It’s lost. The page has drawn it as a spark because it doesn’t know what else to draw.  
**Gang-rim**: We’ll walk it with us till we can see it home. And you with it, I suppose. You’re the living one who came in the front.  

**Choices**

- **Keep your Nubbin in your own arms.**
    - **Deok-choon**: Of course. Of course you would. I would too.
    - **Haewonmak**: I wasn’t going to hurt it. I was going to put it in a jar. A nice jar.
- **Let Deok-choon carry it in her sleeve.**
    - Your Nubbin climbs into the black sleeve and settles. Deok-choon looks as if somebody has given her a medal.
- **Ask Gang-rim if it’s safe here.**
    - **Gang-rim**: Safer than most. The dead here are mostly sad, and sad people are not much danger to anybody.

#### Done `a03-02-coats-done`

_Your Nubbin’s glow steadies. It has decided the black coats are on its side._  

### Step 2: Walk with the guardians and hear what happens next.

Hint: Follow the three black coats down the road of white sand. · check: `walk:a03-guardians:sand-road`

#### Scene `a03-02-paragon`

_The street goes thin and pale, and then it is sand, white and fine, running down a long slope into a grey sky._  
**Gang-rim**: Here is how it works. The dead come before the Ten Kings. Seven trials, in seven hells, in forty-nine days.  
**Gang-rim**: Most of the dead we walk are just the dead. You are a paragon, Kim Ja-hong. You died for someone who wasn’t yours.  
**Deok-choon** _(happy)_: A paragon who passes all seven is born again. A new life! It hardly ever happens. We’re so lucky to have you.  
**Haewonmak**: We’ll see. Paragons are always the ones with the most in the cupboard.  
**Ja-hong**: I’m not a good man. You’ve got the wrong file. I did things.  
**Deok-choon** _(soft)_: That’s what they all say. The good ones, I mean.  
**Gang-rim**: We are your defence. We’ll need evidence for every trial. The courts don’t believe us. They believe what they can see.  

#### Done `a03-02-paragon-done`

**Gang-rim**: Which is where you come in, witness. The living see things here that we have stopped seeing.  
_He hands you a little wooden tag on a black cord. It says, in a very neat hand, Visitor._  

### Step 3: Reach the first gate of the jeoseung.

Hint: Keep going down the sand road until the gate comes into view. · check: `explore:a03-jeoseung:first-gate`

#### Scene `a03-02-gate`

_At the bottom of the slope there is a gate as tall as a cliff, with ten doors set in it, all shut._  
_Other dead are queuing in a long quiet line, most of them old, a few not. Everybody is holding a number._  
**Haewonmak**: Paragon. Front of the queue. Mind out.  
**Deok-choon**: Say sorry when you push past. It’s their last queue. Be nice.  
_Ja-hong says sorry to every single person in the line. Some of them pat his arm as he goes by._  
_Your Nubbin’s glow brightens as you pass an old woman. She smiles at it and gives it her number to hold._  

#### Complete `a03-02-complete`

_The first door opens on its own, slowly, with the sound of somebody very old clearing their throat._  
**Gang-rim**: Welcome to the jeoseung. The world of the dead. You’ll find it better organised than the other one.  
**Haewonmak**: Better paperwork, anyway.  
**Deok-choon** _(think)_: Day one of forty-nine. We can do this. We can. Can’t we?  
_Your Nubbin gives the old woman’s number back to her, very carefully, before you go through._

## A3-3. The Hell of Murder

*Along with the Gods (Joo Ho-min’s webtoon; the films The Two Worlds, 2017, and The Last 49 Days, 2018): the first trial* · audience: teen · where: Nova’s atlas: the Forty-Nine Days page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A3-2, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A3-2

**Cast:** Gang-rim (Gang-rim, leader of the three guardians, in Along with the Gods (webtoon by Joo Ho-min; films 2017 and 2018); original design and dialogue), Haewonmak (Haewonmak, the fierce guardian, in Along with the Gods (webtoon by Joo Ho-min; films 2017 and 2018); original design and dialogue), Deok-choon (Lee Deok-choon, the kind guardian, in Along with the Gods (webtoon by Joo Ho-min; films 2017 and 2018); original design and dialogue), Ja-hong (Kim Ja-hong, the firefighter, in Along with the Gods (webtoon by Joo Ho-min; films 2017 and 2018); original design and dialogue), The Accuser (The courts’ prosecutor, after the films’ prosecutors; original character), The King of the Hell of Murder (One of the Ten Kings, in Along with the Gods (webtoon by Joo Ho-min; films 2017 and 2018); original design and dialogue), The Mirror Clerk (An original clerk of the mirror archive; no source character)

### Step 1: Attend the first trial, in the Hell of Murder.

Hint: Cross the bridge over the fire lake into the court. · check: `visit:a03-court:murder`

#### Scene `a03-03-court`

_The first court sits on a bridge over a lake of slow fire. The heat comes up through your boots._  
_The King of the Hell of Murder looks down from a desk as high as a house. He has the face of someone who has heard every excuse twice._  
**The Accuser**: Kim Ja-hong. Seven years ago, in a fire, his partner died in a stairwell. Ja-hong came out. His partner did not.  
**The Accuser**: He left a man behind to save his own skin. The court calls that a killing.  
_Ja-hong doesn’t argue. He looks at his boots, the way a man does who has said this to himself for seven years._  
**Deok-choon** _(shock)_: Say something! Tell them!  
**Ja-hong**: It’s true. I came out and he didn’t.  
**The King of the Hell of Murder**: The defence?  
**Gang-rim**: We ask the court’s leave to bring the mirror.  

#### Done `a03-03-court-done`

**The King of the Hell of Murder**: Granted. You have until the lake turns over.  
**Haewonmak**: That’s not long. It turns over a lot.  

### Step 2: Find the stairwell in the mirror archive.

Hint: Search the mirrors with your Nubbin; its glow brightens near the right one. · check: `inspect:a03-mirror:stairwell`

#### Scene `a03-03-mirror`

_Under the bridge there is a hall of mirrors, row after row, each one the size of a door and each one a life._  
**The Mirror Clerk**: Kim Ja-hong. Third aisle, seventh shelf. He’s been a firefighter a long time; there’s a lot of smoke in there. Mind your eyes.  
_In the mirror, fires go by like pages: a kitchen, a warehouse, a bus, a block of flats. In every one he goes in first._  
_Your Nubbin presses its paws against the glass at one fire and goes bright, then brighter._  
_A stairwell, two firefighters and a boy. A beam has come down across the older man’s legs._  
_The older man is shouting at Ja-hong. You can’t hear it, but you don’t need to: he is pointing at the boy, then at the door._  
_Ja-hong carries the boy out, and then, in the mirror, he goes back in, twice. The second time they pull him out by his coat._  

**Choices**

- **Take it straight to the court.**
    - **Gang-rim**: Good. Courts like things straight.
- **Show Ja-hong first.**
    - Ja-hong watches himself go back in. He watches it twice, as if the second time it might go differently.
    - **Ja-hong**: I don’t remember going back. I only remember coming out.

#### Done `a03-03-mirror-done`

**The Mirror Clerk**: I’ll unhook it for you. Carry it flat. Lives don’t like being tipped.  

### Step 3: Bring the mirror to the bench.

Hint: Carry it back up to the court before the lake turns over. · check: `deliver:a03-evidence:murder-bench`

#### Scene `a03-03-verdict`

_The mirror plays the stairwell for the whole court. The fire lake goes very still to watch._  
**The Accuser**: He still came out alive.  
**Gang-rim**: He came out because his partner told him to take the child, and he obeyed. Then he went back. The mirror is plain about it.  
_The King of the Hell of Murder looks for a long time. Then he lifts his brush._  
**The King of the Hell of Murder**: Not guilty. A man who goes back into a fire is not a man who left anyone behind.  
**The King of the Hell of Murder**: The court notes that the accused has been holding his own trial on this for seven years. It may stop now.  

#### Complete `a03-03-complete`

_Out on the far bank, Ja-hong sits down on the sand as if his legs have only just found out what happened._  
**Ja-hong**: I should have gone back a third time.  
**Deok-choon** _(sad)_: You did go back. It’s in the mirror. It’s been in the mirror the whole time.  
**Haewonmak**: One down. Don’t get comfortable. Comfortable is how they get you in the next one.  
_Your Nubbin climbs into Ja-hong’s lap and sits there, glowing, until he puts one big careful hand on its back._  

**Rewards**

## A3-4. The Hell of Indolence

*Along with the Gods (Joo Ho-min’s webtoon; the films The Two Worlds, 2017, and The Last 49 Days, 2018): the second trial* · audience: teen · where: Nova’s atlas: the Forty-Nine Days page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A3-3, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A3-3

### Step 1: Attend the second trial, in the Hell of Indolence.

Hint: Walk the endless road to the court at its end. · check: `visit:a03-court:indolence`

#### Scene `a03-04-road`

_The road to the second court rolls backwards under your feet. You walk and walk and the court stays exactly as far away._  
**Haewonmak**: The only way to get there is to stop trying to get there. Don’t ask me. I didn’t build it.  
_You stop, and the road stops. The court is suddenly right in front of you, looking smug._  
_The King of the Hell of Indolence is reclining on cushions. He has not moved in a very long time, on purpose._  
**The Accuser**: The accused had a mother and a brother. For fifteen years he did not go home. Not for a holiday. Not for a single night.  
**The Accuser**: He worked, yes. He hid in work. A man too busy to love is idle where it counts.  
**The King of the Hell of Indolence**: Interesting. Was he busy, or was he hiding? Show me.  

#### Done `a03-04-road-done`

**Gang-rim**: We need what he did with his time. All of it. Rosters. Receipts. Anything with a date on it.  

### Step 2: Collect the record of Ja-hong’s working years.

Hint: The ledger shelves are behind the court; your Nubbin can reach the low ones. · check: `collect:a03-receipts:ledger`

#### Scene `a03-04-ledger`

_Behind the court there are shelves of ledgers, one for every life. His is thick and grubby at the edges._  
_Shift rosters: nights, doubles, holidays, more nights. Somebody else’s Christmas covered, somebody else’s wedding covered._  
_Your Nubbin pulls out a bundle tied with string from the low shelf. It is receipts for money orders, one a month._  
_Every month for fifteen years, to the same address in a small town. Never a letter with them, just the money._  
**Deok-choon** _(sad)_: He sent everything home. He kept just enough to eat. Look, look, he ate ramyeon for a whole year.  
**Ja-hong**: It wasn’t anything. It was just money.  
**Haewonmak**: It was all your money. That’s a different thing.  

**Choices**

- **Argue that he worked too much, not too little.**
    - **Gang-rim**: Good. Not idle. Tired. The court knows the difference, if it is shown.
- **Argue that it was all for his family.**
    - **Deok-choon**: Yes! Every won of it. You can’t call that hiding. Can you?
- **Ask Ja-hong why he never went home.**
    - He looks at the receipts for a while. He takes one out and smooths it flat with his thumb.
    - **Ja-hong**: Because I couldn’t look at her. Sending money was the bit I could do.

#### Done `a03-04-ledger-done`

_The bundle is heavier than paper should be. Your Nubbin insists on carrying it._  

### Step 3: Wait for the king of indolence to rule.

Hint: Stand by the cushions while he reads. He is in no hurry. · check: `wait:a03-court:indolence`

#### Scene `a03-04-ruling`

_Haewonmak sits down on the floor to wait, which nobody has ever done in front of this king. The king approves._  
_The king reads every receipt. It takes him a very long time, and he enjoys every minute of it._  
**The King of the Hell of Indolence**: This man never rested. Not once in fifteen years. That is a sin of a sort, but not the sort this court tries.  
**The King of the Hell of Indolence**: The charge was idleness. The record says the opposite. Whatever he was hiding from, it was not from work.  
**The King of the Hell of Indolence**: Not guilty. Go away, all of you, and someone take that tired man somewhere he can sit.  
_Deok-choon bursts into tears, which she will do at every verdict from here on._  

#### Complete `a03-04-complete`

_The rolling road lets you go without any argument. On the far side, Ja-hong does sit, because a king told him to._  
**Haewonmak**: Two down. And he cried in neither. That’s suspicious. Usually they cry.  
**Deok-choon** _(happy)_: I cried for him. That counts.  
**Gang-rim**: Next is deceit. It is usually the hardest. Everybody has told a lie.  
**Ja-hong**: I wrote some letters. You should know before we go in. I wrote letters.  

**Rewards**

### Step 1: Hear the charge in the Hell of Deceit.

Hint: Go into the court of echoes and stand with the defence. · check: `visit:a03-court:deceit`

#### Scene `a03-05-court`

_In the third court every word comes back twice: once as it was said, and once as it was meant. It is very noisy._  
**The Accuser**: The accused wrote letters to grieving families. He signed them with the names of the people he failed to save.  
**The Accuser**: “Mum, it didn’t hurt.” “Dad, I wasn’t scared.” Written by a stranger, in a dead child’s name. That is forgery.  
_The echo comes back, and the room hears it the second way too: Please don’t lie awake._  
**The King of the Hell of Deceit**: Is this true?  
**Ja-hong**: Yes. Eleven of them. Over the years. The ones I couldn’t get out.  
**The King of the Hell of Deceit**: Why?  
**Ja-hong**: Because I was there, and their families weren’t. Somebody had to tell them something.  

#### Done `a03-05-court-done`

**Gang-rim**: The court will want to see one of them land. Witness, go and find a letter that was kept.  

### Step 2: Find a family who kept one of the letters.

Hint: Ask the Mirror Clerk; your Nubbin will know the right house. · check: `find:a03-letters:bereaved`

#### Scene `a03-05-letters`

**The Mirror Clerk**: Eleven letters, eleven houses. Most were read once and put in a drawer. One was not. Fourth mirror on the left.  
_In the mirror there is a small flat in the evening. A man is eating dinner alone with the radio on._  
_On the wall by the table there is a letter in a frame, in pencil, in big round writing. Under it, a school photo of a girl._  
_He finishes eating, rinses his bowl, and before he turns the light off he touches the frame. Just the corner._  
_Your Nubbin touches the glass of the mirror in the same place, at the same moment._  
**The Mirror Clerk**: He does that every night. Nine years. He knows it isn’t her writing, you know. He worked it out the first week.  
**The Mirror Clerk**: He kept it up anyway.  

#### Done `a03-05-letters-done`

_The Clerk lends you the mirror. You carry it back the way you carried the first one: flat, and very carefully._

**Gang-rim**: We will use one argument, not three. The court hates a defence that can’t make up its mind.  
**Gang-rim**: Witness. You saw the frame. You decide what we say.

**Choices**
