## A31-1. The Hearth

*Popol Vuh (K’iche’ Maya; written down in the 1550s, Ximénez copy c. 1701), retold: the frame: a K’iche’ kitchen today, and the story asked for* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Hero Twins page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time once Nova’s atlas is in the Grove; the first quest on the page.

**Cast:** Grandmother (A K’iche’ Maya grandmother in the Guatemalan highlands today, the storyteller (our own; drawn and named with the advisers)), Granddaughter (Her granddaughter, about nine, who interrupts (our own; drawn and named with the advisers))

### Step 1: Step into the kitchen on the atlas page.

Hint: Put your hand on the warm page, then go in through the doorway. · check: `visit:a31-kitchen:highlands`

#### Scene `a31-01-page`

_Nova’s atlas falls open at a page that smells of woodsmoke. A hearth, a stack of tortillas, and a green mountain with cloud on its shoulder._  
_There is no title on this one. Just a kitchen._  
_You put your hand flat on the page to hold it still, and it is warm. Then the warmth is a fire, and you are standing in a doorway in the highlands of Guatemala._  
_A grandmother sits by the fire, patting dough into rounds and laying them on a clay griddle. Pat, pat, turn._  
_A girl of about nine is doing her homework at the table, badly, because she keeps watching the door._  
**Granddaughter** _(shock)_: Grandmother. Someone’s at the door. With a… what is that?  
**Grandmother** _(happy)_: A guest, and a small guest. Come in, both of you. The cold gets in.  
_Your Nubbin goes straight to the fire and sits down in front of it, as if it has been coming here for years._  
**Grandmother**: That one knows a good kitchen.  

**Choices**

- **Sit on the low stool she points to.**
    - **Grandmother**: Good. Now you’re the right height for a story.
- **Offer to help with the tortillas.**
    - You try. Yours comes out the shape of a country nobody would want to live in.
    - **Grandmother**: You eat that one yourself. That’s the rule.
- **Let your Nubbin warm its paws.**
    - It holds its paws to the fire, then turns round and warms its back, then its front again, very seriously.

#### Done `a31-01-page-done`

**Granddaughter**: Is it a dog?  
**Grandmother**: It’s a guest. You don’t ask a guest what it is. You give it a tortilla.  
_Your Nubbin is given a tortilla, warm from the griddle. It holds it in both paws like a letter from home._  

### Step 2: Sit by the hearth and listen to the old story.

Hint: Sit on the low stool by the fire and watch the smoke go up. · check: `sit:a31-stool:hearth`

#### Scene `a31-01-story`

**Granddaughter** _(happy)_: Grandmother. Tell the old one. The twins one. With the ball.  
**Grandmother**: You’ve heard it a hundred times.  
**Granddaughter**: So it’s a hundred and one. Please.  
**Grandmother**: Our people wrote it down long ago, in our own language, so it wouldn’t be lost.  
**Grandmother** _(happy)_: People still read it. And grandmothers still tell it, which is better, because you can interrupt a grandmother.  
**Granddaughter**: You can’t interrupt a book.  
**Grandmother**: You can. It just doesn’t notice.  
_She lays another tortilla on the griddle. The smoke goes up past the beam, slow and grey, and you watch it go._  
**Grandmother**: Before the sun was the sun, there were two brothers who played ball…  
_The smoke thickens, and the kitchen goes soft at the edges. You can still hear her voice, but now there is grass under your feet._  

**Choices**

- **Pick your Nubbin up first.**
    - You scoop it off the hearth. It is warm right through, like a loaf.
- **Keep your eyes on the grandmother.**
    - **Grandmother**: I’m still here. I’ll be here the whole time. Go on.

#### Complete `a31-01-complete`

_You are standing on a hillside in the early morning, in a world that is mostly still being made._  
_Somewhere close, something heavy and rubbery goes thock against a wall. Then again: thock._  
**Grandmother**: Listen. That’s them.  
_Your Nubbin’s ears go up. Whatever that noise is, it wants it._

## A31-2. The Ballplayers

*Popol Vuh (K’iche’ Maya; written down in the 1550s, Ximénez copy c. 1701), retold: Hun Hunahpu and Vucub Hunahpu play ball, and the owls summon them* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Hero Twins page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A31-1, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A31-1

**Cast:** Grandmother (A K’iche’ Maya grandmother in the Guatemalan highlands today, the storyteller (our own; drawn and named with the advisers)), Hun Hunahpu (Hun Hunahpu (Jun Junajpu), the twins’ father (Popol Vuh)), Vucub Hunahpu (Vucub Hunahpu (Wuqub’ Junajpu), the twins’ uncle (Popol Vuh)), Owls (The four owl messengers of Xibalba, speaking as one (Popol Vuh))

### Step 1: Watch the two brothers play ball.

Hint: Go down the hill to the ballcourt and watch from the wall. · check: `watch:a31-ballgame:fathers`

#### Scene `a31-02-court`

_Below the hill is a ballcourt: two long sloping walls, with a strip of beaten earth between them._  
_Two men are playing on it, and they are loud about it. Every point gets a shout, and every miss gets a louder one._  
**Hun Hunahpu** _(happy)_: Mine! That was mine, and you know it.  
**Vucub Hunahpu**: It hit your knee. Knees don’t count. Knees have never counted.  
_They never touch the ball with their hands. They take it on the hip, the thigh, the shoulder, and it flies off heavy and black._  
**Grandmother**: Hun Hunahpu and his brother, Vucub Hunahpu. Good players. Terrible neighbours.  
_Down under the court, under the earth, a long way under, somebody is trying to sleep._  
_The ball bounces off the end wall, spins once, and rolls straight towards your Nubbin._  

**Choices**

- **Let your Nubbin have a go.**
    - It hip-bumps the ball, and the ball does not move. Your Nubbin does, backwards, and sits down.
    - **Vucub Hunahpu**: Good form. Wrong direction.
- **Roll it back to them.**
    - **Hun Hunahpu**: Hands! Hands on the ball!
    - **Vucub Hunahpu**: Leave it. Guests get one hand-ball. It’s an old rule. I made it up just now.

### Step 2: Help your Nubbin bring back the ball that went over the wall.

Hint: Follow your Nubbin down the slope and carry the ball back up together. · check: `carry:a31-ball:court`

#### Scene `a31-02-fetch`

_The next shot goes high over the wall and away down the hill. Both brothers look at each other, then at your Nubbin._  
**Hun Hunahpu**: Little one. Would you?  
_Your Nubbin is already gone, bouncing down the slope after the ball, bouncing almost as well as the ball does._  
_You go after it, through long grass and over a stream, and find it sitting proudly on the ball, which is nearly as big as it is._  
_The ball is heavier than you expect. It’s made from tree sap, worked until it bounces, and it smells faintly of smoke._  
**Grandmother**: People here made balls like that long before anyone else in the world had one. Remember that, when you play.  
_You carry it back up between you. Your Nubbin carries the important end._  

**Choices**

- **Let your Nubbin hand it over.**
    - It pushes the ball up to Hun Hunahpu with its whole body. He bows to it, very low, and it bows back.
- **Throw it back over the wall.**
    - It sails over and lands with a thock. A cheer from the court: apparently that counts as a point for somebody.

#### Complete `a31-02-complete`

_Then the owls come. Four of them, out of a clear sky, and they land on the end wall in a row._  
**Owls**: From the lords of Xibalba, One Death and Seven Death. You are loud. You are very loud.  
**Owls**: They say: come down and play against them. Bring your gear.  
**Hun Hunahpu** _(happy)_: They want a game. Good. We’re the best there is.  
_That evening the brothers tie their ball and gear up in the roof of their mother Xmucane’s house, where she won’t have to look at it._  
_They tell her not to worry. People always tell people that._

## A31-3. The Road Down

*Popol Vuh (K’iche’ Maya; written down in the 1550s, Ximénez copy c. 1701), retold: the fathers go down to Xibalba and do not come back; the calabash tree* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Hero Twins page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A31-2, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A31-2

**Cast:** Grandmother (A K’iche’ Maya grandmother in the Guatemalan highlands today, the storyteller (our own; drawn and named with the advisers)), Granddaughter (Her granddaughter, about nine, who interrupts (our own; drawn and named with the advisers))

### Step 1: Listen to the grandmother tell what happened on the road down.

Hint: Stay on the empty road and let her tell it. · check: `watch:a31-road:empty`

#### Scene `a31-03-road`

_The page goes quiet. There is a road going down between rocks, and nobody on it._  
**Grandmother**: This part I tell. You don’t need to see it.  
**Grandmother**: They went down and down, across rivers, to a place where four roads meet.  
**Grandmother**: The black road spoke up and said, I am the way. And they believed it, because they were in a hurry.  
**Grandmother**: At the bottom, lords sat in a row. The brothers bowed and said good day, and the lords didn’t answer, because they were made of wood.  
**Granddaughter** _(happy)_: That’s the funny bit.  
**Grandmother**: It is. The lords of Xibalba thought so too.  
**Grandmother**: Then they were offered a seat, and the seat was a hot stone. Then a night in a dark house, and a test nobody could win.  
**Grandmother** _(sad)_: They lost every test. And they didn’t come back up.  
_The road stays empty. A little wind moves the dust on it._  

**Choices**

- **Sit down at the side of the road.**
    - You sit. Your Nubbin sits too, close enough that you can feel it breathing.
- **Ask what happened to them.**
    - **Grandmother**: They stayed down there. That’s enough for now. The story isn’t finished with them.

### Step 2: Look at the tree by the road.

Hint: The tree full of gourds stands just off the road. Go and look; you needn’t touch. · check: `inspect:a31-gourd-tree:road`

#### Scene `a31-03-tree`

_By the side of the road there is a tree you didn’t notice before. It is heavy with round green gourds._  
_Beside it, the atlas prints a line from an old book in English._  
_In the atlas’s margin, in brown type: “The head of Hun Hunahpu was, however, suspended from a tree, which speedily became covered with gourds…”_  
**Granddaughter** _(shock)_: Grandmother. His head?  
**Grandmother** _(soft)_: Among the gourds, and you can’t tell which. The lords said nobody was to touch that tree.  
**Grandmother**: So of course somebody did. Eat your tortilla.  
_Your Nubbin looks up into the branches for a long time. Then it pats the trunk once, the way it pats you when you’re tired._  

**Choices**

- **Look for the gourd that’s different.**
    - You look, and every gourd is the same. That’s the point, you think.
- **Leave the tree be.**
    - You step back onto the road. Some things you don’t need to find.

#### Complete `a31-03-complete`

_The page turns a little, in a wind from nowhere. The road goes on being empty._  
**Grandmother**: Hun Hunahpu had two sons already, and his mother Xmucane at home. They waited. Nobody came up the road.  
**Granddaughter**: And then?  
**Grandmother** _(happy)_: And then a girl went to look at a tree she wasn’t allowed to look at. That’s how most good stories start.

## A31-4. The Calabash Tree

*Popol Vuh (K’iche’ Maya; written down in the 1550s, Ximénez copy c. 1701), retold: Xquic and the gourd, the owls, and the heart of sap* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Hero Twins page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A31-3, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A31-3

**Cast:** Grandmother (A K’iche’ Maya grandmother in the Guatemalan highlands today, the storyteller (our own; drawn and named with the advisers)), Granddaughter (Her granddaughter, about nine, who interrupts (our own; drawn and named with the advisers)), Xquic (Xquic (Xkik’), daughter of a lord of Xibalba, the twins’ mother (Popol Vuh)), The Gourd (The head of Hun Hunahpu in the calabash tree, here only ever “the gourd” (Popol Vuh)), Owls (The four owl messengers of Xibalba, speaking as one (Popol Vuh))

### Step 1: Watch the girl who comes to the forbidden tree.

Hint: Hide behind the rock near the tree and watch. · check: `watch:a31-xquic:tree`

#### Scene `a31-04-xquic`

_Down in Xibalba it is never quite night and never quite day. A girl comes along the road on her own, looking over her shoulder._  
**Grandmother**: Xquic. Daughter of one of the lords. She’d heard about the tree, and she was not good at being told no.  
**Xquic** _(think)_: Everybody says don’t go near it. Nobody says why. If they said why, I might not go.  
_She stops under the branches. You and your Nubbin are behind a rock, which is more or less hiding._  
**Xquic**: Are they sweet? Would it be so terrible if I picked one?  
**The Gourd**: They’re only round things in a tree. You don’t want them. Hold out your hand.  
_In the atlas’s margin, in brown type: “…the maiden stretched out her hand, and the gourd spat into the palm.”_  
**Granddaughter** _(shock)_: Ew.  
**Grandmother** _(happy)_: It’s a blessing. Blessings are often a bit ew.  
**Xquic** _(shock)_: It’s gone. There’s nothing in my hand.  
**The Gourd**: Nothing you can see. Go home. Nothing will hurt you. You’ll understand later.  

**Choices**

- **Stay hidden.**
    - You stay behind the rock. Your Nubbin, who thinks hiding is a game, keeps popping up to see if it’s won.
- **Step out and walk her to the road.**
    - **Xquic**: Who are you? No, don’t tell me. I’ve had enough surprises for one day.

### Step 2: Go with the owls and Xquic into the forest.

Hint: Walk behind the owls through the red-barked trees. · check: `escort:a31-xquic:forest`

#### Scene `a31-04-owls`

_Some months go by in the margin. When the page settles, Xquic stands in her father’s hall, and everybody can see she is expecting a baby._  
**Grandmother**: Her father was angry. Lords of Xibalba are always angry; it’s how you know them.  
**Grandmother**: He told his four owls to take her out to the forest and bring back her heart in a bowl. The owls didn’t like it at all.  
_You walk behind them, through trees with red bark. The owls keep stopping and looking at each other._  
**Owls** _(sad)_: We don’t want to do this. We’ve never wanted to do anything we were told.  
**Xquic**: Then don’t. My heart is mine. It doesn’t belong to them.  
**Owls**: But he’ll want to see something in the bowl.  
**Xquic**: Then let’s give him something. Look at that tree.  

**Choices**

- **Go and look at the tree.**
    - The bark is cut in one place. Where it’s cut, a thick red sap is running, slow as honey.
- **Tell the owls they can be brave.**
    - **Owls**: Can we? Nobody’s ever asked us to.
    - All four owls stand up a little taller.

### Step 3: Help press the red sap into a heart.

Hint: Hold the bowl under the cut bark while the sap runs, then help shape it. · check: `craft:a31-sap-heart:forest`

#### Scene `a31-04-sap`

**Xquic**: Help me. Catch it in the bowl. Then we shape it.  
_You hold the bowl. Your Nubbin, who has never been asked to help with anything this important, holds the edge of it as well._  
_The sap fills it slowly. Xquic presses it into a round, lumpy shape with her thumbs, and the owls tell her where it needs more lump._  
**Owls**: More on that side. Hearts are not neat.  
_When it’s done, it shines dark red in the bowl. It even smells right: warm, and sweet, and a little of woodsmoke._  
**Xquic**: Now go. Tell him you did it. Tell him I’m gone.  
**Owls** _(sad)_: Where will you go?  
**Xquic** _(soft)_: Up. To the world. There’s a grandmother up there who doesn’t know she has me yet.  

**Choices**

- **Press the last of it smooth.**
    - You smooth the top with your thumb. The owls say it is too neat now, and rough it up again.
- **Let your Nubbin leave a pawprint in it.**
    - A tiny paw-shaped dent, right on top, and the owls decide that’s fine. Hearts have marks on them.

#### Complete `a31-04-complete`

_The owls carry the bowl back to Xibalba. The lords put the sap heart on the fire, and the smell fills the hall, and they all lean in._  
**Grandmother** _(happy)_: And while they were sniffing, the owls flew off and showed Xquic the way up. The lords were fooled by a smell.  
**Granddaughter**: By owls!  
**Grandmother**: By owls. Never trust a lord who can’t tell a heart from a tree.  
_Your Nubbin sniffs its paw for a long time afterwards. It still smells a bit of the tree._

## A31-5. Xmucane’s Test

*Popol Vuh (K’iche’ Maya; written down in the 1550s, Ximénez copy c. 1701), retold: Xquic comes to Xmucane, and the net of corn from one plant* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Hero Twins page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A31-4, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A31-4

**Cast:** Grandmother (A K’iche’ Maya grandmother in the Guatemalan highlands today, the storyteller (our own; drawn and named with the advisers)), Granddaughter (Her granddaughter, about nine, who interrupts (our own; drawn and named with the advisers)), Xquic (Xquic (Xkik’), daughter of a lord of Xibalba, the twins’ mother (Popol Vuh)), Xmucane (Xmucane (Ixmukane), the twins’ grandmother (Popol Vuh))

### Step 1: Walk Xquic up to Xmucane’s house.

Hint: Go up the hill to the house with the garden and the fire. · check: `escort:a31-xquic:xmucane-house`

#### Scene `a31-05-door`

_The page turns to a little house of poles and thatch on a hillside, with a garden and a fire. It looks a lot like the kitchen you came in by._  
_An old woman stands in the doorway with two older boys behind her: Xmucane, and Hun Hunahpu’s first sons._  
**Xquic** _(soft)_: Grandmother. I’m your daughter-in-law. I’m carrying your son’s children.  
**Xmucane** _(angry)_: My son is gone, down in Xibalba. Go away. You’re a liar, or a trick, or both.  
**Xquic**: He isn’t gone. Not all of him. You’ll see.  
**Xmucane**: Will I. Then here is a test. My grandsons are hungry.  
**Xmucane**: There’s a garden down the hill. Bring me a whole net of corn, full, and I’ll believe you.  
_You look down the hill. In the garden there is one corn plant: one._  

**Choices**

- **Offer to carry the net.**
    - **Xquic**: Thank you. I think the net is the easy part.
- **Tell Xmucane she might be telling the truth.**
    - **Xmucane**: Might. Everybody might. Go and get the corn.

### Step 2: Go down to the garden with one plant in it.

Hint: Sit down by the corn plant with Xquic and wait. · check: `visit:a31-garden:one-plant`

#### Scene `a31-05-garden`

_One plant, with one ear on it. Xquic stands in front of it with the empty net in her hands._  
**Xquic** _(sad)_: If I pick this, there’s nothing. If I don’t, there’s nothing.  
_She sits down on the earth. Your Nubbin sits next to her, and so, after a moment, do you._  
_Then she says the names of the ones who look after food, softly, as if she is asking friends for a favour._  
_She draws the silk from the one ear, gently, and lays it in the net. And the net is full._  
**Grandmother** _(happy)_: Full, from one ear. That’s the part you hold on to.  
_Your Nubbin climbs into the net to check. It is corn all the way down._  

**Choices**

- **Touch the ear left on the plant.**
    - It’s still there. Whatever happened, the plant wasn’t robbed.
- **Say thank you to nobody in particular.**
    - **Xquic**: Yes. That. Thank you.

### Step 3: Carry the net of corn up to Xmucane.

Hint: Take one end of the net; Xquic takes the other. Go slowly on the hill. · check: `carry:a31-net:xmucane-house`

#### Scene `a31-05-carry`

_The net is heavier than the ball, heavier than the bowl, heavier than anything you’ve ever carried in a book._  
_You take one end and Xquic takes the other. Your Nubbin takes the middle, which mostly means it rides in it._  
_Halfway up, the older brothers come out to watch. They don’t help._  
**Xquic**: Artists, those two. They play the flute, and paint, and sing. They never carry anything.  
_You put the net down in front of Xmucane. She looks at it, and goes on looking for a long time._  
**Xmucane** _(think)_: One plant. I planted it myself.  
_Then she goes down to the garden on her own, and finds the print of the net in the earth, where it lay._  

**Choices**

- **Wait outside the door.**
    - You wait by the door, and your Nubbin waits by your boot. It takes a while.
- **Sit down on the net.**
    - You sit on the corn, and so does your Nubbin. Xquic laughs for the first time since the tree.

#### Complete `a31-05-complete`

**Xmucane** _(soft)_: Well. You’re my daughter-in-law, then. Come inside. It’s cold.  
_That’s all she says. She puts another log on the fire, moves her own stool closer to it, and puts Xquic on it._  
**Grandmother** _(happy)_: That’s how grandmothers say sorry. You get the good stool.  
**Granddaughter**: You never give me the good stool.  
**Grandmother**: You never carried a net of corn up a hill.

## A31-6. The Older Brothers

*Popol Vuh (K’iche’ Maya; written down in the 1550s, Ximénez copy c. 1701), retold: the twins are born; One Monkey and One Artisan go up the tree* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Hero Twins page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A31-5, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A31-5

**Cast:** Grandmother (A K’iche’ Maya grandmother in the Guatemalan highlands today, the storyteller (our own; drawn and named with the advisers)), Hunahpu (Hunahpu (Junajpu), one of the Hero Twins (Popol Vuh)), Xbalanque (Xbalanque (Xb’alanke), one of the Hero Twins (Popol Vuh)), One Monkey (One Monkey (Jun B’atz’), the twins’ older half-brother (Popol Vuh)), One Artisan (One Artisan (Jun Chowen), the twins’ older half-brother (Popol Vuh)), Xmucane (Xmucane (Ixmukane), the twins’ grandmother (Popol Vuh))

### Step 1: Meet the twins.

Hint: Wait by Xmucane’s house until two boys come down the hill. · check: `talk:a31-twins`

#### Scene `a31-06-born`

_Twins are born on the mountainside: Hunahpu and Xbalanque. They are loud from the first minute._  
_The older brothers, One Monkey and One Artisan, don’t like the noise. They don’t much like anybody who isn’t a flute._  
**One Monkey** _(angry)_: Put them outside. They cry. We’re composing.  
**Grandmother** _(happy)_: So the twins grew up outside, mostly. In the hills, with blowguns. And cheeky? You have no idea.  
_The page skips them forward, years in a breath. Two boys come down the slope, each with a long blowgun over his shoulder._  
**Hunahpu** _(happy)_: Who’s this? A guest? Xbalanque, we’ve got a guest. And a… what is it?  
**Xbalanque**: It’s a guest’s guest. Hello, guest’s guest.  
_Your Nubbin looks up at them, and something is settled between the three of them at once: friends._  

**Choices**

- **Say hello.**
    - **Hunahpu**: Good. Are you hungry? Nobody here eats till our brothers have eaten. Which is never, if you’re us.
- **Ask about the blowguns.**
    - **Xbalanque**: Clay pellets. You blow very hard, and you hit what you meant to. Usually. Ask him about the time he didn’t.

### Step 2: Watch the older brothers climb the tree.

Hint: Stand back from the tree. Well back. · check: `watch:a31-tree:grows`

#### Scene `a31-06-tree`

_The twins come home with no birds. The older brothers, who have eaten everyone’s share for years, frown._  
**Hunahpu** _(think)_: We shot plenty. They’re all stuck up a tree. We can’t climb it. Somebody taller should.  
**One Artisan**: Fine. Show us. Artists can climb.  
_The tree is very ordinary. The two older brothers go up it, complaining about the bark._

## A31-17. The Hearth Again

*Popol Vuh (K’iche’ Maya; written down in the 1550s, Ximénez copy c. 1701), retold: the frame: the story finished* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Hero Twins page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A31-16, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A31-16

**Cast:** Grandmother (A K’iche’ Maya grandmother in the Guatemalan highlands today, the storyteller (our own; drawn and named with the advisers)), Granddaughter (Her granddaughter, about nine, who interrupts (our own; drawn and named with the advisers))

### Step 1: Hear the end of the story by the hearth.

Hint: Stay on the low stool while the smoke clears. · check: `sit:a31-hearth:again`

#### Scene `a31-17-hearth`

_The smoke clears. You are on the low stool in the kitchen, with your Nubbin asleep on your feet and a cold tortilla in your hand._  
**Grandmother**: And that’s the story.  
**Granddaughter** _(think)_: Is it true?  
**Grandmother**: The sun came up this morning, didn’t it?  
**Granddaughter**: …Yes.  
**Grandmother** _(happy)_: Then eat a tortilla. Go home.  
_Outside the window, the green mountain has the evening on it. Somebody down the road is calling a dog in for its supper._  

**Choices**

- **Thank her for the story.**
    - **Grandmother**: Thank my grandmother. And hers. I only carried it.
- **Ask if you can come back and hear it again.**
    - **Grandmother**: It’ll be here. It’s been here a long time.

### Step 2: Give your Nubbin a warm tortilla before you go.

Hint: Take a tortilla from the griddle and give it to your Nubbin. · check: `feed:a31-tortilla:nubbin`

#### Scene `a31-17-ball`

_The girl has fetched something from under the table: a rubber ball, black and heavy and scuffed all over._  
**Granddaughter** _(happy)_: Your little one chased the ball the whole story. I could tell. It kept twitching.  
**Granddaughter**: No hands. Hips. Like this.  
_She bumps the ball off her hip against the wall, thock, and your Nubbin is awake at once._  
_It hip-bumps the ball back, and the ball goes the wrong way, as usual. The girl laughs so hard she has to sit down._  
**Granddaughter**: You can have it. I’ve got another. Mine’s better. Don’t tell it.  
**Grandmother**: Give the little one a tortilla first. Nobody leaves this kitchen hungry.  

**Choices**

- **Give your Nubbin a warm tortilla.**
    - It eats it in four bites, slowly, which for a Nubbin is a mark of great respect.
- **Share it, half and half.**
    - Half each. The girl says that’s fair, and her grandmother says that’s how it’s done.

#### Complete `a31-17-complete`

_The smoke rises past the beam, and the kitchen goes soft, and you are back at your own table with the atlas open._  
_There is a black rubber ball on the floor. Your Nubbin is already sitting on it._  
_And caught in the atlas’s spine there is an ear of corn, dry and golden, the colour of a long walk in the sun._  
_You hang it from the middle beam of the cottage. It turns a little in the draught from the door._  
_Out by the pond, the Nubbins come to look at the new ball. Nobody uses hands, and nobody has to be told._  

**Rewards**

- Ear of corn (decor): An ear of corn that hangs from the cottage’s middle beam: golden if you went down with the twins, green if you stayed and watched.
- Rubber ball (item): A black rubber ball, heavy and scuffed, from a girl in the highlands who says hers is better.
- Hip-ball (feature): Nubbins play a hip-bump ballgame on the flat grass by the pond with the rubber ball. No hands allowed.
