# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Pixel's Arcade, cartridge PA1: The Chao Altar

## PA1-1. The Cabinet

- `pa1-01-s1`: Look at Pixel’s big game box. · _It is in the lantern shed, by the back wall._
- `pa1-01-s2`: Push the cartridge in. Step through! · _Push it in the slot. Step on the plate._

#### `sprout:pa1-01-shed` · [main](main.md#pa1-01-shed)

_The lantern shed smells of sawdust. Something is humming._  
_It is a big old game box. It has a dent in the side._  
**Pixel**: Don’t touch the coin slot. A mouse family lived in there!  
**Pixel**: I fixed it. One fuse, four wires. Three whole days.  
**Pixel**: Byte says it might catch fire. Byte says that about toast.  
_Pixel lifts a flap. Inside sit five cartridges in a row._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, ask): count to 5  
> **Pixel**: How many cartridges are in the row? (Count them together.)  
> - ✅ Five → Five! One, two, three, four, five. Five worlds.
> - Three → Let’s count again. One, two, three, four, five. Five!
> _For grown-ups: Your child counted five cartridges._

**Pixel**: Each one is a whole world. You go in. I stay here.  
_The label shows a garden, a pool, and little round creatures._  
_Your Nubbin presses its face to the picture. It goes very still._  

**Choices**

- **They look like our Nubbins.**
    - **Pixel**: Don’t they? Like cousins.
- **Is it safe in there?**
    - **Pixel**: Safe as a game. You can’t get hurt. You might get wet feet.
    - **Pixel**: And I will talk to you the whole time.
- **How old is this thing?**
    - **Pixel**: Very old. Older than the sawmill roof.

#### `sprout:pa1-01-shed-done` · [main](main.md#pa1-01-shed-done)

_Pixel blows the dust off the cartridge. Puff!_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): blowing moves dust  
> _Pixel blows. The dust flies off. What moved the dust?_  
> - ✅ The air from her puff → Yes! Moving air pushes dust away. Puff!
> - Magic → Not magic. Her puff is moving air, and air can push dust. Puff!
> _For grown-ups: Your child noticed that moving air pushes dust._

_She wipes the shiny bit on her sleeve._  
**Pixel**: Clean contacts, happy cartridge. Everyone knows that.  

#### `sprout:pa1-01-shed-done-clank` · [main](main.md#pa1-01-shed-done-clank)

_Variant of `pa1-01-shed-done`._

_Pixel blows the dust off the cartridge. Puff!_  
_She wipes the shiny bit on her sleeve._  
**Pixel**: Clean contacts, happy cartridge. Everyone knows that.  
_Clank sits on top of the cabinet. His legs dangle over the screen._  
**Clank**: Announcement: I am the lookout. Lookouts sit on top. I am on top.  
**Pixel**: I said lookouts sit still.  
**Clank**: I am sitting very still. Look how still. The stillest ever.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Approaches to Learning, ask): listening to what was asked  
> **Pixel**: I asked Clank to sit still. His legs are swinging. Is he sitting still?  
> - ✅ Not quite! His legs are moving → Right. Still means all of you. Legs too, Clank.
> - Yes, very still → Look at those legs swing! Still means all of you. Legs too.
> _For grown-ups: Your child checked whether an instruction was really followed._

_His legs keep swinging. Bonk. Bonk. One heel hits the screen._  

#### `sprout:pa1-01-slot` · [main](main.md#pa1-01-slot)

_Clunk! The cartridge goes in. The screen goes blue, then white._  
_A sound like the sea comes out of the speaker. Shhhh._  
**Pixel**: Player two, ready!  
**Pixel**: Rules. I can see you. I can’t come in. Say if you want out.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, ask): asking for help  
> **Pixel**: If you feel scared in there, what can you do?  
> - ✅ Say so, and Pixel helps → Yes! Just say the word. I am right here.
> - Keep quiet → You don’t have to keep quiet. Say so, and I will help.
> _For grown-ups: Your child learned to ask for help when scared._

_The screen is a window now. Warm air comes through. It smells of grass._  
_Your Nubbin looks at you. Then at the window. Then at you._  
**You**: Together, then.  
_You pick up your Nubbin and lean into the light._  

#### `sprout:pa1-01-slot-done` · [main](main.md#pa1-01-slot-done)

_Behind you, the shed goes small and far away._  
_Like a room seen through a keyhole._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): far-away things look small  
> _The shed looks tiny now. Is it near, or far away?_  
> - ✅ Far away → Yes! Far-away things look small. Near things look big.
> - Near → It looks small because it is far away now.
> _For grown-ups: Your child noticed that far-away things look small._

**Pixel**: See you on the other side! Well, I’ll see you.  

#### `sprout:pa1-01-complete` · [main](main.md#pa1-01-complete)

_Bump! You land on soft grass._  
_There is a pool, a palm tree, and stepping stones._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.2, ask): naming shapes  
> **Pixel**: Your hair has gone square! What shape is a square?  
> - ✅ Four straight sides → Yes! Four sides, four corners. Square hair!
> - Round → Round is a circle. A square has four straight sides and four corners.
> _For grown-ups: Your child described a square._

**Pixel**: You’re in! Your hair has gone a bit square. It suits you.  
_Your Nubbin rolls off your arm. It sits up and stares._  

## PA1-2. A Garden Like Ours

- `pa1-02-s1`: Meet the Chao in the garden. · _Walk round the pool. Your Nubbin will find them._
- `pa1-02-s2`: Look at the little gate. · _It is between the grass and the hotel._

#### `sprout:pa1-02-garden` · [main](main.md#pa1-02-garden)

_It is a garden with a glass roof. You can see the sky._  
_Eggs sit in the grass. Little round creatures wobble about._  
_Each one has a tiny ball floating over its head._  
**Pixel**: Those are Chao. It says so on the sleeve. CHAO.  
_Your Nubbin walks into the middle. A Chao sits on it!_  
_The Chao gets up and pats your Nubbin. Its ball turns into a heart._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): reading a feeling from a sign  
> _The Chao’s ball turned into a heart. How does the Chao feel?_  
> - ✅ Happy → Yes! A heart means happy. It likes your Nubbin.
> - Cross → Cross would be a spiral. A heart means happy!
> _For grown-ups: Your child read a feeling from a sign._

**Pixel**: They’re cousins. They are absolutely cousins.  
_A Chao gives your Nubbin half a fruit. Your Nubbin gives back a pebble._  

#### `sprout:pa1-02-garden-done` · [main](main.md#pa1-02-garden-done)

_You find a watering can by the pool._  
_You fill the trough under the fruit tree. Glug, glug._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, ask): what plants need  
> _You give the tree water. Why does a tree need water?_  
> - ✅ To grow and stay alive → Yes! Plants drink water with their roots, to live and grow.
> - To have a bath → Trees don’t bath! They drink water through their roots, to grow.
> _For grown-ups: Your child learned that plants need water to live._

_Three Chao come to watch, very seriously._  

#### `sprout:pa1-02-gate` · [main](main.md#pa1-02-gate)

_The garden door opens onto the hotel. A radio is on, loud._  
**Radio**: …a creature made of water. Please stay indoors…  
_Far away, sirens. The Chao don’t mind. They don’t know what sirens are._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Language and Communication, ask): what a siren is for  
> _Nee-naw, nee-naw! What is a siren for?_  
> - ✅ To say “make way, hurry!” → Yes! A loud siren means helpers are coming. Make way!
> - To sing a song → It is loud, not a song. It says: helpers are coming, make way!
> _For grown-ups: Your child learned what a siren means._

**Pixel**: Hmm. That’s new.  
_The garden has a little wooden gate, with a latch and a key._  
**Pixel**: We could leave it open, so they can wander, like ours do.  
_A Chao is sitting on the latch, swinging its feet._  

**Choices**

- **Leave it open. Let them come and go.**
    - You prop the gate with a stone. The Chao rides it open. Wheee!
    - **Pixel**: Chao who go out and come home, like ours. All right.
- _(left out in this band; plays as `open`)_

#### `sprout:pa1-02-gate-done` · [main](main.md#pa1-02-gate-done)

_The sirens go off toward the square._  
_Your Nubbin is the last to leave the pool. It looks back twice._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): saying goodbye kindly  
> _Your Nubbin looks back at its new friends. What can you do?_  
> - ✅ Wave and say “see you soon” → Yes! A wave says goodbye nicely. Wave!
> - Run off → Running off is not kind. A wave says: see you soon!
> _For grown-ups: Your child practised a kind goodbye._


#### `sprout:pa1-02-complete` · [main](main.md#pa1-02-complete)

_The radio is still talking. The man turns it up, then down, then up._  
**Pixel**: Let’s go and look. Just look. From far away.  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, ask): keeping a safe distance  
> **Pixel**: Something big and strange is in the square. Where do we stand?  
> - ✅ Far back, where it’s safe → Yes! Far back. Look with your eyes, not your hands.
> - Right up close → Not close! Stand far back and look with your eyes.
> _For grown-ups: Your child learned to keep a safe distance from something strange._

**Pixel**: I said “far away”. Remember I said it.  

## PA1-3. The Water Creature

- `pa1-03-s1`: Go and see what is in the square. · _Through the hotel, out to the tape._
- `pa1-03-s2`: Hide behind the bench and watch. · _Keep your Nubbin behind the bench._

#### `sprout:pa1-03-square` · [main](main.md#pa1-03-square)

_The square is bright glass and fountains. Today it has police tape too._  
_In the middle stands something made of clear water._  
_It is shaped like a person. Its heart glows green._  
**Police Officer**: Stay back, please. Behind the line. You too, and your… whatever that is.  
_Your Nubbin holds your shirt with both hands._  
**Pixel**: Look at its head! Look at the shape!  
_Its head curls up and over. Just like a Chao. Just like your Nubbin._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): spotting what is the same  
> **Pixel**: The creature’s head and your Nubbin’s head. Same, or different?  
> - ✅ The same curl → Yes! Both curl up and over. Same shape!
> - Different → Look again. Both curl up and over. Same shape!
> _For grown-ups: Your child compared two shapes and found a match._


#### `sprout:pa1-03-square-done` · [main](main.md#pa1-03-square-done)

_Then a blue blur zooms down the street. Whoosh!_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.1.4, ask): sound words  
> _Whoosh! What does “whoosh” tell you?_  
> - ✅ Something went past fast → Yes! Whoosh is the sound of fast. Say it: whoosh!
> - Something is asleep → Asleep would be “zzz”. Whoosh means fast! Whoosh!
> _For grown-ups: Your child matched a sound word to its meaning._

_Everyone’s hair stands up._  

#### `sprout:pa1-03-sonic` · [main](main.md#pa1-03-sonic)

**Sonic**: Hey, hey! Everybody back! I’ve got this one.  
_A blue hedgehog skids to a stop. He is grinning._  
_He bounces. The creature splashes. You stay behind the bench._  
_Then the creature pours into a drain, all at once. Glug._  
**Sonic**: Huh. Slippery. Didn’t even say goodbye.  
_Just before it goes, it looks at your Nubbin._  
_It does not look cross. It looks lost._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): naming a feeling  
> _The creature looked lost. How does lost feel?_  
> - ✅ Scared and alone → Yes. Lost feels scared and alone. A friend helps.
> - Happy → Lost is not happy. It feels scared and alone. A friend helps.
> _For grown-ups: Your child named the feeling of being lost._

**Pixel**: Did you see? It wasn’t angry. It was lost.  

**Choices**

- **Tell Sonic it looked lost.**
    - **Sonic**: Lost things still splash. Thanks, though.
- **Look down the drain.**
    - The water runs away fast. It glows green.
- **Say nothing.**

#### `sprout:pa1-03-sonic-done` · [main](main.md#pa1-03-sonic-done)

_Sonic is gone, fast as he came._  

> **Lesson** (science, 2-ESS2-3, ask): water takes the shape of its container  
> **Pixel**: The creature poured down the drain. Why can water do that?  
> - ✅ Water flows and fits any shape → Yes! Water is a liquid. It flows and fits any hole.
> - Water is hard → Ice is hard. Water flows. It fits any hole it finds!
> _For grown-ups: Your child learned that liquid water flows into any shape._

_The police roll up their tape._  
**Pixel**: So that’s the water creature. We will see it again.  

#### `sprout:pa1-03-complete` · [main](main.md#pa1-03-complete)

_A small pink light bobs above the drain. It is plum-sized._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): size words  
> _The light is the size of a plum. Is that big, or small?_  
> - ✅ Small → Yes! Plum-sized is small. Small enough to hold.
> - Big → A plum fits in your hand. That is small!
> _For grown-ups: Your child compared a size to a plum._

_It comes to your face. It circles your Nubbin once. Then it waits._  
**Pixel**: That’s not on the sleeve either.  

## PA1-4. The Light

- `pa1-04-s1`: Follow the pink light. · _It waits for you down the quiet street._
- `pa1-04-s2`: Sit by the pool with Tikal. · _Sit on the warm stone, quietly._

#### `sprout:pa1-04-follow` · [main](main.md#pa1-04-follow)

_The pink light drifts down a quiet street. It waits for you._  
_It is warm. Not hot like a lamp. Warm like a hand._  

> **Lesson** (science, K.MD.A.2, ask): warm and hot  
> _Warm like a hand, or hot like a lamp. Which is safe to touch?_  
> - ✅ Warm like a hand → Yes! Warm is safe. Hot can burn. We don’t touch hot lamps.
> - Hot like a lamp → Hot lamps can burn. Warm, like a hand, is safe to touch.
> _For grown-ups: Your child told warm from hot._

**Pixel**: There’s no button for this. It isn’t me.  
_Your Nubbin reaches up. The light dips to be touched._  
**Pixel**: It likes your Nubbin. That’s a good sign.  
_At the end of the street, the light grows and grows._  

#### `sprout:pa1-04-follow-done` · [main](main.md#pa1-04-follow-done)

_The city sounds go out, like a candle._  
_Birds sing instead. It smells of warm stone._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, ask): listening for sounds  
> _Close your eyes. What can you hear now? (Wait for an answer.)_  
> - ✅ Birds singing → Yes! Tweet, tweet. Birds. The city is gone.
> - Cars and sirens → Not any more! Listen. Tweet, tweet. Birds.
> _For grown-ups: Your child listened for a sound in the story._

**Pixel**: Keeper? The screen’s gone gold. Where are you?  

#### `sprout:pa1-04-shrine` · [main](main.md#pa1-04-shrine)

_You stand on old stone steps by a jungle. It is morning, long ago._  
_On a stone altar sits a huge green jewel. It glows._  
_Around it are pools of clear water, full of Chao._  
_A girl sits with her feet in the pool. She has long braids._  
**Tikal**: Oh! A visitor. And a little one. Sit down quietly.  
**Tikal**: I’m Tikal. Your little one looks like a cousin of ours.  
_Then the water rises. It is the water creature._  
_It is not lost now. It stands tall and watches over the Chao._  
**Tikal**: Don’t be afraid. That’s Chaos. It looks after them.  
**Tikal**: Some say it’s a monster. It isn’t. It just worries the most.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): worrying about someone  
> **Tikal**: Chaos worries about the Chao. Who worries about you?  
> - ✅ The grown-ups who love me → Yes. Worrying is a kind of loving. Chaos loves the Chao.
> - Nobody → Someone does! The grown-ups who love you. Worrying is a kind of loving.
> _For grown-ups: Your child talked about who looks after them._


**Choices**

- **Bow to Chaos.**
    - You bow. Chaos lowers its head, just a little.
- **Ask Tikal what it is.**
    - **Tikal**: It was a Chao once. The jewel’s light changed it.
- **Watch your Nubbin.**
    - Your Nubbin floats to Chaos’s feet. Chaos turns it the right way up.

#### `sprout:pa1-04-shrine-done` · [main](main.md#pa1-04-shrine-done)

_The gold comes back. The birds go quiet._  
_You are in the street, with your hand on a wall._  
**Pixel**: You were gone nine seconds. Nine! You look like it was all afternoon.  

> **Lesson** (maths, ELOF: Mathematics Development, ask): short and long times  
> **Pixel**: Nine seconds, or all afternoon. Which is longer?  
> - ✅ All afternoon → Yes! An afternoon is hours. Nine seconds is a blink.
> - Nine seconds → Count to nine. Done already! An afternoon is much longer.
> _For grown-ups: Your child compared a short time with a long one._


#### `sprout:pa1-04-complete` · [main](main.md#pa1-04-complete)

**Pixel**: That was the water creature. But kind.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, ask): then and now  
> **Pixel**: Long ago, Chaos was kind and calm. Now it looks lost. Which came first?  
> - ✅ Kind and calm → Yes. First kind, long ago. Lost came after. Something happened.
> - Lost → The pool was long, long ago. First kind, then lost. Something happened.
> _For grown-ups: Your child put two story events in order._

**You**: It was looking after them.  
**Pixel**: Then what happened to it?  
_The pink light bobs at your shoulder and goes quiet._

## PA1-6. The Shattered Emerald

- `pa1-06-s1`: Visit the floating island. · _Tails flies you there. Climb the steps._
- `pa1-06-s2`: Help Knuckles find the green pieces. · _Look in the ferns on the left._

#### `sprout:pa1-06-island` · [main](main.md#pa1-06-island)

_High in the sky floats a whole island! Tails flies you there._  
_In the middle is a shrine with steps. On top, the big green jewel._  
_Someone sits on the steps with his arms folded, glaring._  
**Knuckles**: Who let you up here? Nobody comes up here.  
**Knuckles**: I’m Knuckles. I guard the Master Emerald. Don’t touch it. Don’t lick it.  
_Your Nubbin, who was about to lick it, sits down._  
**Knuckles**: …It can stay. It looks like a Chao. Chao lived up here once.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, ask): rules keep things safe  
> _Knuckles says: don’t touch, don’t lick. Why does he have rules?_  
> - ✅ To keep the jewel safe → Yes. The jewel is precious. Rules keep precious things safe.
> - Because he is mean → Not mean. He is a guard. Rules keep precious things safe.
> _For grown-ups: Your child thought about why a rule exists._

_Then the sky goes dark! The jewel flashes. The steps shake._  
_Water rises out of the jewel and strikes it. Crack!_  
_The jewel breaks into a hundred green pieces. They fly everywhere._  
**Dr. Eggman**: Splendid work, Chaos! Enjoy your swim, Knuckles!  
_The island tips, slowly, like a big ship. Then it falls._  

#### `sprout:pa1-06-island-done` · [main](main.md#pa1-06-island-done)

_You hold the steps and hold your Nubbin._  
_SPLASH! The island lands in the sea, with a long sigh._  
_When the spray settles, it floats. Everyone is fine._
