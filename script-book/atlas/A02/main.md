### Step 1: Step into the ink-and-wash page and climb to the top of the mountain.

Hint: Touch the page, then follow the smell of peaches uphill. · check: `explore:a02-mountain:summit`

#### <a id="a02-01-page"></a>Scene `a02-01-page`

_Nova’s atlas falls open at a page painted in ink and wash: a mountain of peach trees, a waterfall, and clouds drawn as curls._  
_In the margin Nova has pencilled: “A very long walk. Take snacks.”_  
_The ink goes wet under your fingers, then warm. You and your Nubbin are standing on warm rock, high above the clouds._  
_This is the Mountain of Flowers and Fruit. Everything on it smells of peaches, even the stones._  
_Right at the top sits an egg made of stone, taller than you are. Your Nubbin walks round it twice, very slowly._  
_It presses its cheek to the stone and listens. Then it looks up at you, solemn as a doctor._  

**Choices**

- **Knock on the egg.**
    - Something inside knocks back. Your Nubbin knocks again, delighted, and it goes on like that for some time.
- **Sit down beside it and wait.**
    - You sit, and your Nubbin sits. The wind comes and sits too, and the egg seems to like the company.

#### <a id="a02-01-page-done"></a>Done `a02-01-page-done`

### Step 2: Watch the stone egg hatch.

Hint: Stay close to the egg. Your Nubbin knows what hatching sounds like. · check: `watch:a02-egg:summit`

#### <a id="a02-01-hatch"></a>Scene `a02-01-hatch`

_The egg cracks with a sound like a dropped plate. A stone ball rolls out, and the ball unfolds into a monkey._  
_He is the grey of the rock, all knees and elbows. He bows to the north, the south, the east and the west._  
_Then he opens his eyes. Two beams of gold shoot up through the clouds, as if he were checking the sky for holes._  
**Monkey** _(happy)_: Ha! Who are you? Are you a rock as well? You’re very round for a rock.  
_Your Nubbin, who also came out of an egg, decides on the spot that this is family._  
**Monkey**: A cousin! I knew there’d be others. Everything up here is mine, I think. You can have some of the view.  
**Monkey**: Not the peaches. Well. One peach.

## A2-2. The Water-Curtain Cave

*Journey to the West (16th-century novel), ch. 1: the leap through the waterfall* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Monkey and the Monk page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A2-1, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A2-1

**Cast:** Monkey (Sun Wukong, the Monkey King (Journey to the West); original design), An old gibbon (The old monkeys of the Mountain of Flowers and Fruit, ch. 1)

### Step 1: Follow the monkeys upstream to the source of the stream.

Hint: Keep to the bank. The noise ahead is the waterfall. · check: `explore:a02-stream:source`

#### <a id="a02-02-stream"></a>Scene `a02-02-stream`

_A hot afternoon. The monkeys splash in the stream until somebody asks where all the water comes from._  
_Nobody knows, so everybody goes to look. You and your Nubbin go too, because everybody means everybody._  
_The stream ends at a waterfall that hangs down the cliff like a white curtain. Behind it, nobody can see anything._  
**An old gibbon**: Whoever goes through that curtain and comes back with all their fur on, we make them king.  
_Every monkey looks at its own feet. A few look at their neighbours’ feet, which is a sort of suggestion._  
**Monkey** _(happy)_: I’ll go! I was born from a rock. Rocks don’t mind water.  
_Your Nubbin steps up beside him, quite serious about it. It has always liked a waterfall._  

**Choices**

- **Tell them both to be careful.**
    - **Monkey**: Careful is for monkeys who aren’t going to be king.
- **Offer to count them in.**
    - **Monkey**: Yes! Count. Kings should start on a number.
    - You count to three. Monkey goes on two.

#### <a id="a02-02-stream-done"></a>Done `a02-02-stream-done`

_He crouches, shuts his eyes tight, and leaps._  

### Step 2: Swim through the waterfall after Monkey.

Hint: Your Nubbin knows the way. Swim straight into the white water. · check: `swim:a02-waterfall:curtain`

#### <a id="a02-02-leap"></a>Scene `a02-02-leap`

_Your Nubbin dives after him, and you follow your Nubbin. The water thunders on your head, and then it stops._  
_Behind the curtain it is dry. An iron bridge crosses to a cave with stone beds, stone pots and stone bowls._  
_Over the doorway someone has carved the cave a name, long ago, in tidy characters._  
**Monkey** _(happy)_: Nobody lives here! It’s got beds. It’s got pots. It’s got a front door made of waterfall!  
_Your Nubbin tries every stone bed in turn and finds them all exactly right._  
**Monkey**: Wait here. If I come back alone they’ll say I made it up.  

#### <a id="a02-02-leap-done"></a>Done `a02-02-leap-done`

_He shoots back out through the curtain, shouting. A moment later, a lot of wet monkeys shoot in._  

### Step 3: Feast with the new king, and listen when he goes quiet.

Hint: Sit at the long stone table in the Water-Curtain Cave. · check: `sit:a02-feast:cave`

#### <a id="a02-02-king"></a>Scene `a02-02-king`

_They make him king, as promised. He takes the name Handsome Monkey King, and nobody dares laugh._  
_The page turns a few years, as easily as a page. The feasts go on and on, and then, one night, the king cries._  
**Monkey** _(sad)_: One day I’ll be old. One day I’ll stop. All this, and one day it won’t be mine.  
_The cave goes very quiet. Your Nubbin climbs into his lap, which is all it knows to do._  
**An old gibbon**: There are some who never grow old, they say. Buddhas, immortals and sages. They live far off, among people.  
**Monkey**: Then I’ll go and find them. I’ll learn it. I’ll learn not to stop.  

**Choices**

- **Tell him growing old isn’t the worst thing.**
    - **Monkey**: You say that because you aren’t a king.
    - He keeps your Nubbin on his lap, though.
- **Tell him to go and find out.**
    - **Monkey**: Yes. That’s the right answer. Cousin’s keeper gives the right answers.

### Complete

#### <a id="a02-02-complete"></a>Complete `a02-02-complete`

_You and your Nubbin are on the raft too. It seems rude to wave from the shore._  
**Monkey**: I’ll be back. When I come back, I won’t ever have to stop.  
_Years go by on the water, as fast as you can turn a page. He learns to wear clothes and to walk like a person._

## A2-3. Lessons in Shape-Changing

*Journey to the West (16th-century novel), ch. 1–2: the teacher, the three knocks, the seventy-two changes* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Monkey and the Monk page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A2-2, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A2-2

**Cast:** Monkey (Sun Wukong, the Monkey King (Journey to the West); original design), Master Subhuti (Patriarch Subhuti, Monkey’s teacher, ch. 1–2)

### Step 1: Find the teacher in the mountain cave and sit in on a lesson.

Hint: The cave is at the top of the pine path, past the woodcutter. · check: `visit:a02-teacher:cave`

#### <a id="a02-03-lesson"></a>Scene `a02-03-lesson`

_High on a quiet mountain there is a cave, and in the cave a teacher with a great many students._  
**Master Subhuti** _(soft)_: A monkey. Then you shall have a name: Sun Wukong, Awake to Emptiness. It suits you. You are mostly noise.  
_The page turns seven years of sweeping floors. Then the teacher offers Monkey a choice of lessons._  
**Master Subhuti**: I could teach you to see fortunes. To sit still. To make medicines. To read the old books.  
**Monkey**: Will any of them make me live for ever?  
**Master Subhuti**: No.  
**Monkey**: Then no, thank you.  
_The teacher climbs down from his seat, taps Monkey three times on the head with his ruler, and walks out with his hands behind his back._  
_The other students are furious. Monkey is grinning from ear to ear._  

**Choices**

- **Ask Monkey why he’s grinning.**
    - **Monkey**: Three taps: come at the third watch of the night. Hands behind his back: use the back door.
    - **Monkey**: He’s going to teach me in secret. Don’t tell the others. Don’t tell Cousin, it can’t keep a secret.
- **Work it out for yourself.**
    - Three taps, hands behind the back. You catch Monkey’s eye, and he winks.

### Step 2: Wait by the back door at the third watch.

Hint: Sit by the back door after dark. Keep your Nubbin awake if you can. · check: `sit:a02-teacher:back-door`

#### <a id="a02-03-night"></a>Scene `a02-03-night`

_At the third watch the back door is open a crack, and Monkey slips through it. You sit outside on the step._  
_Your Nubbin falls asleep against the door almost at once. It snores in a small, polite way._  
_Inside, the teacher talks for a long time in a low voice. You don’t hear the words, and you aren’t meant to._  
_The page turns three more years of nights. Then one evening Monkey comes out walking a foot above the ground._  
**Monkey** _(happy)_: Seventy-two changes! I can be anything. And look, look at this.  
_He does a somersault on a cloud, and is gone. A moment later he is back, a little out of breath._  
**Monkey**: Halfway round the world and back. A hundred and eight thousand li in one go. Cousin, did you see?  
_Your Nubbin, half awake, claps slowly._  

### Step 3: Watch Monkey show off in the yard.

Hint: The students are gathering under the pines. · check: `watch:a02-pine:yard`

#### <a id="a02-03-pine"></a>Scene `a02-03-pine`

_The other students have heard rumours. They gather round Monkey under the pines, all asking at once._  
**Monkey**: A pine tree? Easy.  
_There is a pop, and in Monkey’s place stands a pine tree, very proud of itself. The students cheer._  
_Your Nubbin stands beside it with both arms up, very still. It is being a small pine tree._  
**Master Subhuti** _(angry)_: Who is shouting in my yard?  
_The pine tree turns back into a monkey, quite quickly._  
**Master Subhuti**: You show off. A gift you show off makes other people want it, and then they come for it. And for you.  
**Master Subhuti** _(sad)_: Go home. And never tell anyone who taught you. Not one word.  

**Choices**

- **Tell the teacher Monkey meant no harm.**
    - **Master Subhuti**: I know. That is the trouble with him. He never means any.
- **Stay out of it.**
    - You stay out of it. Your Nubbin, still being a pine tree, stays out of it too.

### Complete

#### <a id="a02-03-complete"></a>Complete `a02-03-complete`

_Monkey bows to his teacher, properly, all the way to the floor. For once he doesn’t say anything clever._  
**Monkey** _(soft)_: Hold on to my tail, Cousin’s keeper. I know a quick way home.  
_One somersault, and the world rolls under you like a rug being shaken. Then there are peaches again._  
_Your Nubbin tumbles off the cloud and lies on the grass, beaming. It would like to do that again, please._

#### <a id="a02-04-stables"></a>Scene `a02-04-stables`

**Monkey**: Wait. What rank is Keeper of the Horses, exactly?

#### <a id="a02-04-garden"></a>Scene `a02-04-garden`

**The garden spirit**: Three thousand six hundred trees, Great Sage. These ripen every three thousand years. These, every six thousand.  
**The garden spirit**: And these at the back, every nine thousand. Eat one, and you live as long as heaven and earth.  
_Your Nubbin has gone very still. It is looking at the peaches the way it looks at you at teatime._  
**Monkey**: Thank you. You may go. I shall inspect the trees alone. Very carefully.

- **Let your Nubbin sniff it, and no more.**
    - Your Nubbin sniffs it for a long, long time. It glows a tiny bit just from the smell, and leaves it be.

_They tell him who is invited: everyone. Everyone except the Great Sage Equal to Heaven._  
**Monkey** _(angry)_: Everyone?  
_He freezes the maidens where they stand with one word, and is off to the Jade Pool before you can blink._  
_He puts the servants to sleep and eats the whole banquet. Then he wanders into Lord Lao’s palace._  
_On the table are five gourds of golden elixir pills. He eats them like roasted beans._  
**Monkey** _(shock)_: Oh. Oh, no. That might have been a mistake.  
**Monkey**: Cousin! Keeper! Home! Now!

## A2-5. Under the Mountain

### Step 1: Watch from a safe corner while Monkey breaks out of Lord Lao’s furnace.

#### <a id="a02-05-furnace"></a>Scene `a02-05-furnace`

_In the end a great warrior and his dog catch Monkey, and nothing in Heaven can harm him. So Lord Lao puts him in his furnace._  
_Forty-nine days the fire burns. Your Nubbin sits by the furnace door the whole time, worried, and won’t be moved._

_On the forty-ninth day the lid comes off with a bang. Out jumps Monkey, with eyes like two little suns._  
**Monkey** _(happy)_: Cousin! You waited. Stand back. I’m going to be very rude to everyone.  
_He kicks the furnace over and runs the length of Heaven, scattering generals like skittles._  

#### <a id="a02-05-furnace-done"></a>Done `a02-05-furnace-done`

**The Jade Emperor** _(sad)_: Fetch the Buddha. Ask him, please, to come.  

### Step 2: Witness the wager on the Buddha’s palm.

Hint: Stand where you can see the Buddha’s open hand. · check: `talk:a02-buddha:palm`

#### <a id="a02-05-palm"></a>Scene `a02-05-palm`

_The Buddha comes in quietly. Everything else goes quiet with him, even Monkey, for a breath._  
**The Buddha** _(soft)_: What is it you want?  
**Monkey**: The Jade Emperor’s throne. He’s had it long enough. It’s my turn.  
**The Buddha**: Then here is a wager. Jump out of my right hand, and the throne is yours. If you cannot, go back down and learn.  
**Monkey** _(happy)_: Your hand? It isn’t a foot across. I go a hundred and eight thousand li in one somersault!  
_He jumps onto the open palm, and is gone in a streak of cloud._  
_Your Nubbin is standing beside you, near the Buddha’s wrist. From here you can see everything._  
_You can see Monkey fly to five pink pillars at the edge of the world, and write his title on the middle one._  
_You can see that the pillars are fingers._  

**Choices**

- **When he lands, tell him gently: it was the hand all along.**
    - **Monkey**: Rubbish. I went to the end of the world. I wrote my name on it.
- **Say nothing. Let the Buddha show him.**
    - You say nothing. The Buddha glances at you, briefly, and something in his face says thank you.

#### <a id="a02-05-palm-done"></a>Done `a02-05-palm-done`

_The Buddha lifts his hand. On the base of one finger, in very small and very familiar writing, is Monkey’s title._  
**Monkey** _(shock)_: That’s… that can’t… I went…  

### Step 3: Stay by Monkey under the Five Elements Mountain.

Hint: Climb down the mountainside to where his head sticks out. · check: `sit:a02-monkey:five-elements`

#### <a id="a02-05-mountain"></a>Scene `a02-05-mountain`

_The Buddha turns his hand over. It comes down gently and becomes a mountain with five peaks._  
_Monkey is underneath, with only his head and one hand sticking out. He is not hurt, only, for the first time, stopped._  
**The Buddha** _(soft)_: Feed him when he is hungry. Give him water when he is thirsty. When he has learned enough, someone will come.  
**Monkey** _(sad)_: How long?  
**The Buddha**: As long as it takes. Not one day more.  
_Your Nubbin climbs down and sits by Monkey’s free hand. Monkey holds on to one of its feet and says nothing at all._  

### Complete

#### <a id="a02-05-complete"></a>Complete `a02-05-complete`

_Heaven holds a feast to thank the Buddha. The Queen Mother of the West brings him a dish of her peaches._  
_She looks at your Nubbin, who is trying very hard not to look at the peaches._  
**The Queen Mother of the West** _(soft)_: You stood by my trees and took nothing. Here. This one is given, not taken. That makes it a different fruit.  
_Your Nubbin eats it slowly, with its eyes shut, and glows all over like a paper lantern._  
**The Queen Mother of the West**: Keep the stone. Plant it somewhere you will be glad to see it.  
_Then the page turns, and turns, and turns. Five hundred years go by, and you sit through all of them with Monkey._  
_Grass grows in his ears, and moss on his hand. Your Nubbin keeps one foot in that hand the whole time._
