# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A2: The Monkey and the Monk

## A2-1. Born from Stone

- `a02-01-s1`: Climb the painted mountain to the stone egg. · _Follow the smell of peaches uphill._
- `a02-01-s2`: Watch the stone egg hatch. · _Stay close to the egg._
- `a02-01-s3`: Look down from the clouds. · _Follow the gold light up._

#### `sprout:a02-01-page` · [main](main.md#a02-01-page)

_Nova’s big book opens to a painted mountain._  
_Peach trees! A waterfall! Clouds like curly hair._  
_Whoosh! You and your Nubbin step into the picture._  
_Sniff, sniff. Everything smells of peaches._  
_At the top sits a big stone egg. It is taller than you!_  
_Your Nubbin walks round the egg. Round once. Round twice._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.4, do): count to 2  
> _(Count the trips round the egg on your fingers. One. Two.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:laps:2`
> _For grown-ups: Your child counted two trips round the egg._


**Choices**

- **Knock on the egg.**
    - Knock, knock! Something inside knocks back.
- **Sit down beside it and wait.**
    - You sit. Your Nubbin sits. The wind sits too.

#### `sprout:a02-01-page-done` · [main](main.md#a02-01-page-done)

_A wind comes and leans on the egg. Push, push, push!_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): wind is moving air that pushes  
> _Wind is air on the move. It can push leaves, kites and eggs._  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that moving air can push things._


#### `sprout:a02-01-hatch` · [main](main.md#a02-01-hatch)

_Crack! The egg breaks. A stone ball rolls out._  
_The ball unfolds into a little grey monkey._  
_He bows to the north. He bows to the south._  
_He bows to the east. He bows to the west._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, do): four directions: north, south, east, west  
> _(Stand up. Bow four ways with Monkey: north, south, east, west.)_  
> Check: `edu:point:compass:four-ways`
> _For grown-ups: Your child named the four compass directions._

**Monkey**: Ha! Who are you? You are very round for a rock.  
_Your Nubbin came out of an egg too. This is family!_  
**Monkey**: A cousin! You can have some of the view.  

#### `sprout:a02-01-hatch-done` · [main](main.md#a02-01-hatch-done)

_Monkey’s eyes shine gold. Two beams of light shoot up high._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, notice): rhyming words: gold, old, cold  
> _Gold! Gold rhymes with old and cold. Can you think of another?_  
> _For grown-ups: Your child played with words that rhyme with "gold"._

_Far above, someone puts down his tea._  

#### `sprout:a02-01-heaven` · [main](main.md#a02-01-heaven)

_The page lifts. Now you sit on a cloud, high up._  
**The Jade Emperor**: Two beams of light! Who is making a fuss?  
**A heavenly herald**: Only a little monkey, Majesty. Born this morning.  
**The Jade Emperor**: Let him play. Small things grow.  
_Your Nubbin leans over the cloud. It waves at a tiny grey dot._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): faraway things look small  
> _Monkey looks so tiny from the cloud. Why?_  
> - ✅ He is far away → Yes! Things that are far away look small.
> - He shrank → He did not shrink. Far-away things just look small. When he is close, he looks big again.
> _For grown-ups: Your child learned that faraway things look smaller._

_The tiny dot waves back!_  

#### `sprout:a02-01-complete` · [main](main.md#a02-01-complete)

_By evening, Monkey has learned to walk, then climb._  
_Next he eats peaches. Last he argues with the other monkeys._  
**Monkey**: Cousin! Tomorrow I will do something enormous.  
_Long ago, twelve animals ran a big race._  
_Monkey came ninth. He still says it was close._  
_He gives your Nubbin a peach. It is mostly bite marks!_  
_Your Nubbin holds it like treasure._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): sharing  
> _Your Nubbin has one peach, and Monkey has none. What is kind?_  
> - ✅ Share it → Yes! Sharing is kind. Now they both have some.
> - Eat it all → Then Monkey would be sad and hungry. Sharing is kinder. Your Nubbin shares.
> _For grown-ups: Your child chose sharing as the kind thing to do._

_Then your Nubbin breaks it in two and gives Monkey half._  

## A2-2. The Water-Curtain Cave

- `a02-02-s1`: Follow the stream to the waterfall. · _Walk up the stream with the monkeys._
- `a02-02-s2`: Jump through the waterfall. · _Dive in after Monkey._
- `a02-02-s3`: Join the monkeys’ feast. · _Sit down in the cave._

#### `sprout:a02-02-stream` · [main](main.md#a02-02-stream)

_It is a hot day. The monkeys splash in the stream._  
**An old gibbon**: Where does all this water come from?  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): streams come from rain on hills  
> _Where does the stream’s water come from?_  
> - ✅ Rain on the hills → Yes! Rain falls on the hills and runs down.
> - A big tap → No tap! Rain falls on the hills, and the water runs down. That is how a stream is born.
> _For grown-ups: Your child learned that streams are fed by rain running off hills._

_So everyone goes to look. Everyone means everyone!_  
_The stream ends at a waterfall. It hangs like a white curtain._  
**An old gibbon**: Who goes through the curtain and comes back? That one is king!  
**Monkey**: I will go! Rocks do not mind water.  
_Your Nubbin steps up beside him. It likes waterfalls._  

**Choices**

- **Tell them both to be careful.**
    - **Monkey**: Careful is for monkeys who are not going to be king.
- **Offer to count them in.**
    - **Monkey**: Yes! Count! Kings should start on a number.
    - You count: one, two, three. Monkey goes on two.

#### `sprout:a02-02-stream-done` · [main](main.md#a02-02-stream-done)

_Monkey crouches down. He shuts his eyes. He leaps!_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, notice): syllables in "waterfall"  
> _Clap the word: wa-ter-fall. Three claps for three parts._  
> _For grown-ups: Your child clapped the three syllables of "waterfall"._


#### `sprout:a02-02-leap` · [main](main.md#a02-02-leap)

_Splash! Your Nubbin dives after him. You dive too._  
_The water thunders. Then it stops._  
_Behind the curtain it is dry! There is a cave._  
_Stone beds. Stone pots. Stone bowls._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.B.3, do): sort and count  
> _(Point to each kind. How many beds? Pots? Bowls? Count together.)_  
> Check: `edu:sort:cave-things:kind`
> _For grown-ups: Your child sorted cave things into kinds and counted each._

**Monkey**: Nobody lives here! It has a front door made of waterfall!  
_Your Nubbin tries every stone bed. They are all just right._  

#### `sprout:a02-02-leap-done` · [main](main.md#a02-02-leap-done)

_Monkey shoots out through the water, shouting._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-PS4-1, notice): sound is made by shaking  
> _A waterfall is loud. Loud sounds are big shakes in the air._  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that sound comes from vibrations._

_Splash, splash, splash! Wet monkeys shoot in._  

#### `sprout:a02-02-king` · [main](main.md#a02-02-king)

_The monkeys make him king. Hooray!_  
_Many feasts go by. Then one night, the king cries._  
**Monkey**: One day I will be old. One day I will stop.  
_Monkey is scared. His eyes are wet._  
_Your Nubbin climbs into his lap, to sit with him._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): what helps when someone is scared  
> _Monkey is sad and scared. What helps?_  
> - ✅ A hug and a friend → Yes! Sitting close helps a lot.
> - Say hush → Saying hush does not help much. A hug and a friend do. That is what Nubbin gives.
> _For grown-ups: Your child practised comforting a scared friend._

**An old gibbon**: Some wise ones live for a very long time. Far away.  
**Monkey**: Then I will go and find them.  

**Choices**

- **Tell him growing old isn’t the worst thing.**
    - **Monkey**: You say that because you are not a king.
    - He keeps your Nubbin in his lap, though.
- **Tell him to go and find out.**
    - **Monkey**: Yes. That is the right answer!

#### `sprout:a02-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a02-02-complete)

_In the morning the monkeys build a raft._  
_They pile on fruit: one, two, three, four, five pieces._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 5  
> _(Count the fruit on the raft together. One, two, three, four, five.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:fruit:5`
> _For grown-ups: Your child counted five pieces of fruit._

_You and your Nubbin hop on too. Waving from shore is rude._  
**Monkey**: I will be back. And I will not ever have to stop.  
_The raft goes out over the big water. Everyone waves and waves._  

## A2-3. Lessons in Shape-Changing

- `a02-03-s1`: Visit Monkey’s teacher. · _Walk up to the cave with Monkey._
- `a02-03-s2`: Wait on the back step. · _Sit outside the back door._
- `a02-03-s3`: Watch Monkey under the pines. · _Stand in the yard._

#### `sprout:a02-03-lesson` · [main](main.md#a02-03-lesson)

_High on a quiet mountain is a cave. A teacher lives there._  
**Master Subhuti**: A monkey. You shall be called Sun Wukong. You are mostly noise.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Literacy, notice): a name can have a meaning  
> _Wukong is a name. It has a meaning, like many names do._  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that names often carry a meaning._

_Seven years go by. Monkey sweeps and sweeps the floor._  
**Master Subhuti**: I can teach you fortunes, or sitting still, or medicines.  
**Monkey**: Will any of them make me live for ever?  
**Master Subhuti**: No.  
**Monkey**: Then no, thank you.  
_The teacher taps Monkey three times on the head. Tap, tap, tap!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.4, do): count to 3  
> _(Gently tap your own head three times. One, two, three.)_  
> Check: `edu:tap:head:3`
> _For grown-ups: Your child counted three taps._

_He walks out with his hands behind his back. Monkey grins._  

**Choices**

- **Ask Monkey why he’s grinning.**
    - **Monkey**: Three taps. Come at the third watch of the night.
    - **Monkey**: Hands behind his back. Use the back door!
    - **Monkey**: It is a secret. Do not tell Cousin. It cannot keep one.
- **Work it out for yourself.**
    - Three taps. Hands behind the back. Monkey winks at you.

#### `sprout:a02-03-night` · [main](main.md#a02-03-night)

_In the deep of the night, the back door opens a crack._  
_Monkey slips in. You sit on the step outside._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-ESS1-1, ask): night and day  
> _Monkey comes in the night. When is it dark outside?_  
> - ✅ At night → Yes! The sky is dark at night, with stars.
> - At lunch time → At lunch time the sun is high and bright. It is dark at night.
> _For grown-ups: Your child linked darkness to night-time._

_Your Nubbin falls asleep. Snore, snore, snore._  
_Years of nights go by. Then Monkey comes out, walking on air!_  
**Monkey**: I can be anything! And look at this!  
_He somersaults on a cloud. Whoosh! He is gone, then back._  

#### `sprout:a02-03-pine` · [main](main.md#a02-03-pine)

_The other students gather under the pine trees._  
**Monkey**: A pine tree? Easy!  
_Pop! Monkey is a pine tree. The students cheer._  
_Your Nubbin copies. It holds its arms up very still._  
**Master Subhuti**: Who is shouting in my yard?  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): listening and feelings  
> _The teacher is cross. How does Monkey feel now?_  
> - ✅ Sorry and sad → Yes. He feels sorry. That is a big feeling.
> - Very proud → He was proud. But now he is sorry and sad. Feelings can change very fast.
> _For grown-ups: Your child named a changing feeling._

_Pop! The pine tree is a monkey again._  
**Master Subhuti**: Showing off makes trouble. Go home, Monkey.  

**Choices**

- **Tell the teacher Monkey meant no harm.**
    - **Master Subhuti**: I know. That is the trouble. He never means any.
- **Stay out of it.**
    - You stay out of it. Your Nubbin, still a pine tree, does too.

#### `sprout:a02-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a02-03-complete)

_Monkey bows to his teacher. All the way to the floor._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, notice): bowing is a polite thing in China  
> _In China, a bow can mean thank you and respect._  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that bowing can show thanks and respect._

**Monkey**: Hold on to my tail, Cousin’s keeper. I know a quick way.  
_One somersault. The world rolls by like a rug._  
_Then there are peaches again!_  
_Your Nubbin tumbles onto the grass. It beams. Again, please!_  

## A2-4. Havoc in Heaven

- `a02-04-s1`: Visit the heavenly stables. · _Go up to Heaven with Monkey._
- `a02-04-s2`: Look at the peaches in the garden. · _Walk among the trees._
- `a02-04-s3`: Watch Monkey at the feast. · _Go to the Jade Pool._

#### `sprout:a02-04-stables` · [main](main.md#a02-04-stables)

_Heaven gives Monkey a job. He looks after the horses._  
**Monkey**: A grand title! They must think a lot of me.  
_The horses grow fat and shiny. Your Nubbin rides one._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, ask): what animals need  
> _The horses grow fat and shiny. What do horses need?_  
> - ✅ Grass, water and care → Yes! Food, water and care keep them well.
> - Only sweets → Sweets are not good for a horse. Horses need grass, water and care.
> _For grown-ups: Your child named what a horse needs to live._

**Monkey**: What rank is horse-keeper, exactly?  
_It is the smallest job in Heaven. Monkey throws down his hat._  
_He goes straight home, cross as a cat._  

#### `sprout:a02-04-stables-done` · [main](main.md#a02-04-stables-done)

**The Jade Emperor**: Give him the Peach Garden to look after. It will keep him busy.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, notice): a big feeling needs a good job to do  
> _When we feel cross, a calm job can help, like digging, painting or walking._  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that a calming activity can help with a cross feeling._


#### `sprout:a02-04-garden` · [main](main.md#a02-04-garden)

**The garden spirit**: So many trees, Great Sage! Seven rows of peaches.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 7  
> _(Count the rows of trees on your fingers. Seven!)_  
> Check: `edu:count:rows:7`
> _For grown-ups: Your child counted seven rows of peach trees._

_Your Nubbin goes very still. It looks at the peaches._  
**Monkey**: Thank you. You may go. I will inspect the trees alone.  
_The spirit goes. Monkey climbs the oldest tree._  
_He eats the best peach, then the next best. Then he naps._  
_A ripe peach drops by your Nubbin’s feet. It glows a little._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): a seed is inside the fruit  
> _Inside a peach is a big stone, and inside the stone is a seed._  
> _The seed can grow into a new peach tree._  
> _For grown-ups: Your child learned that fruit holds a seed that can grow into a tree._


**Choices**

- **Hang the peach back on its branch.**
    - It will not stay. You put it gently at the foot of the tree.
    - Your Nubbin sighs from its toes, and lets it go.
- **Let your Nubbin sniff it, and no more.**
    - Sniff, sniff, sniff! It glows a little, then leaves it be.

#### `sprout:a02-04-banquet` · [main](main.md#a02-04-banquet)

_Seven fairy maidens come to pick peaches._  
_(Count the maidens. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven.)_  
_They tell Monkey who is invited. Everyone, except him._  
**Monkey**: Everyone?  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): being left out and what to do  
> _Monkey was left out. How would that feel?_  
> - ✅ Sad and cross → Yes. Being left out feels sad and cross.
> - Happy → Most people feel sad when they are left out. Monkey felt sad and cross.
> _For grown-ups: Your child named the feelings that come with being left out._

_Monkey is hurt and cross. He makes the maidens freeze like statues._  
_Then he eats the whole feast, and the magic pills too._  
**Monkey**: Oh. That might have been a mistake.  
**Monkey**: Cousin! Home! Now!  

#### `sprout:a02-04-complete` · [main](main.md#a02-04-complete)

_Heaven sends its soldiers. Bonk! Bonk! Helmets ring like bells._  

> **Lesson** (music, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, notice): loud and quiet sounds  
> _A helmet can ring like a drum._  
> _(Tap softly on the table. Now tap loudly. Can you hear the difference?)_  
> _For grown-ups: Your child noticed loud and quiet sounds._

**Monkey**: Equal to Heaven? I am better than equal. Tell them, Cousin.  
_Your Nubbin hides behind a rock. It would rather not._  
_The fairy maidens unfreeze at last and see the empty trees._  
_They hurry off to tell the Queen Mother._  

## A2-5. Under the Mountain

- `a02-05-s1`: Wait by the furnace door. · _Sit by the furnace._
- `a02-05-s2`: Talk with the Buddha. · _Stand near the Buddha’s hand._
- `a02-05-s3`: Sit with Monkey under the mountain. · _Climb down and sit by his hand._

#### `sprout:a02-05-furnace` · [main](main.md#a02-05-furnace)

_Monkey is hard to stop. So Lord Lao puts him in a furnace._  
_For forty-nine days, the fire burns. Your Nubbin waits, like a good friend._  

> **Lesson** (health, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, ask): staying back from fire  
> _The furnace is very hot. What do we do near a hot fire?_  
> - ✅ Stay well back → Yes! Hot things can burn. We stay well back.
> - Touch it → Ouch! Hot things burn. We stay well back from a fire.
> _For grown-ups: Your child learned to stay well back from fire._

_Monkey hides in the one corner that is only wind._  
_Out he jumps! His eyes are two little suns._  
**Monkey**: Cousin! You waited. I am going to be rude to everyone.  
_He runs the length of Heaven. Generals tumble like skittles._  

#### `sprout:a02-05-furnace-done` · [main](main.md#a02-05-furnace-done)

**The Jade Emperor**: Fetch the Buddha. Ask him, please, to come.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, notice): asking for help is brave  
> _Even a big, strong emperor asks for help sometimes. That is wise._  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that asking for help is wise._


#### `sprout:a02-05-palm` · [main](main.md#a02-05-palm)

_The Buddha comes in quietly. Everything goes quiet._  
**The Buddha**: What is it you want?  
**Monkey**: The big throne. It is my turn.  
**The Buddha**: Then jump out of my right hand, and it is yours.  
**Monkey**: Your hand is tiny! I go far in one somersault!  
_Monkey jumps onto the palm. Zoom! He is gone._  
_He flies to five pink pillars at the edge of the world._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.4, do): count to 5 on your fingers  
> _(Hold up one hand. Count the five fingers, one by one.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:fingers:5`
> _For grown-ups: Your child counted five fingers._

_He writes his name on the middle one. But look: the pillars are fingers!_  

**Choices**

- **When he lands, tell him gently: it was the hand all along.**
    - **Monkey**: Rubbish! I went to the end of the world.
- **Say nothing. Let the Buddha show him.**
    - You say nothing. The Buddha glances at you, and his face says thank you.

#### `sprout:a02-05-palm-done` · [main](main.md#a02-05-palm-done)

_The Buddha lifts his hand. On one finger is a name, in tiny writing._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Literacy, ask): writing carries a message  
> _Someone wrote a name on the finger. Who wrote it?_  
> - ✅ Monkey did → Yes. Monkey wrote it, on a finger!
> - The Buddha did → The Buddha did not. Monkey did.
> _For grown-ups: Your child understood that writing records a message from a writer._

**Monkey**: That cannot be. I went…  

#### `sprout:a02-05-mountain` · [main](main.md#a02-05-mountain)

_The Buddha turns his hand over. It becomes a mountain._  
_Monkey is under it. He is not hurt, only stopped._  
**The Buddha**: Feed him when he is hungry. Give him water when he is thirsty.  
**Monkey**: For how long?  
**The Buddha**: Until he has learned. Then someone will come.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): sitting with a friend who is sad  
> _Monkey is sad and stuck. How does your Nubbin help?_  
> - ✅ It stays beside him → Yes. Being there is a big help.
> - It runs away → It does not run away. It stays. Staying beside a sad friend helps.
> _For grown-ups: Your child saw that staying beside a sad friend is a help._

_Your Nubbin sits by his free hand. Monkey holds its foot._  

#### `sprout:a02-05-complete` · [main](main.md#a02-05-complete)

_The Queen Mother of the West brings a dish of peaches._  
**The Queen Mother of the West**: You stood by my trees and took nothing. This one is given.  
_Your Nubbin eats it slowly and glows like a lantern._  
**The Queen Mother of the West**: Keep the stone. Plant it where you will be glad to see it.  
_The page turns, and turns. Many years pass._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): plants grow from seeds, slowly  
> _A peach stone is a seed. It grows into a tree, slowly._  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that some things in nature change very slowly._

_Moss grows on Monkey’s hand. Your Nubbin keeps its foot there._  

## A2-6. The Monk

- `a02-06-s1`: Walk with the monk to the edge of the land. · _Carry his luggage._
- `a02-06-s2`: Climb the mountain to the gold paper. · _Hold the staff and push._
- `a02-06-s3`: Wait by the road. · _Sit and let things calm down._

#### `sprout:a02-06-road` · [main](main.md#a02-06-road)

_A monk comes along the road on a pale horse._  
_He has a bowl, a staff, a robe, and far too much luggage._  
**Tang Sanzang**: Good morning. Is this the road west?  
**Tang Sanzang**: I am Tang Sanzang. I must fetch books from India.  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, ask): the sun sets in the west  
> _The monk walks west. Where does the sun go down?_  
> - ✅ In the west → Yes! The sun sets in the west. The monk follows it.
> - In the east → The sun comes up in the east. It goes down in the west, where the monk is going.
> _For grown-ups: Your child learned that the sun sets in the west._

**Tang Sanzang**: You carry nothing. I carry everything. May I ask for help?  
_You take the luggage. Your Nubbin climbs on top of it._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): heavier and lighter  
> _Your Nubbin sits on the luggage. Is it heavier or lighter now?_  
> - ✅ Heavier → Yes! More weight on top makes it heavier.
> - Lighter → Not lighter. Your Nubbin adds weight. So it is heavier now.
> _For grown-ups: Your child compared a load with and without an extra weight._

**Tang Sanzang**: Thank you. You are my second disciple. I keep forgetting the horse.  

#### `sprout:a02-06-road-done` · [main](main.md#a02-06-road-done)

_From the mountain ahead, a voice shouts. Loud!_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, ask): loud and quiet voices  
> _Is that a shout, or a whisper?_  
> - ✅ A shout → Yes! A shout is a big loud voice.
> - A whisper → A whisper is very quiet. This voice shakes leaves! That is a shout.
> _For grown-ups: Your child told a shout from a whisper._

_It shakes the leaves._  

#### `sprout:a02-06-seal` · [main](main.md#a02-06-seal)

**Monkey**: Master! You came! A lady told me to wait for a monk!  
_Monkey’s head sticks out of the mountain. There is grass in his ears._  
**Monkey**: Cousin! You are still here.  
**Tang Sanzang**: Oh dear. Are you a demon?  
**Monkey**: I am your disciple. Please peel the paper off the top.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, ask): top and bottom  
> _Where is the gold paper? At the top, or the bottom?_  
> - ✅ At the top → Yes! Up at the top of the mountain.
> - At the bottom → Monkey is at the bottom, by the grass. The paper is at the top.
> _For grown-ups: Your child used the words top and bottom._

_The monk climbs. You hold his staff and push._  
_At the top is a strip of gold paper. He peels it, gently._  

#### `sprout:a02-06-seal-done` · [main](main.md#a02-06-seal-done)

_The mountain splits like bread. Monkey bounds out!_  
_He bows to the monk four times. Then he hugs your Nubbin._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): saying thank you  
> _Monkey bows to say thank you. What do we say when someone helps us?_  
> - ✅ Thank you → Yes! Thank you. Say it with Monkey.
