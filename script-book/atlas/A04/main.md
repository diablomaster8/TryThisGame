## A4-1. The River’s Gift

*Baahubali: The Beginning (2015) and Baahubali 2: The Conclusion (2017), S. S. Rajamouli, Telugu and Tamil: the opening at the river* · audience: teen · where: Nova’s atlas: the Waterfall Prince page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time once Nova’s atlas is in the Grove and Mature content is at 13+ or 18+; the first quest on the page.

**Cast:** Sanga (Sanga, the woman of the falls who raises the boy (Baahubali); original design), A Villager (a villager of the falls (Baahubali); original)

### Step 1: Step into the atlas page and find your feet by the river.

Hint: Touch the painted waterfall on the page, then follow the torchlight along the bank. · check: `visit:a4-river:night`

#### <a id="a4-01-river"></a>Scene `a4-01-river`

_Nova’s atlas falls open at a page that is mostly water: a waterfall so tall the top of it is off the paper._  
_You touch the spray. It is cold, and then it is on your face, and the noise arrives all at once._  
_Night, and rain. A river runs fast and brown between black rocks, and somewhere above it the falls are roaring._  
_Torches move on the far bank, a long way off. Men are shouting a name you can’t make out._  
_Your Nubbin grabs your sleeve and points at the river._  
_A woman is in the water, up to her chest, and she is holding something above her head with both hands._  
_It is a baby, wrapped in red cloth. She is keeping him out of the river, and nothing else._  

**Choices**

- **Wade in towards her.**
    - The current takes your legs at the second step. You grab a rock and hold on, and the river goes past you like a crowd.
- **Shout for help along the bank.**
    - Your voice goes nowhere in the noise. Then, downstream, a lamp comes on in a hut, and another.

#### <a id="a4-01-river-done"></a>Done `a4-01-river-done`

_She is past you now, and the falls are close behind her. She doesn’t look at them._  
_Her arms stay up. The river rises to her chin, and then over it, and her arms stay up._  

### Step 2: Help the villagers bring the baby safely to the bank.

Hint: Run downstream to the shallows by the huts. · check: `carry:a4-baby:bank`

#### <a id="a4-01-shallows"></a>Scene `a4-01-shallows`

_Downstream the river spreads out and slows. People from the huts are already in it to their knees, with lamps._  
_The red cloth comes round the bend, held up out of the water. Just a hand and an arm now, still holding._  
**A Villager** _(shock)_: There! Get him, get him!  
_A woman wades out further than anyone else, and takes the baby from the hand._  
_The hand lets go. The river closes over it, gently, as if it had been asked to._  
_The woman stands in the water with the baby against her shoulder, and for a moment nobody says anything._  
_Your Nubbin holds the lamp out over them, as high as it can reach, which isn’t very high._  

**Choices**

- **Give her your coat for the baby.**
    - She takes it without looking at you and wraps him in it, red cloth and all. He is furious about the whole night.
- **Look back at the river.**
    - There’s nothing to see. Just the water going on, and the falls, and the torches on the far bank turning back.

#### <a id="a4-01-shallows-done"></a>Done `a4-01-shallows-done`

_The baby yells. The woman laughs, once, a wet laugh that is almost crying._  
**Sanga** _(soft)_: Listen to him. He’s got the whole river’s voice.  

### Complete

#### <a id="a4-01-complete"></a>Complete `a4-01-complete`

_In the hut, the woman dries him by the fire. The villagers call her Sanga, and tell you she has asked the river for a child for years._  
**Sanga** _(soft)_: I prayed for a son. I didn’t say what sort of road he should come by.  
**Sanga**: She held him up till the end. Whoever she was, he’s mine to hold now.  
_Your Nubbin sits by the baby’s basket and doesn’t move from it all night._  
_Outside, the falls go on roaring. The page turns, slowly, by itself, and years go by in the turning._

## A4-2. The Climb

*Baahubali: The Beginning (2015) and Baahubali 2: The Conclusion (2017), S. S. Rajamouli, Telugu and Tamil: the boy and the waterfall* · audience: teen · where: Nova’s atlas: the Waterfall Prince page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A4-1, from Nova’s atlas. Mature content at 13+ or 18+. · requires: A4-1

**Cast:** Sanga (Sanga, the woman of the falls who raises the boy (Baahubali); original design), Shivudu (Shivudu, later Mahendra Baahubali (Baahubali); original design), A Villager (a villager of the falls (Baahubali); original)

### Step 1: Watch the young man who keeps trying to climb the falls.

Hint: Go to the deep pool at the foot of the waterfall. · check: `watch:a4-shivudu:climb`

#### <a id="a4-02-pool"></a>Scene `a4-02-pool`

_Morning, years on: the village is the same village, a little more patched. The falls are exactly the same._  
_Halfway up the cliff, a young man is clinging to the rock in the spray, grinning like he’s already won._  
_He reaches for the next hold, and it isn’t there. He falls a very long way into the pool, with a shout of pure delight._  
**A Villager**: That’s Shivudu. That’s forty times he’s come off it, by my count, and he goes straight back up every time.  
_Sanga is on the bank with a basket of washing, not looking, in the way of someone who can’t bear to look._  
**Sanga** _(sad)_: One day he’ll get to the top, and then where will I be?  
_Shivudu comes up out of the pool, shaking his hair, and sees your Nubbin on the rocks. Your Nubbin is already climbing._  

#### <a id="a4-02-pool-done"></a>Done `a4-02-pool-done`

**Shivudu** _(happy)_: Oh, you’re good. Look at you. You’ve got no fingers and you’re better at it than me.  
_Your Nubbin slides down a wet rock on its bottom and lands in his lap. They are friends at once._  

### Step 2: Help Shivudu find the handholds up the lower cliff.

Hint: Your Nubbin is small enough to find the cracks in the spray. Follow where it goes. · check: `climb:a4-falls:handholds`

#### <a id="a4-02-holds"></a>Scene `a4-02-holds`

**Shivudu**: There’s a ledge up there, past the green rock. I’ve touched it twice. I’ve never stood on it.  
_The rock is black and slick. The water comes down beside you so loud that you feel it in your teeth._  
_Your Nubbin goes ahead, into cracks too small for a hand, and pokes its head out of each one like a chick in a wall._  
**Shivudu** _(happy)_: There! Where it is! There’s a hold where it is!  
_You learn the trick of it: watch where your Nubbin stops, and put your fingers there._  
_Shivudu climbs beside you, faster, taking holds you wouldn’t have trusted, laughing when they hold._  

**Choices**

- **Take the dry line, away from the water.**
    - Slower, and safer. Shivudu waits for you on every ledge, and pretends that he isn’t.
- **Go straight up through the spray, with him.**
    - You can’t see a thing. You climb by feel and by the small warm weight of your Nubbin pressing your hand to each hold.

#### <a id="a4-02-holds-done"></a>Done `a4-02-holds-done`

_The green-rock ledge. Shivudu stands on it, both feet, and spreads his arms out into the spray._  
**Shivudu** _(happy)_: I’m standing on it! Amma! I’m standing on it!  
_Far below, Sanga does not look up from her washing, very hard._  

### Step 3: See what comes down the falls.

Hint: Stay on the ledge and look up into the water. · check: `watch:a4-mask:falls`

#### <a id="a4-02-mask"></a>Scene `a4-02-mask`

_Something comes down the falls, turning over and over in the white water. It hits the ledge and stops at Shivudu’s foot._  
_A wooden mask. A woman’s face, carved and painted, with a crack across one cheek._  
**Shivudu** _(think)_: That’s come from the top. That’s come from up there.  
_He holds it up against the sky. It looks back at him with its painted eyes._  
**Shivudu**: Somebody lives up there. Somebody dropped this.  
_Your Nubbin looks at the mask, then up at the top of the falls, which nobody has ever seen._  

### Complete

#### <a id="a4-02-complete"></a>Complete `a4-02-complete`

_That evening, Shivudu sits by the pool with the mask in his lap and doesn’t eat._  
**Sanga** _(sad)_: Don’t. Please don’t. Whatever is up there, it didn’t want you, or it would have come down.  
**Shivudu** _(soft)_: It came down, Amma. It just came down slowly.  
_Sanga looks at you, as if you might stop him. You both know you won’t._  
_Your Nubbin falls asleep with one hand on the mask’s chin, as if keeping it from going anywhere without them._  

**Rewards**

**Notes:** A climbing step with the Nubbin as the guide: it scrambles to the next safe hold and the player follows. Two routes (dry or spray), flavour only. "Amma" is mother (Telugu and Tamil both use it; advisers to confirm). The shrine-stone scene of the first film is left out (RELIGION). The mask is an original carving, not a copy of the film prop.

## A4-3. The Mask

*Baahubali: The Beginning (2015) and Baahubali 2: The Conclusion (2017), S. S. **Cast:** Shivudu (Shivudu, later Mahendra Baahubali (Baahubali); original design), Avanthika (Avanthika, a rebel warrior (Baahubali); original design), A Rebel (one of Avanthika’s band (Baahubali); original), A Guard (a soldier of Mahishmati (Baahubali); original)

### Step 1: Climb the rest of the falls with Shivudu and your Nubbin.

Hint: Keep to your Nubbin’s line. Rest on the ledges. · check: `climb:a4-falls:top`

#### <a id="a4-03-top"></a>Scene `a4-03-top`

_You go before dawn, so Sanga won’t have to watch. Shivudu has the mask tied at his back._  
_Past the green rock the cliff leans out. Your Nubbin finds a crack and you all go into it, inch by inch._  
**Shivudu**: If I fall from here, don’t tell her. Say I went to the market.  
_Above the crack there is cloud, and then above the cloud there is a lip of rock, and then there is grass._  
_Shivudu hauls himself over the edge and lies on his back in it, laughing at the sky._  
_Up here it is snowing, just a little. Your Nubbin catches a flake and looks at it, outraged._  

#### <a id="a4-03-top-done"></a>Done `a4-03-top-done`

_Below you, the whole valley is a green bowl with a white thread running down it. You came up that._  
**Shivudu** _(soft)_: A whole world. There was a whole world up here the whole time.  

### Step 2: Find whoever wore the mask.

Hint: Follow the footprints in the snow into the forest. · check: `find:a4-avanthika:forest`

#### <a id="a4-03-forest"></a>Scene `a4-03-forest`

_There are footprints in the light snow, and then there are a great many footprints, and broken branches._  
_In a clearing, a woman in dark leathers is fighting four soldiers at once, and she is winning._  
**A Guard** _(angry)_: That’s one of the rebels! Take her alive!  
_Shivudu goes in without a word. He doesn’t carry a sword, and doesn’t seem to think he needs one._  
_It is over fast. The soldiers run back into the trees, and the woman turns, and her sword is at Shivudu’s throat._  
**Avanthika** _(angry)_: Who are you? Whose are you?  
_Your Nubbin steps between them with both hands up. It barely reaches her knee._  

**Choices**

- **Tell Shivudu to give her the mask.** — sets `{"a4-mask":"returned"}`

- **Tell her you came up the waterfall.** — sets `{"a4-mask":"told"}`
    - **Avanthika**: Nobody comes up the waterfall.
    - Shivudu holds up the mask as proof. She looks at it a long time, and the sword comes down an inch.

#### <a id="a4-03-forest-done"></a>Done `a4-03-forest-done`

**Avanthika**: I dropped that in the river a month ago. I thought it was gone.  
**Shivudu** _(soft)_: It wasn’t gone. It came to me.  
**Avanthika**: Don’t make it into a poem. I haven’t time for poems.  

### Step 3: Hear what Avanthika and her band are fighting for.

Hint: Go with her to the rebels’ camp under the cliffs. · check: `talk:a4-avanthika:mission`

#### <a id="a4-03-camp"></a>Scene `a4-03-camp`

_The rebels’ camp is cold and small. Nobody in it is older than thirty, and nobody laughs much._  
**Avanthika**: There’s a woman in Mahishmati. A queen. She’s been chained in the palace courtyard for twenty-five years.  
**Avanthika**: The king, Bhallaladeva, put her there. She gathers sticks every day for a fire. She says it’s his.  
**A Rebel** _(sad)_: Our fathers swore to bring her out. Then we swore it. We’re not very good at it yet.  
**Avanthika** _(sad)_: The mask was how I’d get past the palace guards. Without it, the plan is me, shouting.  
**A Rebel**: We tried shouting last year. The gate didn’t notice.  
_Shivudu looks at her, and then at the snow, and you can see him decide._  
**Shivudu**: Then it’s my turn. I’ll bring her out. You can owe me.  

**Choices**

- **Say you’ll come too.**
    - **Avanthika**: A stranger and a… what is that?
    - Your Nubbin sits down on her foot, as an answer.
- **Ask Avanthika why it matters to her.**
    - **Avanthika**: Because she hasn’t stopped waiting. Somebody should be worth it.

**Avanthika** _(soft)_: Keep it. You brought it up. It’s yours now.

_Your Nubbin adds a leaf. It is the best leaf it could find in the snow._

#### <a id="a4-04-gate"></a>Scene `a4-04-gate`

_Thousands of workers stream through the gate carrying stone. Overseers walk beside them with whips they mostly don’t use._  
**A Guard** _(angry)_: Faster. The statue doesn’t raise itself.  
_Inside, half the sky is filled with scaffolding. Something enormous is under it, wrapped in cloth and ropes._  
**Shivudu**: What’s that?  
_A woman next to you, with stone dust in her hair, doesn’t look up._  
**The Crowd** _(sad)_: The king. Him. Made out of gold, so we can look at him more.  

**Choices**

- **Pick up a stone and carry it.**
    - It is heavier than it looks. Your Nubbin carries a pebble, very seriously, all the way in.
- **Look up at the statue.**

#### <a id="a4-04-gate-done"></a>Done `a4-04-gate-done`

_Shivudu keeps his head down, which you can see costs him something._  
**Shivudu**: Twenty-five years she’s been in here. Where’s the courtyard?  

### Step 2: Find the chained queen in the palace courtyard.

Hint: Slip away from the work line towards the old courtyard below the palace. · check: `visit:a4-courtyard:chains`

#### <a id="a4-04-chains"></a>Scene `a4-04-chains`

_The courtyard is bare stone, open to the sun. In the middle of it a woman sits in chains, among a heap of dry sticks._  
_Her hair is long and grey and wild. She is picking up one stick at a time, and laying it on the heap, very carefully._  
_Nobody looks at her. People walk round the edge of the courtyard the way you walk round a grave._  
**A Guard**: She does it every day. Every day for twenty-five years. Says it’s for his fire.  
**A Guard**: The king lets her. He likes to watch.  
_She looks up, straight at your Nubbin. Her eyes are clear, and not wild at all._  
**Devasena** _(think)_: A new face. And a very small one.  

#### <a id="a4-04-chains-done"></a>Done `a4-04-chains-done`

_Shivudu has stopped walking. He stands at the edge of the courtyard and doesn’t know why he can’t move._  
**Shivudu** _(shock)_: I know her. I don’t know her. Why do I know her?  

### Step 3: Bring the queen water without being seen.

Hint: Fill a cup at the trough while the guards change. · check: `give:a4-devasena:water`

#### <a id="a4-04-water"></a>Scene `a4-04-water`

_The guards change at noon. For the count of thirty, nobody is watching the middle of the courtyard._  
_You fill a cup at the trough and walk out to her. Your Nubbin walks in front, so you look like you’re following it._  
**Devasena** _(soft)_: Water. Thank you. Nobody brings me water; it’s not allowed. I suppose you didn’t know.  
_She drinks half, and gives the rest to your Nubbin, who drinks it like medicine._  
**Devasena**: I’m Devasena. Once of Kuntala. I am waiting for my son.  
**Devasena**: Everyone says he’s dead. Everyone has said so for twenty-five years. Everyone is wrong.  
_She lays another stick on the heap, without looking, like a stitch._

    - **Devasena**: Someone always says so. You’re kind. Go before the guards come back.

**Bhallaladeva**: Higher. I want them to see it from the river.

**The Crowd** _(happy)_: Baahubali! Baahubali!  
_Shivudu looks round, baffled, with the rope still on his shoulder. He has no idea what they’re shouting._  
_On the balcony, the king has gone white. He isn’t looking at the statue any more._
