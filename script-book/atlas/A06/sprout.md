# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A6: Seven Voyages

## A6-1. Two Sinbads

- `a06-01-s1`: Help your friend Sinbad carry paper to the big library. · _Pick up the basket. Follow Sinbad through the lanes._
- `a06-01-s2`: Sit on the bench in the shade by the big house. · _Sit down beside Sinbad by the gate._
- `a06-01-s3`: Go in and meet the old man of the house. · _Follow the helper through the courtyard._

#### `sprout:a06-01-street` · [main](main.md#a06-01-street)

_A page in Nova’s atlas opens. A round city sits by a river._  
_You touch the river and, whoosh, you are in a busy lane!_  
**Sinbad the Porter**: Steady now! This basket has paper in it.  
**Sinbad the Porter**: Paper is for writing. Clever people write books on it.  
_Your Nubbin rides on top of the paper rolls._  
**Sinbad the Porter**: How many rolls are in your basket? Let’s count.  
_(Point to each roll. Count them together.)_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 5 and say how many  
> **Sinbad the Porter**: Five rolls! Touch each one as you say its number.  
> Check: `edu:count:paper-rolls:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five paper rolls, touching each one._

_One, two, three, four, five!_  

**Choices**

- **Take one of his baskets as well.**
    - **Sinbad the Porter**: Thank you, friend! Now we carry together.
- **Ask what the scholars do with all this paper.**
    - **Sinbad the Porter**: They write and read. They make books about stars, and numbers, and medicine.

#### `sprout:a06-01-street-done` · [main](main.md#a06-01-street-done)

_At the big library, a young man counts the rolls._  
_He pays Sinbad. Clink, clink!_  
**Sinbad the Porter**: This is the House of Wisdom. It is a library.  
**Sinbad the Porter**: People come here to read, and write, and learn.  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.His.2.K-2, notice): a long time ago there were big libraries in cities  
> **Sinbad the Porter**: A very long time ago, people in Baghdad shared books here.  
> **Sinbad the Porter**: They shared what they knew. Sharing is a good thing to do.  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that long ago there was a big library in a city called Baghdad where people shared books._


#### `sprout:a06-01-bench` · [main](main.md#a06-01-bench)

_Sinbad and you walk home. You stop by a big house._  
_Sinbad sits on a stone bench. He puts down his baskets._  
_Yum! You can smell warm food from the gate._  
**Sinbad the Porter**: Somebody in there is eating lamb. I am going home to lentils!  
**Sinbad the Porter**: I carry heavy loads all day. It is not fair!  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): name a feeling  
> _Sinbad’s face is scrunched up. His fists are tight. How does he feel?_  
> - ✅ Cross. → Yes! Sinbad is cross. His face is scrunched. Cross is a feeling, and feelings are allowed.
> - Sleepy. → Not quite. Look at his scrunched face and his tight fists. Sinbad is cross.
> - Silly. → Not quite. Silly people giggle. Sinbad is scrunched up and growly. He is cross.
> _For grown-ups: Named a feeling (cross) from a face and body._

_Sinbad says this very loudly. A window opens up high._  
_Your Nubbin looks up at the window._  

**Choices**

- **Tell him to keep his voice down.**
    - **Sinbad the Porter**: Why? It is true, even in a whisper.
- **Say it isn’t fair.**
    - **Sinbad the Porter**: Thank you! I feel a little better.

#### `sprout:a06-01-bench-done` · [main](main.md#a06-01-bench-done)

_The gate opens. A helper in a neat turban looks out._  
**The Steward**: My master wants to meet the man who was talking.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sound of a name  
> _Sinbad starts with a “sss” sound. Which of these starts with “sss” too?_  
> - ✅ Sun. → Yes! Sss-sun and Sss-Sinbad start the same.
> - Moon. → Mmm-moon starts with “mmm”. Sinbad starts with “sss”. Sss-sun does too!
> - Boat. → Buh-boat starts with “buh”. Sss-sun starts like Sss-Sinbad!
> _For grown-ups: Matched the first sound “s” in Sinbad and sun._

**The Steward**: And his friend. And that.  
_(That is your Nubbin. It waves.)_  

#### `sprout:a06-01-hall` · [main](main.md#a06-01-hall)

_Inside, there is a courtyard with a fountain._  
_Then there is a long room with soft cushions._  
_An old man with a white beard sits at the end._  
**Sinbad the Sailor**: Sit, sit! Eat first. Lamb is better than grumbling.  
**Sinbad the Porter**: You heard me grumble?  
**Sinbad the Sailor**: The whole street heard! What is your name?  
**Sinbad the Porter**: Sinbad. Sinbad the porter.  
**Sinbad the Sailor**: Ha! I am Sinbad too. Sinbad the sailor.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, ask): positions: on, under, beside  
> _Sinbad the porter sits on the very edge of a cushion._  
> _Is he sitting on the cushion, or under it?_  
> - ✅ On it. → Yes! He is on the cushion. (Pat a cushion near you.)
> - Under it. → Not under. A cushion is soft, but nobody sits under it! Sinbad is on the cushion.
> _For grown-ups: Used the position word “on” for where someone sits._


#### `sprout:a06-01-hall-done` · [main](main.md#a06-01-hall-done)

_The porter sits right on the edge of the cushion._  
_He looks a little shy in such a fancy room._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, notice): feeling shy is okay  
> _Sinbad feels shy. Shy means a bit nervous with new people._  
> _That is okay. A smile and a friend help. Look, you are his friend!_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that feeling shy is okay, and that a smile or a friend helps._


#### `sprout:a06-01-complete` · [main](main.md#a06-01-complete)

**Sinbad the Sailor**: Seven times I went to sea. Seven stories!  
**Sinbad the Sailor**: I will tell one story each night. You can take food home.  
**Sinbad the Porter**: My daughter will love that.  
**Sinbad the Sailor**: You too, little friend. Sit close.  
_The old man smiles at you and your Nubbin._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.His.2.K-2, notice): long ago, stories were told out loud  
> _Long ago, many people could not read. So they told stories out loud._  
> _Families still tell stories at bedtime. Does somebody tell you one?_  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that long ago stories were told out loud, and linked it to bedtime stories today._


## A6-2. The Island That Moved

- `a06-02-s1`: Listen to the first story. Step into it. · _Sit on your cushion until the carpet turns to sea._
- `a06-02-s2`: Pick up sticks for the fire on the little island. · _Pick up sticks under the trees. Take them to the fire._
- `a06-02-s3`: Hold on to the washtub in the sea. · _Keep hold of the tub and kick._

#### `sprout:a06-02-telling` · [main](main.md#a06-02-telling)

**Sinbad the Sailor**: When I was young, I spent my money too fast.  
**Sinbad the Sailor**: So I went to a city called Basra to take a ship.  
_The carpet turns blue. It starts to move!_  
_Now you are on a ship. It has a big triangle sail._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.2, ask): name a triangle  
> _The ship’s big sail has three corners and three straight sides._  
> _What shape is it?_  
> - ✅ A triangle. → Yes! Three corners, three sides. A triangle! (Trace one in the air.)
> - A circle. → A circle is round with no corners. This sail has three corners. It is a triangle!
> - A square. → A square has four corners. Count the sail’s corners: one, two, three. A triangle!
> _For grown-ups: Named a triangle by counting its sides and corners._

_A young man leans on the rail. He grins at the sea._  
**Young Sinbad**: You are new! Good. Nobody else has heard my jokes.  

**Choices**

- **Ask to hear a joke.**
    - **Young Sinbad**: What is the difference between a merchant and a sailor?
    - **Young Sinbad**: About three days out of Basra!
- **Ask if he’s been to sea before.**
    - **Young Sinbad**: Never! Isn’t it wonderful?

#### `sprout:a06-02-telling-done` · [main](main.md#a06-02-telling-done)

_Days and days of blue sea go by. Splash, splash!_  
_Then a sailor shouts, “Land!”_  
_A small green island. It is flat as a plate._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS3-1, notice): sea and land are different places  
> **Young Sinbad**: The sea is water, and the island is land.  
> **Young Sinbad**: Fish live in the sea, and trees grow on land.  
> _For grown-ups: Noticed that sea and land are different places where different things live._


#### `sprout:a06-02-island` · [main](main.md#a06-02-island)

**The Captain**: Everybody ashore! Wash your shirts. Back by evening!  
_The island feels soft and bouncy._  
**Young Sinbad**: Sticks for the fire! Let’s cook on dry land.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count sticks to 4  
> **Young Sinbad**: Four sticks make a good fire. Count them with me: one, two, three, four!  
> Check: `edu:count:firewood:4`
> _For grown-ups: Counted four sticks for the fire._

_The sailors light a fire. Somebody sings._  
_Your Nubbin sits down. Then it jumps up!_  
_The ground is warm. It is warm from underneath._  

**Choices**

- **Tell Sinbad the ground is warm.**
    - **Young Sinbad**: It is just the sun, friend. Have a date.
- **Kick sand over the fire.**
    - **Young Sinbad**: Hey! That was our supper!

#### `sprout:a06-02-island-done` · [main](main.md#a06-02-island-done)

_The island shakes. Wobble, wobble!_  
_All the little trees lean the same way._  
**The Captain**: It is not an island! It is a fish! Run!  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, notice): whales are big animals that breathe air  
> _It is a whale! Whales are very big animals. They live in the sea._  
> _Whales breathe air, like you. So they come up to the top to breathe._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a whale is a big sea animal that breathes air._


#### `sprout:a06-02-tub` · [main](main.md#a06-02-tub)

_The whale rolls over, slowly. A great eye opens._  
_Then it dives. Everyone falls into the sea!_  
**Young Sinbad**: Here! The washtub! Hold on!  
_Sinbad holds the tub, and you hold Sinbad. Your Nubbin holds your ear._  
_Far below, something sings one long, low note. Ooooo._  

> **Lesson** (music, ask): high and low sounds  
> _The whale sings a long, low note._  
> _Is a whale’s song high like a mouse, or low like a drum?_  
> - ✅ Low, like a drum. → Yes! Big animals often make deep, low sounds. Ooooo! (Hum it low.)
> - High, like a mouse. → Mice squeak high. The whale is huge, and its note is low. Ooooo!
> _For grown-ups: Linked a big animal with a low sound._

_Then it is quiet._  

**Choices**

- **Kick, hard.**
    - The tub spins round and round. Whee!
- **Float, and let the current take you.**
    - **Young Sinbad**: Good thinking. The sea knows where it is going.

#### `sprout:a06-02-tub-done` · [main](main.md#a06-02-tub-done)

_A day goes by, then a night, then another day._  
_Bump! The tub hits sand._  
**Young Sinbad**: Land! Hooray!  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, ask): put events in order: first, next, last  
> _First there was a day. Next there was a night. What came last?_  
> - ✅ Another day. → Yes! A day, a night, then another day.
> - Another night. → Listen again: a day, a night, and then another day. That came last.
> _For grown-ups: Retold a short sequence in order._


#### `sprout:a06-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a06-02-complete)

_The carpet is a carpet again. The porter’s mouth is open._  
**Sinbad the Porter**: A fish. With trees on it!  
**Sinbad the Sailor**: A whale. We did not know the word then.  
**Sinbad the Porter**: And you lit a fire on its back!  
**Sinbad the Sailor**: It was a very good fire.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, notice): laughing together  
> **Sinbad the Porter**: Everybody laughed. Laughing together makes friends feel close.  
> **Sinbad the Porter**: You can laugh with a friend, and never at them.  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that laughing together helps friends feel close._

_Your Nubbin is still damp. Drip, drip._  

## A6-3. The King’s Stables

- `a06-03-s1`: Go with the groom to meet the king. · _Follow the groom up from the beach._
- `a06-03-s2`: Help Sinbad look at the cargo of a ship. · _Look at the marks on the bales._
- `a06-03-s3`: Help Sinbad show who he is. · _Look inside the bale with the green cord._

#### `sprout:a06-03-shore` · [main](main.md#a06-03-shore)

_You are on a beach. Sinbad sits up and counts his fingers._  
_Down the beach, lovely horses eat grass by the waves._  
_A kind groom takes you both to see his king._  
**The Island King**: A man who came out of the sea in a tub!  
**The Island King**: Tell me about your city.  
**Young Sinbad**: It is round, your majesty. Perfectly round. The palace is in the middle.  
**The Island King**: Round? Why round?  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.2, ask): name a circle  
> _Sinbad’s city is round, like a plate. What shape is a plate?_  
> - ✅ A circle. → Yes! A circle is round, with no corners. (Draw a big circle in the air.)
> - A triangle. → A triangle has three corners. A round city is a circle!
> _For grown-ups: Named a circle as the shape of a round thing._


**Choices**

- **Say: so the palace is the same distance from every gate.**
    - **The Island King**: Fair! I like a fair city.
- **Say: so the markets never come to an end.**
    - **The Island King**: Ha! A city you can’t get out of!

#### `sprout:a06-03-shore-done` · [main](main.md#a06-03-shore-done)

_The king makes Sinbad keeper of the harbour._  
_Sinbad writes down every ship that comes and goes._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Literacy, notice): writing helps us remember  
> **Young Sinbad**: I write the ships in my book. Then I do not forget.  
> **Young Sinbad**: Writing helps us remember!  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that writing things down helps us remember._

_And he asks each captain the way to Baghdad._  

#### `sprout:a06-03-harbour` · [main](main.md#a06-03-harbour)

_Months go by._  
_One day a ship sails in with a patch on its sail._  
**Young Sinbad**: That is our ship! That is my ship!  
**The Captain**: We sell these bales for a merchant who drowned. Sinbad of Baghdad.  
**Young Sinbad**: I am Sinbad! I am him!  
**The Captain**: Everyone says they are Sinbad when there is a bale to be had.  
_Sinbad’s shoulders droop. Nobody believes him._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): feelings when nobody believes you  
> _Sinbad tells the truth, but nobody believes him. How might he feel?_  
> - ✅ Sad, and cross. → Yes. It feels sad and cross when nobody believes you. A friend nearby helps. You are right here!
> - Very, very happy. → Hmm. His shoulders are drooping. He feels sad and cross, and a friend can help.
> _For grown-ups: Thought about how it feels when nobody believes you._


#### `sprout:a06-03-harbour-done` · [main](main.md#a06-03-harbour-done)

_The captain folds his arms._  
_The bales sit on the quay._  
_Your Nubbin sniffs one. Sniff, sniff!_  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, ask): the nose smells  
> _Your Nubbin sniffs a bale. Which part of the body does the smelling?_  
> - ✅ The nose. → Yes! The nose smells. Sniff, sniff! (Touch your nose.)
> - The knee. → Knees bend, but they do not smell! Noses do. Sniff, sniff!
> _For grown-ups: Named the nose as the part that smells._


#### `sprout:a06-03-proof` · [main](main.md#a06-03-proof)

**Young Sinbad**: Ask me anything about the voyage.  
**The Captain**: What is in the bale with the green cord?  
_Sinbad goes blank. He cannot remember._  
_Your Nubbin tugs the cord. A corner of blue cloth peeks out._  
_(Point to the blue. Say “blue”!)_  

> **Lesson** (art, ELOF: Approaches to Learning, do): name the colour blue  
> **Young Sinbad**: Blue! It is blue cloth! It is indigo, from my uncle’s shop!  
> Check: `edu:tap:colour:blue`
> _For grown-ups: Found and named the colour blue._


**Choices**

- **Point at the blue cloth, and let Sinbad say it.**
    - **Young Sinbad**: Indigo! Twenty lengths from my uncle’s shop!
    - **The Captain**: That is right!
- **Whisper, “Your uncle’s shop.”**
    - **Young Sinbad**: My uncle’s indigo! Twenty lengths!
    - **The Captain**: Only Sinbad would know that!

#### `sprout:a06-03-proof-done` · [main](main.md#a06-03-proof-done)

**The Captain**: I am sorry I left you.  
**The Captain**: Please take your bales. And take my hand.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, notice): saying sorry and forgiving  
> _The captain says sorry. Saying sorry is brave._  
> _Sinbad says okay. That is called forgiving. Friends do both._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that saying sorry is brave and that friends can forgive._

_Sinbad shakes the captain’s hand._  

#### `sprout:a06-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a06-03-complete)

_Sinbad sells his bales. He gives the king a gift._  
_He sails home with the captain. They are friends now._  
**Sinbad the Porter**: He left you in the sea, and you shook his hand?  
**Sinbad the Sailor**: He came back with all my things. That is rare.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, ask): who did what in a story  
> _Who said sorry to Sinbad?_  
> - ✅ The captain. → Yes! The captain said sorry, and Sinbad forgave him.
> - The king. → The king was kind, but the captain said sorry. Sinbad forgave the captain.
> _For grown-ups: Remembered who did what in the story._


## A6-4. The Roc’s Egg

- `a06-04-s1`: Find out where the ship has gone. · _Climb the tallest tree and look all round._
- `a06-04-s2`: Walk round the white dome. · _Walk round it. Put your ear to it._
- `a06-04-s3`: Tie yourselves to the big bird’s foot. · _Help Sinbad with the turban cloth._

#### `sprout:a06-04-left` · [main](main.md#a06-04-left)

**Sinbad the Sailor**: This is the second voyage. I got bored, so I sailed again.  
_You are on an island with fruit trees and a cold spring._  
**Young Sinbad**: Rest for an hour. The captain said an hour.  
_You and Sinbad sleep under a tree._  
_You wake up, and the sea is empty. The ship has gone!_  
**Young Sinbad**: Twice now! Why does everyone leave me?  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): what to do when you feel left out  
> _Sinbad feels sad and left out. What could help him?_  
> - ✅ A friend beside him. → Yes! A friend sits close. You are here. That helps a lot.
> - Hiding all alone. → Hiding can make sad feel bigger. A friend sitting close helps more.
> _For grown-ups: Thought about what helps when someone feels sad and left out._


**Choices**

- **Say it isn’t him, it’s captains.**
    - **Young Sinbad**: That is kind. It is probably true.
- **Say you should climb a tree and look.**
    - **Young Sinbad**: Yes! Doing something is better than sulking.

#### `sprout:a06-04-left-done` · [main](main.md#a06-04-left-done)

_You climb the tallest tree on the island._  
_From the top, you can see everything. The bushes below look tiny!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): compare: tallest  
> _You climb the tallest tree._  
> _Is the tallest tree taller or shorter than the bushes?_  
> - ✅ Taller. → Yes! The tallest tree is taller than the bushes. (Stretch up high!)
> - Shorter. → Tallest means the most tall. The tree is taller than the bushes. (Stretch up high!)
> _For grown-ups: Compared heights using “tall” and “taller”._

_On the far side is a great white dome. It shines._  

#### `sprout:a06-04-dome` · [main](main.md#a06-04-dome)

_The white dome is smooth and round. It has no door._  
_Your Nubbin puts an ear to the wall. It goes very still._  
_Tap, tap, tap! Something inside is tapping._  
_The sun goes out. A giant bird lands on the dome._  
**Young Sinbad**: It is an egg! And that is its mother!  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, ask): baby birds hatch from eggs  
> _Tap, tap! Something is inside the egg._  
> _What do baby birds do when they are ready?_  
> - ✅ They hatch out. → Yes! A baby bird taps its way out of the egg. That is hatching!
> - They fly away inside it. → Not yet! A baby bird taps with its beak, and the shell cracks. Out it hatches!
> _For grown-ups: Learned that baby birds hatch out of eggs._


#### `sprout:a06-04-dome-done` · [main](main.md#a06-04-dome-done)

_The giant bird tucks her head under her wing._  
_She snores, zzzzz! The snore lifts your hair._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.2.4, notice): sound words  
> _Zzzzz! Snore is a sound word. Sound words sound like what they mean._  
> _Can you snore like the bird? (Make a big snore.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Enjoyed a sound word (zzzzz) and made the sound._


#### `sprout:a06-04-leg` · [main](main.md#a06-04-leg)

**Young Sinbad**: She will fly at sunrise. We must go with her.  
_Sinbad unwinds his turban. It is very long._  
_You tie yourselves to one great toe, gently._  
**Young Sinbad**: Don’t pull. Don’t tickle. Don’t breathe.  
_At dawn, the bird spreads her wings. Whoosh, up you go!_  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, notice): birds have wings and feathers  
> _Birds fly with wings and feathers._  
> _Their feathers are light, so the wind can lift them._  
> _(Flap your arms like wings.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that birds have wings and feathers, and flapped like a bird._


**Choices**

- **Look down.**
    - The island is a coin, then a crumb, then gone.
- **Shut your eyes.**
    - Your Nubbin keeps its eyes open for both of you.

#### `sprout:a06-04-leg-done` · [main](main.md#a06-04-leg-done)

_At noon, the bird lands on a high ridge._  
_You untie the turban. You are shaking, but you did it._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, notice): being brave when scared  
> _You felt scared up high. Being brave means doing it while you feel scared._  
> _Take a big breath. In... and out. You were brave!_  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that being brave means acting while scared, and tried a calming breath._


#### `sprout:a06-04-complete` · [main](main.md#a06-04-complete)

_The big bird flies back up into the sun._  
_A feather floats down. It is as long as you are tall!_  
_Your Nubbin catches it, and falls over._  
**Young Sinbad**: Keep it. Now people will believe you.  
**Sinbad the Porter**: That is not a chicken.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): compare: longer and shorter  
> _The feather is as long as you are tall._  
> _Is it longer than your hand, or shorter?_  
> - ✅ Longer. → Yes! A feather as long as you is much longer than your hand. (Hold up your hand.)
> - Shorter. → Look again. It is as tall as you! That is longer than a hand.
> _For grown-ups: Compared a long feather with a hand using “longer”._


## A6-5. The Valley of Diamonds

- `a06-05-s1`: Walk down into the valley on tiptoe. · _Step on the bare stone between the sleepy snakes._
- `a06-05-s2`: Work out where the meat comes from. · _Watch the rim of the valley and the eagles._
- `a06-05-s3`: Ride the eagle up out of the valley. · _Hold on to Sinbad’s belt until the top._

#### `sprout:a06-05-valley` · [main](main.md#a06-05-valley)

_You look down into a deep, narrow valley. It sparkles like stars._  
**Young Sinbad**: Diamonds! Look how big they are!  
_Long snakes lie in the sun. They are fast asleep._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, notice): snakes warm up in the sun  
> _Snakes lie in the sun to warm up._  
> _Their bodies do not make much heat._  
> _When the sun warms them, they feel good._  
> _Just like a cat on a warm wall._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that snakes lie in the sun to warm up._

_You tiptoe down, between the snakes. Tip, tip, tip._  
_Your Nubbin tiptoes too. It does it very well._  

**Choices**

- **Hold your breath past the biggest snake.**
    - The snake opens one eye. It is not hungry, so it closes it again.
- **Go the long way round it.**
    - It takes an hour. Sinbad grumbles in a whisper.

#### `sprout:a06-05-valley-done` · [main](main.md#a06-05-valley-done)

_Something falls out of the sky. Splat, it is a big piece of meat!_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, do): clap the beats in a word  
> _Eagles carry the meat up. E-agle. Clap twice: ea-gle! Two claps._  
> Check: `edu:clap:syllables:eagle`
> _For grown-ups: Clapped the two beats in “eagle”._

_Everyone stares at it. What is it doing here?_  

#### `sprout:a06-05-meat` · [main](main.md#a06-05-meat)
