## A7-1. The Ofrenda

- `a7-01-s1`: Step into the marigold page. Find the town square. · _Touch the page. Follow the paper flags down to the square._
- `a7-01-s2`: Help carry the marigolds into the house. · _Take the flowers from the cart. Bring them to Abuelita._
- `a7-01-s3`: Look at the family ofrenda. Meet Mamá Coco. · _Go to the altar at the end of the room. Look at the photos._

#### `sprout:a7-01-plaza` · [main](main.md#a7-01-plaza)

_Nova’s atlas opens at a page of orange flowers and paper flags._  
_Whoosh! You and your Nubbin are in a town in Mexico._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, notice): Mexico is a country, and many people there speak Spanish  
> _Mexico is a country. Many people there speak Spanish._  
> _(Find Mexico on a map or a globe, if you have one.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that Mexico is a country, and that many people there speak Spanish._

_Everyone carries marigolds. They are bright orange!_  
_A boy is shining shoes. He sees your Nubbin._  
**Miguel**: Wow! Are you here for Día de Muertos?  
_That is a festival. Families remember people who have died._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, do): say “hola”, hello in Spanish  
> **Miguel**: Hola! That means hello in Spanish. Say it with me!  
> _(Wave and say “Hola!”)_  
> Check: `edu:say:word:hola`
> _For grown-ups: Said hola, the Spanish word for hello._


**Choices**

- **Say you are visitors from far away.**
    - **Miguel**: Then come and see our ofrenda! Abuelita’s is the best.
- **Ask who the man on the statue is.**
    - **Miguel**: That is Ernesto de la Cruz. He was a famous singer!
- **Let your Nubbin say hello.**
    - Your Nubbin hops onto the shoe box. Miguel laughs and laughs.

#### `sprout:a7-01-plaza-done` · [main](main.md#a7-01-plaza-done)

**Miguel**: I’m Miguel. Come on, I’ll show you my house.  
_A thin, hairless dog trots along behind you. Pant, pant!_  
**Miguel**: That is Dante. He isn’t mine. He thinks he is!  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, ask): what a dog needs to live  
> _Dante is a dog. What does a dog need to live?_  
> - ✅ Food, water and a cosy home → Yes! Dante gets all three, and a lot of cuddles.
> - Only toys → Toys are fun! But Dante needs food, water and a home first.
> - Only sweets → Dante would love that! But dogs need proper food and water.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about what a dog needs to live: food, water and a home._

#### `sprout:a7-01-petals-done` · [main](main.md#a7-01-petals-done)

#### `sprout:a7-01-photo` · [main](main.md#a7-01-photo)

**Choices**

#### `sprout:a7-02-hideout-done` · [main](main.md#a7-02-hideout-done)

> _For grown-ups: Used the words above and below for positions._


#### `sprout:a7-02-square` · [main](main.md#a7-02-square)

_In the square, a sign says, “Talent show tonight!”_  
**Miguel**: I just need a real guitar.  
**Abuelita**: MIGUEL!  
_Abuelita is very cross. Her face is red._  
**Abuelita**: Music! On Día de Muertos!  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): calming down when you feel cross  
> _Abuelita is cross. What helps when we feel cross?_  
> - ✅ Breathe in and out slowly → Yes! In through your nose, out through your mouth. (Try it!)
> - Shout louder → Shouting makes cross feelings bigger. A slow breath helps them shrink.
> - Stamp and throw things → Things can get broken that way. A slow breath is gentler.
> _For grown-ups: Practised slow breathing as a way to calm down when cross._

_She marches Miguel home. You follow, quietly._  
_At the ofrenda, Miguel sees the old photo. The guitar has a skull._  

#### `sprout:a7-02-square-done` · [main](main.md#a7-02-square-done)

_The guitar has a big round hole in the middle._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.2, ask): name a circle  
> _The hole in the guitar is round. What shape is it?_  
> - ✅ A circle → Yes! A circle. Round with no corners.
> - A square → A square has four corners. This hole is round, a circle.
> - A triangle → A triangle has three corners. This hole is round, a circle.
> _For grown-ups: Named a circle._

**Miguel**: He’s my family. I’m meant to be a musician!  

#### `sprout:a7-02-guitar` · [main](main.md#a7-02-guitar)

**Abuelita**: No more guitar.

_Everyone is quiet. Abuelita looks sad too._  
**Miguel**: I don’t want to be in this family!

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.1.4, ask): name a feeling from what a character does

> - ✅ Sad → Yes. Miguel is very sad. His guitar is broken.
> - Silly → Silly is giggly. Miguel is hiding his face, so he is sad.
> - Sleepy → Sleepy is yawny. Miguel is hiding his face, so he is sad.
> _For grown-ups: Named a feeling, sadness, from what a character did._

> _For grown-ups: Talked about asking first when borrowing._
