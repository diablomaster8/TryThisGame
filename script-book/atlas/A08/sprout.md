# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A8: The Twelve Tasks

## A8-1. Caesar’s Challenge

- `a08-01-s1`: Touch the little green dot on the map. · _The map is all red. Look by the sea for one green dot._
- `a08-01-s2`: Go to the village and hear what the Romans want. · _Follow Obelix and the boars. Everyone is in the square._

#### `sprout:a08-01-map` · [main](main.md#a08-01-map)

_Nova’s book opens at a big map._  
_The map is red. Red, red, red!_  
_Red is for the Romans. They have nearly everywhere._  
_But one little dot is green. It sits by the sea._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, ask): a map shows where places are  
> _(Point.) Where is the village? In the red, or the green dot?_  
> - ✅ In the green dot → Yes! The village is the little green dot by the sea.
> - In the red → Not quite: red is the Romans. The village is the green dot.
> _For grown-ups: Read a simple map: found the village as the green dot among the red._

_You touch the green dot and whoosh! You are in a wood._  
_A small white dog zooms out of the bushes._  
_He sniffs your Nubbin. Your Nubbin sniffs back._  
_Wag, wag, wag! They are best friends already._  

**Choices**

- **Let them work it out.**
    - Circle, circle, sneeze! Then they are friends.
- **Crouch down and hold out a hand.**
    - The dog gives your hand one lick. Then he runs back to your Nubbin.

#### `sprout:a08-01-map-done` · [main](main.md#a08-01-map-done)

_Two men come running through the trees._  
_One is small, with a winged helmet. One is very big._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 3  
> _(Count the two men and the dog together. One, two, three!)_  
> _Three! Two men and one dog._  
> Check: `edu:count:friends:3`
> _For grown-ups: Counted three together: two men and a dog._

**Obelix**: Dogmatix! You found a little round friend!  
_The dog wags his whole body._  

#### `sprout:a08-01-square` · [main](main.md#a08-01-square)

_The village is round and noisy. Everyone stands in the square._  
**Caius Pupus**: Salve! That is Latin for hello. I am Caius Pupus.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, do): Latin word: salve means hello  
> _(Say it back: SAL-weh! Wave as you say it.)_  
> **Caius Pupus**: Salve! Lovely. The Romans said it every day.  
> Check: `edu:say:latin-word:salve`
> _For grown-ups: Learned and said the Latin word “salve”, which means hello._

**Caius Pupus**: Caesar says: do twelve hard tasks. Then you are gods.  
**Vitalstatistix**: Twelve? Ha! We will do them all, and have lunch.  
**Asterix**: Who will say if we pass? Not a Roman. Not a Gaul.  
_Everyone looks at you. You are neither!_  

**Choices**

- **“All right. I’ll referee.”**
    - **Vitalstatistix**: Splendid! A referee who is on nobody’s side!
- **“Me? I only came in through a map.”**
    - **Asterix**: That is why you are fair. You do not belong to anyone’s side.

#### `sprout:a08-01-square-done` · [main](main.md#a08-01-square-done)

**Caius Pupus**: Here is your wax tablet. It is for ticking off tasks.  
_Down the side are marks. I, II, III, IV, V…_  
_The Romans wrote numbers with letters. I means one._  

> **Lesson** (maths, notice): Romans wrote numbers with letters; I is one  
> **Caius Pupus**: The Romans wrote one as I. A little twig. Like one finger.  
> **Caius Pupus**: Hold up one finger. That is I!  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that Romans wrote numbers as letters, and that I means one._

_One line for every task. Twelve in all._  

#### `sprout:a08-01-complete` · [main](main.md#a08-01-complete)

_Getafix the druid stirs a big cauldron._  
**Getafix**: This potion is for Asterix. Only a little.  
**Obelix**: Can I have some? Please?  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): naming feelings and what helps  
> _Obelix is sad. How could we help him feel better?_  
> - ✅ Give him a hug → A hug helps. Obelix feels a little bit better.
> - Laugh at him → That would make him feel worse. A hug or a kind word helps.
> - Walk away → He would feel lonely. Staying close helps when we are sad.
> _For grown-ups: Named a sad feeling and chose a kind way to help a friend._

**Getafix**: You fell in it as a baby. You do not need more.  
_Obelix is sad for a moment. Then he thinks of boar._  
_Your Nubbin and the dog curl up asleep by the fire._  

## A8-2. Asbestos

- `a08-02-s1`: Walk along the running road and meet the runner. · _Walk from the start stone to the post. Then say hello._
- `a08-02-s2`: Drop your hand to start the race. · _Drop your hand. Then watch the road._

#### `sprout:a08-02-track` · [main](main.md#a08-02-track)

_Task one is a race on a long dirt road._  
_A young man is stretching. His name is Asbestos._  
**Asbestos**: I am from Greece. I run very, very fast.  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.His.2.K-2, notice): the Olympic Games began in Greece  
> **Asbestos**: The Olympic Games began in Greece, long, long ago.  
> **Asbestos**: People came to Olympia to run. We still have the Games today!  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that the Olympic Games began in Greece, long ago, and still happen today._

**Asterix**: How nice.  
_Your Nubbin and the dog crouch down at the stone._  
_Their bottoms wiggle. They think they are racing too._  

**Choices**

- **Let them race too.**
    - **Caius Pupus**: Animals are not in the rules.
    - You say they are not out of the rules either. Pupus sighs.
- **Scoop them up out of the way.**
    - You lift your Nubbin. The dog sits on your foot.

#### `sprout:a08-02-track-done` · [main](main.md#a08-02-track-done)

_You lift your hand. The runner is still as a statue._  
_Asterix yawns a very big yawn._  
_Everyone waits for you to say the words._  

> **Lesson** (maths, ELOF: Mathematics Development, ask): count down from three  
> _Let us count down to the start. Three, two… What comes next?_  
> - ✅ One! → Yes! Three, two, one… GO!
> - Four → Four comes before three. Counting down goes three, two, one… GO!
> _For grown-ups: Counted down from three to one to start the race._


#### `sprout:a08-02-race` · [main](main.md#a08-02-race)

_Your hand drops. Whoosh, dust everywhere!_  
_The runner is a speck on the road._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Literacy, do): sound words  
> _(Say it with a wave of your arm: Whoosh!)_  
> _Whoosh! That is the sound of something fast._  
> Check: `edu:say:sound-word:whoosh`
> _For grown-ups: Said a sound word, “whoosh”, with an arm movement._

_Asterix walks slowly. He takes one sip of potion._  
_Zoom! Now he sits on the finish post, waving._  
_The runner zooms past. He is too surprised to stop._  

#### `sprout:a08-02-race-done` · [main](main.md#a08-02-race-done)

_Far away, a speck is still running._  
_Obelix shades his eyes and watches._  
**Obelix**: He is very fast. He just was not first.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): it is all right to lose and be kind  
> _Asbestos did not win. How might he feel?_  
> - ✅ Surprised, and maybe a bit sad → Yes. Feelings can be mixed. A kind word helps him.
> - Nothing at all → Everybody feels something. Losing can feel sad or surprising.
> _For grown-ups: Named the feelings of someone who loses a race, and that kindness helps._


#### `sprout:a08-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a08-02-complete)

_You scratch a mark on the wax tablet._  
_It is the first mark. It looks like this: I._  
_Dogmatix drops a twig on the tablet._  
**Caius Pupus**: I! One twig!  
**Caius Pupus**: One task done. Eleven to go.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.4, do): I is one  
> _(Hold up one finger. That is I, for one.)_  
> _Eleven tasks left. We will count them all._  
> Check: `edu:show:fingers:1`
> _For grown-ups: Showed one finger for the Roman numeral I._

_Your Nubbin and the dog look proud, as if they won._  

## A8-3. Verses

- `a08-03-s1`: Carry the three javelins to the line. · _They are long. Carry one at a time._
- `a08-03-s2`: Watch the throws and count your steps. · _Stand by the far marker. Count to the end of the field._

#### `sprout:a08-03-field` · [main](main.md#a08-03-field)

_Task two is a wide field with a white line._  
**Verses**: I am Verses, from Persia. I will throw today.  
_He has three long javelins wrapped in silk._  
**Verses**: This one is Swift. This one is Patient.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Literacy, do): first sound of a name  
> _(Say it slowly: sss-wift. What sound does Swift start with?)_  
> _Sss! Swift starts with sss, like a snake._  
> Check: `edu:say:first-sound:s-swift`
> _For grown-ups: Listened for and said the first sound in the name “Swift”._

**Verses**: And this one is saving herself for later.  
_You will carry them to the line. Three long javelins!_  

**Choices**

- **Carry them very carefully, like Verses would.**
    - **Verses**: You carry them like friends. Thank you.
- **Ask Obelix to help.**
    - Obelix carries all three in one hand. He asks where to put the cart.

#### `sprout:a08-03-field-done` · [main](main.md#a08-03-field-done)

_You lay the javelins down by the line._  
_Your Nubbin sits beside the one called Patient._  
_Swift is long, and Patient is longer. The third one is longest of all._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): compare lengths: long, longer, longest  
> _(Point to the javelins.) Which one is the longest?_  
> - ✅ The third one → Yes! Long, longer, longest. The third one is longest.
> - Swift → Swift is long, but the third one is longer still. It is the longest.
> _For grown-ups: Compared three lengths: long, longer, longest._


#### `sprout:a08-03-throws` · [main](main.md#a08-03-throws)

_Verses throws Swift. It flies like a bird._  
_It lands far down the field. Quiver, quiver!_  
_You walk the field. One step, two steps, three steps…_  
_Then Asterix throws. His javelin flies away, over the hill._  
**Verses**: Where is it going?  
**Asterix**: I just threw it!  
**Verses**: What is your javelin called?  
**Asterix**: Er... Stick.  
_Dogmatix drops a twig at your feet. It is his eleventh today._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, ask): being a good sport  
> _Verses threw so well, and Asterix threw further. What can Verses do?_  
> - ✅ Say “well done” → Well done! Yes. A good sport is kind, win or lose.
> - Stamp and shout → That would feel bad for everyone. “Well done” feels good.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about being a good sport: saying well done when someone else does better._


**Choices**

- **Start walking after it with your tablet.**
    - **Caius Pupus**: Come back! We will be here for a week.
    - You come back and call it a win.
- **Call it: the javelin is still going.**
    - **Verses**: A fair ruling. A throw that is still flying cannot be measured.

#### `sprout:a08-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a08-03-complete)

_Verses wraps Swift up in its silk again._  
**Verses**: A good throw is a good throw. Whoever throws it.  
_You make a mark on the tablet: II._  
_Dogmatix drops another twig. He wags at both._  
_Two twigs, I and I. Two tasks done!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, notice): II is two  
> **Caius Pupus**: Two tasks, so two twigs: I, I. The Romans wrote II.  
> **Caius Pupus**: Hold up two fingers. That is II!  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that the Roman numeral II is two twigs._


## A8-4. Cilindric

- `a08-04-s1`: Read the rule book before the bout. · _The book is on the bench. Open it together._
- `a08-04-s2`: Referee the bout. · _Stand by the rope. Asterix has an idea._

#### `sprout:a08-04-rules` · [main](main.md#a08-04-rules)

_Task three is a ring made of rope._  
_A square man is folding a towel into neat quarters._  
**Cilindric**: I am Cilindric. Please read my rules.  
_The rule book is as thick as a loaf of bread._  
**Cilindric**: Rule one: bow. Rule two: bow again.  
_Rules help everyone play fairly. Even long ones._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, ask): rules help everyone be fair  
> _Why do games have rules?_  
> - ✅ So everyone plays fairly → Yes! Rules help everyone to be fair and safe.
> - So nobody has fun → Not quite. Good rules help us all have fun together.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about why rules in a game help everyone to be fair._


**Choices**

- **Read every rule, properly.**
    - **Cilindric**: A referee who reads! I like you.
- **Skim it and nod wisely.**
    - **Cilindric**: Hm. You skipped the page about nodding.

#### `sprout:a08-04-rules-done` · [main](main.md#a08-04-rules-done)

_You close the book. Cilindric bows to it._  
_Then he bows to you, and to Asterix._  
_Then he bows to the meadow._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, do): Latin word: vale means goodbye  
> _(Bow and say it: WAH-leh!)_  
> **Caius Pupus**: Vale! That is Latin for goodbye. Romans said it when they left.  
> Check: `edu:say:latin-word:vale`
> _For grown-ups: Learned the Latin word “vale”, which means goodbye._


#### `sprout:a08-04-bout` · [main](main.md#a08-04-bout)

_They bow. Cilindric moves like water._  
**Asterix**: Show me that again, slowly, please.  
**Cilindric**: Of course. The hand, here. The foot, here. The turn, so.  
_He turns and tucks, and turns again._  
_Oh! He has tied himself in a knot!_  
**Cilindric**: I beat myself. The rule book says whoever beats himself loses!  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): helping a friend who feels silly  
> _Cilindric is tied in a knot and feels silly. What helps?_  
> - ✅ Help him get free, kindly → Yes. Helping, with no laughing, makes him feel better.
> - Point and laugh → That would make him feel sadder. Helping is kinder.
> _For grown-ups: Chose kind help over laughing at a friend who feels embarrassed._


**Choices**

- **Help untie him.**
    - It takes you, Obelix and both animals. He thanks everyone by name.
- **Look up rule three hundred and twelve to be sure.**
    - **Cilindric**: A referee who checks! Wonderful.

#### `sprout:a08-04-complete` · [main](main.md#a08-04-complete)

**Cilindric**: A tidy result. Please keep the rule book.  
_He folds his towel and walks away._  
_You make a mark: III. Three twigs, I, I, I._  

> **Lesson** (maths, notice): III is three  
> **Caius Pupus**: Three tasks, so three twigs: III. Hold up three fingers!  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that the Roman numeral III is three twigs._

**Obelix**: He was nice too.  

## A8-5. The Isle of Pleasure

- `a08-05-s1`: Row across the lake to the island. · _Take an oar. Pull together with Obelix._
- `a08-05-s2`: Get everyone back to the boat. · _Find your Nubbin and the dog first._

#### `sprout:a08-05-boat` · [main](main.md#a08-05-boat)

_Task four is an island in a blue lake._  
_Soft music floats over the water. It smells of honey cakes._  
**Caius Pupus**: Everyone who goes there wants to stay. That is the hard part.  
_You row with one oar. Obelix rows with the other._  
_Splash, splash! The boat goes sideways, mostly._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): some things float and some sink  
> _The boat floats on the lake. A big stone dropped in the water would…?_  
> - ✅ Sink → Yes! A stone sinks. A boat is shaped to float.
> - Float → Not a stone: stones sink. Boats are shaped so they float.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about floating and sinking: boats float, stones sink._

_The dog stands at the front like a little figurehead._  

#### `sprout:a08-05-boat-done` · [main](main.md#a08-05-boat-done)

_Bump! The boat stops on white sand._  
_There are cushions on the beach. Lots of them._  
_You hear a drip and a splash in the water._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, do): Latin word: aqua means water  
> _(Splash your hands and say it: AH-kwa!)_  
> **Caius Pupus**: Aqua! That is the Latin word for water. We use it in English too.  
> Check: `edu:say:latin-word:aqua`
> _For grown-ups: Learned the Latin word “aqua”, which means water._


#### `sprout:a08-05-isle` · [main](main.md#a08-05-isle)

_Kind women bring plates of cakes and cups of juice._  
**The Island’s Hostess**: Sit down. Rest. You never have to do anything again.  
_Asterix sits on a cushion. His legs did it by themselves._  
**Asterix**: Only for a moment.  
_Your Nubbin and the dog roll in the clover, sleepy._  
**Obelix**: Excuse me. Is there any boar?  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, ask): resting is good, and so is doing things  
> _Resting is lovely. But what else do we need besides resting?_  
> - ✅ Playing, helping and doing things → Yes! We need rest, and play, and helping. A bit of each.
> - Only resting, for ever → Resting is good. But staying on a cushion for ever means missing the fun.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about balance: rest is good, and so are playing, helping and doing things._

**Obelix**: No boar? Then I am leaving.  

**Choices**

- **Go and fetch the Nubbin and the dog.**
    - You lift them out of the clover. They are warm and floppy.
- **Have just one cake first.**
    - It is so yummy that you sit down. Then you get up again, quickly.

#### `sprout:a08-05-isle-done` · [main](main.md#a08-05-isle-done)

_Obelix picks up Asterix like a sack of flour._  
_He carries him back to the boat._  
**Obelix**: Sorry. Lovely island. No boar.  
_Far behind, the soft music plays on. It is very quiet._  

> **Lesson** (music, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): soft and loud  
> _(Hum very softly, like the island music. Now hum a bit louder.)_  
> _Soft and loud. Humming is fun._  
> Check: `edu:hum:soft-loud:lullaby`
> _For grown-ups: Hummed softly and then louder, noticing the difference._


#### `sprout:a08-05-complete` · [main](main.md#a08-05-complete)

_Halfway home, Asterix shakes his head._  
**Asterix**: I was going to stay. I really was.  
**Obelix**: You were not. There was no boar.  
_You make a mark on the tablet: IV._  
_Dogmatix drops a fourth twig. Pupus writes IV instead._  
_Dogmatix looks betrayed._  
_Four tasks done. Only eight to go!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, notice): IV is four  
> **Caius Pupus**: A twig before V takes one away. Five, take away one, is four!  
> **Caius Pupus**: Hold up four fingers!  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that the Roman numeral IV means four: one less than five._
