# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A9: The Comet

## A9-1. Strange Signs

- `a09-01-s1`: Meet Moomintroll and Sniff in the green valley. · _Touch the blue house. Then walk to the garden gate._
- `a09-01-s2`: Follow Sniff’s secret path down to the sea. · _The path is behind the woodshed. It goes through the bushes._
- `a09-01-s3`: Ask the Muskrat in the hammock about the grey dust. · _He is between the two birch trees. He is not getting up._

#### `sprout:a09-01-valley` · [main](main.md#a09-01-valley)

_Nova’s atlas opens on a green valley by the sea._  
_A tall blue house stands in it, round like a stove._  
_It is morning, but a grey one. Grey dust is on everything._  
_A round white friend stands in the garden, frowning at the sky._  
**Moomintroll**: Hello! I am Moomintroll. Are you a visitor?  
_A smaller friend peeks out from behind the water butt._  
**Sniff**: I am Sniff. Is your Nubbin valuable? It would fit my pocket.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): the first sound of a name  
> _Say it slowly: Sss-niff. What sound does Sniff start with?_  
> - ✅ Sss → Yes! Sss, sss, Sniff. The snake sound.
> - Mmm → Mmm is Moomintroll. Sniff starts with sss. Say it: Sss-niff!
> _For grown-ups: Listened for the first sound of a name (Sniff starts with s)._


**Choices**

- **Let your Nubbin try Sniff’s pocket.**
    - Your Nubbin climbs into the pocket. It turns round twice.
    - **Sniff**: It fits! Exactly!
- **Keep your Nubbin in your arms for now.**
    - **Sniff**: Suit yourself. It is a very good pocket.
    - Your Nubbin wriggles down and climbs in anyway.

#### `sprout:a09-01-valley-done` · [main](main.md#a09-01-valley-done)

_Moomintroll holds out a paw. It is soft and sticky with jam._  
**Moomintroll**: Come and see the sea. Something is wrong with it.  
_This story is from Finland, a country in the far north._  
_There is snow in winter, and lakes, and a lot of trees._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, notice): Finland is a real country in the north of the world  
> _The Moomin books come from Finland. Tove Jansson wrote them._  
> _Finland has two languages: Finnish and Swedish._  
> _(Find Finland on a globe, if you have one.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that the Moomin stories come from Finland, a real country in the north with two languages, Finnish and Swedish._


#### `sprout:a09-01-shore` · [main](main.md#a09-01-shore)

**Sniff**: This is my secret path. Only I know it.  
**Moomintroll**: I know it too.  
**Sniff**: Only me and you, then. And now them. It is getting crowded.  
_The path comes out on a stony beach. Five big stones sit there._  
_The sea looks like an old grey spoon._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count five stones  
> _Five big stones by the water._  
> _(Point and count them together: one, two, three, four, five.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:stones:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five objects, touching each one once._

**Moomintroll**: It was blue yesterday. It is always blue.  
_Under a rock there is a cave. Inside, the sand is soft and dry._  

#### `sprout:a09-01-shore-done` · [main](main.md#a09-01-shore-done)

_You sit in the cave mouth and watch the grey sea._  
**Moomintroll**: Mamma would like this cave. It feels cosy and safe.  
_Sniff sits close, very close, and says nothing._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, notice): a safe place helps us feel calm  
> _Moomintroll feels safe in the cave._  
> _What makes a place feel safe to you? A blanket? A hug?_  
> _(Wait for an answer.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Talked about what makes a place feel safe and calm._


#### `sprout:a09-01-hammock` · [main](main.md#a09-01-hammock)

_A Muskrat lies in a hammock between two birch trees._  
**Moomintroll**: Muskrat, the sea has gone grey. Do you know why?  
**The Muskrat**: Something is coming. I think a comet.  
**The Muskrat**: A comet is a star with a tail.  
**Sniff**: Is it coming here? I do not like it.  
_Sniff turns the colour of the sea._  
**Moominpappa**: There are professors in the mountains. They will know.  
**Moominpappa**: We need a brave person and a good raft.  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-ESS1-1, ask): we see comets and stars in the night sky  
> _A comet is not really a star, but it shines like one._  
> _When can we see stars and comets best?_  
> - ✅ At night, when it is dark → Yes! The Sun is too bright by day. Night is for stars.
> - At midday in the sun → The Sun is so bright then. We cannot see the stars. Night is better.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that stars and comets are best seen in the dark night sky._


**Choices**

- **“We’ll go.”**
    - **You**: We will go.
    - **Moomintroll**: Yes! All of us. Sniff too.
    - **Sniff**: Sniff too? Who said Sniff too?
- **Ask the Muskrat if he’s ever been wrong.**
    - **The Muskrat**: Once. I thought I would get a quiet afternoon.
    - **Moomintroll**: So he does not know. We will ask the professors.

#### `sprout:a09-01-hammock-done` · [main](main.md#a09-01-hammock-done)

_That night you all stand on the veranda._  
_Low over the mountains is a new light. It has a tail._  
_The tail is faint and red, like a match just blown out._  

> **Lesson** (art, ask): noticing colours  
> _The sea is grey. The grass is grey. What colour is the comet’s tail?_  
> - ✅ Red → Yes, a faint red. The only bit of colour left!
> - Grey → Nearly! Everything else is grey. The tail is red.
> _For grown-ups: Noticed and named a colour in a picture._


#### `sprout:a09-01-complete` · [main](main.md#a09-01-complete)

**Moominmamma**: If you are going, go properly. Pullovers. Sandwiches.  
**Moominmamma**: And come home when you have found out.  
**Moomintroll**: We will come home, Mamma.  
_Sniff pats his pocket. Your Nubbin pats back._  

> **Lesson** (science, notice): a comet is a ball of ice and dust, with a glowing tail  
> _A comet is a big ball of ice and dust, far out in space._  
> _Near the Sun, it warms up. Gas and dust stream off it._  
> _The Sun lights them up. That shining stream is its tail._  
> _The tail always points away from the Sun, like hair blown by a wind._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that a comet is a ball of ice and dust, and its glowing tail points away from the Sun._


**Notes:** Lessons: first sounds, counting to five, Finland on the map, a safe place, night sky, colours, what a comet is.

## A9-2. The Raft

- `a09-02-s1`: Help Moominpappa build a raft. · _Carry the planks from the woodshed to the river bank._
- `a09-02-s2`: Pack the raft and push off. · _The basket goes in the middle. Sniff will argue about his things._
- `a09-02-s3`: Float down the river until it gets dark. · _Keep to the middle of the river. Watch the banks._

#### `sprout:a09-02-build` · [main](main.md#a09-02-build)

_There are five planks on the river bank._  
**Moominpappa**: A raft must be sound. It needs a mast. And a name.  
**Moomintroll**: Does it need a name?  
**Moominpappa**: Everything that goes places needs a name.  
_You carry planks and Moomintroll ties knots. Sniff gives advice._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count five planks  
> _Carry the planks, one at a time._  
> _(Count them together: one, two, three, four, five.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:planks:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five planks while carrying them._

**Sniff**: That plank is crooked. That one is too short.  
_By noon there is a raft, and it floats!_  
**Moominpappa**: What shall we call her?  

**Choices**

- **Suggest calling it after Moominmamma.**
    - **Moominpappa**: Then she will be sure to bring you home.
    - He paints the name on the back, in blue.
- **Suggest “The Valuable”.**
    - **Sniff**: Oh! That is the best name!
    - Sniff stands next to it for a long time.

#### `sprout:a09-02-build-done` · [main](main.md#a09-02-build-done)

_Moominmamma comes down the bank with a big basket._  
**Moominmamma**: Sandwiches on top. Pullovers underneath.  
_Then she looks at the raft, and the river, and the planks._  

> **Lesson** (science, 2-PS1-1, ask): wood floats, stone sinks  
> _The raft is made of wood._  
> _Which would float on the river: a plank or a stone?_  
> - ✅ A plank → Yes! Wood floats. That is why the raft is wood.
> - A stone → Plop! A stone sinks. Wood floats, so the raft is wood.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that wood floats and stone sinks._


#### `sprout:a09-02-launch` · [main](main.md#a09-02-launch)

_Sniff arrives with a big sack of things._  
_There is a tin box, a broken compass, red glass and buttons._  
**Moomintroll**: Sniff, there is not room for all that.  
**Sniff**: These are valuable. They are all valuable!  
**Moominmamma**: Things are lovely, dear. But they do not row.  
_Your Nubbin looks at the sack. Then it looks at you._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Eco.1.K-2, ask): when there is not enough room, we choose  
> _The raft is small. Not everything fits. What can Sniff do?_  
> - ✅ Choose the thing he loves most → Yes! When we cannot have it all, we choose what matters most.
> - Take everything anyway → The raft would sink low and spin! It is wiser to choose.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about choosing when there is not enough room for everything._


**Choices**

- **Help Sniff choose the one thing he’d miss most.**
    - It takes a long time. He picks the red glass.
    - **Sniff**: The rest can stay with Moominmamma.
- **Find room for all of it.**
    - The raft sits low and turns in a slow circle.
    - Sniff quietly takes the buttons back to Mamma.

#### `sprout:a09-02-launch-done` · [main](main.md#a09-02-launch-done)

_Moominpappa pushes the raft off with his foot._  
_Moominmamma waves her apron until the bend._  
_Sniff’s compass is broken. It points to nothing._  

> **Lesson** (science, notice): a working compass always points north  
> _A real compass has a little needle._  
> _It always points the same way, to the north._  
> _That helps travellers find their way._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that a compass needle points north and helps people find their way._


#### `sprout:a09-02-river` · [main](main.md#a09-02-river)

_The river carries you past woods and meadows._  
_Three birds fly low and quiet, all one way._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count three birds  
> _Look up! Three birds. (Count them on your fingers: one, two, three.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:birds:3`
> _For grown-ups: Counted three birds, matching one number to each bird._

**Moomintroll**: Where are they all going?  
**Sniff**: Away. Which is sensible. We are going toward it.  
**Moomintroll**: We are going toward finding out. That is different.  
**Sniff**: It does not feel different. It feels the same.  
_The river splits round a rock. One way is quick, and one is slow._  

**Choices**

- **Take the quick channel.**
    - Whoosh! The raft spins and splashes, and everyone laughs.
    - **Sniff**: I was not frightened. My pocket was.
- **Take the slow channel.**
    - You drift under green trees. A heron watches.
    - **Moomintroll**: This is nice. You could almost forget.

#### `sprout:a09-02-river-done` · [main](main.md#a09-02-river-done)

_At dusk you pull in to a sandy bank._  
_Moomintroll unpacks the sandwiches._  
_Far up the river, a thin line of smoke rises._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-ESS1-1, notice): dusk is the time between day and night  
> _Dusk is when the Sun goes down and the light gets dim._  
> _It comes after day and before night._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that dusk comes between day and night._


#### `sprout:a09-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a09-02-complete)

_Sniff eats his sandwich very slowly._  
**Sniff**: When I was small, I thought the sky was a lid.  
**Moomintroll**: And now?  
**Sniff**: Now it is just sky. And that is scarier.  
_Your Nubbin climbs on his knee._  
_It is as near to a lid as it can get._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): saying a feeling out loud  
> _Sniff said he feels scared._  
> _Saying a scared feeling out loud makes it smaller._  
> _Moomintroll listened, and that helped._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that saying a scared feeling out loud, to a listener, helps it feel smaller._


**Notes:** Lessons: counting, floating, choosing, compass, dusk, saying a feeling out loud.

## A9-3. Snufkin

- `a09-03-s1`: Follow the smoke and the music upriver. · _Listen for a tune. It goes up like a question._
- `a09-03-s2`: Sit by Snufkin’s fire. · _Sit on the log nearest the flames._
- `a09-03-s3`: Look at the comet with Snufkin. · _Walk down to the water, where the trees open up._

#### `sprout:a09-03-camp` · [main](main.md#a09-03-camp)

_You follow the smoke along the bank._  
_Before you see the fire, you hear a tune._  
_It is a harmonica. The tune goes up like a question._  

> **Lesson** (music, ELOF: Language and Communication, do): a tune can go up and down  
> _Let’s hum Snufkin’s tune._  
> _Up, up, up, like a question._  
> _Down, down, down, like an answer._  
> Check: `edu:hum:tune:up-and-down`
> _For grown-ups: Hummed a tune that goes up and then down, noticing the pitch._

_A small green tent stands by the water._  
_A fisherman in a green hat sits on a stone._  
**Snufkin**: Come and sit, if you are coming. The fish have heard you.  
**Snufkin**: They only pretend to be scared. Then they do not get caught.  

#### `sprout:a09-03-camp-done` · [main](main.md#a09-03-camp-done)

**Snufkin**: I am Snufkin. There is coffee, from the one cup.  
_He passes the cup round. It goes round twice._  
_Nobody minds. Everybody waits for a turn._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): taking turns  
> _There is only one cup, and lots of friends. What do they do?_  
> - ✅ Take turns → Yes! One by one. Everybody gets a go.
> - Grab it first → Then someone would be left out. Taking turns is fairer.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about taking turns when there is only one of something._


#### `sprout:a09-03-fire` · [main](main.md#a09-03-fire)

**Sniff**: Where is all your stuff?  
**Snufkin**: This is all of it: tent, pipe, cup, harmonica, hat.  
**Sniff**: That is hardly anything! What if you want something?  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count five things  
> _Snufkin’s things: tent, pipe, cup, harmonica, hat. (Count them on your fingers. How many?)_  
> Check: `edu:count:snufkin-things:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted the five things in Snufkin’s list._

**Snufkin**: I look at it. Then I leave it. It is still beautiful.  
**Snufkin**: You do not own a friend. You walk beside them.  

**Choices**

- **Ask where he’s going.**
    - **Snufkin**: South, in the end. But I am in no hurry.
    - He looks at the tail in the sky.
- **Ask if he ever gets lonely.**
    - **Snufkin**: Sometimes. Then I find a friend. Both are good.
    - **Moomintroll**: You could come with us.
    - **Snufkin**: I could.

#### `sprout:a09-03-fire-done` · [main](main.md#a09-03-fire-done)

_Snufkin plays again, softly._  
_The tune goes round the fire and over the water._  
_Your Nubbin sits by his boot to listen._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-PS4-1, do): sound is a wiggle, a vibration  
> _Put your fingers on your throat and hum._  
> _(Feel the tickle? That wiggle is a vibration.)_  
> _Sound is made of wiggles._  
> Check: `edu:feel:throat:hum`
> _For grown-ups: Felt a vibration in the throat while humming: sound is a wiggle._


#### `sprout:a09-03-sky` · [main](main.md#a09-03-sky)

_By the water, the trees open up._  
_The comet is brighter than last night. Its tail is longer._  
**Snufkin**: It looks lonely. All that way, on its own.  
**Moomintroll**: Are you frightened of it?  
**Snufkin**: A bit. Being frightened is all right.  
**Snufkin**: It means you have noticed something.  
**Sniff**: I have noticed everything.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): feeling frightened is okay  
> _Snufkin says being a bit frightened is all right._  
> _Is it okay to feel frightened?_  
> - ✅ Yes, all feelings are okay → Yes. Feelings are never naughty. What helps is saying them.
> - No, we must hide it → No need to hide it. Snufkin says it is all right.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about how every feeling, including being frightened, is okay._


#### `sprout:a09-03-sky-done` · [main](main.md#a09-03-sky-done)

**Snufkin**: I will come with you to the mountains.  
_He finds a second harmonica in his tent. It is dented._  
**Snufkin**: I have two. I do not like having two. I like one.  
_He holds it out to you._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.1, ask): take away one from two  
> _Snufkin has two harmonicas. He gives one away._  
> _How many harmonicas does he have now?_  
> - ✅ One → Yes! Two take away one is one. Just how he likes it.
> - Two → He gave one away! (Hold up two fingers, then fold one down.) Now there is one.
> _For grown-ups: Took one away from two to find that one is left._


#### `sprout:a09-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a09-03-complete)

_You try a note. It sounds like a duck in a cupboard._  
**Snufkin**: That is the right note. It is just in the wrong place.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ask): animal sounds  
> _Your note sounded like a duck. What sound does a duck make?_  
> - ✅ Quack! → Quack, quack! Say it with me. You are very good at duck sounds.
> - Moo! → That is a cow. A duck says quack, quack!
> _For grown-ups: Matched an animal to the sound it makes._

_Your Nubbin claps. Sniff claps a beat later._  
**Snufkin**: Play it when you are home. Then the tune will know where it lives.  

**Notes:** Lessons: humming a tune, taking turns, counting five, sound as a vibration, feelings, take-away, animal sounds.

## A9-4. The Observatory

- `a09-04-s1`: Climb the Lonely Mountains to the Observatory. · _Follow the little stone piles. Snufkin ties a rope on the steep bit._
- `a09-04-s2`: Ask the professors when the comet will come. · _They are by the huge telescope. You may need to ask twice._
- `a09-04-s3`: Sit with Sniff on the Observatory step. · _He is on the step outside, holding his pocket._

#### `sprout:a09-04-climb` · [main](main.md#a09-04-climb)

_The Lonely Mountains are grey stone and grey sky._  
_The path zigzags up. Snufkin goes first, and Sniff goes last._  
**Sniff**: My legs are the shortest. So the mountain is taller for me.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): compare lengths  
> _Sniff’s legs are short. Whose legs are longer, Sniff’s or Snufkin’s?_  
> - ✅ Snufkin’s → Yes! Snufkin is taller, so his legs are longer. Sniff’s legs are shorter.
> - Sniff’s → Look again! Snufkin is taller. His legs are longer, and Sniff’s are shorter.
> _For grown-ups: Compared two lengths with the words longer and shorter._

**Snufkin**: Then you will have climbed the most.  
**Sniff**: I had not thought of that.  
_A steep slab needs a rope. Snufkin ties it to a rock._  
_At the top is a round white building with a slot in its roof._  

**Choices**

- **Give Sniff a hand up the slab.**
    - Sniff takes your hand and holds on hard.
    - **Sniff**: I could have done it. I just did not want to.
- **Wait at the top and let Sniff do it himself.**
    - It takes him a while. He gets there!
    - **Sniff**: Did you see? Did everybody see?

#### `sprout:a09-04-climb-done` · [main](main.md#a09-04-climb-done)

_The Observatory door is open._  
_Inside, someone is counting in a whisper._  
_A very long telescope points at the sky._  

> **Lesson** (science, notice): a telescope makes faraway things look close  
> _A telescope is a tube with glass in it._  
> _It makes faraway things, like stars, look big and close._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that a telescope makes faraway things look closer._


#### `sprout:a09-04-telescope` · [main](main.md#a09-04-telescope)

_Two professors stand under the telescope, writing._  
**Moomintroll**: When will the comet come? And where?  
**The Professor**: On the seventh of October, in the evening.  
**The Professor**: At eight forty-two. That is nearly nine o’clock.  
**The Other Professor**: Possibly eight forty-three. We are arguing about that.  
_(Point to a clock, if you have one. Where is nine?)_  
**The Professor**: It will land about here. How interesting!  

> **Lesson** (maths, 1.MD.B.3, notice): a clock tells the time; eight forty-two is nearly nine  
> _A clock tells us the time._  
> _The short hand shows the hour._  
> _At eight forty-two, the short hand is nearly at nine._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that a clock tells the time, and met “nearly nine o’clock”._


#### `sprout:a09-04-telescope-done` · [main](main.md#a09-04-telescope-done)

_Nobody says a word._  
**Moomintroll**: Mamma does not know the date.  
**Snufkin**: Then we had better tell her.  
_Moomintroll is already on his way._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.2.3, ask): what a character does after big news  
> _Moomintroll hears big news. What does he do next?_  
> - ✅ He hurries home to tell Mamma → Yes! He thinks of Mamma first. Telling her helps her get ready.
> - He keeps it a secret → No, he is already on his way. He wants Mamma to know.
> _For grown-ups: Told what a character does after big news, and why._


#### `sprout:a09-04-step` · [main](main.md#a09-04-step)

_The light is going. Moomintroll hops, ready to leave._  
_Sniff sits on the step, holding his pocket tight._  
_You sit down beside him. The comet hangs over the peaks._  
**Sniff**: The seventh is in four days. I counted.  
**Sniff**: Will it hit? Tell me. I am brave once I know.  
_He looks at you._  
_He is a little bit brave, and a little bit scared._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): a worry gets smaller when you say it out loud  
> _Sniff said his worry out loud. That is brave._  
> _A worry feels smaller when it is shared with a friend._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that naming a worry and sharing it makes it feel smaller._


**Choices**

- **“I don’t know.”**
    - **You**: I do not know, Sniff.
    - He nods slowly. It is a true answer.
    - **Sniff**: No. Nobody knows. Not even them.
- **“We’ll be home by then.”**
    - **You**: We will be home by then.
    - Sniff tucks the words away, like a button.
    - **Sniff**: Home by then. All right.

#### `sprout:a09-04-step-done` · [main](main.md#a09-04-step-done)

_Sniff stands up._  
_Then he holds out his paw to help you up._  
_It is the first thing he has ever given anybody._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.2.3, ask): how a character feels from what they do  
> _Sniff helps you up. How do you think Sniff feels?_  
> - ✅ Kind and caring → Yes! He is being kind. Helping is a way of caring.
> - Cross → Not this time. He holds out a paw to help. That is what a kind friend does.
> _For grown-ups: Worked out a character’s feeling from what he did._


#### `sprout:a09-04-complete` · [main](main.md#a09-04-complete)

_You go down the mountain by the comet’s light._  
**Snufkin**: Four days. That is enough to get home.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count four days  
> _Four days to get home._  
> _(Hold up your fingers: one day, two days, three days, four days.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:days:4`
> _For grown-ups: Counted four days on their fingers._

**Sniff**: I always stop to look at things.  
**Snufkin**: So do I. We will look quickly.  

**Notes:** Lessons: longer and shorter, a telescope, the clock, what a character does next, saying a worry out loud, reading feelings, counting days.

## A9-5. The Snork Maiden

- `a09-05-s1`: Look into the glinting crack in the rocks. · _It is beside the path, just below the rope._
- `a09-05-s2`: Run to help whoever is calling in the valley. · _Follow the voice into the trees. Stay behind Moomintroll._
- `a09-05-s3`: See the gold ring find its owner. · _Sit with everyone while she catches her breath._

#### `sprout:a09-05-crevice` · [main](main.md#a09-05-crevice)

_On the way down, something glints in a crack in the rock._  
_Moomintroll reaches in. He pulls out a small gold ring._  
**Moomintroll**: What is it for? It is too small for a crown.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): the first sound of a word
