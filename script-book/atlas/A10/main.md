### Step 1: Step onto the island and find the voice telling a story.

Hint: Touch the page, then follow the children’s laughter to the open house. · check: `explore:a10-motunui:beach`

#### <a id="a10-01-page"></a>Scene `a10-01-page`

_Nova’s atlas falls open at a page washed in sea-blue. There is an island in the middle of it, very green, ringed with white._  
_The paint goes warm under your hand, then wet. Then it is sand, and you and your Nubbin are standing in it._  
_Behind the palms there is a house with no walls, only a roof on posts. A crowd of small children sits inside it._  
_An old woman is telling them a story with her hands. A painted cloth hangs behind her: an island shaped like a sleeping woman._  
**Gramma Tala** _(soft)_: Before the palms, before the fish, there was the sea and nothing else. Then the mother island came up out of it.  
**Gramma Tala** _(happy)_: Her heart could make things grow. She gave that away to every island she could reach. Green, and more green, and more.  
_She spots you at the edge of the shade, and doesn’t miss a beat. She just shuffles up to make room._  
**Gramma Tala**: Sit, sit. Somebody from past the reef. We don’t get many. Your little one can sit on the end.  
_Your Nubbin sits on the end, beside a toddler with a great deal of hair. The toddler pats it, firmly, like a drum._  

#### <a id="a10-01-page-done"></a>Done `a10-01-page-done`

**Gramma Tala** _(think)_: Now. Where was I? The heart. Everyone wanted it. And one of them came to take it.  

### Step 2: Hear the rest of the story, the frightening bit included.

Hint: Stay in the shade with the children until the story is done. · check: `sit:a10-tala:story-house`

#### <a id="a10-01-story"></a>Scene `a10-01-story`

**Gramma Tala**: A trickster. A shapeshifter, with a great fish hook the gods gave him. Maui.  
**Gramma Tala**: He took the heart, and the mother island began to crumble. As he fled, a demon of earth and fire rose up after him.  
_Most of the children squeal and hide behind each other. The toddler leans forward, delighted._  
**Gramma Tala**: Maui was struck from the sky, and the heart and his hook fell into the sea. Nobody has found them since.  
**Gramma Tala** _(sad)_: And the darkness is still spreading, island by island. One day it will reach us.  
_A tall man ducks in under the roof, with the face of a father who has heard this story before._  
**Chief Tui**: Mother. That’s enough. They won’t sleep for a week.  
**Chief Tui**: There is no darkness, and nobody here needs to cross the reef. We have fish, we have coconuts, we have each other. That’s plenty.

**Gramma Tala** _(soft)_: He’s my son, and he’s a good chief. He’s also wrong. Both at once. You’ll find that’s quite common.

## A10-2. The Reef

*Moana (2016): the blight on Motunui, and Moana’s first try past the reef* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Heart of the Island page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A10-1, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A10-1

### Step 1: Walk the village with the chief’s daughter, and see the coconut trees.

Hint: Meet Moana by the chief’s house and go up to the grove. · check: `inspect:a10-coconuts:grove`

#### <a id="a10-02-coconuts"></a>Scene `a10-02-coconuts`

_Moana is sixteen now, and is being taught to be chief, which mostly means people bringing her problems._  
**Moana** _(happy)_: You’re Gramma’s guest! She said you’d come back. She says that about everyone, but she was right this time.  
**Moana**: Come on. The coconut farmers want me. They always want me before breakfast.  
**Coconut farmer**: Look at this. Cracked one open this morning.  
_He holds out a coconut split in half. Inside, where it should be white, it is black and soft and smells of nothing good._  
**Coconut farmer**: Whole trees of them. Not just old trees. Young ones too.  
**Moana** _(think)_: Then we clear those trees and plant a new grove over there. It’s good ground. We just haven’t used it.  
_The farmer nods slowly, which on Motunui means yes, and also means we’ll see._  

**Choices**

- **Offer to dig the first hole for the new grove.**
    - **Coconut farmer**: A guest who digs. Now I’ve seen everything.
    - You dig. Your Nubbin helps by sitting in the hole, to check it is the right size.
- **Ask how long the trees have been sick.**
    - **Coconut farmer**: Since the rains. It started on the far side and walked this way, a tree at a time.
    - Moana looks towards the far side of the island, and then past it, at the sea. She doesn’t say anything.

#### <a id="a10-02-coconuts-done"></a>Done `a10-02-coconuts-done`

**Moana**: That’s one. Next it’ll be the roofs, or the fishing. It’s always the fishing.  

### Step 2: Go down to the lagoon, where the nets have come in empty.

Hint: Find the fishermen at the boat shelter. · check: `talk:a10-fisher:lagoon`

#### <a id="a10-02-lagoon"></a>Scene `a10-02-lagoon`

_The fishing canoes are drawn up on the sand, their nets draped over them, dry. Nothing in them but weed._  
**Old fisherman** _(sad)_: Every day, fewer. Today, none. The lagoon’s been fished out, or the fish have gone somewhere.  
**Moana**: Different nets? The far side of the island?  
**Old fisherman**: Tried both. Nothing.  
_Moana looks past the lagoon, to where the waves break white on the reef. Past that, the sea is a darker blue._  
**Moana** _(think)_: Then we go out past the reef. The fish must be out there somewhere.  
_The fishermen go very quiet. One of them looks over your shoulder, where the chief has just walked up._  
**Chief Tui**: We don’t cross the reef.  
**Chief Tui**: We have one rule, and it keeps us safe. You know it. You’ll be chief. You’ll keep it too.  
_Moana opens her mouth, and closes it again. Your Nubbin, sensing a mood, sits on her foot._  

#### <a id="a10-02-lagoon-done"></a>Done `a10-02-lagoon-done`

_The chief walks off. Moana watches the reef for a long time, chewing the inside of her cheek._  
**Moana** _(angry)_: He didn’t say why. He never says why.  

### Step 3: Go out to the breakers with Moana, and bring her back in.

Hint: Wade out to the reef edge and be ready to pull. · check: `swim:a10-reef:breakers`

#### <a id="a10-02-breakers"></a>Scene `a10-02-breakers`

_An hour later, Moana is pushing a small canoe off the sand, with a spotted pig sitting in the front of it._  
**Moana**: That’s Pua. He’s coming for luck. Don’t tell anyone. I’m only going to look.  
_She paddles well in the lagoon. At the reef, the waves come in bigger and closer, one after another, like a crowd at a door._  
_The third wave lifts the canoe, turns it over, and throws it back towards the beach like a leaf._  
**You** _(shock)_: Moana!  
_You wade in up to your chest. Your Nubbin paddles past you, much faster, its tiny wings going like a fan._  
_Between you, you get hold of her arm, and then Pua, who is very much not enjoying this._  

#### <a id="a10-02-breakers-done"></a>Done `a10-02-breakers-done`

_Moana sits on the sand with a cut on her foot and the canoe in two pieces beside her. Pua stands well up the beach, sulking._  
**Moana** _(sad)_: Fine. Fine. Point taken.  
_Your Nubbin comes out of the shallows last. In its mouth is a small white shell with a spiral on it, like a tiny staircase._  

### Complete

#### <a id="a10-02-complete"></a>Complete `a10-02-complete`

_She looks at the cut on her foot, and not at the reef. It takes some effort._  
_Your Nubbin drops the spiral shell in your hand, very pleased with itself. You put it somewhere safe._

## A10-3. Gramma Tala

*Moana (2016): Gramma Tala dances with the waves* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Heart of the Island page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A10-2, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A10-2

### Step 1: Find Gramma Tala dancing at the water’s edge.

Hint: Walk round the point, to where the waves come in long and low. · check: `visit:a10-tala:point`

#### <a id="a10-03-point"></a>Scene `a10-03-point`

_Round the point, the waves come in long and slow. Gramma Tala is dancing in the edge of them, in a skirt that is soaked to the knee._  
_She moves the way the water moves. Forward when it comes in, back when it goes, as if the two of them had rehearsed._  
_On her back, from shoulder to shoulder, is a tattoo of a manta ray. It is old, and the lines have softened, and it is beautiful._  
**Gramma Tala**: Don’t stand there like a post. The sea doesn’t bite. Not today.  
**Moana**: Gramma, you shouldn’t be out here on your own.  
**Gramma Tala** _(happy)_: I’m not on my own. I’ve got the sea, and a guest, and a round little thing with wings. That’s a crowd.  
**Gramma Tala**: Mantas are my favourites. When I’m finished here, that’s what I’ll be. I’ve told everyone. They don’t have to go to meetings.  
**Moana**: Gramma!  

#### <a id="a10-03-point-done"></a>Done `a10-03-point-done`

_Your Nubbin has crept down into the foam and is trying to copy her. It mostly falls over, very sincerely._  
**Gramma Tala** _(happy)_: Oh, now. That one has the makings.  

### Step 2: Let Gramma Tala teach your Nubbin one step.

Hint: Stand in the shallows with your Nubbin and choose a step. · check: `choose:a10-step:point`

#### <a id="a10-03-step"></a>Scene `a10-03-step`

**Gramma Tala**: Come here, small one. Feet in the water. Feel it pull? That’s the sea saying hello.  
_Your Nubbin stands in the foam, rocking a little each time it goes out._  
**Gramma Tala**: I’ve got two steps for a beginner. One for the manta, one for the wave. You only get one today. I’m old, my knees are older.  
**Gramma Tala** _(think)_: Which shall it be?  

**Choices**

- **The stingray glide.** — sets `{"a10-step":"glide"}`
    - **Gramma Tala**: Arms out wide, and soft. Lean, and let the sea carry you sideways. Don’t flap. Mantas never flap. They fly in the water.
    - Your Nubbin spreads its arms, leans, and slides along the foam on one foot, perfectly level, for very nearly a second.
- **The wave step.** — sets `{"a10-step":"wave"}`
    - **Gramma Tala**: Up on your toes when it comes in. Down and back when it goes. Roll your arms like the water rolls over itself.
    - Your Nubbin rises, sinks, rises, rolling its little arms, a beat behind the sea and then, suddenly, right on it.

#### <a id="a10-03-step-done"></a>Done `a10-03-step-done`

**Gramma Tala** _(soft)_: There. That’s yours now. A step’s like a name. Once somebody’s taught it to you, it doesn’t come off.  

### Step 3: Sit on the sand with Gramma and Moana while the tide turns.

#### <a id="a10-03-sand"></a>Scene `a10-03-sand`

_Gramma sits down on the dry sand with a noise like a sack of shells. Moana sits beside her, and you on the other side._  
**Moana**: I tried to go past the reef this morning. I nearly drowned Pua.  
**Gramma Tala** _(happy)_: I know. I saw. You went out on the wrong side, in the wrong boat, at the wrong tide. Otherwise, very good.  
**Moana**: Dad says the reef keeps us safe.  
**Gramma Tala** _(soft)_: It does. Safe is a good thing. It isn’t the only thing.  
**Gramma Tala**: Your father once had a reason for his rule. You should ask him for it, one day. Gently.  
_She looks at Moana for a long moment, as if she is weighing something in her hand._  
**Gramma Tala** _(think)_: You’ve got a question in you the size of a canoe. You always have. Ask it.  

#### <a id="a10-03-sand-done"></a>Done `a10-03-sand-done`

**Moana** _(think)_: Who we are. Where we came from. Why we never leave.  
**Gramma Tala**: Good. Get your guest. Bring a torch. It’s a walk.

**Gramma Tala** _(soft)_: It’ll come. Everything worth having comes slow. Mantas, grandchildren, steps.

## A10-4. The Cave of Canoes

*Moana (2016): the hidden cave and the ancestors’ voyaging canoes* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Heart of the Island page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A10-3, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A10-3

### Step 1: Follow Gramma Tala behind the waterfall.

Hint: Carry the torch up the stream to where the water falls over the rocks. · check: `walk:a10-cave:waterfall`

#### <a id="a10-04-falls"></a>Scene `a10-04-falls`

_Gramma leads you up a stream to a rock face with a waterfall coming down it, thin and noisy._  
**Gramma Tala**: There. Behind it. I’ve been waiting a long time to show somebody. You first, Moana. Guest second. Round one third.  
_Moana looks at the waterfall, then at Gramma, and steps through. You hear her gasp on the other side._  
_You follow, and the water hits your head like a cold hand. Your Nubbin goes through at a run with its eyes shut._  
_Behind the falls there is a passage in the rock, sloping down, and the sound of the sea somewhere at the bottom of it._  
**Gramma Tala**: Go on, then. Light the way. I’ll be along. My knees take the long way round.  
**Moana**: Gramma, that’s a hundred steps.  
**Gramma Tala**: A hundred and four. I’ve counted them for forty years. They’ve never once been a hundred.  

#### <a id="a10-04-falls-done"></a>Done `a10-04-falls-done`

_The torch makes the wet rock shine. Your Nubbin’s emote ball glows a little too, which is no help but is company._  

### Step 2: Find what is hidden at the bottom of the cave.

Hint: Go down the passage and look at the shapes in the dark. · check: `inspect:a10-cave:canoes`

#### <a id="a10-04-canoes"></a>Scene `a10-04-canoes`

_The passage opens into a cavern as big as the meeting house. A dim blue light comes in from the sea at the far end._  
_Moana lifts the torch, and the shapes in the dark turn into boats._  
_Not fishing canoes. Great voyaging canoes, twin-hulled, with their sails furled and their steering oars laid along the decks._  
_There are dozens of them, under dust and cobweb, and every one is finer than anything on the beach._  
**Moana** _(shock)_: Whose are these? Who built them? We don’t build these. Nobody builds these.  
_Your Nubbin climbs up onto the nearest hull and sits there, very small, under a mast as tall as a tree._  
_At the front of the cave is a great drum. The skin is old, but tight._  

**Choices**

- **Brush the dust off the nearest prow.**
    - Under the dust the wood is carved: waves, stars, and a long line of small figures, each with a hand on the next one’s shoulder.
- **Hold the torch high so Moana can see.**
    - You hold the torch up. Moana walks the length of the cave touching each hull as she passes, the way you’d count heads.

#### <a id="a10-04-canoes-done"></a>Done `a10-04-canoes-done`

**Gramma Tala** _(soft)_: Bang the drum, Moana. Go on. It’s been waiting for you.  

### Step 3: Watch what the drum remembers.

Hint: Stand back while Moana beats the drum. · check: `watch:a10-ancestors:drum`

#### <a id="a10-04-drum"></a>Scene `a10-04-drum`

_Moana beats the drum once. The sound goes all round the cave and comes back bigger._  
_She beats it again, and again, and the torchlight seems to move on the sails, as if a wind were filling them._  
_Then you are not in a cave. You are on the open sea, on the deck of one of those canoes, in full sail under the stars._  
_All round you are more canoes, and on them people steering, singing, reading the sky, laughing at the tops of their voices._  
_A woman at a steering oar looks straight at you and grins, the way you’d grin at someone who has nearly worked out a joke._  
_They find an island, and land, and plant, and build. Then they load the canoes again and sail on to find the next one._  
_Your Nubbin, on the deck beside you, is doing its step without seeming to know it._  

#### <a id="a10-04-drum-done"></a>Done `a10-04-drum-done`

_The drum stops ringing. You are in the cave again, with dust on your feet and the sound of the sea at the far end._  

### Complete

#### <a id="a10-04-complete"></a>Complete `a10-04-complete`

**Moana** _(happy)_: They didn’t stay. They went. Every island, they found it and they left it and went on. We did that.  
**Gramma Tala**: We did. Until the darkness came, and the boats stopped coming home, and the chiefs said: no more. Stay inside the reef.  
**Gramma Tala** _(soft)_: And we hid the boats. And we forgot. All but a few of us, being stubborn.  
_Gramma opens the shell at her neck and takes out the green stone with the spiral on it._  
**Gramma Tala** _(soft)_: The ocean gave you this when you were small. I kept it for you. It’s the heart of Te Fiti, the mother island.  
**Gramma Tala**: The darkness is here, Moana. The coconuts, the fish. Someone has to find Maui and make him put it back.  
**Moana** _(happy)_: I have to tell Dad. Now. Right now.  
_She is out of the cave before Gramma has finished smiling. Your Nubbin runs after her, and then back for you._

## A10-5. Setting Sail

*Moana (2016): the council, Tala’s last night, and Moana leaving Motunui* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Heart of the Island page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A10-4, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A10-4

### Step 1: Stand by Moana while she tells the chief what she found.

Hint: Go to the meeting house, where the elders are gathered. · check: `talk:a10-tui:council`

#### <a id="a10-05-council"></a>Scene `a10-05-council`

_Moana bursts into the meeting house with cobweb in her hair. The elders look up from a very long talk about roof thatch._  
**Moana** _(happy)_: Our people sailed. There are boats in a cave, real boats, and we can find more fish, and Maui, and fix everything.  
**Chief Tui** _(angry)_: The cave. She showed you the cave.  
**Moana**: You knew? You knew, and you never…  
**Chief Tui** _(angry)_: We are not going past the reef. I’ll burn those boats before I see one leave this beach.  
_The meeting house goes very still. Somebody puts down a roof bundle without a sound._  
**Chief Tui** _(sad)_: When I was your age, I went past the reef. My best friend came with me. The sea was rough, and I came home, and he didn’t.  
**Chief Tui** _(sad)_: I won’t lose you to it. I won’t.

### Step 2: Sit with Gramma Tala in her house.

Hint: Go to Gramma’s house and sit by her mat. · check: `sit:a10-tala:bedside`

#### <a id="a10-05-bedside"></a>Scene `a10-05-bedside`

_Gramma lies on her mat with a healer beside her. She is very small all of a sudden, and her hands are cool._  
_Moana kneels by her head, and you sit at the foot. Your Nubbin sits on the mat, carefully, as if it knows._  
**Gramma Tala** _(soft)_: Don’t fuss. I’m old. Old people do this. It’s allowed.  
**Gramma Tala**: You have to go. Tonight. While your father is busy being frightened of the right thing for the wrong reason.  
**Moana** _(sad)_: I can’t leave you. Not now.  
**Gramma Tala** _(soft)_: I’ll be the bit of you that keeps going when you want to stop. That’s not poetry. That’s just how grandmothers work.  
_She presses the shell with the heart in it into Moana’s hand and folds her fingers over it._  
**Gramma Tala** _(happy)_: Find Maui. Take him across the sea. Make him give it back. And mind your guest. Guests are a responsibility.  
_Then she turns her head to your Nubbin, and smiles, and moves one hand on the mat, in the step she taught it._  

#### <a id="a10-05-bedside-done"></a>Done `a10-05-bedside-done`
