# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A10: The Heart of the Island

## A10-1. The Chosen One

- `a10-01-s1`: Step onto the island. Listen to the story. · _Follow the children’s laughter._
- `a10-01-s2`: Hear the rest of the story. · _Sit with the children._
- `a10-01-s3`: Look after the baby turtle. · _Go down to the water._

#### `sprout:a10-01-page` · [main](main.md#a10-01-page)

_Nova’s atlas opens. The page is blue, with a green island._  
_The paint turns to sand. Squish, squish! You are on a beach._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, 2-ESS2-2, notice): a map shows land and water; an island has water all round  
> _Nova’s page is a map. A map shows where land and water are._  
> _Green is land and blue is water. An island has water all round._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that maps show land and water, and what an island is._

_A big house has a roof and no walls. Children sit inside._  
_An old woman tells a story with her hands._  
**Gramma Tala**: Sit, sit! A guest! Your little one can sit on the end.  
_A small girl with lots of hair pats your Nubbin like a drum._  

#### `sprout:a10-01-page-done` · [main](main.md#a10-01-page-done)

**Gramma Tala**: Now, where was I? The heart of the island!  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, ask): name where a story happens  
> _Where is this story? Point to your answer._  
> - ✅ On an island, by the sea → Yes! An island, with sand and palm trees.
> - In a snowy town → Not snow here! Look: sand, sea and palm trees. An island!
> - On the moon → Not the moon! We are on an island by the sea.
> _For grown-ups: Named the setting of the story: an island by the sea._


#### `sprout:a10-01-story` · [main](main.md#a10-01-story)

**Gramma Tala**: Long ago there was only sea. Then an island came up out of it.  
**Gramma Tala**: The island had a heart. It made green things grow.  
**Gramma Tala**: A trickster took the heart. His name was Maui.  
**Gramma Tala**: Maui could turn into a hawk. Then the island began to crumble.  
_Some children squeal and hide behind each other. The small girl leans in._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): naming a scared feeling and what helps  
> _Some children feel scared. What could help them?_  
> - ✅ A cuddle, and a grown-up nearby → Yes. A cuddle helps. Scared feelings get smaller.
> - Hide all alone → Hiding alone feels lonely. A cuddle works better.
> - Pretend not to be scared → It is fine to be scared. Say so, and ask for a hug.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about scared feelings and that a cuddle or a nearby grown-up helps._

**Chief Tui**: Mother, that is enough. The little ones will be scared.  
_Gramma Tala says her son is a good chief. He is also wrong._  

#### `sprout:a10-01-story-done` · [main](main.md#a10-01-story-done)

_Gramma Tala picks up a stick and walks to the shore, humming._  
_You follow her._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.His.2.K-2, notice): oral history: people tell stories out loud to remember the past  
> _Gramma Tala tells her story out loud. She learned it from her gramma._  
> _Long ago, people kept the past alive by telling stories._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that people kept the past alive by telling stories aloud._


#### `sprout:a10-01-turtle` · [main](main.md#a10-01-turtle)

_The small girl has run to the beach. She frowns at something flat._  
_It is a baby turtle. It is trying to reach the sea._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS3-1, ask): where sea turtles live and what they need  
> _The baby turtle wants to get home. Where does a sea turtle live?_  
> - ✅ In the sea → Yes! A sea turtle lives in the sea. It needs the water.
> - Up a tree → Not up a tree! A sea turtle swims, and its home is the sea.
> - In a house → Not in a house! Its home is the big blue sea.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a sea turtle lives in the sea._

_Birds circle above. The girl holds a big leaf over it, like a roof._  

**Choices**

- **Send your Nubbin to walk beside the turtle.**
    - Your Nubbin waddles beside it, arms up. A very slow parade!
- **Wave the birds away.**
    - You flap your arms, and your Nubbin flaps its wings. The birds fly off.

#### `sprout:a10-01-turtle-done` · [main](main.md#a10-01-turtle-done)

_The turtle reaches the foam. Splash! It is gone, and the girl claps._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, notice): helping someone smaller  
> _The little girl helped the turtle. Helping feels good._  
> _Who could you help today?_  
> _For grown-ups: Noticed how good it feels to help someone smaller._


#### `sprout:a10-01-complete` · [main](main.md#a10-01-complete)

_The sea pulls back. It stands up in a wall of water!_  
_Fish hang in it like flecks in glass. The water leans down, gently._  
_It gives her a shell, then another shell. Then a green stone!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 3  
> _(Count the gifts together on your fingers.)_  
> _A shell, a shell, a green stone: one, two, three. Three gifts!_  
> Check: `edu:count:gifts:3`
> _For grown-ups: Counted three gifts from the sea, one finger each._

**Chief Tui**: Moana! Where are you?  
_The water falls back. Gramma Tala picks up the green stone._  
_She keeps it safe in the shell at her neck._  
**Gramma Tala**: The sea picks who it picks. It never says why.  

**Notes:** A10-1. Sets the map, the stories-out-loud idea and the baby turtle.

## A10-2. The Reef

- `a10-02-s1`: Walk with Moana to the coconut trees. · _Go up to the grove._
- `a10-02-s2`: Go to the lagoon. The nets are empty. · _Find the fishermen._
- `a10-02-s3`: Go out to the reef with Moana. · _Wade out and be ready to pull._

#### `sprout:a10-02-coconuts` · [main](main.md#a10-02-coconuts)

_Moana is sixteen now. She is learning to be chief._  
**Moana**: You are Gramma’s guest! I knew you would come back.  
_A farmer holds out a coconut, cracked in half._  
_Inside it is black and soft. It should be white!_  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, notice): plants need water, light and good soil to stay well  
> _Coconut trees need sun, water and good ground to grow._  
> _When a tree gets poorly, its fruit goes soft and dark._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard what plants need to stay healthy._

**Coconut farmer**: Whole trees of them. Young trees too.  
**Moana**: Then we will plant a new grove. There is good ground over there.  

**Choices**

- **Dig the first hole for the new grove.**
    - **Coconut farmer**: A guest who digs!
    - Your Nubbin sits in the hole to check it is the right size.
- **Ask how long the trees have been sick.**
    - **Coconut farmer**: Since the rains. It walked this way, one tree at a time.
    - Moana looks past the island, at the sea.

#### `sprout:a10-02-coconuts-done` · [main](main.md#a10-02-coconuts-done)

**Moana**: That is one trouble. Next will be the fishing. It is always the fishing.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): noticing how someone else feels  
> _The farmer’s trees are poorly. How might he feel?_  
> - ✅ Worried → Yes. He is worried. He cares about his trees.
> - Silly → Not silly. His trees are poorly, so he feels worried.
> - Sleepy → Not sleepy! He is worried about his trees.
> _For grown-ups: Named a feeling (worried) for someone whose trees were poorly._


#### `sprout:a10-02-lagoon` · [main](main.md#a10-02-lagoon)

_The fishing canoes sit on the sand. Their nets are empty._  
**Old fisherman**: Every day, fewer fish. Today, none.  
**Moana**: Then we go out past the reef! The fish are out there.  
_The fishermen go quiet. The chief has walked up._  
**Chief Tui**: We do not cross the reef. It is our rule. It keeps us safe.  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, ask): rules help keep people safe  
> _The chief has a rule. Why might a rule like this help?_  
> - ✅ It keeps people safe → Yes. Rules can keep people safe. Moana will ask why.
> - To be cross → Not to be cross. A good rule helps keep people safe.
> - To spoil the fun → Not to spoil fun. A good rule is there to keep people safe.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about why rules exist: to keep people safe._

_Your Nubbin senses a mood. It sits on Moana’s foot._  

#### `sprout:a10-02-lagoon-done` · [main](main.md#a10-02-lagoon-done)

_The chief walks away. Moana watches the reef._  
_Five canoes lie on the sand, with nothing in them._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 5  
> _(Count the canoes with your finger.)_  
> _One, two, three, four, five. Five empty canoes!_  
> Check: `edu:count:canoes:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five canoes, saying one number for each._

**Moana**: He never says why.  

#### `sprout:a10-02-breakers` · [main](main.md#a10-02-breakers)

_Later, Moana pushes a small canoe out. Pua the pig sits in front._  
**Moana**: I am only going to look!  
_At the reef, the waves come in big and close. Whoosh! Whoosh!_  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS3-1, notice): coral reefs: tiny animals build a reef where fish live  
> _A reef is like a wall in the sea. Tiny coral animals build it._  
> _Lots of fish live in a reef. It is their home._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that tiny coral animals build reefs, and that fish make their homes there._

_The third wave tips the canoe over. Splash!_  
**You**: Moana!  
_You wade in. Your Nubbin paddles past you, fast._  
_Together you pull Moana and Pua to safety._  

#### `sprout:a10-02-breakers-done` · [main](main.md#a10-02-breakers-done)

_Moana sits on the sand, by the canoe in two pieces. Pua sulks._  
**Moana**: Fine. I will not go past the reef. Probably.  
_Your Nubbin comes out of the sea with a spiral shell._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): hear the first sound of a word  
> _Say them slowly: sea, sand, sun. What sound do they all start with?_  
> - ✅ “s”, like a snake → Yes! S-s-sea, s-s-sand, s-s-sun. They all start with “s”.
> - “b”, like a ball → Listen again: sss-sea, sss-sand. They start with “s”.
> - “m”, like Moana → M starts Moana. Sea, sand and sun start with “s”.
> _For grown-ups: Listened for the first sound, “s”, in sea, sand and sun._


#### `sprout:a10-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a10-02-complete)

**Moana**: Dad is right. I nearly drowned the pig.  
_She looks at the cut on her foot, not at the reef._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): naming feelings and what helps  
> _Moana feels sad and cross with herself. What could help?_  
> - ✅ Talk to Gramma or a friend → Yes. Talking to someone you trust helps.
> - Keep it all inside → Keeping it inside is heavy. Telling someone helps.
> - Be cross at the pig → Pua did nothing wrong! Talking to a friend helps more.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about what helps when you feel sad and cross: telling someone you trust._

_Your Nubbin drops the spiral shell in your hand. You keep it safe._  
**Moana**: That is Gramma, round the point. She dances with the sea.  

**Notes:** A10-2. Coconut health, a rule that keeps people safe, and coral reefs.

## A10-3. Gramma Tala

- `a10-03-s1`: Find Gramma Tala dancing by the water. · _Walk round the point._
- `a10-03-s2`: Let Gramma teach your Nubbin one step. · _Stand in the shallows. Choose a step._
- `a10-03-s3`: Sit on the sand with Gramma and Moana. · _Sit beside Gramma Tala._

#### `sprout:a10-03-point` · [main](main.md#a10-03-point)

_Round the point, the waves come in long and slow._  
_Gramma Tala dances at the edge of the water._  
_She moves like the sea: forward, then back. In, then out._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): waves come in and go out all day  
> _Watch the waves. They come in, and they go out._  
> _In, out. In, out. The sea keeps moving all day._  
> _For grown-ups: Noticed that waves move in and out all the time._

**Gramma Tala**: Do not stand there like a post. The sea does not bite!  
**Moana**: Gramma, you should not be out here alone.  
**Gramma Tala**: I have the sea, a guest and a round little thing with wings.  

#### `sprout:a10-03-point-done` · [main](main.md#a10-03-point-done)

_Your Nubbin tries to copy her. It falls over. Plop!_  
**Gramma Tala**: Oh, that one will be a dancer one day!  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, notice): trying again after a wobble is brave  
> _Your Nubbin fell over and did not mind. It got up to try again._  
> _Trying again is brave. Every wobble helps you learn._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that falling over and trying again is a brave way to learn._


#### `sprout:a10-03-step` · [main](main.md#a10-03-step)

**Gramma Tala**: Come here, small one. Feet in the water. Feel it pull?  
_Your Nubbin rocks a little each time the water goes out._  
**Gramma Tala**: I have two steps for a beginner. You get one today.  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): copy a slow, gentle movement  
> _(Stand up. Sway side to side, slowly, like the sea.)_  
> _Swish, swish. Slow and soft. That is how Gramma moves._  
> Check: `edu:move:sway`
> _For grown-ups: Swayed slowly and gently, copying Gramma Tala’s movement._

**Gramma Tala**: Which shall it be?  

**Choices**

- **The stingray glide.**
    - **Gramma Tala**: Arms out wide and soft. Lean. Let the sea carry you.
    - Your Nubbin slides along the foam on one foot. Whee!
- **The wave step.**
    - **Gramma Tala**: Up on your toes when it comes in. Down when it goes.
    - Your Nubbin rises, sinks and rolls its arms. Right on the sea!

#### `sprout:a10-03-step-done` · [main](main.md#a10-03-step-done)

**Gramma Tala**: There. That is yours now. A step is like a name. It stays.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, do): clap the beats in a word  
> _(Clap the beats in each word.)_  
> _Wave and step: one clap each. Man-ta: two claps!_  
> Check: `edu:clap:syllables:manta`
> _For grown-ups: Clapped syllables in wave, step and manta._


#### `sprout:a10-03-sand` · [main](main.md#a10-03-sand)

_Gramma sits on the sand. Moana sits beside her, and so do you._  
**Moana**: I tried to go past the reef. I nearly drowned Pua.  
**Gramma Tala**: I know. Wrong boat, wrong tide. Otherwise, very good.  
**Gramma Tala**: Safe is good. But safe is not the only thing.  
**Gramma Tala**: You have a big question in you. Go on. Ask it.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): asking when you want to know something  
> _Moana has a big question. What can you do when you want to know?_  
> - ✅ Ask a grown-up you trust → Yes! Asking is brave. Good questions help us learn.
> - Never ask → Questions are good! Ask them. People like to help.
> - Guess and hope → Guessing is fine sometimes. But asking gets a real answer.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about asking a trusted grown-up when you want to know something._


#### `sprout:a10-03-sand-done` · [main](main.md#a10-03-sand-done)

**Moana**: Who are we? Where did we come from? Why do we never leave?  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.His.2.K-2, notice): families have stories about long ago  
> _Moana wants to know where her people came from._  
> _Every family has stories about long ago. Ask a grown-up to tell you one._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that families have stories about the past, and was invited to ask for one._

**Gramma Tala**: Good. Get your guest. Bring a torch.  

#### `sprout:a10-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a10-03-complete)

_Gramma gets up. It takes three tries, and a hand from you._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, notice): helping someone kindly, without a fuss  
> _You gave Gramma a hand up, and nobody made a fuss._  
> _Quiet help is kind. It lets people feel strong, not silly._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that helping quietly, without a fuss, is a kind way to help._

_Your Nubbin walks up the beach, doing its step, a bit crooked._  
**Gramma Tala**: It will come. Everything good comes slowly.  

**Notes:** A10-3. Signature choice a10-step is kept as in the original.

## A10-4. The Cave of Canoes

- `a10-04-s1`: Follow Gramma behind the waterfall. · _Carry the torch up the stream._
- `a10-04-s2`: Look at what is hidden in the cave. · _Go down the passage and look._
- `a10-04-s3`: Watch what the drum remembers. · _Stand back while Moana beats the drum._

#### `sprout:a10-04-falls` · [main](main.md#a10-04-falls)

_Gramma leads you up a stream. A waterfall tumbles down a cliff._  
**Gramma Tala**: The secret is behind the waterfall. Moana first, then our guest.  
_Moana steps through. You hear her gasp._  
_You follow. The water hits your head. Splash! Brr!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, ask): words for where things are  
> _Where is the secret passage? Point to your answer._  
> - ✅ Behind the waterfall → Yes! Behind the waterfall. Moana went through the water.
> - On top of the waterfall → Not on top. It is behind the waterfall.
> - Beside a tree → Not beside a tree. The passage is behind the waterfall.
> _For grown-ups: Used a position word (behind) to say where the passage was._

_Behind the falls is a passage in the rock._  

#### `sprout:a10-04-falls-done` · [main](main.md#a10-04-falls-done)

_The torch makes the wet rock shine._  
_Your Nubbin’s emote ball glows too. It is company._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): a friend nearby helps you feel brave  
> _The cave is dark, and a bit spooky. But you are not alone._  
> _A friend nearby helps you feel brave. Who helps you feel brave?_  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that having a friend close by helps us feel brave in the dark._


#### `sprout:a10-04-canoes` · [main](main.md#a10-04-canoes)

_The passage opens into a huge cave. Blue light comes in from the sea._  
_Moana lifts the torch. The shapes in the dark are boats!_  
_They are great canoes, with two hulls each and sails folded up._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 5  
> _(Count the canoes in the front row.)_  
> _One, two, three, four, five. Five great canoes!_  
> Check: `edu:count:canoes:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five canoes, saying one number for each._

**Moana**: Whose are these? Nobody builds these any more!  
_Your Nubbin sits on a hull, under a very tall mast._  
_At the front is a great drum._  

**Choices**

- **Brush the dust off the nearest prow.**
    - Under the dust the wood is carved with waves, stars and tiny people.
- **Hold the torch high so Moana can see.**
    - Moana walks along, touching each hull, like counting heads.

#### `sprout:a10-04-canoes-done` · [main](main.md#a10-04-canoes-done)

**Gramma Tala**: Bang the drum, Moana. It has been waiting for you.  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-PS4-1, notice): a drum makes sound when its skin shakes  
> _When Moana bangs the drum, the skin shakes. Shake, shake!_  
> _The shaking makes the sound you hear._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that a shaking drum skin makes sound._


#### `sprout:a10-04-drum` · [main](main.md#a10-04-drum)

_Moana beats the drum. Boom! The sound goes all round the cave._  
_Then you are not in a cave. You are on the open sea!_  
_Great canoes sail all round, full of singing people._  
_A woman at a steering oar grins right at you._  
_They find an island, land and plant. Then they sail on._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.His.2.K-2, notice): ancestors are family from long ago  
> _These people are Moana’s ancestors. Ancestors are family from long, long ago._  
> _They sailed from island to island, all across a huge ocean._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard what ancestors are, and that Pacific peoples sailed between islands._

_Your Nubbin is doing its step without knowing it._  

#### `sprout:a10-04-drum-done` · [main](main.md#a10-04-drum-done)

_The drum stops ringing. You are in the cave again._  

> **Lesson** (music, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): clap a steady beat  
> _(Clap a slow, steady beat, like a drum.)_  
> _Boom, boom, boom. Keep it steady!_  
> Check: `edu:clap:beat:steady`
> _For grown-ups: Clapped a slow, steady beat._

_There is dust on your feet and sea sounds at the far end._  

#### `sprout:a10-04-complete` · [main](main.md#a10-04-complete)

**Moana**: They did not stay! They sailed on to every island. We did that!  
**Gramma Tala**: We did. Then the dark came, and the chiefs said, stay inside the reef.  
**Gramma Tala**: And we hid the boats and forgot. All but a few of us.  
_Gramma takes out the green stone with the spiral on it._  
**Gramma Tala**: This is the heart of Te Fiti, the mother island.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, ask): remember what happened earlier in a story  
> _Gramma kept the green stone safe. Who gave it to Moana, long ago?_  
> - ✅ The sea → Yes! The sea gave it to her when she was very small.
> - Her dad → Not her dad. The sea gave Moana the stone, on the beach.
> - A turtle → The turtle went home to the sea. The sea gave her the stone.
> _For grown-ups: Remembered an earlier event in the story: the sea gave Moana the stone._

**Gramma Tala**: Maui took it. So Maui must put it back.  
**Moana**: You take it, you bring it back. That is the rule!  
**Moana**: I have to tell Dad. Right now!  

**Notes:** A10-4. The ancestors are shown as real voyagers; the song is never written out.

## A10-5. Setting Sail

- `a10-05-s1`: Stand by Moana while she tells the chief. · _Go to the meeting house._
- `a10-05-s2`: Sit with Gramma Tala in her house. · _Sit by her mat._
- `a10-05-s3`: Carry food and water to the hidden canoe. · _Take the bundles from Sina._

#### `sprout:a10-05-council` · [main](main.md#a10-05-council)

_Moana runs into the meeting house with cobweb in her hair._  
**Moana**: Our people sailed! There are boats in a cave! We can find Maui!  
**Chief Tui**: The cave. She showed you.  
**Chief Tui**: We are not going past the reef.  
**Chief Tui**: When I was young, I went past the reef with my best friend.  
**Chief Tui**: The sea was rough. My friend died there. I came home alone.  
_For a moment he is just a man who has been afraid._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): naming a feeling and why  
> _Tui’s friend died at sea, so he fears for Moana. How does he feel?_  
> - ✅ Sad and scared → Yes. He is sad about his friend, and scared for Moana.
> - Cross → A bit cross, maybe. Mostly he is sad and scared, because he loves her.
> - Proud → He is proud of Moana. Today he is mostly scared for her.
> _For grown-ups: Named feelings (sad, scared) behind a worried parent’s rule._


#### `sprout:a10-05-council-done` · [main](main.md#a10-05-council-done)

_A boy runs in, out of breath. Gramma Tala has fallen by the shore._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, ask): tell what happened first, next and last  
> _First Moana told her dad. Next the boy ran in._  
> _What was the news the boy brought?_  
> - ✅ Gramma Tala has fallen → Yes. Gramma Tala has fallen. Moana will go to her.
> - The fish came back → Not that. The news was about Gramma Tala.
> - It is lunchtime → Not lunch. The news was that Gramma Tala has fallen.
> _For grown-ups: Followed the order of events and said what the news was._


#### `sprout:a10-05-bedside` · [main](main.md#a10-05-bedside)

_Gramma lies on her mat. A healer sits beside her._  
_She is very small now. Her hands are cool._  
**Gramma Tala**: Do not fuss. I am very old, and very poorly.  
**Gramma Tala**: You must go. Tonight. Find Maui. Make him give it back.  
**Moana**: I cannot leave you. Not now.  
**Gramma Tala**: I will be the bit of you that keeps going.  
_She puts the shell with the heart in it into Moana’s hand._  
**Gramma Tala**: And look after our guest. Guests are a big job.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): sad feelings are allowed, and hugs help  
> _Moana feels sad. It is hard when someone you love is very old._  
> _Feeling sad is allowed. A hug, a sit-down and a friend all help._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that sadness is allowed and that hugs and company help._


#### `sprout:a10-05-bedside-done` · [main](main.md#a10-05-bedside-done)

**Gramma Tala**: Go on, my brave girl. I love you very much.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, SL.K.1, do): saying a kind thing out loud to someone you love  
> _(Tell a grown-up one kind thing about them.)_  
> _Kind words feel warm. Gramma’s words stay with Moana._
