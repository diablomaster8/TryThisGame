# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A13: The Wooden Boy

## A13-1. The Piece of Wood

- `a13-01-s1`: Step into the brown-ink page, and out of the rain. · _Touch the page. Find the open door with sawdust on its step._
- `a13-01-s2`: Stay in Master Cherry’s shop. Keep your Nubbin out from under their feet. · _Stand by the bench. Two old men are about to argue._

#### `sprout:a13-01-page` · [main](main.md#a13-01-page)

_Nova’s atlas opens. The page is all brown ink._  
_A hill town. A long white road. The sea at the bottom._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, notice): Italy is a country shaped like a boot; Tuscany is in the middle  
> _This page is in Italy. Italy is a country shaped like a boot._  
> _(Trace a boot in the air.)_  
> _The hill town is in Tuscany, in the middle._  
> _For grown-ups: Met Italy, a country shaped like a boot, and Tuscany, the part in the middle._

_This story begins with a piece of wood. Just wood!_  
_The ink turns to rain. Splash! You and your Nubbin get wet._  
_One door is open. It smells of warm pine. In you go._  
**Master Cherry**: Come in! Shut the weather out. Mind the shavings.  
_Master Cherry has a nose like a ripe red cherry._  
**Master Cherry**: I will make a table leg from this old log.  
_He lifts his axe._  
**A Small Voice**: Ow! Not so hard!  

#### `sprout:a13-01-page-clank` · [main](main.md#a13-01-page-clank)

#### `sprout:a13-01-page-done` · [main](main.md#a13-01-page-done)

_Master Cherry looks under the bench. He looks up the chimney._  
**Master Cherry**: Did you say that? Nobody said that!  
**Master Cherry**: I imagined it. It was only the weather.  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): wood comes from trees, and a tree grows a ring each year  
> _Wood comes from trees. This log was once part of a tree._  
> _Each year a tree grows one new ring inside._  
> _More rings means an older tree._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that wood comes from trees, and that each year a tree grows one ring._


#### `sprout:a13-01-plane` · [main](main.md#a13-01-plane)

_Master Cherry gives the log a whack._  
**A Small Voice**: Ow! That hurt!  
_He sits down on the floor. Then he tries his plane._  
**A Small Voice**: Stop it! You are tickling!  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds: log starts with the sound l  
> _The log says “Ow!” The word log starts with the sound l, l, l._  
> _Which word starts the same? Lamp, or hat?_  
> - ✅ Lamp → Yes! L, l, lamp. L, l, log.
> - Hat → Hat starts with h, h, h. Lamp starts with l, l, l, just like log.
> _For grown-ups: Listened for the first sound of a word (l for log and lamp)._

_In comes a little old man in a bright yellow wig._  
**Geppetto**: Good morning, Master Antonio. I want to carve a puppet.  
**A Small Voice**: Good idea, Polendina!  
_Geppetto goes red. He thinks Master Cherry said it!_  

**Choices**

- **Tell Geppetto it was the log.**
    - **Geppetto**: A log said that? Now I have heard everything.
- **Keep out of it. It is their argument.**
    - You keep out of it. Not for long!

#### `sprout:a13-01-plane-done` · [main](main.md#a13-01-plane-done)

_The two old men shout and tug. They swap wigs by mistake!_  
_Then they swap back and shake hands._  
_Master Cherry gives Geppetto the log. It bounces on his toe._  
_Then it is peace, and the rain has stopped._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): making up after a quarrel  
> _The two old men were cross. How did they make friends again?_  
> - ✅ They shook hands. → Yes. Shaking hands, or a hug, or sorry, can mend a quarrel.
> - They shouted more. → More shouting makes it worse. They stopped, and shook hands. That helped.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about how to make up after a quarrel._

## A13-2. Geppetto Carves a Puppet

- `a13-02-s1`: Hold the lamp while Geppetto carves. · _Stand at his elbow. Watch the nose._
- `a13-02-s2`: Help Geppetto teach the puppet to walk. · _Hold one wooden hand. Mind the door._

#### `sprout:a13-02-carve` · [main](main.md#a13-02-carve)

_Geppetto rolls up his sleeves. He looks at the log._  
**Geppetto**: I shall call him Pinocchio.  
_Geppetto smiles. It is a good name for a pine puppet._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, L.2.4, notice): pinocchio means pine nut in Italian  
> **Geppetto**: Pinocchio is a real Italian word. It means a pine nut.  
> **Geppetto**: Pine nuts are tiny seeds. They hide in pine cones.  
> **Geppetto**: And this puppet is made of pine!  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that pinocchio is the Italian word for a pine nut._

_He carves the hair, then the eyes. The eyes look back!_  
**Geppetto**: Wooden eyes, why are you looking at me?  
_He carves the nose. It grows. And grows!_  
_He carves the mouth. It sticks out its tongue._  

**Choices**

- **Point out that the nose is growing.**
    - **Geppetto**: I know. I have decided not to notice.
- **Keep the lamp steady and say nothing.**
    - You hold the lamp still. Geppetto works faster.

#### `sprout:a13-02-carve-done` · [main](main.md#a13-02-carve-done)

_Geppetto carves the hands. They grab his yellow wig!_  
**Geppetto**: Pinocchio! Give that back!  
_Pinocchio gives it back. His eyes are laughing._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): naming a feeling: Geppetto is cross but also fond  
> _Geppetto shouts. But is he really very angry?_  
> - ✅ A little cross, and a little fond. → Yes! Sometimes we feel two things at once.
> - Only very angry. → He is cross. But look, he is smiling a bit. Two feelings together!
> _For grown-ups: Noticed that people can feel two feelings at once._


#### `sprout:a13-02-legs` · [main](main.md#a13-02-legs)

_Geppetto carves the legs and feet._  
_The first thing the feet do is kick his nose. Bonk!_  
_You hold one wooden hand. Geppetto holds the other._  
_One step. Two steps. Then Pinocchio is running!_  
**Pinocchio**: I have LEGS!  
_Clack, clack, clack! Out of the door and down the street._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.4, ask): clapping beats and counting steps  
> _Clack, clack, clack! That is three clacks. Can you clap three?_  
> - ✅ Clap, clap, clap! → Yes! One, two, three. Three clacks!
> - Clap, clap. → Let us try again. Clack, clack, clack. One, two, three.
> _For grown-ups: Counted three steps by clapping._

**Geppetto**: Catch him! Catch him!  

#### `sprout:a13-02-street` · [main](main.md#a13-02-street)

_At the bottom of the street, a carabineer catches him by the nose._  
_Geppetto arrives, out of breath._  
_Pinocchio lies down on the stones and will not walk._  
_The crowd thinks the old man is being unkind to the puppet._  
**Carabineer**: You, old man. You can come with me.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, L.2.4, notice): babbo is the Tuscan word for dad  
> _Geppetto is Pinocchio’s babbo._  
> _In Tuscany, children say babbo for dad. Say it with me: bab-bo._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned the Italian word babbo, for dad._


#### `sprout:a13-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a13-02-complete)

_So it is Geppetto who is led away. He is sad._  
**Geppetto**: I worked so hard. I should have thought.  
_Your Nubbin hops out of the cap. The street is empty._  
_Pinocchio runs home across the fields, fast as a hare._  
_The room under the stairs is very quiet._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, ask): what helps when a room feels empty and quiet  
> _The room feels lonely. What helps when you feel lonely?_  
> - ✅ Cuddle a friend or a toy. → Yes. A cuddle helps. So does telling someone.
> - ✅ Telling a grown-up. → Yes. Telling someone you trust helps too.
> _For grown-ups: Thought about what helps when we feel lonely._


## A13-3. The Talking-cricket

- `a13-03-s1`: Find what is going cri-cri-cri in the dark. · _Listen along the wall by the hearth._
- `a13-03-s2`: Listen to the Talking-cricket. Then see what happened at the hearth. · _Hear him out. Then go and look._

#### `sprout:a13-03-cri` · [main](main.md#a13-03-cri)

_Pinocchio shuts the door and sighs. A whole room, all his!_  
**Pinocchio**: Mine. The chair, the table, the painted fire.  
_Then, a small sound. Cri, cri, cri._  
**Pinocchio**: Who is that?  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, do): sound words: cri, cri, cri  
> _A cricket goes cri, cri, cri. Can you make the sound?_  
> _What sound does a dog make? A duck?_  
> Check: `edu:say:sound-word:cri`
> _For grown-ups: Made animal and sound words, like cri, cri, cri._

_Your Nubbin’s ears go straight up._  

#### `sprout:a13-03-cri-done` · [main](main.md#a13-03-cri-done)

_On the wall climbs a big cricket. He looks very grand._  
**The Talking-cricket**: I am the Talking-cricket. I have lived here a hundred years.  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS3-1, ask): where a cricket lives and what it does  
> _Crickets chirp by rubbing their wings. Where do real crickets live?_  
> - ✅ In grass, and in cosy cracks. → Yes! They hide in grass, under stones and in warm cracks.
> - In the sea. → Not the sea. Crickets live on land, in grass and in cosy cracks.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that crickets live in grass and cracks and chirp with their wings._


#### `sprout:a13-03-cricket` · [main](main.md#a13-03-cricket)

**The Talking-cricket**: Listen, Pinocchio. Be kind to your babbo. Go to school.  
**Pinocchio**: I would rather chase butterflies.  
**The Talking-cricket**: Then you will be sorry.  
_Pinocchio picks up a mallet and throws it. He is not really aiming._  
_Thump! The cricket tumbles off the wall, into the ash._  
**Pinocchio**: Oh! I did not mean to!  
_(The cricket is only dazed. He is a very tough old cricket.)_  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, ask): what to do after hurting someone by accident: check they are all right  
> _The mallet bumped the cricket. What should Pinocchio do now?_  
> - ✅ Go and check he is all right. → Yes. When we hurt someone, we go and see if they are all right.
> - Pretend nothing happened. → Pretending does not help. Going to check, and saying sorry, does.
> _For grown-ups: Practised what to do after an accident that hurts someone: check, and say sorry._


**Choices**

- **Look in the hearth for the cricket.**
    - You look in the ash. Your Nubbin finds him! Squeak!
    - **The Talking-cricket**: I am not hurt. I am cross. Cross, not hurt.
    - You carry him to the hedge on a leaf. He is safe.
    - **The Talking-cricket**: Thank you, kind friend.
- **Wait. Give the cricket a little time.**
    - You wait. Later you see a tiny track in the ash.
    - It goes to the crack under the door. The cricket walked away, grumbling.

#### `sprout:a13-03-cricket-done` · [main](main.md#a13-03-cricket-done)

_The room is quiet. Pinocchio rests his chin on his fists._  
**Pinocchio**: Anyway. I am hungry.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): naming a feeling: hungry, and also a little sorry  
> _Pinocchio’s tummy is rumbling. But is he feeling something else too?_  
> - ✅ A little sorry. → Yes. He is sorry, and he cannot say it yet.
> - Only hungry. → He is hungry. But his quiet face looks a little sorry too.
> _For grown-ups: Noticed a feeling that is hiding behind another one._


#### `sprout:a13-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a13-03-complete)

_Night comes. Pinocchio’s tummy rumbles like a small dog._  
**Pinocchio**: Is there any supper? Somewhere?  
_Your Nubbin curls up against your boot._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-ESS1-1, notice): day and night: it gets dark at night  
> _Night comes when our side of the Earth turns away from the Sun._  
> _The Sun is still there. We just cannot see it._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that night comes as our side of the Earth turns away from the Sun._

_Outside, the wind is getting up._  

## A13-4. The Flying Egg

#### `sprout:a13-04-hungry` · [main](main.md#a13-04-hungry)

**Pinocchio**: Painted. Even the soup is painted.

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.B.3, do): sort things into groups: things you can eat and things you cannot  
> _Button, cork, nail. Can you eat any of them? No!_  
> _Sort them: how many things can we eat? None!_  
> Check: `edu:sort:drawer-things:edible`
> _For grown-ups: Sorted objects into things we can eat and things we cannot._


**Choices**

- **Dig with your Nubbin.**
    - You dig too. It is not grand, but it is company.
- **Tell Pinocchio there is sure to be something.**
    - **Pinocchio**: There had better be. I am empty all the way through.

#### `sprout:a13-04-hungry-done` · [main](main.md#a13-04-hungry-done)

_In the heap is something round and white. An egg!_  
**Pinocchio**: An egg! I will fry it!  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-LS1-2, ask): eggs come from hens, and a chick grows inside  
> _Who laid the egg? A hen! And what might grow inside a hen’s egg?_  
> - ✅ A chick! → Yes! A chick grows inside, with its mother hen keeping it warm.
> - A fish. → Fish eggs are in the water. A hen’s egg can grow a chick.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a chick can grow inside a hen’s egg._


#### `sprout:a13-04-egg` · [main](main.md#a13-04-egg)

_He cracks the egg. Out pops a fluffy chick!_

_Pinocchio holds the empty shell. His mouth is wide open._  
_Your Nubbin opens its mouth too._  
_The chick flies out of the window and away._  
**Pinocchio**: Oh. The cricket was right. I wish I had not run off.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): saying what you wish you had done differently  
> _Pinocchio wishes he had stayed home. What could he do now?_  
> - ✅ Say sorry, and try again. → Yes. Saying sorry and trying again is brave.
> - Hide under the bed. → Hiding feels safe for a bit. But sorry and trying again help more.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about saying sorry and starting again._


#### `sprout:a13-04-egg-done` · [main](main.md#a13-04-egg-done)

#### `sprout:a13-04-storm-done` · [main](main.md#a13-04-storm-done)

_You walk home together, dripping._  
_Your Nubbin is dry under your coat._  
_Pinocchio puts his wet feet on the brazier. He falls asleep._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-PS3-1, ask): a hot fire dries wet things and can burn wood  
> _Pinocchio’s feet are wet. What dries them faster, something warm or something cold?_  
> - ✅ Something warm. → Yes. Warmth dries things. But wood must not get too close to a fire!
> - Something cold. → Cold is slow. Warm air dries things faster. A fire is very warm, so we keep wood far from it.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that warmth dries wet things, and that wood must stay away from a fire._

## A13-5. Three Pears

- `a13-05-s1`: Let Geppetto in. · _Give Geppetto a hand over the window sill._

#### `sprout:a13-05-window` · [main](main.md#a13-05-window)

_Geppetto climbs in through the window. It is a small window!_  
_You lean out and take his arm. Up he comes._  
**Geppetto**: Now, you young— Oh. Oh, my boy. Your feet.  
_He came to scold. He looks at the feet, and hugs him instead._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): choosing kindness over scolding when someone is hurt  
> _Geppetto wanted to scold. Then he hugged. Why did he change?_  
> - ✅ He saw Pinocchio needed help. → Yes. When someone needs help, a hug comes first.
> - He forgot he was cross. → He did not forget. He chose to be kind first. That is a strong thing to do.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about choosing kindness first when someone needs help._


#### `sprout:a13-05-window-done` · [main](main.md#a13-05-window-done)

> _For grown-ups: Heard that a seed in a pear’s core can grow into a tree._

> - Being silly. → It is not silly. It is sharing, and it is one of the kindest things there is.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about sharing: giving some of what you have to someone else._

**Pinocchio**: Babbo, where is your coat?

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.2.3, ask): how a character feels from what he says and does  
> _Geppetto said “I was too warm.” But it was snowing!_  
> _Why did he say that?_  
> - ✅ So Pinocchio would not feel bad. → Yes. He gave something up, and he did not want Pinocchio to worry.
> - Because he was hot. → It was snowy! He was kind, and he did not want Pinocchio to worry.
> _For grown-ups: Worked out why a character said something kind that was not quite true._

_Pinocchio hugs him tight. He understands._
