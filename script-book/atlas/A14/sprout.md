# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A14: Homecoming

## A14-1. The Loom

- `a14-01-s1`: Step onto the island and wait at the gate. · _Touch the page, then walk to the gate._
- `a14-01-s2`: Sit and listen with Telemachus. · _Sit by the pillar, away from the noisy men._
- `a14-01-s3`: Watch Penelope come down the stairs. · _Stand at the bottom of the stairs._

#### `sprout:a14-01-gate` · [main](main.md#a14-01-gate)

_The atlas page is blue, with a little island on it._  
_You step in. Now you are at a big gate, with your Nubbin._  
_Inside, young men are eating and playing. No one comes to the gate._  
_An old traveller with a spear waits beside you._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, notice): an island is land with water all around it  
> **Mentes**: This little island is called Ithaca.  
> **Mentes**: An island is land with water all around it.  
> _(Trace a circle round the island with your finger.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that an island is land with water all around it, and met Ithaca._

**Mentes**: A good house always welcomes a stranger.  
_A young man sees you. His cheeks go red._  
**Telemachus**: Oh! Strangers at the gate! Please come in and eat.  

**Choices**

- **Wait to be asked in nicely.**
    - **Telemachus**: Now you are asked. Welcome! I am Telemachus.
- **Step in. You are hungry.**
    - You step in. The old traveller smiles.
- **Let your Nubbin go first.**
    - Your Nubbin trots in. Telemachus laughs.

#### `sprout:a14-01-gate-done` · [main](main.md#a14-01-gate-done)

_Telemachus puts the traveller’s spear in a rack._  
_The other spears are dusty. They were his dad’s._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): name a feeling from how someone looks and acts  
> _Telemachus looks at his dad’s dusty spears. He is quiet. How does he feel?_  
> - ✅ Sad, because he misses his dad → Yes. He misses his dad. It is all right to feel sad.
> - Silly → He is not feeling silly. He is quiet and looking at the spears. He feels sad.
> _For grown-ups: Named a feeling (sad, missing someone) from how a character looks and acts._

**Telemachus**: Come this way, away from the noisy men.  

#### `sprout:a14-01-hearth` · [main](main.md#a14-01-hearth)

_A maid pours water over your hands._  
_She brings bread. She brings meat._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count three things  
> _The maid brought water, bread and meat._  
> _(Count them on your fingers together. How many?)_  
> Check: `edu:count:foods:3`
> _For grown-ups: Counted three things (water, bread, meat) to answer how many._

**Mentes**: I am Mentes. I came to meet your dad.  
**Telemachus**: My dad went away when I was a baby.  
**Telemachus**: Some men eat our food every day. It makes me sad.  
**Mentes**: Your dad is alive. He is trying to get home.  

**Choices**

- **Ask Mentes how he knows.**
    - **Mentes**: Some things I have seen. Some I just know.
- **Say you will go with Telemachus.**
    - **Telemachus**: You would? That is kind.
- **Eat, and listen.**
    - You eat the bread. It is the best bread in the house.

#### `sprout:a14-01-hearth-done` · [main](main.md#a14-01-hearth-done)

_Mentes walks across the yard._  
_A bird flies up through the roof._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): smoke and warm air go up  
> _The bird flew up through a hole in the roof._  
> _Which way does smoke from a fire go?_  
> - ✅ Up → Yes. Warm air goes up and takes the smoke with it. That is why the roof has a hole.
> - Down → Smoke floats up, not down. The warm air carries it up and out of the hole.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that smoke and warm air rise._

_Mentes was Athena, a goddess who helps Telemachus!_  
**Telemachus**: Athena can look like anybody. Even a bird. Even a man with a beard.  

#### `sprout:a14-01-stair` · [main](main.md#a14-01-stair)

_Phemius the singer plays a lyre. It has strings._  

> **Lesson** (music, 1-PS4-1, notice): a plucked string wobbles, and the wobble is sound  
> _When Phemius plucks a string, it wobbles._  
> _The wobble is the sound! (Wiggle your fingers like a string and hum: mmmm.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a plucked string wobbles and that the wobble makes sound._

_Penelope comes down the stairs. She looks sad._  
**Penelope**: Please sing a different song.  
**Telemachus**: Mother, let him sing.  
_Penelope goes back up. You spot a big loom with cloth on it._  

#### `sprout:a14-01-stair-done` · [main](main.md#a14-01-stair-done)

**Telemachus**: That cloth has been on the loom for ages.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): hear the first sound of a word  
> _Loom starts with the sound l, l, l._  
> _Which word starts the same: lamp or hat?_  
> - ✅ Lamp → Yes! L, l, lamp. L, l, loom.
> - Hat → Hat starts with h, h, h. Lamp starts with l, l, l, just like loom.
> _For grown-ups: Listened for the first sound of a word (l for loom and lamp)._

**Telemachus**: Ask Antinous. He loves to tell that story.  

#### `sprout:a14-01-complete` · [main](main.md#a14-01-complete)

_Night comes. The noisy men go home to bed._  
**Eurycleia**: Time for sleep, Telemachus. Sweet dreams.  
**Telemachus**: Tomorrow I will call a big meeting. Will you stand with me?  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): sharing is kind  
> _Telemachus shared his bread with you, and you were strangers._  
> _What is a kind way to share?_  
> - ✅ Give a friend half of what you have → Yes. Sharing makes friends feel cared for, and it feels good too.
> - Keep it all → Keeping it all feels safe, but sharing makes friends. Telemachus shared and made a friend.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about sharing as a kind thing to do._

_You and your Nubbin sleep on soft fleeces in the porch._  
_Upstairs, a loom is working, very quietly._  

## A14-2. The Assembly

- `a14-02-s1`: Stand behind Telemachus at the big meeting. · _Stand behind his father’s seat._
- `a14-02-s2`: Listen to Antinous tell about the cloth. · _Stay close to Telemachus._
- `a14-02-s3`: Hold the torch for Penelope. · _Keep the torch high and steady._

#### `sprout:a14-02-assembly` · [main](main.md#a14-02-assembly)

_The men of Ithaca meet in the meeting-place._  
_Telemachus sits in his dad’s seat. You stand behind him._  
**Telemachus**: Please go home. You are eating our food.  
_He puts down the speaker’s staff. His eyes are wet._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, ask): taking turns: one person speaks at a time  
> _Only the person holding the staff may talk. Why?_  
> - ✅ So everyone takes turns → Yes. One person talks, and everyone else listens. Then it is the next person’s turn.
> - So it looks pretty → It does look grand, but the real reason is turns. The staff shows whose turn it is.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a speaker’s staff was a way of taking turns._

_Some men laugh. Telemachus feels very small._  

**Choices**

- **Pick up the staff and give it back.**
    - He takes it and holds it tight.
- **Look kindly at the man who laughed.**
    - The man stops laughing for a moment.
- **Stay right behind him.**
    - Telemachus stands up a little straighter.

#### `sprout:a14-02-assembly-done` · [main](main.md#a14-02-assembly-done)

_A tall man stands up. He is Antinous._  
**Antinous**: It is not our fault! It is Penelope’s fault!  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): saying sorry instead of blaming  
> _Antinous says it is not his fault. What could he say instead?_  
> - ✅ I am sorry. I will go home. → Yes. Saying sorry and putting things right is brave.
> - It is your fault! → Blaming someone else does not help. Saying sorry does.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about saying sorry instead of blaming someone else._


#### `sprout:a14-02-antinous` · [main](main.md#a14-02-antinous)

**Antinous**: Penelope made a big cloth on a big wooden frame.  
**Antinous**: She said, I will marry when it is done.  
**Antinous**: So all day, she wove the cloth.  
**Antinous**: Then at night, she took it all apart!  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, ask): first, next, last: what happened in order  
> _In the day, Penelope wove the cloth. Then night came._  
> _What did she do at night?_  
> - ✅ She took the cloth apart → Yes. First she wove, next it was night, last she took it apart.
> - She sang a song → She did not sing. At night she took the cloth apart, bit by bit.
> _For grown-ups: Put events in order (first she wove, last she took it apart)._

_The page goes soft. Now you are upstairs, holding a torch._  

#### `sprout:a14-02-antinous-done` · [main](main.md#a14-02-antinous-done)

_In front of you stands the great loom._  
_Penelope sits at it, with a small knife._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.1, ask): take one away from one  
> _Penelope weaves 1 row. At night she takes 1 row away._  
> _How many rows are left?_  
> - ✅ None → Yes! 1 take away 1 leaves none. That is why the cloth was never finished.
> - 2 rows → Taking away makes fewer, not more. 1 take away 1 leaves none.
> _For grown-ups: Took away to find that 1 take away 1 leaves none._


#### `sprout:a14-02-loom` · [main](main.md#a14-02-loom)

**Antinous**: Every day she wove. Every night she took it out.  
**Penelope**: Hold the torch a little higher. Thank you.  
**Penelope**: The long threads stay still. The short thread goes back and forth.  
**Penelope**: Over, under, over, under.  

> **Lesson** (art, ELOF: Mathematics Development, do): a weaving pattern: over, under, over, under  
> _Penelope’s thread goes over, then under, over, then under._  
> _(Say the pattern together, and hop it in the air.)_  
> Check: `edu:say:pattern:over-under`
> _For grown-ups: Said and moved to a repeating pattern: over, under, over, under._

_Your Nubbin holds the end of the thread, very seriously._  
_Out in the dark, a floorboard creaks. Someone is listening._  

**Choices**

- **Go and look in the passage.**
    - A maid hurries away round the corner.
- **Tell Penelope someone heard.**
    - **Penelope**: I know. I will finish my row anyway.
- **Hold the torch steady.**
    - You keep the torch still. She keeps weaving.

#### `sprout:a14-02-loom-done` · [main](main.md#a14-02-loom-done)

_Now it is daytime again. You are back at the meeting._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-PS4-2, ask): torches give light in the dark  
> _At night it was dark, so you held a torch._  
> _What gives us light in the daytime?_  
> - ✅ The Sun → Yes. The Sun lights up the day. A torch gives light after the Sun has set.
> - The Moon → The Moon shines at night. In the day, the Sun gives us light.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that the Sun gives light in the day and that a torch gives light at night._

**Antinous**: A maid told us the secret.  
**Antinous**: Penelope had to finish the cloth.  

#### `sprout:a14-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a14-02-complete)

_The meeting ends. No one helps Telemachus._  
_Old Mentor, Telemachus’s friend, comes close._  
**Mentor**: I have a ship. Fill it with food. Tell no one.  
**Eurycleia**: Take care, my dear. Come home safe.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): rhyming words  
> _Sail rhymes with a word. Which one: tail or tree?_  
> - ✅ Tail → Yes! Sail and tail rhyme. They end with the same sound: ail.
> - Tree → Tree ends in ee. Sail ends in ail, just like tail.
> _For grown-ups: Found a rhyme for sail (tail)._

_At nightfall the ship sails out of the harbour._  
_The wind blows from behind. It fills the sail._  

## A14-3. Nestor and Menelaus

- `a14-03-s1`: Land at Pylos and ask old Nestor for news. · _Walk up the beach to the feast._
- `a14-03-s2`: Ride to Sparta in the chariot. · _Climb in behind Telemachus._
- `a14-03-s3`: Hear what Menelaus learned. · _Sit with him by the fire._

#### `sprout:a14-03-pylos` · [main](main.md#a14-03-pylos)

_A sandy beach, at sunrise. People are having a big feast._  
**Telemachus**: I feel nervous. What if I say the wrong thing?  
**Mentor**: Take a breath. Some words will come to you.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): what helps when you feel nervous  
> _Telemachus feels nervous. What can help him feel braver?_  
> - ✅ A slow, deep breath → Yes. Breathe in slowly, and out slowly. (Try it together.)
> - Run away → Running away does not help the nervous feeling go. A slow breath does.
> _For grown-ups: Practised a calming breath for nervous feelings._

_Nestor is very, very old. He is kind._  
**Nestor**: Eat first. Then we talk.  
**Telemachus**: Please, do you know where my dad is?  
**Nestor**: I am sorry. I do not know. Menelaus might.  

**Choices**

- **Ask who might know.**
    - **Nestor**: Menelaus. My son will drive you to Sparta.
- **Ask if there is any hope.**
    - **Nestor**: There is always hope with your dad.
- **Let your Nubbin sit by the old king.**
    - Nestor pats it gently, like a grandchild.

#### `sprout:a14-03-pylos-done` · [main](main.md#a14-03-pylos-done)

_After the feast, Mentor turns into a big sea-eagle._  
_He flies away over the water._  
**Nestor**: That was Athena again! She is looking after you.  
_Your Nubbin checks every bird in the sky for a beard._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, ask): be quiet and kind when others are praying  
> _The people are praying on the beach. What should a good guest do?_  
> - ✅ Stay quiet and kind → Yes. Being quiet shows you respect what matters to them.
> - Shout and run about → Shouting would spoil it for them. A good guest stays quiet and kind.
> _For grown-ups: Learned to be quiet and respectful when others are praying._

_The people on the beach are praying quietly._  
_You stand back and keep quiet._  

#### `sprout:a14-03-sparta` · [main](main.md#a14-03-sparta)

_Two days in a chariot! Your Nubbin leans round the corners._  
_Menelaus’s house is the shiniest house you have ever seen._  
_There are shiny things to see: bronze, gold, silver, amber and ivory._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count five shiny things  
> _Bronze, gold, silver, amber, ivory._  
> _(Count the five shiny things on your fingers together.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:shiny-things:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five things to answer how many._

**Helen**: You look just like Odysseus! You have his feet and hands.  
**Menelaus**: Yes. You are his son.  
_Telemachus holds his cloak up to hide his tears._  

**Choices**

- **Say yes, it is his son.**
    - Menelaus takes Telemachus’s arm gently.
- **Let Telemachus answer.**
    - **Telemachus**: Yes, I am. Is my dad alive?

#### `sprout:a14-03-sparta-done` · [main](main.md#a14-03-sparta-done)

**Menelaus**: Eat and rest now. In the morning I will tell you something.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Approaches to Learning, ask): waiting patiently  
> _Telemachus must wait all night for news. What can he do while he waits?_  
> - ✅ Eat and rest → Yes. Resting helps us wait. Morning will come.
> - Ask and ask again → Asking again and again makes waiting feel longer. Resting is better.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about waiting patiently by resting._


#### `sprout:a14-03-proteus` · [main](main.md#a14-03-proteus)

**Menelaus**: A magic Old Man of the Sea knows many things.  
**Menelaus**: At noon he sleeps among the seals.  
_You hide under a sealskin on the hot beach._  
_He comes up from the sea, and lies down to sleep._  
**Menelaus**: Now! Hold on!  
_He becomes a lion: roar! Then a snake: sss!_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, do): animal sounds  
> _The old man turned into a lion and a snake._  
> _(Roar like a lion. Hiss like a snake.)_  
> Check: `edu:say:animal-sounds:lion-snake`
> _For grown-ups: Made animal sounds (roar, sss) in the story._

_At last he is an old man again, and he answers._  
**Menelaus**: Odysseus is alive. He is on an island.  

**Choices**

- **Ask Menelaus: alive?**
    - **Menelaus**: Alive! He misses home very much.
- **Look at Telemachus.**
    - He jumps to his feet, full of hope.

#### `sprout:a14-03-proteus-done` · [main](main.md#a14-03-proteus-done)

**Telemachus**: He is alive! I must go home and tell my mum!  
**Menelaus**: Take a gift: a silver bowl with a gold rim.  
**Menelaus**: A guest always goes home with a gift.  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.His.2.K-2, notice): hosts long ago gave guests gifts  
> _In Greece long ago, a host gave a guest a gift._  
> _It showed they cared. Gifts can say, we are friends._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that hosts long ago gave guests gifts to show they cared._


#### `sprout:a14-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a14-03-complete)

_Two more days in the chariot, and you ride back to the ship._  
_Helen waves from the gate until you are far away._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.2, ask): add two and two  
> _Two days riding there. Two days riding back. How many days is that?_  
> - ✅ Four → Yes! 2 and 2 make 4. (Hold up four fingers.)
> - Two → That is only one way. Two more days back make four.
> _For grown-ups: Added 2 + 2 to find 4 days._

_Telemachus stands at the front of the ship._  
_Then the atlas turns its page. You are somewhere new._  

## A14-4. Calypso’s Island

- `a14-04-s1`: Walk to Calypso’s cave. · _Follow the young man with golden sandals._
- `a14-04-s2`: Find Odysseus on the shore. · _Go down to the rocks above the beach._
- `a14-04-s3`: Help him build the raft. · _Carry the pegs to the shore._

#### `sprout:a14-04-ogygia` · [main](main.md#a14-04-ogygia)

_A quiet island far across the sea._  
_There are trees, and four springs, and soft green meadows._  
_Owls and sea birds nest in the trees._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS3-1, ask): animals live where they find what they need  
> _Birds nest in these trees. Why do birds like this island?_  
> - ✅ It has water, food and trees → Yes. Animals live where they find water, food and a safe home.
> - It has no trees → There are lots of trees! Birds nest in trees. They need a safe home.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that animals live where they find water, food and shelter._

_A young man with golden sandals lands beside you._  
**Hermes**: I am Hermes. I carry messages. Walk with me.  
_In a cave, a woman sits weaving and singing._  
_She is Calypso. She gives you bread and figs to eat._  
**Hermes**: Zeus says: let the man go home.  
**Calypso**: I am sad. But I will let him go.  

**Choices**

- **Thank Calypso for the figs.**
    - **Calypso**: You are welcome, little guest.
- **Ask Hermes where Odysseus is.**
    - **Hermes**: On the shore. He is always on the shore.

#### `sprout:a14-04-ogygia-done` · [main](main.md#a14-04-ogygia-done)

_Hermes goes away over the sea._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.His.2.K-2, notice): in old Greek stories, Hermes carried messages  
> _In the old Greek stories, Hermes carried messages for the king of the gods._  
> _Some people in Greece still honour these gods today._  
> _We tell their stories with care._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that in old Greek stories Hermes carried messages, and that some people still honour these gods._

_Calypso puts down her loom. She goes to find Odysseus._  

#### `sprout:a14-04-shore` · [main](main.md#a14-04-shore)

_A man sits on a rock above the beach._  
_He looks at the sea. He is crying, very quietly._  
**Odysseus**: I miss my home. I miss it so much.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): name homesickness and say what helps  
> _Odysseus misses home. That feeling is called homesick. What can help?_  
> - ✅ Talk about home with a friend → Yes. Sharing a feeling makes it lighter. A hug helps too.
> - Hide the feeling → Hiding a feeling can make it heavier. Talking helps.
> _For grown-ups: Named the feeling of being homesick and talked about what helps._

**Odysseus**: Every morning I come here and look at the sea.  
_Your Nubbin sits beside him and looks at the sea too._  

**Choices**

- **Sit with him and say nothing.**
    - You sit together. The sea goes in and out.
- **Ask him about home.**
    - **Odysseus**: My son was a baby. Now he is a man.
- **Tell him you met his son.**
    - **Odysseus**: My son? Is he tall? Tell me!

#### `sprout:a14-04-shore-done` · [main](main.md#a14-04-shore-done)

**Calypso**: Do not cry. I will let you go.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.A.1, do): count to seven on your fingers

> _(Count to seven on your fingers together.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:years:7`
> _For grown-ups: Counted to seven on fingers._

**Calypso**: Build a raft. I will give you food and a fair wind.  
**Odysseus**: Promise it, please.  
**Calypso**: I promise. I am sorry you are sad.  

#### `sprout:a14-04-raft` · [main](main.md#a14-04-raft)

_Odysseus cuts down trees with a bronze axe._  
_You carry the pegs. Your Nubbin carries one, so proudly._  
_He works four days. Now there is a raft with a sail._  
**Calypso**: Stay with me, and nothing bad will ever happen.  
**Odysseus**: I want to go home. My family is there.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): home is where people who love you are  
> _Odysseus could stay safe here, but he wants to go home. Why?_  
> - ✅ His family is there → Yes. Home is where the people who love us are.
> - He is bored → Not bored. He misses his family, and his family misses him.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about why home matters: the people who love us._

**Odysseus**: Would you stay, little guest?  

**Choices**

- **Say, “No. Home.”**
    - **Odysseus**: Home. Yes! Home is where I want to be.
- **Say, “I do not know.”**
    - **Odysseus**: That is an honest answer.
- **Say, “Not if nothing ever happens.”**
    - **Odysseus**: A safe cave is nice. But I want to be happy, with my family.

#### `sprout:a14-04-raft-done` · [main](main.md#a14-04-raft-done)

> **Lesson** (science, 1-ESS1-1, notice): stars shine at night

> _Sailors use stars to find their way. (Point up and say: twinkle, twinkle.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that stars shine at night and that sailors steer by them._

_You watch the island get small, and then it is gone._

## A14-5. The Raft

- `a14-05-s1`: Hold on in the storm. · _Hold your plank, and keep your Nubbin close._
- `a14-05-s2`: Swim to the shore. · _Swim along the coast to the river._
- `a14-05-s3`: Find a dry place to sleep. · _Climb up to the two olive bushes._

#### `sprout:a14-05-storm` · [main](main.md#a14-05-storm)

_A big storm comes. The wind howls._  
_A big wave hits the raft. The mast snaps._  
_You hold on to a plank. Your Nubbin holds on to you._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, do): wood floats on water  
> _Your plank is wood, and wood floats!_  
> _(Pretend: wood floats, a stone sinks. Show with your hands.)_  
> Check: `edu:sort:float-or-sink:wood`
> _For grown-ups: Learned that wood floats and a stone sinks._

_A white sea bird lands on the raft. She is a kind goddess._  
**Ino**: Here, take my scarf. It will help you float.  
**Odysseus**: I will hold on to the raft, and trust her too.  

**Choices**

- **Say, “She means it. Take it.”**
    - **Odysseus**: Maybe. I will hold on to the raft too.
- **Hold on, and say nothing.**
    - You hold on tight. So does your Nubbin.
