# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A15: The Humbug

## A15-1. The Grey Prairie

- `a15-01-s1`: Step into the grey page and find the little house. · _Touch the page. Then walk across the flat grass to the one house._
- `a15-01-s2`: Help Uncle Henry and Aunt Em get ready for the storm. · _Look at the dark sky. Then help at the little door in the floor._
- `a15-01-s3`: Ride the storm in the house. Then step out of the page. · _Hold on to your Nubbin. When the page curls, step through._

#### `sprout:a15-01-page` · [main](main.md#a15-01-page)

_Nova’s book opens at a grey page._  
_Grey grass. Grey sky._  
_One little grey house._  
_Long ago the house was painted. The sun and rain rubbed it off._  
_You step in. The dry grass goes crunch, crunch._  
_There are no hills. No trees._  
_Just sky._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, ask): flat land is called a prairie  
> _Look all around. Is this land flat, or bumpy with hills?_  
> - ✅ Flat → Yes! Flat all the way to the sky. Wide, flat, grassy land is a prairie.
> - Bumpy → Look again. No hills here! It is flat. Flat grassland is a prairie.
> _For grown-ups: Described a place: a prairie is wide, flat, grassy land._

_A small black dog runs up. Woof, woof!_  
**Dorothy**: I am Dorothy. This is Toto.  
_Toto sniffs your Nubbin. Your Nubbin sniffs Toto._  

**Choices**

- **Tell her your name.**
    - **Dorothy**: What a nice name!
- **Ask why everything is grey.**
    - **Dorothy**: The sun and wind are rough on it.
- **Let your Nubbin play with Toto.**
    - They race round you and tumble in the dust. Giggle!

#### `sprout:a15-01-page-done` · [main](main.md#a15-01-page-done)

**Dorothy**: Come and meet Aunt Em. Follow me and Toto!  
_Dorothy’s dress is pale. The grass is brown._  
_The house is grey._  
_Toto starts with the sound t. Say it: t, t, Toto!_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Literacy, do): first sound of a name  
> _(Say t, t, Toto together. Then think of another t word: tree, toe, ten.)_  
> Check: `edu:say:first-sound:t`
> _For grown-ups: Practised hearing and saying the first sound of a name._


#### `sprout:a15-01-storm` · [main](main.md#a15-01-storm)

_Dark clouds roll in. The wind goes whoo, whoo!_  
**Uncle Henry**: A big storm is coming, Em!  
_The grass bends flat. Whoosh!_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): wind is moving air  
> _What do we call air that moves? (Point to the bending grass.)_  
> - ✅ Wind → Yes! Wind is air that moves. It bends the grass.
> - Rain → Rain falls from clouds. Air that moves is wind. Whoo!
> - Snow → Snow is cold and white. Air that moves is wind. Whoo!
> _For grown-ups: Named wind as moving air and saw what it does._

**Aunt Em**: Dorothy! Into the cellar, now!  
_A cellar is a little room under the floor._  
_Toto hides under the bed. Dorothy kneels down for him._  

**Choices**

- **Hold the trap door open for her.**
    - You hold it tight. The floor shakes.
- **Reach under the bed for Toto.**
    - You grab his collar and pass him up. Safe!
- **Grab your Nubbin first.**
    - Your Nubbin is in your arms. It is shaking.

#### `sprout:a15-01-storm-done` · [main](main.md#a15-01-storm-done)

_The wind screams. The house shakes._  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, notice): go to a safe place in a storm  
> **Aunt Em**: When a big storm comes, we go to a safe place inside.  
> **Aunt Em**: A cellar is safe. So is a room with no windows.  
> _(Ask your grown-up: where is our safe place?)_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that in a big storm we go to a safe place inside, and talked about ours._

_Then the house turns round and round._  
_It lifts up, up, up, like a balloon._  
**Dorothy**: Hold tight, Toto!  

#### `sprout:a15-01-cyclone` · [main](main.md#a15-01-cyclone)

_It rocks like a cradle. Rock, rock, rock._  
_Toto peeks out of the trap door. Uh-oh!_  
_Dorothy catches him and pulls him back._  
**Dorothy**: That is enough adventure for one little dog.  
_Dorothy lies down to sleep. Toto cuddles close._

> _(Count on your fingers. One, two, three, four!)_  
> Check: `edu:count:travellers:4`
> _For grown-ups: Counted four travellers, matching one number to each._

_A gap opens in the page. You see green grass!_  

**Choices**

- **Step through the gap with your Nubbin.**
    - You step out. Bump!
    - The house lands behind you.
- **Take a last look at Dorothy, asleep.**
    - She sleeps with one arm round Toto. You step through.

#### `sprout:a15-01-cyclone-done` · [main](main.md#a15-01-cyclone-done)

_You land on soft green grass._  
_Everything is bright. Green trees._  
_A blue brook. Singing birds._  
_Your Nubbin rolls over and over. Wheee!_  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): naming a feeling about something new  
> _Dorothy lands in a brand new place. How might she feel?_  
> - ✅ Surprised → Yes! Surprised, and maybe a bit scared. New places can feel big.
> - Bored → Not bored! Her eyes go wide. A new place is a big surprise.
> _For grown-ups: Named a feeling a character might have in a new place._

## A15-2. The Council with the Munchkins

- `a15-02-s1`: Say hello to the little people and the old woman in white. · _Walk round the house to where they are waiting._
- `a15-02-s2`: Ask the way home. Watch the old woman’s hat. · _Watch the hat while she counts._
- `a15-02-s3`: Help Boq lay the tables for the night. · _Carry the blue dishes from the shelf to the long tables._

#### `sprout:a15-02-council` · [main](main.md#a15-02-council)

_Little people in blue gather round. Their hats have tiny bells._  
_An old woman in white bows to Dorothy._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, ask): finding east on a map  
> _The Munchkins live in the east. The sun comes up in the east._  
> _Which way is east?_  
> - ✅ Where the sun comes up → Yes! East is where the sun comes up. The Munchkins are that way.
> - Where it goes to bed → That is west. East is where the sun wakes up!
> _For grown-ups: Linked east with sunrise on a story map._

**The Witch of the North**: Welcome, my dear. You have set these people free.  
**Dorothy**: I did not do anything!  
**The Witch of the North**: Your house did. Look under the corner.  
_Only two silver shoes are left._  
_The very old Witch has dried up, like a puddle in the sun._  
**A Munchkin**: We are free! Hooray!  
**The Witch of the North**: The silver shoes are yours now.  

**Choices**

- **Tell Dorothy to try them on.**
    - **Dorothy**: They fit exactly!
- **Tell her to be careful with them.**
    - **The Witch of the North**: Wise. Know a charm before you use it.
- **Let your Nubbin sniff the shoes.**
    - Sniff, sniff, achoo! Your Nubbin sits on them.

#### `sprout:a15-02-council-done` · [main](main.md#a15-02-council-done)

**Dorothy**: Please, can you help me get home to Aunt Em?  
**Dorothy**: She will be so worried.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): asking for help when you are worried  
> _Dorothy is worried. What does she do?_  
> - ✅ Asks for help → Yes. Asking is brave and it helps. Now we can work it out.
> - Hides → Hiding keeps the worry big. Asking helps, and Dorothy asked.
> _For grown-ups: Saw that asking for help is a good way to deal with a worry._

**The Witch of the North**: Ah. That is hard.  
**The Witch of the North**: Let me think.  

#### `sprout:a15-02-slate` · [main](main.md#a15-02-slate)

**A Munchkin**: Desert to the east. Desert to the west.  
**A Munchkin**: Nobody crosses.  
_The old woman takes off her hat. She counts: one, two, three._

_They tell Dorothy to go to the Emerald City._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Literacy, do): first letter of a word

> _(Say eh, eh, Emerald. It starts with the letter E.)_  
> Check: `edu:say:first-sound:e`
> _For grown-ups: Heard the first sound of Emerald and met the letter E._

**The Witch of the North**: Keep to the road of yellow bricks. It will take you there.  
_She kisses Dorothy on the forehead. A round mark shines._  

**Choices**

- **Ask if she will kiss you too.**
    - **The Witch of the North**: You carry your own luck, visitor. Share it.
- **Look closely at the mark.**
    - It shines softly, like a coin in a well.
- **Hold your Nubbin up to say goodbye.**
    - She taps its nose. It goes cross-eyed, proud as can be.

#### `sprout:a15-02-slate-done` · [main](main.md#a15-02-slate-done)

_She spins round three times on her left heel._  
_Pop! She is gone._  
_Toto barks at the empty place._  
_Tinkle, tinkle. The tiny hat bells ring._  

> **Lesson** (music, ELOF: Approaches to Learning, do): steady beat  
> _The bells go tinkle, tinkle, tinkle, tinkle._  
> _(Clap a steady beat with them. Clap, clap, clap, clap!)_  
> Check: `edu:clap:beat:bells`
> _For grown-ups: Clapped a steady beat along with a sound._


#### `sprout:a15-02-boq` · [main](main.md#a15-02-boq)

_You walk along the yellow road. Evening comes._  
_A big house. A big feast._  
_Boq waves hello._

**Dorothy**: I am only Dorothy.  
**Boq**: Then you are a kind witch. That is best.

> _(Point to each table. Say one number for each.)_

_You carry dishes to the tables. Plates, cups, jugs._  

**Choices**

#### `sprout:a15-02-boq-done` · [main](main.md#a15-02-boq-done)

> - The cups → Look again. The cups are the paler ones. The plates are darker.
> _For grown-ups: Compared two shades of one colour: darker and paler._

_You sit with Boq on his step. The yellow road glows._  
**Boq**: It is a long way. But she has the kiss, the shoes, and you.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): what helps a friend on a long trip  
> _Dorothy has a long way to go. What helps most?_  
> - ✅ Friends who go with her → Yes. A long road is lighter with friends beside you.
> - Going alone → Alone is harder. Friends make a long road feel short.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about how friends help on a long, hard journey._

_Your Nubbin curls up next to the silver shoes._  

## A15-3. The Scarecrow

- `a15-03-s1`: Help lift the Scarecrow down from his pole. · _Climb into the cornfield. Hold the pole steady._
- `a15-03-s2`: Help the Scarecrow find his feet. · _Walk beside him on the yellow bricks. Catch him if he tips._
- `a15-03-s3`: Sit by the road and listen to Dorothy talk about Kansas. · _Sit on the bank with them while Dorothy eats her bread._

#### `sprout:a15-03-pole` · [main](main.md#a15-03-pole)

_A big cornfield. High on a pole stands a Scarecrow._  
_His head is a sack stuffed with straw. He wears a blue hat._  

> **Lesson** (science, 2-PS1-1, ask): straw is light and soft  
> _The Scarecrow is made of straw._  
> _Is straw heavy and hard, or light and soft?_  
> - ✅ Light and soft → Yes! Straw is light and soft. That is why he is easy to lift.
> - Heavy and hard → Not straw! Rocks are heavy and hard. Straw is light and soft.
> _For grown-ups: Described a material: straw is light and soft._

_He winks!_  
**The Scarecrow**: Good morning. Would you lift me down, please?  
_Someone must hold the pole steady._  

**Choices**

- **Hold the pole steady.**
    - You hold tight. Dorothy lifts him down.
- **Stand underneath, ready to catch him.**
    - Flump! He lands on you.
    - Like a big, soft pillow.

#### `sprout:a15-03-pole-done` · [main](main.md#a15-03-pole-done)

**The Scarecrow**: Thank you! I feel much more like a person.  
_He stretches. He yawns._  
_He falls flat on his face!_  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): balance  
> **The Scarecrow**: Walking is hard at first. Try standing on one foot.  
> _(Stand on one foot and count to 3. Then switch feet!)_  
> Check: `edu:stand:one-foot:balance`
> _For grown-ups: Practised balancing on one foot, as the Scarecrow learns to stand._

_He gets up. He falls over sideways._  
_Flop!_  

#### `sprout:a15-03-feet` · [main](main.md#a15-03-feet)

_The Scarecrow has never walked. His legs do their own thing._  
**Dorothy**: Come with me to the Emerald City. Oz may give you brains.  
**The Scarecrow**: Then I shall come. What a comfort!  
_Step. Step._  
_A hole! Plop!_  
_He falls down._  
_Step. Step._  
_Another hole! Plop!_  
_Then a brick gets in the way. Bump!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 3  
> _How many times did he fall? Plop, plop, bump._  
> _(Count: one, two, three!)_  
> Check: `edu:count:falls:3`
> _For grown-ups: Counted three falls, one number for each._


**Choices**

- **Offer him your arm.**
    - **The Scarecrow**: An arm! Just the thing.
- **Walk ahead and call out the holes.**
    - Each time you call “Hole!”, he steps round it.
- **Let your Nubbin lead him.**
    - Your Nubbin walks slowly. He copies every step.

#### `sprout:a15-03-feet-done` · [main](main.md#a15-03-feet-done)

_First he can only fall._  
_Next he can take small steps._  
_Last he can walk in a nearly straight line._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, ask): first, next, last  
> _What did the Scarecrow do last?_  
> - ✅ Walked nearly straight → Yes! First he fell, next he stepped, last he walked.
> - Fell down → That was first! Last, he walked in a nearly straight line.
> _For grown-ups: Put three story events in order: first, next, last._


#### `sprout:a15-03-kansas` · [main](main.md#a15-03-kansas)

_You sit on the bank. Dorothy shares her bread._  
**The Scarecrow**: Tell me about your home. Kansas?  
**Dorothy**: It is grey. The ground, the house, the sky.  
**The Scarecrow**: Here there is green. Why go back?  
**Dorothy**: Even if home is plain, it is where my people are.  
**Dorothy**: Nowhere else is home.  
**The Scarecrow**: I see. Home is where the people you love are.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): what makes a place feel like home  
> _What makes a place feel like home to you? (Wait for an answer.)_  
> - ✅ The people there → Yes. Home is the people you love, not the paint.
> - The things in it → Things are nice. But the people matter most.
> - The smell of food → A lovely answer. Food made with love feels like home.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about what makes a place feel like home._


**Choices**

- **Tell him what you miss about home.**
    - **The Scarecrow**: Everyone has a place to miss.
- **Ask him where his home is.**
    - **The Scarecrow**: A pole in a field. I do not miss it at all.
- **Watch your Nubbin, asleep in Dorothy’s lap.**
    - Your Nubbin sleeps in her lap. It has found a home.

#### `sprout:a15-03-kansas-done` · [main](main.md#a15-03-kansas-done)

_The road goes on. The corn fields fade away._  
_Now there are trees on both sides._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, ask): plants grow in different places  
> _Corn grows in open fields. Which grows in the woods?_  
> - ✅ Trees → Yes! Lots of trees grow in woods.
> - Corn rows → Corn likes open fields. Woods have trees.
> _For grown-ups: Noticed that different plants grow in different places._

_The Scarecrow walks between you, nearly straight._  

#### `sprout:a15-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a15-03-complete)

**The Scarecrow**: Shall I tell you a secret?  
**The Scarecrow**: I am afraid of one thing. A lit match.

> **The Scarecrow**: Fire is hot and can hurt.  
> **The Scarecrow**: If you ever find matches, do not touch. Tell a grown-up.  
> _For grown-ups: Learned to leave matches alone and tell a grown-up._

_You make sure he stands far away from the campfire._  
_That night he stands guard. It is the most useful he has felt._  

## A15-4. The Tin Woodman

- `a15-04-s1`: Follow the groaning sound into the forest. · _Leave the road where the trees are thickest. Listen._
- `a15-04-s2`: Find the oil-can in the Woodman’s cottage. · _Look on the shelf for the can with the long thin spout._
- `a15-04-s3`: Oil the Woodman’s joints, one after another. · _Start with his neck. Then his arms. Then his legs._

#### `sprout:a15-04-groan` · [main](main.md#a15-04-groan)

_In the forest, someone groans. Mmmm._  
**Dorothy**: Who is that?  
_A man made of tin stands still. His axe is up high._  
**The Tin Woodman**: Mmm. I got wet in the rain.  
**The Tin Woodman**: Now I am stuck.  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): rain can make metal rusty  
> _Rain made his joints rusty. What can make metal rusty?_  
> _Rain or sunshine?_  
> - ✅ Rain → Yes. Water on metal makes rust. Rusty parts get stuck.
> - Sunshine → Not sunshine. Wet makes rust. Rusty parts get stuck.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that water can make iron rusty, and rusty parts stick._

**The Tin Woodman**: Please oil me. The oil-can is in my cottage.  

**Choices**

- **Say you will fetch it.**
    - **The Tin Woodman**: Thank you. Mind the step.
- **Ask how it happened.**
    - **The Tin Woodman**: The rain. I forgot to shelter.
    - **The Tin Woodman**: That is all.

#### `sprout:a15-04-groan-done` · [main](main.md#a15-04-groan-done)

_The cottage is just through the trees._  
_You hurry. Your Nubbin is at your heels._  
**The Tin Woodman**: Thank you for stopping to look.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): helping a friend who is stuck  
> _The Woodman was stuck for a year. You stopped to help._  
> _What do we do for a friend who is stuck?_  
> - ✅ Stop and help → Yes! Stopping to help is kind.
> - Walk past → Walking past leaves them stuck. Stopping to help is kind.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about stopping to help someone who is stuck._


#### `sprout:a15-04-cottage` · [main](main.md#a15-04-cottage)

_The cottage is small and tidy._  
_On a shelf stand three oil-cans._  
_One is big and square. One is small and round._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): biggest and smallest  
> _Point to the biggest oil-can._  
> - ✅ The big square one → Yes! The big square can is the biggest.
> - The small round one → That one is the smallest. The big square can is biggest.
> _For grown-ups: Compared sizes: biggest and smallest._

_One has a long thin spout._  
_Your Nubbin looks up at them._  

**Choices**

- **Take the one with the long spout.**
    - It will reach the joints. It sloshes.
    - It is full.
- **Take the big square one, to be sure.**
    - The big one is empty. You take the full one.
- **Look at the picture a little longer.**
    - A girl, drawn in pencil, smiles. You take the can and go.

#### `sprout:a15-04-cottage-done` · [main](main.md#a15-04-cottage-done)

_You run back through the trees._  
_You still see the pencil picture in your mind._  
_The girl in it has a kind face._  

> **Lesson** (art, ELOF: Approaches to Learning, do): drawing a face  
> _Draw a face like the one in the picture._  
> _Two eyes, a nose, a smile._  
> Check: `edu:draw:face:smile`
> _For grown-ups: Drew a simple face, naming the parts._


#### `sprout:a15-04-oil` · [main](main.md#a15-04-oil)

**Dorothy**: Where shall we start?  
_You start with his neck. Drip, drip._  
_His head turns. Creak, creak._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-2-ETS1-1, ask): oil helps parts slide  
> _The joints are stuck. What does the oil do?_  
> - ✅ Helps them slide → Yes! Oil helps parts slide, so they can move.
> - Makes them stickier → The other way! Oil helps parts slide.
> _For grown-ups: Saw how oil can fix a stuck joint._

_Then his arms. They go down at last._  
**The Tin Woodman**: Ahh. Thank you.  
**The Tin Woodman**: Now my legs, please.  

**Choices**

- **Do his legs.**
    - Drip, drip. He takes three steps for fun.
- **Give his jaw another drop first.**
    - **The Tin Woodman**: Ah. Much better.
- **Oil everything you can find, twice.**
    - He shines like a new kettle!

#### `sprout:a15-04-oil-done` · [main](main.md#a15-04-oil-done)

_First you oiled his neck._  
_Next you oiled his arms._  
_Last you oiled his legs._  
**The Tin Woodman**: Thank you. One more year and I would be a statue.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, ask): first, next, last  
> _What did you oil first?_  
> - ✅ His neck → Yes! First the neck, next the arms, last the legs.
> - His legs → Legs came last. First was the neck.
> _For grown-ups: Retold three steps in order._


#### `sprout:a15-04-complete` · [main](main.md#a15-04-complete)

**The Tin Woodman**: Where are you all going?  
**Dorothy**: To the Emerald City, to see Oz.  
**The Tin Woodman**: May I come? I want a heart.  
**The Scarecrow**: I want brains.  
_The Woodman taps his chest. Clong._  
_It is empty._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): being kind  
> **The Tin Woodman**: A heart helps me be kind. What is one kind thing to do?  
> - ✅ Help a friend → Yes! Helping is kind.
> - ✅ Share a snack → Yes! Sharing is kind too.
> - Take a toy → That makes people sad. Helping and sharing are kind.
> _For grown-ups: Named kind actions._

_Dorothy hands you the oil-can. It is yours to carry now._  

## A15-5. The Cowardly Lion

- `a15-05-s1`: Walk on through the deep forest. · _Stay together on the yellow bricks._
- `a15-05-s2`: Let your Nubbin decide about the Lion, in its own time. · _Sit by the road and wait. Do not push your Nubbin._

#### `sprout:a15-05-roar` · [main](main.md#a15-05-roar)

_The woods are dim. Something growls far away._  
_Your Nubbin walks so close that you tread on it._  
_ROAR! A big Lion leaps out._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): lions roar to talk to each other  
> _Lions live in families called prides._  
> _A lion roars to call to its family. A roar is very loud._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that lions live in prides and roar to call to each other._

_He bats the Scarecrow. Flump!_  
_He scratches the Woodman. Ting!_
