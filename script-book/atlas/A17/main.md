### Step 1: Step into the summer page and find Granny at the bus stop.

Hint: Touch the page, then walk down the country road to the bus stop. · check: `explore:a17-road:bus-stop`

#### <a id="a17-01-road"></a>Scene `a17-01-road`

_Nova’s atlas falls open at a country road in summer. There is a bus stop, green rice fields, and a tree on a hill._  
_The tree is drawn so big that it runs off the top of the page._  
_Then the page is warm under your hand, and it hums. It is cicadas, thousands of them, and you are standing in the road._  
_An old woman is waiting at the bus stop with a cloth bundle and a straw hat. She looks you up and down, and your Nubbin too._  
**Granny**: You’ll be the one who’s stopping with me for the summer. Good. There’s plenty to do.  
**Granny**: And that’s your little one, is it? It can sleep in the kitchen. It looks like it eats.  
_A boy in a vest is sitting on the fence behind her, pretending not to look at you. He is looking at you._  
**Granny**: That’s Kanta. My grandson. Say hello, Kanta.  
**Kanta**: …lo.  
_Down the road comes a small three-wheeled lorry, piled with furniture. Two girls are in the back, waving at everything._  

**Choices**

- **Wave back.**
    - You wave. The smaller girl waves with both arms, so hard she nearly falls out, and the bigger one grabs her dress.
- **Ask Granny who they are.**
    - **Granny**: The new family for the old house. A professor and his two girls. Their mother’s in the hospital over the hill.
- **Ask Kanta who they are.**
    - **Kanta**: Dunno.
    - He knows. He watches the lorry all the way to the turning, and goes red when you notice.

#### <a id="a17-01-road-done"></a>Done `a17-01-road-done`

**Granny**: Go on, then. They’ll want hands for all that. I’ll have supper on.  
_Your Nubbin is already trotting after the lorry, in the dust._  

### Step 2: Help the Kusakabes unload the lorry at the old house.

Hint: Carry the boxes from the lorry to the step, and take your boots off before you go in. · check: `carry:a17-boxes:old-house`

#### <a id="a17-01-unload"></a>Scene `a17-01-unload`

_The old house sits back from the road behind a hedge: a wooden house, a little tall, with a porch at one side._  
_A man in round glasses is lifting a chest off the lorry. He gives you the other end of it as if he’d been expecting you._  
**Mr Kusakabe**: Oh, hello! Are you from next door? Wonderful. Mind the step, it bites.  
_At the step everybody slips their shoes off and lines them up, and so do you. Your Nubbin has no shoes, but it waits anyway._  
_Inside it smells of dust and old wood. The girls run from room to room, sliding every door open as they go._  
**Satsuki** _(happy)_: Mei! The porch post is rotten! Look, it wobbles!  
**Mei** _(happy)_: It’s rotten! It’s going to fall down!  
_They push the post together, shrieking, and then run off, because that is the best thing to do with a rotten post._  
_Something drops off the porch roof and bounces at your feet: an acorn. Then another, from nowhere._  
**Mei**: Acorns! Onee-chan, there are acorns!  
_There are no oaks anywhere near the house. Your Nubbin looks up at the roof, and keeps looking._  

**Choices**

- **Put the acorn in your pocket.**
    - It sits in your pocket all afternoon, small and smooth. Every so often you check it’s still there.
- **Give it to Mei.**
    - **Mei**: Thank you! Now I’ve got two.
    - She puts both in her pocket and looks up at the roof, the same way your Nubbin does.
- **Leave it where it fell.**
    - You leave it on the step. When you come back with the next box, it’s gone.

#### <a id="a17-01-unload-done"></a>Done `a17-01-unload-done`

**Mr Kusakabe**: That’s the last of it. Thank you. Now, Satsuki, Mei, which of you wants to find the well?  
_Both of them do, at once, and so does your Nubbin._  

### Step 3: Look at the great tree on the hill with the family.

Hint: Stand in the back garden and look up the hill behind the house. · check: `watch:a17-camphor:hill`

#### <a id="a17-01-tree"></a>Scene `a17-01-tree`

_Behind the house the ground rises into woods, and out of the woods rises the tree._  
_It is the biggest thing you have ever seen. Not the biggest tree: the biggest thing._  
**Satsuki**: Dad, what kind of tree is that?  
**Mr Kusakabe**: A camphor. Kusunoki. It must be very old. Older than the house, older than the village, I should think.  
**Mei**: It’s the biggest in the world.  
**Mr Kusakabe**: Very possibly, Mei.  
_The wind comes down the hill, and the whole tree moves at once, slowly, like something breathing._  
_Your Nubbin sits down in the grass and looks at it with its mouth a little open._

## A17-2. Soot Sprites

*My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988): the soot sprites in the old house* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Camphor Tree page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A17-1, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A17-1

**Cast:** Satsuki (Satsuki Kusakabe, the elder sister, about eleven (My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988)); licensed design), Mei (Mei Kusakabe, the younger sister, four (My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988)); licensed design), Granny (Granny, the Kusakabes’ elderly neighbour (My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988)); licensed design), Kanta (Kanta Ōgaki, Granny’s grandson, about eleven (My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988)); licensed design)

### Step 1: Find the stairs up to the dark room at the top of the house.

Hint: The stairs are behind a plain door in the kitchen. Take a lantern up. · check: `find:a17-stairs:old-house`

#### <a id="a17-02-stairs"></a>Scene `a17-02-stairs`

**Satsuki**: There’s a window up top, but we can’t find any stairs. Will you help us look?  
_They aren’t in the hall, or behind any of the sliding doors. Your Nubbin finds them: behind a plain wooden door in the kitchen._  
_They are steep and narrow, and at the top there is only dark._  
**Mei**: You go first, Onee-chan.  
**Satsuki**: No, you.  
_In the end they go up together, side by side, which the stairs are not wide enough for. You follow with the lantern._  
_At the top something rustles. Dozens of small black things scatter from the light, like soot with somewhere to be._  
_They pour into the cracks in the boards, and are gone. Your Nubbin saw them before the lantern did._  
**Mei**: I’ll catch one!  

**Choices**

- **Hold the lantern high, so she can see.**
    - You lift the lantern. Mei slaps both hands flat on the boards, very pleased with herself, and holds them up.
- **Sit still on the top stair and wait.**
    - You sit down on the top stair, and the room goes quiet. After a while, one small black thing peeps out of a crack.
    - Mei pounces. She holds her hands up, very pleased with herself.

#### <a id="a17-02-stairs-done"></a>Done `a17-02-stairs-done`

_Her hands are empty. Her palms are black, as if she had patted a stove._  
**Mei** _(happy)_: I got one! It got away. Look, I’ve got its black on me!  
**Satsuki**: Mei, your feet are black too!  

### Step 2: Ask Granny what the black things are.

Hint: Granny has come over to help clean. Find her on the porch with the rags. · check: `talk:a17-granny:old-house`

#### <a id="a17-02-granny"></a>Scene `a17-02-granny`

_Granny has come over with rags and a bucket, to help the family clean. Mei shows her the black hands and the black feet._  
**Mei**: Obaa-chan, there are black things in our house! Little round ones! They ran away!  
**Granny**: Soot sprites, were they? Well I never. I saw them myself, when I was your age.  
**Satsuki** _(shock)_: Really? Are they bad?  
**Granny**: Bad? No. They live in empty houses. They get into the dust and the dark, and they like it quiet.  
**Granny**: When people come back to a house, they move on. Quietly, one night, and nobody sees them go.  
**Granny**: So you laugh a lot, and live here properly, and they’ll take the hint. They don’t care for a cheerful house.  
**Mei**: I’ll laugh. I’m very good at it.  
_A face appears over the hedge: Kanta, with a basket his mother has sent. He holds it out without coming any closer._  
**Kanta**: Your house is haunted!  
_He drops the basket on the step and runs, as fast as he can go._  

**Choices**

- **Call after him: “Thank you for the basket!”**
    - Kanta runs faster. Granny shakes her head at the hedge.
    - **Granny**: He has manners. He keeps them somewhere safe.
- **Laugh. It’s a lovely haunted house.**
    - **Satsuki**: It is, isn’t it? I always wanted to live in a haunted house.
    - **Mei**: Me too! I wanted it first!
- **Say nothing and pick up the basket.**
    - Under the cloth there are rice cakes, still warm. Granny looks at them and sighs.
    - **Granny**: His mother sent those. He’ll have eaten one.

#### <a id="a17-02-granny-done"></a>Done `a17-02-granny-done`

**Granny**: Now. Rags. That floor won’t clean itself, and the sprites won’t do it either.  

### Complete

#### <a id="a17-02-complete"></a>Complete `a17-02-complete`

_You all clean until dusk. The girls run the rags down the long boards in a race, bottoms in the air._  
_Your Nubbin rinses the rags in the bucket by the hand pump, mostly by sitting in it._  
_Every so often, in a corner, something small and black watches you all. Nobody minds any more._  
**Granny**: There. A house with its windows open. That’s more like it.

## A17-3. The Bath in the Storm

*My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988): the windy night and the bath* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Camphor Tree page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A17-2, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A17-2

**Cast:** Mr Kusakabe (Tatsuo Kusakabe, the girls’ father, a university lecturer (My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988)); licensed design), Satsuki (Satsuki Kusakabe, the elder sister, about eleven (My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988)); licensed design), Mei (Mei Kusakabe, the younger sister, four (My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988)); licensed design), Granny (Granny, the Kusakabes’ elderly neighbour (My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988)); licensed design)

### Step 1: Help the family slide the rain shutters shut before the storm.

Hint: Granny sent you over. Pull each wooden shutter along its groove until the house is closed. · check: `carry:a17-shutters:old-house`

#### <a id="a17-03-shutters"></a>Scene `a17-03-shutters`

_In the evening the wind gets up. The rice fields go silver, then dark, as the gusts run across them._  
**Granny**: Go and help them with the shutters. A professor won’t know which way they slide.  
_At the old house, Mr Kusakabe is pulling at a rain shutter that is stuck halfway along its groove._  
**Mr Kusakabe**: Ah! Reinforcements. I think it slides. I think it slides this way. Or possibly the other.  
_You lift it a little and it runs, rattling, all the way along. Then the next one, and the next, until the house is shut._  
_The wind finds every gap anyway. The boards creak, and the house leans and groans like a ship._  
**Mei** _(sad)_: Dad, the house is going to fall down.  
**Mr Kusakabe**: It’s only stretching, Mei. Old houses do. It’s been sitting still for a long time.  

#### <a id="a17-03-shutters-done"></a>Done `a17-03-shutters-done`

**Mr Kusakabe**: Stay till the wind drops. Granny won’t mind. The bath’s nearly hot, and we’ve got plenty of tea.  
_Your Nubbin sits by the stove in the kitchen and watches the shutters jump._  

### Step 2: Wait in the main room while the family has their bath.

Hint: Sit by the low table. When Mr Kusakabe calls out, join in. · check: `wait:a17-bath:main-room`

#### <a id="a17-03-bath"></a>Scene `a17-03-bath`

_The family take their bath together in the bathhouse at the back, father and both girls in one deep wooden tub._  
_You wait in the main room with your Nubbin, by the low table. Steam drifts under the door._  
_A gust hits the house. Something bangs outside, and the lamp swings on its cord._  
**Mei** _(shock)_: Dad! Dad, I heard something!  
**Satsuki** _(shock)_: So did I. Is it the black things? Are they coming back?  
**Mr Kusakabe** _(think)_: Shall I tell you the best thing to do when you’re frightened?  
**Mr Kusakabe**: Laugh. As loud as you can. Then anything that’s listening will think we’re far too jolly to bother with.  
**Mr Kusakabe**: You too, out there! Everybody laugh!  
_From the bathhouse comes the biggest, silliest laugh you have ever heard from a grown man, and then two small ones._  

**Choices**

- **Laugh as loud as you can.**
    - You laugh as loud as you can. It sounds ridiculous, which makes you laugh properly.
- **Tickle your Nubbin until it squeaks.**
    - Your Nubbin squeaks and rolls, and you laugh at it, and from the bathhouse three people laugh at you laughing.
- **Try to laugh and produce a honk.**
    - What comes out is a honk. There is a silence in the bathhouse.
    - **Mei**: Do it again!

#### <a id="a17-03-bath-done"></a>Done `a17-03-bath-done`

_The wind blows on. Inside, nobody seems to mind it any more._  
_Mei comes out pink and steaming, wrapped in a towel, and falls asleep on the floor before anybody can stop her._  

### Step 3: Watch from Granny’s step when the wind drops.

Hint: Go home through the hedge, then sit on Granny’s step and look at the hill. · check: `watch:a17-sprites:camphor`

#### <a id="a17-03-moon"></a>Scene `a17-03-moon`

_By the time you go home, the wind has blown itself out. The moon is up, and the fields are wet and shining._  
_You sit on Granny’s step to take your boots off, and don’t get round to it._  
_Your Nubbin is looking at the old house. At the dark window at the top of the stairs._  
_Out of the eaves, something is drifting. A thread of black, thin as smoke, and then more._  
_A whole cloud of soot sprites rises over the roof in the moonlight, and floats away up the hill to the camphor tree._  
_In the old house, a window slides open. A small face looks out, then a bigger one beside it._  
_Satsuki sees you on the step. She doesn’t call out; she just lifts her hand._  

**Choices**

- **Lift your hand back.**
    - You lift your hand. Across the hedge, in the moonlight, two people who have seen the same thing agree to say nothing.
- **Point up the hill, at the tree.**
    - You point at the tree. Satsuki nods, very seriously, as if you have both signed something.

**Granny** _(soft)_: Still up? They’ve gone, have they? Good. They’ll find another empty place. There’s always one.

## A17-5. The Small Ones

*My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988): Mei and the two small spirits* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Camphor Tree page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A17-4, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A17-4

**Cast:** Mei (Mei Kusakabe, the younger sister, four (My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988)); licensed design), Mr Kusakabe (Tatsuo Kusakabe, the girls’ father, a university lecturer (My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988)); licensed design)

### Step 1: Keep an eye on Mei in the garden while her father works.

Hint: Sit at the edge of the garden. Mei will find something before you do. · check: `watch:a17-mei:garden`

#### <a id="a17-05-garden"></a>Scene `a17-05-garden`

_Satsuki is at school. Mr Kusakabe is at his desk in the room that opens on the garden, surrounded by books._  
**Mr Kusakabe**: Would you keep half an eye on Mei? I’m only in here. She’s no trouble. Mostly.  
_Mei is playing in the long grass with a tin bucket, picking up acorns. Your Nubbin is picking them up too, and giving them to her._  
_Then your Nubbin stops dead, with an acorn in its mouth. It is looking at the grass under the house._  
_Something small and white is walking through the grass. It is about the size of a cat, and you can see the grass through it._  
**Mei**: Oh!  
_Behind it comes a second one, bigger, and blue. It has a little sack over its shoulder, and acorns are dribbling out of a hole._  
_Your Nubbin is already following them, in the same careful way they walk._  
**Mei**: Wait! Wait for me!  

**Choices**

- **Call to Mr Kusakabe.**
    - **You**: Mr Kusakabe? Mei’s… following something.
    - **Mr Kusakabe**: Is she? Good for her. Don’t let her go out of the garden.
    - He doesn’t look up. Mei is already at the end of the garden.
- **Follow quietly. Don’t frighten them.**
    - You follow on tiptoe. The small ones look back, see Mei and your Nubbin, and walk a little faster.

#### <a id="a17-05-garden-done"></a>Done `a17-05-garden-done`

_The two small ones duck under the house and out the other side, into the hedge at the back of the garden._  
**Mei**: They went in there! There’s a tunnel!  

### Step 2: Crawl after Mei and your Nubbin through the tunnel in the hedge.

Hint: Get down on your elbows and go in after them. Don’t lose sight of Mei’s hat. · check: `explore:a17-hedge:tunnel`

#### <a id="a17-05-hedge"></a>Scene `a17-05-hedge`

**Cast:** Satsuki (Satsuki Kusakabe, the elder sister, about eleven (My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988)); licensed design), Kanta (Kanta Ōgaki, Granny’s grandson, about eleven (My Neighbor Totoro (Studio Ghibli, 1988)); licensed design)

### Step 1: Follow Satsuki through the hedge.

Hint: Run after her into the old house garden, and go in at the gap in the hedge. · check: `explore:a17-tunnel:camphor`

#### <a id="a17-14-tunnel"></a>Scene `a17-14-tunnel`

_Satsuki runs into the old house garden, straight at the hedge, and goes in at the gap on her hands and knees._  
_You go in after her. Last time this gap went nowhere; this time it is the tunnel, green and gold, going on and on._  
_It is in a hurry tonight. It seems to pull you along._  
**Satsuki**: Please. Please be there.  
_She comes out among the roots of the camphor tree and drops straight down into the hollow, on to something soft._  
_The great spirit opens one eye. Then both._  
**Satsuki** _(sad)_: Please help me. Mei’s lost. She went to find our mum, and it’s getting dark, and I can’t find her anywhere.  
_She is crying properly now, holding on to his fur with both hands._  
_The great spirit looks at her for a long moment. Then he grins, the widest grin in the world._  

**Choices**

- **Stand back. This is hers to ask.**
    - You stay on the root at the edge of the hollow. It is hers to ask, and he is listening to her.
- **Tell him your Nubbin is with Mei too.**
    - **You**: My Nubbin’s with her. Wherever she is.
    - He turns his great eyes to you and blinks once, slowly, as if he knew that already.

#### <a id="a17-14-tunnel-hunt"></a>Variant `a17-14-tunnel-hunt`

_Variant of `a17-14-tunnel`._

_Satsuki runs into the old house garden, straight at the hedge, and goes in at the gap on her hands and knees._  
_You dive in after her. For one moment it is the tunnel, green and gold, with Satsuki’s heels ahead of you._  
_Then the branches close in front of your face. It is a hedge, just a hedge, and you are stuck in it._  
_By the time you back out, scratched, there is no sound from the other side at all._  
_A bicycle bell rings in the lane. Kanta, on a bicycle much too big for him, stops with one foot down._  
**Kanta**: Where’s Satsuki? I saw her run in here.  
**You**: She’s gone to get help. Somewhere I can’t follow.  
**Kanta**: Then we look on our own. Get on. My uncle’s bike. He doesn’t know.  

**Choices**

- **Get on the back.**
    - You climb on the carrier with Kanta’s schoolbag digging into your knees, and he pushes off.
- **Look at the hedge once more.**
    - You look back at the hedge. You did go looking for the way in, and it remembers.

_Variant of `a17-14-tunnel-hunt`._

#### <a id="a17-14-tunnel-done"></a>Done `a17-14-tunnel-done`

### Step 2: See what the great spirit calls.

Hint: Hold on at the top of the tree and watch the woods. · check: `watch:a17-catbus:sign`

#### <a id="a17-14-call"></a>Scene `a17-14-call`

_At the very top of the camphor tree he lifts his head and roars. It rolls out over the valley like thunder._  
_Far off, over the woods, two yellow lights come bounding._  
_The Catbus comes running along the treetops, its legs a blur, and lands on the branch beside you with a thump._  
_Its door opens, and Satsuki climbs in. The sign up top turns, like a rolling drum, to a new word._  
_It is a name. MEI._  
**Satsuki** _(happy)_: It says Mei! It knows where she is!  
_The Catbus looks at you with its great grin. The door stays open._  

**Choices**

- **Climb aboard.**
    - You climb aboard. The seats are soft and warm and furry, and they purr.
- **Look back at the great spirit first.**
    - He is sitting on the branch, grinning, enormous against the evening. You climb aboard, and the seats purr.

#### <a id="a17-14-call-hunt"></a>Variant `a17-14-call-hunt`

_Variant of `a17-14-call`._

_From the hill behind you comes a roar. It rolls out over the whole valley like thunder._  
**Kanta** _(shock)_: What was that? Thunder? There’s no clouds.  
_A wind comes over the rice from behind you. The rice bends flat in a long line, as if something huge is running across it._  
_Two yellow lights go over your heads, along the power lines, and away towards the hills. Nobody in the fields looks up._  
**Kanta**: Did you see that? What did you see?  
_Up top, as it goes, there is a sign. You can just read it: MEI._  
**You**: That way. Follow it. Follow the wind.  

**Choices**

- **Point Kanta after the lights.**
    - **Kanta**: That’s the old road. Out by the statues. Hold on!
    - He stands on the pedals. The bicycle rattles like a tin of nails.
- **Tell Kanta what you saw.**
    - **Kanta**: A bus. Shaped like a cat. Running on the wires.
    - He doesn’t laugh. He turns the bicycle that way, and pedals.

_Variant of `a17-14-call-hunt`._

_Satsuki sits at the front with her face to the glass. You sit beside her and hold her hand, and she lets you._

## A17-15. The Roadside Statues

### Step 1: Find Mei.

Hint: Get down from the Catbus at the row of statues by the road. · check: `find:a17-mei:jizo-row`

#### <a id="a17-15-statues"></a>Scene `a17-15-statues`

_The Catbus comes down on a country road far out among the fields, where six small stone statues stand by the verge._  
_They are Jizo, in a row, each in a red bib. Someone has put fresh flowers at their feet._  
_Beside them, on the grass, sits Mei, with her knees up and her ear of corn in her lap. Your Nubbin is sitting next to her._  
**Satsuki**: Mei!  
**Mei** _(sad)_: Onee-chan!  
_Satsuki is out of the door before it is properly open. Mei gets up, and the sisters run into each other and hold on._  
**Mei** _(sad)_: I got lost. I’m sorry. I wanted to give Mum the corn.  
**Satsuki** _(sad)_: No, I’m sorry. I’m sorry I shouted. I’m so sorry, Mei.  
_Your Nubbin comes to you and leans on your leg, hard, as if it has been holding something heavy all afternoon._  

**Choices**

- **Stand back and let them have this.**
    - You stand back by the Catbus. The sisters hold on to each other for a long time, and nobody hurries them.
- **Pick your Nubbin up.**
    - You pick your Nubbin up. It is warm and dusty, and it has walked a very long way on very short legs.
- **Check the corn is all right.**
    - **Mei**: I held it the whole way. It’s not even bent.
    - It isn’t. She has carried it all afternoon like an egg.

### Step 1: Look in at the hospital window.

Hint: Keep still. Nobody inside must know you’re there. · check: `watch:a17-window:hospital`

#### <a id="a17-16-window"></a>Scene `a17-16-window`

_The Catbus settles in a tall pine outside the hospital, as quietly as a cat settling on a wall._  
_Through its window, and through the open window of the hospital, you can see the room and the bed._  
_Mrs Kusakabe is sitting up, and Mr Kusakabe is sitting beside her. He came straight from town, and still has his bag._  
_She is laughing at something he has said. She looks tired, but she is laughing._  
**Mrs Kusakabe** _(happy)_: You needn’t have come all this way. It’s only a cold. The doctor’s fussing.  
**Mr Kusakabe**: You’d have come, if it were me.  
**Mrs Kusakabe** _(soft)_: I just wanted to see the girls this weekend. That’s all. I wanted to see them so much.  
_In the Catbus, Satsuki has both hands over her mouth. Mei is smiling, with tears on her face, holding the corn._  
**Satsuki**: She’s laughing. She’s all right.  

**Choices**

- **Stay quiet. Let them look.**
    - You don’t make a sound, and neither does the Catbus. It holds its breath, which for a bus is a lot of breath.
- **Lift your Nubbin up so it can see.**
    - You lift your Nubbin to the glass. It looks for a long time at the lady who stroked its head without looking.

_The shade falls exactly where he used to sit._  
_The Nubbins have found it already. They are asleep in a heap in its shade, all of them, breathing slowly together._  
_Something drops through the leaves and lands in your hand. An acorn: the oak’s first._  
_You thread it on to the old string, where his used to be. It fits as if it had been measured._  
_You sit down on the flat stone a while, under the oak, and wait with them. It seems the right thing to do here._  

#### <a id="a17-grove-camphor"></a>Variant `a17-grove-camphor`

_You step out of Nova’s atlas into the Grove with a pot in your arms. In it is a young camphor tree, as high as your knee._  
_Its leaves are glossy and new, and they smell clean and sharp, like a medicine chest kept in a forest._  
_Your Nubbin, still in its leaf hat, sits down beside the pot with its chin on its knees._  
_It has learned how you wait for a tree. It means to be very good at it._
