# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A17: The Camphor Tree

## A17-1. The Old House

- `a17-01-s1`: Go into the summer page. Say hello to Granny at the bus stop. · _Touch the page. Then walk down the road to the bus stop._
- `a17-01-s2`: Help carry boxes at the old house. · _Take the boxes from the lorry to the step. Take your boots off at the door._
- `a17-01-s3`: Look at the big tree on the hill. · _Stand in the back garden and look up the hill._

#### `sprout:a17-01-road` · [main](main.md#a17-01-road)

_Nova’s atlas opens. You see a country road in summer._  
_A bus stop. Green rice fields._  
_A huge tree on a hill._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, notice): rice grows in watery fields called paddies; Japan is a country of islands  
> _Look at the green fields. This is rice._  
> _Rice grows with its feet in water. The fields are called paddies._  
> _This story is in Japan, a country made of islands._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that rice grows in flooded fields called paddies, and that Japan is a country of islands._

_Cicadas buzz. Zzzzz!_  
_You are standing in the road._  
_An old woman waits at the bus stop. She has a straw hat._  
**Granny**: You are the one who is staying with me. Good!  
_A boy sits on the fence. He is called Kanta._  
**Kanta**: …lo.  
_A lorry comes down the road. Two girls wave from the back._  

**Choices**

- **Wave back.**
    - You wave. The little girl waves with both arms.
- **Ask Granny who they are.**
    - **Granny**: The new family. Their mum is in hospital.
- **Ask Kanta who they are.**
    - **Kanta**: Dunno.
    - He does know. He goes pink.

#### `sprout:a17-01-road-done` · [main](main.md#a17-01-road-done)

**Granny**: Go on, then. They will want help.  
**Granny**: I will make supper.  
_The lorry is little and has wheels at the front and the back._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 3  
> _Count the wheels on the little lorry._  
> _(Point and count together: one, two, three.)_  
> _Three wheels. A car has four._  
> Check: `edu:count:lorry-wheels:3`
> _For grown-ups: Counted three wheels aloud, matching one number to each wheel._

_Your Nubbin trots after it in the dust._  

#### `sprout:a17-01-unload` · [main](main.md#a17-01-unload)

_The old house has a hedge and a little porch._  
**Mr Kusakabe**: Hello! Please mind the step.  
**Mr Kusakabe**: Thank you for helping.  
_At the step, everyone takes off their shoes. They put them in a line._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, notice): taking shoes off at the door is a way of showing respect and keeping the floor clean  
> _In many homes in Japan, shoes come off at the door._  
> _It keeps the floor clean. It is a way of being polite._  
> _(Where do shoes go in your house?)_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that taking shoes off at the door is a polite, ordinary custom in Japan._

_Your Nubbin has no shoes. It waits anyway._  
**Satsuki**: Mei! The post is wobbly!  
_Plop! An acorn bounces at your feet._  
_Plop! Another._  

**Choices**

- **Put the acorn in your pocket.**
    - It sits in your pocket, small and smooth.
- **Give it to Mei.**
    - **Mei**: Thank you! Now I have two!
- **Leave it where it fell.**
    - Later, it is gone.

#### `sprout:a17-01-unload-done` · [main](main.md#a17-01-unload-done)

**Mr Kusakabe**: That is the last box. Who wants to find the well?  
_Mei wants to. Satsuki wants to._  
_Your Nubbin wants to!_  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): taking turns when everyone wants to go first  
> _Three friends all want to go first. What can they do?_  
> - ✅ Take turns, one then the next. → Yes! Taking turns is fair. Everyone gets a go.
> - Push to the front. → Pushing makes someone sad. Taking turns is kinder.
> - Nobody goes. → The well is fun! Taking turns lets everyone have a go.
> _For grown-ups: Chose taking turns as a fair way for everyone to go first._


#### `sprout:a17-01-tree` · [main](main.md#a17-01-tree)

**Mr Kusakabe**: A camphor tree. It is very, very old.

> _A tree this big makes a cool, shady place._  
> _Birds, bugs and little animals can live in it._  
> _Its leaves stay green all year. They smell clean and sharp._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a big tree gives shade and homes to small animals._

_Your Nubbin sits down in the grass and looks up._

## A17-2. Soot Sprites

- `a17-02-s1`: Find the stairs to the dark room at the top of the house. · _The stairs are behind a plain door in the kitchen. Take a lantern up._
- `a17-02-s2`: Ask Granny about the little black things. · _Granny is on the porch with her rags._

#### `sprout:a17-02-stairs` · [main](main.md#a17-02-stairs)

**Satsuki**: Where are the stairs? Please will you help us look?  
_Your Nubbin finds a plain door in the kitchen._  
_Behind it, steep stairs. At the top, only dark._  
_You go up together with your lantern._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-PS4-2, ask): we see when there is light  
> _The stairs are dark. What helps you see?_  
> - ✅ A lantern, to make light. → Yes! We see things when there is light.
> - Closing my eyes. → Eyes shut means no seeing. A lantern makes light, so we can see.
> - Going very fast. → Fast feet make no light. A lantern makes light, so we can see.
> _For grown-ups: Chose light as the thing that lets us see in the dark._

_Scritch, scratch! Little black things run away from the light._  
**Mei**: I will catch one!  

**Choices**

- **Hold the lantern up high.**
    - Mei slaps her hands on the floor. Pat!
- **Sit still and wait.**
    - A black thing peeps out. Mei pounces.
    - Pat!

#### `sprout:a17-02-stairs-done` · [main](main.md#a17-02-stairs-done)

_Mei holds up her hands. They are empty._  
_But her hands are black. Her feet are black too._  

> **Lesson** (art, ELOF: Approaches to Learning, do): name a colour and find it around you  
> _Mei has black hands. What colour is that?_  
> _(Say it: black. Now find something black near you.)_  
> Check: `edu:name:colour:black`
> _For grown-ups: Named the colour black and found a black thing nearby._

**Mei**: I got its black on me!  

#### `sprout:a17-02-granny` · [main](main.md#a17-02-granny)

_Granny comes with rags and a bucket._  
**Mei**: Obaa-chan! There are black things in our house!  
**Granny**: Soot sprites. I saw them when I was little.  
**Granny**: They like quiet, dusty houses. A cheerful house makes them go.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, ask): a way to calm down when something feels a bit scary: laugh and share  
> **Granny**: Something feels a little scary. What can we do?  
> - ✅ Laugh and sing with a friend. → Yes! It makes scary things feel smaller.
> - Run away and hide alone. → Laughing with a friend helps a worry feel smaller.
> - Stay still and say nothing. → Telling a friend, or laughing together, helps more.
> _For grown-ups: Thought of calming ways to feel braver: laughing and being with someone._

**Mei**: I will laugh. I am good at it!  
_A boy looks over the hedge. It is Kanta._  
_He puts a basket on the step._  
**Kanta**: Your house is haunted!  
_He runs away, very fast. The basket stays._  

**Choices**

- **Call: “Thank you for the basket!”**
    - Kanta runs faster.
    - **Granny**: He has good manners. He keeps them hidden.
- **Laugh. It is a lovely house.**
    - **Satsuki**: It is! I always wanted one!
- **Pick up the basket.**
    - Inside are warm rice cakes.

#### `sprout:a17-02-granny-done` · [main](main.md#a17-02-granny-done)

**Granny**: Now, rags. Everybody gets one.  
_Granny, Satsuki, Mei and you. One rag each._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 4  
> _How many rags? Count them._  
> _Granny, Satsuki, Mei, you._  
> _(Count on your fingers: one, two, three, four.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:rags:4`
> _For grown-ups: Counted four rags, one for each person._

_Then the scrubbing begins. Swish, swish!_  

#### `sprout:a17-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a17-02-complete)

_You all clean until dusk. The girls race the rags down the floor._  
_Your Nubbin rinses a rag in the bucket. It sits in it._  
**Granny**: A house with its windows open. That is more like it.  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, notice): fresh air and a clean room help us stay well  
> **Granny**: Open windows let fresh air in.  
> **Granny**: Clean floors and fresh air help us feel well.  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that fresh air and a clean room help us stay healthy._


## A17-3. The Bath in the Storm

- `a17-03-s1`: Help shut the rain shutters before the storm. · _Pull each wooden shutter along until the house is closed._
- `a17-03-s2`: Wait in the main room while the family has their bath. · _Sit by the low table. Join in when Mr Kusakabe calls._
- `a17-03-s3`: Sit on Granny’s step and watch the hill. · _Go home through the hedge. Sit on the step and look up._

#### `sprout:a17-03-shutters` · [main](main.md#a17-03-shutters)

_The wind gets up. Whoosh!_  
_The rice fields go silver._  
**Granny**: Go and help with the shutters.  
**Mr Kusakabe**: One is stuck. Can you help?  
_You lift it a little. Slide!_  
_It runs all the way along._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-2-ETS1-1, ask): a stuck thing may move when we lift and push in a better way  
> _The shutter is stuck. What can we try?_  
> - ✅ Lift a little, then push. → Yes! Trying a new way solves a stuck problem.
> - Walk away. → The shutter stays stuck. A new way, like lift and push, can fix it.
> - Pull the wrong way harder. → Harder is not always better. Lifting a little and pushing works best.
> _For grown-ups: Chose a new way of lifting and pushing to solve a stuck shutter._

_The boards creak. The house groans like a ship._  
**Mr Kusakabe**: The house is only stretching, Mei. Old houses do.  

#### `sprout:a17-03-shutters-done` · [main](main.md#a17-03-shutters-done)

**Mr Kusakabe**: Stay till the wind drops. The bath is nearly hot.  
_Your Nubbin sits by the stove. Rattle!_  
_The shutters jump._  
_Creak! Bang!_  
_The house makes lots of noises._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.1.4, do): sound words that copy noises: creak, rattle, bang  
> _The old house is noisy! Say the sounds with me._  
> _(Say them in a big voice: creak, rattle, bang.)_  
> Check: `edu:say:sound-words:creak`
> _For grown-ups: Said sound words that copy noises: creak, rattle and bang._


#### `sprout:a17-03-bath` · [main](main.md#a17-03-bath)

_The family has a bath at the back. You wait in the main room._  
_A gust hits the house. Bang!_  
_The lamp swings._  
**Mei**: Dad! I heard something!  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): telling a trusted grown-up when we feel scared  
> _Mei heard a noise and felt scared. What did she do?_  
> - ✅ She told her dad. → Yes! Telling someone you trust is a good thing to do.
> - She hid and said nothing. → Hiding keeps the worry big. Mei told her dad, and that helped.
> - She ran out into the storm. → Not safe! Mei told her dad. Telling a grown-up is brave.
> _For grown-ups: Noticed that telling a trusted adult helps when we feel scared._

**Mr Kusakabe**: Do you know what to do? Laugh, as loud as you can!  
_From the back comes a big, silly laugh. Ha ha ha!_  

**Choices**

- **Laugh as loud as you can.**
    - You laugh. It sounds so silly, you laugh more.
- **Tickle your Nubbin.**
    - Your Nubbin squeaks and rolls. Everyone laughs.
- **Try to laugh. Out comes a honk.**
    - **Mei**: Do it again!

#### `sprout:a17-03-bath-done` · [main](main.md#a17-03-bath-done)

_The wind blows on. Inside, nobody minds any more._  
_Mei comes out pink and warm in a towel._  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, notice): a warm bath makes us clean and sleepy, and sleep helps us grow  
> _A warm bath makes you clean. It makes you sleepy too._  
> _When you sleep, your body rests and grows._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a warm bath cleans us, and that sleep helps our bodies rest and grow._

_She falls asleep on the floor. Snore!_  

#### `sprout:a17-03-moon` · [main](main.md#a17-03-moon)

_The wind is gone. The Moon is up._  
_The fields are wet and shiny._  
_You sit on Granny’s step. Your Nubbin looks at the old house._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-ESS1-1, notice): the Moon does not make its own light; the Sun lights it up  
> _Look at the Moon. It does not make its own light._  
> _The Sun lights it up, like a torch shining on a ball._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that the Moon shines because the Sun lights it._

_Soft black things float up from the eaves. Up, up, up the hill._  
_Satsuki sees you. She lifts her hand._  

**Choices**

- **Lift your hand back.**
    - You both nod. You keep the secret.
- **Point up at the tree.**
    - Satsuki nods. You both know.

#### `sprout:a17-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a17-03-complete)

_The last little black thing goes into the dark tree._  
**Granny**: Still up? They have gone.  
**Granny**: They will find another empty house.  
_Granny brings barley tea. She sits with you and says nothing._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, notice): sitting quietly beside someone is a kind thing to do  
> _Granny does not talk. She just sits beside you._  
> _Sometimes being close is the kindest thing._  
> _For grown-ups: Noticed that sitting quietly beside someone can be a kindness._


## A17-4. The Hospital

- `a17-04-s1`: Ride bicycles with the family to the hospital. · _Borrow Granny’s bicycle. Follow Mr Kusakabe along the road._
- `a17-04-s2`: Go in with the family to see Mrs Kusakabe. · _Follow them down the long corridor to the last room._
- `a17-04-s3`: Wait in the corridor so the family can be together. · _Sit on the bench outside the door. They will come out._

#### `sprout:a17-04-ride` · [main](main.md#a17-04-ride)

**Mr Kusakabe**: We are visiting their mum today. Would you like to come?  
_Granny lends you her bicycle. Squeak, squeak, squeak!_  
_The road goes past the fields and a pond. Frogs everywhere._  
**Mei**: Faster, Dad! Faster than the Keeper!  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): a bicycle goes faster downhill and needs more push uphill  
> _Which way is easier on a bike: down the hill, or up?_  
> - ✅ Down the hill. → Yes! The hill helps you roll, whoosh! Uphill needs lots of push.
> - Up the hill. → Uphill is hard work. Pedal, pedal, puff! Down the hill is easier.
> _For grown-ups: Compared riding downhill and uphill: downhill is easier._


**Choices**

- **Race them to the top.**
    - Mr Kusakabe wins by a wheel. Then he puffs.
- **Ride slowly and let them win.**
    - Mei shouts happily all the way down.

#### `sprout:a17-04-ride-done` · [main](main.md#a17-04-ride-done)

_The hospital is long and white, among pine trees._  
_All its windows are open._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, do): clap the three beats in hospital  
> _This is a hospital. Clap its beats: hos-pi-tal._  
> _(Clap three times: hos, pi, tal.)_  
> Check: `edu:clap:syllables:hospital`
> _For grown-ups: Clapped the three syllables in the word hospital._

**Satsuki**: Mum’s window is the third one. Mei, walk!  
_Mei runs._  

#### `sprout:a17-04-room` · [main](main.md#a17-04-room)

_Mrs Kusakabe sits up in bed by the window. She looks tired and kind._  
**Mei**: Mum!  
**Mrs Kusakabe**: Mei! Satsuki!  
**Mrs Kusakabe**: Come here, both of you.  
_Mei climbs on the bed. Satsuki holds her mum’s hand._  
**Mrs Kusakabe**: The nurses and doctors are looking after me. I am getting better.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): naming the feeling of missing someone, and what helps  
> _Mei and Satsuki miss their mum. What helps when we miss someone?_  
> - ✅ A big hug. → Yes! A hug is a good help.
> - ✅ Talking about them. → Yes! Talking about someone helps too.
> - Pretend we do not miss them. → Missing someone is okay. A hug helps. So does talking about them.
> _For grown-ups: Named missing someone as a real feeling, and chose hugs and talking as help._


#### `sprout:a17-04-room-done` · [main](main.md#a17-04-room-done)

**Mrs Kusakabe**: Soon I will come home for a weekend. Then a little longer.  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, notice): rest, good food and kind helpers all help a body get well  
> _Mrs Kusakabe is resting. Rest helps a body get well._  
> _Nurses, doctors, good food and sleep help too._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that rest, food and caring helpers all help a body get well._

_Your Nubbin puts its chin on the bed._  
_Mrs Kusakabe strokes its head._  

#### `sprout:a17-04-corridor` · [main](main.md#a17-04-corridor)

_You sit on the bench outside the door._  
_Through the gap you see the window and the pine trees._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): giving a family private time to be together  
> _The family wants a quiet moment together. What does the Keeper do?_  
> - ✅ Wait outside, quietly. → Yes! Waiting gives them space to be together.
> - Go back in and chatter. → Chattering would break their quiet moment. Waiting outside gives them space.
> _For grown-ups: Understood that giving people quiet time together is kind._

**Mrs Kusakabe**: You have been looking after everyone. I will take a turn.  
_Satsuki shuts her eyes. A nurse goes by and smiles._  

**Choices**

- **Keep waiting. There is no hurry.**
    - The corridor smells of soap. A clock ticks.
- **Pull the door gently shut.**
    - Behind it you hear laughing.

#### `sprout:a17-04-complete` · [main](main.md#a17-04-complete)

_On the way home it is uphill. Everybody walks the bicycles._  
_Mei sleeps on the seat. Her dad keeps a hand on her back._

> _Two bicycles are walking home. Count the wheels._  
> _(One bicycle has two wheels. Count them all: one, two, three, four.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:bike-wheels:4`
> _For grown-ups: Counted the four wheels on two bicycles._

**Satsuki**: She looked better. Didn’t she?  
**Mr Kusakabe**: She did. Much better.  

## A17-5. The Small Ones

- `a17-05-s1`: Keep an eye on Mei in the garden. · _Sit at the edge of the garden. Mei will find something first._
- `a17-05-s2`: Crawl after Mei through the tunnel in the hedge. · _Get down low. Follow Mei’s yellow hat._

#### `sprout:a17-05-garden` · [main](main.md#a17-05-garden)

**Mr Kusakabe**: Please watch Mei for me. I am just in here, with my books.  
_Mei is picking up acorns in a tin bucket. She has three._  
_Your Nubbin gives her two more._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): acorns are the seeds of oak trees  
> _Mei has five acorns. An acorn is a seed._  
> _It is the seed of an oak tree._  
> _A tiny oak can grow from it!_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that an acorn is the seed of an oak tree._

_Then your Nubbin stops. Something small and white walks through the grass._  
_A bigger blue one follows. It has a little sack of acorns._  
**Mei**: Oh! Wait for me!  

**Choices**

- **Call to Mr Kusakabe.**
    - **You**: Mr Kusakabe! Mei is following something!
    - **Mr Kusakabe**: Is she? Good for her.
    - He does not look up, and turns a page.
    - Mei is nearly at the hedge.
- **Follow quietly.**
    - You tiptoe. The small ones walk a little faster.

#### `sprout:a17-05-garden-done` · [main](main.md#a17-05-garden-done)

_The small ones go under the house and out the other side._  
_Into the hedge they go._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, ask): where things are: under, through, in  
> _The small ones went under the house. Where did they go?_  
> - ✅ Under the house. → Yes! Under means below.
> - On top of the roof. → On top is up high. They went down low. They went under the house.
> _For grown-ups: Used position words: under, in and through._

#### `sprout:a17-05-hedge` · [main](main.md#a17-05-hedge)

_Mei goes in first. Your Nubbin goes next._

_Mei’s yellow hat bobs out into the sunshine._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, notice): telling a grown-up where we are going keeps us safe  
> _Mei followed the small ones very fast._  
> _It is safer to tell a grown-up where you are going._  
> _Then they will know where to find you._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that telling a grown-up where we are going is a safe choice._


**Choices**

- **Hurry after her.**
    - The tunnel stretches. You pop out on soft moss.
- **Let your Nubbin lead.**
    - Its tail knows the way. You pop out on soft moss.

#### `sprout:a17-05-complete` · [main](main.md#a17-05-complete)

_You are in an old, quiet wood. Long lines of light come down._  
_A tree root is as wide as a road._  
_Above it, the camphor tree goes up and up._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, notice): roots take water from the ground and hold the tree up  
> _The roots go down into the ground._  
> _They drink water. They hold the tree up too._

_Mei’s hat is on the moss. Mei is not in it._  

## A17-6. The Camphor Tree

- `a17-06-s1`: Find Mei under the camphor tree. · _Look between the roots for a way in._
- `a17-06-s2`: Help Satsuki and her dad look for the way Mei went. · _Go back to the hedge with the family. Mei will show you._
- `a17-06-s3`: Walk up the hill with the family to the camphor tree. · _Follow the path up past the little shrine. Stand with the others._

#### `sprout:a17-06-hollow` · [main](main.md#a17-06-hollow)

_Your Nubbin finds a gap between two roots._  
_You slide down into a warm, dim hollow. It smells of earth and leaves._

_Out. In._  
_Out._  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): slow breathing: belly goes up and down  
> _Listen to the big sleeper breathe. In._  
> _Out._  
> _(Hand on your tummy. Breathe in slowly, then out, three times.)_  
> Check: `edu:breathe:belly:3`
> _For grown-ups: Practised three slow belly breaths._

_A great grey-and-white shape is fast asleep._  
_Mei lies on his tummy. It goes up and down._  
**Mei**: Shh. He is asleep.  
**Mei**: He is so soft.  

**Choices**

- **Sit and watch.**
    - You sit on a root. Your eyes close for a moment.
- **Touch his fur.**
    - It is soft like warm grass. Your eyes close.

#### `sprout:a17-06-hollow-done` · [main](main.md#a17-06-hollow-done)

> - ✅ Blue and yellow. → Yes! Blue and yellow together make green.
> - Red and pink. → Red and pink make a deeper pink. Blue and yellow make green.
