# Atlas, page A18: The Doll in the Pocket

## A18-1. The Counterpane

*Vasilisa the Fair (Afanasyev; Magnus’s translation, 1915): the mother’s blessing* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Doll in the Pocket page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time once Nova’s atlas is in the Grove; the first quest on the page.

**Cast:** The Merchant (Vasilisa’s father, the merchant (Afanasyev)), Vasilisa (Vasilisa the Fair (Afanasyev; Magnus 1915); original design), Vasilisa’s Mother (The merchant’s first wife, Vasilisa’s mother (Afanasyev))

### Step 1: Step into the painted page and knock at the merchant’s house.

Hint: Touch the page, then walk up to the wooden house with the carved shutters. · check: `visit:a18-merchant:house`

#### <a id="a18-01-house"></a>Scene `a18-01-house`

_Nova’s atlas falls open at a page painted in egg yellow and soot black: a wooden house, a birch wood, and far off, a fence of pale sticks._  
_The paint smells of woodsmoke. Then it is woodsmoke, and you and your Nubbin are standing on a swept step with snow at its edges._  
_A big man in a fur hat opens the door before you can knock, as if he has been watching the road._  
**The Merchant** _(happy)_: You have the traveller’s book. The woman with the map! She sold me needles once and bought half my ribbons, and I still don’t know who won.  
**The Merchant**: Any friend of hers lodges here. There’s a room over the stable, and it’s warm, because the horse is.  
_Behind him the house is very quiet. Somebody is keeping it quiet on purpose._  
**The Merchant** _(sad)_: My wife is not well. We walk softly. You’ll walk softly too, I think.  
_A girl of eight looks round his knee. She has a plait to her waist, and she looks at your Nubbin the way you would look at a cake._  
**Vasilisa**: What is it? Is it a doll? Does it eat?  

**Choices**

- **Tell her it’s a Nubbin, and it eats everything.**
    - **Vasilisa**: Everything? Even porridge? Even the burnt bit?
    - Your Nubbin looks at her as if the burnt bit is the best bit, which it is.
- **Let your Nubbin answer for itself.**
    - Your Nubbin walks up to the girl and sits on her shoe. She goes very still, the way you do when a bird lands near you.
- **Ask her name first.**
    - **Vasilisa**: You’re supposed to say yours first. That’s manners. But I’ll tell you anyway, because you asked nicely.

#### <a id="a18-01-house-done"></a>Done `a18-01-house-done`

**Vasilisa**: I’m Vasilisa. Mother says I’m to be polite to lodgers, so I will be. Mostly.  
**The Merchant** _(soft)_: Go and wash your hands, Vasilisa. Your mother may want you later.  
_She goes, but slowly, looking back at your Nubbin all the way up the stairs._  

### Step 2: Walk Vasilisa to her mother’s door, and wait there.

Hint: Stand at the bedroom door with your Nubbin. Don’t go in. · check: `wait:a18-mother:door`

#### <a id="a18-01-counterpane"></a>Scene `a18-01-counterpane`

_That evening a voice at the end of the passage calls Vasilisa’s name. It is a quiet voice, and the whole house hears it._  
**Vasilisa**: Will you come? Only to the door. I don’t like the passage when it’s dark.  
_You walk her to the door with your Nubbin, and stop there. The room is warm and dim, and smells of beeswax._  
_Her mother sits up against the pillows, under a counterpane sewn from a hundred bright scraps._  
**Vasilisa’s Mother** _(soft)_: Vasilísushka, hear my last words. I am dying, and I will leave you my mother’s blessing and this doll.  
_From under the counterpane she takes a little doll, no longer than your hand, with a painted face and a scrap of red for a scarf._  
**Vasilisa’s Mother**: Keep this doll always by you, but show it to nobody, and no misfortune can befall you.  
**Vasilisa’s Mother**: And listen. When you’re in trouble, give her something to eat first, and then ask her. She will tell you what to do.  
**Vasilisa** _(think)_: Something to eat first. Then ask. And show nobody.  
**Vasilisa’s Mother** _(soft)_: Nobody. Good girl.  
_Her mother looks past her, to the door, to you. She doesn’t send you away._  

**Choices**

- **Stay where you are, by the door.**
    - You stay. Your Nubbin sits on your foot and does not fidget, for once.
- **Step back into the passage, to give them the room.**
    - You step back. Through the gap you see her mother lift a hand and lay it on Vasilisa’s head, and leave it there.
- **Nod to her: you’ll look after the girl.**
    - **Vasilisa’s Mother**: Thank you. She will need a friend who can keep quiet.

#### <a id="a18-01-counterpane-done"></a>Done `a18-01-counterpane-done`

_Vasilisa comes out with both hands under her apron, holding something very carefully._  
**Vasilisa** _(sad)_: I’m not to show you. So I’m not showing you.  
_She walks back down the passage with her hands still under her apron, and doesn’t let go._  

### Complete

#### <a id="a18-01-complete"></a>Complete `a18-01-complete`

_In the night the house goes quieter than quiet. Nobody needs to tell you why._  
_In the morning the merchant sits at the kitchen table with his hat in his hands, turning it round and round._  
_Vasilisa sits on the bottom stair, wrapped in her mother’s counterpane. Somebody has put it round her._  
_You don’t know what to say, so you don’t say anything. You sit down on the stair beside her._  
_After a while your Nubbin climbs up between you, and sits too._  
**Vasilisa** _(sad)_: She said I’m to be good and not cry too much. I think I’m crying the right amount.  
**You**: I think so too.  
_Under the counterpane, her hand is closed round something small. She holds on to it all day._

## A18-2. Dollet, Eat

*Vasilisa the Fair (Afanasyev; Magnus’s translation, 1915): the doll is fed* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Doll in the Pocket page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A18-1, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A18-1

**Cast:** The Merchant (Vasilisa’s father, the merchant (Afanasyev)), Vasilisa (Vasilisa the Fair (Afanasyev; Magnus 1915); original design), Dollet (The mother’s doll, “Dollet” in Magnus’s translation; original design)

### Step 1: Follow Vasilisa down to the cellar with a candle.

Hint: Take the candle from the kitchen shelf and go down after her. · check: `find:a18-vasilisa:cellar`

#### <a id="a18-02-supper"></a>Scene `a18-02-supper`

_The days after are grey and quiet. People come with pies, stand in the kitchen, and go away again._  
_At supper Vasilisa eats almost nothing. The best bits go off her plate and into her apron: the crust, the egg, the honey cake._  
**The Merchant** _(sad)_: Eat, little one. You’re getting thin.  
**Vasilisa** _(think)_: I am eating, Father. Look.  
_She chews a bit of turnip very hard, for him to see. Then she slips off her bench and out of the room._  
_Your Nubbin looks at you. It has noticed the honey cake too._  

**Choices**

- **Take the candle and follow her.**
    - The cellar door is open a crack. Below there is no light at all, and a very small voice.
- **Finish your supper first, then follow.**
    - You finish, because you are hungry, and take the candle down after her.
    - She hasn’t got far. She was waiting in the dark for somebody with a light.

#### <a id="a18-02-supper-done"></a>Done `a18-02-supper-done`

**Vasilisa**: Oh. It’s you. Shut the door. Properly, it sticks.  
_You shut it properly. It does stick, like a gate you know, a long way from here._  

### Step 2: Hold the light while Vasilisa feeds the doll, and keep still.

Hint: Keep the candle steady and don’t speak. · check: `watch:a18-doll:cellar`

#### <a id="a18-02-cellar"></a>Scene `a18-02-cellar`

_Vasilisa kneels on an upturned tub between the potatoes and the pickle crocks. She sets the doll on the tub, facing her._  
_Out of her apron come the crust, the egg and the honey cake. She lays them in front of the doll in a careful row._  
_The old book says it plainly: “Vasilísa often ate nothing herself, and kept the tastiest morsels for the doll;” tonight you see it._  
**Vasilisa** _(sad)_: Dollet, eat and listen to my misery.  
**Vasilisa** _(sad)_: Mother’s gone and Father’s sad and everybody brings pies. I don’t want pies. I want to know what to do.  
_For a moment nothing happens. Then the doll’s painted eyes begin to shine, like two drops of lamp oil with a light behind them._  
_The crust is gone, and the egg. Your Nubbin leans forward to see where the honey cake went, and it is gone too._  
**Dollet** _(soft)_: Don’t be afraid, Vasilisa. Grief is heavy, but you don’t carry it on your own. Go up and sleep.  
**Dollet**: Tomorrow you will be a little lighter, and the day after, a little more.  
_Your Nubbin is staring at the doll. The doll, as far as anyone can tell, is staring back._  
**Vasilisa** _(happy)_: She ate. She always eats. Mother said she would.  

**Choices**

- **Keep the candle very still.**
    - The flame doesn’t even lean. Vasilisa notices, and gives you the smallest nod.
- **Let your Nubbin go closer.**
    - Your Nubbin creeps up to the tub and sniffs the doll. The doll doesn’t move, and somehow it is clear that it allows this.

#### <a id="a18-02-cellar-done"></a>Done `a18-02-cellar-done`

**Vasilisa**: She won’t help on an empty stomach. Mother said so.  
**Vasilisa** _(soft)_: Nobody should have to.  

### Step 3: Learn how to set the food out, and make Vasilisa a promise.

Hint: Lay the morsels in a row in front of the doll, then answer Vasilisa. · check: `place:a18-morsels:cellar`

#### <a id="a18-02-promise"></a>Scene `a18-02-promise`

**Vasilisa**: You saw. So now you have to learn it properly, in case I’m ever ill, or away, or I forget.  
**Vasilisa**: The best bits. Not the leftovers. The best bits, and first, before I have mine.  
**Vasilisa**: In a row, so she can see them all. Then you say the words, and you wait. You mustn’t poke her.  
_She shows you twice. Your Nubbin watches as if there will be a test, which there may be._  
**Vasilisa** _(think)_: Now you have to promise. Never tell. Not Father, not anybody. Mother said show nobody.  
**Vasilisa**: You’re not nobody, you’re the lodger. That’s different. I’ve decided.  

**Choices**

- **Promise. Never, not anybody.**
    - **Vasilisa**: Good. Shake.
    - Her hand is very small and very serious.
- **Cross your heart, and your Nubbin’s too.**
    - You cross your heart, then your Nubbin’s, which tickles it. Vasilisa decides this counts double.
- **Ask why it has to be a secret.**
    - **Vasilisa**: Because people would want her. People always want things that help.
    - **Vasilisa**: And because Mother said so. That’s two reasons. Promise.
    - You promise.

#### <a id="a18-02-promise-done"></a>Done `a18-02-promise-done`

_Climbing the cellar steps, you think of a rule you know. It is in your gran’s handwriting, the first of three._  
_Feed them before you feed yourself._  

### Complete

#### <a id="a18-02-complete"></a>Complete `a18-02-complete`

_After that, the best bits of Vasilisa’s supper always go missing. Her father worries that she’s thin._  
_She is, a little. But she sleeps, and in the mornings she sings while she plaits her hair._  
_Some nights she sends you down instead, with a bundle in a napkin. You lay it out in a row, the way she showed you._  
_You say her words, and wait, and don’t poke. The doll’s eyes shine, and the food is gone._  
_It never says a word to you. It only ever speaks to her._  
_Your Nubbin, on the way back up, looks hopefully into the empty napkin every single time._

## A18-3. The Widow

*Vasilisa the Fair (Afanasyev; Magnus’s translation, 1915): the stepmother* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Doll in the Pocket page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A18-2, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A18-2

**Cast:** The Merchant (Vasilisa’s father, the merchant (Afanasyev)), Vasilisa (Vasilisa the Fair (Afanasyev; Magnus 1915); original design), The Stepmother (The widow the merchant marries (Afanasyev)), The Elder Sister (The stepmother’s elder daughter (Afanasyev)), The Younger Sister (The stepmother’s younger daughter (Afanasyev))

### Step 1: Meet the merchant’s new wife and her daughters.

Hint: Wait in the yard when the cart comes in at the gate. · check: `talk:a18-stepmother:yard`

#### <a id="a18-03-cart"></a>Scene `a18-03-cart`

_The atlas turns a page by itself. When you step back in, it is spring, and the birches have their small green coins on._  
**The Merchant** _(think)_: A house wants a mistress, and a girl wants a mother. I’ve found a widow, with two daughters near Vasilisa’s age.  
**The Merchant**: She seemed a kind woman. Mostly she seemed a woman with a lot of trunks.  
_A cart turns in at the gate, piled with trunks. On top sit three people in good shawls, looking at the house as if pricing it._  
**The Stepmother**: So this is the house. And this is the girl.  
_The stepmother looks Vasilisa up and down. Her daughters do the same, one after the other, like an echo._  
**The Elder Sister** _(angry)_: She’s very pretty.  
**The Younger Sister** _(angry)_: Too pretty. It isn’t fair.  
**The Stepmother**: Pretty fades, my loves. Sun and wind and work see to that, and there’s a great deal of work in a house like this.  
_Vasilisa curtsies and says nothing, with one hand in her apron pocket._  

**Choices**

- **Offer to carry a trunk.**
    - **The Stepmother**: The lodger. Yes. You may carry all of them.
    - You carry all of them. Your Nubbin carries a hatbox, very seriously, with its eyes shut.
- **Stand beside Vasilisa and say nothing.**
    - You stand beside her. The stepmother’s eyes rest on you a moment and move on, the way you would look past a chair.

#### <a id="a18-03-cart-done"></a>Done `a18-03-cart-done`

**The Stepmother**: Vasilisa will see to the garden, the water, the stove and the floors. A girl should be useful.  
**The Elder Sister** _(happy)_: And we’ll see to the sitting down.  
_Vasilisa curtsies again. In her pocket, something small is very still._  

### Step 2: Get up before dawn and see how the work is getting done.

Hint: Go out to the garden before anyone else is up. · check: `watch:a18-doll:garden`

#### <a id="a18-03-dawn"></a>Scene `a18-03-dawn`

_Before dawn you hear the cellar door. You go down to the garden with your Nubbin, in your socks._  
_Vasilisa is sitting in the shade of the apple tree, making a crown of dandelions._  
_The beds are weeded and the water butts are full._  
_Smoke is already rising from the kitchen chimney, and the stove inside is hot._  
**Vasilisa** _(happy)_: Shh. She did it. I fed her last night and asked her, and she said go to sleep. So I did.  
_In the cabbage bed you find the doll, no taller than a weed, pulling up the last dandelion with both arms._  
_Your Nubbin rushes to help and grabs the dandelion too, and the doll lets it._  
_They fall over backwards together, and the dandelion comes out._  
**Vasilisa**: She’s made me a salve as well, for the sun. Out of something. I don’t ask what.  
_Vasilisa puts her dandelion crown on your Nubbin and the doll back in her pocket, and the morning starts._  

#### <a id="a18-03-dawn-done"></a>Done `a18-03-dawn-done`

**Vasilisa** _(think)_: Now I have to look tired. Can you look tired? I can’t. I’ve been asleep.  

### Step 3: Help Vasilisa look busy when her stepmother comes round.

Hint: When the back door opens, give Vasilisa something to be doing. · check: `puzzle:a18-busy:yard`

#### <a id="a18-03-busy"></a>Scene `a18-03-busy`

_The back door bangs. The stepmother comes out on to the step, a daughter on each side, to see how thin and brown Vasilisa has got._  
**The Stepmother**: Well? Is the garden done? Is the water in? I don’t suppose it is.  
_Vasilisa is holding a dandelion crown and nothing else. She looks at you, very fast._  
**The Elder Sister** _(angry)_: She’s been picking flowers! I can tell. She’s got pollen on her nose.  
**The Stepmother** _(angry)_: Where is your hoe, girl? Where is your bucket?  
_You have about a second._  

**Choices**

- **Hand her an empty bucket.**
    - She takes it and swings it as if it were full and heavy. It is a very good performance.
    - **The Stepmother**: Hm. Well. Carry on, then.
- **Tip the dipper from the water butt over her apron.**
    - Splash. She looks soaked and hard done by, which is perfect.
    - **The Younger Sister**: She’s all wet! Ha!
    - **The Stepmother**: Hush. She looks… well, actually. How does she look so well?
- **Get your Nubbin to stand very still, like a weed.**
    - Your Nubbin stands stock still in the cabbage bed, still wearing the dandelion crown.
    - The stepmother walks right past it and never sees it. You are very proud of it.

#### <a id="a18-03-busy-done"></a>Done `a18-03-busy-done`

_The stepmother walks round the whole garden and finds nothing wrong with it. This makes her crosser than anything could._  
**The Stepmother** _(angry)_: Sun and wind. Every day. We shall see.  

### Complete

#### <a id="a18-03-complete"></a>Complete `a18-03-complete`

_Spring turns into summer. Every morning the work is done, and every evening the best bits go down to the cellar._  
_The stepmother’s daughters sit in the shade and grow sour and pale from all the sitting._  
_Vasilisa works in the sun, or seems to, and grows browner and stronger and lovelier every week._  
**The Elder Sister** _(angry)_: It isn’t fair. She works and works and gets prettier. We sit and sit and get worse.  
_Your Nubbin, who naps in the cabbage bed on the doll’s working mornings, has never looked better either._

**Notes:** The page turn at the start is the atlas skipping time (a soft riffle of pages, the season changing). ## A18-4. Suitors

*Vasilisa the Fair (Afanasyev; Magnus’s translation, 1915): the suitors turned away* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Doll in the Pocket page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A18-3, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A18-3

**Cast:** Vasilisa (Vasilisa the Fair (Afanasyev; Magnus 1915); original design), The Stepmother (The widow the merchant marries (Afanasyev)), The Elder Sister (The stepmother’s elder daughter (Afanasyev)), The Younger Sister (The stepmother’s younger daughter (Afanasyev)), A Young Man (One of the suitors the stepmother turns away (Afanasyev))

### Step 1: Step back into the page, some years on.

Hint: Turn the atlas page and walk up the path to the merchant’s gate. · check: `walk:a18-page:years`

#### <a id="a18-04-years"></a>Scene `a18-04-years`

_The atlas turns several pages at once, with a sound like a flock of birds getting up._  
_When you step in, the birches by the house are twice as tall. The apple tree has a swing on it, too small now for anyone._  
_Vasilisa comes down the path. She is grown: taller than you, her plait wound round her head, a basket on her arm._  
**Vasilisa** _(shock)_: You! You haven’t changed at all. Not a hair. And neither has it.  
_Your Nubbin walks round her twice, puzzled, as if she were a tree it used to know as a sapling._  
**Vasilisa** _(soft)_: I’m glad. Everything else changes. It’s nice when something doesn’t.  
**Vasilisa**: Stay a while? There’s been a lot of knocking at the gate lately, and I’d like somebody sensible about.  

**Choices**

- **Ask who’s been knocking.**
    - **Vasilisa**: Young men. For me. Not for my sisters. You can imagine how that’s going.
    - **Vasilisa**: One brought a goose. I think he hoped the goose would do the talking.
    - Your Nubbin looks round for the goose. It is a Nubbin with a lot of time for geese.
- **Ask, very quietly, about her pocket.**
    - **Vasilisa**: Still there. Still eats better than I do. She says hello.
    - The doll has said no such thing. Vasilisa is being kind to your Nubbin.

#### <a id="a18-04-years-done"></a>Done `a18-04-years-done`

_Behind her, down at the gate, somebody is already knocking._  

### Step 2: See what the stepmother says to the young men at the gate.

Hint: Stand near the gate and listen. · check: `watch:a18-stepmother:gate`

#### <a id="a18-04-gate"></a>Scene `a18-04-gate`

_At the gate stands a young man in a clean shirt, holding his hat. There is another behind him, and another coming up the lane._  
**The Stepmother** _(angry)_: Who is it for? Not for my girls?  
_The first young man goes pink and says Vasilisa’s name._  
**The Stepmother**: Then go home. The youngest doesn’t marry before the elders. That is the custom in this house.  
**The Stepmother**: Come back when you have something to say to my daughters.  
_He goes. The second one doesn’t get his name out before the gate swings shut on him._  
**The Elder Sister** _(sad)_: Nobody comes for us. Not one. It’s her face.  
**The Younger Sister** _(angry)_: It’s her fault for having it.  
_Upstairs, a window closes very quietly. Vasilisa doesn’t come down until they have all gone._  

#### <a id="a18-04-gate-done"></a>Done `a18-04-gate-done`

**The Stepmother** _(angry)_: Lodger. You’ll keep that gate tomorrow. Nobody comes in for her. Do you understand me?  

### Step 3: Hold the gate for the last young man, and see him go.

Hint: Open the gate when he knocks. You can’t let him in. · check: `wait:a18-suitor:gate`

#### <a id="a18-04-last"></a>Scene `a18-04-last`

_The next evening one more comes. He is younger than the others, with mended sleeves and a bunch of cornflowers._  
**A Young Man**: Is she in? I’ve walked from the mill. I don’t want anything. I only wanted to ask her.  
_Behind you the stepmother is at the window. Above her, at another window, so is Vasilisa._  
**A Young Man** _(sad)_: I suppose it’s no. I suppose it was always going to be no.  
_Your Nubbin takes hold of the gate with both paws, as if that will help somebody. It isn’t clear who._  
_The hinge creaks. It is the kind of creak that tells a house when somebody leaves, and when they come back._  

**Choices**

- **Tell him she’d like the cornflowers.**
    - **A Young Man**: Would she? Then give them to her. Don’t say who. She’ll guess, or she won’t.
    - He goes off down the lane lighter than he came.
- **Say nothing. Just hold the gate while he goes.**
    - He nods to you as if you were a friend, and walks off down the lane without looking back.
- **Whisper that the stepmother goes to market on Thursdays.**
    - He grins. Behind you, the window bangs open.
    - **The Stepmother**: I heard that!
    - He runs. Your Nubbin runs a little way after him, out of sympathy.

#### <a id="a18-04-last-done"></a>Done `a18-04-last-done`

_Later, at the cellar door, Vasilisa thanks you for minding the gate. She doesn’t say which part she means._  

### Complete

#### <a id="a18-04-complete"></a>Complete `a18-04-complete`

_That winter the knocking stops. Word has got round the lanes._  
**The Stepmother** _(angry)_: That girl is a curse on this house. While she’s in it, my daughters are nobody.  
_She says it to the stove, but loudly enough for the whole kitchen._  
_In the cellar that night, Vasilisa feeds the doll and asks it nothing at all. She only sits with it._  
_You sit on the step above, with your Nubbin, and nobody says a word. That seems to be what everybody needed._
