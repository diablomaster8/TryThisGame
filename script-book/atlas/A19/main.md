# Atlas, page A19: The Dragon Who Fell

## A19-1. This Is Berk

*How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010): the night raid* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Dragon Who Fell page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time once Nova’s atlas is in the Grove; the first quest on the page.

**Cast:** Hiccup (Hiccup, the chief’s son, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design), Gobber (Gobber, the one-handed smith, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design)

### Step 1: Come ashore on Berk and find somewhere to sleep.

Hint: Touch the page, then walk up the jetty to the boy counting barrels. · check: `visit:a19-forge:loft`

#### <a id="a19-01-arrive"></a>Scene `a19-01-arrive`

_Nova’s atlas falls open at a page drawn in charcoal: a grey sea, a crooked island, and rain scratched in at a slant._  
_In the corner someone has drawn a pair of black wings, then rubbed them out, then drawn them again._  
_The charcoal smudges under your thumb and goes cold and wet. Then it is spray, and you and your Nubbin are on the deck of a small boat._  
_Ahead, an island of cliffs and wooden houses climbs out of the sea, with sheep on every ledge that will hold one._  
_On the jetty a skinny boy in a fur waistcoat is counting barrels, badly, because he keeps looking at the sky._  
**You**: Where is this?  
**Hiccup**: It’s twelve days north of Hopeless, and a few degrees south of Freezing to Death.  
**Hiccup**: Berk. Home. I’m Hiccup. Yes, it’s a real name. No, I didn’t pick it.  
_A big man stumps down the jetty, with a hammer in his one hand and a hook where the other should be._  
**Gobber** _(think)_: Iron for the forge, at last. And what’s that?  
_Your Nubbin, who has been sick twice over the side, peers up at him._  
**Gobber**: A hatchling. Something from the south, by the colour. Small, round, no teeth to speak of.  
**Gobber**: No. Don’t tell me. If I don’t know what it is, I don’t have to do anything about it.  

**Choices**

- **Tell him it’s a Nubbin.**
    - **Gobber**: A Nubbin. Right. Never heard of one, so it can’t be on the list of things to hit.
- **Let your Nubbin wave a wing at him.**
    - Your Nubbin waves one stubby wing. Gobber waves his hook back, gravely, as one professional to another.
- **Say nothing at all.**
    - **Hiccup**: That’s the right answer with Gobber. He likes a mystery he doesn’t have to solve.

#### <a id="a19-01-arrive-done"></a>Done `a19-01-arrive-done`

**Gobber**: You’ll want work. Everyone on Berk works or gets eaten. There’s a loft over the forge, and bellows with nobody on them.  
**Hiccup** _(happy)_: Welcome to the forge. Mind the sword rack. And the axe rack. And the ceiling.  

### Step 2: Keep the forge fire hot while the raid goes on outside.

Hint: Pump the bellows in time; the fire wants long, steady pushes. · check: `minigame:a19-bellows:forge`

#### <a id="a19-01-raid"></a>Scene `a19-01-raid`

_You wake in the dark to horns, and shouting, and a red light under the loft door that isn’t morning._  
_Out in the lane a roof is burning. Something with wings lifts a sheep off a wall, and the sheep looks more surprised than anyone._  
**Gobber**: Keeper! Bellows! Every axe on Berk comes back blunt by midnight, and every one of them wants an edge!  
_You pump, and the fire roars white. Your Nubbin sits on the end of the anvil, flinching at every bang from outside._  
**Hiccup** _(sad)_: Sorry I’m late. I was out there. Briefly. Everybody told me to come back in here, very firmly.  
**Gobber**: Because out there you’re a danger to yourself and everyone near you, and in here you’re only a danger to me.  
**Hiccup** _(think)_: That’s fair. That’s actually fair.  
_Vikings run in with blunt axes and run out with sharp ones. Nobody looks at Hiccup, except to step round him._  
**Hiccup**: Every raid, the same. Gronckles, Nadders, a Zippleback if we’re lucky. And one that nobody has ever seen.  
**Gobber**: Don’t start.  
**Hiccup** _(think)_: I’m only saying. You bring down one of those, you’re somebody here.  

#### <a id="a19-01-raid-done"></a>Done `a19-01-raid-done`

_By the end your arms are jelly, and your Nubbin has fallen asleep on the anvil, which nobody else on Berk could do._  
**Gobber**: Not bad, for the help. Hiccup, stay here. Stay. Here.  

### Step 3: Help Hiccup get his home-made launcher out of the forge.

Hint: Take one end of the launcher and wheel it out of the forge door. · check: `carry:a19-launcher:forge-door`

#### <a id="a19-01-launcher"></a>Scene `a19-01-launcher`

_The moment Gobber is out of the door, Hiccup is under his workbench, pulling a sheet off something with wheels._  
_It is a wooden frame with a crank, and three stones tied together on ropes, folded into its mouth like a sneeze._  
**Hiccup**: Bola launcher. I made it. The ropes wrap round the wings, the dragon comes down, and I stop being… whatever they call me.  
**You**: What do they call you?  
**Hiccup** _(sad)_: Mostly they don’t. That’s the problem.  
**Hiccup**: It needs to go up on the cliff. It’s heavy, it’s badly balanced, and it will probably go off on the way.  
_Your Nubbin, awake now, looks at the launcher, then at the open door, then at you._  

**Choices**

- **Take the back end.**
    - You take the back end. It is heavier than it looks, and it creaks in a way that sounds like a warning.
    - **Hiccup**: Thank you. Nobody ever takes the back end.
- **Gobber said to stay here.**
    - **Hiccup**: Gobber also says the soup is fish. It isn’t always fish.
    - He starts pushing it out on his own. You find you have taken the back end anyway.

#### <a id="a19-01-launcher-done"></a>Done `a19-01-launcher-done`

_Out into the lane you go, past a burning cart and a Viking chasing his own helmet down the hill._  
_Halfway up, the launcher coughs, and a bola wraps a passing sheep up like a parcel. The sheep goes on chewing._  
**Hiccup**: That’s fine. I brought spares. I always bring spares.  

### Complete

#### <a id="a19-01-complete"></a>Complete `a19-01-complete`

_You get the launcher up the slope behind the forge, to a lip of rock over the dark sea._  
**Hiccup**: There. Now we wait for the one nobody sees.  
_Hiccup kneels behind the crank. His hands are shaking a bit, but he holds them still on purpose._  
**Hiccup** _(soft)_: If I hit it, they’ll have to look at me. That’s all I want. One look.  
_Out over the water, very high, something screams like tearing cloth._

## A19-2. The Night Fury

*How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010): the shot in the dark* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Dragon Who Fell page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A19-1, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A19-1

**Cast:** Hiccup (Hiccup, the chief’s son, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design), Stoick (Stoick the Vast, chief of Berk, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design), Gobber (Gobber, the one-handed smith, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design)

### Step 1: Watch the sky with Hiccup, and see where the shot goes.

Hint: Stay by the launcher on the cliff and keep your eyes on the stars. · check: `watch:a19-sky:cliff`

#### <a id="a19-02-shot"></a>Scene `a19-02-shot`

_Below, a tower of torches burns, and Vikings throw everything they have at the sky._  
_Then there is that sound again, a high rising whistle, and a tower bursts apart in blue-white fire._  
**Hiccup**: That’s it. That’s the one. It never steals, never shows itself, never misses.  
_You look up and see nothing. Only stars, and then not stars, where something black has crossed in front of them._  
**Hiccup**: Come on. Give me something. Anything to aim at.  
_The whistle comes again. Hiccup sees the flash of it, swings the launcher round, and pulls._  
_The bola spins up into the dark. Far out there is a shriek, and a shape tumbles out of the sky behind the ridge._  
**Hiccup** _(shock)_: I hit it. I hit it! Did anybody see that?  
_Down in the village, nobody did. Your Nubbin did, and so did you._  

**Choices**

- **Tell him you saw it.**
    - **Hiccup**: You saw it. Somebody saw it. Right. Good. I’m not mad.
- **Point to where it fell.**
    - You point at the dark ridge. Hiccup lines up your arm with his eye and learns the spot like a map.
- **Ask if he’s sure.**
    - **Hiccup**: No. Yes. It fell. Things don’t fall like that unless something has gone wrong with them.

#### <a id="a19-02-shot-done"></a>Done `a19-02-shot-done`

_Behind you, a wooden stake snaps. Something large has landed on the cliff, and it is not black, and it is very awake._  

### Step 2: Get Hiccup down to the village in one piece.

Hint: Run for the torch posts in the square and keep behind them. · check: `escort:a19-hiccup:square`

#### <a id="a19-02-chase"></a>Scene `a19-02-chase`

_A Monstrous Nightmare pulls itself over the lip of the cliff, red and long, and on fire along its whole back._  
**Hiccup** _(shock)_: Ah. Not that one. Run. Running would be good.  
_You run, all three of you, down the slope, with the heat of it on your neck._  
_Your Nubbin runs faster than you knew it could, straight through Hiccup’s legs, twice._  
**Hiccup**: Left! No, other left! Behind the torch post!  
_You throw yourselves behind a torch post. The fire splits round it, and the wood begins to smoke._  
_Then a man the size of a door steps out in front of you, and meets the Nightmare with nothing but his shoulder and a roar._  
_The Nightmare thinks about it, and goes. Above it, the last of the raid goes too, with half the village’s sheep._  

#### <a id="a19-02-chase-done"></a>Done `a19-02-chase-done`

**Stoick**: Hiccup.  
**Hiccup** _(sad)_: Hi, Dad.  

### Step 3: Stand by Hiccup while he tells his father.

Hint: Stay with Hiccup in the square while Stoick listens, or doesn’t. · check: `talk:a19-stoick:square`

#### <a id="a19-02-nobody"></a>Scene `a19-02-nobody`

_Stoick the Vast is the chief of Berk. He is very large, very tired, and covered in soot._  
**Hiccup**: I hit a Night Fury. Up on the cliff. It came down past the ridge, you could send someone to…  
**Stoick** _(angry)_: Stop. Every time you step outside, something gets broken. Tonight it was a torch post and the fish store.  
**Stoick**: I’ve a village to feed, and the winter coming, and the dragons have had the sheep.  
**Hiccup**: I know what I saw.  
_Behind Stoick, a few Vikings are laughing: not loudly, just enough._  
**Stoick** _(think)_: Gobber, see him home. And who’s this?  
**Gobber**: The new help, Stoick. Good arms. The small one’s some sort of hatchling. Southern. Harmless.  

**Choices**

- **Say you saw it too.**
    - **Stoick**: Then you were both looking at the dark. It’s easy to see things in the dark.
    - He isn’t unkind about it. He just has no room for it tonight.
- **Say nothing. Stand next to Hiccup.**
    - You say nothing. You stand next to Hiccup, close enough that he can tell.
    - **Hiccup**: Thanks.

### Complete

#### <a id="a19-02-complete"></a>Complete `a19-02-complete`

_Gobber walks you both back to the forge, and leaves you at the door with a look that means stay put this time._  
**Hiccup** _(sad)_: He thinks I’m a joke. They all do. I’ll find it tomorrow. I’ll find it, and I’ll…  
_He doesn’t finish. He goes up to his corner and draws by candlelight for a long time instead._  
_From the loft you can hear the charcoal scratching. It sounds like someone trying to remember exactly what he saw._

## A19-3. The Fleet Sails

*How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010): Stoick sails for the nest* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Dragon Who Fell page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A19-2, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A19-2

**Cast:** Hiccup (Hiccup, the chief’s son, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design), Stoick (Stoick the Vast, chief of Berk, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design), Gobber (Gobber, the one-handed smith, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design)

### Step 1: Go up to the Great Hall and hear what the chief has decided.

Hint: The Great Hall is at the top of the village; stand near the back. · check: `visit:a19-hall:council`

#### <a id="a19-03-hall"></a>Scene `a19-03-hall`

_In the morning the Great Hall smells of smoke and wet wool. Stoick stands at the head of the long table with a map._  
**Stoick**: The nest. Find it, and the raids stop. I’m taking the ships out one more time, before the ice.  
_Nobody cheers. The last time the ships went out, fewer came back._  
**Stoick**: I want every ship. Gobber stays. Somebody has to train the young ones.  
**Gobber**: Lovely. Left behind with the children and the dragons. My two favourite things that bite.  
**Gobber**: And Hiccup?  
_Stoick looks down the table at his son, who is trying to be very small behind a jug._  
**Stoick** _(think)_: Hiccup trains with the rest. It’s time.  
**Hiccup**: Dad, I really don’t think that…  
**Stoick**: You’ll have an axe. You’ll have Gobber. You’ll learn. That’s the end of it.  

#### <a id="a19-03-hall-done"></a>Done `a19-03-hall-done`

**Gobber**: Dragon training, then. Morning, in the arena. Bring the lad. Bring the hatchling, too. It can learn what not to be.  

### Step 2: Help load the fleet before it sails.

Hint: Carry stores from the fish sheds down to the ships on the jetty. · check: `carry:a19-stores:docks`

#### <a id="a19-03-docks"></a>Scene `a19-03-docks`

_All day you carry: barrels of salt fish, coils of rope, shields stacked like plates._  
_Your Nubbin carries one oar, end first, with tremendous seriousness. It takes it most of the afternoon._  
_Hiccup comes down to the jetty at the last minute. His father is checking a rope that doesn’t need checking._  
**Stoick**: Gobber will teach you. Listen to him.  
**Hiccup**: I will.  
**Stoick**: Good.  
_That seems to be everything. Then Stoick puts one great hand on Hiccup’s head, quickly, as if it might be seen, and goes aboard._  
_The ships go out on the grey tide. Hiccup watches until he can’t tell which sail is which._  

**Choices**

- **Wave the ships off.**
    - You wave. Your Nubbin waves its oar, which nearly takes Hiccup’s ear off.
- **Stand with Hiccup until the sails are gone.**
    - You stand with him until there is nothing left to watch but grey water.

#### <a id="a19-03-docks-done"></a>Done `a19-03-docks-done`

**Hiccup** _(soft)_: He used to do that when I was little. The hand. I’d forgotten.  

### Step 3: Look at what Hiccup has been drawing.

Hint: His notebook is open on the forge bench while he fetches charcoal. · check: `inspect:a19-notebook:forge`

#### <a id="a19-03-notebook"></a>Scene `a19-03-notebook`

_That night at the forge, Hiccup leaves his notebook open on the bench while he fetches more charcoal._  
_Every page is a dragon. Wings, teeth, fire, and next to each one, in small neat letters, how to bring it down._  
_Gronckle: hit it under the jaw; Nadder: stay out of its blind spot; Nightmare: don’t._  
_The last page has nothing drawn on it at all. Just two words at the top: Night Fury._  
**Hiccup**: Nobody’s ever seen one. So nobody can draw one. So nobody knows how to bring one down.  
**Hiccup** _(think)_: Which means, if somebody did…  

**Choices**

- **Ask why the page is still empty.**
    - **Hiccup**: Because I didn’t see it properly. Just the shape. I want to get it right.
    - He says it like an artist, not a hunter. He doesn’t notice that.
- **Ask what he’ll do if he finds it.**
    - **Hiccup**: Finish it, obviously. Bring it home. Be somebody.
    - He says it fast, the way you say a thing you have practised in the dark.
- **Say his drawings are good.**
    - **Hiccup**: They’re for killing things. They’re not supposed to be good.
    - But he looks at the Gronckle for a moment, as if he might fix the eye.

### Complete

#### <a id="a19-03-complete"></a>Complete `a19-03-complete`

**Hiccup**: The ridge. Where it fell. I’m going out there tomorrow, before training.  
**Hiccup**: Are you coming? You don’t have to. It’s probably dead. Or very angry.  
_Your Nubbin is already at the door. It doesn’t know where you are going, but it likes going._

## A19-4. The Bound Dragon

*How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010): Hiccup finds the Night Fury* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Dragon Who Fell page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A19-3, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A19-3

**Cast:** Hiccup (Hiccup, the chief’s son, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design)

### Step 1: Search the woods behind the ridge for whatever fell.

Hint: Follow Hiccup’s map; look for broken trees. · check: `explore:a19-woods:furrow`

#### <a id="a19-04-woods"></a>Scene `a19-04-woods`

_The woods behind the ridge are dripping and grey. Hiccup has a map with crosses on, and every cross is crossed out._  
**Hiccup** _(sad)_: Nothing. Nothing. Nothing. Some people lose a knife, or a shoe. I lose a whole dragon.  
_He shoves a branch out of the way and it swings back and hits him. Your Nubbin ducks it neatly._  
_Then you all stop. Ahead, a pine is snapped clean in half, and beyond it the earth is torn up in a long dark furrow._  
**Hiccup** _(shock)_: That’s… that’s it. That’s where it came down.  
_The furrow runs over the crest of a little rise. Something is breathing on the other side of it._  

### Step 2: Stay with Hiccup while he faces what he brought down.

Hint: Keep back at the top of the rise and watch. · check: `watch:a19-hiccup:ropes`

#### <a id="a19-04-bound"></a>Scene `a19-04-bound`

_At the bottom of the rise lies a dragon, black as wet slate, tangled in Hiccup’s ropes from nose to tail._  
_It is long and lean, the size of a big horse, and it is not moving._  
**Hiccup** _(shock)_: I did it. I actually… I brought down a Night Fury.  
_He takes the knife out of his belt. He holds it up in front of him with both hands._  
**Hiccup**: Right. I’m a Viking. This is what Vikings do. This is the whole point of me.  
_The dragon opens one eye. It is green and round, and it looks at him, and then it closes, as if it is too tired to watch._  
_Hiccup stands there a long time. The knife goes up a little, and down a little, and nowhere at all._  
**Hiccup** _(sad)_: I can’t. I did this. I did this to you.  
_He cuts the ropes instead. All of them, one after another, fast, before he can change his mind._  
_The dragon is up in a black rush. It pins him flat against a rock with one foot, and roars right into his face._  
_Then it is gone, in a crooked, lurching flight, crashing away through the trees._  
_Your Nubbin has bolted behind a stump. It is shaking all over, and its ball has gone dark and small._  

**Choices**

- **Scoop your Nubbin up and run.** — sets `{"a19-first":"ran"}`
    - You scoop it up and run. It is so frightened that its claws go through your sleeve.
    - At the edge of the trees you stop, out of breath. You are holding it too tight, and it is holding you too tight back.
- **Crouch where you are, and let it come to you.** — sets `{"a19-first":"crouch"}`
    - You crouch down in the wet bracken, and you don’t reach for it. You put one hand flat on the ground, and wait.
    - It takes a long time. Then a small shaking shape comes out from behind the stump and pushes hard against your knee.

#### <a id="a19-04-bound-done"></a>Done `a19-04-bound-done`

_Hiccup sits up. He looks at the knife in his hand as if somebody else had been holding it._  
**Hiccup** _(sad)_: I couldn’t. Why couldn’t I?  

### Step 3: Walk back to the village with Hiccup.

Hint: Take the long way round, through the woods, to the arena. · check: `walk:a19-hiccup:village`

#### <a id="a19-04-walk"></a>Scene `a19-04-walk`

_You walk back to the village together, the long way round, without a lot to say._  
**Hiccup**: Don’t tell Gobber. Or anyone. They’ll want to know why I didn’t, and I don’t…  
_He stops in the path. Your Nubbin stops too, and looks up at him._  
**Hiccup**: I don’t know why I didn’t.  
_Down in the village, the arena horn is blowing for the first day of training._  
**Hiccup** _(sad)_: Oh, good. Brilliant. My favourite. Let’s go and get set on fire.  

**Choices**

- **Promise you won’t tell.**
    - **Hiccup**: Thanks. Really.
    - It is the first secret anyone on Berk has trusted you with. You carry it carefully all day.
- **Ask him why he didn’t.**
    - **Hiccup**: I don’t know. I’ll tell you when I know. Is that all right?
    - You say it is. It turns out to be a promise, too.

### Complete

#### <a id="a19-04-complete"></a>Complete `a19-04-complete`

_Hiccup goes ahead to the arena with his shoulders up round his ears._  
_You follow, slower. Your Nubbin walks close beside you, and every few steps it checks that you are still there._  
_Far behind you in the woods, something crashes down through the branches, and goes quiet._  

**Rewards**

**Notes:** THE SIGNATURE CHOICE (a19-04-bound): a19-first = ran | crouch. It pays off in A19-8 (step 3), A19-11 (step 3) and A19-18 (step 2 done and the complete scene). Neither is wrong: ran is the frightened, loving thing, and the page lets it be slow to mend. The knife is never brought down; the cut ropes are the only thing it touches. Toothless is shown clearly for the first time here: our own design, long and lean, green eyes, one tail fin torn (the torn fin is first noticed in A19-6).

## A19-5. Dragon Training

*How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010): the first day in the arena* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Dragon Who Fell page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A19-4, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A19-4

**Cast:** Hiccup (Hiccup, the chief’s son, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design), Gobber (Gobber, the one-handed smith, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design), Astrid (Astrid, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design), Fishlegs (Fishlegs, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design), Snotlout (Snotlout, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design), Ruffnut (Ruffnut, one of the twins, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design), Tuffnut (Tuffnut, one of the twins, in How to Train Your Dragon (DreamWorks Animation, 2010; after Cressida Cowell); original design)

### Step 1: Report to the arena for dragon training.

Hint: The arena is the stone pit with the chain roof; go in by the big gate. · check: `visit:a19-arena:gate`

#### <a id="a19-05-arena"></a>Scene `a19-05-arena`

_The arena is a stone pit with a roof of iron chains, and doors round the edge with things breathing behind them._  
**Gobber**: Right! Dragon training! Lesson one: everything in here wants to eat you. Lesson two: let it try somebody else.  
_Five young Vikings stand in the middle. A tall girl with a braid and an axe is already in her stance._  
**Astrid**: Astrid. Stay out of my way.  
**Fishlegs** _(happy)_: Fishlegs. Hello! Is that a hatchling? What class is it? It isn’t like any of the classes. Does it breathe anything?  
**Snotlout**: Snotlout. Remember the name. You’ll be telling people you met me.  
**Ruffnut**: I’m Ruffnut. He’s Tuffnut.  
**Tuffnut**: I’m Tuffnut. She’s the other one.  
_Hiccup slips in at the back, late, in a helmet too big for him._  
**Snotlout**: Hiccup’s doing training? Then I’m safe. They always go for the slowest one first.  
**Gobber**: Hiccup trains. The Keeper helps. And the hatchling stands by the wall and looks fierce.  

#### <a id="a19-05-arena-done"></a>Done `a19-05-arena-done`

_Your Nubbin goes to the wall and does its fiercest face. A twin pats it on the head._  

### Step 2: Hand out the shields before the Gronckle gets loose.
