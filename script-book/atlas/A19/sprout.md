# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A19: The Dragon Who Fell

## A19-1. This Is Berk

- `a19-01-s1`: Sail to Berk. Find a place to sleep. · _Walk up the jetty to Hiccup and Gobber._
- `a19-01-s2`: Pump the bellows. Keep the forge fire hot. · _Push long and slow. Push, push, push._
- `a19-01-s3`: Help Hiccup wheel his machine out. · _Take the back end, and push._

#### `sprout:a19-01-arrive` · [main](main.md#a19-01-arrive)

_Your boat bumps into a grey island. It is cold and rainy._  
_This is Berk. Berk is made up, a place in a story._  
_But real Vikings lived on islands like it, long ago._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, ask): an island has water all round it  
> _(Point to the sea.) Land with sea on every side is called what?_  
> - ✅ An island → Yes! An island. You can sail all the way round it.
> - A hill → A hill is a bump of land. An island has sea all round.
> - A pond → A pond is a little pool. An island has sea all round.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that an island is land with water all around it, and that Berk is a made-up island._

**Hiccup**: Hello! I am Hiccup. I live here.  
_A big man comes down the jetty. He has a hook for a hand._  
**Gobber**: I am Gobber. What is that little round one?  
_Your Nubbin waves a stubby wing. Gobber waves his hook._  

**Choices**

- **Tell him it’s a Nubbin.**
    - **Gobber**: A Nubbin! Never heard of one. It cannot be dangerous.
- **Let your Nubbin wave a wing at him.**
    - Your Nubbin waves. Gobber waves his hook back, very politely.
- **Say nothing at all.**
    - **Hiccup**: Good idea. Gobber likes a mystery.

#### `sprout:a19-01-arrive-done` · [main](main.md#a19-01-arrive-done)

**Gobber**: You need a job. Come and work at my forge.  
_A forge is where people make things out of iron._  
**Hiccup**: Mind the swords. Mind the axes. Mind the ceiling.  
_Your Nubbin bonks its head on the ceiling. Bonk!_  
**Hiccup**: …Told you.  
_Over the forge is a cosy loft. That is your bed._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, do): first sounds: h  
> _(Say it with me: h-h-hammer, h-h-hook, h-h-Hiccup.)_  
> _They all start with the same sound: h._  
> Check: `edu:say:first-sound:h`
> _For grown-ups: Listened for the first sound h in hammer, hook and Hiccup._


#### `sprout:a19-01-raid` · [main](main.md#a19-01-raid)

_Horns toot in the night. A dragon lifts a sheep off a wall._  
_The sheep looks very surprised._  
**Gobber**: Bellows! Pump, pump, pump! Make the fire hot!  
_You push the bellows. Whoosh, the fire gets bigger!_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, do): a fire needs air to burn  
> _(Puff on your hand. Whoosh, can you feel the air?)_  
> **Gobber**: Bellows push air at the fire. Air makes it burn hot.  
> Check: `edu:blow:air:hand`
> _For grown-ups: Felt moving air on their hand and learned that a fire needs air, which is why a forge has bellows._

_Your Nubbin sits on the anvil. Bang, bang, and it covers its ears!_  
**Hiccup**: I want to help out there. But everyone says stay in here.  
**Gobber**: Out there you might get hurt. In here you are safe.  
_Hiccup sighs. Then he gets on with his work._  

#### `sprout:a19-01-raid-done` · [main](main.md#a19-01-raid-done)

_Your arms feel like jelly._  
_Your Nubbin is asleep on the anvil. Snore, snore._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 5  
> _(Pump your arms like the bellows. Count them out.)_  
> _One, two, three, four, five! Five big pumps._  
> Check: `edu:count:pumps:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five bellows pumps out loud._

**Gobber**: Good work, helper.  
**Gobber**: Hiccup, stay here. Stay. Here.  

#### `sprout:a19-01-launcher` · [main](main.md#a19-01-launcher)

_Gobber goes out. Hiccup pulls a sheet off a machine._  
_It has wheels, a crank and three stones on ropes._  
**Hiccup**: I made it. It catches a dragon in ropes.  
**Hiccup**: It is heavy. Please, can you help me push?  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): helping a friend who asks  
> _Hiccup needs a hand. What is a good thing to do?_  
> - ✅ Say yes and help → Thank you! Many hands make it easy.
> - Walk away → That is a bit lonely for me. Helping is kinder.
> - Hide → Hiding will not move my machine. A helper will!
> _For grown-ups: Talked about how a good friend helps when asked._

_Your Nubbin looks at the wheels. It wants to help, too._  

**Choices**

- **Take the back end.**
    - You take the back end. It is heavy!
    - **Hiccup**: Thank you.
- **Gobber said to stay here.**
    - **Hiccup**: Gobber says lots of things. Come on.
    - You find you are pushing the back end, too.

#### `sprout:a19-01-launcher-done` · [main](main.md#a19-01-launcher-done)

_Out into the lane you go._  
_Creak, creak, creak go the wheels._  
_Round and round, the wheels turn._  
_A wheel is the shape of a circle._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.2, do): name a circle  
> _(Point to something round, like a plate, a clock or a wheel.)_  
> _A circle! It rolls and rolls._  
> Check: `edu:tap:shape:circle`
> _For grown-ups: Found and named a circle, and saw that wheels are circles._


#### `sprout:a19-01-complete` · [main](main.md#a19-01-complete)

_Up the hill you go with the machine._  
**Hiccup**: Now we wait. Shh.  
_Hiccup’s hands shake a little._  
**Hiccup**: I am a bit scared. I will take slow breaths.  
_In and out. In and out._  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): slow breaths help when we are nervous  
> _(Breathe in slowly, then breathe out slowly. Do it three times.)_  
> **Hiccup**: Slow breaths help my hands stop shaking.  
> Check: `edu:breathe:slow:3`
> _For grown-ups: Practised three slow breaths, a way to feel calmer when nervous._

_High in the sky, something screeches._  

## A19-2. The Night Fury

- `a19-02-s1`: Watch the sky with Hiccup. See where the shot goes. · _Stay by the machine and look at the stars._
- `a19-02-s2`: Run down the hill with Hiccup. · _Hide behind the torch posts in the square._
- `a19-02-s3`: Stand next to Hiccup while he talks to his dad. · _Stay close. You do not have to say anything._

#### `sprout:a19-02-shot` · [main](main.md#a19-02-shot)

_It is a dark night. Torches glow on the hill._  
_Wheeeee! A high whistle flies through the sky._  
**Hiccup**: That is the dragon nobody sees. Shh. Watch the stars.  
_You look up. The stars twinkle, and something dark hides a few._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-ESS1-1, ask): we see stars at night  
> _(Point up.) At night, what twinkles in the dark sky?_  
> - ✅ Stars → Yes! Stars. They are always there. We see them best when it is dark.
> - The Sun → The Sun shines by day. At night we see the Moon and stars.
> - A rainbow → Rainbows come in the day, when sun and rain meet. At night: stars!
> _For grown-ups: Named the stars as what we see twinkling in the night sky._

**Hiccup**: There! I will aim, and fire!  
_Zoom, the ropes fly up! Something falls behind the ridge._  
**Hiccup**: I hit it! Did anyone see?  
_You saw. Your Nubbin saw, too._  

**Choices**

- **Tell him you saw it.**
    - **Hiccup**: You saw it! Somebody saw it. Good.
- **Point to where it fell.**
    - You point at the dark ridge. Hiccup learns the spot, like a map.
- **Ask if he’s sure.**
    - **Hiccup**: Yes. It fell. Things only fall like that if something is wrong.

#### `sprout:a19-02-shot-done` · [main](main.md#a19-02-shot-done)

_Crack! A wooden post snaps behind you._  
_Something big has landed on the cliff._  
_It is red and bumpy. It does not look happy._  
_It is wide awake, and it has seen you._  

> **Lesson** (music, 1-PS4-1, do): high sounds and low sounds  
> _(Make a high whistle. Now make a low rumble.)_  
> _The night whistle was high. The big red dragon’s grumble is low._  
> Check: `edu:make:sound:high-low`
> _For grown-ups: Made a high sound and a low sound and compared them._


#### `sprout:a19-02-chase` · [main](main.md#a19-02-chase)

_The big red dragon sniffs the air. Run!_  
**Hiccup**: Not that one! Run! Running is good!  
_You run down the hill. Thump, thump, thump!_  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, ask): our heart beats faster when we run  
> _(Put a hand on your chest.) When we run, what does our heart do?_  
> - ✅ Beats faster → Yes, thump-thump-thump! It pumps blood to your legs to help them run.
> - Stops → Your heart keeps going. It beats faster to help your legs run.
> - Goes to sleep → It stays awake. It beats faster when you run, then slows down to rest.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that the heart beats faster during running and slows when we rest._

_Your Nubbin runs through Hiccup’s legs. Twice!_  
**Hiccup**: Left! No, the other left! Behind the post!  
_You hide. A giant man steps out and shoos the dragon away._  
_The dragons fly off. The night goes quiet._  

#### `sprout:a19-02-chase-done` · [main](main.md#a19-02-chase-done)

_The giant man is Stoick. He is Hiccup’s dad._  
**Stoick**: Hiccup.  
**Hiccup**: Hi, Dad.  
_Hiccup’s shoulders go down. He looks small._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): naming a feeling in someone else  
> _How do you think Hiccup feels?_  
> - ✅ Worried and a bit sad → Yes. His shoulders went down, and that tells us how he feels.
> - Very silly and giggly → Not quite. Look at his face and shoulders: he looks worried.
> - Sleepy → It is late, but his face is worried, not sleepy.
> _For grown-ups: Read a character’s body language to name a feeling._


#### `sprout:a19-02-nobody` · [main](main.md#a19-02-nobody)

_Stoick is the chief. He is big and very tired._  
**Hiccup**: I hit a dragon! Up on the cliff!  
**Stoick**: Something always gets broken when you go out.  
**Stoick**: I have a village to feed. Winter is coming.  
**Hiccup**: I know what I saw.  
_Some people laugh. Hiccup looks at his feet._  
**Gobber**: This is our new helper. And a little hatchling.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, do): tell what happened in order: first, next, last  
> _(Let us tell it in order. First, Hiccup shot at the dark.)_  
> _Next, something fell. Last, nobody believed him._  
> _(Say it together: first, next, last.)_  
> Check: `edu:order:story:first-next-last`
> _For grown-ups: Retold part of the story in order, using first, next and last._


**Choices**

- **Say you saw it too.**
    - **Stoick**: It is easy to see things in the dark.
    - He is not unkind. He is just very busy tonight.
- **Say nothing. Stand next to Hiccup.**
    - You stand close. He can tell you are there.
    - **Hiccup**: Thanks.

#### `sprout:a19-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a19-02-complete)

_Gobber takes you home to the forge._  
**Hiccup**: They think I am a joke.  
_Hiccup goes up to his corner. He takes out his charcoal._  
_Scratch, scratch, scratch. He draws what he saw._  

> **Lesson** (art, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): making marks with charcoal  
> _(Draw a long line with a black crayon. Now a curly one.)_  
> _Charcoal is burnt wood. It makes black marks, like your crayon._  
> Check: `edu:draw:charcoal:line`
> _For grown-ups: Made lines with a black crayon and learned that charcoal is burnt wood._

_He does not want to forget it._  

## A19-3. The Fleet Sails

- `a19-03-s1`: Go to the Great Hall. Hear what the chief says. · _The hall is at the top of the village. Stand at the back._
- `a19-03-s2`: Help load the ships. · _Carry things from the sheds down to the jetty._
- `a19-03-s3`: Look at Hiccup’s notebook. · _It is open on the bench in the forge._

#### `sprout:a19-03-hall` · [main](main.md#a19-03-hall)

_In the morning, the Great Hall smells of smoke and wool._  
_A fire burns in a long line down the middle._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.His.2.K-2, notice): long ago, villages met in a big hall with a fire  
> _Long ago, Viking villages had a big hall._  
> _It had a fire in the middle. People met there to talk and eat._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that Viking villages had a long hall with a fire in the middle, where people met._

**Stoick**: I will take the ships and find the dragons’ home.  
_Nobody cheers. It is a long, cold trip._  
**Stoick**: Hiccup, you will go to dragon school.  
**Hiccup**: Oh dear.  

#### `sprout:a19-03-hall-done` · [main](main.md#a19-03-hall-done)

**Gobber**: Dragon school starts tomorrow, in the arena.  
**Gobber**: Bring the lad. And bring the hatchling.  
_Gobber winks. Your Nubbin salutes with a wing._  
_Who is going? Hiccup, you, and your Nubbin._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count three things  
> _(Hold up one finger for Hiccup, one for you, and one for the Nubbin.)_  
> _One, two, three! Three are going to school._  
> Check: `edu:count:friends:3`
> _For grown-ups: Counted three friends on their fingers._


#### `sprout:a19-03-docks` · [main](main.md#a19-03-docks)

_All day you carry things to the ships._  
_Barrels of fish, coils of rope and big round shields._  
_The ships are made of wood. Wood floats on water._  

> **Lesson** (science, 2-PS1-1, ask): wood floats and stones sink  
> _Wood floats. Which of these would float on the water?_  
> - ✅ A wooden spoon → Yes! Wood floats, so a wooden spoon would bob along.
> - A stone → Plop! A stone sinks, because it is heavy for its size.
> - An iron nail → Plop! A nail sinks too, and wood is the one that floats.
> _For grown-ups: Sorted things that float from things that sink, starting with wood._

**Stoick**: Listen to Gobber, Hiccup.  
**Hiccup**: I will.  
_Stoick gives Hiccup a quick pat on the head. Then he goes aboard._  
_The ships sail away. Hiccup watches them go._  

**Choices**

- **Wave the ships off.**
    - You wave, and your Nubbin waves an oar. Careful, Nubbin!
- **Stand with Hiccup until the sails are gone.**
    - You stand with him until there is only grey water.

#### `sprout:a19-03-docks-done` · [main](main.md#a19-03-docks-done)

**Hiccup**: He did that when I was little.  
_A pat on the head. It is a hug with no arms._  
_Hiccup misses his dad already._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, ask): what helps when we miss someone  
> _Hiccup feels sad. What helps when we miss someone?_  
> - ✅ A hug or a chat with a friend → Yes. It helps me to have you here.
> - Being all alone → That makes it feel bigger. A friend nearby is better.
> - Pretending to feel fine → It is all right to feel sad. Saying so helps.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about what helps when you miss someone: a hug and a friend._

_That is all right. Missing someone is a big feeling._  

#### `sprout:a19-03-notebook` · [main](main.md#a19-03-notebook)

_At night Hiccup leaves his notebook open._  
_Every page has a dragon. Each one is drawn with care._  
_Look at the wings, the eyes, the tails._  

> **Lesson** (art, K.G.A.2, do): look for shapes in a drawing  
> _(Look at the dragon’s eye. Can you see a circle?)_  
> _(Now the wings. Can you see a triangle?)_  
> Check: `edu:look:dragon:shapes`
> _For grown-ups: Looked for circles and triangles in the dragon drawings._

_The last page is empty. It only says: Night Fury._  
**Hiccup**: Nobody has seen one up close. Nobody can draw it.  
_Hiccup looks at the empty page for a long time._  

**Choices**

- **Ask why the page is still empty.**
    - **Hiccup**: I did not see it properly. I want to get it right.
    - He talks like an artist. He does not notice.
- **Ask what he’ll do if he finds it.**
    - **Hiccup**: Bring it home. Then people will see me.
    - He says it fast, as if he practised.
- **Say his drawings are good.**
    - **Hiccup**: Thank you!
    - He looks at the dragon’s eye, as if he might fix it.

#### `sprout:a19-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a19-03-complete)

**Hiccup**: Tomorrow I will go to the ridge.  
**Hiccup**: Do you want to come? You do not have to.  
_Your Nubbin is already at the door._  
_Nubbin, Night Fury and No: they all start with n._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, do): first sounds: n  
> _(Say it with me: n-n-Nubbin, n-n-night, n-n-no.)_  
> _They all start with n!_  
> Check: `edu:say:first-sound:n`
> _For grown-ups: Listened for the first sound n in Nubbin, night and no._


## A19-4. The Bound Dragon

- `a19-04-s1`: Search the woods for what fell. · _Follow Hiccup. Look for broken trees._
- `a19-04-s2`: Stay with Hiccup. Keep back and watch. · _Stand at the top of the rise._
- `a19-04-s3`: Walk back to the village with Hiccup. · _Take the long way round, through the woods._

#### `sprout:a19-04-woods` · [main](main.md#a19-04-woods)

_The woods are wet and grey. Hiccup has a map with crosses._  
**Hiccup**: Nothing here. Nothing here. Nothing here.  
_A branch swings back and bonks him. Your Nubbin ducks._  
_Then you stop. A pine tree is snapped in two._  
_The ground has a long, dark scratch in it._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS2-2, ask): animals leave signs behind  
> _(Point to the scratch.) What signs might an animal leave when it passes?_  
> - ✅ Footprints and broken twigs → Yes, footprints, scratches and broken twigs! Hiccup is a good tracker.
> - Nothing at all → Look! The scratch and the broken tree are signs that something came this way.
> - A rainbow → A rainbow is made by sun and rain. Animals leave footprints and twigs.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that animals leave signs such as footprints, scratches and broken twigs._

**Hiccup**: That is where it came down!  
_Something is breathing on the other side of the hill._  

#### `sprout:a19-04-bound` · [main](main.md#a19-04-bound)

_A black dragon lies in the ropes. It is very still._  
_Hiccup takes out his knife. He holds it up._  
**Hiccup**: This is what Vikings do.  
_The dragon opens one green eye. It looks tired._  
**Hiccup**: I cannot. I did this to you.  
_Hiccup cuts the ropes instead. Snip, snip, snip._  
_The dragon jumps up and roars. Then it flies off._  
_Your Nubbin is scared. It hides behind a stump and shakes._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): what helps a scared friend  
> _Your Nubbin is scared. What helps a scared friend?_  
> - ✅ Stay close and be gentle → Yes. Staying close and being gentle makes a scared friend feel safe.
> - Shout at it → Shouting makes scared feelings bigger. Gentle is better.
> - Walk far away → A scared friend needs someone near. Stay close and be calm.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about how to help a scared friend: stay close, be gentle and calm._


**Choices**

- **Scoop your Nubbin up and run.**
    - You scoop it up and walk away fast.
    - It holds on tight, and so do you. You are both very scared.
- **Crouch where you are, and let it come to you.**
    - You crouch low and wait, one hand flat on the ground.
    - It takes a long time. Then a small shape snuggles your knee.

#### `sprout:a19-04-bound-done` · [main](main.md#a19-04-bound-done)

_Hiccup sits up. He looks at his knife._  
**Hiccup**: I could not. Why could I not?  
_Thump, thump goes his heart. Slowly it calms down._  
_A calm heart has a slow, steady beat._  

> **Lesson** (music, 1-PS4-1, do): a slow beat and a fast beat  
> _(Clap a slow, steady beat. Now clap a fast one.)_  
> _Which beat feels calmer? The slow one!_  
> Check: `edu:clap:beat:slow-fast`
> _For grown-ups: Clapped a slow beat and a fast beat and noticed which feels calmer._


#### `sprout:a19-04-walk` · [main](main.md#a19-04-walk)

_You walk back to the village together._  
**Hiccup**: Please keep a secret for me.  
_He stops. Your Nubbin looks up at him._  
**Hiccup**: I do not know why I did not do it.  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, ask): a good friend keeps a promise  
> _Hiccup trusts you. What does a good friend do with a promise?_  
> - ✅ Keep it → Yes! Kept promises build trust. A worrying secret? Tell a grown-up.
> - Forget it → A promise is something to remember. A good friend remembers.
> - Tell it to everyone → That would not be keeping it. A friend keeps promises safe.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about keeping a promise to a friend, and telling a trusted grown-up about any secret that worries you._

_The arena horn blows. School is starting._  
**Hiccup**: Oh good. My favourite.  

**Choices**

- **Promise you won’t tell.**
    - **Hiccup**: Thank you. Really.
    - You carry the promise carefully all day.
- **Ask him why he didn’t.**
    - **Hiccup**: I will tell you when I know. Is that all right?
    - You say it is.

#### `sprout:a19-04-complete` · [main](main.md#a19-04-complete)

_Hiccup goes ahead to the arena._  
_You follow, slower._  
_Your Nubbin stays close. It keeps checking you are there._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-LS1-2, notice): young animals stay close to a grown-up who keeps them safe  
> _Baby ducks follow their mother in a line. Lambs stay close to their mums._  
> _Young animals stay near someone who keeps them safe._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that many young animals stay close to a grown-up who keeps them safe._

_Far away in the woods, branches crash. Then it goes quiet._  

## A19-5. Dragon Training

- `a19-05-s1`: Go to the arena for dragon school. · _The arena is the stone pit with the chain roof._
- `a19-05-s2`: Hand out the shields. · _Run a shield to each young Viking._
- `a19-05-s3`: Watch the round. Stay by the wall. · _Stay near the gate, where Gobber can reach you._

#### `sprout:a19-05-arena` · [main](main.md#a19-05-arena)

_The arena is a stone pit. It has a roof of chains._  
**Gobber**: Dragon school! Lesson one: be careful.  
_Young Vikings stand in the middle._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, notice): saying your name is a friendly way to start  
> _Astrid and Fishlegs said their names. That is a friendly start._  
> _Then the other person knows what to call you._  
> _For grown-ups: Noticed that saying your own name is a friendly way to meet someone new._

**Astrid**: I am Astrid. Stay out of my way.  
**Fishlegs**: I am Fishlegs! Is that a hatchling?  
_Hiccup creeps in last, in a helmet too big for him._  

#### `sprout:a19-05-arena-done` · [main](main.md#a19-05-arena-done)

_Three more young Vikings come over._  
**Snotlout**: I am Snotlout. Remember my name.  
**Ruffnut**: I am Ruffnut.  
**Tuffnut**: I am Tuffnut. She is the other one.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.1, do): count to 5, then add one  
> _(Count on your fingers: Astrid, Fishlegs, Snotlout, Ruffnut, Tuffnut.)_  
> _Five! Hiccup is here too, and five and one more is six._  
> Check: `edu:count:vikings:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five young Vikings, then added one more to make six._

_Your Nubbin goes to the wall._  
_It does its fiercest face. Grrr!_  
_A twin pats it on the head._  
_Fish-legs, Ruff-nut, Snot-lout. Each name has two parts._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, do): clap the parts of a word  
> _(Clap the parts: Fish-legs. Two claps!)_  
> _(Now Hic-cup. Two claps again!)_  
> Check: `edu:clap:syllables:fishlegs`
> _For grown-ups: Clapped the two parts of names such as Fish-legs and Hic-cup._


#### `sprout:a19-05-shields` · [main](main.md#a19-05-shields)

**Gobber**: Behind this door is a Gronckle. Everyone needs a shield!  
_You run along with shields in your arms._  
_The door bangs. Out rolls a round brown dragon!_  
**Fishlegs**: A Gronckle! It eats rocks!  
_The twins tug on one shield. Tug, tug, tug!_  
**Ruffnut**: It is mine.  
**Tuffnut**: I touched it first.  
_Now there is one shield left, and two people with none._
