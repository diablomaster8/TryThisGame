# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A20: The Weeping

## A20-1. The Bright One

- `a20-01-s1`: Step into the grey and gold page. Find Frigg’s hall. · _Touch the page. Then go in at the door._
- `a20-01-s2`: Carry Frigg’s cloak up the hill. · _Follow the path up to the pale hall._
- `a20-01-s3`: Sit with Baldr while your Nubbin sleeps. · _Sit on the bench. There is no hurry._

#### `sprout:a20-01-page` · [main](main.md#a20-01-page)

_Nova’s atlas opens at a grey and gold page._  
_This is a very old story, from a land called Iceland._  

> **Lesson** (science, 2-ESS2-2, ask): Iceland is an island: land with water all around  
> _Iceland is an island, far away to the north._  
> _(Point to the sea on the page.) What is all around an island?_  
> - ✅ Water → Yes! An island has water all the way around it.
> - Sand → Some islands have sand. But water goes all the way around.
> - A fence → A fence around it? Creak! The sea is what goes all the way around.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that Iceland is an island, with sea all around it._

_A man named Snorri wrote it down, long, long ago._  
_His book tells one way the story goes._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.His.2.K-2, notice): books were made by hand long ago  
> _Snorri wrote with a pen, on pages made of calfskin._  
> _There were no printers. Every book was made by hand._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that Snorri wrote the stories in a handmade book long ago._

_Some people still honour these gods today._  
_Whoosh! The page goes soft, and you step inside._  
_This is Frigg’s hall. Her spindle goes whirr, whirr, whirr._  
**Frigg**: Come in, guest. This is Fensalir. Will you run errands for me?  
**Fulla**: Can the little one carry things?  
_Your Nubbin holds up both arms. Yes!_  

**Choices**

- **Bow to Frigg, nice and low.**
    - Frigg bows back, just as low.
    - **Frigg**: Guests and hosts bow the same, in this house.
- **Say your name.**
    - **Frigg**: A good name. I will remember it.
- **Ask what to carry first.**
    - **Fulla**: Oh, I like this one!

#### `sprout:a20-01-page-done` · [main](main.md#a20-01-page-done)

**Frigg**: The first errand is easy. Take this cloak up the hill to my son.  
**Fulla**: The hall is called Breidablik. You cannot miss it.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, do): clap the parts of a name  
> _Let’s clap the name. Brei - da - blik._  
> _(Clap three times.)_  
> _Three claps, three parts. (Now try Fen - sa - lir.)_  
> Check: `edu:clap:syllables:breidablik`
> _For grown-ups: Clapped the syllables in Breidablik and Fensalir._


#### `sprout:a20-01-hall` · [main](main.md#a20-01-hall)

_Breidablik stands high on a hill. Its roof is pale as the sky._  
_A man sits at a long table, mending a strap._  
_He is so kind and so glad that he seems to shine._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-PS4-2, ask): we see things when there is light  
> _Baldr’s light fills the hall, so we can see everything._  
> _What do we need to see?_  
> - ✅ Light → Yes! We see things when there is light.
> - Quiet → Quiet is nice for sleeping. But to see, we need light.
> - Cold → Cold does not help us see. Light does.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that we need light to see._

**Baldr**: You came from my mother. I know her cloak.  
**Baldr**: Put it down, and sit. Everyone who climbs should rest.  
_Your Nubbin sits in his light. Blink._  
_Blink. Then it tips over, fast asleep._  
**Baldr**: Shh. The small ones do that. Let it sleep.  

#### `sprout:a20-01-hall-done` · [main](main.md#a20-01-hall-done)

_Your Nubbin is fast asleep in the brightest patch of floor._  
_It snores a tiny snore. Pff._  
_Pff._  
_Baldr tiptoes round it, and bumps the table. Bonk!_  
**Baldr**: Ow. Sorry, table.  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, ask): sleep helps bodies rest and grow  
> _Little ones need lots of sleep. Why?_  
> - ✅ It helps their bodies rest and grow → Yes! Sleep is how little bodies rest and grow.
> - It makes them noisy → Not noisy! Sleep helps bodies rest and grow.
> - They do not need it → Everybody needs sleep. It helps bodies rest and grow.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about why sleep matters for growing bodies._

**Baldr**: Stay till it wakes. I would like the company.  

#### `sprout:a20-01-sit` · [main](main.md#a20-01-sit)

_You sit on the bench. Baldr mends his strap._  
_Stitch, stitch._  
**Baldr**: Everyone climbs up here with a question.  
**Baldr**: I like the part of the day when I listen.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): being kind by sitting quietly beside someone  
> _A friend is quiet and does not want to talk. What can we do?_  
> - ✅ Sit beside them → Yes. Sitting close shows we care, even with no words.
> - Shout at them → Shouting is too loud. Sitting quietly beside them is kinder.
> - Run away → Staying close is kinder. Sit beside them.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about staying close to a quiet friend._

**Baldr**: Ask me something. Or don’t.  

**Choices**

- **Ask why he shines.**
    - **Baldr**: I do not know. I have never been anything else.
- **Ask about his brothers.**
    - **Baldr**: Hödr. He cannot see, but he hears everything.
- **Just sit.**
    - So you sit. Baldr sits too.
    - Nobody says a word.
    - **Baldr**: That was a good question.

#### `sprout:a20-01-complete` · [main](main.md#a20-01-complete)

_Your Nubbin wakes when the light moves across the floor._  
**Baldr**: It will follow me now. The small ones do.  
_He folds the cloak and puts it on the bench._  
**Baldr**: Tell my mother I will wear it.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, ask): on and under  
> _Baldr put the cloak on the bench._  
> _Is it on the bench, or under it?_  
> - ✅ On the bench → Yes! On means on top. The cloak is on the bench.
> - Under the bench → Under means below. The cloak is on top, so it is on the bench.
> _For grown-ups: Used the words on and under for where things are._

_On the way down, your Nubbin keeps looking back._  

#### `sprout:a20-01-ember` · [main](main.md#a20-01-ember)

_Variant of `a20-01-complete`._

_Your Nubbin has a little lantern. A red pebble glows inside._  
_The pebble is warm, but there is no flame._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-PS3-1, ask): sunshine and warm things  
> _The pebble makes hands feel warm. What else can warm us up?_  
> - ✅ A sunny spot → Yes! Sunshine warms the ground, and it warms us.
> - An ice cube → Brr! An ice cube is cold. It makes hands colder.
> - A cold wind → A cold wind makes us shiver. Sunshine is better.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about things that warm us, like the sun._

**Fulla**: My hands feel warm, just being near it.  
**Frigg**: Someone gave that away. Keep it close, little one.  

## A20-2. Bad Dreams

- `a20-02-s1`: Take lamp oil up to Baldr. · _Fulla has the jar. Carry it up the hill._
- `a20-02-s2`: Walk down with Baldr to tell the gods. · _Go with Baldr and wait by the door._
- `a20-02-s3`: Help in the hall while the gods talk. · _Feed the fire. Fill the cups. Or just listen._

#### `sprout:a20-02-oil` · [main](main.md#a20-02-oil)

_It is late. Fulla holds a jar of lamp oil._  
**Fulla**: His lamp burns all night. Take this up to him?  
_Your Nubbin carries the lid. It holds it up like a shield!_  
_Baldr sits at his table. His cup has gone cold._  
**Baldr**: Oh, it is you. I am glad it is you.  
**Baldr**: I keep having a bad dream. I go down a long road.  
**Baldr**: It makes me worried. I have not told anyone.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): telling someone when you are worried  
> _Baldr is worried. What could help him?_  
> - ✅ Tell someone he trusts → Yes. Telling someone helps. It is hard alone.
> - Keep it a secret → Secrets make a worry feel bigger. Telling helps.
> - Pretend it is not there → I tried that. The dream stayed. Telling helps more.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about telling someone you trust when you are worried._

_Your Nubbin climbs up beside him and sits very close._  

**Choices**

- **Say a dream is only a dream.**
    - **Baldr**: I keep telling myself that. It is hard to believe.
- **Say he should tell the others.**
    - **Baldr**: Yes. You are right. It is worse alone.
- **Sit and wait.**
    - You sit. He puts his hand on your Nubbin’s head.

#### `sprout:a20-02-oil-done` · [main](main.md#a20-02-oil-done)

**Baldr**: In the morning I will tell them all. Walk with me?  
_He sleeps a little. The lamp stays lit, and you keep watch._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-ESS1-1, ask): the sun comes up in the morning  
> _The lamp is lit because it is night. When does the sun come up?_  
> - ✅ In the morning → Yes! The sun comes up in the morning, and the dark goes.
> - At bedtime → At bedtime it is getting dark. The sun comes up in the morning.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about night and morning._


#### `sprout:a20-02-telling` · [main](main.md#a20-02-telling)

_In the morning the gods meet in their great hall._  
_Baldr tells them about the road, and the bad dream._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds  
> _Baldr starts with the sound buh. Which word starts with buh too?_  
> - ✅ Bear → Yes! Buh-buh-bear. Baldr and bear both start with buh.
> - Fox → Fox starts with fuh. Baldr starts with buh, like bear.
> - Hare → Hare starts with huh. Baldr starts with buh, like bear.
> _For grown-ups: Matched words by their first sound (b for Baldr and bear)._

**Thor**: A dream is only smoke. Show me the trouble and I will fix it.  
**Odin**: Not every trouble is easy to see.  
**Frigg**: Then we will find it. Whatever it is.  
_The talk goes on and on. You find a stool by the door._  

#### `sprout:a20-02-telling-done` · [main](main.md#a20-02-telling-done)

_The talk goes on all morning, and into the night._  
_You carry three cups to the table. Then two more._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.2, ask): add 3 and 2  
> _You carried three cups, then two more. How many cups is that?_  
> - ✅ Five → Yes! One, two, three, four, five. Five cups.
> - Four → Let’s count: one, two, three, four, five. It is five.
> - Six → Let’s count: one, two, three, four, five. Five cups.
> _For grown-ups: Added 3 and 2 using cups._

_You listen to everything._  

#### `sprout:a20-02-council` · [main](main.md#a20-02-council)

_The hall gets dark. The fire is low._  
**Thor**: We could stand round him, day and night.  
**Odin**: Then he would live in a ring of spears. No.  
**Baldr**: I would rather not. I would miss people.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): who we like to be with  
> _Baldr would miss people. Who do you like to be with?_  
> _(Wait for an answer.)_  
> - My family → Family is a good answer. Being together feels safe.
> - My friends → Friends are a good answer. Being together feels happy.
> - My pet → A pet is a good answer. Being together feels cosy.
> _For grown-ups: Named the people and pets they like to be with._

**Frigg**: Then I will ask. Everything in the world will promise not to hurt him.  
**Thor**: Everything? That is a great deal of everything.  
**Frigg**: Then I had better start early.  

**Choices**

- **Build the fire up.**
    - You build up the fire. Odin nods at you.
- **Fill the cups.**
    - You fill the cups. Thor’s hands are steady now.
- **Sit by the door and listen.**
    - You sit by the door and listen.

#### `sprout:a20-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a20-02-complete)

_By dawn the gods have a plan._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, ask): what happens first, next and last  
> _Let’s tell it in order. First, Baldr told his dream._  
> _Next, the gods made a plan. What will they do last, tomorrow?_  
> - ✅ Walk and ask the whole world → Yes! First the dream, next the plan, last the walk.
> - Go to sleep for a year → Sleep for a year? Snore! They have work to do. Last comes the walk.
> - Have a big party → Not a party. Last comes the walk, to ask the world.
> _For grown-ups: Put the story in order: first, next, last._

**Frigg**: Tomorrow we walk. We will ask the whole world.  
**Baldr**: Mother, you do not have to.  
**Frigg**: I know.  
_Your Nubbin falls asleep. You carry it down the hill._  

## A20-3. The Oaths

- `a20-03-s1`: Walk with Frigg while she asks the fire, the water and the stones. · _Keep one step behind Frigg._
- `a20-03-s2`: Carry Frigg’s asking to the animals and birds. · _Go over the meadows and along the edge of the wood._
- `a20-03-s3`: Let your Nubbin make its own promise. · _Bring your Nubbin to Frigg on the step._

#### `sprout:a20-03-asking` · [main](main.md#a20-03-asking)

_Frigg walks out at first light. Fulla, you and your Nubbin follow._  
_She asks the fire. The flames lie down low._  
_She asks the water. It goes still as glass._  
_She asks the iron. Every blade on the wall rings._  
_She asks the stones and the trees, one by one._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, do): sorting materials: stone, wood, iron, water  
> _Frigg asked iron, stone, wood and water. (Point to each one in the picture.)_  
> _Which of these is hard and strong: iron, or water? (Tap the hard one.)_  
> Check: `edu:sort:materials:hard-or-soft`
> _For grown-ups: Compared materials: hard iron and stone, and flowing water._

**Frigg**: Everything can be asked. It only takes time.  
_Past an old oak, you see a tiny green sprig. It has two leaves._  
_Your Nubbin stops to look. Frigg walks on._  

#### `sprout:a20-03-asking-done` · [main](main.md#a20-03-asking-done)

**Frigg**: The animals and birds now. My feet are tired. Will you carry my asking?  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): asking for help, and saying yes  
> _Frigg is tired and asks for help._  
> _Is asking for help a good thing to do?_  
> - ✅ Yes, it is brave and sensible → Yes. Asking for help is a good thing. Thank you.
> - No, we should do it all alone → Doing it all alone is very hard. Asking is wise.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about how asking for help is a good thing to do._


#### `sprout:a20-03-beasts` · [main](main.md#a20-03-beasts)

_You carry Frigg’s asking over the meadows._  
_The hare thumps the ground. The bear lies down flat._  
_The reindeer lowers her antlers to the grass._  
_The birds all fly up in one big cloud, and come down again._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS3-1, ask): animals live where their needs are met  
> _Birds live in trees and nests. What do birds need from trees?_  
> - ✅ A safe place to rest and make a nest → Yes! Trees give birds a safe home.
> - A swimming pool → Not a pool! Birds use trees for homes and nests.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that trees are homes for birds._

_Your Nubbin tugs your sleeve. Nobody has asked it._  

#### `sprout:a20-03-promise` · [main](main.md#a20-03-promise)

_Frigg sits on the step. Your Nubbin walks up to her._  
_It stands up as straight as it can._  
**Fulla**: Oh. It wants to be asked.  
**Frigg**: Of course. Everything should be. Come here, little one.  
**Frigg**: Will you promise not to hurt my son?  
_Your Nubbin thinks about it very seriously._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, ask): keeping a promise  
> _A promise is something we say, and then we do._  
> _What do we do with a promise?_  
> - ✅ Keep it → Yes. We keep our promises. It is how friends trust us.
> - Forget it → Forgetting can hurt a friend. We try to keep promises.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about what a promise means and keeping it._


**Choices**

- **Let it put its paw in Frigg’s hand.**
    - Your Nubbin puts its paw in her hand.
    - **Frigg**: That will hold.
- **Let it bow, like the hare.**
    - It bows so low it nearly topples.
    - **Frigg**: Twice promised!
- **Promise for it.**
    - **Frigg**: No. Let it promise for itself.
    - Your Nubbin nods hard, three times. Frigg nods back.

#### `sprout:a20-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a20-03-complete)

_By evening, everything has promised._  
_Fire, water, iron, stones and trees all said yes._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 5  
> _Let’s count the promises on your fingers. Fire._  
> _Water. Iron._  
> _Stones. Trees._  
> _(Count each one. How many did you get?)_  
> Check: `edu:count:promises:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five things on their fingers._

**Frigg**: Everything has promised. Everything I could think of.  
_Frigg gives you her spindle. Your Nubbin falls asleep on the step._  

## A20-4. The Game

- `a20-04-s1`: Go with the gods to the Thing-field. · _Follow the crowd to the ring of stones._
- `a20-04-s2`: Join the game, or watch from the edge. · _Thor will give you a pebble. You can say no._
- `a20-04-s3`: Find Hödr outside the ring. Tell him what is happening. · _Walk round the outside of the stones._

#### `sprout:a20-04-field` · [main](main.md#a20-04-field)

_The Thing-field is a wide green place with a ring of stones._  
_Baldr stands in the middle. The gods throw things at him, for fun._  
_A stone bumps his shoulder. Plop!_  
_It lands in the grass._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.1.4, ask): sound words  
> _The stone goes plop! Which one is a sound word?_  
> - ✅ Plop → Yes! Plop is a sound word. It sounds like what it is.
> - Grass → Grass is a thing. Plop is the sound word.
> _For grown-ups: Found a sound word (plop) in the story._

_Nothing can hurt him, because everything promised._  
**Thor**: Your turn, brother! Stand still!  
_Thor’s big stone stops. Then drop!_  
_It falls to the ground._  
_Everyone laughs. Baldr laughs the most._  
**Thor**: That was a small one. Fetch me the millstone!  
**Fulla**: Thor, the mill is using it.  
_The millstone rolls up to Baldr._  
_It lies down flat, like a dog wanting a tummy rub._  

#### `sprout:a20-04-field-done` · [main](main.md#a20-04-field-done)

_Your Nubbin finds the edge of Baldr’s light._  
_It lies down in it like a cat in a sunbeam._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-PS3-1, ask): sunlight warms  
> _Cats and Nubbins love a sunbeam. Why is a sunny spot cosy?_  
> - ✅ Sunlight makes it warm → Yes! Sunlight warms the ground, so it feels cosy.
> - Sunlight makes it cold → Sunlight warms things up. That is why it feels cosy.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that sunlight makes things warm._


#### `sprout:a20-04-turn` · [main](main.md#a20-04-turn)

**Thor**: Guest! Here is a pebble for you to throw.  
_It is small and smooth. Baldr smiles._  
**Baldr**: Go on. It only tickles.  
_Outside the ring a tall man stands alone, with his hands empty._  
**Fulla**: That is Hödr, Baldr’s brother. He cannot see to throw.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, ask): choosing, and watching is fine too  
> _You may throw, or watch. Is it all right to say, “I will watch”?_  
> - ✅ Yes, watching is fine → Yes. You can choose to watch, and that is fine.
> - No, you must join in → You never have to join in. Choosing to watch is fine.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about making a choice, and that it is fine to watch._


**Choices**

- **Throw the pebble, gently.**
    - Your pebble bumps his knee and rolls back. Everyone cheers.
- **Give it back. Watch from the edge.**
    - **Thor**: Fair enough. Somebody should keep the score.
- **Let your Nubbin have the throw.**
    - It rolls the pebble to Baldr’s feet. He hands it back.
    - **Baldr**: A draw!

#### `sprout:a20-04-hodr` · [main](main.md#a20-04-hodr)

_You walk round to where Hödr stands. He turns his head._  
**Hödr**: You are the guest. I know your footsteps.  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): we hear with our ears  
> _Hödr cannot see, but he hears the laughing. What do we hear with?_  
> - ✅ Our ears → Yes! Ears hear sounds. (Touch your ears.)
> - Our knees → Not knees! (Touch your ears.) Ears are for hearing.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that we hear with our ears, and can still know things without sight._

**Hödr**: Tell me what they are doing. I can hear laughing.  
_So you tell him about Thor’s big stone, and your Nubbin asleep._  
**Hödr**: Thank you. I like to know what is funny.  

#### `sprout:a20-04-hodr-done` · [main](main.md#a20-04-hodr-done)

**Hödr**: Come and tell me the next good one.  
**Hödr**: You tell it better than they do.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, ask): a story has a beginning, middle and end  
> _Every story has a first part._  
> _What comes first in your day: waking up, or going to bed?_  
> - ✅ Waking up → Yes! First you wake up. Bed comes at the end.
> - Going to bed → Bed comes last. First, we wake up.
> _For grown-ups: Put parts of a day in order, as a story has a beginning and an end._


#### `sprout:a20-04-complete` · [main](main.md#a20-04-complete)

_The game goes on till the light goes. Nobody is hurt._  
_Baldr puts his arm round his brother._  
**Baldr**: Did you hear Thor’s big stone go drop?  
**Hödr**: I heard everything. The guest told me the rest.  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, ask): including everyone in the game  
> _The guest told Hödr what he could not see._  
> _How does this help him join in?_  
> - ✅ He can be part of the fun → Yes! Telling someone helps them be part of things.
> - It does not help → It does help. Telling Hödr lets him share the fun.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about including a friend who cannot see._

_Your Nubbin follows them home, holding Hödr’s bootlace._  

## A20-5. The Old Woman

- `a20-05-s1`: Fetch water for Fensalir. · _Take the pails to the spring and carry them back._
- `a20-05-s2`: Sit by the fire while Frigg talks with the traveller. · _Wind the wool, and stay in the room._

#### `sprout:a20-05-water` · [main](main.md#a20-05-water)

_You fetch water. You take two pails._  
_Your Nubbin takes a thimble._  
_It brings back one drop. It carries it like a crown!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): which holds more  
> _You carry pails, and your Nubbin carries a thimble. Which holds more water?_  
> - ✅ The pail → Yes! The pail is big, so it holds more. The thimble holds a drop.
> - The thimble → The thimble is tiny. The pail is bigger, so it holds more.
> _For grown-ups: Compared a pail and a thimble to see which holds more._

_At the door stands an old woman, wrapped in shawls._  
_You can only see her hands._  
**Fulla**: A traveller! Bring the water in.  
_Your Nubbin looks at her. Then it hides behind your legs._  
_It has never done that before._  

#### `sprout:a20-05-talk` · [main](main.md#a20-05-talk)

_Frigg gives the old woman the best seat, and a cup of water._  
**The Old Woman**: What are the gods doing at the Thing-field, lady?  
**Frigg**: Playing. Nothing can hurt my son. Everything promised.  
**The Old Woman**: Everything? Every single thing?  
**Frigg**: Every thing but one little sprout. It was too young to ask.  
_The old woman puts her cup down very carefully._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): feeling unsure, and what helps  
> _Your Nubbin hid behind your legs. It felt unsure._  
> _What helps when we feel unsure?_  
> - ✅ Stay close to a friend or a grown-up → Yes. Staying close helps. Feeling unsure is allowed.
> - Pretend we are fine → It is fine to feel unsure. Staying close helps more.
> - Run off alone → Staying close to someone safe helps more.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about feeling unsure and staying close to someone safe._

**The Old Woman**: Too young. Yes. Very wise.  

**Choices**

- **Keep winding the wool.**
    - You keep winding, round and round.
- **Look up at the old woman.**
    - You look up. She smiles under the shawls.
    - Then it is only shawls.
- **Pick up your Nubbin.**
    - You hug your Nubbin. It stops shaking.

#### `sprout:a20-05-talk-done` · [main](main.md#a20-05-talk-done)

_The old woman thanks Frigg, and goes out of the door._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds  
> _Door starts with the sound duh. Which word starts with duh too?_
