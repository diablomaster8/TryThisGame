## A22-1. The Candle’s Story

*Encanto (Walt Disney Animation Studios, 2021): the flight, the river and the miracle* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Candle page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time once Nova’s atlas is in the Grove; the first quest on the page.

**Cast:** Abuela Alma (Alma Madrigal, the grandmother who keeps the candle (Encanto (Walt Disney Animation Studios, 2021)); licensed design), Mirabel (Mirabel Madrigal, the one without a gift (Encanto (Walt Disney Animation Studios, 2021)); licensed design)

### Step 1: Step into the painted page and hear how the candle began.

Hint: Touch the candle in the window on the atlas page, then sit at the edge of the painted bed. · check: `explore:a22-page:painted-valley`

#### <a id="a22-01-story"></a>Scene `a22-01-story`

_Nova’s atlas falls open at a valley ringed by green mountains. White walls, red roofs, tall palms, and a candle in one window._  
_The page doesn’t come alive at once. It stays painted, and you step into the paint._  
_It is night, years ago. In a bedroom made of brushstrokes, an old woman sits on a bed beside a little girl in round glasses._  
_The little girl is far too excited to sleep. The old woman looks up at you, as if you had always been sitting there._  
**Abuela Alma**: Come in, then. You may as well hear it too. Mirabel has heard it a hundred times, and she still wants it.  
**Mirabel** _(happy)_: Tell the river part!  
**Abuela Alma**: First the beginning. When I was young there was trouble in the land where we lived, and we had to leave our home.  
_The painting changes. A young couple on a road at night, with three babies wrapped against the cold._  
**Abuela Alma**: My Pedro carried two of them, and I carried one. We walked for days, with the others who had to leave too.  
_Far behind them, on a ridge, there is a thin dark line of riders. The painting does not bring them any closer._  
**Abuela Alma**: At a river, they caught up with us. Pedro told me to cross with the babies. He stayed on the bank, so that we could.  
**Abuela Alma** _(sad)_: He did not come.  
_In the painting, the candle in her hand blazes. The mountains rise round the river like hands closing round a flame._  
**Abuela Alma** _(soft)_: And the candle made us a home, where no one could ever take it from us. A house that loves us. A miracle.  

**Choices**

- **Keep listening.**
    - You keep very still. The little girl has her fist in the blanket, holding on to the part about the river.
- **Ask about Pedro.**
    - **Abuela Alma**: He was kind, and he was brave, and he was not very good at dancing. That is enough for one night.
    - She says it lightly. Her hand on the blanket doesn’t move at all.

#### <a id="a22-01-story-done"></a>Done `a22-01-story-done`

**Abuela Alma**: Every child in this family is given a gift, when they are old enough. Tomorrow is yours, Mirabel.  
**Mirabel**: What will it be?  
**Abuela Alma** _(soft)_: Sleep, and see.  

### Step 2: Walk into the valley on the morning of Antonio’s ceremony and meet the family.

Hint: Let the page turn, then climb the hill from the market square to the house with green shutters. · check: `visit:a22-casita:gate`

#### <a id="a22-01-casita"></a>Scene `a22-01-casita`

_The page turns by itself. The paint goes thin and bright and real: morning, ten years later, and a market full of noise._  
_Up the hill is a house with white walls, green shutters and a red roof. Its shutters open and close by themselves._  
_A young woman in round glasses and an embroidered skirt comes down the steps two at a time, with a list._  
_You know the glasses. The little girl from the painting has grown up, and the house is the one the candle made._  
**Mirabel**: Hi! Hello! You’re the traveller. Abuela said there’d be a traveller. Sorry, are you a traveller?  
_The floor tiles clack in a ripple across the doorway, like a dog thumping its tail. Mirabel pats the doorframe._  
**Mirabel**: That’s Casita. Casita, be nice. Guest.  
_A shutter swings out and taps you gently on the shoulder._  
**Mirabel**: I’m Mirabel. I don’t have a gift, before anyone tells you. It’s fine. It’s totally fine.  

**Choices**

- **Say you are the traveller.**
    - **Mirabel**: Great. Perfect. You’re in the blue room. The house likes guests. Mostly.
- **Point out that your Nubbin is travelling too.**
    - **Mirabel**: Oh, hello! Look at you. Antonio is going to lose his mind. In a good way.
- **Ask what the list is for.**
    - **Mirabel**: Tonight. Antonio’s gift ceremony. My little cousin. Everything has to be perfect, so it’s a long list.

**Abuela Alma**: Nobody in this house walks about in the dark. Come.  
_She lights it from the candle in the window. The flame is gold and steady, and it isn’t yours, and it is very kind of her._

### Step 1: Go down to the market with Mirabel.

Hint: Follow Mirabel down the hill to the square. · check: `walk:a22-market:town-square`

#### <a id="a22-02-market"></a>Scene `a22-02-market`

_Market morning. The square is full of stalls: oranges, coffee sacks, bolts of bright cloth and somebody’s goat._  
**Mirabel**: Okay. Quick tour. Everyone in my family has a gift, and everyone in town knows exactly what it is.  
_A tall young woman walks past with a donkey over each shoulder._  
**Mirabel**: That’s Luisa, my sister. Super strong. Hi, Luisa!  
**Luisa**: Hi. Can’t stop. The church is crooked again.  
_A woman at a stall has a small grey cloud over her head. It is raining on her onions._  
**Mirabel**: Tía Pepa. Her weather is her mood. Tía, the cloud.  
**Tía Pepa**: What cloud? There is no cloud. I am perfectly calm.  
_The cloud thunders, very quietly. A man in a yellow shirt hurries over and fans it away with his hat, laughing._  
**Mirabel**: Tío Félix. He says his gift is Tía Pepa.  
**Tío Félix** _(happy)_: It is a very big gift. I am a lucky man!  

**Choices**

- **Tell Tía Pepa the cloud is fine.**
    - **Tía Pepa**: It is fine, isn’t it?
    - The cloud turns into a small, pleased rainbow.
- **Ask Félix how he copes.**
    - **Tío Félix**: Umbrellas. Many umbrellas. And I tell her she’s beautiful, which is true, so it’s easy.
- **Check on your Nubbin.**
    - Your Nubbin has found the goat, and the goat has found your Nubbin. They are deciding whether to be friends.

#### <a id="a22-02-market-done"></a>Done `a22-02-market-done`

_Mirabel ticks something off her list and nearly walks into a post._  
**Mirabel**: Next, my cousins. Brace yourself.  

### Step 2: Meet the rest of the Madrigals at the house.

Hint: Go back up the hill with Mirabel and talk to the family in the courtyard. · check: `talk:a22-madrigals:courtyard`

#### <a id="a22-02-cousins"></a>Scene `a22-02-cousins`

_In the courtyard a young woman steps out of a cloud of petals. Perfect hair, perfect smile, roses at her feet._  
**Mirabel**: My sister Isabela. Flowers. Everyone loves her. I love her!  
**Isabela**: Mirabel. And a guest. How nice.  
_She smiles at you, and an orchid grows out of the wall beside your head. It is a lovely smile, and it doesn’t move at all._  
_A girl in a pale dress drifts past, whispering very fast to nobody._  
**Mirabel**: Cousin Dolores. She hears everything. If you sneeze in the market, she knows.  
**Dolores**: You did sneeze in the market. Bless you.  
_A boy turns round, and he is Mirabel, then Félix. Then he is himself again, a lanky teenager, grinning._  
**Camilo**: Camilo. Faces. I can do yours if you stand still.  

**Choices**

- **Stand still.**
    - Camilo looks at you hard, and then you are looking at yourself, a little taller, with better hair.
    - **Camilo**: I improved you. You’re welcome.
- **Don’t stand still.**
    - **Camilo**: Fair. People hate it. Mostly my sisters. Mostly when I do them.

### Step 3: Eat in the kitchen with Julieta and Agustín.

Hint: Follow the smell of arepas to the kitchen. · check: `visit:a22-kitchen:casita`

#### <a id="a22-02-kitchen"></a>Scene `a22-02-kitchen`

_In the kitchen a woman with a kind, tired face is cooking arepas. A boy with a burnt finger eats one, and the burn is gone._  
**Julieta**: You must be hungry. Everyone who comes up that hill is hungry. Sit.  
_The arepa is hot and buttery, with cheese in the middle. The ache in your legs from the long page just stops._  
**Agustín**: Welcome! Welcome. Don’t mind the bee. I’m used to the bee.  
_A gentle man in glasses waves from the doorway. A bee is sitting on his nose._  
**Mirabel**: Mamá and Papá. So that’s everyone. Everyone with a gift, I mean, and me.  
_She counts on her fingers, gets to the end of one hand, and stops on a finger as if it were sore._  
_Across the courtyard, at the far end of the house, there is a tower with a dark door. Everybody manages not to look at it, all at once._  
_Félix starts to say a name. Pepa’s cloud cracks with thunder, and Félix turns it into a cough._  
_Then everybody talks at once, about chairs._  

**Choices**

- **Ask about the tower.**
    - **Mirabel**: Oh! That’s nobody. Nobody’s up there. It’s just a room.
    - She says it in a rush. Julieta puts another arepa on your plate, which is also an answer.
- **Let it go.**
    - You let it go. Mirabel gives you a small grateful look, and the house rattles a single tile, like a sigh.
- **Watch your Nubbin.**
    - Your Nubbin is at the courtyard’s edge, looking up at the tower. A small grey face looks back from a crack, and vanishes.

#### <a id="a22-03-hiding"></a>Scene `a22-03-hiding`

_Evening: the courtyard is full of the whole town in their best clothes, every one holding a candle. Antonio is not._  
**Tía Pepa** _(shock)_: Where is he? Where is my baby?  
**Mirabel**: I’ll find him. Come on. He likes hiding places.  
_You find him under the bed in the nursery. He is five, he is lying on his tummy with his chin on his fists, and he is not coming out._  
**Antonio** _(sad)_: What if I don’t get one?  
_Mirabel lies down flat on the floor, so their faces are level. You do too, and so does your Nubbin._  
**Mirabel**: Then you get to be you, which is already the best thing in this house.  
**Antonio**: You didn’t get one.  
**Mirabel** _(soft)_: No. I didn’t.  

**Choices**

- **Hold out your hand.**
    - Antonio looks at your hand for a long time, then takes Mirabel’s instead. That is the right answer, and you both know it.
- **Let your Nubbin go under first.**
    - Your Nubbin wriggles under the bed and lies down beside him, nose to nose. Antonio giggles before he can stop himself.
- **Wait.**
    - Nobody hurries him. After a while a small hand comes out from under the bed and finds Mirabel’s sleeve.

#### <a id="a22-03-hiding-done"></a>Done `a22-03-hiding-done`

**Antonio**: Will you walk me? Both of you?  
**Mirabel** _(soft)_: All the way to the door.

#### <a id="a22-03-door"></a>Scene `a22-03-door`

_The stairs are lined with family. On the landing a new door glows faintly, with no picture on it yet._  
_Mirabel walks Antonio up, holding his hand. You walk two steps behind, between rows of candlelit faces._  
_Halfway up, Mirabel slows down. You remember the painting: a little girl, ten years ago, on these same stairs._  
_For a moment you see that night instead. Little Mirabel at a glowing door, and Abuela stooping to her._  
**Abuela Alma** _(soft)_: Whatever Gift awaits will be just as special as you.  
_In the memory, little Mirabel reaches for the handle. The door’s light goes out, and the door fades back into wall._  
_Then it is now again. Antonio is tugging her hand, and Abuela, at the top, is holding out the candle._  
_Antonio touches his door. It blazes with light, and a picture of him grows on it, with animals all round._  
_A toucan swoops down and lands on his head. A jaguar pads out of the jungle in his new room, then capybaras, then coatis._  
**Antonio** _(happy)_: They’re talking! I can hear them!  
_The courtyard roars. Mirabel cheers louder than anybody, and goes on cheering a beat too long._  

**Choices**

- **Cheer with Mirabel.**
    - You cheer with her, until she stops, and then you stop too.
- **Look at Mirabel.**
    - She catches you looking and smiles hard. Her glasses have steamed up, and she says it’s the candles.

_Your Nubbin trills at the jaguar. Antonio listens, delighted._  
**Antonio** _(happy)_: She says it smells like a garden. And your Nubbin says you snore.

#### <a id="a22-04-party"></a>Scene `a22-04-party`

_The party fills the house. Music, dancing, food on every surface, Luisa holding up the piano so people can dance under it._  
_Mirabel slips away to the edge of the courtyard, where it’s quieter. You follow her._  
**Mirabel** _(sad)_: I’m happy for him. I am. I’m so happy for him.  
_There is a crack in the plaster by her hand. As you watch, it moves: it runs up the wall like a crack in ice, and splits._  
_The cracks race across the tiles, under the dancers’ feet, up the stairs and along the landing, towards the candle._  
_In the window, the candle’s flame shudders, and shrinks, and shudders._  
**Mirabel** _(shock)_: No. No, no, no.  
_Mirabel runs. You run after her, through the dancers, and nobody else looks down._  

**Choices**

- **Follow the cracks.**
    - You keep your eyes on the cracks. They are racing ahead of you, and they are all going one way: to the candle.
- **Keep up with Mirabel.**
    - You keep at her elbow, through a crowd that keeps stepping back into your way, all smiling.

#### <a id="a22-04-party-done"></a>Done `a22-04-party-done`

_You reach the landing with her. The cracks reach it with you._  

### Step 2: Help Mirabel show the family the cracks.

Hint: Stand on the landing by the candle with Mirabel. · check: `inspect:a22-cracks:landing`

#### <a id="a22-04-cracks"></a>Scene `a22-04-cracks`

_The music stops. The whole town turns round and looks up at the landing._  
_The wall behind her is smooth, and the tiles are whole. Casita has mended every crack in the time it took to shout._  
**Abuela Alma** _(think)_: Mirabel. What cracks?  
**Mirabel**: There were cracks. Right here, all through the house, and the candle was…  
_The candle burns tall and still in the window, as if it had never moved._  
**Isabela**: Mirabel. It’s Antonio’s night.  
**Abuela Alma** _(happy)_: Everything is fine. The miracle is strong. Music, please!  
_The music starts again, louder. People dance a little too fast, and nobody quite looks at Mirabel._  

**Choices**

    - Abuela looks at you, polite and closed, the way you look at a guest who has said something odd at dinner.
    - **Abuela Alma**: Our guest is tired. It was a long journey.
- **Say nothing, and stay by Mirabel.**
    - You stand beside her and say nothing. It isn’t much, but she isn’t standing there alone.
