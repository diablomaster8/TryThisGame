# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A22: The Candle

## A22-1. The Candle’s Story

- `a22-01-s1`: Step into the painted page and hear how the candle began. · _Touch the candle in the window. Then sit at the edge of the painted bed._
- `a22-01-s2`: Walk into the valley and meet the family. · _Climb the hill from the market to the house with green shutters._

#### `sprout:a22-01-story` · [main](main.md#a22-01-story)

_A big book opens. It shows tall green mountains._  
_A candle glows in one window. You step into the picture._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, do): find Colombia’s mountains and seas  
> _This is a real place. It is a country called Colombia._  
> _It has tall mountains. It has sea on two sides._  
> _(Point to the tall green mountains in the picture.)_  
> Check: `edu:point:mountains:colombia`
> _For grown-ups: Learned that Colombia is a real country in South America, with tall mountains and sea on two sides._

_It is night, long ago. Abuela sits by a little girl in round glasses._  
**Abuela Alma**: Come in. Listen too. Mirabel loves this story.  
**Abuela Alma**: Long ago, we had to leave our home. It was not safe.  
_Abuela and Pedro walked for days with three babies._  
_Trouble was far behind them, small as a thread._  
**Abuela Alma**: At a river, Pedro stayed behind so we could cross.  
**Abuela Alma**: Pedro died that night. I miss him every day.  
**Abuela Alma**: Then a candle lit, and a warm house grew around us.  

**Choices**

- **Keep listening.**
    - You sit very still. The little girl holds her blanket tight.
- **Ask about Pedro.**
    - **Abuela Alma**: Pedro was kind. He was brave. He was a funny dancer.
    - Abuela smiles a little. Her eyes are wet.

#### `sprout:a22-01-story-done` · [main](main.md#a22-01-story-done)

**Abuela Alma**: Every child in this family gets a gift one day.  
**Abuela Alma**: Tomorrow is yours, Mirabel.  
**Mirabel**: What will it be?  
**Abuela Alma**: Sleep, and see.  
_Mirabel feels excited and nervous, both at once._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): naming two feelings at once  
> _Mirabel feels excited and nervous. Can we feel two feelings together?_  
> - ✅ Yes, we can → Yes! Feelings can mix, like colours. Both are okay to feel.
> - No, only one → It can feel like one, but often two come together. Both are okay.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about feeling excited and nervous at the same time._


#### `sprout:a22-01-casita` · [main](main.md#a22-01-casita)

_The page turns. It is morning, ten years later._  
_A market is full of noise. Up the hill is a house._  
_It has white walls and green shutters. The shutters open by themselves!_  
_A young woman in round glasses runs down the steps._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, do): clap the three beats of abuela  
> _Abuela is Spanish for grandmother. Say it with me: a-bue-la._  
> _(Clap each beat: a, bue, la. That is three claps.)_  
> Check: `edu:clap:syllables:abuela`
> _For grown-ups: Learned that abuela is Spanish for grandmother and clapped its three syllables._

**Mirabel**: Hello! You are the traveller. Abuela said you would come.  
_The floor tiles clack, like a dog wagging its tail._  
**Mirabel**: That is Casita. Casita, be nice. We have a guest.  
_A shutter taps your shoulder, very gently._  
**Mirabel**: I am Mirabel. I do not have a gift. It is fine.  

**Choices**

- **Say you are the traveller.**
    - **Mirabel**: Great! You are in the blue room. The house likes guests.
- **Point out your Nubbin is travelling too.**
    - **Mirabel**: Oh, hello! Antonio will love you.
- **Ask what the list is for.**
    - **Mirabel**: Tonight is Antonio’s gift night. Everything must be just right.

#### `sprout:a22-01-lamp` · [main](main.md#a22-01-lamp)

_Abuela meets you in the courtyard. She is tall and straight._  
_You have a little lamp. Its glass is dark._  
**Abuela Alma**: Nobody in this house walks in the dark. Come.  
_She lights your lamp from the candle in the window._  
_The flame is gold and steady. It is very kind of her._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-PS4-2, ask): we need light to see  
> _Your lamp is dark, then lit. What does a lit lamp help us do?_  
> - ✅ See in the dark → Yes! We see things when there is light.
> - Hear far away → Light helps our eyes, not our ears. We see when there is light.
> - Smell flowers → Light helps our eyes. A lamp helps us see in the dark.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that we see things when there is light._


#### `sprout:a22-01-lamp-flicker` · [main](main.md#a22-01-lamp-flicker)

_She tips her candle to your wick._  
_Now two flames lean together, one gold and one blue._

> _For grown-ups: Learned that two flames give more light than one._


#### `sprout:a22-01-lamp-lit` · [main](main.md#a22-01-lamp-lit)

_Variant of `a22-01-lamp`._

_Your lamp is lit already. The flame stands up straight and steady._  
**Abuela Alma**: Someone looks after that one well. Good.  
_She nods at it, the way you nod to a neighbour._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): a flame is warm  
> _Is the flame in your lamp warm or cold?_  
> - ✅ Warm → Yes! Flames are hot. Never touch one.
> - Cold → Flames are hot, not cold. Never touch one.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a flame is hot and must never be touched._


#### `sprout:a22-01-lamp-warm` · [main](main.md#a22-01-lamp-warm)

_Variant of `a22-01-lamp`._

_Abuela meets you in the courtyard. She is tall and straight._  
_Your lamp burns a deep red-gold. The glass is warm._  
**Abuela Alma**: That one burns as if somebody is glad of it.  
_She holds her hands to it, as to a fire. Then she remembers herself._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): flames give heat  
> _A flame gives light, and it gives heat._  
> _That is why the glass is warm._  
> _Never touch a flame, or hot glass. Leave candles to grown-ups._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that flames give heat as well as light._

## A22-2. The Family

- `a22-02-s1`: Go down to the market with Mirabel. · _Follow Mirabel down the hill to the square._
- `a22-02-s2`: Meet the rest of the family at the house. · _Go back up the hill and say hello in the courtyard._
- `a22-02-s3`: Eat in the kitchen with Julieta and Agustín. · _Follow the smell of arepas to the kitchen._

#### `sprout:a22-02-market` · [main](main.md#a22-02-market)

_It is market morning. There are oranges, bread and sacks of coffee beans._  
**Mirabel**: Quick tour! Everyone in my family has a special gift.  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, ask): coffee grows on mountain bushes  
> _Those sacks hold coffee beans. Where does coffee grow here?_  
> - ✅ On bushes in the mountains → Yes! Coffee grows on bushes on the mountain slopes in Colombia.
> - In the sea → Not in the sea. Coffee grows on bushes on the mountain slopes.
> - In the sky → Not in the sky. It grows on bushes, high on the mountains.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that coffee grows on bushes in the mountains of Colombia._

_Luisa walks by. She carries two donkeys, one on each shoulder!_  
**Luisa**: Hi! Can’t stop. The church is crooked.  
_Tía Pepa has a little grey cloud above her head._  
_Plip, plip, plip! It rains on her onions._  
**Tío Félix**: Don’t worry, my love. We have umbrellas!  

**Choices**

- **Tell Tía Pepa the cloud is fine.**
    - **Tía Pepa**: It is fine, isn’t it?
    - The cloud turns into a small, pleased rainbow.
- **Ask Félix how he copes.**
    - **Tío Félix**: Umbrellas! And I tell her she is lovely, which is true.
- **Check on your Nubbin.**
    - Your Nubbin has found the goat. They are deciding to be friends.

#### `sprout:a22-02-market-done` · [main](main.md#a22-02-market-done)

_Mirabel has ticked three things on her list._  
_She ticks one more. Then she nearly walks into a post._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.2, ask): add one more: 3 and 1 make 4  
> _Three ticks, and one more tick. How many ticks now?_  
> - ✅ Four → Yes! Three and one more is four.
> - Three → Let’s count: one, two, three, and one more. That is four.
> - Five → Let’s count again: one, two, three, four. Four ticks.
> _For grown-ups: Added one to three and got four._

**Mirabel**: Next, my cousins. Hold on tight!  

#### `sprout:a22-02-cousins` · [main](main.md#a22-02-cousins)

_In the courtyard, flowers pop up! Pink, red and yellow._  
**Mirabel**: My sister Isabela grows flowers. Everyone loves her.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): what makes you you  
> _Camilo can look like anyone. What makes you you?_  
> - ✅ My own smile, laugh and heart → Yes! Nobody has your smile, your laugh and your heart. That is you.
> - My hair only → Hair is just one bit. Your smile, laugh and heart are you too.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about what makes each person themselves._

**Isabela**: Hello, guest. How nice.  
_A girl in a pale dress whispers to herself. That is Dolores._  
**Dolores**: You sneezed in the market. Bless you.  
_A boy turns into Mirabel! Then Félix!_  
_Then himself. That is Camilo._  
**Camilo**: I can do your face. Stand still!  

**Choices**

- **Stand still.**
    - Camilo looks hard. Then you are looking at yourself, a little taller.
    - **Camilo**: I improved you. You are welcome.
- **Don’t stand still.**
    - **Camilo**: Fair. People hate it. Mostly my sisters.

#### `sprout:a22-02-kitchen` · [main](main.md#a22-02-kitchen)

_In the kitchen, Julieta is cooking arepas. They smell warm and buttery._  
**Julieta**: You look hungry. Sit down and eat.  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, ask): wash hands before eating  
> _Julieta says, wash your hands before you eat. Why do we do that?_  
> - ✅ To wash away germs → Yes! Soap and water wash germs away, so we stay well.
> - To make our hands cold → Not for cold. We wash to wash germs away and stay well.
> - So the arepa jumps → Ha! No. We wash to wash germs away and stay well.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that washing hands with soap and water washes germs away._

_An arepa is a round corn cake. This one has melted cheese inside._  
_Yum! Your tummy feels full and happy._  
**Agustín**: Welcome! Don’t mind the bee on my nose.  
_At the end of the house is a tower with a dark door._  
_Nobody looks at it. Everybody talks about chairs._  

**Choices**

- **Ask about the tower.**
    - **Mirabel**: That is nobody. Nobody is up there. It is just a room.
    - Julieta puts another arepa on your plate. That is an answer too.
- **Let it go.**
    - You let it go. Mirabel gives you a small, thankful look.
- **Watch your Nubbin.**
    - Your Nubbin looks up at the tower.
    - A small grey face looks back, then hides.

#### `sprout:a22-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a22-02-complete)

_All day, Mirabel hangs lanterns. She carries chairs._  
_She ties ribbons._  
_Nobody asks her to. She does it anyway._  
**Mirabel**: It is fine. Somebody has to do the not-magic jobs.  
_She smiles, but her smile looks small._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.1.4, ask): a feeling can hide behind “fine”  
> _Mirabel says, “It is fine.” But her smile looks small._  
> _How does she feel?_  
> - ✅ A little sad → Yes. Her words say fine, but her small smile shows sad.
> - Very silly → Look at her small smile. She feels a little sad.
> - Sleepy → Her small smile shows a feeling. She feels a little sad.
> _For grown-ups: Noticed that a face and a voice can show a feeling the words hide._

_Your Nubbin leans on her ankle. Lean, lean, lean._  

## A22-3. Antonio’s Door

- `a22-03-s1`: Find Antonio before his gift night. · _Look in the nursery, and under the bed._
- `a22-03-s2`: Walk Antonio up to his door. · _Climb the stairs two steps behind Mirabel and Antonio._

#### `sprout:a22-03-hiding` · [main](main.md#a22-03-hiding)

_It is evening. Everyone wears their best clothes and holds a candle._  
**Tía Pepa**: Where is Antonio?  
_You find him under the bed. He is five, and he is scared._  
**Antonio**: What if I do not get a gift?  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): what helps a scared friend  
> _Antonio is scared. What helps a scared friend?_  
> - ✅ Staying close and listening → Yes! A friend who stays close and listens helps a lot.
> - Shouting at him → Shouting makes scared feelings bigger. Staying close helps.
> - Leaving him alone → Alone can feel worse. Staying close and listening helps.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about how to help a scared friend: stay close and listen._

_Mirabel lies flat on the floor, so her face is by his._  
**Mirabel**: Then you get to be you. That is the best thing in this house.  
**Antonio**: You did not get one.  
**Mirabel**: No. I did not. And I am still me.  

**Choices**

- **Hold out your hand.**
    - Antonio looks at your hand. Then he takes Mirabel’s.
    - That is just right.
- **Let your Nubbin go under first.**
    - Your Nubbin wriggles under the bed. Antonio giggles before he can stop.
- **Wait.**
    - Nobody hurries him. A small hand comes out and finds Mirabel’s sleeve.

#### `sprout:a22-03-hiding-done` · [main](main.md#a22-03-hiding-done)

**Antonio**: Will you walk me? Both of you?  
**Mirabel**: All the way to the door.  
_Antonio’s heart goes thump, thump, thump. Let’s help it slow down._  

> **Lesson** (health, CASEL: Self-management, do): slow breaths calm a worried body  
> _(Breathe in slowly through your nose. Breathe out like blowing a candle.)_  
> _(Do it three times with Antonio: in, out; in, out; in, out.)_  
> Check: `edu:breathe:slowly:3`
> _For grown-ups: Practised three slow breaths to calm a worried body._


#### `sprout:a22-03-door` · [main](main.md#a22-03-door)

_The stairs are full of family, holding candles._  
_A new door glows softly. It has no picture yet._  
_Mirabel holds Antonio’s hand. You walk behind._  
_Halfway up, Mirabel slows down. You see a memory._  
_Long ago, a little Mirabel stood at a glowing door. The door went dark._  
**Abuela Alma**: Whatever happens, you are loved, little one.  
_Then it is now. Antonio touches his door, and it glows!_  
_A toucan lands on his head. A jaguar and capybaras come out!_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): a bird has wings and a beak  
> _Which of these animals has wings and a big beak?_  
> - ✅ The toucan → Yes! A toucan has wings to fly and a big beak to eat fruit.
> - The jaguar → A jaguar has paws and fur, not wings.
> - The capybara → A capybara has no wings. The toucan has wings and a big beak.
> _For grown-ups: Told a bird from other animals by its wings and beak._

**Antonio**: I can hear them! They are talking!  
_Everyone cheers. Mirabel cheers the loudest, for a long time._  

**Choices**

- **Cheer with Mirabel.**
    - You cheer with her. When she stops, you stop too.
- **Look at Mirabel.**
    - She catches you looking and smiles hard. Her glasses are steamy.

#### `sprout:a22-03-door-done` · [main](main.md#a22-03-door-done)

_The jaguar sniffs your Nubbin. Sniff, sniff, sniff!_  
_Your Nubbin sniffs back. Then it trills._  
_Trrrill!_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sound: sniff and snore start with sn  
> _Sniff starts with “sn”. Which word starts the same way?_  
> - ✅ Snore → Yes! Snore and sniff both start with sn.
> - Toucan → Toucan starts with t. Snore starts with sn, like sniff.
> - Jaguar → Jaguar starts with j. Snore starts with sn, like sniff.
> _For grown-ups: Matched words that begin with the same first sounds._

**Antonio**: She says it smells like a garden. Your Nubbin says you snore!  

#### `sprout:a22-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a22-03-complete)

_A photograph is taken on the stairs._  
_Everyone crowds round Antonio. The photographer waves Mirabel away._  
_She ends up at the edge, holding a vase._  
_Your Nubbin sits by her feet._  
_Now Mirabel, the vase and the Nubbin are in the picture._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count three things at the edge of a picture  
> _Count the things at the edge of the photo._  
> _(Count with me: Mirabel is one, the vase is two, your Nubbin is three.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:photo-things:3`
> _For grown-ups: Counted three things in a picture._

**Mirabel**: Thanks.  

## A22-4. The Cracks

- `a22-04-s1`: Follow Mirabel through the party. · _Stay close to Mirabel at the edge of the courtyard._
- `a22-04-s2`: Help Mirabel show the family the cracks. · _Stand on the landing by the candle with Mirabel._

#### `sprout:a22-04-party` · [main](main.md#a22-04-party)

_The party is loud and bright. There is music and dancing._  

> **Lesson** (music, ELOF: Approaches to Learning, do): clap a steady beat  
> _A musician plays a tiple. That is a small guitar from Colombia._  
> _(Clap a steady beat with the music: one, two, three, four.)_  
> Check: `edu:clap:beat:party`
> _For grown-ups: Clapped a steady beat and met the tiple, a small Colombian guitar._

_Luisa holds up the piano, so people can dance under it!_  
_Mirabel slips to the quiet edge. You follow._  
**Mirabel**: I am happy for him. I really am.  
_A crack runs up the wall by her hand. Crrrack!_  
_Cracks race across the tiles, towards the candle._  
_The candle’s flame shakes and shrinks._  
**Mirabel**: Oh no. The candle!  
_Mirabel runs. You run after her._  
_Nobody else looks down._  

**Choices**

- **Follow the cracks.**
    - You watch the cracks. They race ahead, and they all go one way.
- **Keep up with Mirabel.**
    - You stay at her elbow, through a crowd that keeps smiling.

#### `sprout:a22-04-party-done` · [main](main.md#a22-04-party-done)

_You reach the landing with her. The cracks reach it with you._  
_The floor tiles make a pattern: red, blue, red, blue._  

> **Lesson** (maths, ELOF: Mathematics Development, ask): a colour pattern: what comes next  
> _Red, blue, red, blue. What colour comes next?_  
> - ✅ Red → Yes! Red, blue, red, blue, red. The pattern repeats.
> - Blue → Blue came just before. After blue, the pattern goes back to red.
> - Yellow → There is no yellow tile. The pattern repeats: red comes next.
> _For grown-ups: Continued a repeating colour pattern._


#### `sprout:a22-04-cracks` · [main](main.md#a22-04-cracks)

**Mirabel**: Everybody! The house is in danger! Look at the cracks!  
_The music stops. Everyone looks up._  
_But the wall is smooth. The tiles are whole._  
_Casita mended them._  
**Abuela Alma**: Mirabel. What cracks?  
_Mirabel points. There is nothing there._  
**Abuela Alma**: Everything is fine. Music, please!  
_The music starts again. Nobody looks at Mirabel._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): how it feels when no one believes you  
> _Nobody believes Mirabel. How does she feel?_  
> - ✅ Lonely and worried → Yes. A friend who stays beside her helps.
> - Very happy → Look at her face. She feels lonely and worried.
> - Sleepy → She is wide awake, and she feels lonely and worried.
> _For grown-ups: Named the feelings of someone who is not believed: lonely and worried._


**Choices**

- **Say you saw them too.**
    - You say, “I saw them.” Abuela looks at you, polite and closed.
    - **Abuela Alma**: Our guest is tired. It was a long journey.
- **Say nothing, and stay by Mirabel.**
    - You stand beside her. It is not much, but she is not alone.

#### `sprout:a22-04-lamp` · [main](main.md#a22-04-lamp)

_Your lamp has a gold flame. Abuela lit it._  
_The flame shakes, just like the candle._  
_Then it goes still, when the candle does._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): moving air makes a flame wobble  
> _In the real world, what makes a candle flame wobble?_  
> - ✅ Moving air, like a breeze → Yes! A breeze or a puff makes a flame dance.
> - A song → Not a song. Moving air, like a breeze, makes a flame wobble.
> - A colour → Not a colour. Moving air, like a breeze, makes a flame wobble.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that moving air makes a flame wobble._

**Mirabel**: Yours too! You saw it!  
_She squeezes your wrist, then lets go._

## A22-5. Abuela’s Prayer

- `a22-05-s1`: Follow the light on the landing at midnight. · _Creep out to the banister and stay out of sight._
- `a22-05-s2`: Decide how you will help Mirabel: eyes, ears or hands. · _Choose the job you like best. All three help._

#### `sprout:a22-05-prayer` · [main](main.md#a22-05-prayer)

_Late at night, a soft light shines on the landing._  
_Mirabel hides behind the banister. You hide too._  
_Abuela stands by the candle. A painting hangs beside it._  
_It shows a young man with a kind face. It is Pedro._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.His.2.K-2, ask): families remember people with pictures and stories  
> _Abuela keeps a painting of Pedro. How do families remember people?_  
> - ✅ With pictures and stories → Yes! Pictures and stories keep people close in our hearts.
> - They never talk about them → Talking about people we miss helps. Pictures and stories keep them close.
