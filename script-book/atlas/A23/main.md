# Atlas, page A23: Remember Who You Are

## A23-1. The Presentation

*The Lion King (Disney, 1994): the presentation of the new cub at Pride Rock* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Remember Who You Are page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time once Nova’s atlas is in the Grove; the first quest on the page.

**Cast:** Rafiki (Rafiki, the old mandrill who keeps the baobab (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film), Zazu (Zazu, the king’s hornbill majordomo (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film), Mufasa (Mufasa, the king (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film), Sarabi (Sarabi, the queen, Simba’s mother (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film)

### Step 1: Step down into the page and find Rafiki’s baobab.

Hint: Touch the red rock on the page, then follow the path down to the big tree. · check: `explore:a23-baobab:plain`

#### <a id="a23-01-page"></a>Scene `a23-01-page`

_Nova’s atlas falls open at a page of red and gold: a great rock standing over a plain, and the sun just coming up behind it._  
_The light spills off the paper and over your hands, warm as bathwater._  
_Then there is dry grass to your knees, a smell of dust and dew, and a tree with a trunk as fat as a cottage._  
_Somebody is sitting in the fork of it, cracking a gourd on his knee: an old mandrill with a white beard and a long stick._  
**Rafiki** _(happy)_: Ah. There you are. The page said someone was coming. It didn’t say when, so I made tea for nobody, twice.  
**Rafiki**: Karibu. That means welcome. Come up, come up. Mind the third branch; it has opinions.  
_Your Nubbin is up the trunk before you, hand over hand. It sits down in the fork as if it had always lived there._  
**Rafiki**: I am Rafiki. This is my tree. Today a king shows his son to the world, and I need somebody to carry gourds.  
**You**: I can carry gourds.  
**Rafiki** _(think)_: Good. Everyone can. Not everyone does. Take the small one; it is full of something you must not drink.  

**Choices**

- **Take the small gourd, carefully.**
    - **Rafiki**: Carefully. Good. Either you have done this before, or you are frightened of me. Both are useful.
- **Ask what your Nubbin should carry.**
    - **Rafiki**: Its own self, today. That is plenty for something so new.
- **Say you’d better hurry.**
    - **Rafiki**: Polepole. Slowly. The sun is coming up whether we run or not.
    - It is the first time he says it to you. It won’t be the last.

#### <a id="a23-01-page-done"></a>Done `a23-01-page-done`

_Rafiki swings down from the tree on his stick and sets off across the grass without looking back. You follow._  
**Rafiki**: Polepole. The sun is not going anywhere. Well. Up. But not anywhere.  

### Step 2: Walk with Rafiki to the great rock, through the gathering animals.

Hint: Keep close behind Rafiki’s stick. The herds will part for him. · check: `walk:a23-pride-rock:plain`

#### <a id="a23-01-gathering"></a>Scene `a23-01-gathering`

_The whole plain is walking the same way: zebra, antelope, a family of elephants, and birds in clouds over all of them._  
_Giraffes go past with their heads up in the morning, swaying like slow ships._  
**Rafiki**: Twiga. Giraffe. Say it.  
**You**: Twiga.  
**Rafiki** _(happy)_: Good. Now you know one thing more than you did. Do that every day and you will be old and clever, like me.  
_The herds part for Rafiki. Elephants lift their trunks to him, and small antelope bow their heads as he passes._  
_Up on the rock a big lion with a dark mane stands waiting, and a lioness beside him with something small between her paws._  
_A blue-black hornbill flutters down onto a stone in front of you, very important, and looks you up and down._  
**Zazu** _(think)_: Name? Business? Are you on the list? There is a list. I keep the list.  
**Rafiki**: They are with me, Zazu.  
**Zazu**: Everything strange is with you. Very well. Go up. Mind the king’s tail; he’s very particular about his tail.  

**Choices**

- **Ask to see the list.**
    - **Zazu**: Certainly not. It is mostly in my head, which is where lists are safest.
- **Bow to Zazu.**
    - **Zazu**: Oh! Well. Somebody was raised properly.
    - He bows back, and nearly falls off his stone.
- **Let your Nubbin say hello.**
    - Your Nubbin waves. Zazu looks at it for a long moment, then files it in his head under “miscellaneous”.

#### <a id="a23-01-gathering-done"></a>Done `a23-01-gathering-done`

_The king comes down to meet Rafiki, and they hold each other, the big lion and the old mandrill, the way old friends do._  
**Mufasa**: Rafiki. You came.  
**Rafiki** _(happy)_: I always come. Now. Let us see him.  

### Step 3: Watch Rafiki present the new cub from the top of the rock.

Hint: Stand at the back with the gourd. Your Nubbin will want a better view. · check: `watch:a23-presentation:pride-rock`

#### <a id="a23-01-presentation"></a>Scene `a23-01-presentation`

_The lioness, Sarabi, moves her paws aside. Between them is a cub the colour of new bread, blinking at all of you._  
**Sarabi** _(soft)_: Gently. He’s three days old, and already he thinks he owns the place.  
_Rafiki shakes a rattle of gourds over the cub. The cub sneezes, and the king laughs, low, like thunder a long way off._  
**Rafiki**: The gourd. Now.  
_You hand it over. Rafiki cracks it on his stick and dips his thumb in the juice inside._  
_He draws one line across the cub’s brow, red as ripe fruit._  
_Your Nubbin climbs your back, then your shoulder, then your head, to see._  
_Rafiki walks to the very edge of the rock and holds the cub up in both hands, high, over everything._  
_Down on the plain, every animal sees him at once. The sound comes up the rock like the sea._  
_Elephants trumpet and zebra stamp. The birds go up in one great wheel, and then the whole plain kneels._  
_A shaft of light comes down through the clouds and lands on the cub. He squints at it, crossly._  

**Choices**

- **Kneel with the plain.**
    - You kneel on the warm stone. Your Nubbin, still on your head, kneels too, which is harder than it looks.
- **Stay standing, and watch the king’s face.**
    - The king isn’t looking at the plain. He is looking at his son, the way you look at a fire on a cold night.

### Complete

#### <a id="a23-01-complete"></a>Complete `a23-01-complete`

_The cub goes back to his mother and falls asleep at once, worn out by being important._  
**Mufasa**: His name is Simba. Rafiki tells me you are a guest. Then you are welcome on this rock, guest.  
_Rafiki is quiet on the walk home. Halfway there he stops and looks back at the rock._  
**Rafiki** _(think)_: There was one face missing up there. The king’s brother. He did not come.  
**Rafiki**: Well. He will come or he will not. Carry the gourds. We have painting to do.

## A23-2. Rafiki’s Tree

*The Lion King (Disney, 1994): Rafiki paints the new cub inside his baobab* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Remember Who You Are page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A23-1, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A23-1

**Cast:** Rafiki (Rafiki, the old mandrill who keeps the baobab (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film)

### Step 1: Mix Rafiki’s paints: ochre from the riverbank, and red from the fruit.

Hint: Bring yellow earth from the dry riverbed and ripe fruit from the tree, then grind them in the shell. · check: `craft:a23-paint:baobab`

#### <a id="a23-02-paints"></a>Scene `a23-02-paints`

_On the way in, the third branch creaks at you. You find you have said sorry to it._  
**Rafiki** _(think)_: Good. It likes manners. Last week it threw out a bird.  
_Inside, the baobab is hollow and cool, and taller than it looks. The walls are painted all the way up._  
_Lions, mostly: kings and queens and cubs in rust and white and yellow, some so old they have gone the colour of tea._  
**Rafiki**: My family. Not my family. The family I watch. Which is the same thing, if you watch long enough.  
**Rafiki**: Now. The cub must go up on the wall. For that I need ochre, and red. You will mix them, because my knees have retired.  
_He gives you a tortoise shell for a bowl and a smooth stone for grinding._  
**Rafiki** _(happy)_: Ochre is the yellow earth by the dry river. Red is the fruit of this tree: the ripe ones, not the ones that make you sorry.  
_Your Nubbin has already found a fruit. It has red juice up to the elbows and looks thrilled._  
**Rafiki**: Hm. That one has started without you.  

**Choices**

- **Grind it slowly, the way he showed you.**
    - **Rafiki**: Polepole. Yes. Slow paint stays on the wall. Fast paint ends up on the visitor.
- **Grind it fast, to get it done.**
    - A puff of yellow dust goes up your nose. You sneeze it all over the floor.
    - **Rafiki**: Polepole.
- **Wonder aloud whether the fruit is nice to eat.**
    - **Rafiki**: It is. Eat one first. A hungry painter paints nothing but food.

#### <a id="a23-02-paints-done"></a>Done `a23-02-paints-done`

_The paint comes up thick and warm: a yellow like dry grass at noon, and a red like the inside of the fruit._  
**Rafiki** _(soft)_: Good. Asante. That is thank you. You will hear it from me more than you expect.  

### Step 2: Hold the shell while Rafiki paints the cub on the wall of his tree.

Hint: Stand still beside him with the paints. Your Nubbin will try to help. · check: `watch:a23-painting:baobab`

#### <a id="a23-02-wall"></a>Scene `a23-02-wall`

_Rafiki climbs the inside of the tree with the shell hooked on his stick, up to a bare patch of bark at the height of your head._  
_He paints with his fingers, quick and sure: a round body, four fat paws, ears like two little hills._  
**Rafiki** _(happy)_: There. Simba. Not a good likeness. He is much crosser in life.  
_Last, with one dab of red, he gives the painted cub the line across its brow._  
**Rafiki** _(soft)_: Now he is on the wall. As long as the wall stands, someone will know he was here.  
_Your Nubbin looks at the painted cub. Then it reaches into the shell and puts a red paw-print on its own forehead._  
**Rafiki** _(happy)_: Ha! It wants to be on the wall as well.  
**Rafiki** _(think)_: Not today, small one. Painting someone is a serious thing. It means you mean to remember them.  

**Choices**

- **Ask why he paints them.**
    - **Rafiki**: Because a lion is quick, and a wall is slow. Somebody has to hold on to things for the quick ones.
- **Look at the oldest paintings, near the top.**
    - Up near the roof, a lion is painted so faint you can only see it when you stop trying. Rafiki doesn’t say who it was.
- **Wipe your Nubbin’s forehead.**
    - **Rafiki**: Leave it. It suits it. Red, for a small brave one. It will wash off. Most things do.

### Complete

#### <a id="a23-02-complete"></a>Complete `a23-02-complete`

_Rafiki climbs down and wipes his hands on the grass. He looks up at the wall for a long time._  
**Rafiki**: You will come again. The page will bring you. Here, for the small one.  
_He threads a tiny dried gourd onto a grass string and hangs it round your Nubbin’s neck._  
**Rafiki** _(happy)_: For carrying. Nothing in it yet. That is the best sort of gourd: the sort that is waiting.  
_Your Nubbin shakes it. It makes a small, dry, important noise._  

**Rewards**

- Tiny Gourd (cosmetic): A little dried gourd on a grass string, from Rafiki. Your Nubbin wears it round its neck. It rattles when it runs.

## A23-3. Everything the Light Touches

*The Lion King (Disney, 1994): Mufasa shows Simba the kingdom at sunrise; the pouncing lesson* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Remember Who You Are page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A23-2, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A23-2

**Cast:** Mufasa (Mufasa, the king (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film), Simba (Simba, Mufasa’s son, as a cub and grown (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film), Zazu (Zazu, the king’s hornbill majordomo (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film)

### Step 1: Carry Rafiki’s message up to the king before sunrise.

Hint: Climb the back path to the top of the rock. Arrive quietly. · check: `deliver:a23-message:pride-rock`

#### <a id="a23-03-dawn"></a>Scene `a23-03-dawn`

_The page turns. Simba is bigger now, all feet and ears, and he has been awake since long before the sun._  
_Rafiki has sent you up the rock with a message for the king: the rains are late, and Rafiki is watching._  
_Before you can give it, a small lion lands on your feet._  
**Simba** _(happy)_: Are you the guest? Dad says you’re the guest. Are you staying? Do you want to see me pounce?  
_He doesn’t wait for an answer. He pounces on your Nubbin, and the two of them roll off down the rock in one ball._  
**Mufasa** _(think)_: Simba. Guests are not for pouncing.  
**Simba**: It started it!  
_It did not start it. Your Nubbin comes up out of the grass looking delighted, and dives straight back in._  
**Mufasa**: Rafiki’s message?  
**You**: The rains are late. And he’s watching.  
**Mufasa** _(soft)_: He is always watching. That is a comfort, mostly. Come. There is something I want my son to see.  

**Choices**

- **Let them wrestle.**
    - The two of them roll about in the grass until they are both the same colour.
- **Pull them apart before anyone cries.**
    - Nobody cries. Simba thinks being lifted by the scruff is a new game, and asks for another go.

#### <a id="a23-03-dawn-done"></a>Done `a23-03-dawn-done`

_The king walks to the very top of the rock, and Simba scrambles after him. You follow with your Nubbin under your arm._  

### Step 2: Stand with the king and his son as the sun comes up over the plain.

Hint: Find a spot at the top of the rock, a little behind them. Watch where the light goes. · check: `watch:a23-sunrise:pride-rock`

#### <a id="a23-03-light"></a>Scene `a23-03-light`

_The sun comes up over the edge of the world, and the light runs across the plain like water spilt on a table._  
**Mufasa**: Look, Simba. As far as the light goes. One day all of it will be yours to look after.  
**Simba** _(shock)_: All of it? Even the bit with the river?  
**Mufasa**: Even the river.  
**Simba**: What about over there? The dark bit?  
_Far off, past the edge of the light, there is a grey place where nothing seems to grow._  
**Mufasa** _(think)_: That is beyond our borders. You must never go there.  
**Simba**: But I thought a king could go anywhere.  
**Mufasa**: A king does what he must, not what he likes. That is the whole trick of it, and it isn’t easy.  
**Mufasa** _(soft)_: Everything here is joined. The grass feeds the antelope, the antelope feed us, and when we lie down at the end, we feed the grass.  
**Mufasa**: So we are all part of each other: the lion and the beetle and the grass. A good king remembers that.  
**Simba** _(think)_: So we eat the antelope, and then the antelope… eat us? A bit?  
**Mufasa** _(happy)_: A bit. Later. Much later.  

**Choices**

- **Hold your Nubbin up to see the light.**
    - It squints at the sun the way Simba did on his first morning, and sneezes.
- **Ask the king what the grey place is.**
    - **Mufasa**: Somewhere I hope none of you ever learn about.

### Step 3: Watch the pouncing lesson, and let your Nubbin have a go.

Hint: Keep low in the grass behind Simba. Zazu is the target, whether he knows it or not. · check: `watch:a23-pounce:grass`

#### <a id="a23-03-pounce"></a>Scene `a23-03-pounce`

_Zazu lands on a rock beside the king with the morning report. It is long, and he has clearly practised it._  
**Zazu**: Giraffes: tall as ever. Elephants: no complaints. The cheetahs are hungry, and the hippos would like a word about mud.  
**Mufasa**: Simba. Come here. Low down in the grass. Stay very still.  
**Simba** _(think)_: What are we doing?  
**Mufasa**: A pouncing lesson. Watch his tail feathers. Slowly. Slower than that.  
**Zazu**: …and the warthogs report… Sorry, sir. What is the boy doing?  
**Mufasa**: Nothing. Turn round.  
_Simba wiggles his bottom, creeps three steps, and launches himself at the hornbill with a squeak._  
_He lands on Zazu with all four paws. Zazu says something that is not in the morning report._  
_Your Nubbin has watched all of it with great seriousness. It wiggles, it creeps, and it pounces._  
_It misses Zazu by a whole lion’s length and lands in a bush._  

**Choices**

- **Cheer anyway.**
    - Your Nubbin climbs out of the bush and bows. Simba cheers too, much louder.
- **Help it out of the bush.**
    - It comes out covered in burrs and thoroughly pleased with itself.

### Complete

#### <a id="a23-03-complete"></a>Complete `a23-03-complete`

**Zazu** _(angry)_: This is what comes of having guests. Everybody gets ideas.  
**Mufasa** _(happy)_: Your small one will make a fine hunter one day. In a year or two. With a bigger bush.  
_Simba lies on his back in the grass with his paws in the air, out of breath and very proud._  
**Simba**: When I’m in charge, everyone will have to let me pounce on them.  
**Mufasa** _(soft)_: When you’re in charge, you’ll know better. Go and find your mother.  
_Simba races off down the rock. The king watches him all the way down._  

**Rewards**

## A23-4. Uncle Scar

*The Lion King (Disney, 1994): Scar tells Simba about the elephant graveyard* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Remember Who You Are page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A23-3, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A23-3

**Cast:** Scar (Scar, Mufasa’s brother (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film), Simba (Simba, Mufasa’s son, as a cub and grown (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film), Rafiki (Rafiki, the old mandrill who keeps the baobab (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film)

### Step 1: Follow Simba round to his uncle’s ledge.

Hint: Simba has gone round the shady side of the rock. Keep up. · check: `walk:a23-ledge:pride-rock`

#### <a id="a23-04-ledge"></a>Scene `a23-04-ledge`

_Simba can’t stop talking about the sunrise. He is going to tell everybody, and the first everybody is his uncle._  
_Round the shady side of the rock, on a ledge the sun never quite reaches, a thin dark lion lies with his chin on his paws._  
_His mane is black and his eyes are green, and an old scar runs down over one of them._  
**Simba** _(happy)_: Uncle Scar! Guess what! Dad showed me the whole kingdom, and I’m going to look after all of it!  
**Scar** _(think)_: How lovely for you. Do excuse me if I don’t get up. Getting up is for lions with somewhere to be.  
_He looks at you over Simba’s head. He takes a long time about it, and then he smiles._  
**Scar**: And a guest. Rafiki’s, I suppose. He does collect strays.  
**Simba**: Uncle Scar, when I’m king, what will you be?  
**Scar** _(think)_: Older. Thinner. Still on this ledge.  

**Choices**

- **Say good morning politely.**
    - **Scar**: Good morning. Such manners. Somebody ought to bottle them.
- **Look straight back at him.**
    - He holds your look and doesn’t blink, and you are the one who looks away first.
- **Keep your Nubbin behind your legs.**
    - Your Nubbin has climbed on every animal on the plain. It does not want to go anywhere near this one.

#### <a id="a23-04-ledge-done"></a>Done `a23-04-ledge-done`

_Scar stretches, slow and careful, like someone with a bad back who wants you to ask about it._  

### Step 2: Listen to what Scar lets slip.

Hint: Stay close to Simba and listen. Scar is choosing his words. · check: `talk:a23-scar:ledge`

#### <a id="a23-04-slip"></a>Scene `a23-04-slip`

**Scar**: Did your father show you everything, Simba? Even what lies past the northern border?  
**Simba** _(sad)_: He said I can’t go there.  
**Scar** _(think)_: And he’s quite right. It’s a place for grown lions. Very bold ones. Not you, of course.  
**Simba**: I’m bold! What’s out there?  
**Scar**: Oh, no. No, I couldn’t. I’ve said too much already.  
_He turns away as if he were sorry. He isn’t, and you can tell from the end of his tail._  
**Scar**: Bones, Simba. Old ones, as big as trees. Where the elephants went to lie down. No place for a cub.  
**Simba** _(shock)_: An elephant… what?  
**Scar** _(soft)_: Promise me you’ll keep well away. Just between us two. And don’t tell your father I mentioned it.  
_Simba’s eyes have gone round and shiny. He isn’t listening to the promise part at all._  

**Choices**

- **Say you’ll tell the king.**
    - **Scar**: Tell him what? That I warned his son to be careful? Do. He’ll be so grateful.
    - You can’t think of an answer. That was the point.
- **Tell Simba it sounds like a horrible place.**
    - **Simba**: It sounds amazing.
- **Take Simba away from the ledge.**
    - Simba comes with you, but he looks back twice. Scar is watching both times.

### Complete

#### <a id="a23-04-complete"></a>Complete `a23-04-complete`

_Back at the baobab, you tell Rafiki what Scar said. He stops painting and puts his finger down very carefully._  
**Rafiki** _(think)_: He told the boy where not to go. That is the same as drawing him a map.  
**Rafiki**: Some lions want the plain. Some only want the rock at the top of it. That one has always wanted the rock.  
**You**: What do we do?  
**Rafiki** _(soft)_: We watch. And we fill the gourds, because cubs get thirsty, wherever they run.  

**Rewards**

## A23-5. Losing Zazu

*The Lion King (Disney, 1994): Simba and Nala give Zazu the slip on the way to the graveyard* · audience: all · where: Nova’s atlas: the Remember Who You Are page (Atlas campaign) · when: Any time after A23-4, from Nova’s atlas. · requires: A23-4

**Cast:** Simba (Simba, Mufasa’s son, as a cub and grown (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film), Nala (Nala, Simba’s friend, as a cub and grown (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film), Zazu (Zazu, the king’s hornbill majordomo (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film), Rafiki (Rafiki, the old mandrill who keeps the baobab (The Lion King); licensed design, exactly as drawn in the 1994 film)

### Step 1: Set off with Simba, Nala and Zazu, to “the waterhole”.

Hint: Zazu is in charge of the walk. Stay with the group, for as long as there is a group. · check: `escort:a23-cubs:plain`

#### <a id="a23-05-walk"></a>Scene `a23-05-walk`

_Next morning Simba turns up at the baobab with a friend: a cub with pale fur and the look of a planned escape._  
**Simba** _(happy)_: This is Nala. We’re going to the waterhole. A really good waterhole. You should come.  
**Nala**: It isn’t the waterhole. Tell them.  
**Simba**: Shh! Zazu’s coming.  
_Zazu is coming. Their mothers said they could go as long as he went too, and he is taking it extremely seriously._  
**Zazu** _(think)_: In a line, please. Single file. Nobody is to be eaten, trampled, or rude to a rhinoceros.  
**Rafiki** _(think)_: Go with them. Take water. And keep your eyes open.  
_Your Nubbin trots between the two cubs, very pleased to be counted as one of the gang._  
**Nala**: What is it?  
**You**: A Nubbin.  
**Nala**: Can it pounce?  
**Simba** _(happy)_: It tries.  

**Choices**

- **Walk at the front with the cubs.**
    - **Nala**: You know it’s not the waterhole, right? You’ve worked that out?
    - **You**: I’ve worked that out.

#### <a id="a23-05-walk-done"></a>Done `a23-05-walk-done`

_Halfway across the plain, Simba gives Nala a look, and Nala gives it back. Then both of them run._  

### Step 2: Keep up with the cubs as they lose Zazu among the herds.
