#### `sprout:a23-01-page-done` · [main](main.md#a23-01-page-done)

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, ask): slowing down when we feel hurried  
> _Your tummy bounces and you want to rush. What helps you slow down?_  
> - ✅ Take a slow, deep breath → Yes. In slowly, out slowly. Polepole.
> - Stamp your feet → Stamping is fun, but breathing slowly works better.
> - Run faster → Running makes the bounce bigger. Try a slow breath.
> _For grown-ups: Practised a calming strategy: breathing slowly when we feel rushed._

**Rafiki**: Polepole. Slowly.

> **Lesson** (art, ELOF: Approaches to Learning, ask): mixing red and yellow makes orange  
> _Rafiki mixes red paint and yellow paint. What colour does he make?_  
> - ✅ Orange → Orange! Like the sun when it comes up.
> - Blue → Blue needs another paint. Red and yellow make orange.
> - Green → Green is yellow and blue. Red and yellow make orange.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that red and yellow paint mix to make orange._

> _For grown-ups: Thought about why we say thank you (asante) when someone helps._

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): asking before you play rough  
> _Simba pounced without asking. What could he do first?_

> - Pounce even harder → Hard pouncing can hurt. Ask first, then play gently.
> - Run away → Running away is not asking. A kind question comes first.
> _For grown-ups: Practised asking a friend before playing, so play stays kind._

## A23-4. Uncle Scar

#### `sprout:a23-04-ledge` · [main](main.md#a23-04-ledge)

_Simba runs round the shady side of the rock._  
_A thin dark lion lies on a ledge. It is Uncle Scar._  
**Simba**: Uncle Scar! Dad showed me the whole kingdom!  
**Scar**: How lovely. I will not get up.  
_Scar looks at you for a long time. Your tummy feels funny._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): noticing an uneasy feeling and telling a trusted grown-up  
> _Your tummy feels funny near Scar. What can you do?_  
> - ✅ Tell a grown-up you trust → Yes. A funny tummy is a clue. A trusted grown-up can help.
> - Pretend it is nothing → Better to listen to that funny feeling. Tell someone you trust.
> _For grown-ups: Practised noticing an uneasy feeling and deciding to tell a trusted adult._

_Your Nubbin hides behind your leg._  

**Choices**

- **Say good morning politely.**
    - **Scar**: Good morning. Such good manners.
- **Look straight back at him.**
    - He does not blink. You look away first.
- **Keep your Nubbin behind your legs.**
    - Your Nubbin wants to stay very far from this lion.

#### `sprout:a23-04-ledge-done` · [main](main.md#a23-04-ledge-done)

_Scar stretches, slowly and carefully._  
_He lies back in the cool shade._  
_The sun never reaches his ledge._  
_The rock is in the way._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-PS3-1, ask): shade is cooler because something blocks the sun  
> _The ledge is cool and shady. Why?_  
> - ✅ The rock blocks the sunlight → Yes. When something blocks the light, we get shade, and shade is cooler.
> - The sun is asleep → The sun is still up. The rock just makes shade.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that shade happens when something blocks sunlight, and it is cooler._
