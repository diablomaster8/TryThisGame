# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A24: The Law of the Jungle

## A24-1. The Cave

- `a24-01-s1`: Step into the hills and find the wolves’ cave. · _Touch the page where the cave is. Then climb up._
- `a24-01-s2`: Sit quietly in the cave. · _Sit by the wall. Watch the door._
- `a24-01-s3`: Stand behind Mother Wolf. · _Stay close to Mother Wolf. She is brave._

#### `sprout:a24-01-page` · [main](main.md#a24-01-page)

_Nova’s big book opens. Warm hills!_  
_A blue evening sky!_  
_A card is tucked inside. Nova wrote it small._  
_“A man named Kipling wrote this story long ago. He loved India.”_  
_“He also believed some things that were not fair. We can think for ourselves.”_  
_You step into the page. Dry grass crunches under your boots._  
_There is a cave in the hills. A big flat rock sits above it._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, ask): India is a big country; a map shows where places are  
> _India is a big country. The wolves live in its hills._  
> _(Point to the hills in the picture.) What is India?_  
> - ✅ A country. → Yes! India is a big country with hills, jungle and cities.
> - A tiny pond. → A tiny pond? Splash! India is much bigger, a whole country.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that India is a big country, and that the story is set in its hills._

**Father Wolf**: Who is at the door? A guest!  
**Father Wolf**: A very small one.  
**Mother Wolf**: Come in, if you come politely. Mind the cubs’ tails.  

**Choices**

- **Bow and wait to be asked.**
    - **Mother Wolf**: Good manners! Come in.
- **Let your Nubbin go in first.**
    - Your Nubbin trots in. The cubs sniff it all over.
- **Sit down outside.**
    - **Father Wolf**: You sit so politely. Come in out of the dew.

#### `sprout:a24-01-page-done` · [main](main.md#a24-01-page-done)

_The cave smells of warm fur. It is cosy inside._  
_Mother Wolf’s eyes shine like two little lamps._  
**Mother Wolf**: This is our den. It is our home.  
**Mother Wolf**: You can sit by the wall.  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS3-1, ask): animals live in homes that meet their needs  
> _A wolf’s home is called a den. It keeps the cubs warm and safe._  
> _Where do wolves sleep?_  
> - ✅ In a den. → Yes! A den is a cosy home for wolves.
> - In a boat. → Not a boat! Wolves sleep in a den.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that wolves live in dens, which keep their young warm and safe._


#### `sprout:a24-01-cub` · [main](main.md#a24-01-cub)

_A thin jackal slides in. His name is Tabaqui._  
**Tabaqui**: What a lovely cave! What lovely cubs!  
**Tabaqui**: Have you a bone for me?  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds: T for Tabaqui  
> _Tabaqui begins with a sound: t, t, t._  
> _Which word begins like Tabaqui: tiger or wolf?_  
> - ✅ Tiger. → Yes! Tabaqui, tiger: t, t, t!
> - Wolf. → Wolf begins with w. Tabaqui and tiger both begin with t!
> _For grown-ups: Practised hearing the first sound t in Tabaqui and tiger._

**Father Wolf**: There is a bone by the wall. Take it and go.  
_Tabaqui crunches the bone and slinks away._  
_A roar rumbles in the valley. It is Shere Khan, the tiger!_  
_Then something rustles. A tiny brown baby toddles in._  
_He is not scared. He snuggles in with the cubs to get warm._  

**Choices**

- **Make room for the baby.**
    - Your Nubbin scoots over. The baby leans on it like a cushion.
- **Keep very still.**
    - You are so still. Mother Wolf smiles at you.

#### `sprout:a24-01-cub-done` · [main](main.md#a24-01-cub-done)

**Mother Wolf**: A baby with no fur at all! And he is not scared.  
_Four wolf cubs sleep in a heap. The baby sleeps beside them._  
_(Count the cubs on your fingers: one, two, three, four.)_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.1, ask): add one more: 4 cubs and 1 baby make 5  
> _Four cubs are asleep. One baby boy snuggles in too._  
> _How many sleepers is that?_  
> - ✅ 5. → Yes! Four and one more makes five.
> - 3. → Count again: four cubs, and one more baby. That makes five!
> _For grown-ups: Added one to four to find five sleepers._


#### `sprout:a24-01-door` · [main](main.md#a24-01-door)

_The moonlight goes dark. A big striped head fills the door._  
_It is Shere Khan, the tiger! He cannot fit inside._  
**Shere Khan**: Give me the baby!  
**Mother Wolf**: No. The baby is mine.  
**Mother Wolf**: He is safe here.  
**Mother Wolf**: Go away, Shere Khan. Go!  
_Shere Khan backs out, grumbling._  
**Mother Wolf**: I will call you Mowgli the Frog, little one. Sleep now.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): being scared and brave together; what helps when we feel scared  
> _Mother Wolf felt scared, and she was brave._  
> _What can you do when you feel scared?_  
> - ✅ Take a slow breath and ask for help. → Yes! Breathe in, breathe out, and tell someone.
> - Hide and never tell anyone. → Hiding can feel safe. But telling someone helps most.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about feeling scared and being brave, and that slow breaths and asking for help can help._


**Choices**

- **Say she was brave.**
    - **Mother Wolf**: I was just a mother. It looks the same!
- **Breathe out slowly.**
    - You breathe out. So does Father Wolf.
    - Whoosh!

#### `sprout:a24-01-door-done` · [main](main.md#a24-01-door-done)

**Father Wolf**: We must show the baby to the pack.  
**Mother Wolf**: We will show him at the full moon.  
_A pale moon hangs above the cave. It is nearly round._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-ESS1-1, ask): we see the Moon at night  
> _(Point to the moon.) When do we see the moon in the sky?_  
> - ✅ At night. → Yes! The moon shines in the night sky.
> - At lunchtime. → Sometimes we see it in the day too. But it shines brightest at night.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that the Moon is seen in the night sky._


#### `sprout:a24-01-complete` · [main](main.md#a24-01-complete)

_Night comes. Mother Wolf licks the baby clean._  
_Then the cubs. Then your Nubbin!_  
_It falls over, surprised._  
_Your Nubbin curls up with the cubs and sleeps._  
**Mother Wolf**: A pack is a family. You are in ours now.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): a family is people who love and look after each other  
> _Mother Wolf says a family looks after each other. What makes a family?_  
> - ✅ People who love and look after you. → Yes! Families come in all shapes. Love makes them.
> - People who look the same. → A family of copies? Hee hee! Families can look very different. Love makes them.
> _For grown-ups: Heard that a family is made by people loving and looking after one another._

_You lie by the wall and hear everyone breathe. Soft and slow._  

## A24-2. The Council Rock

- `a24-02-s1`: Go up to the Council Rock with the wolves. · _Walk behind Mother Wolf. Sit at the edge._
- `a24-02-s2`: Watch the wolves decide about the baby. · _Stay quiet. Only the wolves talk._

#### `sprout:a24-02-climb` · [main](main.md#a24-02-climb)

_At the full moon, the wolves climb up to the Council Rock._  
_Mowgli toddles beside Mother Wolf. The cubs tumble round him._  
_A great grey wolf lies on the flat rock. It is Akela._  
**Father Wolf**: Akela is strong, and clever, and fair.  
_Your Nubbin looks at the moon. It howls!_  
_Awooo!_  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-PS4-1, do): sounds are made when things wiggle (vibrate)  
> _Put a hand on your throat. Hum like a wolf._  
> _Mmmmm. What do you feel?_  
> _A buzz!_  
> Check: `edu:feel:throat:hum`
> _For grown-ups: Felt the buzz of a hum on the throat, learning that sounds are made by vibrations._

**Young Wolf**: What was that?  
**Mother Wolf**: A guest. Do not stare.  
**Mother Wolf**: It is rude.  

**Choices**

- **Hush your Nubbin gently.**
    - It hushes. Then it practises quietly.
- **Tell it that was a fine howl.**
    - It sits up very tall and looks at the moon.

#### `sprout:a24-02-climb-done` · [main](main.md#a24-02-climb-done)

_You sit at the edge, among the rocks._  
_Mowgli sits in the moonlight and plays with pebbles._  
**Mowgli**: Three white pebbles. Two grey pebbles.  
**Mowgli**: Look!  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.2, ask): add small groups: 3 + 2 = 5  
> **Mowgli**: How many pebbles do I have? (Count them on your fingers.)  
> - ✅ 5. → Yes! Three and two makes five.
> - 2. → Count them all: one, two, three, four, five. Five pebbles!
> _For grown-ups: Added 3 and 2 within 10 to find 5._


#### `sprout:a24-02-council` · [main](main.md#a24-02-council)

**Akela**: Look closely, wolves. Look closely at the baby.  
_A roar comes from behind the rocks. Shere Khan is here._  
**Shere Khan**: Give me the man’s cub!  
**Akela**: Who speaks for the baby?  
_A big brown bear stands up. It is Baloo._  
**Baloo**: I will speak for him. I will teach him myself.  
_A black panther drops down. It is Bagheera._  
**Bagheera**: I speak for him too. I will give a gift to the pack.  
_The wolves look at the baby. They decide he can stay._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, ask): friends can speak up for someone; fair rules  
> _Baloo and Bagheera speak up for Mowgli._  
> _Why is it good to have friends who speak up for you?_  
> - ✅ They help when you cannot speak for yourself. → Yes! Good friends help each other.
> - They make you do chores. → Friends making you do chores? Sweep, sweep! Good friends help and care.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about friends speaking up for someone, and why fair groups listen to more than one voice._


**Choices**

- **Stay still and quiet.**
    - You are so quiet. Your Nubbin forgets to blink.
- **Put a hand on your Nubbin.**
    - Your Nubbin leans on you. Mother Wolf nods.

#### `sprout:a24-02-council-done` · [main](main.md#a24-02-council-done)

_The wolves come, one by one, to look at Mowgli._  
**Akela**: He is the pack’s now. Take him home.  
_Baloo, Bagheera, Bear. B, B, B!_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds: B for Baloo, Bagheera and bear  
> _Baloo, Bagheera, bear. They all begin with b, b, b._  
> _Which begins with b: ball or sun?_  
> - ✅ Ball. → Yes! Ball, bear, Baloo: b, b, b!
> - Sun. → Sun begins with s. Ball begins with b, like Baloo!
> _For grown-ups: Heard the first sound b in Baloo, Bagheera, bear and ball._


#### `sprout:a24-02-council-done-fern` · [main](main.md#a24-02-council-done-fern)

_Variant of `a24-02-council-done`._

_The wolves come, one by one, to look at Mowgli._  
_A soft voice comes from the edge of the page. It is Fern, remembering._  
**Fern**: That is what Bramble did for the strays.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, ask): naming a character from the story  
> _Fern remembers someone who helped little strays._  
> _Who did Fern remember: Bramble, or a bear?_  
> - ✅ Bramble. → Yes! Fern remembers Bramble, who helped strays.
> - A bear. → It was Bramble. Fern remembers her.
> _For grown-ups: Named the character, Bramble, that Fern remembers._

**Akela**: He is the pack’s now. Take him home.  

#### `sprout:a24-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a24-02-complete)

_Shere Khan roars. Nobody answers him._  
**Bagheera**: Shout all you like. The baby will grow.  
_Bagheera looks at you with long yellow eyes._  
**Bagheera**: You were still and quiet. That is kind.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, ask): listening with a quiet body and a quiet voice  
> _Bagheera liked how you listened. How do we listen to a friend?_  
> - ✅ Look at them, stay quiet and wait. → Yes! That is good listening.
> - Talk at the same time. → Then we cannot hear! Quiet is how we listen.
> _For grown-ups: Practised what good listening looks like: looking, staying quiet and waiting._

**Baloo**: Lessons start soon. You too.  
**Baloo**: And that!  
_“That” is your Nubbin, trying to howl again, very small._  

## A24-3. Baloo’s Lessons

- `a24-03-s1`: Join Mowgli at Baloo’s lessons. · _The glade is by the fallen tree. Bring your Nubbin._
- `a24-03-s2`: Learn the Master Words from Baloo. · _Say them slowly, in the right voice._
- `a24-03-s3`: Practise the calls with Baloo. · _Growl for hunters. Whistle for birds. Hiss for snakes._

#### `sprout:a24-03-glade` · [main](main.md#a24-03-glade)

_Time passes. Mowgli is seven now._  
_He is quick and brown._  
_Baloo sits on a fallen tree with his eyes half shut._  
**Baloo**: Sit down, all three of you. The Law is old, and long.  
**Mowgli**: I am bored. You have taught me for ever!  
**Baloo**: I am a sloth bear. I love ants, fruit and honey!  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, ask): a sloth bear eats ants, fruit and honey  
> **Baloo**: I am a sloth bear.  
> **Baloo**: What do I like to eat: ants and honey, or pizza?  
> - ✅ Ants and honey. → Yes! Sloth bears dig for ants and love honey.
> - Pizza. → Ha! No pizza in the jungle. Ants and honey for me!
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a sloth bear digs for ants and loves honey and fruit._

**Baloo**: Be polite to the bees. Say sorry to a bat you wake.  
_Your Nubbin looks at Baloo, the bees and the bat. Its eyes cross!_  

**Choices**

- **Listen hard and say each one back.**
    - **Baloo**: A guest who listens. Good!
- **Whisper to Mowgli that you are bored too.**
    - **Baloo**: I heard that. Bears hear everything.

#### `sprout:a24-03-glade-done` · [main](main.md#a24-03-glade-done)

**Baloo**: First, the Strangers’ Call. You give it before you hunt on someone’s land.  
**Baloo**: It means: may I, please?  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, ask): asking first shows respect  
> _Baloo says: ask first._  
> _What do you do before you go into a friend’s house?_  
> - ✅ Ask first. → Yes! Asking first is polite.
> - Run in without a word. → We ask first. It shows we care.
> _For grown-ups: Understood that asking before using someone else’s space shows respect._

**Mowgli**: Why ask? I am hungry!  
_Your Nubbin tiptoes politely across the glade._  

#### `sprout:a24-03-words` · [main](main.md#a24-03-words)

**Baloo**: Now the Master Words. They matter most of all.  
**Baloo**: Say them for the hunters. Say them for the birds.  
**Baloo**: Say them for the snakes.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, do): clapping the syllables of a name  
> _Clap the parts of the bear’s name. Ba-loo._  
> _How many claps? Two!_  
> Check: `edu:clap:syllables:baloo`
> _For grown-ups: Clapped the two syllables of Baloo._

_Mowgli gabbles them. Baloo gives a gentle cuff._  
**Mowgli**: Ow! You always hit me!  
**Mowgli**: I hate you!  
**Bagheera**: He wants you safe, little brother.  
**Baloo**: You and I are family, all of us, under the same sky.  
_That is the Master Word. Say it with a growl, a whistle, a hiss._  

**Choices**

- **Say the words slowly.**
    - You say them in a deep voice. Baloo closes his eyes.
    - That is praise.
- **Ask Baloo why the words work.**
    - **Baloo**: Because everyone keeps them. That is what a law is.
- **Sit beside Mowgli.**
    - You sit and say nothing. Soon Mowgli stops sulking.

#### `sprout:a24-03-words-done` · [main](main.md#a24-03-words-done)

_Your Nubbin puffs up. It gives a small hiss._  
**Baloo**: That is not wrong! The snakes might like that.  
**Mowgli**: Baloo hit me. Then he praised Nubbin.  
**Mowgli**: It is not fair.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): saying “I feel cross” in words  
> _Mowgli is cross. What could he say to Baloo with words?_  
> - ✅ “I feel cross. That hurt.” → Yes! Telling someone how we feel helps.
> - “I hate you!” → That can hurt. “I feel cross” is kinder and clearer.
> _For grown-ups: Practised using words to say “I feel cross” instead of shouting something hurtful._


#### `sprout:a24-03-calls` · [main](main.md#a24-03-calls)

**Baloo**: Now we practise. I am the jungle!  
_Baloo hides behind a bush._  
_He is a bad bird, a bad snake, a bear._  
**Mowgli**: He is the worst bird in the world!  
_Your Nubbin hisses at the bear and growls at the bird._  
**Baloo**: Growl. Whistle.  
**Baloo**: Hiss. Growl.  
**Baloo**: Whistle. Hiss.  

> **Lesson** (maths, ELOF: Mathematics Development, ask): patterns: growl, whistle, hiss, growl, whistle, ...  
> **Baloo**: Growl, whistle, hiss, growl, whistle... what comes next?  
> - ✅ Hiss. → Yes! The pattern goes round and round.
> - Growl. → Listen again: growl, whistle, hiss. Next is hiss.
> _For grown-ups: Found the next item in a repeating pattern._


#### `sprout:a24-03-calls-done` · [main](main.md#a24-03-calls-done)

**Baloo**: Enough. The cub knows it.  
**Baloo**: The guest is learning.  
_Your Nubbin hisses at a stick insect. The insect does not move._  
**Baloo**: A snake has no ears you can see.  
**Baloo**: It feels your feet on the ground.  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): snakes feel the ground through their bodies  
> _Snakes have no ears you can see. How do they feel footsteps?_  
> - ✅ Through the ground. → Yes! A snake feels the ground wiggle.
> - Through its tail feathers. → Snakes have no feathers! They feel the ground.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that snakes feel footsteps through the ground._


#### `sprout:a24-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a24-03-complete)

_Baloo lies back with a happy grunt._  
**Baloo**: The Law is like a creeper on a tree. It holds things up.  
_A creeper is a plant that climbs a tree._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, ask): a new word: creeper (a climbing plant)  
> _A creeper is a plant that climbs up a tree._  
> _What does a creeper do?_  
> - ✅ Climbs. → Yes! A creeper climbs and holds on.
> - Swims. → A creeper that swims? Splash! A creeper climbs up trees.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a creeper is a plant that climbs._

**Mowgli**: Can I go now?  
**Baloo**: Go. But stay away from the Monkey-People!  
_Mowgli looks away fast._  

## A24-4. The Monkey-People

- `a24-04-s1`: Look for Mowgli under the trees. · _Follow the chattering. Monkeys are never quiet._
- `a24-04-s2`: Watch for Chil the kite. · _Look up! Chil circles in the sky._
- `a24-04-s3`: Go with Baloo and Bagheera to find Kaa. · _Kaa is on a warm ledge. Speak politely._

#### `sprout:a24-04-trees` · [main](main.md#a24-04-trees)

_Mowgli rests on Bagheera’s back in the warm sun._  
**Mowgli**: The monkeys are nice to me. They give me nuts!  
**Baloo**: The Monkey-People forget everything. Do not trust them.  
_Mowgli goes red. He stomps away to sulk under a tree._  
_Above him, the leaves whisper. Many grey hands reach down!_  
**Bandar-log**: Hoo hoo! Look how clever we are!  
**Bandar-log**: Ooh, a nut!  
_The monkeys swing up into the trees with Mowgli._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS3-1, ask): monkeys live together in groups  
> _Monkeys live together in a big group. Do monkeys live alone, or together?_  
> - ✅ Together. → Yes! A group of monkeys is called a troop.
> - Alone. → Not alone! Monkeys live together, in a troop.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that monkeys live together in groups called troops._

**Mowgli**: Baloo! Bagheera!  

**Choices**

- **Shout for Baloo and Bagheera.**
    - They run! Baloo runs like a rolling rock.
    - Bagheera runs like a shadow.
- **Run underneath and keep watching.**
    - You run. Your Nubbin watches the treetops.
    - Bonk! It walks into a tree.

#### `sprout:a24-04-trees-done` · [main](main.md#a24-04-trees-done)

_The monkeys swing along the treetops. They are high and fast._  
_Below, nobody can keep up._  
_Up in the sky, a kite is circling. It sees everything._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, ask): a view from above is like a map  
> _A kite flies high in the sky._  
> _Who can see more: a kite up high, or a mouse on the ground?_  
> - ✅ The kite. → Yes! From high up you see the whole place, like a map.
> - The mouse. → The mouse sees only a little. The kite sees much more.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a view from high above shows the whole place, like a map._
