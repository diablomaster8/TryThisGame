# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A27: The White-Haired Prince

## A27-1. A Son for Sam

- `a27-01-s1`: Step into the mountain page and find the house in Sistan. · _Touch the green city at the bottom of the page._
- `a27-01-s2`: Go to the back rooms and meet the baby. · _Follow the nurse down the passage with the lamps._
- `a27-01-s3`: Go with the nurse to tell Sam. · _Walk behind her. Stay by the door._

#### `sprout:a27-01-page` · [main](main.md#a27-01-page)

_Nova’s atlas opens. A mountain!_  
_A big nest! A green city far below._  
_A card says this story comes from a very old book._  
_The book is the Shahnameh. That means Book of Kings._  
_A poet named Ferdowsi wrote it, long, long ago._  
_People still love it. They read it out loud together._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, ask): Iran is a country on the map  
> _This story comes from Iran. (Point to the green city.)_  
> _What is Iran?_  
> - ✅ A country. → Yes! Iran is a country, with mountains, cities and gardens.
> - A snack. → Not a snack! Iran is a country, with mountains and gardens.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that Iran is a country, and that the Shahnameh is a book people there love._

_You step in. Dust, a gate, and a house full of lamps._  
**The Nurse**: Come in, traveller! There is room for everyone here.  
_Your Nubbin sniffs the air. It smells bread._  

**Choices**

- **Thank her, and wipe your feet.**
    - **The Nurse**: Good manners! Put your shoes by the door.
- **Ask about the baby.**
    - **The Nurse**: A boy! He is perfect.
    - **The Nurse**: We are all very…
    - She stops. Then she smiles.
    - “Come and see him.”
- **Let your Nubbin follow its nose.**
    - It finds the kitchen. The cook gives it a crust.

#### `sprout:a27-01-page-done` · [main](main.md#a27-01-page-done)

**The Nurse**: Stay as long as you like. The master will thank every guest.  
_She whispers the last part to herself: “When he knows.”_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds: w for whisper  
> _She whispers. Whisper begins with w, w, w._  
> _Which starts like whisper: water or sun?_  
> - ✅ Water. → Yes! Whisper, water: w, w, w!
> - Sun. → Sun begins with s. Whisper and water both begin with w!
> _For grown-ups: Practised hearing the first sound w in whisper and water._


#### `sprout:a27-01-birth` · [main](main.md#a27-01-birth)

_In the warm back rooms, a baby sleeps in a cradle._  
_His fists are up by his ears._  
_His hair is white, like frost on a branch._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): some people are born with very pale hair; it is how they are made  
> **The Nurse**: Some babies are born with very pale hair. Their bodies make less colour.  
> **The Nurse**: It is just how they are made. It is not bad at all.  
> _For grown-ups: Heard, plainly and kindly, that some people are born with very pale hair and skin, and that it is just how they are._

**The Nurse**: He is eight days old. His father has not met him yet.  
**The Nurse**: Nobody dares to tell him about the hair. I think it is lovely.  
_Your Nubbin climbs up and looks in. The baby looks back._  

**Choices**

- **“He is beautiful.”**
    - **The Nurse**: He is. Every bit of him.
- **“It is only hair.”**
    - **The Nurse**: That is what I keep saying. To the wall, mostly.
- **“Someone should tell his father.”**
    - **The Nurse**: Someone should. It looks as if it will be me.

#### `sprout:a27-01-birth-done` · [main](main.md#a27-01-birth-done)

_Your Nubbin touches the white hair with one paw._  
_The baby grabs the paw and holds on tight._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.4, do): count the five fingers of a hand  
> _The baby holds on with one hand._  
> _(Count his fingers: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:fingers:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five fingers, matching one number to each finger._


#### `sprout:a27-01-hall` · [main](main.md#a27-01-hall)

_Sam sits in his big hall, with a sword on his knees._  
**The Nurse**: Good news, my lord. You have a son.  
**The Nurse**: He is strong and well.  
**The Nurse**: His hair is white. Remember, a baby is a gift.  
**Sam**: White?  
_The sword slides to the floor. Sam stands up._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, ask): a big feeling can be calmed with a slow breath  
> _Sam has a big feeling. What can help when a feeling is big?_  
> - ✅ A slow breath. → Yes! A slow breath helps us think. In, and out.
> - Stamp and shout. → Stamping can feel good. A slow breath helps us think, too.
> _For grown-ups: Named a calming strategy for a big feeling: a slow breath._

**Sam**: Show me.  

#### `sprout:a27-01-hall-done` · [main](main.md#a27-01-hall-done)

_Sam walks fast down the passage._  
_The nurse looks at you. She says nothing at all._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, do): clap the syllables in baby  
> _Clap the word baby. Ba-by._  
> _Two claps!_  
> Check: `edu:clap:syllables:baby`
> _For grown-ups: Clapped the two syllables in baby._


#### `sprout:a27-01-complete` · [main](main.md#a27-01-complete)

_Sam stands by the cradle for a long time._  
_The baby looks up at him with dark, steady eyes._  
_Sam does not pick him up. That is the first wrong thing._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, ask): fairness: everyone deserves love whatever they look like  
> _A baby needs love, whatever his hair is like._  
> _Is it fair to love him less?_  
> - ✅ No. Not fair. → Right. Hair is only hair. Everyone deserves love.
> - Yes. → No. Everyone deserves love. Hair is only hair.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about fairness: nobody should be loved less for how they look._

**Sam**: Leave me, all of you. I need to think.  
_The nurse holds your Nubbin’s paw, to be kind._  

## A27-2. Shame

- `a27-02-s1`: Be in the hall when Sam gives his order. · _Everyone gathers in the hall at first light._
- `a27-02-s2`: Follow the servants up the mountain path. · _Stay close behind the old servant._
- `a27-02-s3`: Stay with the baby by the high rocks. · _Sit with him by the big stone._

#### `sprout:a27-02-order` · [main](main.md#a27-02-order)

_In the morning, everyone gathers in the hall. Sam did not sleep._  
**Sam**: I am afraid people will laugh at my son.  
_That is a wrong thing to fear. A baby is only a baby._  
**Sam**: Take the baby away to the mountain.  
**The Nurse**: My lord, he is a gift.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, ask): speaking up kindly when something is not fair  
> _Sam is making a wrong choice. What could someone say to him?_  
> - ✅ Please be kind to your baby. → Yes. Kind words can help someone think again.
> - Nothing at all. → Sometimes it is hard to speak. Standing close helps. Kind words can help, too.
> _For grown-ups: Practised thinking of kind words to say when something is not fair._

**Sam**: Go.  
_Do not worry. Help is on its way._  

**Choices**

- **Speak up for the baby.**
    - **You**: Please. He is your son.
    - **You**: He has done nothing wrong.
    - **Sam**: A guest does not tell me about my son.
    - Your hands shake. But you said it, and the nurse heard.
- **Look Sam in the eye, and say nothing.**
    - He looks back. Then he looks at the floor.
- **Go and stand with the nurse.**
    - She leans on your shoulder, just a little.

#### `sprout:a27-02-order-done` · [main](main.md#a27-02-order-done)

_Sam goes out. The hall is very quiet._  
**The Nurse**: Go with them. Please.  
**The Nurse**: Somebody kind should be there.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds: sh for shh and ship  
> _Shh! Shh begins with sh, sh, sh._  
> _Which starts like shh: ship or sun?_  
> - ✅ Ship. → Yes! Shh, ship: sh, sh, sh!
> - Sun. → Sun begins with s. Shh and ship both begin with sh!
> _For grown-ups: Practised hearing the first sound sh._


#### `sprout:a27-02-path` · [main](main.md#a27-02-path)

_Two servants carry the baby in a basket. You follow._  
_The road becomes a path. The path goes up, up, up._  
_The wind blows. Rain falls: pitter, patter!_  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS2-1, ask): rain falls from clouds, which are made of tiny drops of water  
> _Rain falls from clouds._  
> _What are clouds made of: tiny drops of water, or sand?_  
> - ✅ Tiny drops of water. → Yes! Lots of tiny drops, floating high up.
> - Sand. → Not sand. Clouds are tiny drops of water, floating high up.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that clouds are made of tiny drops of water, and rain falls from them._

_Your Nubbin holds the basket up from below._  

#### `sprout:a27-02-path-done` · [main](main.md#a27-02-path-done)

_The storm is all around. You cannot see the city now._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, ask): count the three kinds of ground: road, path, stones  
> _First the road. Then the path._  
> _Then the stones. How many kinds of ground?_  
> - ✅ Three. → Yes! Road, path, stones. Three!
> - One. → Count again: road, path, stones. That makes three!
> _For grown-ups: Counted three kinds of ground on the journey._


#### `sprout:a27-02-rocks` · [main](main.md#a27-02-rocks)

_At the big rocks, the servants put the basket down._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, ask): the Alborz are real mountains in Iran  
> _These are the Alborz, real mountains in Iran._  
> _Are mountains very high, or very low?_  
> - ✅ Very high. → Yes! Mountains are very high.
> - Very low. → Mountains are very high. Look how far up the path goes!
> _For grown-ups: Learned that the Alborz are real mountains in Iran._

_The old servant is sad. He does not want to leave._  
_He goes. You stay._  
_You and your Nubbin keep the baby warm._  

**Choices**

- **Wrap him in your coat.**
    - He stops crying. He is cross about the coat instead.
- **Hum to him.**
    - You hum. The wind is loud, but he hears.
    - He goes quiet.
- **Hold him, with your back to the wind.**
    - He fits against your chest. He is warm.

#### `sprout:a27-02-rocks-done` · [main](main.md#a27-02-rocks-done)

_Above you, something very big flies over._  
_For a moment the rain stops, as if a roof had been put up._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.1.4, do): sound words: whoosh  
> _Big wings go whoosh! Say it with me: whoosh!_  
> Check: `edu:say:sound-word:whoosh`
> _For grown-ups: Said the sound word whoosh and linked it to what wings sound like._


#### `sprout:a27-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a27-02-complete)

_The shadow comes again. The wind bends the grass._  
_You hold the baby. Your Nubbin holds you._  
_You are a little scared. That is okay._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, ask): holding a friend helps with a scared feeling  
> _You feel scared. What helps a scared feeling?_  
> - ✅ Hold a friend. → Yes. Being close to someone helps. You are not alone.
> - Hide alone. → Hiding alone can feel lonely. Holding a friend helps.
> _For grown-ups: Named a way to feel better when scared: staying close to someone._

_Then the cloud opens. Something comes down!_  

## A27-3. The Simurgh

- `a27-03-s1`: Hold on to the baby when the great bird comes down. · _Do not let go of the basket._
- `a27-03-s2`: Arrive at the nest on the peak. · _There is nothing to do but be carried._

#### `sprout:a27-03-descent` · [main](main.md#a27-03-descent)

_A bird comes out of the cloud. She is bigger than a house!_  
_Her feathers shine like a sunset. Her eye is gold._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): wings help birds fly  
> _The Simurgh has wings, a beak and claws._  
> _What do wings help a bird do?_  
> - ✅ Fly. → Yes! Wings help most birds fly.
> - Dig. → Not dig! Wings help most birds fly. Claws can grip.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that wings help birds fly._

_She lands on the rock. The rock creaks._  
_She lifts the baby gently._  
_Then she lifts you too, and your Nubbin._  

**Choices**

- **Shout.**
    - Whoosh! The wind takes your shout away.
- **Hold on, with your eyes open.**
    - Below, the mountain gets small. The storm is a grey floor.

#### `sprout:a27-03-descent-done` · [main](main.md#a27-03-descent-done)

_Up through the cloud, and out into the sun!_  
_The sky is big and blue and quiet._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count three clouds  
> _Look down at the clouds. (Count them with me: 1, 2, 3.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:clouds:3`
> _For grown-ups: Counted three clouds, one number for each._


#### `sprout:a27-03-nest` · [main](main.md#a27-03-nest)

_The nest sits on the very top of the mountain._  
_It is made of sweet-smelling wood, and it is as big as a cottage._  

> **Lesson** (art, ELOF: Approaches to Learning, ask): describing the colours in a painting  
> _Long ago, painters drew the Simurgh with bright colours and a long, swirly tail._  
> _Which colour would you choose for her? There is no wrong answer._  
> - Gold. → Gold shines like the sun.
> - Blue. → Blue is like the sky above the clouds.
> - Red. → Red glows like a sunset.
> _For grown-ups: Chose a colour for the Simurgh, as painters of old Persian books did._

_Four fluffy chicks crowd close. They are curious!_  
_Your Nubbin stands in front of the baby, arms wide._  
**The Simurgh**: No. Not him.  
**The Simurgh**: He is your brother.  

#### `sprout:a27-03-nest-done` · [main](main.md#a27-03-nest-done)

_The chicks pull their beaks back. The smallest sits down with a plop._  
**The Simurgh**: These two are your nestmates too. Make room!  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): making room: sharing space with a newcomer  
> **The Simurgh**: What does it mean to make room for someone?  
> - ✅ Budge over and share. → Yes! Sharing space makes friends feel welcome.
> - Keep it all. → Sharing makes room for friends. Try budging over!
> _For grown-ups: Talked about making room for a newcomer, and welcoming someone by sharing._


#### `sprout:a27-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a27-03-complete)

_The chicks shuffle. A warm gap opens in the middle._  
_You, your Nubbin and the baby squeeze in._  
_A big wing comes down like a blanket._  
**The Simurgh**: Sleep. The storm cannot reach us here.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): rhymes: wing and sing  
> _Wing rhymes with sing. Which word rhymes with wing: sing or sun?_  
> - ✅ Sing. → Yes! Wing, sing, ring, king!
> - Sun. → Sun does not rhyme. Wing, sing, ring: they end the same!
> _For grown-ups: Practised rhyming: wing, sing, ring, king._

_The chick beside you snores. Snore, snore!_  

## A27-4. The Nest

- `a27-04-s1`: Help the Simurgh feed everyone in the nest. · _Chicks first, then the baby, then you._
- `a27-04-s2`: Keep the baby warm through the cold night. · _Sit with him under the big wing._
- `a27-04-s3`: Keep the chicks from sitting on the baby. · _Lift him out of the way._

#### `sprout:a27-04-feed` · [main](main.md#a27-04-feed)

_At dawn the Simurgh brings breakfast for everyone._  
**The Simurgh**: You have hands. Share it out.  
**The Simurgh**: Chicks first.  
_Four chicks eat fast. Munch, munch, munch!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count four chicks  
> _Four chicks eat. (Count them on your fingers: 1, 2, 3, 4.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:chicks:4`
> _For grown-ups: Counted four chicks, one number for each._

_The baby has soft figs and honey._  
**The Simurgh**: Feed the others before yourself. That is the rule.  
_One small fig is left._  

**Choices**

- **Eat the fig.**
    - It is the best fig you ever had. The Simurgh brings you two more.
- **Split it with your Nubbin.**
    - Half each. Nubbin eats its half, and looks at yours.
- **Give it to the smallest chick.**
    - The smallest chick is always at the back. It swallows the fig, very politely.
    - **The Simurgh**: You learn quickly.

#### `sprout:a27-04-feed-done` · [main](main.md#a27-04-feed-done)

_Your Nubbin is round as an apple. It still wants more!_  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Eco.1.K-2, ask): when there is not enough, share fairly  
> _When there is not enough for everyone, what is a kind way to choose?_  
> - ✅ Share fairly. → Yes! Sharing fairly means everyone gets some.
> - Take it all. → Then others go hungry. Sharing fairly is kinder.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about what to do when there is not enough: share fairly._


#### `sprout:a27-04-night` · [main](main.md#a27-04-night)

_The nights on the peak are very cold. The stars are close!_  
_The baby sleeps between you and your Nubbin, under the big wing._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): feathers trap warm air and keep animals warm  
> _The Simurgh’s wing is warm and fluffy. What keeps you warm at night?_  
> - ✅ A blanket. → Yes! A blanket holds warm air close, just like feathers.
> - An ice cube. → Brr! A blanket keeps warm air close, just like feathers.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that feathers, like a blanket, keep warm air close to the body._

**The Simurgh**: His father left him at the foot of my mountain.  
**The Simurgh**: This one is mine now, until someone is ready to love him.  
_Her heart goes thump, thump, thump._  

#### `sprout:a27-04-night-done` · [main](main.md#a27-04-night-done)

_In the morning, frost is on top of her wings._  
_Under them, there is none._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.1.4, ask): opposites: cold and warm  
> _Outside her wing is cold. Under her wing is...?_  
> - ✅ Warm. → Yes! Cold outside, warm under the wing.
> - Icy. → Under her wing it is warm and snug. The opposite of cold is warm.
> _For grown-ups: Practised opposites: cold and warm._


#### `sprout:a27-04-sitting` · [main](main.md#a27-04-sitting)

_The chicks love their new brother._  
_They try to sit on him! That is how chicks say I love you._  
_But he is small. Squish!_  
_You scoop him up just in time. The chick sits on your Nubbin._  
_Flump!_  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): showing love gently  
> _The chicks love the baby, but sitting is not gentle._  
> _What is a gentle way to show love?_  
> - ✅ A soft pat. → Yes! A soft pat is gentle and safe.
> - A big sit. → A big sit squashes. A soft pat is gentle and safe.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about showing love gently, with soft hands._

**The Simurgh**: Ha! They will learn.  
**The Simurgh**: Keep minding him.  

#### `sprout:a27-04-sitting-done` · [main](main.md#a27-04-sitting-done)

_Your Nubbin comes out flatter than before. It does not mind._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): compare size: bigger and smaller  
> _The chick is huge. Your Nubbin is small._  
> _Who is bigger?_  
> - ✅ The chick. → Yes! The chick is much bigger.
> - Your Nubbin. → Look again. The chick is much bigger.
> _For grown-ups: Compared two sizes: the chick is bigger and the Nubbin smaller._


#### `sprout:a27-04-complete` · [main](main.md#a27-04-complete)

_Weeks pass. Then months._  
_Nobody comes._  
**The Simurgh**: His name is for his father to give. Until then, he is my chick.  
_The baby learns to crawl in the big nest._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-LS1-2, ask): parents feed and keep their young safe  
> _The Simurgh feeds the baby and keeps him warm._  
> _What do parents do for their young?_  
> - ✅ Feed them and keep them safe. → Yes! Parents feed their young and keep them safe.
> - Nothing. → They do a lot! They feed their young and keep them safe.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that parents feed and protect their young, as the Simurgh does._

_His first word sounds like a bird call!_  
_Your Nubbin teaches him to clap._  

## A27-5. The Youth on the Mountain

- `a27-05-s1`: Teach the young man a new word. · _Sit with him at the nest’s edge and talk._
- `a27-05-s2`: Climb down to the pass and meet the caravan. · _The caravan road crosses the mountain, below the cloud._

#### `sprout:a27-05-words` · [main](main.md#a27-05-words)

_Years go by in the atlas. You and your Nubbin stay the same._  
_The little one grows tall and strong. His hair is still white._  
_He has only ever met you and the birds._  
**The Youth**: There are words I do not know. What is a market?  
**You**: Lots of people, buying and calling out.  
**The Youth**: I think I would like a market.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, notice): a word for friend in Persian: doost  
> _In Persian, a friend is a doost._  
> _Say it together: doost!_  
> _For grown-ups: Heard the Persian word for friend, doost, and said it aloud._

**The Youth**: Teach me one more word.  

**Choices**

- **Teach him “friend”.**
    - **You**: Friend. Someone who stays.
    - **The Youth**: Friend. Then you are my friend.
    - **The Youth**: And the small one too.
- **Teach him “home”.**
    - **You**: Home. The place you come back to.
    - **The Youth**: Then this is home. One day I will go away, and come back.
- **Teach him “sorry”.**
    - **You**: Sorry.
    - **You**: What you say when you did a wrong thing, and wish you had not.
    - **The Youth**: Nobody ever said it to me. Is that good?
    - You do not know what to say, so you stay close.

#### `sprout:a27-05-words-done` · [main](main.md#a27-05-words-done)

#### `sprout:a27-05-caravan` · [main](main.md#a27-05-caravan)

_Far below, a road crosses the mountain. Camels go by: ding-a-ling!_  
_The Simurgh sets you down above the pass._  
**A Merchant**: Have you seen the young man on the peak, with white hair?  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, ask): roads join places together; travellers carry news  
> _A road joins one town to the next. (Trace the road with your finger.)_  
> _What is a road for?_  
> - ✅ Getting to places. → Yes! Roads take us from place to place.
> - Swimming. → Not swimming! Roads take us from place to place.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that roads join places, and that travellers carry news along them._

**A Merchant**: We tell his story in every town. My wife says he must be cold!  
_Your Nubbin stares at the merchant’s bread._  

**Choices**

- **“He is a prince. He does not know it yet.”**
    - **A Merchant**: A prince! I will tell them in Sistan.
- **“He is a person. A kind one.”**
    - **A Merchant**: A kind person. My wife will be glad.
- **Smile, and say nothing.**
    - **A Merchant**: A secret! Secrets travel fast.

#### `sprout:a27-05-caravan-done` · [main](main.md#a27-05-caravan-done)

_The merchant gives your Nubbin a whole round loaf._  
**A Merchant**: By midsummer, every town will know the story.  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): camels have humps that store fat  
> _Camels can walk far in the hot sun._  
> _What do camels have on their backs?_  
> - ✅ A hump, or two! → Yes! A camel’s hump stores fat for food.
> - Wings. → No wings! Camels have a hump, or two.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that camels have a hump, which stores fat, and can walk far._


#### `sprout:a27-05-complete` · [main](main.md#a27-05-complete)

_The story goes down the mountain with the caravans._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, ask): choosing to tell kind stories  
> _A story can travel very far._  
> _Would you tell a kind story, or a mean one?_  
> - ✅ A kind story. → Yes! Kind stories make people feel good.
> - A mean story. → Mean stories hurt. Kind stories make people feel good.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about choosing kind words, since stories travel._

_Your Nubbin shares the loaf with the chicks. Smallest first!_  
_Someone sews Nubbin a tiny white cap, to match its brother._  

## A27-6. Sam’s Dream

- `a27-06-s2`: Be in the hall when Sam wakes. · _Wait by the hall fire._
- `a27-06-s3`: Listen to the wise men and answer Sam. · _Stand by the fire._
