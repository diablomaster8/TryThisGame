# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Atlas, page A31: The Hero Twins

## A31-1. The Hearth

- `a31-01-s1`: Step into the kitchen on the atlas page. · _Touch the warm page, then go in._
- `a31-01-s2`: Sit by the fire and listen to the old story. · _Sit on the low stool._

#### `sprout:a31-01-page` · [main](main.md#a31-01-page)

_You touch the warm page. Whoosh!_  
_You are in a kitchen in Guatemala._  
_A grandmother pats dough into round tortillas. Pat, pat, turn._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, notice): Guatemala is a country with mountains; K’iche’ Maya people live there and speak K’iche’ today  
> **Grandmother**: This kitchen is in Guatemala. It is a country with green mountains.  
> **Grandmother**: Many people here are K’iche’ Maya. They speak K’iche’, in kitchens like this one.  
> _(Point to the green mountain on the atlas page.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that Guatemala is a country with mountains, and that K’iche’ Maya people live there and speak K’iche’ today._

_A girl sits at the table. She keeps watching the door._  
**Granddaughter**: Grandmother! Someone is at the door!  
**Grandmother**: A guest, and a small guest. Come in, both of you.  
_Your Nubbin goes straight to the warm fire and sits down._  

**Choices**

- **Sit on the low stool she points to.**
    - **Grandmother**: Good. Now you are the right height for a story.
- **Offer to help with the tortillas.**
    - Your tortilla comes out a funny shape.
    - **Grandmother**: You eat that one yourself. That is the rule.
- **Let your Nubbin warm its paws.**
    - It warms its paws, then its back, then its paws again.

#### `sprout:a31-01-page-done` · [main](main.md#a31-01-page-done)

**Granddaughter**: Is it a dog?  
**Grandmother**: It is a guest. You do not ask a guest what it is.  
**Grandmother**: You give it a tortilla.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): welcoming a guest kindly  
> _A new guest comes to your door. How can you help them feel welcome?_  
> - ✅ Say hello and share something nice → Yes! A hello and a share make a guest feel welcome.
> - Stare and point → Staring can make a guest feel shy. A hello and a share feel warmer.
> _For grown-ups: Thought about how to make a new guest feel welcome._

_Your Nubbin gets a warm tortilla. It holds it in both paws._  

#### `sprout:a31-01-story` · [main](main.md#a31-01-story)

**Granddaughter**: Grandmother, tell the old story, please!  
**Grandmother**: You have heard it a hundred times.  
**Granddaughter**: So now it is one hundred and one!  
**Grandmother**: Long ago my people wrote it down, in our own language.  
**Grandmother**: And grandmothers still tell it today.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, notice): a story can live in a book and in a person’s voice  
> **Grandmother**: A story can live in two places.  
> **Grandmother**: It can live in a book. It can live in a voice.  
> **Grandmother**: When we tell it out loud, it stays alive.  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a story can be written down and also told aloud._

_Smoke curls up past the beam. Your Nubbin watches it go._  
**Grandmother**: Before the sun was the sun, two brothers played ball…  
_The kitchen goes soft. Now there is grass under your feet._  

**Choices**

- **Pick your Nubbin up first.**
    - It is warm right through, like a loaf.
- **Keep your eyes on the grandmother.**
    - **Grandmother**: I am still here. I will be here the whole time.

#### `sprout:a31-01-complete` · [main](main.md#a31-01-complete)

_You stand on a hillside in the early morning._  
_Thock! Something heavy and bouncy hits a wall._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-PS4-1, do): a sound is a shake you can feel  
> **Grandmother**: Thock! The ball hits the wall, and the wall shakes a tiny bit.  
> **Grandmother**: That shake is the sound.  
> _(Tap your knees: thock, thock, thock. Can you feel the shake?)_  
> Check: `edu:clap:thock:3`
> _For grown-ups: Learned that sound is a tiny shake (vibration), and felt it by tapping._

**Grandmother**: Listen. That is them.  
_Your Nubbin’s ears go up._

## A31-2. The Ballplayers

- `a31-02-s1`: Watch the two brothers play ball. · _Go down the hill and watch from the wall._
- `a31-02-s2`: Bring the ball back over the wall. · _Follow your Nubbin and carry the ball together._

#### `sprout:a31-02-court` · [main](main.md#a31-02-court)

_Below the hill is a ballcourt with two long sloping walls._  
_Two men play on it. They shout at every point._  
**Hun Hunahpu**: That was mine!  
**Vucub Hunahpu**: It hit your knee. Knees do not count.  
_They never use their hands. They bump the ball with hips and shoulders._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 5 bumps  
> **Hun Hunahpu**: Hip! Hip!  
> **Hun Hunahpu**: Shoulder! Hip!  
> **Hun Hunahpu**: Hip!  
> _(Count the bumps together: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. How many?)_  
> Check: `edu:count:bumps:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five ball bumps, one for each count._

**Grandmother**: Hun Hunahpu and Vucub Hunahpu. Good players.  
**Grandmother**: Noisy neighbours.  
_Thock! The ball rolls to your Nubbin._  

**Choices**

- **Let your Nubbin have a go.**
    - It bumps the ball, and the ball does not move. Your Nubbin does, backwards!
    - **Vucub Hunahpu**: Good form. Wrong direction.
- **Roll it back to them.**
    - **Hun Hunahpu**: No hands!
    - **Vucub Hunahpu**: Guests get one hand-ball. It is an old rule.
    - **Vucub Hunahpu**: I made it up just now.

#### `sprout:a31-02-fetch` · [main](main.md#a31-02-fetch)

_The ball flies over the wall and rolls down the hill._  
**Hun Hunahpu**: Little one, would you fetch it?  
_Your Nubbin bounces down after it. Boing, boing!_  
_The ball is as big as your Nubbin. It is black and heavy._  
**Grandmother**: People here made balls from tree sap, long ago, before anyone else.  

> **Lesson** (science, 2-PS1-1, notice): rubber comes from the sticky, milky sap of a tree  
> **Grandmother**: The sap is white and sticky, like milky glue.  
> **Grandmother**: It comes from a rubber tree. When it dries, it bounces!  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that rubber comes from tree sap, and that sap dries into a bouncy material._

_You carry it back up together._  

**Choices**

- **Let your Nubbin hand it over.**
    - It pushes the ball up to Hun Hunahpu.
    - He bows to it, and it bows back.
- **Throw it back over the wall.**
    - Thock! A cheer comes from the court.

#### `sprout:a31-02-complete` · [main](main.md#a31-02-complete)

_Four owls land on the wall, all in a row._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): words that rhyme  
> _Four owls! (Say it slowly: owl.)_  
> _Which word rhymes with owl?_  
> - ✅ Howl → Yes! Owl, howl. They rhyme.
> - Hat → Owl, hat. Not quite. Owl, howl: those two rhyme.
> - Tree → Owl, tree. Not quite. Owl, howl: those two rhyme.
> _For grown-ups: Found a rhyme for “owl”._

**Owls**: We come from the lords of Xibalba. You are too loud!  
**Owls**: They say: come and play. Bring your gear.  
**Hun Hunahpu**: A game! We are the best!  
_The brothers tie their gear up high in their mum’s roof._  
_They tell her not to worry. She worries anyway._  

## A31-3. The Road Down

- `a31-03-s1`: Listen to the grandmother tell about the road. · _Stay on the empty road and listen._
- `a31-03-s2`: Look at the tree by the road. · _Go and look. You do not have to touch._

#### `sprout:a31-03-road` · [main](main.md#a31-03-road)

_The page goes quiet. A road goes down between rocks._  
_Nobody is on it._  
**Grandmother**: This part I tell. You do not need to see it.  
**Grandmother**: The brothers went down, down, to a place called Xibalba.  
**Grandmother**: They did not come back up. They stayed there, far below.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): what helps when we miss someone  
> _Their mum, Xmucane, will miss them. What helps when we miss someone?_  
> - ✅ Telling stories about them → Yes. Stories keep them close. It is all right to feel sad too.
> - Pretending we do not miss them → Missing someone is a big feeling. It helps to say it, and to tell stories.
> _For grown-ups: Thought about what helps when we miss someone: feelings are allowed, and stories keep them close._

_The road stays empty. A little wind moves the dust._  

**Choices**

- **Sit down at the side of the road.**
    - You sit. Your Nubbin sits close beside you.
- **Ask what happened to them.**
    - **Grandmother**: They stayed down there. That is enough for now.
    - **Grandmother**: The story is not finished.

#### `sprout:a31-03-tree` · [main](main.md#a31-03-tree)

_By the road is a tree full of round green gourds._  
_The atlas margin has a little drawing of it, all green and round._  
**Granddaughter**: Grandmother, why can nobody go near it?  
**Grandmother**: The lords said nobody may touch it. So of course somebody does.  

> **Lesson** (science, 2-PS1-1, notice): a dried gourd shell is hard, so it makes a bowl or a cup  
> **Grandmother**: A gourd is a fruit. When it dries, its shell goes hard.  
> **Grandmother**: People here still use dry gourds as cups and bowls.  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a dried gourd has a hard shell and can be used as a cup or bowl._

_Your Nubbin pats the trunk, the way it pats you when you are tired._  

**Choices**

- **Look for the gourd that is different.**
    - Every gourd looks the same. That is the point.
- **Leave the tree be.**
    - You step back onto the road. Some things you do not need to find.

#### `sprout:a31-03-complete` · [main](main.md#a31-03-complete)

_Back at home, Xmucane waits by the fire with two small grandsons._  
**Grandmother**: One grandmother, and two boys.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 3  
> _(Count the people by the fire: Xmucane, one boy, two boys. How many?)_  
> Check: `edu:count:people:3`
> _For grown-ups: Counted three people to answer “how many?”._

**Grandmother**: Then a girl went to look at a tree she must not touch.  
**Granddaughter**: And then?  
**Grandmother**: Ah. That is how most good stories start.  

## A31-4. The Calabash Tree

- `a31-04-s1`: Watch the girl who comes to the tree. · _Hide behind the rock and watch._
- `a31-04-s2`: Go with the owls and Xquic. · _Walk behind the owls through the red trees._
- `a31-04-s3`: Help press the red sap into a heart shape. · _Hold the bowl under the sap._

#### `sprout:a31-04-xquic` · [main](main.md#a31-04-xquic)

_Down in Xibalba it is never quite night or day._  
_A girl walks alone and looks over her shoulder._  
**Grandmother**: Xquic. She heard about the tree.  
**Grandmother**: She did not like being told no.  
**Xquic**: Everyone says do not go near it. Nobody says why.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, ask): why a character does something  
> _Xquic goes to the tree, even though she was told no._  
> _Why does she go?_  
> - ✅ She is curious. She wants to know why → Yes. Xquic is curious, and nobody told her why.
> - She is hungry → She does not say she is hungry. She says nobody told her why. She is curious.
> - She wants to hide → She walks up to the tree. She is curious about it.
> _For grown-ups: Worked out why a character acts as she does: curiosity._

**The Gourd**: They are only round things in a tree. Hold out your hand.  
_She holds out her hand. Something magic happens in it._  
**Xquic**: It is gone! My hand is empty!  
**The Gourd**: Nothing to see. Go home.  
**The Gourd**: You will understand later.  

**Choices**

- **Stay hidden.**
    - Your Nubbin keeps popping up to see if hiding is finished.
- **Step out and walk her to the road.**
    - **Xquic**: Who are you? No, do not tell me.
    - **Xquic**: I have had enough surprises today.

#### `sprout:a31-04-owls` · [main](main.md#a31-04-owls)

_Months go by in the margin. Xquic is going to have a baby._  
**Grandmother**: Her father was cross. He told four owls to take her far away.  
**Grandmother**: He wanted proof, to show she was gone.  
**Grandmother**: The owls did not like it at all.  
**Owls**: We do not want to. We have never wanted to.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, ask): saying no to something unkind  
> _The owls were told to do something unkind. They said no._  
> _Was that brave?_  
> - ✅ Yes. Saying no to something unkind is brave → Yes. It is brave to say no to something unkind.
> - No. They should do what they are told → Doing what we are told is good, but not when it is unkind. The owls were brave.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about why saying no to something unkind is brave._

**Xquic**: Then do not. We will make him some pretend proof.  
_She points to a tree with red bark._  
_Red sap drips down, slow as honey._  

**Choices**

- **Go and look at the tree.**
    - Where the bark is cut, thick red sap runs slowly.
- **Tell the owls they can be brave.**
    - **Owls**: Can we? Nobody has ever asked us to.
    - All four owls stand up a little taller.

#### `sprout:a31-04-sap` · [main](main.md#a31-04-sap)

**Xquic**: Help me. Catch the sap in the bowl.  
_You hold the bowl. Your Nubbin holds the edge too._  
_Xquic squishes the sap into a round, lumpy heart shape._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, notice): trees carry water and food inside them in sap  
> **Grandmother**: Trees carry water and food inside them, in a juice called sap.  
> **Grandmother**: Some sap is runny. Some goes sticky and firm.  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that trees carry water and food in sap, and that sap can go sticky and firm._

**Owls**: More lump on that side. Hearts are not neat.  
_It shines dark red. It is only sap, from a tree._  
**Xquic**: Now go. Tell him you did it.  
**Xquic**: I am going up to the world.  

**Choices**

- **Press the last of it smooth.**
    - The owls say it is too neat now, and rough it up again.
- **Let your Nubbin leave a pawprint in it.**
    - A tiny dent on top. The owls say that is fine.

#### `sprout:a31-04-complete` · [main](main.md#a31-04-complete)

_The owls carry the bowl back. The lords sniff the smell and lean in._  
**Grandmother**: One, two, three, four owls. All four helped.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count four owls  
> _(Count the owls on your fingers: 1, 2, 3, 4. How many owls?)_  
> Check: `edu:count:owls:4`
> _For grown-ups: Counted four owls on their fingers._

**Grandmother**: The lords were fooled by a smell!  
**Granddaughter**: By owls!  
_Your Nubbin sniffs its paw. It still smells like the tree._  

## A31-5. Xmucane’s Test

- `a31-05-s1`: Walk Xquic up to Xmucane’s house. · _Go up the hill to the house with the fire._
- `a31-05-s2`: Go down to the garden with one plant. · _Sit by the corn plant with Xquic._
- `a31-05-s3`: Carry the net of corn up to Xmucane. · _Take one end of the net. Go slowly._

#### `sprout:a31-05-door` · [main](main.md#a31-05-door)

_A little house of poles and thatch sits on a hill._  
_It looks like the kitchen you came from._  
**Xquic**: Grandmother, I am your son’s wife. I carry his children.  
**Xmucane**: I do not believe you. Go away.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): naming a feeling from how someone is treated  
> _Xmucane says, “I do not believe you.” How might Xquic feel?_  
> - ✅ Sad, and a bit scared → Yes. It hurts when no one believes us. Xquic feels sad.
> - Silly → Xquic is not feeling silly. Nobody believes her. She feels sad and a bit scared.
> _For grown-ups: Named a feeling (sad, scared) from how a character is treated._

**Xmucane**: Here is a test! Bring me a net of corn from the garden.  
_You look down the hill. In the garden is one corn plant._  
_Just one._  

**Choices**

- **Offer to carry the net.**
    - **Xquic**: Thank you. I think the net is the easy part.
- **Tell Xmucane she might be telling the truth.**
    - **Xmucane**: Might. Everybody might.
    - **Xmucane**: Go and get the corn.

#### `sprout:a31-05-garden` · [main](main.md#a31-05-garden)

_One plant. One ear._  
_Xquic sits on the earth with the empty net. Your Nubbin sits too._  
_She asks for help, softly, the way you ask friends for a favour._  
_She lays the silk of the ear in the net. The net is full!_  

> **Lesson** (science, 3-LS1-1, notice): a seed grows into a plant, and a plant makes new seeds  
> **Grandmother**: One corn seed grows into one corn plant.  
> **Grandmother**: One plant makes ears. One ear has lots of kernels.  
> **Grandmother**: Each kernel is a seed. It can grow into a new plant!  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a corn seed grows into a plant that makes ears full of new seeds._

**Grandmother**: Full from one ear! Hold on to that.  

**Choices**

- **Touch the ear left on the plant.**
    - It is still there. The plant was not robbed.
- **Say thank you to nobody in particular.**
    - **Xquic**: Yes. That.
    - **Xquic**: Thank you.

#### `sprout:a31-05-carry` · [main](main.md#a31-05-carry)

_The net is heavy. You take one end._  
_Xquic takes the other._  
_Your Nubbin rides in the middle._  
**Xquic**: The older brothers will not help. They only play flutes.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): compare: which is heavier  
> _The net is full of corn._  
> _Which is heavier: the whole net, or one kernel?_  
> - ✅ The whole net → Yes! The whole net is heavier. A kernel is tiny.
> - One kernel → One kernel is tiny. The whole net of corn is much heavier.
> _For grown-ups: Compared two things and said which is heavier._

_You set the net down by Xmucane. She looks for a long time._  
**Xmucane**: One plant. I planted it myself.  

**Choices**

- **Wait outside the door.**
    - You wait by the door. Your Nubbin waits by your boot.
- **Sit down on the net.**
    - You sit on the corn, and so does your Nubbin. Xquic laughs.

#### `sprout:a31-05-complete` · [main](main.md#a31-05-complete)

**Xmucane**: Well. You are my son’s wife, then.  
**Xmucane**: Come in. It is cold.  
_She puts a log on the fire. She gives Xquic the good stool._  
**Grandmother**: That is how grandmothers say sorry.  
**Granddaughter**: You never give me the good stool!  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): saying sorry and making it better  
> _Xmucane said sorry by giving the good stool. How can you say sorry?_  
> - ✅ Say sorry, and help to fix it → Yes. Words and kind actions together say sorry best.
> - Run away → Running away leaves the sorry feeling stuck. Saying sorry and helping works better.
> _For grown-ups: Practised how to say sorry with words and kind actions._

**Grandmother**: You never carried a net of corn.  

## A31-6. The Older Brothers

- `a31-06-s1`: Meet the twins. · _Wait by the house until two boys come down the hill._
- `a31-06-s2`: Watch the older brothers climb the tree. · _Stand well back from the tree._
- `a31-06-s3`: Wait with Xmucane by the door. · _Stand by the door and watch the trees._

#### `sprout:a31-06-born` · [main](main.md#a31-06-born)

_Twins are born on the mountain: Hunahpu and Xbalanque._  
_They are loud from the first minute._  
**One Monkey**: Put them outside! We are busy!  
**Grandmother**: The twins grew up outside, with blowguns. And very cheeky!  
_Years pass in one breath. Two boys come down the hill._  
**Hunahpu**: Hello! A guest, and a guest’s guest!  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds  
> _Hunahpu starts with “h”. (Say it: h, h, Hunahpu.)_  
> _Which of these starts with “h” too?_  
> - ✅ Hat → Yes! Hunahpu, hat. Both start with h.
> - Sun → Sun starts with “s”. Hunahpu and hat both start with “h”.
> - Ball → Ball starts with “b”. Hunahpu and hat both start with “h”.
> _For grown-ups: Matched a first sound (“h” in Hunahpu and hat)._

_Your Nubbin and the twins are friends at once._  

**Choices**

- **Say hello.**
    - **Hunahpu**: Good. Are you hungry?
    - **Hunahpu**: Nobody here eats till our brothers eat.
- **Ask about the blowguns.**
    - **Xbalanque**: A long tube. You blow, and a clay ball pops out at a target.

#### `sprout:a31-06-tree` · [main](main.md#a31-06-tree)

**Hunahpu**: We found lovely birds up that tree. Somebody taller should look.  
**One Artisan**: Fine. Artists can climb.  
_The two older brothers climb up. Then the tree begins to grow!_  
**One Monkey**: It is getting bigger! Why?  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, ask): positions: above and below  
> _The brothers are up in the tree._  
> _Are they above the ground, or below it?_  
> - ✅ Above the ground → Yes! Up in the tree is above the ground.
> - Below the ground → Below is under the ground. The brothers are up in the tree, above it.
> _For grown-ups: Used the position words above and below._

**Xbalanque**: Tie your cloths behind you, and you can climb down!  
_They do. The ends of the cloths turn into curly tails!_  

**Choices**

- **Laugh.**
    - Your Nubbin falls over backwards. That is how it laughs.
- **Ask if they are all right.**
    - **Hunahpu**: They are fine. Look.
    - **Hunahpu**: They have never been happier in a tree.

#### `sprout:a31-06-laugh` · [main](main.md#a31-06-laugh)

_The brothers swing off into the trees as monkeys._  
**Xmucane**: Where are my older boys?  
**Hunahpu**: Do not laugh when you see them, Grandmother.  
_They swing back, with long arms and curly tails and very serious faces._  

> **Lesson** (science, 4-LS1-1, notice): monkeys use arms and tails to swing and hold on  
> **Grandmother**: Monkeys swing with their long arms.  
> **Grandmother**: In Guatemala, spider monkeys use their tails like a fifth hand!  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that monkeys’ arms and tails help them climb, and spider monkeys grip with their tails._

**Xmucane**: I am not laughing. I am not.  
_She laughs! They swing back up into the trees._  
**Grandmother**: Do not be sad for them. They still paint and play flutes up there.  

**Choices**

- **Try not to laugh too.**
    - Your face goes red. Your Nubbin does not even try.
- **Wave to the monkeys.**
    - One waves back, upside down, and plays three notes on a flute.

#### `sprout:a31-06-complete` · [main](main.md#a31-06-complete)

_That night there is flute music in the trees._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): kind laughing and unkind laughing  
> _Which is a kind laugh?_  
> - ✅ Laughing together at something funny → Yes! Laughing together is kind.
> - Laughing at someone who is crying → That would hurt. A kind laugh is a laugh together.
> _For grown-ups: Told a kind laugh (together) from an unkind laugh (at someone who is hurt)._

**Xmucane**: Did I do that?  
**Xbalanque**: You laughed, Grandmother. They are happy up there.  
**Hunahpu**: Also, it was funny.  
_Xmucane gives the twins supper first, for the first time._  

## A31-7. Seven Macaw

- `a31-07-s1`: Go with the twins to the shining bird’s tree. · _Follow the twins and keep low._
- `a31-07-s2`: Carry the bag of white corn. · _Walk behind the old couple with the bag._

#### `sprout:a31-07-macaw` · [main](main.md#a31-07-macaw)

_The sky has no sun yet. Only a big, shining bird._  
**Seven Macaw**: Look at me! Silver eyes!  
**Seven Macaw**: Jewel teeth! I am the sun!  
**Grandmother**: He was not the sun. He was just shiny.  
**Xbalanque**: The sun would be warm. He is not warm.  

> **Lesson** (science, 4-LS1-1, notice): birds have no teeth; they have beaks  
> **Grandmother**: Real birds have no teeth! They have a beak instead.  
> **Grandmother**: Seven Macaw’s jewel teeth are only in the story.  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that real birds have beaks and no teeth._

_Hunahpu blows a clay ball. Thup!_  
_It hits the bird’s beak._  
**Seven Macaw**: My teeth!  
_Seven Macaw swoops down. He snatches the blowgun and flies home._  
**Hunahpu**: I am all right. Just annoyed.  

**Choices**

- **Run to Hunahpu.**
    - **Hunahpu**: I am all right. Mostly annoyed.
