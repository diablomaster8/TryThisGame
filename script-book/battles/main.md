# Battles: the Grove Circuit, Play-fight Day and the story battles

## Variant scenes

Variants with no parent scene in this storyline (Grove-side, campaign-side or shared scenes).

#### <a id="bc-playday-open"></a>Variant `bc-playday-open`

_Fern has painted a sign on the green: PLAY-FIGHT DAY. BRING A NUBBIN. BRING A SNACK._  
**Fern** _(happy)_: Nobody gets hurt. Nobody gets cross. A tired Nubbin has a nap. That’s the whole rulebook.  
**Clover**: I wrote it. It’s one page. Byte wanted a second page. We said no.  
_Everybody borrows a practice Nubbin today. Yours is a Swim type. Clover’s is a Fly type._  
_A row of little hearts floats over each one. When the hearts run out, it’s nap time._  
_Yours has seven hearts. Clover’s has five._  
**Clover** _(think)_: Seven is more than five. Don’t let it go to your head.  
**Fern**: Hers is small but clever. Like Clover.  

#### <a id="bc-playday-bout"></a>Variant `bc-playday-bout`

_Along the top, a strip of little faces shows whose turn comes next._  
**Clover** _(soft)_: Turns go round. Yours, mine, yours, mine. Nobody pushes in.  
_Clover’s Fly type hovers over yours, looking smug._  
**Fern**: Swim soaks Fly! Wet wings are slow wings. Go on.  
_Splash. Clover’s flyer lands with a small damp bump and looks at its feet._  
**Clover**: Fly goes over Run. Run is too quick for Power. Power makes a bigger wave than Swim.  
**Clover**: Round it goes, like a wheel. Everyone beats something. Everyone is beaten by something.  
**Fern** _(think)_: Like plums and wasps.  
_Clover’s flyer sits down, yawns, and naps under a leaf._  

#### <a id="bc-playday-hug"></a>Variant `bc-playday-hug`

_The bout is over. Both Nubbins toddle to the middle and hug, the way Clover said they would._  
**Clover** _(happy)_: That’s how every bout ends here. A hug. Then a snack.  
**Fern**: Then a nap. Then, if I’m honest, another snack.  
_Your Nubbin breaks its plum in half and gives the bigger half away. Nobody told it to._  
**Clover** _(soft)_: Winning is nice. Sharing is nicer. Ask anybody who’s been given half a plum.  

#### <a id="bc-fern-pre"></a>Variant `bc-fern-pre`

_Fern has chalked a ring on the grass behind her orchard. It is nearly round._  
**Fern** _(happy)_: Right. Ribbon bout. I’ve been practising my serious face.  
**Fern** _(think)_: This is it.  
_It is her ordinary face, but squintier._  
**Fern**: Mine are Normal types, before you ask. Good at hugs. Rubbish at plans.  
**Fern**: If yours win, they get a ribbon. If mine win, they get a biscuit. They get a biscuit anyway.  
_Her Nubbins toddle into the ring and wave at yours. Yours wave back. Nobody has explained the competitive bit._  

#### <a id="bc-fern-post"></a>Variant `bc-fern-post`

_Fern’s last Nubbin sits down, yawns, and naps under a leaf, still smiling._  
**Fern** _(sad)_: Beaten by my neighbour. On my own grass. In front of my own plums.  
**Fern** _(happy)_: Here. I sewed the ribbon myself, so one end’s longer. That’s how you know it’s real.  
_Both teams share the biscuits. Fern takes the broken one and says she prefers it._  

#### <a id="bc-clover-pre"></a>Variant `bc-clover-pre`

_Clover’s ring is a circle of soft moss, with cushions round the edge for anyone who needs a sit-down._  
**Clover** _(soft)_: Gentle hands, gentle bouts. If anyone gets upset, we stop and have a cuddle. That’s the only rule.  
**Clover** _(think)_: That and no biting. Two rules.  
_Her Nubbins are the shy, slow ones nobody else picked. One is already hiding behind her boot._  
**Clover**: They’re braver than they look. That’s rather the point of them.  

#### <a id="bc-clover-post"></a>Variant `bc-clover-post`

_The last of Clover’s team curls up on a cushion and naps, one hand still waving._  
**Clover** _(happy)_: You didn’t rush them. I watched. That’s worth more than a ribbon, but have the ribbon too.  
_It is pale green and smells faintly of lavender. You have to fight the urge to nap on it._  

#### <a id="bc-marina-pre"></a>Variant `bc-marina-pre`

_Marina has flooded the flat rocks below the tide pools. Every step goes splish._  
**Marina** _(think)_: Puddle field. Swim moves land harder here. Mine know that. Yours are about to.  
**Marina**: Rule one: nobody’s brave. Rule two: if you slip, you sit. Sitting is allowed.  
_Her Nubbins slide into the shallows as if they’ve been poured in._  
_One of yours steps in a puddle, looks at its foot, and is deeply offended._  
**Marina**: Careful beats brave. On the ledge and in the ring.  

#### <a id="bc-marina-post"></a>Variant `bc-marina-post`

_Marina’s last Nubbin floats on its back in the biggest puddle, fast asleep._  
**Marina** _(happy)_: You kept your feet. Most people don’t, the first time.  
**Marina**: Ribbon’s blue. Don’t drop it in a pool. I am not fishing it out again.  
_She wrings out her sleeve and almost smiles. With Marina, almost counts._  

#### <a id="bc-sparks-pre"></a>Variant `bc-sparks-pre`

**Sparks** _(happy)_: RIBBON BOUT! Are you ready? I’m ready! I’ve been ready since breakfast!  
_Her runners are already bouncing. One is bouncing in a circle and barely touching the ground._  
**Sparks**: Run types. Mine go first, mostly. That doesn’t mean they win. It means they’re early.  
**Sparks**: If yours get puffed, shout. I’ve got buns in my bag. I’ve always got buns in my bag.  
_You have known Sparks long enough to know this is true. Nobody knows where the buns come from._  

#### <a id="bc-sparks-post"></a>Variant `bc-sparks-post`

_Her fastest runner finally stops, flops over, and naps with its legs still going a little._  
**Sparks** _(happy)_: You beat me! That was BRILLIANT! I hate it! I love it! I love it more!  
**Sparks**: Yellow ribbon. Hand-stitched. Don’t ask whose hand. It was Byte’s. Don’t tell him I told you.  
_She hugs your Nubbins, then hers, then you, then a fence post by accident._  

#### <a id="bc-pixel-pre"></a>Variant `bc-pixel-pre`

_Pixel’s ring is laid out on a grid of string, with a little clock on a pole at every corner._  
**Pixel** _(think)_: Fly types. Up is a direction most people forget about. My team doesn’t.  
**Pixel**: The clocks time the turns. They all disagree by a second or so. Byte finds that unbearable.  
**Pixel**: Ruling: anyone tuckered out naps on the blanket. Ruling: the blanket is also on a timer.  
_Her Nubbins hover a hand’s width off the grass and look down at yours with polite interest._  

#### <a id="bc-pixel-post"></a>Variant `bc-pixel-post`

_Pixel’s flyers drift down one by one and nap in a row, like washing on a line._  
**Pixel** _(happy)_: Ruling: you won. Ruling: I’m annoyed. Ruling: I’m pleased. All three stand.  
**Pixel**: Mint green ribbon. I tested six greens. This one doesn’t fade.  

#### <a id="bc-luna-pre"></a>Variant `bc-luna-pre`

_Luna won’t hold a bout before dark. You meet her in the night garden as the white flowers open._  
**Luna** _(soft)_: Night field. Moon moves land a little harder after sunset. Mine are used to that.  
**Luna**: Yours might be sleepy. That’s all right. So are the moths, and they still get about.  
_Her Nubbins flicker between the flowers, pale as the petals and very hard to count._  
**Luna**: Go gently near the beds. Some of these only open one night a year.  

#### <a id="bc-luna-post"></a>Variant `bc-luna-post`

_Luna’s last flyer settles in a flower bed and falls asleep with its face in a petal._  
**Luna** _(happy)_: You were patient with them in the dark. Most people flap.  
**Luna**: A silver ribbon. It looks best by moonlight. Most things do. So do I, I’m told.  

#### <a id="bc-dusty-pre"></a>Variant `bc-dusty-pre`

_Dusty’s ring is a dug-out hollow at the foot of their cliff. It is mostly a very comfortable hole._  
**Dusty** _(think)_: Fair warning. My lot nap. A lot. Mid-bout, mid-move, mid-yawn.  
**Dusty**: It isn’t a trick. It’s who they are. You wait till they wake up, and then they’re fierce.  
_One of Dusty’s team is already asleep in the ring. Dusty tucks a leaf round it and calls it warming up._  
**Dusty**: Swim types. They like mud. Mud is only water that’s made plans.  

#### <a id="bc-dusty-post"></a>Variant `bc-dusty-post`

**Dusty** _(happy)_: That’s a win. Probably. Hard to tell with mine. They look the same either way.  
**Dusty**: Brown ribbon. I found it in a drawer, not in the ground. Mostly.

**Ember** _(think)_: …I’m a bit impressed. Don’t tell anyone.

#### <a id="bc-byte-pre"></a>Variant `bc-byte-pre`

#### <a id="bs-exhibition"></a>Variant `bs-exhibition`

_Before the race, the Circuit holds an exhibition bout on the infield. Nobody wins anything. Sparks has entered anyway._  
**Sparks** _(happy)_: Three on three! A warm-up! Loosens the legs, wakes the brain, scares the frost off the rails!  
**Byte** _(think)_: It does not scare the frost off the rails. I have measured the rails.  
**Sparks**: Then it scares Byte. Even better.  
_Her three runners bounce on the spot. One bounces sideways into the next, and both pretend it was planned._  
**Sparks**: Play-fight only. Tired ones nap. Winner buys the buns. Loser also buys the buns. I just like buns.  
_Your Nubbin looks at the white rails, then at Sparks, and decides she is the warmer of the two._  
**Byte**: I’ll time it. For no reason. The stopwatches are out anyway.  

**Choices**

- **Three on three. Warm those legs up.** — sets `{"bs-exhibition-fight":true}`
    - **Sparks**: YES! Lanes, everyone! Wrong lane! Other wrong lane! Close enough!
- **Save their legs for the race.** — sets `{"bs-exhibition-skip":true}`
    - **Sparks**: Sensible. Hate it. Respect it.
    - She holds the exhibition against Byte instead. He loses, mostly by stopping to time it.

#### <a id="bs-rumble"></a>Variant `bs-rumble`

_Past the falls, the Hollow path drops into a round room of roots. On the far side is a gap, and firefly light beyond it._  
**Nova** _(soft)_: Hazel’s camp is through there. I’d know that tarpaulin anywhere. It’s held up by string and opinions.  
_Between you and the gap, four young greys tumble about in the roots. They are playing at guarding it._  
_They are grey, but they are not still. One pounces on a beetle and misses by a long way._  
**Marina** _(think)_: They aren’t cross. They’re bored. Nobody’s played with them since she went in there.  
**Nova**: A rumble, then. Get one of yours through the gap to the camp. Nobody chases anybody off.  
**Marina**: Careful, not brave. Same as the ledge.  
_The greys see your four and freeze. Then the smallest wiggles its bottom, which is the oldest invitation there is._  

**Choices**

- **Four on four. Play, and make for the gap.** — sets `{"bs-rumble-fight":true}`
    - **Nova**: Spread out. The roots make corners, and corners are where the fun is.
- **Sit on a root and wait for them to wander off.** — sets `{"bs-rumble-skip":true}`
    - You sit. After a while the greys forget to guard anything, and drift off after a moth.
    - **Marina**: Hazel’s way. Takes longer. Works.

#### <a id="bs-corvin"></a>Variant `bs-corvin`

_Ember wants to see the garden before she has to see him. You come round by the back wall first._  
_Outside the railings is one square bed the frost has not taken: winter kale, dark green and ragged._  
_It is the only green thing for half a mile._  
**Ember** _(think)_: That’s been there as long as I can remember. He hates it.  
**Corvin Vale**: I don’t hate it. I clear it. Every dusk.  
_He is on the other side of the railings, coat buttoned, left hand flat over his breast pocket._  
**Corvin Vale** _(think)_: It comes back every time. Nothing I keep in here comes back to anything.  
**Corvin Vale**: Mine will clear it now. They won’t hurt anything. They never do anything I haven’t asked.  
_A line of grey runners files out of the side gate towards the bed, perfectly in step._  
**Ember** _(angry)_: Stop them, then. Or don’t. I don’t know which is worse.  

**Choices**

- **Stand your Nubbins round the bed and hold it till dark.** — sets `{"bs-corvin-defended":true,"bs-corvin-fight":true}`
    - **Corvin Vale**: A play-fight. Of course. She would have done the same.
    - Your Nubbins plant their feet among the kale. The grey line stops, and waits to be told what to do about it.
- **Lean on the wall and ask him who planted it.** — sets `{"bs-corvin-talked":true,"bs-corvin-skip":true}`
    - **Corvin Vale**: Nobody. It was here when I came. I assumed it was a mistake.
    - He lifts one hand. The grey runners stop mid-step and stand there in the dusk.
    - **Corvin Vale**: Come round to the front. We’ll do this properly.
    - He goes in to meet you at his own gate, as if none of this counted.

#### <a id="bs-remembering"></a>Variant `bs-remembering`

_A spring evening, long after. Moss is on his flat stone on the hill, and he has been waiting for you._  
**Moss** _(soft)_: There is one I never told you of. It is not in the Tale of the Four. It is older than the tale’s ending.  
**Moss**: In the White Year, when the cold came down, one of the old Shadows went into the Hollow and lay down under the roots.  
**Moss** _(sad)_: The stone is unmade. The cold has gone out of the valley. Nobody told that one.  
_Down past the falls, where the roots meet the rock, frost lies on a single patch of ground. It does not melt._  
**Moss**: It is not wicked. It has only been cold for a very long time, and has forgotten there is anything else.  
**Moss** _(think)_: You cannot warm a thing by beating it. You stand near, and you stay, until it remembers.  
**Moss**: Bring six. The gentle ones as well as the strong. It will want to be sure.  
_He gets up slowly, one hand on the stone, and does not ask whether you are coming._  

**Choices**

- **Go down with him. Bring six.** — sets `{"bs-remembering-fight":true}`
    - **Moss**: Good. Slow hands, then.
- **Not tonight. Sit with him here instead.** — sets `{"bs-remembering-skip":true}`
    - You sit. Moss says nothing more about it, and the evening goes blue around you both.
    - **Moss**: It has waited a long while. It can wait for a kinder night.
