# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Battles: the Grove Circuit, Play-fight Day and the story battles

## Variant scenes

Versions of the variants with no parent scene in this storyline.

#### `sprout:bc-playday-open` · [main](main.md#bc-playday-open)

_It’s Play-fight Day on the green! Hooray!_  
**Fern**: Nobody gets hurt. Nobody gets cross.  
**Fern**: A tired Nubbin has a nap. That’s the rule!  
_Clover’s Nubbin sneezes. Achoo! A leaf lands on its head._  
_Look! Little hearts float over each Nubbin._  
_Your Nubbin has 7 hearts. Clover’s Nubbin has 5 hearts._  
**Clover**: Which number is bigger, 7 or 5?  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.C.6, ask): compare two numbers: 7 is more than 5  
> _(Count the hearts together: 7 here, 5 there.)_  
> _Who has more hearts?_  
> - ✅ Your Nubbin, with 7 → Yes! 7 is more than 5. Yours has 2 more hearts.
> - Clover’s Nubbin, with 5 → Five hearts, bobbing like balloons! So pretty. Now count yours: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7. Seven is more than five.
> - They have the same → The same, like two peas in a pod? Let’s look again. 7 hearts and 5 hearts. 7 is bigger. Yours has more!
> _For grown-ups: Compared two groups of hearts and found that 7 is more than 5._


#### `sprout:bc-playday-bout` · [main](main.md#bc-playday-bout)

_Look at the top. Little faces wait in a line._  
**Clover**: That line shows whose turn is next.  
_Clap, clap! Your turn. Clap, clap! My turn._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): take turns and wait for your go  
> _Clover’s Nubbin wants to go now. But it’s your turn._  
> _What should it do?_  
> - ✅ Wait for its turn → Yes. It waits. Then it gets its go. Fair for everyone!
> - Push in → Push in? Bump, wobble, everyone falls over in a heap! Waiting is fairer. Then everyone gets a go.
> - Go off in a huff → A huffy Nubbin, stomping off with its nose in the air! Silly. If it waits a little, its turn comes next.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about waiting for your turn, and why that is fair._

_Your Nubbin is a Swim one. Clover’s is a Fly one._  
**Fern**: Swim soaks Fly! Splash!  
_Splish! Clover’s Nubbin lands with a soggy bump. It looks at its wet feet._  
_It yawns. It has a nap under a leaf. Shh!_  

#### `sprout:bc-playday-hug` · [main](main.md#bc-playday-hug)

_The bout is over. The two Nubbins run to the middle._  
_Squeeze! A big Nubbin hug._  
**Clover**: Every bout ends with a hug. Every single one.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): hear words that rhyme with “hug”  
> _Hug. Say it slowly: h-ug._  
> _Which word rhymes with hug?_  
> - ✅ Bug → Hug, bug! A hug for a bug! They rhyme.
> - Plum → A plum giving a hug? Squish! That’s funny. Plum ends with “um”. Hug and bug both end with “ug”.
> - Nap → A nap-hug, all snuggly! Lovely. Nap ends with “ap”. Hug rhymes with bug: “ug”, “ug”.
> _For grown-ups: Listened for a word that rhymes with “hug”._

_Your Nubbin has one plum. Crack! It breaks it in two._  
_It gives half to Clover’s Nubbin. That’s sharing._  
**Fern**: Then a nap. Then another snack. Best day ever.  
_Munch, munch. Two happy Nubbins._
