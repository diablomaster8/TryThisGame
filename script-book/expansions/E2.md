# Expansion E2: Nova’s Season

## E2. Nova’s Season

*Original (the lifespan clock, Digimon-style short lives)* · audience: main · where: The gate · when: A morning after the ending, once the square has begun

### Step 1: Hatch Nova’s egg.

Hint: It hatches in a few minutes. Shake it while held to hurry it. · check: `hatch:dandy`

#### <a id="e2-egg"></a>Scene `e2-egg`

_Nova is at the gate before the dew is off. She has an egg under her coat, held the way you hold a hot drink._  
**Nova**: Short line. Twelve days, hatch to hill. I don’t have a garden that’s quiet enough.  
**Nova**: The strays’ ring is no place for it. It wants a step and a tree and somebody who stays.  
**Nova**: You can say no. People do.  

**Choices**

- **Twelve days is enough.** — sets `{"e2-answer":"enough"}`
    - **Nova**: That’s what I hoped someone would say.
- **That’s not long.** — sets `{"e2-answer":"short"}`
    - **Nova**: No. But it’s all of it, for them. That’s the thing.

#### <a id="e2-egg-done"></a>Done `e2-egg-done`

_It hatches small, even for a Nubbin. It looks up as if it has been waiting for you specifically._  
_There is a freckle on its brow, one, off-centre, the kind a thumb would find._  
_You call it Dandy. Nova, at the gate, writes the name on the back of her hand._  
**Nova**: Day one. I’ll come on four. Don’t count out loud, it hears you.  

### Step 2: On day four, pet Dandy.

Hint: The stats sheet shows the day. · check: `pet:dandy`

#### <a id="e2-day4"></a>Scene `e2-day4`

_Day four. Nova comes by with nothing in her hands, which for Nova is a kind of gift._  
**Nova**: A third gone. Doesn’t look it, does it. They never do till day nine.  
_Dandy has found the step, and the tree, and the place under the tree where the step is in shade._  
**Nova**: It’s got the freckle. The whole line has it. I don’t know why I find that so steadying.  
**Nova**: Pet it for me. I don’t want to be a stranger it liked once.  

#### <a id="e2-day4-slips"></a>Variant `e2-day4-slips`

_Variant of `e2-day4`._

_Day four. Nova comes by and watches Dandy for a while before she says anything._  
**Nova**: It’s gone hungry twice. Sleepy once. I’m not telling you off. I’m telling you the clock.  
**Nova**: On a long life that’s a bad week. On twelve days it’s a bad year. They carry it.  
_Dandy, who does not know it is carrying anything, climbs her boot._  
**Nova**: Pet it for me. There isn’t a lot of day four left.  

### Step 3: On day eight, feed Dandy.

Hint: Fruit from a tree, or something better. · check: `feed:nubbin`

#### <a id="e2-day8"></a>Scene `e2-day8`

_Day eight. Dandy is grown now, whatever grown means in a week. It sits where the sun lands first._  
**Nova**: Past the middle. This is the part I always want to stretch.  
_She sits down on the step. Dandy moves over, exactly a Nova’s width, without looking._  
**Nova**: It did that for the last one too. The step thing. I never taught it. Nobody teaches it.  
**Nova**: Feed it something good. The last third should taste of something.  

#### <a id="e2-day8-moody"></a>Variant `e2-day8-moody`

_Variant of `e2-day8`._

_Day eight. Dandy is grown and grumpy with it, a small cloud that follows the sun around._  
**Nova**: It came up moody. That’s on the days we missed, not on it.  
_It sits on the far end of the step from her. It is still on the step._  
**Nova**: See that. Grumpy, and on the step. That’s the whole of what I know about love.  
**Nova**: Feed it something good anyway. Grumpy isn’t the same as unloved.  

### Step 4: On day eleven, sit with Dandy till dark.

Hint: Stay near. Dusk comes on its own. · check: `wait:dusk`

#### <a id="e2-day11"></a>Scene `e2-day11`

_Day eleven. Nova sits down on the step beside you without asking, and stays._  
**Nova**: Tomorrow it goes into the cocoon. One or the other kind. You’ll know which by the light.  
_Dandy is asleep between you, heavy for its size, the freckle turned up to the sky._  

**Choices**

- **Ask her which she thinks.** — sets `{"e2-asked":"asked"}`
    - **Nova**: I think it liked you. That’s the only thing I’ve ever been able to tell in advance.
    - **Nova**: The rest is the light. Sit with it till dark. That’s all there is to do, and it’s a lot.
- **Don’t ask.** — sets `{"e2-asked":"quiet"}`
    - **You**: You don’t ask which she thinks. She doesn’t say.
    - **Nova**: Sit with it till dark. That’s all there is to do, and it’s a lot.

### Step 5: See the cocoon through.

Hint: Rebirth needs a happy life and real affection. Departure leaves a flower. · check: `choose:outcome`

#### <a id="e2-end"></a>Scene `e2-end`

_Morning. The cocoon on the grass is still, and the light on it has decided._  
_Nova is at the gate already. She has not come in. She is waiting to be told which._  

### Complete

#### <a id="e2-complete-reborn"></a>Complete `e2-complete-reborn`

_The cocoon glows from the inside, slow, like a lamp turned up. It splits. An egg, warm, with the same freckle on it._  
**Nova** _(happy)_: Twelve days and it wanted twelve more. That’s what you did.  
_When it hatches it greets you with a heart, and remembers its name before you say it._  
_It goes to the step, and moves over a Nova’s width, and waits._  
**Nova**: I’ll come on day four. Don’t be a stranger it liked once.  

#### <a id="e2-complete-departed"></a>Variant `e2-complete-departed`

_Variant within E2; it has no single parent scene._

_The cocoon is soft and grey and getting softer. There is no bang and no door. It simply isn’t there, and then a flower is._  
**Nova** _(sad)_: Twelve days. It had all twelve. That’s the whole of what there was, and it was yours.  
_The flower has a mark on one petal, off-centre, the kind a thumb would find._  
_Nova stays until the flower has opened. Then she goes, and comes back the next morning anyway._  
**Nova** _(soft)_: Short line. I keep taking them on. You’d think I’d learn.  

**Rewards**

- Twelve Days (title): You kept a short line company from hatch to hill.
