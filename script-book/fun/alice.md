# Fun quests: Alice in Wonderland (Q66–Q75)

## Q66. Down the Rabbit-Hole

*Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland: the rabbit-hole and the pool of tears* · audience: all · where: The stream bank, in the shade of the hedge · when: A hot summer afternoon, any time after chapter 2 (never during Chapters 9–11)

**Cast:** The White Rabbit (The White Rabbit (Wonderland)), Alice (Alice (Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland))

### Step 1: Follow the White Rabbit under the hedge.

Hint: Walk to the hedge where the Rabbit vanished. Your Nubbin is already trotting after it. · check: `walk:q66-rabbit-hole`

#### <a id="q66-intro"></a>Scene `q66-intro`

_It is a hot afternoon, the kind that makes the stream sound sleepy. Your Nubbin is a warm weight on your chest._  
_A rabbit trots past in a waistcoat. It takes a watch out of its pocket and looks at it._  
**The White Rabbit** _(shock)_: Oh dear! Oh dear! I shall be late!  
_It dives under the hedge. A girl in a blue pinafore dives in after it, without stopping to think._  
_Your Nubbin opens one eye, considers the hedge, and decides that it is interested._  
**You**: Well. If it is going to be a dream, I may as well see it properly.  

#### <a id="q66-fall"></a>Done `q66-fall`

_The hole runs straight on like a tunnel, then dips, and then you are falling._  
_You fall slowly, so slowly that you have time to look at the cupboards and bookshelves on the walls._  
_A jar goes by, labelled ORANGE MARMALADE, and empty. Alice puts it back on a shelf as she passes, politely._  
**Alice**: I shall never grumble about stairs again. Not after this.  
_Your Nubbin turns gently, with its arms out. It would like this to go on for a while._  

### Step 2: Swim your Nubbin out of the pool of tears.

Hint: Drink from the bottle, then swim across the pool to the dry edge. Your Nubbin paddles beside you. · check: `swim:q66-tear-pool`

#### <a id="q66-hall"></a>Scene `q66-hall`

_You land in a long, low hall full of doors, every one of them locked._  
_On a glass table lie a tiny golden key and a bottle with a paper label: DRINK ME._  
**Alice**: It does not say POISON. I suppose that is something.  
_Alice drinks, and your Nubbin takes a very small sip, and so do you. The hall seems to lift up around you._  
_Everyone is ten inches high. The key on the table is now a mile above your head._  
**Alice** _(shock)_: Curiouser and curiouser!  
_Alice sits down and cries, a great deal, for a girl that size. The tears gather in a pool round your boots._  
_Your Nubbin is already paddling in it, with its chin up._  

**Choices**

- **Lift your Nubbin onto your shoulders and wade.**
    - Your Nubbin hangs on to your hair. It is still very wet.
- **Swim beside your Nubbin and let it set the pace.**
    - It sets a brisk paddle, ears up. You follow.

#### <a id="q66-bank"></a>Done `q66-bank`

_You drag yourselves out on the far side. A crowd of dripping creatures is already there._  
_Your Nubbin shakes itself like a dog. Everyone near it gets wetter._  
**Alice**: I am sorry. I did not mean to cry so much. I shall try to be a little less enormous.  

### Complete

#### <a id="q66-complete"></a>Complete `q66-complete`

_The bottle is still in your pocket, with its label and its cork. When you shake it, nothing at all moves inside._  
_Alice looks at it with great respect. She says it is the most honest bottle she has ever seen._  
**You**: It says DRINK ME. It does not say why.  
**Alice** _(happy)_: That is very Wonderland of it.  
_The wet creatures are muttering about getting dry. Someone is bound to have an idea._  
_Your Nubbin is already running toward them. You follow, as usual, a step behind._  

**Rewards**

- DRINK ME Bottle (decor): A small glass bottle with a paper label. It does nothing at all.

**Learning Grove swaps**

- `q66-intro#2` → Late, late, late! And my watch has stopped at teatime again!
- `q66-hall#5` → Stranger and stranger! Is that the right word? It is now.

**Notes:** The dream opens with a soft dissolve into green (stream, dappled light). The fall is a slow vertical scroll with shelves passing; no hard landing. Step 2 uses the Grove swim animation in a shallow pool of tears (the Nubbin swims even if it is not a Swim type). Everyone is shrunk by a camera zoom, not a model change. The bottle decor does nothing when used. "Oh dear! Oh dear! I shall be late!" and "Curiouser and curiouser!" are each used once, both swapped in childLines. Alice is the heroine; the player only follows.

## Q67. A Caucus-Race

*Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland: the Caucus-race* · audience: all · where: The stream bank, in the shade of the hedge · when: Straight after Q66 (never during Chapters 9–11) · requires: Q66

**Cast:** The Dodo (The Dodo (Wonderland)), The Mouse (The Mouse (Wonderland)), Alice (Alice (Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland))

### Step 1: Run your Nubbin round the Dodo’s caucus-course.

Hint: The course is a loose circle on the bank. Run your Nubbin round it with the Mouse, the Duck and the Lory, any speed, any direction. · check: `race:caucus:finish`

#### <a id="q67-intro"></a>Scene `q67-intro`

_The creatures on the bank are soaked, cross and cold. A large bird with a very upright manner is addressing them._  
**The Dodo** _(think)_: The best way to get dry is a Caucus-Race. I propose it at once.  
_The Dodo marks out a course in a sort of circle, using its beak and some hard looks._  
**The Dodo**: Nobody starts when I say. Everybody starts when they like. Everybody stops when they like.  
**The Mouse** _(sad)_: How will we know when it is over?  
**The Dodo** _(shock)_: That is a very good question. Nobody has ever asked it before. Off you go.  
_Your Nubbin is already running, the Duck is running the other way, and the Lory has gone round twice._  

#### <a id="q67-over"></a>Done `q67-over`

_After half an hour, everyone is quite dry. The Dodo ends the race by announcing that it has ended._  
**The Dodo** _(happy)_: The race is over!  
**The Mouse** _(think)_: But who won?  
_The Dodo sits and thinks for a long time, with one claw on its forehead._  

### Step 2: Hand out Alice’s comfits as prizes.

Hint: Alice has a box of comfits in her pocket. Give one to every racer, then one to the Dodo. · check: `give:item:comfit:caucus-racers`

#### <a id="q67-prizes"></a>Scene `q67-prizes`

**The Dodo** _(happy)_: EVERYBODY has won, and all must have prizes.  
**Alice**: Then who is going to give them out?  
**The Dodo**: Why, you are, of course.  
_Alice finds a box of comfits in her pocket, rather squashed, and hands them round with great care._  
_There is exactly one each, for every creature. You hold the box while Alice does the counting._  
_Your Nubbin takes its comfit and puts it on top of its head, for later._  
**The Mouse**: And what about you? You must have a prize too.  
**Alice** _(soft)_: I have only a thimble in my pocket.  
_The Dodo solemnly presents Alice with her own thimble. Everyone cheers, politely._  

### Complete

#### <a id="q67-complete"></a>Complete `q67-complete`

_The Dodo gives a short speech, which is mostly the word “elegant”._  
**The Dodo** _(happy)_: Splendid! Quite splendid! I could not have arranged it better myself.  
_Your Nubbin has fallen asleep with its comfit on its head, and no one wishes to disturb it._  
**You**: That is the best race I have ever run.  
**Alice** _(happy)_: You did not win it, though. Nobody did.  
**You**: That is why.  
_Alice laughs. The Dodo says “Hear, hear!” without knowing why._  

**Rewards**

- Thimble Hat (cosmetic): A silver thimble that fits a Nubbin like a very small helmet.
- Caucus Mode (feature): A fun race on the race track with no placings. Run it whenever you like; everybody gets a prize.

**Learning Grove swaps**

- `q67-prizes#0` → The race is over! Who won? All of you. Who lost? Nobody. Splendid.

**Notes:** The caucus course is an unmarked ring; use the race scene with a flat circle, no lanes. The Duck and Lory are background critters (they do not speak). The check passes when the player Nubbin has circled at least once and stopped. Caucus mode unlocks as a race option with no placings and no timer, with a prize screen that always lists everyone. The approved quote "EVERYBODY has won, and all must have prizes." is used once; swapped in childLines with the Learning Grove Dodo line.

## Q68. Advice from a Caterpillar

*Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland: Advice from a Caterpillar* · audience: all · where: A toadstool in the long grass, near the Rabbit’s house · when: Straight after Q67 (never during Chapters 9–11) · requires: Q67

**Cast:** The Caterpillar (The Caterpillar (Wonderland)), Alice (Alice (Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland))

### Step 1: Answer the Caterpillar, if you can.

Hint: Sit on the grass in front of the mushroom. Speak when the Caterpillar asks. · check: `talk:caterpillar`

#### <a id="q68-intro"></a>Scene `q68-intro`

_Alice is too big for the Rabbit’s house, and then too small for everything else. She has stopped keeping track._  
_On top of a mushroom sits a blue Caterpillar, blowing bubbles from a long pipe and looking at the sky._  
**The Caterpillar**: Who are YOU?  
**Alice**: I am not sure. I have been so many different sizes since breakfast.  
**The Caterpillar** _(angry)_: That is not an answer. Explain yourself.  
_Your Nubbin hides behind your leg. The Caterpillar turns its sleepy eyes on you._  
**You**: I am… the one holding the Nubbin.  
**The Caterpillar** _(think)_: That is as good an answer as any.  

**Choices**

- **Say nothing else. Wait.**
    - The Caterpillar waits with you. It is better at waiting than anyone you have ever met.
- **Ask what size is best.**
    - **The Caterpillar**: Three inches. It is a very good height.

#### <a id="q68-mushroom"></a>Done `q68-mushroom`

**The Caterpillar** _(think)_: One side of the mushroom makes you tall. The other side makes you small.  
**Alice**: One side of what? The other side of what?  
**The Caterpillar** _(soft)_: Of the mushroom.  
_It slides off its seat and creeps away through the grass. It leaves the mushroom behind, as a favour._  

### Step 2: Feed your Nubbin the other side of the mushroom until it is the right size again.

Hint: Break a piece off each side. Feed your Nubbin small bites of the shrinking side until it is back to its own size. · check: `feed:q68-nubbin:mushroom-shrink`

#### <a id="q68-nibble"></a>Scene `q68-nibble`

_Your Nubbin has nibbled the wrong side already. Its head is level with the treetops, and it looks surprised._  
_It waves a hand the size of a cartwheel. Birds scatter._  
**Alice** _(shock)_: Quick! The other side! Small bites, mind. A very small bite.  
_You climb up its arm with a piece of mushroom. It bends down, politely, and opens its mouth._  
_One crumb makes it a house tall. Another brings it to the size of a cow, and then a dog._  
_It stops at a perfect Nubbin size, and sits on a toadstool to think about things._  

### Complete

#### <a id="q68-complete"></a>Complete `q68-complete`

_Alice keeps the two halves in separate pockets, and marks them with her handkerchief._  
**Alice**: I do not feel any different. But I suppose I should not feel different. I should only be different.  
**You**: That sounds like the Caterpillar’s advice.  
**Alice** _(happy)_: It is the only kind of advice he has.  
_Your Nubbin is at its own size at last. It pats itself all over to be sure._  

**Rewards**

- Two-Tone Mushroom Stool (decor): A mushroom, red on one side and blue on the other, that makes an excellent stool.

**Learning Grove swaps**

- `q68-intro#2` → And who might you be? Take your time. I have all afternoon.

**Notes:** The Caterpillar is blue, and its pipe produces only soap bubbles in this version. Step 2: the Nubbin model is scaled by feed bites (five tiers); it recovers its size whichever bites you give, but the right side is faster. No stat change. "Who are YOU?" used once; swapped in childLines.

## Q69. A Mad Tea-Party

*Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland: A Mad Tea-Party* · audience: all · where: A fork in the path under a tree · when: Straight after Q68 (never during Chapters 9–11) · requires: Q68

**Cast:** The Cheshire Cat (The Cheshire Cat (Wonderland)), The Hatter (The Hatter (Wonderland)), The March Hare (The March Hare (Wonderland)), Alice (Alice (Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland))

### Step 1: Ask the Cheshire Cat the way.

Hint: Stand under the tree where the grin is. Speak to the Cat. · check: `talk:cheshire-cat`

#### <a id="q69-cat"></a>Scene `q69-cat`

_A grin is hanging in a tree. It is soon followed by a cat, and then, more slowly, by the rest of the cat._  
**Alice**: Which way should we go from here, please?  
**The Cheshire Cat** _(soft)_: That way lives a Hatter. That way lives a March Hare. Visit whichever you like.  
**Alice**: But I do not want to go among mad people.  
**The Cheshire Cat** _(happy)_: We’re all mad here. I’m mad. You’re mad.  
_Your Nubbin sits at the foot of the tree, looking up. The Cat looks down at it for a long time._  
_Your Nubbin says nothing. The Cat nods, as if it were the sensible answer._  

#### <a id="q69-cat-gone"></a>Done `q69-cat-gone`

**The Cheshire Cat** _(soft)_: It hardly matters which, you see. They will both be at tea.  
_It fades from the tail forward. The grin stays a moment after, then follows._  

### Step 2: Sit down at the long table and take tea with the Hatter and the Hare.

Hint: Take a seat at the long tea-table. Pass the cups when asked. · check: `sit:q69-tea-table`

#### <a id="q69-table"></a>Scene `q69-table`

_A table is laid under a tree, for twelve at least, though only three sit at one end._  
**The Hatter** _(angry)_: Full up! Not a seat to spare!  
**Alice**: There are dozens of empty seats.  
_The Dormouse is asleep in the teapot, with only its nose showing. Your Nubbin tries to climb in beside it._  
**The Hatter** _(think)_: Why is a raven like a writing-desk?  
**Alice**: I believe I can guess that.  
**The Hatter**: You mean you think you can find the answer?  
**The March Hare** _(happy)_: Take some more tea,  
**Alice**: I’ve had nothing yet,  
**The Hatter** _(happy)_: You mean you can’t take LESS. It’s very easy to take MORE than nothing.  

**Choices**

- **Pour tea for the Hare, to be polite.**
    - **The March Hare**: Wrong cup! Everybody move down one place!
- **Take a guess at the riddle.**
    - **The Hatter**: Splendid! I have not the slightest idea either.

#### <a id="q69-move-down"></a>Done `q69-move-down`

_Everyone moves one place round the table. The table does not get any cleaner._  
_Your Nubbin ends up in a different chair every ten seconds. It is having a wonderful time._  

### Step 3: Help Alice leave the tea-party without being rude.

Hint: Thank the Hatter, the Hare and the Dormouse, in whatever order feels politest. It is harder than it sounds. · check: `choose:q69-leave`

#### <a id="q69-leave"></a>Scene `q69-leave`

**Alice** _(soft)_: Thank you for the tea. I am afraid I must go.  
**The Hatter** _(angry)_: Move along! Clean cups! Nobody has used these, but move along!  
_The Hare is offering Alice the butter. It is not clear whether it is meant as an apology._  
**Alice**: I do not wish to be rude. But I do wish to leave.  
_She looks at you. You look at your Nubbin, which is wearing the Dormouse as a hat._  

**Choices**

- **Bow to each of them in turn, and take Alice’s arm.**
    - **The Hatter**: How very formal. We shall have to be formal back.
- **Leave a clean cup at your place.**
    - **The Hatter**: Ah. You are one of those who put things back. How rare.

### Complete

#### <a id="q69-complete"></a>Complete `q69-complete`

_You leave the table the way you came, with only a little tea on your boots._  
_Behind you the Hatter is still arguing about the time. It is always six o’clock there._  
**Alice** _(happy)_: That was the silliest tea-party I have ever been to.  
**You**: Did you like it?  
**Alice** _(happy)_: Very much. I shall never say so.  
_The Hatter runs after you with his hat, which has a paper ticket in the band. He presses it into your hands._  

**Rewards**

- The Hatter’s Hat (cosmetic): A tall hat with a paper ticket in the band that reads “In this Style 10/6”.
- Long Tea-Table (decor): A very long table laid for twelve, with every cup a different cup.

**Learning Grove swaps**

- `q69-cat#4` → We are all a bit silly here. I am. You are.
- `q69-table#4` → Why is a raven like a writing-desk? Nobody knows!
- `q69-table#7` → Have some more tea.
- `q69-table#8` → I have not had any yet, so I cannot have more.
- `q69-table#9` → You mean you cannot have LESS. It is very easy to have MORE than none.

**Notes:** The Hatter’s table has rotating seats; each move-down shifts the cups. The Dormouse speaks rarely and drowsily (as a sound only). Your Nubbin tries to climb into the teapot twice. The hat reward carries the paper ticket as a texture detail. "we’re all mad here. I’m mad. You’re mad.", "Why is a raven like a writing-desk?" and the "Take some more tea" exchange (lines 7 to 9, each speaker finishing the last’s sentence) are each used once; all swapped in childLines.

## Q70. The Queen’s Croquet-Ground

*Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland: The Queen’s Croquet-Ground* · audience: all · where: A garden of rose-trees behind high hedges · when: Straight after Q69 (never during Chapters 9–11) · requires: Q69

**Cast:** The Queen of Hearts (The Queen of Hearts (Wonderland)), The King of Hearts (The King of Hearts (Wonderland)), Two (The card-gardeners (Wonderland)), Five (The card-gardeners (Wonderland)), Seven (The card-gardeners (Wonderland)), The Gryphon (The Gryphon (Wonderland)), Alice (Alice (Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland))

### Step 1: Help the three card-gardeners hide the white roses.

Hint: Carry a paint pot to each white rose and brush it red before the Queen arrives. · check: `place:q70-rose:painted`

#### <a id="q70-roses"></a>Scene `q70-roses`

_Three playing cards are on their knees by a rose-tree, painting its white roses red as fast as they can._  
_One of them whispers that they planted a white rose-tree by mistake. The others do not whisper for long._  
**Two**: It was Five’s fault. He planted the white one.  
**Five**: Seven jogged my elbow!  
**Seven**: Nobody jogged anything. Keep painting.  
**Alice**: Why are you painting them?  
_The cards go quiet, and then start painting faster. A trumpet sounds close by._  
_Your Nubbin picks up a brush in its mouth and takes a long red stripe off the lowest rose._  

#### <a id="q70-painted"></a>Done `q70-painted`

_The last rose is red just as the procession arrives: cards, a King, and a Queen with an extremely small crown._  
_The Queen looks at the roses, and then at you, for slightly too long._  

### Step 2: Say no to the Queen of Hearts.

Hint: The Queen is holding out a small round Nubbin. Choose what you do with it. · check: `choose:q70-croquet`

#### <a id="q70-game"></a>Scene `q70-game`

_The game starts at once. The mallets are flamingos and the balls are curled-up hedgehogs._  
_The hedgehogs keep unrolling and walking off, which is rather against the rules._  
**The Queen of Hearts** _(angry)_: Where is my other ball? Somebody has lost a ball!  
_She lifts a small round Nubbin out from under a rose-bush, by the scruff, and puts it in your hands._  
**The Queen of Hearts** _(angry)_: You. Use this. Hit it with the flamingo.  
_It is the size of a hedgehog, and it is shivering._  
_You think of Hazel’s second rule. You think of it quite clearly._  
**You** _(angry)_: No.  
**The Queen of Hearts** _(shock)_: NO?  

**Choices**

- **Tuck it inside your coat, against your chest.** — sets `{"q70-coat":true}`
    - You button your coat over it. It is as warm as a loaf from the oven, and it stops shivering.
- **Set it down and let it run off under the hedge.** — sets `{"q70-garden":true}`
    - You put it on the grass. It looks at you for one second and bolts under the hedge, small as a leaf.
- **Give it to Alice, who pockets it beside your own.** — sets `{"q70-mirror":true}`
    - Alice does not ask a single question. She drops it into her apron with your Nubbin, and the two of them stare out.

#### <a id="q70-off-with"></a>Done `q70-off-with`

**The Queen of Hearts** _(angry)_: Off with their heads!  
_The cards come forward, looking unhappy about it. Alice takes a step back and stands in front of you both._  
**Alice** _(angry)_: Nonsense!  
_The King lays a timid hand on the Queen’s arm._  
**The King of Hearts**: Consider, my dear. She is only a child.  
_The Queen goes the colour of a beetroot, and says nothing at all._  

### Complete

#### <a id="q70-complete"></a>Complete `q70-complete`

_The cards have lowered their spears and drifted away, one at a time, so as not to be noticed._  
_The Gryphon appears beside you, with a large, tired smile._  
**The Gryphon** _(happy)_: It’s all her fancy, that: they never executes nobody, you know.  
**You**: Nobody?  
**The Gryphon** _(happy)_: Not so you would notice. Come along!  
_The croquet ground is still in a muddle. The flamingos have gone off to look for a quieter game._  

**Rewards**

- Croquet Lawn (decor): A neat lawn with hoops. Nubbins push a wooden ball round it. Nobody hits anything.

**Learning Grove swaps**

- `q70-off-with#0` → Take them away! Take them all away! …Where did everyone go?
- `q70-complete#2` → Oh, nobody is ever really sent away here. The Queen only shouts.

**Notes:** THE PACK’S ONE CHOICE. The three options set pack-local flags q70-coat, q70-garden or q70-mirror (the third is the give-it-to-Alice option). Q71 step 2 plays the matching witness scene (base q71-witness is the coat version; variants q71-witness-garden and q71-witness-mirror). Q75 plays the matching waking scene (base q75-wake is the coat version; variants q75-wake-garden and q75-wake-mirror). All three are warm. The Queen’s cry is Carroll’s "Off with their heads!" (Wonderland ch. VIII), aimed at the Keeper. The small Nubbin is a seeded child-type Nubbin with no name; it is NEVER added to the player’s roster. "they never executes nobody, you know" (Gryphon) and "Off with their heads!" each used once; both swapped in childLines.

### Step 1: Sit with the Mock Turtle and hear his lessons.

Hint: Sit on the shingle beside the Mock Turtle, and listen to the Gryphon’s story. · check: `sit:q71-mock-turtle`

#### <a id="q71-turtle"></a>Scene `q71-turtle`

**Alice**: And how many hours of lessons did you have?
