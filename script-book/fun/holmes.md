# Fun quests: Sherlock Holmes (Q76–Q85)

## Q76. You Have Been at the Tide Pools

*A Study in Scarlet and A Scandal in Bohemia: the first meeting and the art of noticing* · audience: all · where: The shop door, on the first misty morning you pass it after Chapter 2 · when: The first misty morning you pass the shop after Chapter 2, in the Free Grove or the campaign (never during Chapters 9–11)

**Cast:** Mr Hollis Holme (Sherlock Holmes (Arthur Conan Doyle)), Dr Weston Warde (Dr John Watson (Arthur Conan Doyle))

### Step 1: Take Dr Warde to the shop with the room to share.

Hint: Walk with the stranger along the lane; he follows. The card in the shop window is the place. · check: `escort:watson:shop`

#### <a id="q76-intro"></a>Scene `q76-intro`

_The mist has come in off the pond and settled in the lane like a cat. A man in a travel-worn coat is reading a card in the shop window._  
_ROOM ABOVE THE SHOP TO SHARE. The card has a ring on it where somebody has put down a very hot mug._  
**Dr Weston Warde** _(sad)_: Forgive me. I have just come back from the war with a limp and an allowance, and I am looking for cheap rooms.  
**Dr Weston Warde**: Dr Warde. Weston, to my friends, once I have some.  
**You**: I know the owner of the shop. I can take you in.  
**Dr Weston Warde** _(happy)_: That is the most useful sentence anyone has said to me this month.  

#### <a id="q76-arrive"></a>Done `q76-arrive`

_The shop smells of jam and rain. In the back, a thin man in his shirtsleeves is holding a jar of plum jam up to the lamp._  
_He is dusting the lid with flour and looking at it through a glass. There are a great many lids on the table._  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(think)_: Fingerprints, you see. Somebody in this valley has been into the plum jam without paying, and I intend to find who.  
_Byte is by the counter, holding a jar of plum jam. He puts it back, slowly, when Mr Holme looks up._  

### Step 2: Stay in the room above the shop while Mr Holme reads you.

Hint: Climb the stairs behind him and wait. He will do the talking. · check: `visit:room-above-shop:holmes`

#### <a id="q76-reading"></a>Scene `q76-reading`

_Byte follows you up the stairs, hands in his pockets, well away from the jam._  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: Ah. A new tenant. Mr Holme. I notice you have not taken off your hat for ten minutes, which means it is a good one.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(think)_: You have been in Afghanistan, I perceive.  
**Dr Weston Warde** _(shock)_: How on earth did you know that?  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: I never guess. It is a shocking habit. Your arm is held stiffly, your face is tanned only to the cuff, and you carry yourself like a doctor.  
_He turns his attention to you, which is a little like a lighthouse turning._  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(think)_: And you. Salt on both boots, and a damp hem. The tide pools, before nine, in the dark.  
**Mr Hollis Holme**: Fluff from a Nubbin on your left sleeve only. Ink on your thumb, from a letter you have read many times.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: You see, but you do not observe. The stairs, for example. How many?  
**You**: I… do not know.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(soft)_: Seventeen. I have climbed them hundreds of times. You have climbed them once. I have the better view.  

**Choices**

- **Ask how he knew about the letter.**
    - **Mr Hollis Holme**: Folded and refolded along one crease. The ink on your thumb is where the corner lives. It is a loved letter.
    - You decide to say nothing about who it is from. He seems to know that too.
- **Wait for Byte to say something.**
    - **Byte**: It is a trick. He asked someone in the village. Nobody can know all that from a sleeve.
    - **Mr Hollis Holme**: Then you may ask the village, Mr Byte. They will tell you I have not yet spoken to a soul in the valley.
    - **Byte**: …I hate that this is going to be a thing.

### Complete

#### <a id="q76-complete"></a>Complete `q76-complete`

_By noon, Dr Warde’s bag is on a chair and Mr Holme’s violin is on the other. They have agreed on everything except what to call the room._  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: We shall call it 221B. The shop has no number, but the room should have a good one.  
**Dr Weston Warde** _(think)_: Why 221B?  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(soft)_: Because I like how it sounds, Watson. A name is a good thing to have already.  
**Dr Weston Warde** _(happy)_: It is Warde, actually. But I think I am about to be called Watson from now on.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: Then call me Holmes, with an s. It seems only fair.  
**Dr Weston Warde** _(think)_: What does the s stand for?  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(soft)_: Showing off.  
_Holmes presses a brass magnifying glass into your hand. It has a dent in the rim, from a fall in some other country._  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(soft)_: You introduced us, so it is yours. Hold it to anything and you will see more of it, which is the whole of my method.  
_From now on, in Photo mode, you can zoom in on anything the Grove has to show._  

**Rewards**

- Magnifying Glass (feature): In Photo mode, a magnifying glass that zooms in on anything. Unlimited.

**Learning Grove swaps**

**Notes:** Misty morning in the lane, the first one after Chapter 2. Holmes is thin, long-jawed, always testing something (jam, then later clay, then bees). Watson walks with a stick and a limp. The shop gets a new upstairs window and a brass "221B" plaque. The magnifying glass is a Photo mode zoom tool, usable on any subject, unlimited. Approved quotes "You have been in Afghanistan, I perceive." and "You see, but you do not observe." each used once; both swapped in childLines. The tide-pool reading is already the Learning Grove line and is not swapped. Byte is the valley sceptic and recurs through the pack: he keeps trying to catch Holmes guessing and never does.

## Q77. The Red-Crested League

*The Red-Headed League: the copying job and the cellar* · audience: all · where: The seed shop, from the morning after Q76 · when: After the one before it, in the Free Grove or the campaign (never during Chapters 9–11) · requires: Q76

**Cast:** Holmes (Sherlock Holmes (Arthur Conan Doyle)), Dr Weston Warde (Dr John Watson (Arthur Conan Doyle)), Inspector Larch (Inspector Lestrade (Arthur Conan Doyle)), Old Mr Willet (Jabez Wilson, the red-headed pawnbroker (The Red-Headed League))

### Step 1: Hear Mr Willet’s story and look at the League’s office door.

Hint: Go to the lane behind the seed shop. The door with the notice is on the first floor. · check: `visit:league-office:door`

#### <a id="q77-intro"></a>Scene `q77-intro`

_Old Mr Willet’s Nubbin has a crest of bright red feathers that sticks up like a flame. It is very proud of this, and a little embarrassed by it._  
**Old Mr Willet** _(sad)_: It paid four Shells a morning. All I had to do was sit in an office and copy out the Collection book, from Apples onward.  
**Old Mr Willet**: Eight weeks. Then today I came and the door said THE LEAGUE IS DISSOLVED. I have lost my four Shells, and my good opinion of people.  
**Dr Weston Warde** _(think)_: That seems an odd sort of job.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: Charming. It is the oddest I have seen all week, and I have only been here since Tuesday.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(think)_: Watson, the lane outside. If you please.  
_Holmes taps the lane with his stick, three times, then listens, like a man tuning a piano with his foot._  

#### <a id="q77-door"></a>Done `q77-door`

_The door has a card tacked up with one pin and a certain amount of hope. It is neat, and new, and not quite level._  
**Old Mr Willet**: My new assistant found me the job. A lovely lad. Hardly ever at his desk.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(think)_: Look at the knees of Mr Willet’s eager new assistant, who got him the job. They are worn through, and stained with clay.  
**Mr Hollis Holme**: A man who spends his days at a desk does not wear his knees like that. A man who digs does.  

### Step 2: Wait in the dark cellar under the Keepers’ hall with Holmes and the Inspector.

Hint: Go to the hall after nightfall. Stand by the crates and keep very still. · check: `wait:cellar:keepers-hall:midnight`

#### <a id="q77-cellar"></a>Scene `q77-cellar`

_Under the Keepers’ hall it is cold, dark and smells of old apples. The Harvest Show’s prize Shells sit in a locked chest in the corner._  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(think)_: It is quite a three pipe problem, and I beg that you won’t speak to me for fifty minutes.  
_He holds a pipe in his teeth. It is cold, and he never lights it._  
**Dr Weston Warde** _(soft)_: You gave those up, Holmes.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: The problems didn’t.  
_Inspector Larch, up from the market town, arrives with a lantern and a deeply uncomfortable look. He sits on a barrel that turns out to be a pickle barrel._  
**Inspector Larch** _(sad)_: Do you think this is wise? I have a dinner to go to.  
**Mr Hollis Holme**: You may go at midnight. Not before.  
_At midnight there is a scrape from under the floor, and a flagstone lifts._  
_Two men climb out of a tunnel with a spade and a sack, and find a lantern in their faces._  

**Choices**

- **Hold the dark lantern steady.**
    - Your hand does not wobble, even when you very much want it to. Holmes notices, and says nothing, kindly.
- **Shout “Stop!” as loudly as you can.**
    - It echoes. The two men jump a foot, and one drops the sack onto the other’s toe.
    - **Inspector Larch**: Well. That did the job, though I would have chosen a quieter way.

#### <a id="q77-caught"></a>Done `q77-caught`

**Inspector Larch** _(shock)_: The Harvest Show prize Shells, and an entire tunnel to the seed shop’s cellar. Clever. Thoroughly clever.  
**Inspector Larch**: Come along, you two. This will make a very long statement.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(think)_: The League was a way to get Mr Willet out of the shop for eight weeks so they could dig under it. The red crest was only the bait.  

### Complete

#### <a id="q77-complete"></a>Complete `q77-complete`

_The seed shop is quiet again. Mr Willet sits on a sack of barley with his Nubbin on his lap, and looks, at last, a little less foolish._  
**Old Mr Willet** _(sad)_: Eight weeks of apples, and for what? I wish I had known they were digging. I would have brought them tea.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(soft)_: You have lost nothing but four Shells a morning, and your belief in the honesty of notices. I suggest you keep the second.  
**Old Mr Willet**: And my Nubbin’s crest? Is it worth anything now?  
_Holmes presents the Nubbin with a ribbon of red cloth, tied in a bow._  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: It is worth a good deal. It is the only reason I was called. Wear it well.  
_At dusk Holmes sits at the window above the shop with a cold pipe, thinking. It is not the same problem, but it is the same kind of silence._  

**Rewards**

- Red Crest (cosmetic): A tall red crest of feathers for any Nubbin.
- Dark Lantern (decor): A shuttered brass lantern, for the shelf or the hand.

**Learning Grove swaps**

- `q77-cellar#1` → This is a three-cup-of-tea problem. Please don’t talk for a bit.

**Notes:** The Keepers’ hall cellar is a new interior with a pickle barrel and a flagstone that lifts. The clay-stained knees are a prop on a silent assistant figure (only seen in the lane). Holmes holds the cold pipe in the cellar and never lights it. Approved quote "It is quite a three pipe problem, and I beg that you won’t speak to me for fifty minutes." is used once and swapped in childLines. Watson’s "You gave those up" exchange is from the outline. The red crest is a Nubbin cosmetic. Holmes does not ask "Well?" in this quest.

## Q78. The Blue Bead

*The Blue Carbuncle: the hat, the goose and the second chance* · audience: all · where: The lane outside the shop, the week of the Harvest Supper · when: The week of the Harvest Supper (autumn), after Q77, in the Free Grove or the campaign (never during Chapters 9–11) · requires: Q77

**Cast:** Holmes (Sherlock Holmes (Arthur Conan Doyle)), Dr Weston Warde (Dr John Watson (Arthur Conan Doyle)), Inspector Larch (Inspector Lestrade (Arthur Conan Doyle)), Jem Rudd (James Ryder, the hotel attendant (The Blue Carbuncle)), Lady Marrowby (The Countess of Morcar (The Blue Carbuncle))

### Step 1: Bring the battered hat and the cross goose upstairs to Holmes.

Hint: Pick up the hat from the lane. The goose will walk along behind it, complaining. · check: `carry:item:battered-hat:holmes`

#### <a id="q78-intro"></a>Scene `q78-intro`

_A scuffle in the lane, a shout, and then silence. Where it happened there is a battered hat, and a goose who is extremely cross about everything._  
_The goose has never been so insulted. It hisses at you, then at the hat, then at the sky for good measure._  
**Dr Weston Warde** _(think)_: Someone has run off and left a perfectly good goose. That is not the behaviour of a thief.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: Or it is exactly the behaviour of a thief who had a bad morning. Bring them both up, if you would.  

#### <a id="q78-hat"></a>Done `q78-hat`

_Holmes holds the hat up to the window, turns it over, and looks at the sweatband through his glass._  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(think)_: He is a man of some intelligence. He has fallen on hard times, though not lately. His wife no longer loves him.  
**Mr Hollis Holme**: He goes out very little, and his house has no gas. And the hat has, I suspect, a very small connection to this goose.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: Well?  

**Choices**

- **It sounds right to me.**
    - **Mr Hollis Holme**: Of course it does. It is nearly always right.
- **The hat is much too clean for someone whose wife has stopped caring.** — sets `{"q78-contradicted":true}`
    - **Mr Hollis Holme**: Hm. Sharp. I had not considered that she might brush it out of guilt.
    - **Mr Hollis Holme**: I stand corrected, by half. Do go on contradicting me. It is the best exercise I get.

### Step 2: Follow the trail to Jem Rudd and hear what he has to say.

Hint: The goose came from his sister’s farm. Find the young porter at the Supper hall. · check: `talk:jem-ryder:supper-hall`

#### <a id="q78-trail"></a>Scene `q78-trail`

_The goose coughs in the shop, politely at first and then not. Something small and blue skitters across the floor._  
_It is a bead the size of a pea, as blue as deep water. It throws a tiny shaft of light onto the ceiling._  
**Dr Weston Warde** _(shock)_: The Countess’s bead. It was taken from the Supper prize table on Tuesday, and a travelling tinker has been blamed for it.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: The tinker did nothing at all. The man who took it has a sister with a farm, and a very bad sense of geese.  
_At the Supper hall, a young porter is stacking tables. He has the look of someone listening to every footstep._  
**Jem Rudd** _(shock)_: Oh. It is you. Is it, I mean, is something the matter?  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(think)_: Mr Rudd. I believe you hid something in a goose.  
_Jem sits down on the nearest table. All the colour goes out of his face, and a little of it comes back as tears._  
**Jem Rudd** _(sad)_: I only meant to hide it until the fuss went down. Then I took the wrong goose. They all looked the same!  
**Jem Rudd** _(sad)_: I never meant for the tinker to be blamed. I could not sleep for it.  

### Step 3: Decide what is to happen to Jem Rudd.

Hint: Holmes will ask you. There is no wrong answer, but each one has a different ending. · check: `choose:q78:jem`

#### <a id="q78-choice"></a>Scene `q78-choice`

**Mr Hollis Holme** _(soft)_: He is frightened enough to stop. But you found the hat, and you carried the goose. I think you should decide.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(think)_: I suppose that I am commuting a felony, but it is just possible that I am saving a soul.  
**Mr Hollis Holme**: What would you do?  

**Choices**

- **Let him go, with a warning.** — sets `{"q78-jem":"freed"}`
    - **Mr Hollis Holme**: Then go, Mr Rudd, and do not do it again. I shall tell Lady Marrowby the bead has been found, and say nothing more.
    - **Jem Rudd**: I will. I will not. I mean, thank you. Thank you.
    - He is out of the door before the sentence is done. The goose, to be fair, goes with him as far as the corner.
- **Send for Inspector Larch.** — sets `{"q78-jem":"lestrade"}`
    - **Inspector Larch**: A theft is a theft, even a small one. Come along, young man.
    - **Jem Rudd**: I only wanted…
    - Jem goes quietly. At the door he looks back at you once, and you look at the floor.
- **Make him walk to the Countess and say it himself.** — sets `{"q78-jem":"owned"}`
    - **Mr Hollis Holme**: Then up the hill we go, Mr Rudd. It will be the hardest walk you have ever done, and the shortest.
    - Lady Marrowby hears him out in her great, cold hall. Her face does something complicated, and then she hands him her handkerchief.
    - **Lady Marrowby**: You have given it back, and you have said it to my face. Do not make me regret my handkerchief.

**Dr Weston Warde** _(think)_: You said the man was intelligent and unloved. He was neither.

## Q79. The Gate in the Night-Time

*Silver Blaze: the curious incident* · audience: all · where: Your own gate, the morning before the autumn Cup · when: The night before the autumn Cup at the race track, after Q78, in the Free Grove or the campaign (never during Chapters 9–11) · requires: Q78

**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: The gate did nothing in the night-time.

**Mr Hollis Holme** _(think)_: Well?

## Q80. The Nose

*The Sign of Four: Toby and the tar trail* · audience: all · where: Pixel’s workshop · when: After the one before it, in the Free Grove or the campaign (never during Chapters 9–11) · requires: Q79

**Cast:** Holmes (Sherlock Holmes (Arthur Conan Doyle)), Dr Weston Warde (Dr John Watson (Arthur Conan Doyle))

#### <a id="q80-intro"></a>Scene `q80-intro`

### Step 2: Follow Toby across the valley to the end of the trail.

## Q85. The Bees

### Step 1: Help Holmes build three hives.

Hint: Bring wood from the Workshop and set the hives where Holmes points. · check: `build:decor:beehive:3`

#### <a id="q85-intro"></a>Scene `q85-intro`

**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: I am retiring, Watson. I intend to keep bees on the sunny bank above the orchard.  
**Dr Weston Warde** _(soft)_: You have been retiring for a month. You will be bored in a week.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: Bees are not boring. They are the most reliable witnesses I have ever met.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: Here is the fruit of my leisured ease, the magnum opus of my latter years!

#### <a id="q85-hives"></a>Done `q85-hives`

_You build three hives in the Workshop yard. Holmes inspects every lid, and adds a fourth nail to each, in case of burglars._  
_The first bee arrives before the lids are on. She inspects everyone, and approves of nobody in particular._  

### Step 2: Carry the hives up and settle the bees.

Hint: Lift the hives onto the bank, and stay calm and slow while the bees move in. · check: `carry:decor:beehive:bank`

#### <a id="q85-settle"></a>Scene `q85-settle`

_The first hive is heavy and the second is heavier. The third, for no reason you can name, weighs about the same as a small piano._  
_Holmes walks ahead with a smoker in one hand and his book in the other, narrating the whole thing like a lecturer on a ship._  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(happy)_: Slowly. Bees dislike surprises. Which is, you will note, the same as most criminals.  
_Someone is coming up the bank behind you._  

#### <a id="q85-hive-done"></a>Done `q85-hive-done`

_The bees move into the hives in the early afternoon, in a long dark ribbon through the sun. Somebody has to hold the lid, and it is you._  
**Dr Weston Warde** _(soft)_: They are rather beautiful, Holmes. I had never noticed.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(soft)_: That is the problem with noticing. You have to practise it on small things first.  
_Byte has come up the bank behind you, with his arms folded and a jar under one elbow._  
**Byte** _(sad)_: I came to catch you guessing. Ten quests. Not once.  
**Mr Hollis Holme** _(think)_: And the plum jam, Mr Byte?  
**Byte** _(sad)_: …I was testing whether it was real. It was. I will pay.
