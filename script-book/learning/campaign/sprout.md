# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): the campaign

## sprout:book1

- Chapter 1 (The Letter): Find an egg in the long grass. Help it hatch. Then pet your new Nubbin! · The eggs are in the long grass. Give one a gentle shake. Tap your Nubbin, then tap Pet.
- Chapter 2 (Shells and Promises): Pick fruit from the trees. Feed your Nubbin from your hand. Do it two times! · Tap a tree to shake it. Tap a fruit to pick it up. Tap your Nubbin, then tap Feed.
- Chapter 3 (The One Who Hides): Help Sable trust you a little bit. · Sable is scared. Put fruit near her. Sit and wait. Pet her when she lets you. Never throw her.
- Chapter 4 (Moss Remembers): Pet Moss on the hill. Then light all four lanterns. · Moss sits on the hill. Tap a lantern post to light it. The fourth one only flickers. That’s fine!

### Endings

- **The Collector** (`collector`): This ending is not in the Little Sprouts story. Here the stone always goes back to the water.
- **The Empty Lantern** (`empty-lantern`): Sable gets too scared and runs away, and doesn’t come back. Slow, gentle hands keep her near.
- **Eternal Dawn (secret)** (`eternal-dawn`): Help one of your own Nubbins grow all the way into an Eternal, and be Sable’s best friend.
- **Wild Grove** (`wild-grove`): Open all of Corvin’s pens at once. Then take the gate away at the end.
- **Gran Hazel’s Grove** (`hazels-grove`): Be Sable’s best friend. Give Moss lots of pats. Make friends with Corvin. Send the stone back. Either gate choice at the end is fine.
- **Moss’s Flower** (`moss-sleeps`): Be Sable’s best friend, but give Moss only a few pats before the Long Night.
- **The Shadow’s Keeper** (`shadow-keeper`): Be Sable’s best friend and give Moss lots of pats, but don’t make friends with Corvin.
- **The Long Road** (`long-road`): Sable is still with you, but not your best friend yet. Keep sitting with her.
