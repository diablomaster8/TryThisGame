# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Fun quests: Avatar: The Last Airbender (Q46–Q65)

## Q46. Not the Cabbages!

- `q46-s1`: Find the 8 cabbages. Help fix the wheel. · _Cabbages roll downhill. Look by the stream and under the bushes._
- `q46-s2`: Shake the big tree. Then fix the cart. · _Tap the tree until nothing else falls. Planks and rope are in the shed._
- `q46-s3`: Build a cart that cannot tip over. · _Make it low and wide. Tuk’s drawing is in your journal._

#### `sprout:q46-crash` · [main](../../../fun/avatar.md#q46-crash)

_Creak, creak! A cart comes up the path._  
_It is full of round, green cabbages._  
**The cabbage merchant**: Hello! Crunchy cabbages! Lovely cabbages! Cabbages for everyone!  
_Here comes a Nubbin. It is racing. Zoom!_  
_It does not look where it is going. Bump!_  
_The cart tips over. Wobble, wobble, CRASH!_  
**The cabbage merchant**: Oh no! Not the cabbages! Not again!  
_The cabbages roll away down the hill. Roll, roll, roll!_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): round things roll  
> **The cabbage merchant**: Which one rolls away: a round cabbage or a square box?  
> _(Point to the round cabbages.)_  
> - ✅ The round cabbage → Yes! Round things roll. Boxes sit still.
> - The square box → A box has flat sides. It sits still. The round cabbage rolls away. Round things roll!
> _For grown-ups: Your child compared shapes and found that round things roll and flat-sided things stay put._

**The cabbage merchant**: And the wheel came off. Oh dear.  

**Choices**

- **Say sorry for your Nubbin**
    - **You**: Sorry! My Nubbin is still learning to look.
    - **The cabbage merchant**: Thank you. A sorry helps a lot.
- **Offer to buy the bumpy ones**
    - **The cabbage merchant**: Bumpy cabbages are still yummy cabbages!
- **Point to the race track sign**
    - **You**: Look. This is the race track.
    - **The cabbage merchant**: Oh! There is a sign. I am standing on it.

#### `sprout:q46-crash-done` · [main](../../../fun/avatar.md#q46-crash-done)

_You find the cabbages by the stream and under the bushes._  
_You put the wheel back on. Click!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 8  
> **The cabbage merchant**: Let’s count them back into the cart.  
> _(Count together on your fingers, one to eight.)_  
> **The cabbage merchant**: One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight!  
> Check: `edu:count:cabbages:8`
> _For grown-ups: Your child counted eight cabbages, one for each number._

**The cabbage merchant**: Every single one! A bit muddy. That’s fine!  
**The cabbage merchant**: Thank you for helping me.  

#### `sprout:q46-fruit` · [main](../../../fun/avatar.md#q46-fruit)

_The man parks his cart under a big tree._

> - ✅ Down → Yes! Things fall down to the ground.
> - Up → Let’s drop a leaf and watch. Down it goes! Things always fall down.
> _For grown-ups: Your child predicted that things fall down to the ground._

_It lands on the cart. The cart folds in half._

#### `sprout:q46-fruit-done` · [main](../../../fun/avatar.md#q46-fruit-done)

**The cabbage merchant**: Now I will park in the middle of the meadow.

> - ✅ Share it → Yes! Half for you, half for me. Sharing is kind.
> - Grab it → Grabbing makes someone sad. Let’s share it, half each. That feels better!
> _For grown-ups: Your child thought about how two friends can share one thing._

_They share it. Munch, munch. Everyone is happy._  

#### `sprout:q46-glider` · [main](../../../fun/avatar.md#q46-glider)

_Look up! A boy flies by on a wooden glider. Whoosh!_  
**Pemba**: Hi! Hello! Is that a cabbage cart? I love cabbages!  
_He waves. The glider dips. Uh-oh._  
_Pemba lands. He bounces into the cart. CRASH!_  
**The cabbage merchant**: Oh, my poor leafy darlings! Not again!  
**Pemba**: Oh no. That was me. I’m Pemba. I’m so sorry.  
**Pemba**: Can I help you fix it?  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, notice): saying sorry  
> _Pemba said sorry. Then he asked to help._  
> _That is a really good sorry._  
> _(Ask: when did you last say sorry?)_  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard what a good sorry sounds like: say sorry, then offer to help._

**Tuk**: Hi. I’m Tuk. I make plans. With drawings!  
**The cabbage merchant**: Three crashes today! I need a cart that cannot tip.  
**Tuk**: Then let’s build one!  

**Choices**

- **Make it wide**
    - **Tuk**: Wide, like a duck sitting down. Ducks don’t tip!
- **Make it low**
    - **Tuk**: Low and heavy at the bottom. Hard to knock over!
- **Give it a bell**
    - **The cabbage merchant**: A bell! Ding-ding! They will hear me coming!
    - **Tuk**: And we’ll make it low and wide too.

#### `sprout:q46-glider-done` · [main](../../../fun/avatar.md#q46-glider-done)

_You build the new cart with Tuk._  
_It sits low and wide, like a duck sitting down._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): low, wide things are steady  
> **Tuk**: Which cart is harder to tip over?  
> _(Hold up a hand tall and thin. Now lay it flat.)_  
> - ✅ Low and wide → Yes! Low and wide stays put. Like a duck.
> - Tall and thin → Tall and thin wobbles and tips. Low and wide stays put!
> _For grown-ups: Your child learned that a low, wide base is steadier than a tall, thin one._

**Pemba**: Can I test it? Please, please, please?  
**Tuk**: Go on, then. Push it!

> _For grown-ups: Your child listened for the first sound in “cabbage” and “cart”._

**Pemba**: Do you get sad when your cabbages fall?  
**The cabbage merchant**: Yes. But someone always stops to help.  
**The cabbage merchant**: That is how I make new friends.

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): rhymes

> - ✅ Doo → Yes! Blue, you, doo. They rhyme!

> _For grown-ups: Your child listened for rhyming words in a silly song._

**Wandering Juno**: Hmm. This part of the song isn’t written yet.

## Q48. The Great Divide

- `q48-s1`: Lead both groups down the canyon. No fighting! · _Walk in the middle. Stop for a rest when they get grumpy._
- `q48-s2`: Don’t laugh! Keep a straight face for Pemba’s story. · _Tap when the giggle meter is green. Don’t look at your Nubbin!_

#### `sprout:q48-canyon` · [main](../../../fun/avatar.md#q48-canyon)

_Two groups stand at the top of a big canyon._  
_They frown at each other. Hmph!_  
**Elder Maud**: We are the Hollins. We go first!  
**Chief Brack**: We are the Drays. WE go first!  
**Tuk**: Their guide went home. Who will lead them down?  
**Pemba**: Me! It’s just walking downhill!  
**Nalu**: Both groups want to go first. Hmm.  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, ask): taking turns is fair  
> **Nalu**: What is a fair way to choose?  
> - ✅ Take turns → Yes! One group now, the other next time.
> - The loudest goes first → Then quiet people never get a turn. Taking turns is fair.
> _For grown-ups: Your child thought about fairness and taking turns._


**Choices**

- **Hollins go first**
    - **Chief Brack**: Drays next time, then!
    - The Drays follow. Stomp, stomp.
- **Drays go first**
    - **Elder Maud**: Hollins next time, then.
    - The Hollins follow. Sniff, sniff.
- **Walk side by side**
    - **Elder Maud**: Hmph!
    - **Chief Brack**: Hmph!
    - But they walk side by side. The path is narrow!

#### `sprout:q48-canyon-done` · [main](../../../fun/avatar.md#q48-canyon-done)

_Down, down, down you go. Now you are at the bottom._  

> **Lesson** (science, 2-ESS1-1, notice): how canyons form  
> **Nalu**: Look how deep this canyon is!  
> **Nalu**: A river made it. The water cut the rock, bit by bit.  
> **Nalu**: It took a very, very long time.  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that rivers slowly cut deep canyons into rock._

**Tuk**: Nobody pushed anybody. Hooray!  
**Elder Maud**: Hollins sleep on the left.  
**Chief Brack**: Drays sleep on the left of the left!  
**Nalu**: That’s the same place, silly. You’re all together.  

#### `sprout:q48-story` · [main](../../../fun/avatar.md#q48-story)

**Elder Maud**: Your great-great-grandpa took our ball!  
**Chief Brack**: Your great-great-grandma kicked it in the river!  
**Pemba**: Wait! I know a story about them!  
**Pemba**: They were twins! They played ball together.  
**Pemba**: They laughed so hard, the canyon cracked open!

**Choices**

    - **Chief Brack**: Drays sing badly!

    - **Nalu**: Okay. But you’ll tell them one day, Pemba.
