# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Fun quests: Mr. Bean (Q21–Q30)

## Q21. Bear Comes Too

- `q21-s1`: Look for a clue by the pond. · _Search the reeds by the pond. Clues sparkle._
- `q21-s2`: Follow the little wild Nubbin up the hill. · _It nests in the long grass on the western hill. Walk slowly!_
- `q21-s3`: Bring Bear back to Mr. Tweed. · _He is on his rock by the pond. Take him the bear. Tap Do it._

#### `sprout:q21-intro` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q21-intro)

_A man in a tweed jacket pats all his pockets._  
_He looks in his hat. He looks under a sleeping duck!_  
_This is Mr. Tweed. He hardly ever says a whole sentence._  
**Mr. Tweed**: Hnn.  
_He holds his hands apart. Then he hugs the empty air._  
**You**: Something is missing?  
**Mr. Tweed**: Bear.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): naming feelings  
> _Mr. Tweed has lost his bear. How does he feel?_  
> - ✅ Sad → Yes. Losing a special toy feels sad. We can help.
> - Happy → Look at his droopy face. He feels sad. We can help him.
> _For grown-ups: Your child named Mr. Tweed’s feeling when he lost his special toy._

_He makes two little ears with his fingers. A bear!_  
_Then he points down to the pond._  

**Choices**

- **I’ll help you look!**
    - **Mr. Tweed**: Mm!
    - He shakes your hand with both hands. For a long time.
- **What does the bear look like?**
    - He shows you: small, brown, one chewed ear.
    - And a very proud face. Somehow he shows that too.
- **Ask your Nubbin to sniff for it.**
    - Your Nubbin sniffs his sleeve. Achoo! Off it trots.
    - **Mr. Tweed**: Ah!

#### `sprout:q21-scarf` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q21-scarf)

_In the reeds, you find a tiny knitted scarf._  
_It’s bear-sized. And a bit damp._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.1.4, ask): describing words  
> _The scarf is damp. What does damp mean?_  
> - ✅ A little bit wet → Yes! Damp means a little bit wet. Like a towel after a bath.
> - Very hot → Hot is warm, warm, warm. Damp means a little bit wet.
> _For grown-ups: Your child learned the describing word “damp”._

_Mr. Tweed hurries over and hugs it. Squeak!_  
**Mr. Tweed**: Ohh.  
_Then he sulks. His whole face folds up._  

#### `sprout:q21-sulk` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q21-sulk)

_Plop! Mr. Tweed sits on a rock and sulks._  
_Bottom lip out. Arms folded. A big sulk._  
_He peeks up the hill. Then away. Then up again._  
_A wild Nubbin is waddling uphill, holding something brown!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, ask): positions: up and down  
> _Is the wild Nubbin going up the hill, or down?_  
> - ✅ Up → Yes! Up, up, up the hill. Let’s follow!
> - Down → Look: it’s climbing. Up the hill!
> _For grown-ups: Your child used the position words up and down._

**Mr. Tweed**: Hm!  
_He stands up… remembers he’s sulking… and sits down._  
_He waves you on. This is your job now._  

#### `sprout:q21-nest` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q21-nest)

_At the top of the hill is a nest of grass._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS2-2, notice): animals build nests  
> _Lots of animals make nests to sleep in._  
> _Birds build nests from grass, twigs and moss._  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that many animals build nests, changing the place around them._

_A wild Nubbin is fast asleep, cuddling a small brown bear._  
_The bear has one chewed ear._  
_Shh. Tiptoe, tiptoe. Not a squeak!_  

**Choices**

- **Ease the bear out gently.**
    - The Nubbin sighs and lets go.
    - You leave it a fruit to cuddle instead.
- **Swap the bear for the little scarf.**
    - The Nubbin puts the scarf on. Very smart! A fair swap.
    - Mr. Tweed may have feelings about that.

#### `sprout:q21-carry` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q21-carry)

_The bear is very light._  
_Your Nubbin carries it, very carefully._  
_Down by the pond, Mr. Tweed is still sulking._  
_Your Nubbin hides the bear behind its back. Pop!_  
_The bear is wider than its back!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): wider and narrower  
> _The bear is wider than the Nubbin’s back. Is it hidden?_  
> - ✅ No, we can still see it! → Yes! Wider things poke out. Peekaboo, bear!
> - Yes, all hidden → Look: the bear sticks out on both sides. Wider things poke out!
> _For grown-ups: Your child compared widths to see if the bear could hide._

**You**: Ready? Surprise!  

#### `sprout:q21-complete` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q21-complete)

_Mr. Tweed sees the bear. His face opens up like a deckchair._  
**Mr. Tweed**: Bear! Oh, Bear!  
_He hugs it. Squeak! He checks one ear. Then the other._  
_He tucks it in his top pocket. Only its head peeks out._  
_Your Nubbin looks at the bear, wishing._  
_Mr. Tweed digs in his pockets… and his sock!_  
_He finds a teeny bear and puts it on your shoulder._  
**Mr. Tweed**: Bear.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, notice): kindness goes round  
> _You helped Mr. Tweed. Then he was kind back._  
> _Kindness goes round and round!_  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard how one kind act led to another._

_That night, he sets two plates. One very small._  

## Q22. The Armchair Ride

- `q22-s1`: Help Mr. Tweed tie the armchair on top of his cart. · _Use rope from your bag. Tap each corner of the chair._
- `q22-s2`: Clear the path before the cart gets there. · _Move the branch. Carry the sleepy Nubbin aside. Unhook the washing line._

#### `sprout:q22-shop` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q22-shop)

_Thump! At the market, Mr. Tweed buys a big green armchair._  
_His hand cart is tiny. About the size of the cushion._  
_He lifts one end. Then the other end. Still too big!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): will it fit? big and small  
> _The chair is big. The cart is small._  
> _Will the chair fit inside the cart?_  
> - ✅ No, it’s too big → Right! Big things don’t fit in small spaces. So… on top!
> - Yes → Look: the chair is much bigger than the cart. It won’t fit inside!
> _For grown-ups: Your child worked out that a big thing won’t fit inside a small space._

**Mr. Tweed**: Hnn.  
_Then, an idea! He points at the cart top and mimes a knot._  
**Mr. Tweed**: Rope!  
_He looks at you. You look like someone with rope._  

**Choices**

- **Give him some rope.**
    - He bows. He ties a knot… round himself.
- **Is this a good idea?**
    - **Mr. Tweed**: Mm.
    - He nods firmly. You fetch rope.
- **We could just carry it.**
    - He looks at the long road. Then he shakes his head, kindly.

#### `sprout:q22-tied` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q22-tied)

_The chair sits on the cart, tied at all four corners._  
_And once, by mistake, to a lamp post!_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): rhyming words  
> _Which word rhymes with rope?_  
> - ✅ Soap → Yes! Rope, soap, hope. They rhyme!
> - Chair → Chair rhymes with bear! Rope rhymes with soap.
> _For grown-ups: Your child found a word that rhymes with “rope”._

_You untie the lamp post. Mr. Tweed climbs into the chair._  
_He pushes the ground with a long stick. Off they roll! Whee!_  

#### `sprout:q22-road` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q22-road)

_The cart wobbles down the lane._  
_Mr. Tweed steers by leaning. He waves at the birds._  
**Mr. Tweed**: Ooh.  
_Ahead: a branch, a sleeping Nubbin, and a low washing line!_  
_Run ahead and clear the way!_  
_Now the lane goes downhill. The cart goes faster. Whee!_  

> **Lesson** (science, ask): rolling down slopes  
> _Why does the cart go faster now?_  
> - ✅ It’s going downhill → Yes! Things roll faster down a slope.
> - It’s hungry → Ha! Carts don’t eat. Going downhill makes things roll faster.
> _For grown-ups: Your child noticed that things roll faster down a slope._

_Mr. Tweed lifts his feet. As if that helps!_  

**Choices**

- **Run ahead!**
    - You run. Mr. Tweed hums a little driving song.
- **Send your Nubbin to wake the sleeper.**
    - Your Nubbin pokes it. It rolls over. Still asleep!

#### `sprout:q22-arrive` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q22-arrive)

_The cart stops at his gate. Bonk!_  
_His hat flies off. He catches it with his foot!_  
_Nothing fell off. He checks himself, the chair and the stick._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, ask): position: beside  
> _Where did you put the sleepy Nubbin, to keep it safe?_  
> - ✅ Beside the path → Yes! Beside the path, out of the way. Still snoring.
> - In the middle of the path → The cart would bump it! We put it beside the path.
> _For grown-ups: Your child used the position word “beside”._

**Mr. Tweed**: Ha!  

#### `sprout:q22-complete` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q22-complete)

_Mr. Tweed looks at the chair. Then at his little door. Creak!_  
_The chair won’t fit through! He measures with his hands. Three times._  
**Mr. Tweed**: Hnn.  
_He pats the chair and pushes it to you. It’s yours!_  
_He pats the cart too. Yours as well._  
_Then he sits outside on a small kitchen chair, in the sun._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, notice): coping with disappointment  
> _Mr. Tweed was disappointed. That’s OK._  
> _Then he made a new plan: a small chair in the sun._  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard how Mr. Tweed felt let down, then made a new plan._

_He pats the space beside him. There is no space._  
_You sit on the wall. You watch the lane together._  

## Q23. Pumpkin Head

- `q23-s1`: Calm the wandering pumpkin. · _Tap the pumpkin and choose Pet._
- `q23-s2`: Free the Nubbin with three gentle wiggles. · _Tap when the pumpkin glows. Tap, tap, tap. No pulling!_

#### `sprout:q23-patch` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q23-patch)

_It’s harvest time. The pumpkins are big and orange._  

> **Lesson** (science, notice): how pumpkins grow  
> _Pumpkins grow on long, twisty vines, along the ground._  
> _Inside, they’re full of seeds!_  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that pumpkins grow on vines and are full of seeds._

_One pumpkin is moving! Left. Right. Bonk! “Mff.”_  
_Mr. Tweed knocks on it. Knock, knock._  
_The pumpkin knocks back!_  
**Mr. Tweed**: Oh!  
_Two little feet stick out. A Nubbin climbed in, and it’s stuck!_  
_Mr. Tweed grabs the stalk, ready to pull hard._  

**Choices**

- **Stop! Not like that.**
    - He freezes. He lets go of the stalk.
    - **Mr. Tweed**: Mm.
- **Watch which way it wiggles.**
    - Left, right, left. The Nubbin is trying to wiggle out!
- _(left out in this band; plays as `stop`)_

#### `sprout:q23-calm` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q23-calm)

_You pat the pumpkin. Pat, pat._  
_It stops wandering and sits. Thump._  
_From inside comes a happy hum._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): gentle hands  
> _The Nubbin was stuck and scared. What helped?_  
> - ✅ Gentle pats and a calm voice → Yes! Gentle and calm helps someone feel safe.
> - Pulling hard → Pulling could hurt and scare it. Gentle and calm helps.
> _For grown-ups: Your child chose gentle pats and a calm voice to help a scared creature._

_Mr. Tweed listens with his ear. He nods._  

#### `sprout:q23-wiggle` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q23-wiggle)

_Mr. Tweed holds up three fingers._  
_He mimes: wiggle, wiggle, wiggle… pop!_  
_You put both hands on the pumpkin._  
_Inside, the Nubbin goes still and listens._  
**Mr. Tweed**: One…  
_He waves his arms like a band leader. The Nubbin hums along._  
**You**: Gently. On the beat.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.4, do): count to 3  
> _(Wiggle together! Count each one.)_  
> _One… two… three!_  
> Check: `edu:count:wiggles:3`
> _For grown-ups: Your child counted three gentle wiggles._


#### `sprout:q23-pop` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q23-pop)

_On wiggle three… pop! Out slides the Nubbin._  
_Seeds fly everywhere!_  
_It is orange up to its elbows. It smells lovely._  

> **Lesson** (art, ask): colour mixing: orange  
> _What colour is the Nubbin now?_  
> - ✅ Orange → Yes! Orange. Red and yellow paint mixed together make orange.
> - Purple → Purple is like grapes. The Nubbin is orange, like the pumpkin!
> _For grown-ups: Your child named orange and heard that red and yellow mix to make it._

_It looks at the empty pumpkin. Its lip wobbles._  

#### `sprout:q23-complete` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q23-complete)

_The Nubbin climbs straight back into the pumpkin. Head first!_  
_Mr. Tweed lifts it off and scoops it clean with a spoon._  
_He finds a butter knife in his sock. He cuts two eyeholes._  
_He pops it on the Nubbin, like a hat._  
_It fits! The Nubbin spins round, delighted._  
_Mr. Tweed tries one on too. Bonk! Into a fence post._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): bigger and smaller  
> _The pumpkin hat fits the Nubbin, but not Mr. Tweed. Whose head is bigger?_  
> - ✅ Mr. Tweed’s → Yes! His head is bigger, so the little hat is too tight.
> - The Nubbin’s → The Nubbin is small! Mr. Tweed’s head is bigger.
> _For grown-ups: Your child compared head sizes to see why one hat fitted and one didn’t._

**Mr. Tweed**: Mff.  
_It takes three tugs to get it off. He’s orange to the eyebrows!_  
_He gives you a smaller pumpkin hat with a curly stalk._  
_Some creatures just like a snug place to be._  

**Notes:** Sprout leaves out the “pull” choice in q23-patch (pulling a stuck creature is not a kind choice); defaultChoice is “stop”.

## Q24. Test Day

- `q24-s1`: Move your Nubbin’s paper three times, so Buttons can’t copy. · _When Buttons leans over, tap your Nubbin’s paper to move it._
- `q24-s2`: At break, help Buttons learn shapes. Then both Nubbins pass! · _Practise circles with Buttons on the chalkboard._

#### `sprout:q24-desks` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q24-desks)

**Mrs Inkwell**: Test day! Shapes, colours, and counting to five!  
_Your Nubbin sits at one desk. Buttons, Mr. Tweed’s Nubbin, at the other._  
_Buttons wears a tiny tweed waistcoat._  
**Mrs Inkwell**: Grown-ups may stay. Quietly. Mr. Tweed, that means you.  
_He zips his lips and puts the key in his pocket._  
_Your Nubbin draws a perfect circle._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.2, ask): circles  
> _Your Nubbin drew a circle. Which of these is a circle?_  
> - ✅ A round plate → Yes! A plate is round, like a circle.
> - A square window → A window has four corners. A plate is round, like a circle.
> _For grown-ups: Your child picked out a circle from everyday shapes._

_Buttons draws nothing. Buttons leans… slowly… to the left._  

**Choices**

- **Help your Nubbin cover its paper.**
    - Your Nubbin hides its paper. Buttons leans more. Buttons’ chair goes creak!
- **Let Buttons look. Sharing is nice.**
    - **Mrs Inkwell**: Eyes on your own paper, please, Buttons.
    - Buttons sits up and tries to whistle. Nubbins can’t whistle.
- **Look at Mr. Tweed.**
    - He looks at the ceiling. As if he’s never met Buttons.

#### `sprout:q24-shuffled` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q24-shuffled)

_Left. Right. Under the desk!_  
_Buttons follows the paper with Buttons’ whole body…_  
_And falls off its chair. Thump!_  
_Mr. Tweed falls over too._  
**Mrs Inkwell**: Buttons! Are you all right?  
_Buttons is fine. But Buttons’ paper is still empty._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, notice): doing your own work  
> **Mrs Inkwell**: In a test, we each do our own work.  
> **Mrs Inkwell**: If something is tricky, we can ask for help after.  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that in a test we each do our own work, and ask for help afterwards._


#### `sprout:q24-break` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q24-break)

_Buttons looks at its empty paper. Its ears droop._  
_Mr. Tweed’s ears droop too, somehow._  
**Mr. Tweed**: Hnn…  
**Mrs Inkwell**: Break time! You may help each other now. That’s learning.  
**You**: Right, Buttons. Circles first.  
_Buttons looks up at you. It has never drawn a circle._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.2, do): drawing a circle  
> _(Draw a circle in the air with your finger.)_  
> _Round and round, back to the start. A circle!_  
> Check: `edu:draw:shape:circle`
> _For grown-ups: Your child drew a circle in the air, going round and back to the start._


#### `sprout:q24-pass` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q24-pass)

**Mrs Inkwell**: Two passes! Well done!  
**Mrs Inkwell**: Buttons, that’s the roundest circle I’ve ever seen. Nearly.  
_Buttons holds its paper up to Mr. Tweed._  
_Both arms. Very proud. Ding!_  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): feeling proud  
> _How does Buttons feel?_  
> - ✅ Proud → Yes! Proud means happy about something you worked hard on.
> - Bored → Look at those arms up high! Buttons feels proud.
> _For grown-ups: Your child named the feeling of being proud after working hard._


#### `sprout:q24-complete` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q24-complete)

_Mr. Tweed lifts Buttons up high. Whee! He walks round the room._  
**Mrs Inkwell**: Mr. Tweed. Please put the Nubbin down.  
_He shakes everyone’s hand. Even Mrs Inkwell’s._  
**Mrs Inkwell**: I did not offer it.  
**Mrs Inkwell**: These two learn better together.  

> **Lesson** (art, do): colour patterns  
> **Mrs Inkwell**: Let’s colour our circles. Red, blue, red, blue…  
> _(What comes next? Red!)_  
> Check: `edu:colour:circles:red-blue`
> _For grown-ups: Your child coloured circles in a red, blue pattern._

_Buttons and your Nubbin draw circles on each other’s papers._  
_Mr. Tweed holds up one finger. Hopeful._  
**Mrs Inkwell**: …One paper. Just one.  
_He squeezes into a tiny desk. He draws a circle, tongue out._  
_It’s a wobbly circle. Buttons claps anyway._  

## Q25. The High Ledge

- `q25-s1`: Help Mr. Tweed down from the high ledge. · _Help him down, step by step. Tap Do it._
- `q25-s2`: Build a low diving board at the pond. · _Put the plank on the flat rock. Tap three times to hammer it._

#### `sprout:q25-ledge` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q25-ledge)

_Mr. Tweed is at the waterfall in a stripy swimsuit._  
_He points at the high ledge. He mimes a perfect dive._  
**Mr. Tweed**: Hup!  
_He climbs up, up, up. At the top, he looks down._  
_He freezes like a statue. A bird lands on his head._  
_Three little Nubbins squeeze past his ankles. Splash, splash, splash!_  
**Mr. Tweed**: Hnn.  
_He wiggles two fingers backwards. He wants to come down._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, ask): feeling scared is OK  
> _Mr. Tweed is scared. Is it OK to feel scared?_  
> - ✅ Yes, everyone feels scared sometimes → Yes. Everyone does. A friend nearby helps.
> - No → It is OK! Everyone feels scared sometimes. A friend nearby helps.
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that everyone feels scared sometimes, and a friend helps._


**Choices**

- **Talk him down, one step at a time.**
    - **You**: You’re OK! One foot back. Just one.
    - **Mr. Tweed**: Mm…
- **Ask a Swim Nubbin to show him.**
    - Your Nubbin looks at the water. Then at you. Yes!

#### `sprout:q25-down` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q25-down)

_Step by step, or splash by splash, he gets down._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, ask): high and low  
> _The ledge is high. The water is…_  
> - ✅ Low → Yes! High up top, low down below.
> - Higher → The water is down at the bottom. It is low!
> _For grown-ups: Your child used the words high and low._

_The last bit is mostly sliding. Wheee!_  
_He lands in the shallow water. Splash!_  
_The bird is still on his head._  
**Mr. Tweed**: Mm!  

#### `sprout:q25-board` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q25-board)

_Mr. Tweed dries off with a tiny towel._  
_He looks at the high ledge. Then at the low rock by the pond._  
_He mimes: saw, hammer, boing. A low diving board!_  

> **Lesson** (health, notice): water safety  
> _Before jumping in, a grown-up checks the water._  
> _Is it deep enough? Is it safe? Then splash!_  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that a grown-up checks the water before anyone jumps in._

**Mr. Tweed**: Small.  
_Your Nubbin bounces on the spot. It wants to go first._  
_Mr. Tweed pulls a plank out of his towel!_  

#### `sprout:q25-complete` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q25-complete)

_The diving board is low and bouncy. A bit crooked. Perfect!_  
_Your Nubbin goes first. Bounce, bounce, SPLASH!_  
_Mr. Tweed walks to the end. He looks down. He freezes._  
_The water is just below his toes._  
_You wait. Your Nubbin waits. The bird comes back._  
_He holds his nose, shuts his eyes… and steps off. Plop!_  
_The teeny bear on your shoulder covers its eyes. Then it peeks._  
**Mr. Tweed**: Ha!  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.A.1, do): count to 10  
> _(Count his jumps! Clap for each one.)_  
> _One, two, three… all the way to ten!_  
> Check: `edu:count:jumps:10`
> _For grown-ups: Your child counted ten jumps, clapping for each one._

_He climbs out and does it again, and again, and again!_  

## Q26. The Bench Sandwich

- `q26-s1`: Hand Mr. Tweed the right things, in the right order. · _Watch his hands. Sawing means bread. Shaking means pepper._
- `q26-s2`: Spread the picnic blanket and share the sandwich with every Nubbin. · _Put the blanket on the grass, then feed each Nubbin._

#### `sprout:q26-bench` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q26-bench)

_Mr. Tweed sits on his bench by the pond._  
_He opens his coat. Pop! Out comes lunch!_  

> **Lesson** (health, notice): washing hands before eating  
> _Wait! Before lunch, we wash our hands._  
> _(Pretend to wash: soap, rub, rinse, dry!)_  
> _For grown-ups: Your child heard that we wash our hands before we eat._

_Bread from his sleeve. Lettuce from his pocket._  
_Jam from near his knee. A tomato from under his hat!_  
_Butter from his top pocket. Of course._  
_He spreads jam… on the lettuce._  
**Mr. Tweed**: Hm?  
_Something has gone wrong. He doesn’t know what._  

**Choices**

- **Offer to pass him things.**
    - He nods and holds out his hand, like a doctor.
- **Try the jam lettuce.**
    - Crunchy, sweet… and wrong!
    - Mr. Tweed nods. He knew it.
- **Let your Nubbin have the jam.**
    - Your Nubbin puts its whole face in the jar.
    - Mr. Tweed finds another jar near his other knee.

#### `sprout:q26-sandwich` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q26-sandwich)

_Bread. Butter. Lettuce. Tomato. Pepper. Bread on top._  
_Pat, pat. Ding! A sandwich!_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, ask): first, next, last  
> _What goes on first, and what goes on last?_  
> - ✅ Bread first, bread last → Yes! Bread first, fillings next, bread last.
> - Pepper first → Pepper would fall off! Bread first, fillings next, bread last.
> _For grown-ups: Your child put the steps of making a sandwich in order._

_Mr. Tweed holds it up, like treasure._  
_A duck lands on the bench. It looks at the sandwich._  
_The duck flies off with the jam lettuce. Fair enough._  
**Mr. Tweed**: Ahh.  

#### `sprout:q26-share` · [main](../../../fun/bean.md#q26-share)

_He’s about to bite. Then he sees: you have no sandwich._  
_Your Nubbin has none. Wild Nubbins behind the bench have none._  
_Mr. Tweed sighs, all the way from his boots._  
_He takes out a ruler. Crunch! He cuts the sandwich into squares._  

> **Lesson** (maths, 1.G.A.3, ask): quarters  
> _One cut down, one cut across. How many squares?_  
> _(Draw it in the air: down… across!)_  
> - ✅ Four → Yes! Four squares the same size. Each one is a quarter.
> - Two → Let’s count: one, two at the top. Three, four at the bottom. Four!
> _For grown-ups: Your child found that two cuts make four equal squares, called quarters._

**Mr. Tweed**: Picnic.
