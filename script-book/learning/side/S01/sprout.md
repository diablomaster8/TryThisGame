# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S1: The Wardens’ Walk

## S1. The Wardens’ Walk

- `s1-step1`: Walk with Moss to the stream lantern. Go slowly! · _Stay right beside Moss. If you go too fast, he stops and waits._
- `s1-step-boats`: Float a leaf-boat down the stream to the pond. · _Tap the water behind the boat. Keep it away from the reeds. You can always try again._
- `s1-step2`: Walk with Moss to the lantern by the old wall. · _The old wall is on the north side of the Grove. Go on a quiet evening._
- `s1-step3`: Walk with Moss to his own lantern, by the pond. · _Go in the evening. Moss likes the evening light._
- `s1-step4`: Moss will tell you about the last lantern later. · _Keep playing the story. Moss will come and find you._

#### `sprout:s1-ask` · [main](../../../side/S01.md#s1-ask)

_Night. Three lanterns glow. One post is dark._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.1, do): count to 4; 3 and 1 more make 4  
> _(Count the posts together: one, two, three, four.)_  
> **Moss**: Three bright ones, and one dark one. That makes four.  
> Check: `edu:count:lantern-posts:4`
> _For grown-ups: Counted four lantern posts and saw that three and one more make four._

_Moss sits by your boot. He looks at the dark post._  
**Moss**: Not tonight. Ask me something else.  
**Moss**: Each lantern post keeps a little memory.  
**Moss**: Four posts. Four memories. Shall we walk to them?  
**Moss**: Slowly, mind. I am older than the trees.  

**Choices**

- **Walk slowly beside him.**
    - Step… step… step. Slow and steady.
    - **Moss**: Good. You didn’t huff. Hazel huffed for years.
- **Offer to carry him.**
    - **Moss**: No, thank you. I have legs.
    - **Moss**: Short ones. Old ones. But mine.
- **Ask which lantern comes first.**
    - **Moss**: Briar’s, by the stream. She hated waiting!

#### `sprout:s1-briar` · [main](../../../side/S01.md#s1-briar)

_The stream lantern hisses in the damp. Fssss._  
**Moss**: This was Briar’s post. Briar loved to laugh.  
**Moss**: She laughed at frogs. She laughed at rain. She laughed at me!  
**Moss**: When Briar was very, very old, she died.  
**Moss**: That means her body stopped working. It won’t start again.  
_The light turns gold. A happy voice whispers._  
**Briar**: If I’m not back by spring, plant something nice!  
**Moss**: A yellow flower grew where she sat. The very first one.  
**Moss**: When I see it, I remember her laugh.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, notice): remembering someone we love who has died  
> **Moss**: We keep remembering the ones we love. With flowers. With stories.  
> _(Ask: what makes you laugh, like Briar? Wait for an answer.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Heard plainly what dying means, and that flowers and stories help us remember people we love._


#### `sprout:s1-boats-ask` · [main](../../../side/S01.md#s1-boats-ask)

**Moss**: Briar had a game. Leaf-boats!  
**Moss**: A leaf, a candle a grown-up lights, and off down the stream.  
**Moss**: If yours reaches the pond, you must laugh out loud.  
**Moss**: That’s the rule. Briar made it up.  
_Moss folds a big green leaf. Fold, fold, fold._  
**Moss**: Mine always tips over. Every single year.  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, do): wind pushes a sail  
> **Moss**: A sail catches the wind. The wind pushes the boat along.  
> _(Blow like the wind together. Whoosh!)_  
> Check: `edu:blow:wind:sail`
> _For grown-ups: Learned that wind pushes on a sail and moves a boat, and blew like the wind._


**Choices**

- **Make yours wide and flat.**
    - **Moss**: Briar’s way! Flat as a pancake.
- **Give yours a sail.**
    - **Moss**: A sail! Nobody ever thought of a sail!
    - **Moss**: Briar would have loved that.

#### `sprout:s1-boats` · [main](../../../side/S01.md#s1-boats)

_Splash! Moss’s boat tips over at once._  
**Moss**: Every year.  
_Your boat bobs past the stepping stones._  
_It spins at the reeds. Round and round!_  
_Then it slides onto the pond. The candle is still lit!_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, ask): first, next, last  
> **Moss**: Tell me the boat’s trip. Where did it go first?  
> - ✅ Past the stepping stones → Yes! First the stones. Next the reeds. Last, the pond!
> - Round the reeds → That came next. First it passed the stones. Then the reeds. Then the pond.
> - Onto the pond → That came last! First the stones, next the reeds, last the pond.
> _For grown-ups: Retold a short journey in order using first, next and last._

_Your Nubbin sits down, plop, in surprise._  
**Moss**: Well? Rules are rules!  
_(Laugh out loud together. Ha ha ha!)_  
_Your laugh rings out across the water._  
**Moss**: There. That’s Briar’s sound. I missed it.  
_Moss’s boat floats back to the bank, upside down._  
**Moss**: Maybe this was Briar’s joke. My boat never gets there.  
**Moss**: She folded my first boat. I still fold it her way. Hmm.  
_Moss looks at his boat. He does not trust it at all._  

#### `sprout:s1-wall-ask` · [main](../../../side/S01.md#s1-wall-ask)

_A few evenings later, Moss waits at the gate._  
**Moss**: The old wall next. Hush’s post.  
_Byte leans on the wall with a notebook._  
**Byte**: Is he really talking? Ask him something only he knows!  
**Moss**: Tell Byte his shoelace is undone.  
_Byte looks down. It is! Byte goes very quiet._  
**Moss**: Hush was the quiet one. Hush was a good listener.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, do): quiet, careful listening  
> **Moss**: Let’s listen like Hush. Shh.  
> _(Sit very still. Can you name one sound you hear?)_  
> Check: `edu:listen:sounds:quiet`
> _For grown-ups: Practised sitting still and listening carefully, then named a sound they heard._

**You**: What did Hush listen to?  
**Moss**: Everything. Come on. Mind the step.  

#### `sprout:s1-hush` · [main](../../../side/S01.md#s1-hush)

_The lantern by the old wall burns low and quiet._  
**Moss**: After the long white winter, some Nubbins couldn’t move.  
**Moss**: They sat very still, just where they were put.  
**Moss**: Everyone else was busy. Hush was not.  
**Moss**: Hush sat with them. Every single day.  
**Moss**: She didn’t try to fix them. She kept them company.  
**Moss**: So they were never on their own.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): helping a sad friend by staying with them  
> **Moss**: If a friend feels sad, what could you do?  
> - ✅ Sit beside them and stay → Yes. That’s what Hush did. Staying close helps.
> - Leave them on their own → They might feel lonely then. Hush would sit beside them. Staying helps.
> - Tell them to stop being sad → Sad feelings don’t stop when we’re told. Sitting with them helps more.
> _For grown-ups: Thought about how to help a sad friend: staying close and keeping them company._

**Moss**: Hush said that was a kind of keeping.  

#### `sprout:s1-pond-ask` · [main](../../../side/S01.md#s1-pond-ask)

_Moss is by the pond, at his own post._  
**Moss**: My lantern. I like this one best.  
**Moss**: Sit beside me. Closer. There.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, do): position words: beside, on, behind  
> _(Where are you sitting? Beside Moss!)_  
> **Moss**: Beside me. Not on me. Not behind me. Beside.  
> Check: `edu:sit:beside:moss`
> _For grown-ups: Used the position words beside, on and behind._

**Moss**: That’s where Hazel sat. Long, long ago.  

**Choices**

- **Sit where Hazel sat.**
    - The grass is flat there. She sat here lots.
    - **Moss**: Just right.
- **Ask why he chose her.**
    - **Moss**: Patience! I’m getting to it.

#### `sprout:s1-hazel` · [main](../../../side/S01.md#s1-hazel)

**Moss**: Hazel came here young. She was in a big hurry.  
**Moss**: She wanted the Nubbins to love her by Tuesday!  
_The light turns gold. It’s a memory. Snow falls._  
_A young Hazel sits by the frozen pond. Her coat is too big!_  

> **Lesson** (science, 2-ESS2-3, notice): water freezes into ice and melts back  
> **Moss**: In winter, the pond freezes. Cold turns water into ice.  
> **Moss**: When it warms up, the ice melts back into water. Splash!  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that cold turns water into ice, and warmth melts it back into water._

**Young Hazel**: I don’t know what to do. Do you?  
**Moss**: I sat beside her all winter. I said nothing.  
**Moss**: She wriggled and fidgeted. But she never asked me to talk.  
**Moss**: When the ice melted, I looked up and said, “You’ll do.”  
**Moss**: She laughed so hard, she fell in the pond!  
**Moss**: Sit next to them and wait. That’s Hazel’s third rule.  

#### `sprout:s1-wait` · [main](../../../side/S01.md#s1-wait)

_You look at the last post, by the long grass._  
**Moss**: Not that one. Not yet.  
_Down the lane, children skip and sing._  
_“Cinder held them close and tight, / kept them warm one winter night;”_  
_“when the morning came to call, / they did not move at all.”_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): hearing rhymes  
> _(Say the rhyme again together.)_  
> **Moss**: Which word sounds like “tight”?  
> - ✅ night → Yes! Tight, night. They rhyme!
> - cold → Cold and tight don’t sound alike. Tight… night! Those rhyme.
> - snow → Snow rhymes with go. Tight rhymes with night!
> _For grown-ups: Listened to a skipping rhyme and picked out a rhyming pair (tight, night)._

**Moss**: They have the words right. But they have her wrong.  
**Moss**: I’ll tell you about Cinder. Another day.  
**Moss**: Now: feed them, and off to bed.  

#### `sprout:s1-cinder` · [main](../../../side/S01.md#s1-cinder)

_Winter now. Frost creeps out of the long grass._  
_Moss is at your door. He looks very faint._  
**Moss**: All four are home. You may carry me now. What an honour!  
_You carry him gently. He is light as a leaf._  
**Moss**: Let me tell you about Cinder’s last warm day.  
**Moss**: It was so hot! The Nubbins lay flat in the sun.  
**Moss**: Cinder carried them into the shade. One at a time.  

> **Lesson** (science, K-PS3-1, notice): shade is cooler than sunshine  
> **Moss**: The Sun warms the ground. Shade blocks some of the Sun.  
> **Moss**: So shade is cooler than sunshine. Cinder knew that.  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that sunlight warms the ground and shade is cooler._

**Cinder**: Out of the sun, you silly things! I’ve got you!  
**Moss**: That night, snow came. In summer! It did not stop.  
**Moss**: Cinder was scared. She hugged her Nubbins close.  
**Moss**: She would not let go. Not even to let them play.  
**Moss**: The Nubbins could not run. They were not happy.  
**Moss**: Cinder loved them. But her hug was too tight.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): gentle hugs, and asking first  
> **Moss**: How should we hug a small friend?  
> - ✅ Gently, and only if they want a hug → Yes. Gentle hugs, and we ask first.
> - As tight as we can → Tight hugs can hurt small friends. Gentle is best. And we ask first.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about gentle hugs and asking first, through Cinder’s love that held too tight._


#### `sprout:s1-complete` · [main](../../../side/S01.md#s1-complete)

**Moss**: There. Now two of us remember her warm.  
_Moss pushes something to your boot. An acorn on a string!_  
**Moss**: Hazel gave me that, long ago. Now it’s yours.  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): an acorn is a seed that grows into an oak tree  
> **Moss**: An acorn is a seed. Plant it, and wait…  
> **Moss**: …a long, long time. It grows into a big oak tree!  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that an acorn is the seed of an oak tree._

_On the post hangs a soft hat with a tiny lantern._  
_The note says: “I MADE YOU THIS! —SPARKS”_  
_That night, you draw the four posts in your book._  
_Moss falls asleep on your windowsill. Shh._  

**Notes:** Every scene keeps Moss’s four memories. Hush’s fading is left out of the sprout version of s1-hush; it keeps the heart (keeping company) without a second death in one quest.
