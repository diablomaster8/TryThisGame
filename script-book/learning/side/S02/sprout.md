# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S2: Wisp

## S2. Wisp

- `s2-step1`: Sit up at night by Sable’s fruit. Keep very still. · _Leave fruit by the long grass. Then wait nearby, still as a post._
- `s2-step2`: Watch out for Wisp. When the big rain comes, find where he sleeps. · _This happens later, in the rain. Warm things went somewhere warm._
- `s2-step3`: Keep making friends with Sable. Wisp is never far away. · _Later, when Sable leans on you, visit Luna’s garden at night._

#### `sprout:s2-fruit` · [main](../../../side/S02.md#s2-fruit)

_Morning. The fruit for Sable is gone._  
_But Sable looks thin. She didn’t eat it._  
_Crunch! There is frost on the grass round her. In summer!_  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS2-1, ask): frost comes when it is very cold  
> **Luna**: Frost is tiny bits of ice. When do we see frost?  
> - ✅ When it is very cold → Yes! Frost comes when it’s very cold. That’s why summer frost is strange.
> - On a hot summer day → Frost on a hot day? Plop! It would melt like ice cream. Summer is warm, so frost should melt. Frost comes when it’s very cold. That’s why this is strange!
> _For grown-ups: Learned that frost is ice that comes in cold weather, so frost in summer is strange._

**Luna**: Something else came in the dark. It took the fruit.  
**You**: Another Nubbin?  
**Luna**: A grey one. Even thinner than Sable.  
**Luna**: Sit up tonight. Keep still. Still as a post.  
**Luna**: Let’s find out what he wants.  

#### `sprout:s2-thief` · [main](../../../side/S02.md#s2-thief)

_Night. Sable creeps out. She takes one bite._  
_Then a thin grey Nubbin comes. One ear is torn._  
_He pushes Sable aside. He pulls the fruit away._  
_The fruit is bigger than his head._  
_He drags it anyway. Bump, bump, bump._  
_He sees your lantern. He presses his cheek on the warm glass._  
_He is shaking. Shiver, shiver._  
**Wisp**: Hsss! Hers! Not yours!  
_Then, very small, a second voice._  
**Wisp**: …she ate. she ate today. good.  

> **Lesson** (music, ELOF: Language and Communication, do): loud and quiet voices  
> _(Hiss like Wisp: hsss! Now whisper softly: she ate.)_  
> **Luna**: One loud voice. One quiet voice.  
> Check: `edu:voice:loud-quiet`
> _For grown-ups: Played with a loud voice and a quiet voice._

_Whoosh. He’s gone._  
**Luna**: Let’s call him Wisp.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, do): first sound w  
> _(Say it together: w-w-Wisp.)_  
> **Luna**: Wisp starts with a “w” sound. Like wind and water!  
> Check: `edu:say:first-sound:w`
> _For grown-ups: Heard and said the first sound “w” in Wisp, wind and water._


#### `sprout:s2-missing` · [main](../../../side/S02.md#s2-missing)

_Next day, more things go missing. Warm things._  
**Fern**: My left glove is gone! My right hand is warm. My left is cold!  
_Fern waves her cold left hand. Brrr. Achoo!_  
_Your sock. A candle. The tea cosy. Gran Hazel’s warm egg stone._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 5  
> _(Count the missing things on your fingers.)_  
> **Fern**: Glove, sock, candle, tea cosy, stone. Five!  
> Check: `edu:count:missing-things:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five missing things on their fingers._

**Clover**: He’s cold, that’s all. Can we feed him too?  
**Luna**: He hisses at Sable. And she listens to him.  
**Clover**: Somebody taught him to be scared.  
_Up in the fruit tree, Wisp watches you._  

**Choices**

- **Put down two piles of fruit. One each.**
    - Wisp eats his pile. Then most of hers!
    - **Clover**: Not quite fair. But he ate.
- **Clap and shoo him away.**
    - Clap! Wisp runs. Sable jumps too.
    - **Luna**: Claps scare small things. Use a soft voice next time.
- **Sit down and wait.**
    - You sit. Wisp hisses, and then he stops.
    - Sable eats. She watches you both.
    - **Luna**: Waiting works on him too!

#### `sprout:s2-rain` · [main](../../../side/S02.md#s2-rain)

_It rains all day and all night. Pitter-patter._  
_The stream is big and brown and loud._  
_Behind the cottage is a soggy nest._  
_Fern’s glove. The sock. The tea cosy. All wet._  
_Wisp sits on a log, shaking. He holds Gran Hazel’s egg stone._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): shivering helps a cold body warm up  
> **Clover**: Bodies shake when they’re cold. Shivering helps them warm up.  
> _(Shiver like Wisp. Brrr!)_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that shivering is the body’s way of warming up when it is cold._

**Wisp**: mine. warm. mine… was warm.  
**Clover**: The bathhouse is warm. Please, let’s help him.  
**Luna**: He might wriggle. Hold him gently anyway.  

**Choices**

- **Pick him up. Hold on gently.**
    - He wriggles. Then he stops.
    - In the warm bathhouse, he bites your hand. Ouch!
    - **Wisp**: Hsss. Hands!
    - **Clover**: He’s scared of hands. But look. He’s warm now.
    - He is warm. But he is still shaking.
    - Clover gives him a pear. Munch, munch, gulp.
- **Get a blanket. Let Clover lift him.**
    - Clover wraps him up like a loaf of bread.
    - He bites her, right through the blanket. Ouch!
    - **Clover**: Ow. That’s fair. He’s scared.
    - He is warm now. But he is still shaking.
    - Clover gives him a pear. Munch, munch, gulp.

#### `sprout:s2-number` · [main](../../../side/S02.md#s2-number)

**Clover**: Warm. Dry. Fed. And still shaking.  
**Luna**: So it was never the cold.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): naming a feeling: scared  
> **Luna**: Wisp is warm now. But he still shakes. How does he feel?  
> - ✅ Scared → Yes. Wisp is scared. Being scared can make us shake too.
> - Cold → Cold? He’s wrapped up as warm as a bun! He was cold before. He’s warm now. So it’s fear. Wisp feels scared.
> - Sleepy → Sleepy? A yawn would wobble like jelly. Hee, hee! Sleepy Nubbins go floppy, not shaky. Wisp feels scared.
> _For grown-ups: Named a feeling (scared) from clues, and learned that fear can make us shake._

_Rain drums on the roof. Drip, drip, drip._  
_On Wisp’s side is an old grey number. Painted on._  
**Luna**: I’ve seen paint like that before. I’ll ask around.  
_Outside, Sable sniffs your hands. She doesn’t run!_  

#### `sprout:s2-night` · [main](../../../side/S02.md#s2-night)

_Night in Luna’s garden. Pop! The moonflowers open._  
_Wisp sits close to Sable. He whispers to her._  
**Wisp**: hands are scary. come away. come to the dark.  
_Sable looks at Wisp. Then at your window._  
**Luna**: Wisp wants Sable to leave the Grove.  
_Clover comes in her dressing gown, with fruit._  
**Clover**: Wisp is cold and scared. Kind people feed scared Nubbins.  
**Luna**: I care about Sable. I want Sable safe.  
**Clover**: And Wisp has nobody at all.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, notice): friends can disagree and both be kind  
> _Luna and Clover want different things._  
> _They are both being kind. Friends can disagree and stay friends._  
> _For grown-ups: Saw two friends disagree kindly, each caring about someone different._

_Luna and Clover both look at you._  

**Choices**

- **Keep feeding him. Every single day.**
    - Each morning, two piles of fruit. One for Sable. One for Wisp.
    - On the fourth morning, Wisp eats facing you.
    - **Wisp**: hands… maybe not hers. maybe not.
    - Sable sees. She comes one step closer.
    - **Luna**: I hope you’re right.
- **Let Sable decide.**
    - You put down one pile of fruit. You step back.
    - Sable pushes half the pile to Wisp. She shares!
    - **Wisp**: …she gave. hers. she gave.
    - **Luna**: That’s a kinder answer than mine.
- _(left out in this band; plays as `spared`)_

#### `sprout:s2-complete` · [main](../../../side/S02.md#s2-complete)

_Morning. Crunch! Two little frost circles by the long grass._  
_Two grey Nubbins slept there. Wisp has the egg stone._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.2, do): circle shape  
> _(Draw a circle in the air with your finger.)_  
> **Moss**: Round and round and back again. A circle!  
> Check: `edu:draw:shape:circle`
> _For grown-ups: Drew a circle in the air and named the shape._

**Moss**: He’ll never give that stone back.  
**Moss**: Feed them before you feed yourself. Hazel’s first rule.  
**Moss**: Hazel never said, feed only the nice ones.  
**Moss**: I asked her. Twice.  
**Moss**: Grey ones remember who fed them.  

#### `sprout:s2-complete-sable` · [main](../../../side/S02.md#s2-complete-sable)

_Variant of `s2-complete`._

_Morning. Two circles of frost, side by side._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, do): position words: side by side  
> _(Put your two hands side by side, like the circles.)_  
> **Moss**: Side by side. Close, but not on top.  
> Check: `edu:place:hands:side-by-side`
> _For grown-ups: Placed their hands side by side to show the position word._

_Wisp stays at the edge. He holds the egg stone._  
_He hasn’t come in. He hasn’t gone away, either._  
**Moss**: Sable chose. That was hers to choose.  
**Moss**: Feed them before you feed yourself. Hazel’s first rule.  
**Moss**: Grey ones remember who fed them.  

#### `sprout:s2-complete-driven` · [main](../../../side/S02.md#s2-complete-driven)

_Variant of `s2-complete`._

_Morning. One circle of frost by the long grass._  
_Wisp has gone away, through the hedge._  
_A sock hangs on a thorn._  
_Sable sits by the gap for a long time._  
_Then she comes back. She eats all her fruit._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): missing someone is a sad feeling, and that is all right  
> _Sable misses Wisp. Missing someone feels sad._  
> _It’s all right to feel sad. Sitting together helps._  
> _For grown-ups: Named missing someone as a sad feeling and heard that sitting together helps._

**Luna**: She’s eating. That’s good. I still feel sad.  
**Clover**: I was cross yesterday. I’m not cross now.  
**Clover**: I hung a blanket on the hedge. Just in case Wisp is cold.  
**Moss**: It was a good idea. It still feels sad.  
**Moss**: Both can be true.  

**Notes:** Sprout leaves out “drive him off” in s2-night (defaultChoice spared). The s2-complete-driven variant is still written, gently, in case a save carries wisp = driven.
