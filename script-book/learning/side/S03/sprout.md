# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S3: The Cycle

## S3. Sun and Moon

- `s3-step1`: Ask a Sun friend and a Moon friend about the big race. · _Sun: Fern, Marina, Sparks, Clover, Pixel. Moon: Luna, Ember, Byte, Dusty, Nova. Ask one from each side._
- `s3-step2`: Help Sparks and Byte make the old race pictures. · _Go to Sparks’s workshop. Rock the tray when Byte counts._
- `s3-step3`: Show the pictures to Fern and to Ember. · _Fern is in her orchard. Ember is by the race fence in the evening._
- `s3-step4`: Call everyone to a big meeting at the pond. · _Later on, tap the pond and choose Call a Moot. All ten friends will come._

#### `sprout:s3-stands` · [main](../../../side/S03.md#s3-stands)

_The race stands. Sun friends sit on the left._  
_Moon friends sit on the right. An empty bench sits in the middle._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, ask): left, right and middle  
> _(Point left. Point right. Now point to the middle.)_  
> **Fern**: So where is the empty bench?  
> - ✅ In the middle → Yes! Sun on the left, Moon on the right, the gap in the middle.
> - On the left → The left is full of Sun friends. The empty bench is in the middle.
> - On the right → The right is full of Moon friends. The empty bench is in the middle.
> _For grown-ups: Used left, right and middle to find a place._

**Fern**: Don’t sit there! That’s the gap.  
**Fern**: Your Gran Hazel sat in the gap. Just to be cheeky.  
_Two big tea pots. One has a sun. One has a moon._  
_Each side says the other pot’s tea is worse._  
_A Nubbin sips from the sun pot. Then the moon pot._  
_It can’t tell. It sips both again, to be sure._  
**Sparks**: Long ago, at the big race, Fern’s Nubbin Bramble fell!  
**Sparks**: Sun says one thing. Moon says another. Now nobody races together!  
**Sparks**: Oops. I didn’t say that. That was the wind.  
_High up, Corvin watches the people, not the race._  

**Choices**

- **Sit in the gap, like Hazel.**
    - You sit in the middle. Everybody turns to look!
    - **Fern**: Oh, you’re Hazel’s, all right.
- **Sit with the Sun side.**
    - **Sparks**: Yes! Sun gets the new Keeper!
- **Sit with the Moon side.**
    - **Byte**: Welcome. Our biscuits are better. I checked.
    - **Fern**: Oh no! Lovely to see you anyway.

#### `sprout:s3-two-stories` · [main](../../../side/S03.md#s3-two-stories)

_You hear two stories. They are not the same._  
_Sun says: Ember’s Nubbin Ash bumped Fern’s Nubbin Bramble at the bend._  
_Moon says: the flag was in the wrong place._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): listening to both sides  
> **Byte**: Two sides tell it two ways. What should we do?  
> - ✅ Listen to both sides → Yes. Hear everyone first. Then look for clues.
> - Only listen to one side → Then we’d miss half the story. Let’s listen to both sides.
> _For grown-ups: Learned to listen to both sides before deciding what happened._

**Byte**: I made a chart. Sun is yellow. Moon is blue.  
**Ember**: After that day, nobody would race with Ash.  
**Ember**: Only Corvin. He said he’d make Ash a champion.  
_Nobody talks about Bramble. Where did she go?_  

#### `sprout:s3-plates` · [main](../../../side/S03.md#s3-plates)

_Sparks is at your gate. She is fizzing!_  
**Sparks**: I have pictures from that race. I never looked at them!  
**Sparks**: I was scared of what they might show.  
**Sparks**: But my timer is broken. I need help.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): asking for help kindly  
> **Sparks**: Somebody has a timer. How do I ask for help?  
> - ✅ “Please will you help me?” → Please. Right. I can say please. Probably.
> - “Give me your timer!” → They’d say no! Asking kindly works better. “Please will you help me?”
> _For grown-ups: Practised asking for help kindly, with please._

_She points up the lane, at Byte’s house._  
**Sparks**: Him!  

**Choices**

- **Fetch Byte. Say it’s for science.**
    - **Byte**: Science? I’ll bring three timers!
- **Fetch Byte. Say Sparks asked nicely.**
    - **Byte**: She did not.
    - You say, “No, she didn’t.”
    - **Byte**: I’ll come anyway.

#### `sprout:s3-flag` · [main](../../../side/S03.md#s3-flag)

_Sparks’s darkroom is red and dark. It smells of vinegar._  
**Byte**: Rock the tray on the count. One, and two…  

> **Lesson** (music, do): keeping a steady beat  
> _(Clap with Byte: one, and two, and one, and two.)_  
> **Byte**: Steady. Not fast, not slow. Like a clock.  
> Check: `edu:clap:beat:steady`
> _For grown-ups: Clapped a steady beat with Byte’s count._

**Sparks**: I rock on FEELING, Byte!  
_Slosh! The tray tips. Byte’s sleeve is very wet._  
**Byte**: That was not a rock. That was a wave.  
_The first picture appears. It’s… a giant thumb._  
**Sparks**: It’s a GOOD thumb!  
_The next picture: the cliff bend, before the race._  
_A man in a grey coat is moving the flag!_  
**Byte**: He moved it two posts nearer the edge.  
**Byte**: The flag said “go wide”. So Ash went wide, into Bramble.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, ask): near and far  
> **Byte**: Where did the flag go? Nearer the edge, or further away?  
> - ✅ Nearer the edge → Yes. Nearer the edge. That’s why the Nubbins went wide.
> - Further away → Look again. Old hole here. New hole there, nearer the edge.
> _For grown-ups: Compared positions using nearer and further._

**Sparks**: Nobody from Sun or Moon moved it. It was a race helper!  
**Sparks**: We fought about the wrong thing. For years!  
**Byte**: There’s a thumb on the last picture too. In the corner.  
**Sparks**: That’s how I sign my pictures!  

#### `sprout:s3-courier` · [main](../../../side/S03.md#s3-courier)

_Sparks wraps the pictures in a sun tea towel._  
_Byte wraps a moon tea towel round that._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds s and m  
> _(Say them slowly: Sun… Moon.)_  
> **Byte**: Which one starts with a “mmm” sound?  
> - ✅ Moon → Yes! Mmm-Moon. And Sun starts with “sss”.
> - Sun → Sun starts with “sss”, like a snake. Mmm-Moon starts with “mmm”.
> _For grown-ups: Heard the first sounds in Sun and Moon._

**Sparks**: You show them. Fern and Ember both like you.  
**Byte**: A helper from neither side. Good.  

**Choices**

- **Show them both at the same time.**
    - It takes a whole week to get them together!
    - **Fern**: Sneaky! Hazel did that to me once.
- **Show them one at a time.**
    - Fern first. Then Ember.
    - Each one asks: has the other one seen it?
    - By evening, both are at the same fence. They say it’s an accident.

#### `sprout:s3-truce` · [main](../../../side/S03.md#s3-truce)

_Fern looks at the pictures. Ember looks too._  
**Ember**: It wasn’t Ash. It wasn’t Fern. It was the flag.  
**Fern**: I was cross with you for years. Over a flag.  
**Fern**: I’m sorry, Ember.  
**Ember**: I’m sorry too.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): saying sorry  
> _Fern was cross with Ember for a long time._  
> _What can we say when we’ve been unkind?_  
> - ✅ “I’m sorry.” → Yes. Saying sorry is brave. It helps friends start again.
> - “It’s your fault.” → That keeps the cross feeling going. “I’m sorry” helps friends start again.
> _For grown-ups: Practised saying sorry, and saw how it helps friends make up._

**Fern**: No, I’m sorrier.  
**Ember**: No, I’M sorrier.  
_It is their best argument in years._  
**Ember**: Let’s have a big meeting. Everyone. Sun and Moon.  
**Fern**: And just one tea pot.  
**Ember**: One tea pot.  

#### `sprout:s3-invitations` · [main](../../../side/S03.md#s3-invitations)

_Pixel makes the invitations. Gold ink or silver?_  
**Pixel**: Gold is for Sun. Silver is for Moon.  
**Clover**: What about green? Nobody is green.  
**Pixel**: Clover, that’s brilliant! Green!  

> **Lesson** (art, do): blue and yellow paint make green  
> **Pixel**: Mix blue paint and yellow paint, and you get green!  
> _(Find something green near you. Point to it!)_  
> Check: `edu:find:colour:green`
> _For grown-ups: Learned that blue and yellow paint mix to make green, and found something green._

**Pixel**: Green is for everybody. Sun and Moon.  
_Dusty digs a hole for the one tea pot. Nobody asked._  
**Nova**: Am I Sun or Moon? I’ve decided I’m weather.  
_Ember says she’s coming. That’s Ember for yes._  

#### `sprout:s3-moot` · [main](../../../side/S03.md#s3-moot)

_Evening at the pond. Ten friends. One tea pot._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 10; 5 and 5 make 10  
> _(Count the friends: five Sun, five Moon.)_  
> _(Five and five make ten!)_  
> Check: `edu:count:keepers:10`
> _For grown-ups: Counted ten friends as two groups of five._

_Dusty dug a hole. The pot sits in it. Dusty is proud._  
**Gran Hazel**: A Moot! That’s an old word for a big meeting.  
**Gran Hazel**: I haven’t been to one in years!  
_You put the pictures on the bench in the middle._  
_Gran Hazel sits right in the middle. She smiles at both sides._  
_Everyone shuffles in close to look. Now there is no gap!_  
**Fern**: She did it again! Still cheeky, after all these years.  
**Sparks**: It was the flag. I had the pictures all along.  
**Fern**: Bramble fell because a man moved a flag. Not because of us.  
**Byte**: I tasted both teas. Same tea. It always was.  
**Ember**: So. A race for everyone. And I check the flags.  
**Fern**: We check the flags.  
**Byte**: I’ll check the checking.  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, notice): a fair rule is the same for everyone  
> _A rule is fair when it’s the same for everyone._  
> _Everybody checks the flags. That keeps the race fair._  
> _For grown-ups: Talked about fair rules that are the same for everyone._

**Gran Hazel**: Now, who bangs the spoon? Someone must keep the Moot.  

**Choices**

- **Bang the spoon on the pot.**
    - Bong! Everybody stops talking.
    - Now one person talks at a time. Taking turns!
    - **Byte**: That spoon is now a very important spoon.
    - **Pixel**: Ribbons for every racer! Gold, silver and green!
    - **Gran Hazel**: Moot-Keeper. It suits you.
- **Let Fern and Ember share the spoon.**
    - Fern and Ember hold the spoon together. Bong! They both miss!
    - Everybody cheers.
    - **Pixel**: Ribbons for every racer! Gold, silver and green. Mine came out wonky.
    - **Gran Hazel**: You’re still the Moot-Keeper, love.

**Notes:** Bramble’s fall is “fell at the cliff bend and got lost” (S8 reunites them). The steward is “a man in a grey coat” / “a race helper”. The urns are “tea pots” for read-aloud. s3-step4 has no done scene in the source.
