# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S4: The Wellspring

## S4. The Waterfall

- `s4-step1`: Look at Gran’s white line by the waterfall. · _Go past the pond to the falls. Tap the white mark on the rock._
- `s4-step2`: Send a swimmer up the stream to look. · _Choose Swim at the top of the pool. Marina can teach a Nubbin first._
- `s4-step3`: Walk the path behind the waterfall with Marina. · _Meet Marina in the evening. Stay close behind her._
- `s4-step4`: Open the water gate at night. · _Go with Marina and a strong Nubbin. The gate sticks. Lift and push!_

#### `sprout:s4-pools` · [main](../../../side/S04.md#s4-pools)

**Marina**: Come and see something.  
_Marina’s rock pools step down like bowls._  
_Half of them are dry. No water at all._  
**Marina**: The stream fills my pools. This summer it gives less.  
**Marina**: And the water is cold. Cold, in July!  
**Marina**: Hazel painted a line by the waterfall. Go and look.  
**You**: Why don’t you look?  
**Marina**: I’ve looked lots. I hope I’m wrong. Please look too.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): naming a feeling: worried  
> _Marina keeps looking and looking. How does she feel?_  
> - ✅ Worried → Yes. I’m worried. A friend checking helps.
> - Silly → Not silly. Worried. When we worry, a friend can help.
> _For grown-ups: Named the feeling worried, and heard that a friend’s help eases worry._

**Marina**: Mind the moss on the rocks. Not that Moss! The slippy kind.  

#### `sprout:s4-line` · [main](../../../side/S04.md#s4-line)

_The falls are loud and white. Splash, splash!_  
_On the dark rock is a thick white line._  
_The water is a hand below the line._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): higher and lower; measuring with a hand  
> **Marina**: Is the water higher or lower than the line?  
> - ✅ Lower → Yes. Lower, by a whole hand. (Show a hand’s width.)
> - Higher → Look again. The line is up here. The water is down there. Lower!
> _For grown-ups: Compared heights (higher, lower) and measured a gap with a hand._

_Your Nubbin dips a paw in. Brr! It looks at you._  
**Marina**: A whole hand too low.  
**Marina**: Something up the stream is taking the water.  

#### `sprout:s4-lesson` · [main](../../../side/S04.md#s4-lesson)

**Marina**: Hazel’s Nubbins learned to swim with me.  
**Marina**: Fossil swam sideways, on purpose. I still look for her in the pool.  
**Marina**: Lesson one: Nubbins float better than you’d think.  
_Marina holds your Nubbin under its chin. Like a cup._  
_It kicks. It doesn’t sink!_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, do): float or sink  
> _(Guess: does it float or sink? A leaf, a stone, an apple.)_  
> **Marina**: Leaf floats. Apple floats. Stone sinks. Plop!  
> Check: `edu:sort:float-sink:leaf-stone-apple`
> _For grown-ups: Guessed and sorted things that float and sink._

**Marina**: Now swim up the stream. Just a little way.  
**Marina**: Lesson two: near water, Keepers stay close to a grown-up.  

**Choices**

- **Wade in after them.**
    - You wade in. Brrr! It’s very cold!
    - **Marina**: Lesson three: Keepers stay on the bank. Out you get!
- **Stay on the bank. Trust them.**
    - **Marina**: Good. Staying on the bank is the hardest part.

#### `sprout:s4-dam` · [main](../../../side/S04.md#s4-dam)

_Your swimmer comes back. Shake, shake! You get wet._  
_In its fur: a grey thread and a bit of new cement._  
_Up the stream is a new stone wall. It holds the water back._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS2-2, notice): a dam holds water back; beavers build dams  
> **Marina**: A wall that holds water back is called a dam.  
> **Marina**: Beavers build dams too! With sticks and mud.  
> _For grown-ups: Learned what a dam is, and that beavers build dams that change their surroundings._

_Behind it is flat, still, blue water._  
_There are swimming lanes, and a sign: NO SPLASHING._  
**Marina**: No splashing? Splashing is what water is for!  
**Marina**: The wall keeps the water here. So my pools stay dry.  
**Marina**: I wrote three letters. Nobody helped.  

#### `sprout:s4-ledge-ask` · [main](../../../side/S04.md#s4-ledge-ask)

**Marina**: Before we do anything, I’ll show you something.  
**Marina**: Hazel knew this path. Now you should too.  
_By the falls, a thin ledge goes behind the water._  
**Marina**: Back to the rock. Eyes on my heels. Stay close.  
**Marina**: Rule five: don’t be me when I was little.  
**Marina**: I ran on the wet rocks. Splat! Every time.  

> **Lesson** (health, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, ask): staying close to a grown-up near water  
> **Marina**: Near water, where do we stay?  
> - ✅ Close to a grown-up → Yes. Close to me, all the way.
> - Run on ahead → Not near water. Wet rocks are slippy. We stay close to a grown-up.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about staying close to a grown-up near water._


#### `sprout:s4-ledge` · [main](../../../side/S04.md#s4-ledge)

_Behind the falls, it’s all noise and spray. Whoosh!_  
_The rock is wet and soft with moss._  
_A warm breath of air comes from the dark._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, do): using our senses  
> **Marina**: Use your senses. Ears hear the roar. Skin feels the spray.  
> _(Point to your ears. Now your nose. What does a nose do?)_  
> Check: `edu:point:senses:ears-skin-nose`
> _For grown-ups: Named senses (hearing, touch, smell) and the body parts that use them._

**Marina**: That’s the warm spring. Where the first Nubbins came from.  
**Marina**: We stop here. Further is for when it’s needed.  
**Marina**: Now you know the way. That’s what a path is.  

#### `sprout:s4-sluice` · [main](../../../side/S04.md#s4-sluice)

**Marina**: Three letters. Nobody listened. Now we do something.  
**Marina**: We won’t break anything. We’ll open a gate.  
_Night. The dam is a dark wall. The gate is wood._  
_In a hut, a guard sleeps in his chair._  
_He snores. In… out… whistle! In… out… whistle!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, ELOF: Mathematics Development, do): copying and continuing a pattern  
> _(Do the snore pattern together: in, out, whistle!)_  
> **Marina**: In, out, whistle. What comes next? In!  
> Check: `edu:copy:pattern:snore`
> _For grown-ups: Copied a repeating sound pattern and said what comes next._

**Marina**: Move on the snore. Freeze on the whistle.  
_(Whistle! Everyone say “Freeze!”)_  
_You freeze. Your Nubbin freezes too, one foot up, like a statue._  
_Snore! You move. Your Nubbin stays still. And still._  
**Marina**: You can put your foot down now, pet.  
_The foot comes down. Wobble, wobble… plop!_  
_Your Nubbin sits on its bottom. The guard snores on._  
_You creep to the gate. You push. It’s stuck!_  

**Choices**

- **Lift, then push.**
    - Lift and push, like your gate at home. Creak! It opens!
    - **Marina**: Where did you learn that?
    - **You**: Home.
- **Let your strongest Nubbin try.**
    - Your Nubbin pushes. Nothing. Then it slips, and lifts!
    - The gate swings open.
    - **Marina**: Even the Nubbins know Hazel’s trick!
- **Pin a polite note on the gate first.**
    - You write: “Opened for the rock pools. Thank you!”
    - Then you lift and push. It opens.
    - **Marina**: Polite! Lovely.
    - In his sleep, the guard pats the air, looking for the note.

#### `sprout:s4-water` · [main](../../../side/S04.md#s4-water)

_Whoosh! Water rushes through the gate._  
**Marina**: Listen.  
_The waterfall sounds bigger now. Deep and full._  

> **Lesson** (music, ask): louder and quieter sounds  
> **Marina**: Is the waterfall louder now, or quieter?  
> - ✅ Louder → Yes! More water, more noise. Roar!
> - Quieter → Listen again. More water is falling. It’s louder!
> _For grown-ups: Listened and compared louder and quieter sounds._

_The guard mumbles, “Got it. Thank you.” Then he snores again._  
_In… out… whistle!_  
**Marina**: Someone may shut it again. Then someone opens it again.  
**Marina**: That’s most of looking after things.  

#### `sprout:s4-complete` · [main](../../../side/S04.md#s4-complete)

_A week later, the rock pools are full!_  
_The anemones open up, like little flowers._  
**Marina**: Look at them. Showing off!  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS3-1, notice): anemones are animals that live in water  
> **Marina**: Anemones look like flowers. But they’re animals!  
> **Marina**: They need water. When the water goes, they close up tight.  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that sea anemones are animals that need water to live._

_In the deepest pool, something round and white shines._  
_Your swimmer dives! Kick, kick, bubbles!_  
_It comes up with the thing on its head. Achoo!_  
_It’s an egg! A pearl-white egg!_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): animals that hatch from eggs  
> **Marina**: Lots of animals hatch from eggs. Which one does?  
> - ✅ A duck → Yes! Ducklings hatch from eggs. So do Nubbins!
> - A kitten → Kittens grow inside their mum, not in an egg. Ducks hatch from eggs!
> _For grown-ups: Learned that some animals, like ducks, hatch from eggs._

**Marina**: Oh, you beauty! It’s yours. You opened the gate.  
**Marina**: Take some shells for the Grove, too.  

**Notes:** The steward is “a guard” for read-aloud; he only ever sleeps. The ledge stops at the bend as in the source; the Wellspring is not shown.
