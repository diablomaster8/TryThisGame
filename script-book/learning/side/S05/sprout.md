# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S5: Fossil’s Last Walk

## S5. Fossil’s Walk

- `s5-step1`: Carry Fossil to the cliff where Dusty found her egg. · _The cliff is past the stream. Walk, don’t run. She likes to look at things._
- `s5-step2`: Take Fossil to Gran’s first tree. Shake it once. · _It’s the low, crooked tree over the stream. Just one shake!_
- `s5-step3`: Help Fossil float in the pond. · _Go to the edge of the pond and choose Swim. A swimmer or Marina can hold her._
- `s5-step4`: Sit with Fossil and your littlest Nubbin until dark. · _Stay still near them until night comes._

#### `sprout:s5-list` · [main](../../../side/S05.md#s5-list)

_Dusty is at the gate with a very old Nubbin._  
_She is the colour of sand. She blinks very slowly._  
**Dusty**: This is Fossil. I dug her egg out of a cliff!  
**Dusty**: Fossil is very old now. She is fading.  
**Dusty**: Fading means her life is nearly over.  
**Dusty**: She has three wishes. But my back hurts.  
**Dusty**: Will you carry her for me? Please?  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, ask): being gentle with someone old and frail  
> **Dusty**: How do we carry a very old Nubbin?  
> - ✅ Gently, with both hands → That’s it. Slow and gentle.
> - Fast, swinging her about → Oh, that would hurt her. Gently, with both hands.
> _For grown-ups: Thought about being gentle and careful with someone old._

**Dusty**: One, the cliff. Two, Hazel’s first tree. Three, the water.  
_Fossil holds up her arms. Up, please!_  

**Choices**

- **Lift her with both hands, like an egg.**
    - You lift her gently. She snuggles in.
    - **Dusty**: That’s it. Just right.
- **Let her ride on your shoulder.**
    - She climbs up slowly. She sits like a queen.
    - **Dusty**: Oh, she likes you!

#### `sprout:s5-cliff` · [main](../../../side/S05.md#s5-cliff)

_The cliff has stripes. Brown, red, grey, brown._  

> **Lesson** (maths, ELOF: Mathematics Development, ask): colour patterns: what comes next  
> _(Say the stripes together: brown, red, grey, brown.)_  
> **Dusty**: Which colour comes after red?  
> - ✅ Grey → Yes! Brown, red, grey, brown.
> - Brown → Brown comes first and last. After red comes grey.
> _For grown-ups: Read a colour sequence and said which colour came next._

**Dusty**: I dug Fossil’s egg out with a big, strong trowel!  
_Fossil taps the rock. Tap, tap! (Tap along!)_  
**Dusty**: A spoon. A small spoon.  
_Tap!_  
**Dusty**: A teaspoon. Fine.  
_Fossil rests her head on the rock. Everyone is quiet._  
_A baby fern grows in a crack. It is curled up tight._  
_Next to it, pressed in the stone, is a very old fern._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): a fossil is a very old plant or animal kept in stone  
> **Dusty**: That old fern is a fossil. A plant from long, long ago.  
> **Dusty**: Its shape got kept in the stone. That’s where Fossil got her name!  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a fossil is the shape of a very old living thing kept in rock._


#### `sprout:s5-tree-ask` · [main](../../../side/S05.md#s5-tree-ask)

**Dusty**: Next, Hazel’s first tree. The crooked one.  

> **Lesson** (art, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): straight and crooked lines  
> _(Make your arm straight. Now make it crooked, like the tree!)_  
> **Dusty**: Bendy and bumpy. That’s crooked.  
> Check: `edu:make:shape:straight-crooked`
> _For grown-ups: Made straight and crooked shapes with their body._

**Dusty**: Shake it once. Only once!  
_Fossil falls asleep on the way. Zzz._  
_She wakes up just as you get there!_  

#### `sprout:s5-tree` · [main](../../../side/S05.md#s5-tree)

_All your Nubbins follow. They sit in a row._  
_They all open their mouths. Ahhh!_  
**Dusty**: Once! Not twice.  
_You shake the tree, once. One fruit falls._  
_It misses every mouth. Plop! It lands in Fossil’s lap._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.C.6, ask): more and fewer  
> **Dusty**: Lots of mouths. One fruit. Is there one for everyone?  
> - ✅ No, not enough → Right. More mouths than fruit. Fewer fruit than mouths!
> - Yes, one each → Count them: lots of mouths, only one fruit. Not enough to go round!
> _For grown-ups: Compared groups to see that there were fewer fruits than mouths._

**Dusty**: Hazel gave her one of these when she hatched.  
_Fossil eats a little. She gives the rest to Dusty._  
**Dusty**: Tastes like when I was little.  

#### `sprout:s5-water-ask` · [main](../../../side/S05.md#s5-water-ask)

**Dusty**: Last wish: the water. She can’t swim alone now.  
**Dusty**: Marina taught her. She swam sideways!  
_Marina is at the pond, with a towel. What a surprise!_  
**Marina**: I was just passing. With a towel.  

> **Lesson** (health, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, notice): always swim with a helper nearby  
> **Marina**: Swimmers always have a helper close by. Even good swimmers.  
> _(Ask: who helps you near water? Wait for an answer.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that swimmers always have a helper close by, and named who helps them._


**Choices**

- **Let one of your swimmers hold her.**
    - Your swimmer wades out. It looks very serious.
    - **Marina**: Paw under her chin. Like a cup.
- **Ask Marina to hold her.**
    - Marina wades in and holds out her hands.
    - **Marina**: Come on, old girl. Same as always.

#### `sprout:s5-water` · [main](../../../side/S05.md#s5-water)

_Fossil goes into the water. She floats!_  
_Then, very slowly, she kicks._  
**Dusty**: Look! Her sideways swim!  
**Dusty**: To the reeds and back, Fos.  
_Slowly, slowly, she swims. Nobody hurries her._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, ask): opposites: slow and fast  
> **Dusty**: Fossil swims slowly. What’s the opposite of slow?  
> - ✅ Fast → Yes! Slow and fast are opposites.
> - Wet → She is wet! But the opposite of slow is fast.
> _For grown-ups: Named a pair of opposites: slow and fast._

_She comes out shivering, with bright eyes._  
_She looks at the Grove. She has a wish of her own._  

#### `sprout:s5-secret` · [main](../../../side/S05.md#s5-secret)

_Fossil walks across the Grove. Step… by… step._  
_She sits down by your littlest Nubbin._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): comparing: older and younger  
> _Who is older: Fossil, or your littlest Nubbin?_  
> - ✅ Fossil → Yes. Fossil is the oldest. Your littlest is the youngest.
> - My littlest Nubbin → Your littlest is brand new! Fossil is much, much older.
> _For grown-ups: Compared two characters by age using older and younger._

_Your littlest was falling over a fruit. Now it stops._  
_They look at each other. Then they just sit._  
**Dusty**: She did that for me once, on a bad day.  
**Dusty**: She didn’t fix anything. She just sat with me.  

#### `sprout:s5-gift` · [main](../../../side/S05.md#s5-gift)

_The Sun goes down behind the hill._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-ESS1-1, notice): sunset, then night  
> _The Sun goes down in the evening. That’s called sunset._  
> _Then the sky gets dark. Night time!_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that the Sun sets in the evening and then night comes._

_Nobody moves. Not Fossil. Not your littlest. Not you._  
_When it’s dark, Fossil touches noses with your littlest._  
_Your littlest sits up tall and proud. A gift from Fossil!_  

#### `sprout:s5-home` · [main](../../../side/S05.md#s5-home)

_Dusty picks Fossil up to carry her home._  
_The little ball above her head is very pale now._  
**Dusty**: Fossil won’t be with us much longer. I’m glad we did her wishes.  
**Dusty**: Thank you for walking with us.  
_Fossil gives you a baby fern, curled up tight._  
**Dusty**: She wants you to plant it. A fossil-fern!  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, ask): what a plant needs to grow  
> **Dusty**: What does a little fern need to grow?  
> - ✅ Soil, water and light → Yes. Soil, water and light. And time.
> - A box in the dark → In a dark box it won’t grow. It needs soil, water and light.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that plants need soil, water and light to grow._

**Dusty**: And take my old trowel. For digging.  
_They go out of the gate. Dusty lifts and pushes._  
_On the hill, Moss watches them go._  

**Notes:** Fossil’s death is not shown here (it belongs to chapter 6); sprout says plainly that she is very old and will not live much longer. The fossil-fern is a Grove plant; sprout calls Fossil’s gift a baby fern, not a seed (real ferns grow from spores, not seeds).
