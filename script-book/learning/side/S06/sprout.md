# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S6: Ash’s True Name

## S6. Ash’s Name

- `s6-s1`: Ember asked you to come. Go and see Ash. · _Ember’s house is on the Moon side. Knock on the door._
- `s6-s2`: Find what Gran wrote about Ash’s egg. · _In the cottage, tap the bookshelf by the stove. Look on the bottom shelf._
- `s6-s3`: Take the book to Ember. Practise the three notes by the pond. · _Pick the notes Gran wrote down. Try as many times as you like._
- `s6-s4`: Be in Ember’s kitchen when she whistles to Ash. · _Go to Ember’s house after dark. Stand quietly by the door._

#### `sprout:s6-kitchen` · [main](../../../side/S06.md#s6-kitchen)

_A note under your door: COME OVER. PLEASE._  
_Ember’s house is very, very tidy._  
_A grey Nubbin sits in the corner. She faces the wall._  
**Ember**: Ash. Ash, love. It’s me.  
_Ash does not turn round._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): understanding how a frightened friend feels  
> _Ash faces the wall. How might she feel?_  
> - ✅ Scared and lost → Yes. Scared and lost. She needs time.
> - Happy and silly → Happy Nubbins play and turn round. Ash feels scared and lost.
> _For grown-ups: Read clues to work out that a character felt scared and lost._

**Ember**: I’ve said her name so many times.  
**Ember**: She was away so long. She forgot her name.  

**Choices**

- **Sit on the floor near Ash, and wait.**
    - You sit for a long time. Ash relaxes, just a little.
    - **Ember**: Gran used to sit on the floor too. Like it was a chair!
- **Say “Ash” yourself, gently.**
    - Ash doesn’t turn.
    - **Ember**: See? It isn’t just me.
- **Ask what Corvin called her.**
    - **Ember**: Nothing. He never said her name.
    - **Ember**: If nobody says your name, you might forget it too.

#### `sprout:s6-egg` · [main](../../../side/S06.md#s6-egg)

_Ember puts the kettle on._  
**Ember**: When she was tiny, she answered to everything.  
**Ember**: Her name. My name. Even the kettle!  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds in names  
> _(Say the names slowly: Ash, Ember, Fern.)_  
> **Ember**: Which name starts with an “eh” sound?  
> - ✅ Ember → Yes. Eh-Ember. That’s me.
> - Ash → Ash starts with “a”, like apple. Eh-Ember starts with “eh”.
> - Fern → Fern starts with “fff”. Eh-Ember starts with “eh”.
> _For grown-ups: Listened for the first sound in three names._

**Ember**: Go home now. Thanks for coming.  

#### `sprout:s6-fern` · [main](../../../side/S06.md#s6-fern)

_Fern leans on the fence, eating an apple. Your apple!_  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): asking before taking  
> _Fern took your apple without asking. What should she do next time?_  
> - ✅ Ask, “May I have one?” → Oops. You’re right. May I have an apple, please?
> - Just take it → Hmm. That’s what I did. Asking first is kinder. May I?
> _For grown-ups: Talked about asking before taking something that belongs to someone else._

**Fern**: You’ve been at Ember’s. You have that look.  
**You**: Ash won’t answer to her name.  
**Fern**: Oh, poor Ash. Poor Ember.  
**You**: Ash came from one of Gran’s eggs.  
**Fern**: Then she’s in Gran’s book! Gran wrote every egg down.  
**Fern**: And the first sound each baby made.  
**Fern**: Bottom shelf, behind the jam. Don’t eat the jam!  

#### `sprout:s6-register` · [main](../../../side/S06.md#s6-register)

_Behind the jam: a fat old book._  
_On the front, Gran wrote: “EGGS.”_  
_And smaller: “Do not lend this to Byte.”_  
_Inside, a page says: “Given away.”_  
**Gran Hazel**: “Egg 7. Given to Sparks. First sound: a bang!”  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.4, do): show 7 on fingers  
> _(Egg 7! Hold up seven fingers together.)_  
> _(Five on one hand, and two more. Seven!)_  
> Check: `edu:show:fingers:7`
> _For grown-ups: Showed seven on their fingers as five and two more._

**Gran Hazel**: “Egg 23. Given to Byte. First sound: a hiccup.”  
**Gran Hazel**: “Egg 41. Given to Ember, aged fifteen. She promised to feed it first.”  
**Gran Hazel**: “First sound: three notes. Low, high, and a little one.”  
_Gran drew three dots. Low, high, and a tiny one going up._  

#### `sprout:s6-practise` · [main](../../../side/S06.md#s6-practise)

_Ember reads the page. Twice._  
**Ember**: What if I call her and she doesn’t come?  
_She comes to the pond anyway, frowning._  
**Ember**: I can’t sing. But I can whistle.  
**Ember**: You go first. Low, high, little one. Like a question.  

> **Lesson** (music, do): low and high notes  
> _(Sing a low note. Now a high note. Then a little one going up!)_  
> **Ember**: Low… high… little one? Like a question!  
> Check: `edu:sing:notes:low-high-up`
> _For grown-ups: Sang low and high notes and a little rising note, like a question._

_Your Nubbins sit on the bank to watch._  
**Ember**: If a duck answers, we’re stopping.  

**Choices**

- **Whistle: high, low, a little one.**
    - One of your Nubbins sneezes. Ember laughs.
    - **Ember**: Wrong way round! High comes second. Again.
- **Whistle: low, a little one, high.**
    - The last note goes up and up, like a kettle!
    - **Ember**: Nearly! The little one comes last. Again.
- **Whistle: low, high, and a little one.**
    - Low, high, and a little one, like a question.
    - Every Nubbin on the bank turns its head!
    - **Ember**: …Oh.
    - **Ember**: Again. Slower.

#### `sprout:s6-ember-tries` · [main](../../../side/S06.md#s6-ember-tries)

_Ember whistles the three notes. Quietly._  
_From across the pond, a duck answers. QUACK!_  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-LS3-1, ask): animal babies: a baby duck is a duckling  
> _Ducks say quack. What is a baby duck called?_  
> - ✅ A duckling → Yes! A duckling. It looks like a small, fluffy duck.
> - A kitten → A kitten is a baby cat. A baby duck is a duckling.
> - A puppy → A puppy is a baby dog. A baby duck is a duckling.
> _For grown-ups: Named a duck’s baby (duckling) and other animal babies._

**Ember**: No.  
_Ember glares at the duck. The duck does not care._  
_But Ember heard herself do it._  
**Ember**: Tonight. You’re coming. Don’t say anything.  

#### `sprout:s6-whistle` · [main](../../../side/S06.md#s6-whistle)

_Ember’s kitchen at night. One lamp is lit._  
_Ash faces the wall. Ember kneels a little way off._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, notice): giving a scared friend space  
> _Ember doesn’t go too close. She gives Ash space._  
> _Space helps a scared friend feel safe._  
> _For grown-ups: Saw how giving space helps a scared friend feel safe._

_Ember whistles. Low, high, and a little one._  
_Ash’s ear moves. Just a little._  
_Ember whistles again. Slower. Her hands shake._  
_Ash turns round._  
_Then a tiny sound: pip. wirr. a?_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, do): sound words  
> _(Make Ash’s sound together: pip, wirr, a?)_  
> _Pip, wirr, a. That’s Ash’s very own sound._  
> Check: `edu:say:sound:ash`
> _For grown-ups: Copied a character’s sound word aloud._

_Step by step, Ash comes. She leans on Ember’s knee._  
**Ember**: Hello, you. It’s me. It was always me.  

**Choices**

- **Stay by the door, very quietly.**
    - Ember cries quietly. Happy tears.
    - Ash lets her.
- **Tiptoe out. This is their time.**
    - You lift the latch slowly. No click.
    - Behind you, Ember whistles. Ash answers every time.

#### `sprout:s6-complete` · [main](../../../side/S06.md#s6-complete)

_Next morning, Ember is at your gate. Ash walks close._  
_Ash is still grey._  
**Ember**: Clover says the grey stays. You can only make it lighter.  
**Ember**: She ate breakfast with me in the room. That’s lighter.  
**Ember**: Here. Ash’s first ribbon. Give it to one of yours.  
**Ember**: Now. Two fingers, like that. Blow!  
_Try one: pfff. Try two: pfff. Try three. Try four._  
_Try five: wheee! Three Nubbins come running!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 5; keep trying  
> _(Count the tries on your fingers: one, two, three, four, five!)_  
> **Ember**: Five tries. You kept going. That’s how you learn.  
> Check: `edu:count:tries:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five tries and heard that practice is how we learn._

**Ember**: Don’t tell anyone I like you.  

**Notes:** Corvin appears only by name (“he never said her name”). s6-s4 has no done scene in the source.
