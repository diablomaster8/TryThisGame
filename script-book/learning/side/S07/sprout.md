# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S7: Unverified

## S7. Null and the Clock

- `s7-s1`: Find Byte after Moss’s talk. · _Byte is on the low wall by the long grass, writing._
- `s7-s2`: See the clock Pixel built for Null. · _Pixel’s bench is behind the lantern shed. Follow the tick-tock._
- `s7-s3`: After a week, go and see Null. · _Visit Byte’s house when the journal says a week has gone by._
- `s7-s4`: Spend an evening with Null. No clock. Wait for Null to come to you. · _Sit still near Null. Only pet Null when it comes close by itself._

#### `sprout:s7-unverified` · [main](../../../side/S07.md#s7-unverified)

_After Moss’s talk, Byte sits on the wall, writing._  
_Beside him is a pale blue Nubbin. That’s Null._  
**Byte**: Null is four years old. And some weeks.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.4, do): show 4 on fingers; how old are you  
> _(Null is four. Hold up four fingers!)_  
> _(How old are you? Show me on your fingers.)_  
> Check: `edu:show:fingers:4`
> _For grown-ups: Showed four on their fingers and then their own age._

**Moss**: Love can help a Nubbin live a very, very long time.  
**Byte**: How much love? A cupful? A bucketful? I’ll measure it!  
**Moss**: Your shoelace is untied.  
_Byte looks down. It is. It always is! Hee, hee!_  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): checking our shoes are done up  
> _(Look at your shoes. Laces, buckles or sticky straps?)_  
> **Moss**: Done-up shoes stop you tripping. Always worth a look.  
> Check: `edu:check:shoes:fastened`
> _For grown-ups: Checked their own shoes and talked about laces, buckles and straps._

**Moss**: Things on a clock learn to wait for the bell, Byte.  
**Moss**: Safe is not the same as well.  
_Byte writes it down. Then he writes: NOT CHECKED._  

**Choices**

- **I’ll help. What do you need?**
    - **Byte**: Fruit, a bed, and a helper who watches.
- **Moss is usually right, Byte.**
    - **Byte**: “Usually” isn’t always!
    - Moss pretends to sleep. He is bad at it.
- **Pet Null.**
    - Null leans into your hand. Then leans more!
    - **Byte**: Fine. Once.

#### `sprout:s7-minder` · [main](../../../side/S07.md#s7-minder)

_Pixel’s bench: a clock, three little bells, and a tiny bed._  
**Pixel**: It was my lantern clock. Now it’s Null’s clock.  
**Pixel**: Bell one: fruit drops. Bell two: the bed warms.  
**Pixel**: Bell three: petting! Byte does the petting.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.K.3, ask): first, next, last  
> **Pixel**: Bell one, bell two, bell three. What happens first?  
> - ✅ Fruit drops → Yes! First fruit. Next the bed. Last, petting!
> - The bed warms → The bed first? Snore! Plop, the fruit comes down before that. That’s next. First the fruit drops. Then the bed. Then petting.
> - Petting → Petting first? Hee, hee! Petting comes last of all. That’s last! First fruit, next bed, last petting.
> _For grown-ups: Put three steps in order using first, next and last._

**Byte**: Exactly ninety seconds. I tested it.  
**Pixel**: We call it the Minder.  
_Tick. A plum shoots out. Bonk! It hits Byte._  
**Pixel**: That was a test.  
**Byte**: That was a plum.  
**Pixel**: Byte and I are married. He argues with my clocks.  
**Pixel**: A clock doesn’t care. I’m trying to care for it.  

**Choices**

- **What if Null is hungry before the bell?**
    - **Byte**: Null won’t be. The bell is set.
    - **Pixel**: I asked him that too.
- **Does it have an off switch?**
    - **Pixel**: Yes! A lever. I always add a lever.
    - **Pixel**: Thank you for asking!

#### `sprout:s7-first-bell` · [main](../../../side/S07.md#s7-first-bell)

_Byte puts Null in the little bed. Ding! Bell one._  
_Null looks at Byte. Then at the bell. Then eats._  
**Byte**: Day one. Come back in a week.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.A.1, do): a week is seven days; count to 7  
> **Byte**: A week is seven days.  
> _(Count to seven together. One whole week!)_  
> Check: `edu:count:days:7`
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a week has seven days and counted to seven._

**Pixel**: It’s just a clock. Clocks are fine.  

#### `sprout:s7-week` · [main](../../../side/S07.md#s7-week)

_A week later. Null sits up in its bed, waiting._  
**Byte**: Best week ever! Null eats so neatly.  
**Pixel**: Watch this.  
_Pixel rolls a ball. Null doesn’t chase it._  
_Ding! Null eats. It only looks at the bell._  
_Null’s blue has gone pale at the edges._  
**Byte**: It used to chase that ball everywhere. Why won’t it play?  
**Pixel**: I think Null waits for the bell. Only the bell.  

> **Lesson** (health, ask): we need food, rest, play and love  
> **Pixel**: What does a Nubbin need to be well?  
> - ✅ Food, rest, play and love → Yes. All of them. Not just the ones on a clock.
> - Just food and a bed → Food and rest keep it alive. Play and love make it well. It needs them all.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about needing play and love as well as food and rest._

**Byte**: Moss told me. And I didn’t listen.  

**Choices**

- **Pull the lever.**
    - Pixel pulls it at once. The ticking stops.
    - It is very, very quiet.
    - **Pixel**: I always add a lever.
- **Sit down by Null, Byte. And wait.**
    - **Byte**: For how long?
    - **You**: Until Null stops waiting for the bell.
    - Byte pulls the lever himself.

#### `sprout:s7-same-graph` · [main](../../../side/S07.md#s7-same-graph)

**Byte**: I wanted to keep Null safe. I kept it too still.  
**Byte**: Moss said it. Safe is not the same as well.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): saying “I was wrong”  
> **Byte**: I was wrong.  
> _Saying “I was wrong” is hard. It’s also brave._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard a character admit a mistake, and that this is brave._

_Null waits for a bell that doesn’t come. No ding, no dong._  
_It looks a little lost._  
**Pixel**: Tomorrow, no clock. We’ll just sit. We’re good at sitting.  

#### `sprout:s7-no-clock` · [main](../../../side/S07.md#s7-no-clock)

_Evening in Byte’s garden. No bells._  
_Byte holds his stopwatch. His thumb wiggles._  
_He sits on his thumb._  
_Null sits in the grass. For a long time, nothing._  
**Pixel**: Slow, Byte. Slower than that.  
_Then a moth flutters past. Swish! Null’s head turns!_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): moths fly at night  
> _Most moths fly at night. They like lights._  
> _Most butterflies fly in the day._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that most moths fly at night and most butterflies fly by day._

**Byte**: Did you see that? That’s not on any list!  
_Null takes one step after the moth. Then it looks at you._  

**Choices**

- **Nod. “Go on.”**
    - Null runs. It falls into the hedge!
    - It comes out with a leaf on its head.
    - Null says prrp! Byte does not write that down.
- **Put the stopwatch down, Byte.**
    - Byte puts it face down in the grass.
    - **Byte**: It’s still ticking. But I can’t see it. That’s a start.

#### `sprout:s7-lap` · [main](../../../side/S07.md#s7-lap)

_Near dark, Null climbs into Byte’s lap._  
_It falls asleep at once._  
_Byte sits still for an hour. His nose itches. Nearly an achoo!_  
_He lets it itch. He doesn’t check the time._  
**Byte**: Its edges look bluer. Don’t tell me if I’m wrong.  

> **Lesson** (art, do): naming the colour blue  
> _(Find something blue near you. Point to it!)_  
> _(Is it pale blue, like Null? Or dark blue?)_  
> Check: `edu:find:colour:blue`
> _For grown-ups: Found something blue and compared pale and dark blue._


#### `sprout:s7-complete` · [main](../../../side/S07.md#s7-complete)

_A few days later, Pixel knocks. She is very quiet._  
_Pixel takes your hand._  
_Null is under Byte’s hedge. Null is old now._  
_Null’s long life is ending. Around it, a glowing cocoon grows._  
_Byte sits with his hand on it. He doesn’t count._  
_The cocoon glows gold. Pop! It opens. An egg!_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): life cycles: caterpillar, cocoon, moth  
> _In real life, a caterpillar makes a cocoon._  
> _Later, a moth comes out! Living things change as they grow._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard about a real life cycle: caterpillar, cocoon, moth._

**Byte**: Null gets to start again. And I’ll remember it.  
**Byte**: Here. My stopwatch. Please keep it.  
**Pixel**: And the clock. It only counts lanterns now.  
_Byte’s shoelace is tied. Nobody told him to. It won’t last!_  

**Notes:** UNVERIFIED is “NOT CHECKED” for read-aloud. Null’s end of life is said plainly and ends in rebirth, as in the source. s7-s1 has no done scene in the source.
