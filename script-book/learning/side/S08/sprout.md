# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S8: The Warren in the Hollow

## S8. The Ring of Roots

- `s8-s1`: Follow Nova and the fireflies past Gran’s camp. · _Walk when the fireflies float. Stop when they stop._
- `s8-s2`: Leave fruit by the ring of roots, a little at a time. · _Bring twelve fruits over three evenings. Put them down, then step back._
- `s8-s3`: Bring Fern to the ring of roots. · _Fern is at her gate next door. Walk with her to the Hollow._
- `s8-s4`: Sit by the ring with Fern. Let the old Nubbin come to her. · _Sit down by the roots and wait. Don’t reach in._

#### `sprout:s8-further` · [main](../../../side/S08.md#s8-further)

_Nova steps out of the trees._  
**Nova**: This is Gran’s camp. But it isn’t all of it.  
**Nova**: Gran wrote, “Fed nineteen today.” But there are only twelve bowls.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.C.6, ask): more and fewer  
> **Nova**: Nineteen Nubbins. Twelve bowls. Are there more Nubbins, or more bowls?  
> - ✅ More Nubbins → Yes. Nineteen is more than twelve. Not enough bowls!
> - More bowls → Twelve is fewer than nineteen. So there were more Nubbins than bowls.
> _For grown-ups: Compared nineteen and twelve to see which is more._

**Nova**: So she carried food further in.  
_A firefly lands on the notebook. Blink, blink!_  
_You blink back. The firefly flies off._  
**Nova**: The fireflies go the same way every night.  
**Nova**: Bring fruit. Walk slowly. Slower than that.  
_(Walk your fingers on your knee. Slow… slower… slower than that!)_  

**Choices**

- **Lead the way.**
    - **Nova**: I don’t lead. I follow fireflies. You follow me.
- **Should my Nubbin wait here?**
    - **Nova**: No. Bring a Nubbin who trusts you. Then they’ll trust you too.

#### `sprout:s8-ring` · [main](../../../side/S08.md#s8-ring)

_The fireflies stop over a ring of old roots._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): fireflies make their own light  
> **Nova**: Fireflies are little beetles. They make their own light!  
> _(Open and shut your hands, like a firefly. Blink, blink!)_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that fireflies are beetles that make their own light._

_Inside sit lots of grey Nubbins, in a circle._  
_In the middle sits an old grey Nubbin._  
_Her ear folds over at the tip. A faded ribbon is tied on it._  
**Nova**: They aren’t lost. They know where they are.  
**Nova**: That one’s in charge. I call her the Old Girl.  

#### `sprout:s8-edge` · [main](../../../side/S08.md#s8-edge)

_You step towards the roots. Every grey head turns. Swish!_  
**Nova**: Yes. They do that.  
**Nova**: Don’t go in. This is their home.  
**Nova**: We don’t walk into a home without being asked.  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Civ.8.K-2, ask): respecting other people’s homes  
> **Nova**: You visit a friend’s home. What do you do first?  
> - ✅ Knock, and wait to be asked in → Yes. Knock and wait. That’s respect.
> - Walk straight in → That might scare them. We knock and wait to be asked in.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about knocking and waiting to be invited into someone’s home._

**You**: How did Gran feed them?  
**Nova**: She left food at the edge and stepped back. Every night.  
_By the Old Girl sits a very thin grey Nubbin._  
_The thin one’s ear folds at the tip. Like the Old Girl’s!_  
**Nova**: Her baby, I think. She doesn’t eat much.  

**Choices**

- **Leave the softest fruit near the thin one.**
    - **Nova**: That’s just what Gran did!
- **Put the fruit down. Sit a little way off.**
    - You sit. At last, one grey Nubbin takes a fruit.
    - **Nova**: Good. Now, three nights of this.

#### `sprout:s8-gap` · [main](../../../side/S08.md#s8-gap)

_On the third night, the Old Girl stands up._  
_She steps aside. There’s a gap, just for you!_  
**Nova**: I sit by these roots whenever I pass. She never did that for me!  
_The Old Girl’s ribbon is brown. A leaf is sewn on it._  
_The leaf has five points and a curly stem._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 5  
> _(Hold up one hand. Five fingers, like five leaf points!)_  
> _(Count them: one, two, three, four, five.)_  
> Check: `edu:count:leaf-points:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five points on a leaf using the fingers of one hand._

_You’ve seen that leaf before. On Fern’s gloves! On Fern’s gate!_  

#### `sprout:s8-fern-gate` · [main](../../../side/S08.md#s8-fern-gate)

_Fern is at her gate, sewing a leaf on a glove._  
**Fern**: I put leaves on everything. Once I put one on a ribbon.  
**Fern**: That was for Bramble, my Nubbin.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sound b  
> _(Say it slowly: b-b-Bramble.)_  
> _Which word starts with “b” too?_  
> - ✅ Ball → Yes! B-b-ball, b-b-Bramble.
> - Fish → Fish starts with “fff”. Ball starts with “b”, like Bramble!
> - Sun → Sun starts with “sss”. Ball starts with “b”, like Bramble!
> _For grown-ups: Matched the first sound “b” in Bramble and ball._

**Fern**: At a race, long ago, she fell at the cliff bend.  
**Fern**: I looked till dark. I never found her.  
**Fern**: I still miss her.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): missing someone, and talking about it  
> _Fern misses Bramble. Missing someone can last a long time._  
> _Talking about it, like Fern does, can help._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that missing someone can last a long time and that talking helps._


**Choices**

- **Fern. I think I’ve found her.**
    - **Fern**: Don’t say that. Not unless it’s true.
    - **You**: Bring soft fruit.
    - Fern runs through her gate. She still holds her needle!
- **Will you help me carry fruit tonight?**
    - **Fern**: The Hollow? At night? …Fine.
    - You don’t tell her why. Not yet.

#### `sprout:s8-arrive` · [main](../../../side/S08.md#s8-arrive)

_The fireflies float ahead of you both._  
**Fern**: It’s so dark! I can’t see my own feet!  
_Bonk. Fern walks into a tree. “Sorry, tree.”_  
_Bonk. The same tree again!_  
**Fern**: I’m carrying the soft fruit. I sat on two.  
_At the stream, Fern takes her boots off and wades across._  
_She puts them back on the wrong feet!_  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): left and right  
> _(Wiggle your left foot. Now wiggle your right foot!)_  
> **Fern**: Left boot, left foot. Right boot, right foot. Oops!  
> Check: `edu:wiggle:feet:left-right`
> _For grown-ups: Practised left and right with their own feet._

_Then the fireflies stop. Fern goes very quiet._  

#### `sprout:s8-reunion` · [main](../../../side/S08.md#s8-reunion)

_Fern stops at the roots. She looks at the middle._  
_The Old Girl stands up. Her brown ribbon hangs from her ear._  
**Fern**: Bramble? Oh, you’ve gone grey. That’s all right. So have I.  
_Nobody moves._  
_Then Bramble walks out. Step by step. All the way to Fern._  
_Bramble says nothing. She leans on Fern’s leg._  
**Fern**: I’m sorry I stopped looking. I’m so sorry.  
_Bramble sits down on Fern’s boots. Both boots! And she stays._  
**Fern**: I’m going to cry now. Happy crying. Hold my gloves!  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): happy tears  
> _People can cry when they’re happy, too._  
> _Big feelings sometimes come out as tears._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that people can cry when they are happy._


**Choices**

- **Hold her gloves.**
    - You hold them. Fern sits in the mud with Bramble in her lap.
    - She cries a long time. Bramble lets her.
- **Give Fern a ribbon from your pocket.**
    - **Fern**: You carry ribbons? Of course you do!
    - Fern sews a leaf on it. She ties it under the old one.
    - **Fern**: Now she has two!

#### `sprout:s8-one-short` · [main](../../../side/S08.md#s8-one-short)

_Later, Bramble goes back to the middle of the ring._  
**Fern**: She always did like being in charge.  
_Bramble looks up the dark path._  
**Nova**: Bramble’s thin baby isn’t here tonight.  
**Fern**: Then we leave soft fruit at the edge. Till she comes back.  
_Fern puts a plum down. Not one she sat on!_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): soft and hard  
> **Fern**: Soft fruit is easy for a little one. Which is softer?  
> - ✅ A ripe banana → Yes! Squishy and soft. Easy to eat.
> - A hard nut → A nut is hard. Crunch! A ripe banana is softer.
> _For grown-ups: Sorted foods by how they feel: soft and hard._


#### `sprout:s8-complete` · [main](../../../side/S08.md#s8-complete)

_Gran sits by the pond, holding a thin grey baby._  
**Gran Hazel**: I found her by a ring of roots. Too tired to stand.  
**Gran Hazel**: So I carried her. Her mother runs that place.  
**You**: Fern found her mother. Her name is Bramble.  
**Gran Hazel**: Did she? Oh, Fern.  
**Gran Hazel**: Take this little one home, love.  
_At the ring, the little one sees her mum. She wiggles with joy!_  
_Bramble washes her, ear to ear._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-LS1-2, notice): animal parents look after their babies  
> _Many animal mums and dads look after their babies._  
> _They clean them, feed them and keep them warm._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that many animal parents clean, feed and warm their young._

_Then Bramble drops her old ribbon at your feet. A thank-you._  
**Nova**: That’s you invited. Come any time. Bring soft fruit.

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.4, do): twice means two times

> **Gran Hazel**: Two times. I liked both of them.
