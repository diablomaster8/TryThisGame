# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S10: Fourteen Months

## S10. Gran’s Three Wishes

- `s10-step1`: Wish one: write your Nubbins in Gran’s big book. · _The book is on the cottage shelf. Add every Nubbin you have hatched._
- `s10-step2`: Wish two: run a race while Gran watches. · _Open Races from the top bar. Any race will do. Winning doesn’t matter._
- `s10-step3`: Wish three: walk with Gran to the other three lanterns. · _Go at Gran’s pace. Stop when she stops._
- `s10-step4`: Walk with Gran to the bottom of the hill. · _After the big choice, follow Gran across the Grove to the hill path._

#### `sprout:s10-step` · [main](../../../side/S10.md#s10-step)

_Evening. Gran sits on the step. Her feet are in warm water._  
**Gran Hazel**: Sit. There. I like to see the pond.  
_Gran Hazel is very poorly. The doctors can’t make her better._  
**Gran Hazel**: The doctor said one year. I’ve had fourteen months!  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.C.6, ask): a year has 12 months; 14 is more than 12  
> _A year has twelve months. Gran has had fourteen._  
> _Is fourteen more than twelve, or less?_  
> - ✅ More → More! Two whole months more. I’m very pleased with myself.
> - Less → Less? Boing! The number hops up, not down. Count on from twelve: thirteen, fourteen. Fourteen is more!
> _For grown-ups: Learned that a year has twelve months and compared fourteen with twelve._

_Soon Gran will die. That means her body will stop working._  
**Gran Hazel**: Don’t be brave at me. Sad is allowed.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): all feelings are allowed, and what helps  
> _Sad, worried, cross: all feelings are allowed._  
> _A hug or sitting close to someone can help._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that every feeling is allowed and that closeness and hugs can help._

**Gran Hazel**: I have three little wishes. And one favour.  
**Gran Hazel**: This is my news. Can I share it my own way?  

**Choices**

- **All right, Gran. Share it your own way.**
    - **Gran Hazel**: Thank you, love.
    - **Gran Hazel**: I’ll write them a letter. I’m good at letters.
    - **Gran Hazel**: Wish one: write your Nubbins in my big book.
- **They love you. They should say goodbye.**
    - **Gran Hazel**: They’ll make a fuss. Clover will cry on me.
    - **Gran Hazel**: All right. Tell them.
    - **Gran Hazel**: Wish one: write your Nubbins in my big book.

#### `sprout:s10-wish2` · [main](../../../side/S10.md#s10-wish2)

**Gran Hazel**: Wish two: one race. I’ll sit at the back with a pie.  
**Sparks**: Hazel is coming to a RACE?  
**Sparks**: I’ll save you a seat! Front row!  
**Gran Hazel**: Sparks, love. I want the back row.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, ask): front and back  
> _Where does Gran want to sit?_  
> - ✅ The back row → The back row. Behind everyone. Fine!
> - The front row → That’s what I wanted! But Gran said the back row.
> _For grown-ups: Used the position words front and back._

**Sparks**: …Back row. Right.  
**Gran Hazel**: Bring any Nubbin. Fast or slow. I don’t mind about winning.  
**Sparks**: She does mind. Whoosh! She once threw a hat at the finish line!  
**Gran Hazel**: It was a very small hat.  

**Choices**

- **Bring the fast one!**
    - **Gran Hazel**: Oh, lovely! I’ll cheer till I’ve no voice left.
- **Bring the slow one. The one who stops to look.**
    - **Gran Hazel**: The slow one? Good! I’ll get a second pie.

#### `sprout:s10-cheer` · [main](../../../side/S10.md#s10-cheer)

_The crowd roars! From the back row, one voice is loudest._  
_Sparks has lost her voice. She cheers with no sound!_  
_In the back row sits a duck. A very sure duck._  
**Gran Hazel**: Leave the duck. It paid for its seat!  
_Your Nubbin crosses the line. Gran cheers, munch, munch, with a full mouth!_  
_Win or lose, Gran stands up, beaming. Half a pie in her hand._  
**Gran Hazel**: That one runs for you. That’s love. Never spend that.  
_Then Gran sits down, all of a sudden. She is tired._  
_Nobody hurries her._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, notice): waiting kindly with someone who is tired  
> _When someone is tired, we can wait with them._  
> _Waiting kindly is a way to show love._  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that waiting patiently with someone who is tired is a kind thing to do._


#### `sprout:s10-wish3` · [main](../../../side/S10.md#s10-wish3)

_Evening. Gran’s third wish has a little drawing: four posts._  
**Gran Hazel**: Wish three. Walk the four lanterns with me. Slowly.  
_Gran holds your arm at the pond lantern._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.1, ask): counting on: 1 and 3 more make 4  
> **Gran Hazel**: Four posts. We’re at the first one. How many more to go?  
> - ✅ Three → Yes. One here, three more. Four in all.
> - Two → Let’s count on: this is one. Two, three, four. Three more!
> - Four → Four in all. But we’re at one already. So three more!
> _For grown-ups: Counted on from one to four to find how many more._

**Gran Hazel**: This is Moss’s post. When I was young, I came here.  
_A memory. Snow. A young Hazel sits by the frozen pond._  
**Young Hazel**: I don’t know what I’m doing.  
_A small green-grey Nubbin sits near her. Day after day. All winter._  
_When the snow melts, it looks up at her._  

> **Lesson** (science, 2-ESS2-3, notice): snow and ice melt when it gets warmer  
> _In spring, the sunshine gets warmer._  
> _Snow and ice melt into water. Drip, drip!_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that snow and ice melt into water when the weather warms in spring._

**Moss**: …You’ll do.  
**Gran Hazel**: Rule three. Moss taught it to me. Just by sitting.

#### `sprout:s10-lanterns` · [main](../../../side/S10.md#s10-lanterns)

_At the stream lantern, Gran laughs. Briar would have._  
_At the old wall, Gran puts her hand on the post. Quiet._  
_Then the long grass. The last post glows bright and steady._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-PS4-2, notice): we need light to see  
> _Lanterns make light in the dark._  
> _We need light to see. No light, no seeing!_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that we need light to see things._

**Gran Hazel**: For years it only flickered. I looked every night.  
**Gran Hazel**: I stopped hoping. But I never stopped looking.  
**Gran Hazel**: Can I ask you something? I never asked anyone.  
**Gran Hazel**: Should I have gone after Corvin? That night, long ago?  

**Choices**

- **You were right to wait. He had to come back himself.**
    - **Gran Hazel**: Maybe. You can’t carry anyone home.
    - **Gran Hazel**: They have to walk back on their own.
    - **Gran Hazel**: That was the one I wanted. Thank you, love.
- **Somebody should have gone after him.**
    - **Gran Hazel**: Yes. Some nights I think so too.
    - **Gran Hazel**: I’ll never know. That’s all right.
    - **Gran Hazel**: That was the one I wanted. Thank you, love.

#### `sprout:s10-night` · [main](../../../side/S10.md#s10-night)

_You walk Gran home. She stops at the door._  
**Gran Hazel**: Tomorrow, everyone comes. You’ll tell them about the Grove.  
**Gran Hazel**: Then I’ll go up the hill. No fuss.  
_Going up the hill is Gran’s way of saying she will die._  
**Gran Hazel**: Being ordinary has been lovely.  
**Gran Hazel**: Now go home. Or the hens will sleep on you!  
_Inside, Gran sits on her bed for a long time._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): telling a grown-up when we feel sad  
> _If you feel sad, you can tell a grown-up._  
> _A hug and a cuddle can help._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that when they feel sad they can tell a grown-up and get a hug._

> _For grown-ups: Thought about kind ways to comfort a friend who is crying._

#### `sprout:s10-letter` · [main](../../../side/S10.md#s10-letter)

_Morning. Gran Hazel has died._  
_That means her body stopped working. It won’t start again._  
_Under the signpost stone is a letter from Gran._  
**Gran Hazel**: “Don’t make a big fuss. Well, maybe a little fuss.”  
**Gran Hazel**: “You kept my three rules. Here is one more.”  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.1, ask): add 1: three and one more make four  
> _Three rules, and one more. How many rules now?_  
> - ✅ Four → Yes. Three and one more make four. (Count them on your fingers: one, two, three… four!)
> - Three → Three, and then one more. Let’s count up: one, two, three… four!
> - Five → Let’s count: one, two, three rules. One more makes four.
> _For grown-ups: Added one more to three to make four._

**Gran Hazel**: “Show up. Every morning. Same sticky gate.”  
**Gran Hazel**: “I love you. I always will. Love, Gran.”  
_Behind you, the gate creaks. Fern. Sparks. Everyone._  
_They hug you. Nobody needs to say anything._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): feeling sad when someone dies, and remembering together  
> _When someone dies, we feel sad. Crying is all right._  
> _We remember them together. That helps._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that sadness and crying are natural when someone dies, and that remembering together helps._

_Byte’s shoelaces are untied. Pixel ties them. Nobody says anything._

**Notes:** Hazel is “very poorly” and no illness is named. Her death is said plainly by the narrator (s10-step, s10-night, s10-letter), never as sleep; Hazel herself stays funny. The secret/told choice is framed as Gran choosing how to share her own news. The foot-of-the-hill scene is built by s10Foot as in the source; s10-foot-msc is here and the other seven are in the variants. LINE VARIANT (letThemGo): with “Open” there is no gate, so in s10-letter “Behind you, the gate creaks.” becomes “Behind you, footsteps.”, and in s10-letter-told “already at the gate” becomes “already at the gateposts”.
