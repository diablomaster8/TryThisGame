# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S12: The Hum

## S12. The Hum

- `s12-step1`: Carry the little beetle home to the old wall. · _The old wall is on the north side of the Grove. Carry the teacup flat and steady._
- `s12-step2`: Put your ear to the old wall at dusk. Listen! · _Lie down by the bottom stone, near the lantern post. Keep very still and quiet._
- `s12-step3`: Sit with Solo in Luna’s garden until morning. · _Sit on the bench by the moon flowers after dark. Don’t get up._
- `s12-step4`: Follow the buzzing swarm with Pixel’s tuning fork. · _Tap the fork and turn slowly. It buzzes most when you face the swarm._

#### `sprout:s12-solo` · [main](../../../side/S12.md#s12-solo)

_Evening in Luna’s night garden. The moon flowers open._  
**Luna**: Keeper! Over here. I’ve got something in a teacup.  
_In the cup is a round bronze beetle. It’s fuzzy, like a peach._  
_It’s a Nubbin! Its wings buzz when it talks._  
**Luna**: It’s a Thrum. Lots of them live in the old wall.  
**Luna**: They hum together. They never come out alone.  
**Solo**: We are— no.  
**Solo**: I. I am. Hello. I think I am sorry.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, ask): the words “I” and “we”: one and lots  
> **Luna**: It’s just one beetle. Should it say “I” or “we”?  
> - ✅ “I” → Yes! One on your own says “I”. Lots together say “we”.
> - “We” → “We” for one tiny bee? Buzz! That’s a lonely we. “We” is for lots together. One on its own says “I”.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that we say “I” about one person and “we” about a group._

**Luna**: It needs a name, I think.  

**Choices**

- **Call it Solo.**
    - **Solo**: Solo. That means just one. Yes. That’s me.
    - **Luna**: Good name.
- **Give it a name of your own.**
    - You say a name. The beetle says it three times, quietly.
    - **Solo**: Nobody ever said a thing just to me before!
- **Ask what it would like to be called.**
    - **Solo**: Like? I never liked anything on my own before.
    - **Solo**: I would like you to choose. That’s my first like!
    - You call it Solo. It fits.

#### `sprout:s12-wall` · [main](../../../side/S12.md#s12-wall)

_The old wall hums. Put your hand on it. Buzzzz!_  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-PS4-1, do): sounds are made by shaking (vibration)  
> _(Hum a long hmmm. Touch your throat. Can you feel the buzz?)_  
> _Sounds are made by things shaking very fast. That’s the buzz!_  
> Check: `edu:hum:feel:throat`
> _For grown-ups: Felt their throat buzz while humming: sounds are made by vibrations._

_It smells a little of honey._  
_Every crack has a tiny round door. Every door has a face._  
**The Thrums**: Hello, Keeper. Thank you for bringing the one home.  
**The Thrums**: The one is welcome. Wait. We are checking we all agree.  
_A long, buzzy pause._  
**The Thrums**: We agree! Welcome! Sorry. We are very many.  
_But Solo climbs up your sleeve instead. And stays there._  
**Solo**: In a minute. I’ll go in a minute.  

#### `sprout:s12-flat` · [main](../../../side/S12.md#s12-flat)

_Next morning, Pixel is at the wall. She has a tuning fork._  
**Pixel**: I tune my lantern clock to this wall. It always hums one note.  
_She taps the fork. Ding! It sings. The wall sings back, a bit lower._  

> **Lesson** (music, do): high and low notes  
> _(Sing a high note together: laaa! Now a low note: looo.)_  
> **Pixel**: High and low! The wall went a little bit lower.  
> Check: `edu:sing:notes:high-low`
> _For grown-ups: Sang a high note and a low note and heard the difference._

**Pixel**: It’s gone flat! Walls don’t do that!  
**The Thrums**: Sorry. It’s our Queen-Mother. She is getting quieter.  
**The Thrums**: Soon, half of us will fly away to a new home.  
**Pixel**: That’s the saddest thing a wall ever said to me.  
_In your sleeve, Solo has gone very still._  

#### `sprout:s12-queen` · [main](../../../side/S12.md#s12-queen)

_At dusk, you lie down with your ear on the bottom stone._  
_Deep inside, one slow note. Hmmmm. Like a big, sleepy bee._  
**The Queen-Mother**: Ah. The Keeper. And the little one in your sleeve.  
**The Queen-Mother**: I am the middle of the hum. It’s a bit lonely here.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): helping someone who feels lonely  
> _The Queen-Mother feels lonely. What helps when someone is lonely?_  
> - ✅ A friend to talk to → Yes. You’re here, talking to me. I feel less lonely already.
> - Being told to cheer up → “Cheer up!” Pop! It doesn’t pop the sad feeling. That doesn’t help much. A friend who listens helps most.
> - Going away → Going away? Whoosh! Then I’d be even lonelier. Then I’d feel lonelier! A friend nearby helps.
> _For grown-ups: Thought about what helps a lonely friend: company and listening._

**The Queen-Mother**: Soon, half will go. The little one must choose.  
**The Queen-Mother**: Go, or stay. Don’t choose for it. Let it choose.  
_Solo makes a tiny sound. Peep? Like a question._  

#### `sprout:s12-dark` · [main](../../../side/S12.md#s12-dark)

_Solo won’t go into the wall. So you go to Luna’s garden._  
_It is night. The paths shine a little in the dark._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): day and night animals  
> **Luna**: Some animals wake up at night. Which one?  
> - ✅ An owl → Yes! Lots of owls wake up at night. So do moths and bats.
> - A butterfly → A butterfly at night? Snore! It’s fast asleep. Butterflies rest at night. Owls, moths and bats wake up!
> - A hen → A hen at midnight? Cluck! She’s snug in bed. Hens go to bed at night! Owls, moths and bats wake up.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that some animals, like owls, moths and bats, are awake at night._

_Pop! A night flower opens beside you._  
**Solo**: In the hum, I’m warm all over. Nothing is scary.  
**Solo**: Out here, the dark is big. I feel scared.  
**Solo**: But I want to see what happens next. Is that greedy?  
**Luna**: I garden at night. Some nights I’m a bit scared too.  
**Luna**: I come out anyway. Wanting to see isn’t greedy. It’s brave.  

#### `sprout:s12-swarm-goes` · [main](../../../side/S12.md#s12-swarm-goes)

_You wake up on the bench. The morning is warm and still._  
_Across the Grove, the old wall ROARS!_  
_Bronze Thrums pour out of every crack, sparkling in the sun._  
**The Thrums**: Goodbye! Some of us are leaving. We are deciding which!  
_When a bee family gets too big, some fly off. That’s a swarm._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.1, ask): taking away leaves fewer  
> **Pixel**: Lots fly out. Now, are there more or fewer in the wall?  
> - ✅ Fewer → Yes! When some go away, there are fewer left.
> - More → More? Buzz, buzz! Some flew away, so there are fewer. They flew away, so there are fewer. Fewer left behind.
> _For grown-ups: Understood that when some of a group leave, fewer are left._

**The Thrums**: Sorry it’s slow. We’re trying to be quick and polite.  
**Solo**: I want to see where they go. Just see.  
**Pixel**: Come on. I’ve got my tuning fork!  

#### `sprout:s12-follow` · [main](../../../side/S12.md#s12-follow)

_Pixel gives you her tuning fork. PROPERTY OF PIXEL, it says._  
**Pixel**: Tap it on your knee. Hold it up. Turn slowly.  
**Pixel**: When it buzzes in your fingers, that’s them. Walk that way.  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, do): our ears can tell where a sound comes from  
> _(Close your eyes. Your grown-up says “buzz” from somewhere.)_  
> _(Point to where the sound came from!)_  
> **Pixel**: Good ears! Ears can tell where a sound comes from.  
> Check: `edu:point:sound:where-from`
> _For grown-ups: Listened with eyes closed and pointed to where a sound came from._

**Pixel**: And don’t walk into the stream. People do.  
**Pixel**: Not me. Other people.  

#### `sprout:s12-settled` · [main](../../../side/S12.md#s12-settled)

_The fork leads you over the stream to Hazel’s crooked tree._  
_The swarm hangs from a branch, like a big pear. Buzz, buzz!_  
**Pixel**: It was never one note! It’s hundreds of tiny hums together!  
**The Thrums**: We are a new we. Solo, will you come in?  
**Solo**: Keeper. I don’t know what to do.  
**Solo**: Sit with me. No. Tell me. No. Both?  
_You sit down beside Solo. You wait for Solo to choose._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Responsible decision-making, ask): making a hard choice slowly, with a friend  
> _Solo can’t decide. What helps when you can’t decide?_  
> - ✅ Take your time, with a friend → Slowly. With you here. Yes. That helps.
> - Hurry up and pick! → Hurry up! Zoom, wobble! My wings go all wibbly. Hurrying makes my wings wobble. Slowly, with a friend, helps.
> - Let someone else pick → Let someone else pick? Pfft! It’s my choice. The Queen-Mother said I must choose. With a friend beside me.
> _For grown-ups: Thought about making a hard choice: slowly, with a friend beside you._


**Choices**

- **Solo picks the hum. You nod.**
    - You carry Solo back to the old wall.
    - **Solo**: Will you remember this for me?
    - Solo walks in through a little round door.
    - **Pixel**: Listen. The hum sounds just right again.
- **Solo picks a home of its own. You nod.**
    - You find a little hole in the gatepost. It looks at the pond.
    - **Solo**: Mine! I chose it!
    - Every evening, one tiny hum comes from the gatepost.
- **Solo asks the hum for a little room. You nod.**
    - **Solo**: Could Thrums go out, and come back in?
    - **The Thrums**: We will talk about it.
    - They talk until lunch. Pixel eats two sandwiches. Then yours.
    - **The Thrums**: We agree! Ones may go out, and come back in.
    - **Solo**: In for supper. Out for the stars. Yes!

#### `sprout:s12-complete` · [main](../../../side/S12.md#s12-complete)

_A week later, the Queen-Mother dies, in the night._  
_She was very, very old. Her body stopped working._  
_Her whole family hummed around her. She wasn’t alone._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): feeling sad when someone dies; remembering together  
> _It’s all right to feel sad when someone dies._  
> _Remembering them together helps._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that sadness after a death is normal, and that remembering together helps._

_In the morning, there’s a present on your doorstep._  
_A little clay hive. It hums a tune you know!_  
**The Thrums**: For the Keeper. We taught it to hum. We hope that’s not rude.  

> **Lesson** (music, do): speaking in chorus, at the same time  
> _(Say “hello” at the very same time as your grown-up. Ready?)_  
> _Together, like the Thrums! That’s called a chorus._  
> Check: `edu:say:chorus:hello`
> _For grown-ups: Said a word in chorus, at the same time as their grown-up._

_Beside it is a thimble of golden nectar. It smells of summer._  
**Pixel**: I’ve changed my lantern clock. It listens to the whole crowd now.  

**Notes:** All choices kept. The Queen-Mother’s death is said plainly and gently in s12-complete. No lesson depends on the letters of the name “Solo”, so the “own” renaming is safe.
