# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S14: Tilly’s Tabletop

## S14. Tilly’s Tabletop

- `s14-step1`: Play Tilly’s game: the Bog of Mild Peril. · _Roll the dice when Tilly says. Count the dots!_
- `s14-step2`: Get up the Tower of the Tax Toad. · _The Toad wants shells for everything. Try paying with silly things!_
- `s14-step3`: Find Tilly. · _Listen for her whistle: low, then high._
- `s14-step4`: Get past the Grey Grandmother’s geese. · _Roll the dice three times. You can’t lose. The worst thing that happens is: you get a goose._
- `s14-step5`: Sit next to the dragon, and wait. · _Put the dice down. Don’t move. Just wait._

#### `sprout:s14-table` · [main](../../../side/S14.md#s14-table)

_Rain on the window. Pitter, patter._  
_In comes Sparks, with a small girl in big wellies._  
**Sparks**: This is Tilly. My cousin. She’s nine. She’s the boss now. Sorry!  
**Tilly**: I’m the Game Mistress. It’s like a boss, but more.  
_She spreads a felt-tip map on Hazel’s table._  
_Then the dice. Cardboard, cut by hand, with tape on the corners._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 6  
> _(A die has six sides. Count them: one, two, three, four, five, six.)_  
> **Tilly**: Five sides have dots. The sixth side just says LOTS.  
> Check: `edu:count:dice-sides:6`
> _For grown-ups: Counted the six sides of a die._

**Tilly**: Rule one: the Game Mistress is always right. Rule two: see rule one.  
**Tilly**: Rule three: snacks at the end. Only at the end.  
**Tilly**: Pick your hero. Ten seconds. Nine. Four!  

**Choices**

- **A knight with a slightly bent sword.**
    - **Tilly**: Sir Bent Sword! Bent swords can hit round corners.
- **A wizard who mostly makes soup.**
    - **Tilly**: Soup Wizard! Your spells are hot. Blow on them first.
- **A sneaky hero dressed as a hat stand.**
    - **Tilly**: Nobody suspects a hat stand. Not even other hat stands.

#### `sprout:s14-bog` · [main](../../../side/S14.md#s14-bog)

_The Bog of Mild Peril is the pond, coloured green._  
_A felt-tip frog sits in it. He does not look impressed._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS3-1, ask): animal homes: frogs live in ponds  
> **Tilly**: Where do real frogs like to live?  
> - ✅ In ponds and wet places → Yes! Frogs need water. Ponds are perfect frog homes.
> - In a dry desert → Too dry! Frogs need water. They live in ponds and wet places.
> - Up a chimney → Ha! No. Frogs live in ponds and wet places.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that frogs live in ponds and wet places because they need water._

**Sparks**: I ATTACK THE BOG!  
**Tilly**: You can’t attack a bog. Roll anyway.  
_Sparks rolls. One dot._  
**Tilly**: A one! Your sword is now a cheese sandwich.  
**Byte**: Actually, that’s not how—  
**Tilly**: Rule four! Say “actually”, lose a point.  
_You roll a LOTS. The bog likes you. Everybody gets across!_  
**Tilly**: Game over. Nobody’s hurt. Somebody’s a sandwich.  

#### `sprout:s14-tower` · [main](../../../side/S14.md#s14-tower)

_Next day there’s a new tower on the map._  
**Tilly**: The Tower of the Tax Toad! He makes you pay for everything.  
**Tilly**: Ribbit! That’s two shells for the ribbit.  
**Tilly**: And one shell for the stairs!  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.2, ask): add 1 more  
> **Tilly**: Two shells for the ribbit. One for the stairs. How many shells?  
> - ✅ 3 → Three shells! Two and one more make three.
> - 2 → Count with me: one, two… and one more. Three!
> - 5 → Too many! Two and one more make three.
> _For grown-ups: Added two shells and one more shell to make three._

**Pixel**: Can I build a machine to beat him?  
**Tilly**: Roll. Three dots. Your machine is a toad now. It works for him.  
**Byte**: Is there a tax on paying the tax?  
**Tilly**: …Yes. Thanks SO much, Byte.  

#### `sprout:s14-toad` · [main](../../../side/S14.md#s14-toad)

**Byte**: The Toad owes… no, we owe… I’ve muddled it.  
**Tilly**: The Toad doesn’t do sums. The Toad does feelings.  
_Then Pixel’s Nubbin wakes up. It climbs onto the map._  
_Chomp! It eats the cardboard Tax Toad._  
**Tilly**: The Toad has been eaten! No more paying for anyone!  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds: t  
> **Tilly**: Tilly. Tax. Toad. They all start with the same sound. T-t-t!  
> **Tilly**: Which one starts with t too?  
> - ✅ Table → T-t-table! Yes!
> - Frog → F-f-frog starts with f. Listen: t-t-table. Table!
> - Map → M-m-map starts with m. T-t-table starts with t!
> _For grown-ups: Heard the “t” sound at the start of Tilly, Tax and Toad, and found another t word._

**Sparks**: BEST GAME EVER!  
_Tilly laughs so much she lies on the floor._  
_Then she stops. She looks at one corner of the map._  
_It’s coloured grey. So hard, it went right through the paper._  

#### `sprout:s14-sparks` · [main](../../../side/S14.md#s14-sparks)

_Next morning, Sparks is at your gate. Being quiet. Sparks is never quiet!_  
**Sparks**: The dragon in the corner of the map keeps getting bigger.  
**Sparks**: Last night it got a cardigan. It’s Hazel. Of course it’s Hazel.  
**Sparks**: Hazel went away on a long trip. She didn’t say goodbye.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): missing someone: two feelings at once  
> **Sparks**: Tilly misses Hazel. She feels sad and cross at the same time.  
> _Missing someone can feel like that. Both feelings are all right._  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that missing someone can make us feel sad and cross at once, and that both are all right._

**Sparks**: Hazel left Tilly a note. She won’t open it. It’s in her welly.  
**Sparks**: Go and find her. She likes you. You’re not a sandwich.  

#### `sprout:s14-gate` · [main](../../../side/S14.md#s14-gate)

_You hear a whistle. Low, then going up high. Wheee-oo!_  

> **Lesson** (music, do): low and high sounds  
> _(Make the supper call with your voice: start low, then slide up high! Wheee-oo!)_  
> _Low… then high! That’s how Hazel did it._  
> Check: `edu:sing:supper-call:low-high`
> _For grown-ups: Made a sound that slides from low to high, like Hazel’s supper whistle._

_Every Nubbin in the Grove looks up._  
_Tilly sits on the gate that sticks, kicking it._  
**Tilly**: They think it’s supper. It isn’t. I just do it.  
**Tilly**: Hazel taught me. She said whoever loves you comes running.  
_The Nubbins sit in a row in front of her wellies._  
**Tilly**: See. It works on them.  

**Choices**

- **Ask her about Hazel.**
    - **Tilly**: I’m not talking about her.
    - A Nubbin licks her welly.
    - **Tilly**: She didn’t say goodbye. You’re supposed to say goodbye!
    - She takes out the note. She looks at it. She puts it back.
    - **Tilly**: Come and play. I’ve got a dragon to do.
- **Ask her about the dragon.**
    - **Tilly**: She’s the Grey Grandmother. Very old. Very rude.
    - **Tilly**: She flew away without saying goodbye. She keeps biscuit tins.
    - **Tilly**: She’s the last boss. Come on.

#### `sprout:s14-lair` · [main](../../../side/S14.md#s14-lair)

_The last game. Curtains shut. A torch under a bowl, for spooky light._  
**Tilly**: The Lair of the Grey Grandmother. First, get past her geese!  
**Sparks**: I HIT THE GOOSE WITH MY SANDWICH!  
**Tilly**: Roll. LOTS! The goose eats the sandwich. Now it’s your friend.  
_Then you roll. LOTS again! Another goose is your friend._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.1, ask): add 1 more  
> **Tilly**: One friendly goose. Then one more. How many geese?  
> - ✅ 2 → Two geese! One and one more make two. Honk, honk!
> - 1 → One… and one more! That makes two. Honk, honk!
> - 4 → Not that many! One and one more make two.
> _For grown-ups: Added one goose and one more goose to make two._

**Byte**: Do I still have seven points?  
**Tilly**: Four. You said “do” in an “actually” voice.  

#### `sprout:s14-dragon` · [main](../../../side/S14.md#s14-dragon)

_Tilly lifts a cereal box off the corner. Under it is the dragon._  
_She’s cut from a biscuit-tin lid. Grey, with a felt-tip cardigan._  
**Tilly**: The Grey Grandmother. She flew away. She’s the boss.  
**Sparks**: Right! I roll to hit the—  
_Tilly grabs the dice. Rrrip! One tears._  
**Tilly**: No! Nobody hits her. Nobody.  
_The kitchen goes very quiet. The rain has stopped._  
**Tilly**: I don’t know how it ends. Not if nobody can hit her.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): helping a friend who is upset  
> _Tilly is upset. She doesn’t know what to do._  
> _What can a friend do?_  
> - ✅ Sit close by, and wait → Yes. Sometimes the kindest thing is to sit close, and wait.
> - Run away → Running away leaves her on her own. Sitting close, and waiting, helps more.
> _For grown-ups: Thought about how to help an upset friend: stay close and wait with them._


**Choices**

- **“My hero puts down the sword and sits by the dragon.”**
    - **Tilly**: That’s not a move.
    - **Tilly**: …What for?
    - **You**: Hazel’s third rule. When you don’t know what to do, sit and wait.
- **Roll to see if your hero can sit down.**
    - You roll the torn die. It lands on the torn side. No number!
    - **Tilly**: That’s… not a number.
    - **You**: Then you decide. My hero would like to sit next to the dragon.
    - Tilly looks at the torn die for a long time.
    - **Tilly**: The dice says… the dragon says yes.
- **Ask the Game Mistress what the dragon wants.**
    - **Tilly**: She doesn’t WANT anything. She just WENT.
    - You don’t say anything. Tilly looks at the dragon for a long time.
    - **Tilly**: …She wants someone to sit with her. Fine. FINE.

#### `sprout:s14-wait` · [main](../../../side/S14.md#s14-wait)

_You move your button to the dragon’s square. You leave it there._  
**Tilly**: She doesn’t do anything. She just sits there.  
**You**: That’s all right. We’re waiting.  
_Sparks puts a button next to yours. Then Pixel. Then Byte._  
**Byte**: Is there a rule against it?  
**Tilly**: …No.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 4  
> _(Count the buttons round the dragon: one, two, three, four.)_  
> _Four friends, sitting and waiting together._  
> Check: `edu:count:buttons:4`
> _For grown-ups: Counted four buttons waiting beside the dragon._

_A Nubbin taps the window. It hopes it’s supper._  

#### `sprout:s14-tears` · [main](../../../side/S14.md#s14-tears)

**Tilly**: The dragon says—  
_Tilly picks up the dragon. She holds it in both hands._  
**Tilly**: She says she had something important to do.  
**Tilly**: She didn’t say goodbye, because she would have cried.  
**Tilly**: I don’t know what else she says. She didn’t SAY!  
_Tilly cries with her whole face, all at once._  
_Sparks comes round the table and hugs her. Not a word._  
_Pixel passes a tea towel. Byte passes a better one._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, notice): crying is all right; comfort helps  
> _It’s all right to cry. Crying lets the sad out._  
> _A quiet hug, and friends nearby, help a lot._  
> _For grown-ups: Saw friends comfort someone who was crying, and heard that crying is all right._

_You keep your button right where it is._  

#### `sprout:s14-complete` · [main](../../../side/S14.md#s14-complete)

**Tilly**: Game over. Snacks for everyone. That was always a rule.  
_Crisps, Fern’s burnt biscuits, and grapes the Nubbins already found._  
_Tilly eats with the dragon in her lap._  
**Tilly**: The Grey Grandmother isn’t the boss now. You can visit her.  
**Tilly**: And Byte gets all his points back. For the tea towel.  
_She puts her cardboard crown on your head. GAME MISTRESS!_  
_Later, you look at the map. The grey corner is coloured yellow now._  

> **Lesson** (art, ask): naming colours  
> _The corner was grey. What colour is it now?_  
> - ✅ Yellow → Yellow! A bright, warm colour, like sunshine.
> - Grey → It was grey. Now Tilly has coloured it yellow, bright as sunshine.
> - Green → The bog is green! The corner is yellow now.
> _For grown-ups: Named the colour yellow, and saw how Tilly changed a sad grey corner into a bright one._

_It says: HERE BE HAZEL. SHE IS ALLOWED._  
_Out by the gate, someone practises the supper call. Wheee-oo!_  

**Notes:** S14 sprout. The cardboard dice are the counting thread: sides of a die (6), shells (2 + 1), geese (1 + 1) and buttons (4), with other domains in between. Hazel is away on a trip at this point in the story (true: she is in the Hollow), so Tilly’s feelings are about missing her and not getting a goodbye. Never say where Hazel is. Hazel left Tilly a short note (not a letter), which stays folded in her welly. Tilly is not Hazel’s grandchild, so the map corner says HERE BE HAZEL. Only the “sit” choice names Hazel’s third rule; “roll” ends with the dice and “ask” with Tilly working it out. All choices kept.
