# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S16: The Workyard

## S16. The Workyard

- `s16-step1`: Walk up the river with Sparks and Clover to the Workyard. · _Follow the river path. Listen for the saws: bzzz!_
- `s16-step2`: Watch the Workyard Nubbins quietly. What do they like to do? · _Hide behind the log piles. If a Nubbin sees you, it works to be polite. Just try again!_
- `s16-step3`: Make a Fair Roster. Each Nubbin picks the job it likes. · _Put each Nubbin’s card under the job it chose. Then hold Sparks’s ladder._
- `s16-step4`: Go back to the Workyard in a week. How is it going? · _Wait seven days. Then follow the river path. Brisk will have the kettle on._

#### `sprout:s16-sparks` · [main](../../../side/S16.md#s16-sparks)

_Sparks arrives with wire looped over both shoulders._  
**Sparks**: Job up the river! A sawmill needs lights!  
**Sparks**: I need someone to hold the ladder and say “wow”.  
**Clover**: I’m not good with ladders. I’m good at not falling off.  
_You can hear the saws far away. Buzz! Like wasps._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.1.4, do): sound words  
> _(Make the saw sound together: bzzzzz!)_  
> **Sparks**: Bzzz! That’s a sound word. Say it loud!  
> Check: `edu:say:sound-word:bzzz`
> _For grown-ups: Said a sound word (“bzzz”) that sounds like what it names._

**Sparks**: Brisk says his Nubbins love it there. He wrote it twice!  
**Clover**: Twice is a lot.  

#### `sprout:s16-yard` · [main](../../../side/S16.md#s16-yard)

_The Workyard! Sheds, logs and lots of busy Nubbins._  
_Each one wears a tiny yellow hard hat._  
_A sign says: WORK IS PLAY!_  
_Squeak, squeak! Three Nubbins run inside a big wooden wheel. Round and round._  
**Brisk Hollins**: Welcome! I’m Brisk Hollins! My Nubbins love working!  
_A big Nubbin carries a log on his back. He hums. That’s Lug._  
**Brisk Hollins**: Lug’s my best worker. I can’t stop him!  
_Behind the logs sit five Nubbins. Their colours look faded._  
**Clover**: Those ones are tired. Not lazy. Tired.  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, ask): tired bodies need rest  
> **Clover**: What does a tired body need?  
> - ✅ Rest → Yes. Rest helps a tired body feel better.
> - More work → More work? Puff, huff! The tired ones would flop flat. More work makes tired bodies more tired. Rest helps.
> - Run faster → Run faster? Puff, puff! A tired body would flop. Running makes us puff! A tired body needs rest.
> _For grown-ups: Learned that when our bodies are tired, they need rest._


#### `sprout:s16-shadow` · [main](../../../side/S16.md#s16-shadow)

**Brisk Hollins**: Tired? Nonsense! Look at my chart!  
_Whoosh! His chart line goes up so high it runs off the paper!_  
**Clover**: That chart is about logs. I’m asking about Nubbins.  
**Sparks**: Brisk, what do they do when nobody’s watching?  
_Brisk opens his mouth. Then he shuts it. He doesn’t know!_  
**Brisk Hollins**: Good question! Go and find out!  
**Clover**: Hide where they can’t see you. Or they’ll work to be polite.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, do): position words: behind, beside, under  
> _(Where can you hide? Behind the log pile! Point behind you.)_  
> **Clover**: Behind the logs. Beside the shed. Under the bench. Good hiding places.  
> Check: `edu:point:position:behind`
> _For grown-ups: Used the position words behind, beside and under._


**Choices**

- **Watch Lug first.**
    - Lug sees you. He picks up a bigger log, to show off!
    - You’ll need to be sneakier.
- **Watch the tired ones first.**
    - **Clover**: Good. Start with the ones nobody’s counting.
- **Watch the big wheel first.**
    - **Sparks**: That wheel! It’s wired backwards, for a start.

#### `sprout:s16-notes` · [main](../../../side/S16.md#s16-notes)

_By sunset, your clipboard is full._  
_The choppers chop even on their breaks. They like the thump!_  
_The wheel runners sneak out and hide under the bench._  
_The five tired ones just lie in the sun._  
_And Lug carries little planks out to the reeds._  
_You follow him. In the reeds is a tiny house!_  
_The five tired Nubbins are asleep inside._  
**Clover**: He built them a house!  
**Sparks**: Nobody asked him to. He just did.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): noticing kindness and how others feel  
> **Clover**: How do the tired Nubbins feel in their little house?  
> - ✅ Safe and cosy → Yes. Lug made them feel safe. That’s real kindness.
> - Cross → Cross faces, all snoring? Hee, hee! I don’t think so. They’re snoozing! They feel safe and cosy.
> - Scared → Scared, and snuggled in a heap? Hee, no! Look how they snuggle. They feel safe. Lug was kind.
> _For grown-ups: Noticed a kind act and thought about how it made others feel._


#### `sprout:s16-roster` · [main](../../../side/S16.md#s16-roster)

_You show Brisk the clipboard. And Lug’s little house._  
**Brisk Hollins**: I thought they loved it. They were always so busy.  
**Brisk Hollins**: Busy isn’t the same as happy, is it?  
_Creak! He takes down WORK IS PLAY. He puts up a new sign._  
_It says: WORK IS… OFTEN PLAY!_  
**Sparks**: That’s worse.  
**Brisk Hollins**: I know. I’m working on it.  
**Clover**: Each Nubbin gets a card. They pick the job they like.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.B.3, do): sorting into groups  
> _(Help sort the cards! Choppers here. Stackers there. Resters over here.)_  
> **Clover**: Every Nubbin in its own group. Now let’s count each group!  
> Check: `edu:sort:cards:jobs`
> _For grown-ups: Sorted job cards into groups and counted each group._

**Sparks**: And the big wheel comes off! The water wheel can work the lights.  

#### `sprout:s16-bell` · [main](../../../side/S16.md#s16-bell)

_The Fair Roster hangs by the gate. A card for every Nubbin._  
_Next to it hangs a little bell. Low down, so everyone can reach._  
_Clover paints on it: NO THANK YOU._  
_The big wheel stops. A Nubbin is asleep inside it._  
_A wheel runner walks to the bell. It rings it, softly. Ding._  

> **Lesson** (music, do): soft and loud sounds  
> _(Ring a pretend bell softly: ding. Now loudly: DING!)_  
> **Clover**: Soft and loud. A soft ding says “No, thank you” nicely.  
> Check: `edu:ring:bell:soft-loud`
> _For grown-ups: Made a pretend bell sound softly and then loudly._

_Then it sits in the sun. Nobody stops it!_  
**Brisk Hollins**: Well I never! Fewer logs! Happier Nubbins!  

#### `sprout:s16-question` · [main](../../../side/S16.md#s16-question)

_Brisk walks you to the river path. Splish, splash!_  
**Brisk Hollins**: Can I ask you something, Keeper?  
**Brisk Hollins**: Your Nubbins fetch you apples. Do you ask them first?  
_You think about your Nubbins. They bring you apples. They look happy._  
**You**: I don’t know.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): asking first; letting others say yes or no  
> **Brisk Hollins**: Before a friend helps you, what can you say?  
> - ✅ “Would you like to help?” → Yes! Ask first. Then they can say yes, or no thank you.
> - “Do it now!” → “Do it now!” Squeak! That sounds like a bossy trumpet. That’s a bit bossy, isn’t it? Asking first is kinder.
> - Say nothing → Say nothing? Shh… and nobody gets a say. Then they never get to choose. Ask first!
> _For grown-ups: Practised asking before someone helps, so they can choose yes or no._

**Brisk Hollins**: Me neither. Now, the five tired ones. What shall I do?  

**Choices**

- **Bring them to the Grove to rest.**
    - **Brisk Hollins**: Yes. That’s kind. Lug will want to visit.
    - Lug picks up their whole little house, with them inside!
- **Keep them here as the cheer-up team.**
    - **Brisk Hollins**: Cheer-up team! Their job is sitting in the sun, being lovely!
    - **Sparks**: Is that just a job with a nicer hat?
- **Let them go and live by the river.**
    - **Brisk Hollins**: By the river? Who’ll look after them?
    - **Clover**: They can look after themselves. Some Nubbins like being free.
    - Brisk looks up the river. He doesn’t say no.

#### `sprout:s16-week` · [main](../../../side/S16.md#s16-week)

_A week later, the Workyard is louder. And slower._  
_Everybody stops for fruit, morning and afternoon._  
_Someone has hung up a hammock! Creak, creak!_  
_The new sign says: ASK FIRST._  
**Brisk Hollins**: Fewer logs. But the lights stay on! The water wheel does it.  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): moving water can push and turn things  
> **Sparks**: The river pushes the water wheel round and round.  
> **Sparks**: The turning wheel makes the lights work. Water power!  
> _For grown-ups: Heard how moving river water pushes a wheel round, and the wheel can power lights._

**Sparks**: I knew it. I said so. Loudly.  
_Lug walks past with a log, humming. He just likes it._  

#### `sprout:s16-complete` · [main](../../../side/S16.md#s16-complete)

**Brisk Hollins**: Here! Every Keeper needs a hard hat.  
_Plop! A yellow hard hat, just your size._  
_On the brim, in Lug’s pencil: KEEPER._  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds: k  
> **Sparks**: KEEPER! What sound does KEEPER start with?  
> - ✅ “k”, like kite → Yes! K-k-keeper. Like kite and kitten!
> - “s”, like sun → Keeper in a sun hat? Hee, hee! Listen: k, k, kite. Listen: k-k-keeper. It starts with k, like kite!
> - “m”, like Moss → Keeper like Moss? Hee! Moss starts with mmm. M is for Moss! Keeper starts with k-k-k.
> _For grown-ups: Heard the first sound “k” in the word “Keeper”._

**Brisk Hollins**: Try the Fair Roster at the Grove. Let them pick.  
_Back home, you hang the roster by the door._  
_By morning, three Nubbins have picked jobs._  
_Watering. Fetching apples. And sitting with Moss._  
**Sparks**: That last one isn’t a job!  
**Clover**: It is to Moss.  

**Notes:** All choices kept. Brisk’s Circuit line is left out for this band. The tired Nubbins are “faded”, never “grey”.
