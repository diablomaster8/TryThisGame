# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S17: Nightjar

## S17. Nightjar

- `s17-step1`: Look for clues around the nursery. · _Look at the straw, the roof and the top of the fence. Show Luna what you find._
- `s17-step2`: Creep quietly through Luna’s garden to the hiding place. · _Walk on the soft moss, not the crunchy gravel. Move when the wind blows the leaves._
- `s17-step3`: Climb up to Nightjar’s nest, slowly. · _Hold on where the ferns grow. Stop when she turns her head._
- `s17-step4`: Carry the eggs home, one at a time. · _Hold each egg in both hands. Walk, don’t run._
- `s17-step5`: Visit Nightjar at night, when the little egg hatches. · _Go up the cliff path after dark. Sit and wait._

#### `sprout:s17-missing` · [main](../../../side/S17.md#s17-missing)

_Autumn. Shiny conkers lie on the ground._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): signs of autumn: conkers and falling leaves  
> **Luna**: Conkers fall in autumn. What else falls off trees in autumn?  
> - ✅ Leaves → Yes! Leaves fall in autumn. Crunch, crunch!
> - Snowflakes → Snow comes later, in winter. In autumn, leaves fall. Crunch!
> _For grown-ups: Named signs of autumn: conkers and falling leaves._

_In the straw is an empty, warm dip. An egg is gone!_  
**Luna**: One egg a night. Always the warmest one.  
**Luna**: Never a crack. Whoever it is, they’re careful.  
**Nova**: Morning! I’m not stopping. I heard there’s a mystery.  
**Nova**: Badger.  
**Luna**: It’s not a badger.  
**Nova**: A tall badger.  
**Luna**: Let’s look for clues first. Gently.  

#### `sprout:s17-clues` · [main](../../../side/S17.md#s17-clues)

_On the fence: a big grey feather. As long as your arm!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, do): comparing lengths: longer, shorter, the same  
> _(Stretch out your arm. Is the feather longer, shorter, or the same?)_  
> **Luna**: The same! This feather is as long as your arm.  
> Check: `edu:compare:length:feather-arm`
> _For grown-ups: Compared two lengths using the words longer, shorter and the same._

_On the roof: tiny scratches. Something landed very gently._  
_The dip in the straw is still warm._  
**Luna**: That’s an owl feather. A really big owl.  
**Nova**: Two badgers in a coat, with an owl feather!  
**Luna**: Nova.  
**Nova**: Fine. Tonight, then. I’m not stopping. I’m just staying up.  

#### `sprout:s17-night` · [main](../../../side/S17.md#s17-night)

_At night, Luna’s garden glows. The white flowers are open._  
**Luna**: We hide in here and watch the nursery.  
**Luna**: Stay on the soft moss. The gravel goes crunch!  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RL.1.4, do): loud and quiet sound words  
> _(Say it with Luna: gravel goes CRUNCH. Moss goes… shhh.)_  
> **Luna**: Crunch is a loud word. Shhh is a quiet word.  
> Check: `edu:say:sound-words:crunch-shh`
> _For grown-ups: Said loud and quiet sound words: “crunch” and “shhh”._

**Luna**: If something lands, don’t stare. Owls don’t like it. Nor do I.  
**Nova**: Can I bring my list?  
**Luna**: In your head. Quietly.  

**Choices**

- **Follow Luna’s footsteps exactly.**
    - **Luna**: Good. Step on the thyme. It smells nice.
- **Find your own way.**
    - Crack! You step on an empty snail shell. Everyone freezes.
    - **Luna**: …Your own way is very loud.

#### `sprout:s17-reveal` · [main](../../../side/S17.md#s17-reveal)

_Midnight. Something flies across the moon. Not a sound._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): owls fly quietly on soft feathers  
> **Luna**: Owls have soft, fluffy feathers.  
> **Luna**: That makes their flying very, very quiet.  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that owls’ soft feathers let them fly almost silently._

_A huge grey owl lands on the nursery roof._  
_She lifts an egg very gently in her feet._  
_She turns her head nearly all the way round. She looks at you._  
**Nightjar**: Hoo-oo.  
_Then she flies off to the cliffs, holding the egg._  
**Nova**: …I’m crossing off the badgers.  
**Luna**: That’s Nightjar. Hazel told me about her.  
**Luna**: The biggest owl in the valley. And the saddest.  

#### `sprout:s17-cliffs` · [main](../../../side/S17.md#s17-cliffs)

_Next night, you follow her to the high cliffs._  
_Halfway up is a nest. It’s as big as a cart wheel!_  
**Nova**: I slept up here once. An owl called all night. Two notes.  
**Luna**: She’ll go hunting soon. We could climb up then.  
**Luna**: Or we could wait, and let her see us coming.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): being gentle with a shy animal  
> **Luna**: How do we meet a shy animal?  
> - ✅ Slowly and quietly → Yes. Slow and quiet, so she isn’t scared.
> - Run up fast → That might scare her. Slow and quiet is kinder.
> - Shout hello → Loud sounds scare shy animals. Slow and quiet is better.
> _For grown-ups: Thought about how to be gentle and calm around a shy animal._


**Choices**

- **Climb while she’s out hunting.**
    - You climb up, hand over hand.
    - Whoosh! A quiet shadow. She’s home already.
    - **Nova**: She was always going to be home, wasn’t she.
- **Wait where she can see you.**
    - You sit on a rock below the nest. She comes home.
    - She looks at you a long time. Then she moves over, a little.
    - **Luna**: I think that means come up. Slowly.

#### `sprout:s17-nest` · [main](../../../side/S17.md#s17-nest)

_The nest is made of heather, wool and soft grey feathers._  
_Under Nightjar are eggs. All whole. All warm._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.1, do): count to 5; 4 and 1 make 5  
> _(Count the eggs together.)_  
> _One, two, three, four, five. Five eggs!_  
> _Four glow, like Grove eggs. One doesn’t. Four and one make five._  
> Check: `edu:count:eggs:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five eggs and saw that four and one more make five._

_Deep in the nest are two old, cracked eggshells._  
**Luna**: Hazel told me. Long ago, a cold night came in spring.  
**Luna**: Nightjar’s own two eggs got too cold. The babies inside died.  
**Luna**: They never hatched. She has been sad ever since.  
_Now she keeps other eggs warm. It helps her feel a little better._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, notice): understanding grief; being gentle with someone sad  
> **Luna**: Someone can stay sad for a long time when someone dies.  
> **Luna**: Being kind and gentle helps. Let’s be gentle with her.  
> _For grown-ups: Heard, gently, that grief can last a long time, and that kindness helps._

**Nightjar**: Hoo-oo.  
_She settles over the eggs, like a blanket._  

#### `sprout:s17-return` · [main](../../../side/S17.md#s17-return)

_Nightjar won’t let you take them all at once._  
_You lift two. Clack! goes her beak._  
**Luna**: One at a time, then. Let her watch each one go.  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): careful, gentle movement  
> _(Pretend to carry an egg. Two hands. Slow, gentle steps.)_  
> **Luna**: Two hands, slow feet. That’s how to carry an egg.  
> Check: `edu:carry:gently:two-hands`
> _For grown-ups: Practised moving slowly and carefully with both hands, as if carrying an egg._

**Nova**: That’s four climbs. In the dark!  
**Luna**: That’s four climbs. In the dark.  
_Nightjar watches the first egg all the way down._  

#### `sprout:s17-wild` · [main](../../../side/S17.md#s17-wild)

_Morning. Four eggs are back in the nursery._  
_One egg is still under Nightjar. It’s small and speckled._  
**Nova**: That’s a wild egg. Its mummy never came back.  
**Luna**: Let’s leave it with her. She can keep it warm.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): thinking about how someone else feels  
> **Luna**: How might Nightjar feel, with a baby egg to keep warm?  
> - ✅ Happier → Yes. Caring for someone can help a sad heart.
> - Cross → Look how gently she sits. I think she feels happier.
> _For grown-ups: Imagined how caring for someone small can help a sad heart feel better._

_On the way down, Luna stops._  
**Luna**: Next spring, she might come back to the nursery. What shall we do?  

**Choices**

- **Leave the nursery open, with a lantern for her.**
    - **Nova**: A night nurse with a beak!
    - **Luna**: She can sit on our eggs, where we can see. Like Hazel would.
- **Move the nursery inside, by the warm stove.**
    - **Luna**: That’s fair. The eggs come first.
    - **Nova**: She’ll sit on the roof and look at the chimney.
    - **Luna**: I’ll leave her a snack on the step sometimes.

#### `sprout:s17-feathers` · [main](../../../side/S17.md#s17-feathers)

_Some weeks later, a grey feather is on your windowsill._  
_The next night, there’s another one._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.1, ask): adding one more  
> _One feather, then one more. How many feathers now?_  
> - ✅ 2 → Yes! One and one more make two.
> - 3 → Let’s count: one… two. Two feathers!
> - 1 → One, and one more… two feathers!
> _For grown-ups: Added one more to one to make two._

**Luna**: She’s calling you! The little egg must be hatching.  
**Luna**: Go after dark. Just go and sit.  
**Nova**: I’m not coming. I’ll just be on the path. By accident.  

#### `sprout:s17-hatching` · [main](../../../side/S17.md#s17-hatching)

_The egg is rocking. Nightjar moves over a little._  
_Crack! A tiny beak pokes out._  
_Out comes a fluffy grey baby. All eyes. Very cross!_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): animal babies: an owlet  
> **Luna**: A baby owl has a special name. What is it?  
> - ✅ An owlet → Yes! A baby owl is an owlet.
> - A kitten → A kitten is a baby cat. A baby owl is an owlet!
> - A puppy → A puppy is a baby dog. A baby owl is an owlet!
> _For grown-ups: Learned the name for a baby owl: an owlet._

**Nightjar**: Hoo-oo. Hoo-oo.  
_The same two notes. But now they sound happy._  
_The baby owl tumbles into your lap. Nightjar lets it._  
**Luna**: She’s sharing. Look at that.  

#### `sprout:s17-complete` · [main](../../../side/S17.md#s17-complete)

_At the end of autumn, Nightjar drops her old feathers._  
_The wind blows them down into the Grove._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): birds moult: old feathers fall out, new ones grow  
> **Luna**: Birds drop their old feathers. New ones grow in their place.  
> **Luna**: It’s called moulting. Lots of birds do it every year.  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that birds moult: old feathers fall out and new ones grow._

**Luna**: I sewed them into a cloak for you!  
_It’s light as a breath. Warm as a bird._  
_Nightjar swoops down. She lifts you gently in her feet._  
_The cloak keeps you warm. Whoosh! You fly home over the valley._  
**Nova**: Show-off.  
**Nova**: …Take me next time.  

**Notes:** All choices kept. Nightjar’s lost chicks are said plainly in s17-nest. The “twenty-two years” hint is left out for this band. The snail shell in s17-night is an empty one. No ordinary frost in S17 (chapter 7 frost is the shard’s sign), so s17-missing opens with conkers.
