# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S19: Cinder’s Last Light

## S19. Cinder’s Last Light

- `s19-step1`: Ask Marina how to climb up the snowy hill. · _She’s at the rock pools, breaking the ice._
- `s19-step2`: Climb up the snowfield. · _Step in Marina’s footprints. When the wind blows hard, crouch down and wait._
- `s19-step3`: Sit by the little red light, and wait. · _Sit very still and very quiet. It takes a long time._
- `s19-step4`: Stay with Cinder. · _Stay sitting. You don’t have to do anything. Just stay._

#### `sprout:s19-warmth` · [main](../../../side/S19.md#s19-warmth)

_Two days after the Long Night. Snow everywhere. Very quiet._  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS2-1, ask): winter brings snow and cold  
> _Snow on the ground. Cold air. What season is it?_  
> - ✅ Winter → Winter. The coldest time of year.
> - Summer → Summer is warm and sunny. Snow comes in winter.
> _For grown-ups: Named winter as the snowy, cold season._

_Moss sits on the step, looking up at the high snowy hill._  
_He is very thin now. You can almost see through him._  
**Moss**: There is something warm up there.  
**Moss**: There shouldn’t be. Nothing has been warm up there for ages.  
**Moss**: I’m too thin to climb. Will you carry me in your coat?  
**Moss**: I won’t talk much.  
**Moss**: That was a fib. Ask Marina. She knows the path.

_Sable sits at the top of the garden. She hasn’t eaten her breakfast._  
_She stares up at the high snowy hill._  
_Moss faded at the Long Night. That means he died._  
_His pale light stayed with Sable. It leans up the hill too._  
_Up in the white snow there is a tiny red dot._

> _(Find something red near you. Point to it!)_

**You**: All right. We’ll ask Marina.  

#### `sprout:s19-warmth-alone` · [main](../../../side/S19.md#s19-warmth-alone)

_Variant of `s19-warmth`._

_Two days after the Long Night. Snow everywhere. Very quiet._  
_You look out of the window, with a warm drink._  
_Up on the snowy hill, there’s a tiny red dot!_  
_You look away. You look back. It’s still there._  
_Moss would want to see it. But Moss has died._  
_You miss him. You pull on your boots._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): missing someone, and what helps  
> _You miss Moss. Missing someone is a sad feeling. What can help?_  
> - ✅ Talking about them → Yes. Talking about someone we miss keeps them close. (Who do you miss? Wait for an answer.)
> - Never saying their name → Saying their name is all right. Talking about them helps us feel close.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about missing someone who has died, and how talking about them can help._

**You**: Marina knows the way. Let’s ask her!  

#### `sprout:s19-marina` · [main](../../../side/S19.md#s19-marina)

_Marina is in a rock pool, breaking the ice. She’s very happy._  
**Marina**: The high pass? In winter? Lovely! It’s freezing up there. I love it.  
**Marina**: All our water comes down from that snow. I know the way.  
**Marina**: Boots. A rope. Two pairs of socks.  
**Marina**: Go slowly. Be careful.  
**Marina**: Two pairs. I will check!  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): dressing warmly for cold weather  
> _(What keeps us warm in the snow?)_  
> _(Point to your feet, your hands and your head.)_  
> **Marina**: Socks for feet. Mittens for hands. A hat for your head!  
> Check: `edu:point:clothes:warm`
> _For grown-ups: Matched warm clothes to body parts to get ready for cold weather._


#### `sprout:s19-climb` · [main](../../../side/S19.md#s19-climb)

_First light. Marina ties a rope to you. Then Moss to your button._  
_He rides in your coat, facing you. Just his little face pokes out._  
**Moss**: Left.  
**Marina**: Right.  
_Marina points at a rock. Moss points at the same rock._  
**Moss**: Her right is my left. We both mean that rock!  

> **Lesson** (maths, ELOF: Mathematics Development, do): left and right  
> _(Wave your left hand, like Moss. Now your right, like Marina!)_  
> **Moss**: Left and right. Both good. Both up the hill.  
> Check: `edu:wave:hand:left-right`
> _For grown-ups: Practised left and right by waving each hand._

_Crunch, crunch, crunch. The snow sounds like biting toast._  
**Marina**: Lovely wind! It makes your cheeks tingle!  
**Moss**: Your heart is very loud. I don’t mind. It’s warm.  

#### `sprout:s19-climb-sable` · [main](../../../side/S19.md#s19-climb-sable)

_Variant of `s19-climb`._

_First light. Marina ties a rope to you. Sable won’t have a rope._  
_She trots ahead, a grey shape on the white snow._  
_Moss’s pale light floats by her shoulder._  
_Crunch, crunch, crunch. Your boots sink in._  
_Sable is so light, she doesn’t sink at all._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.MD.A.2, ask): heavier and lighter  
> _You sink in the snow. Sable doesn’t. Who is heavier?_  
> - ✅ Me! → Yes! You’re heavier, so you sink. Sable is light.
> - Sable → Look at the footprints. Yours are deep. Hers are tiny. You’re heavier!
> _For grown-ups: Compared weight by looking at deep and shallow footprints in snow._

**Marina**: She’s not stopping! Is she always like this?  
**You**: Only when it matters.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds: s  
> _Sable. Snow. S-s-s!_  
> _Which one starts with s too?_  
> - ✅ Sun → S-s-sun! Yes!
> - Moon → M-m-moon starts with m. S-s-sun starts with s!
> - Tree → T-t-tree starts with t. S-s-sun starts with s!
> _For grown-ups: Heard the “s” sound at the start of Sable and snow, and found another s word._

#### `sprout:s19-hollow` · [main](../../../side/S19.md#s19-hollow)

_Near the top, a rock leans over, like a hand over a cup._  
_Under it there is no snow. The rock is dry and a little warm._  
_On a flat stone sits a tiny red light. Smaller than your fingernail._  
**Marina**: I’ll wait up on the edge. Shout if you need me.  
_Moss has gone very still in your coat._  
**Moss**: …Cinder.  
**Moss**: She was a warden, like me. Long, long ago.  
**Moss**: She can’t hear us yet. We have to sit still for a long time.  
**Moss**: Let me sit by her. You sit by me. We’ll wait.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, do): being still and calm, and waiting  
> _(Let’s be very still and quiet too. Breathe in… and out.)_  
> _(Take three slow breaths together: one… two… three.)_  
> Check: `edu:sit:still:quiet`
> _For grown-ups: Practised sitting still and breathing slowly while waiting._

#### `sprout:s19-sit` · [main](../../../side/S19.md#s19-sit)

_You sit on the dry rock. The wind goes over. Whoooo._  
_Far below is the whole valley. The cottage looks tiny._  
_The waterfall is frozen white. It doesn’t move at all._  

> **Lesson** (science, 2-ESS2-3, ask): very cold water turns to ice  
> _Why is the waterfall white and still?_  
> - ✅ It got so cold, the water froze into ice → Yes! When water gets very cold, it turns into ice.
> - It got tired → Waterfalls don’t get tired! It got so cold, the water turned into ice.
> _For grown-ups: Explained a frozen waterfall: very cold water turns into ice._

_You wait. Your feet get cold._  
_Grrrrr! Your tummy is very loud in all this quiet._  
_Up on the edge, Marina coughs. A very polite cough._  
_You keep waiting._  

#### `sprout:s19-question` · [main](../../../side/S19.md#s19-question)

_After a long time, the red light grows a tiny bit bigger._  
**Cinder**: Are they safe?  
**Moss**: Not just safe. They’re well.  
**Moss**: Safe is sitting still. Well is running, and playing, and too many plums.  
**Cinder**: Well. Well is better. I didn’t know.  
**Cinder**: Long ago, I held them too tight. To keep them safe.  
**Cinder**: They went still and grey. I am so sorry.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): hear words that rhyme  
> **Cinder**: Do they still say my name, down there?  
> _Children skip to a rhyme about her:_  
> **You**: Cinder held them close and tight, kept them warm one winter night.  
> _Which word rhymes with “tight”?_  
> - ✅ Night → Tight, night. That’s right. They still say my name.
> - Warm → Listen: tight… night. Night rhymes with tight. They still say my name.
> _For grown-ups: Heard the valley’s skipping rhyme and found the rhyming pair “tight” and “night”._

**Moss**: Every day. They skip to it. Badly. Very, very loudly.  
_The little light flickers. It might be a laugh._  
**Moss**: Cinder, I forgave you long ago. I still do.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, notice): saying sorry and forgiving  
> _Saying sorry is brave. Forgiving is kind._  
> _(Ask: when did someone forgive you? Wait for an answer.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Saw someone say sorry and be forgiven, and talked about a time they were forgiven._

**Cinder**: Thank you, Moss. Keeper, the grey Nubbins live by you.  
**Cinder**: Do the grey Nubbins forgive me?  

**Choices**

- **“You’re forgiven.”**
    - **You**: You’re forgiven.
    - The light goes warm, like a fire with the kettle on.
    - **Cinder**: Oh. I thought being forgiven would be heavy. It’s light!
    - **Moss**: Yes. This one, you just put down.
- **“Only the grey Nubbins can say. But we’ll remember you.”**
    - **You**: Only the grey Nubbins can say. But we’ll remember you.
    - **Cinder**: Remembered. That’s what I should have asked for.
    - **Cinder**: Will you tell them I loved them?
    - **You**: All of it.
    - **Moss**: We are made of being remembered. I told her that once.

#### `sprout:s19-after` · [main](../../../side/S19.md#s19-after)

_The red light is getting smaller. Slowly. Gently._  
**Cinder**: I kept my little light on for so long. In case there was news.  
**Cinder**: Now there’s been news. I can stop.  
_Cinder is fading. That means she is dying._  
_Her light will stop, and it won’t start again._  
**Cinder**: Will you stay? You don’t have to do anything. Just stay.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, do): staying close to someone who needs us  
> _(Hold your grown-up’s hand. Staying close is a kind thing to do.)_  
> **Cinder**: Thank you.  
> Check: `edu:hold:hand:stay`
> _For grown-ups: Heard plainly what fading means, and learned that staying close is a way to care for someone._


#### `sprout:s19-fade` · [main](../../../side/S19.md#s19-fade)

_You stay. Moss sits right next to the little light._  
_The light gets smaller and smaller. Slowly. Gently._  
**Cinder**: Moss. It isn’t cold, like the grey Nubbins were.  
**Moss**: No. This is the gentle kind.  
_Then the light is gone. Cinder has died._  
_There is only the flat stone, and the wind._  
**Moss**: That is fading done right. Gentle. Then you let go.  
_Moss sits there a long time. You sit with him._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, notice): feeling sad when someone dies, and what helps  
> _Moss is sad. You might feel sad too. That’s all right._  
> _Staying close together helps. So does remembering._  
> _(Give your grown-up a hug, if you like.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Heard plainly that Cinder died, and that feeling sad is all right; closeness and remembering help._

#### `sprout:s19-complete` · [main](../../../side/S19.md#s19-complete)

_Where the light was, the stone is still warm. Like a hand._  
_On it sits a little red pebble. It stays warm all the way home._  
_Marina’s eyes are red. She has been crying a bit._  
**Marina**: It’s the wind. Mostly.  
**Marina**: Come on. Feet in my footprints. Home before dark.  
_That night, the lantern by the long grass feels warm to stand by._  
_You put the red pebble on the windowsill._  
_By morning, five Nubbins are asleep in a heap under it. Snore!_  
_One wakes up and licks the red pebble. Not supper!_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 5  
> _(Count the sleepy Nubbins: one, two, three, four, five.)_  
> _Five Nubbins, warm and snug. Shh!_  
> Check: `edu:count:nubbins:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five sleeping Nubbins._


**Notes:** S19 sprout. Cinder is introduced simply: a warden who, long ago, held the Nubbins too tight to keep them safe, and they went still and grey. Her fading is honest: s19-after says what fading means (“she is dying… her light will stop, and it won’t start again”) and s19-fade says “Cinder has died”, followed by comfort. Moss’s “No. They’re well.” becomes “Not just safe. They’re well.” so a young child hears the difference. Both choices kept. Scene-level set on s19-warmth (s19-with = moss) still comes from the original. The Sable route uses the -sable versions in EDU_SIDE_NEW_B_SPROUT_VARIANTS; s19-warmth-sable says plainly that Moss died at the Long Night, with the comfort of his pale light staying with Sable. If Moss has left the world and Sable is not bonded, the -alone versions (you and Marina only) play instead; you answer Cinder yourself.
