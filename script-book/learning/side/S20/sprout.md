# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S20: Fort Night

## S20. Fort Night

- `s20-step1`: Round one: gather snow and sticks, and build your wall. · _Put lots of children on gathering at the start. Wall first!_
- `s20-step2`: Round two: send Zip to peek at the other fort. · _Hide Zip behind the snowdrifts. Run when the lookout turns away._
- `s20-step3`: Round three: send the snow-fighters that beat theirs. · _Speedy beats Splashy. Splashy beats Sturdy. Sturdy beats Speedy._
- `s20-step4`: Build one big shelter, and get every child inside. · _Count everyone. Wren is little and easy to miss._

#### `sprout:s20-declaration` · [main](../../../side/S20.md#s20-declaration)

_Deep, squeaky snow. Every child in the valley is out in it._  
_A card on your gate says: WAR! FORT NIGHT! BRING MITTENS!_  
_Tilly has come for the holidays. She is Sparks’s cousin._  
_And there is snow!_  
**Tilly**: You lead one fort. I lead the other. I’m going to win!  
**Pixel**: I’m the referee. I wrote the rules. Forty-one pages.  
**Pixel**: My husband Byte read them all. He had notes.  
**Pixel**: Three kinds of snow-fighter: Speedy, Splashy and Sturdy.  
**Pixel**: Speedy beats Splashy. Splashy beats Sturdy. Sturdy beats Speedy!  

> **Lesson** (maths, ELOF: Mathematics Development, ask): what comes next in a pattern  
> **Pixel**: It goes round and round, like a pattern.  
> **Pixel**: Speedy, Splashy, Sturdy, Speedy, Splashy… what comes next?  
> - ✅ Sturdy → Sturdy! Then the pattern goes round again.
> - Speedy → Speedy comes after Sturdy. Listen: Speedy, Splashy, STURDY.
> - Gordon → Ha! Gordon isn’t in the pattern. Speedy, Splashy, Sturdy!
> _For grown-ups: Continued a repeating pattern of three._

_Bonk! A snowball hits a small boy called Gordon._  
**Gordon**: I’m all right!  
**Wren**: Commander! I’m Wren! What’s our battle cry?  

**Choices**

- **“FOR THE FORT!”**
    - **Wren**: FOR THE FORT!
    - **Pixel**: Classic. Approved.
- **“FOR WREN’S NAN’S GOOD MITTENS!”**
    - **Wren**: FOR MY NAN’S GOOD MITTENS!
    - **Wren**: She knitted them. They have thumbs!
- **“REMEMBER GORDON!”**
    - **Gordon**: I’m right here.
    - Everyone shouts it anyway. Then a snowball hits Gordon.

#### `sprout:s20-round1` · [main](../../../side/S20.md#s20-round1)

_Everyone gathers snow and sticks. Pat, pat, pat!_  
_Every time you point at Wren, she salutes._  
**Wren**: Yes, commander! Yes, commander! That’s nine times. I’m only little!  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): saying sorry and thank you  
> _Wren is cross. She has been asked nine times!_  
> _What could you say to Wren?_  
> - ✅ “Sorry, Wren. Thank you for helping!” → Oh! You’re welcome, commander. Sorry and thank you make friends feel good.
> - “Faster, Wren!” → Hmm. Wren is only little. Let’s try: sorry, Wren, and thank you! Oh! You’re welcome, commander.
> _For grown-ups: Chose kind words, sorry and thank you, for a helper who felt cross._

**Pixel**: Rule: don’t hit the snowman. He has feelings. He’s called Derek.  
_The whistle blows. Your wall is short and fat and lumpy._  
_Their wall is tall and thin, with pointy bits on top._  
**Tilly**: I put everyone on snow from the start!  
**Pixel**: Round one: a draw. Both of them are walls.  

#### `sprout:s20-scout` · [main](../../../side/S20.md#s20-scout)

**Pixel**: Round two: peeking. You can’t win if you can’t see.  
_Sparks lends you a fast little Nubbin called Zip._  
_Zip wiggles in your mittens. Snow melts off his ears. Drip!_  

> **Lesson** (science, 2-ESS2-3, ask): snow is frozen water and melts when warm  
> **Pixel**: Why does the snow melt on your mitten?  
> - ✅ My hand is warm → Yes! Snow is frozen water. Warm it up, and it turns back into water.
> - Zip is magic → Zip is fast, not magic! Your warm hand melts the snow back into water.
> _For grown-ups: Explained that snow is frozen water that melts when it gets warm._

**Pixel**: Send Zip round their fort. Count what they’ve got.  
**Gordon**: Can I go?  
**Pixel**: No, Gordon. You’d get hit.  
**Gordon**: That’s fair.  

#### `sprout:s20-intel` · [main](../../../side/S20.md#s20-intel)

_Zip runs back. He has snow on his eyebrows._  
_He runs round you nine times. That means: news!_  
**Wren**: Nine! Same as me. Nobody counts Zip’s.  
_Their wall is big. But behind it, hardly anyone._  
_Five children were behind their wall. Then three went home!_  
_One lost a mitten, one lost a boot, and one wanted his tea._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.2, ask): take away within 5  
> **Pixel**: Five children were behind their wall. Three went home. How many are left?  
> - ✅ 2 → Two! Five take away three leaves two. (Show five fingers. Fold three down. Two left!)
> - 3 → Three went home. Let’s use fingers: show five, fold three down… two left!
> - 5 → Some went home, so there are fewer now. Five take away three leaves two.
> _For grown-ups: Took three away from five, using fingers, to find two._

**Tilly**: We’re FINE! We have LOADS of people!  
**Tilly**: Some are IN the wall. They’re bricks!  
**Pixel**: Rule: children are not bricks.  
**Pixel**: …Mostly not.  

**Choices**

- **Charge now, while there are only a few. It’s a snowball game!**
    - **Wren**: CHARGE! Yes, commander!
    - Your fort runs across the snow. Whoosh!
    - **Tilly**: That’s not FAIR!
    - **Tilly**: …That’s just what I would have done.
    - **Pixel**: Round two to your fort.
- **Wait till their three come back. Beating half a fort isn’t winning.**
    - Zip takes them a snowball with a smiley face. It means: we’ll wait.
    - Soon a mitten, a boot and a full tummy come back.
    - **Tilly**: You waited for us? That’s nice. Or sneaky.
    - **Pixel**: Round two: a draw. Being fair gets a point. New rule!

#### `sprout:s20-counters` · [main](../../../side/S20.md#s20-counters)

**Pixel**: Round three. The last one.  
**Pixel**: Look at what they send. Send what beats it.  
_Over the ditch, big children roll big snowballs. Sturdy ones!_  
**Tilly**: GIVE UP OR GET ROLLED!  
_Your fort shouts back: “FOR DEREK!” “I’M BRAVE, NOT COLD!”_  
_Above the hill, the sky goes dark grey, like a pigeon._  
_Nobody looks at it. Only Pixel._  
**Pixel**: Hm.  
**Gordon**: Is that sky going to hit me?  
**Pixel**: This time, Gordon, it’s going to hit everybody.  

> **Lesson** (science, K-ESS2-1, notice): dark clouds can mean a storm  
> **Pixel**: Dark grey clouds can mean a storm is coming.  
> **Pixel**: When the sky changes, it’s good to look up and notice.  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that dark grey clouds can be a sign of a storm._


#### `sprout:s20-blizzard` · [main](../../../side/S20.md#s20-blizzard)

_You’re nearly at their flag. Then the snow goes sideways!_  
_White everywhere. You can’t see their fort. You can’t see yours._  
**Pixel**: STOP! Everybody stop! This is a real blizzard!  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): keeping safe in a storm  
> **Pixel**: In a big storm: stop, stay together, and find a grown-up.  
> **Pixel**: Today, the grown-up is me. Hold hands!  
> _(Hold hands with your grown-up. Stay together!)_  
> Check: `edu:hold:hands:together`
> _For grown-ups: Learned what to do in a storm: stop, stay together and find a grown-up._

_Somebody small is crying. You can’t tell who._  
**Tilly**: Our wall fell in! I can’t find everyone!  
_Your wall still stands. Short, fat and lumpy._  
_But it’s too small for everybody._  

#### `sprout:s20-merge` · [main](../../../side/S20.md#s20-merge)

_Through the snow you can see children from both forts._  
_They stand about, like dropped gloves._  
**Pixel**: Referee’s rule…  
**Pixel**: I don’t have one. You’ve got a wall. They’ve got snow.  
**Tilly**: Is this a trick?  
**You**: No. It’s a plan. One fort, for everyone.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, notice): working together  
> _Working together, everyone can build something big._  
> _(Ask: what do you like to build with a friend? Wait for an answer.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Saw two teams join up to build one shelter, and talked about building things with friends._


**Choices**

- **Kick open your wall and shout them all in.**
    - You kick a gap. “IN! EVERYONE IN!”
    - Your fort pulls the others in, by their scarves.
- **Carry your wall to them, snowball by snowball.**
    - Your team is good at moving snow fast. You taught them!
    - That’s what it was for, all along.
- **Meet in the middle. Both walls, one shelter.**
    - Both forts bring snow. Both bring sticks.
    - Their pointy sticks are the roof. Derek the snowman is the door.

#### `sprout:s20-count` · [main](../../../side/S20.md#s20-count)

**Pixel**: Head count!  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 20  
> _(Count everyone in with Pixel: one, two, three… all the way to twenty!)_  
> **Pixel**: Twenty. Everyone’s inside.  
> Check: `edu:count:children:20`
> _For grown-ups: Counted to twenty to check every child was safe inside._

**Pixel**: …Everyone. Even Gordon.  
_The small one who cried was Wren. She lost a mitten._  
_Pixel finds it. It was on Wren’s head!_  
**Gordon**: I got hit by a whole sky!  
_Inside it’s blue and snug. It smells of wet wool._  
**Tilly**: That bit of the roof is mine.  
**Wren**: Commander? Which fort won?  
_The storm blows for hours. Pixel sings a song with forty-one verses._  
_Then the wind stops. Someone pokes a hole, and it’s night!_  
_A spoon pokes through the hole. Then a face, upside down!_  
_It’s Granny Brock. She lives by the Common. She brings soup!_  
**Granny Brock**: Soup. Somebody rang my bell this morning.  
**Gordon**: That was me!  
**Granny Brock**: I know. Eat.  

#### `sprout:s20-complete` · [main](../../../side/S20.md#s20-complete)

_The sky is clear and black. So many stars!_  
_Both forts lie in one fort, looking up._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-ESS1-1, notice): stars are there in the day too  
> **Pixel**: Stars are in the sky in the daytime too.  
> **Pixel**: The sun is so bright, we just can’t see them.  
> _(Look for stars tonight, with your grown-up!)_  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that stars are always in the sky, but we only see them when the sun has gone._

**Wren**: That one’s a mitten. That one’s Gordon. That one’s Derek!  
**Pixel**: Final rule: both forts win. I wrote it in pen.  
**Tilly**: So what was it all for? The snow and the peeking?  
**You**: So we knew how to build fast. When the storm came, we did!  
**Tilly**: …That’s a really good answer.  
_Up at the cottage, someone in a blanket waves from the window._  
_Everybody waves back._  
**Pixel**: Prizes! A commander’s hat. It’s a tea cosy.
