# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S21: CL-4NK

## S21. CL-4NK

- `s21-step1`: Show Clank round the Grove. · _Go round things, not over them. He has only one wheel._
- `s21-step2`: Tidy up after Clank’s helping. · _Dry the cat. Prop up the tree. Then try to catch Clank!_
- `s21-step3`: Find Clank. · _Follow his wheel-track through the herbs._
- `s21-step4`: Help Byte build a ramp for the step. · _Byte measures. You saw. Measure twice!_
- `s21-step5`: Stay with Clank until the sun goes down. · _Feed the Nubbins first. Then sit by the step and just be there._

#### `sprout:s21-unveil` · [main](../../../side/S21.md#s21-unveil)

_Squeak, squeak! Pixel pushes a wheelbarrow up the lane._  
**Pixel**: I had spare lantern parts. So I made a garden helper!  
_A lantern head. A glass tummy. A brass nose. One wheel._  
_Pixel winds the nose. Wheee! He whistles like a happy kettle._  
**Clank**: HELLO! I am CL-4NK! Call me Clank!  

> **Lesson** (literacy, RF.K.2, ask): first sounds: c  
> **Clank**: Clank! C-c-clank! What else starts with that sound?  
> - ✅ Cat → C-c-cat! Clank and cat start the same!
> - Dog → D-d-dog starts with d. C-c-cat starts like Clank!
> - Sun → S-s-sun starts with s. C-c-cat starts like Clank!
> _For grown-ups: Heard the first sound of “Clank” and matched it with “cat”._

**Clank**: You are my BEST FRIEND!  
**You**: We’ve only just—  
_He rolls forward. Off the step. Bump! Into the herbs._  
**Clank**: Announcement! I have found the step!  
**Pixel**: That’s one bump.  

#### `sprout:s21-tour` · [main](../../../side/S21.md#s21-tour)

**Clank**: Announcement: this is a POND! This is a GATE! Best friend!  
_Soon every Nubbin follows him in a line. Like ducklings!_  
_One climbs on his head. Then three more._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.1, ask): add 3 more  
> _One Nubbin, then three more. How many on Clank?_  
> - ✅ 4 → FOUR! One and three more make four!
> - 3 → Count them: one… then two, three, four. Four!
> - 2 → More than that! One and three more make four.
> _For grown-ups: Added one and three more to make four._

_He turns his light down low for them. They purr._  
**Clank**: They like me. Announcement: they LIKE me.  
**Clank**: I didn’t know that could happen.  

#### `sprout:s21-help` · [main](../../../side/S21.md#s21-help)

_At dawn, a whistle wakes you. WHEEEE!_  
**Clank**: GOOD MORNING! I have done SO MUCH HELPING!  
_Fern holds her cat, Mustard. Drip, drip, drip._  
_Mustard says MROWW. That means very, very cross._  
**Fern**: He watered my cat. To be fair, Mustard was in the beans.  
**Clank**: Cats are plants that run! I think!  

> **Lesson** (science, K-LS1-1, ask): what plants and animals need  
> _Do cats need watering, like plants?_  
> - ✅ No! Cats drink from a bowl → Right. Plants drink through their roots. Cats lap water from a bowl.
> - Yes → Ha! No. Plants need watering. Cats drink from a bowl. And they hate baths.
> _For grown-ups: Sorted out what plants need (watering) from what cats need (a drink from a bowl)._

_Snip, snip, snip! Fern’s best apple tree is now a duck._  
**Fern**: It’s a very good duck. It was a better tree.  
**Clank**: And I rescued a little old one! He was stuck up the hill!  
_Round the pond zooms Clank, holding the little grey one like a pie._  

#### `sprout:s21-moss` · [main](../../../side/S21.md#s21-moss)

_Clank reaches the cottage. And the step. Bump!_  
_The little grey one flies out of his hands. He lands on his feet._  
**Clank**: Announcement… I found the step again.  
_The little grey one gives you a long, long look._  
_Then he walks all the way back up the hill. Slowly._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): asking before we pick someone up  
> _Clank picked up the little grey one without asking. He didn’t like it._  
> _What should we do before we pick someone up?_  
> - ✅ Ask first → Yes! Always ask first. Then they can say yes or no.
> - Just grab them → He didn’t like being grabbed. We always ask first, and listen.
> _For grown-ups: Learned to ask before picking up a pet or a person, and to listen to the answer._

**Pixel**: That’s two bumps.  

#### `sprout:s21-log` · [main](../../../side/S21.md#s21-log)

_Byte, Pixel’s husband, comes with a clipboard and a pencil behind each ear._  
**Byte**: One wet cat. One duck-shaped tree. One old Nubbin, zoomed.  
**Byte**: He means to help. But next time, someone could get hurt.  
**Byte**: Pixel should switch Clank off. Tonight.  
**Pixel**: You said that about my clocks.  
**Byte**: Your clocks shout at me.  
_Behind you, a little whistle starts, then stops._  
_Clank heard. His light goes very dim._  
_He rolls backwards, off the step. Bump. He doesn’t say a thing._  
**Pixel**: …That’s three bumps.  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 3  
> _(Count Clank’s bumps with Pixel, on your fingers: one, two, three.)_  
> _Three bumps down the step. Poor Clank._  
> Check: `edu:count:falls:3`
> _For grown-ups: Counted Clank’s three bumps on their fingers, keeping track like Pixel._

_Then he’s gone. A wheel-track leads to the lantern shed._  
**Byte**: I didn’t know he could hear. I didn’t know he could… mind.  

**Choices**

- **Tell Byte he had a point. Clank does need to learn.**
    - **Byte**: I did have a point. It feels worse when it hurts someone.
    - **Pixel**: He needs a bit of slow. I can help with that.
- **Tell Byte that wasn’t fair. Clank is only days old.**
    - **Byte**: Days old. I forgot that.
    - **Byte**: I was days old once. I’m told I bit a goose.

#### `sprout:s21-shed` · [main](../../../side/S21.md#s21-shed)

_The shed smells of old string. Clank is on a high shelf._  
_He’s pretending to be a lantern. But lanterns don’t shake._  
**Clank**: Announcement. I’m a lantern now. Nobody switches off a lantern.  
**Clank**: I watered a cat. I made a duck tree. I zoomed the old one.  
**Clank**: Am I a friend? Or just a thing that does jobs?  
**Clank**: If I stop helping, will you still…  
_Click. Click. He looks for a word. He can’t find it._  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, notice): friends like you for who you are  
> _Friends like you for who you are. Not for the jobs you do._  
> _(Ask: what do you like about a friend of yours? Wait for an answer.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that friends like us for who we are, and named something they like about a friend._


**Choices**

- **Sit down below the shelf, and wait.**
    - You sit on the floor. It’s not comfy. You stay.
    - **Clank**: Is it a job?
    - **You**: No. I’m just here.
    - After a long time, Clank comes down. He parks beside you.
- **Teach him: it’s all right to ask for help.**
    - **You**: Do you know why you fall down the step?
    - **Clank**: Because I’m brave?
    - **You**: Because you never ask anyone to help.
    - **You**: Everybody needs a hand sometimes. Even me.
    - Clank thinks so hard, his whistle goes off, very softly.

#### `sprout:s21-ramp` · [main](../../../side/S21.md#s21-ramp)

_Next day, Byte comes with a plank and a saw._  
**Byte**: I measured the step. It’s too tall for one wheel.  
_Byte measures again. And again. Eleven times!_  
**Byte**: Still too tall. I checked.  
**Byte**: So it isn’t Clank. It’s the step.  

> **Lesson** (science, K-2-ETS1-1, ask): solving a problem: a ramp helps wheels  
> **Byte**: How can a wheel get down a step safely?  
> - ✅ A ramp: a gentle slope → Yes. A ramp. The wheel rolls down slowly. No bump.
> - Jump off → Bump! That’s what keeps happening. A gentle slope is better: a ramp.
> - Fly → He hasn’t got wings. A ramp is a gentle slope he can roll down.
> _For grown-ups: Worked out that a ramp solves the problem of a wheel and a step._

**Byte**: All the Nubbins follow him everywhere. Eight out of eight. That matters.  
**Clank**: Byte! Does that mean you’re my best—  
**Byte**: Maybe.  

#### `sprout:s21-down` · [main](../../../side/S21.md#s21-down)

_The ramp is done. Small, smooth and safe._  
**Clank**: Announcement. I am going down.  
**Clank**: Would somebody come with me? The first time?  
_Byte holds one side. You hold the other._  
_Down he goes. Slow as a snail. He reaches the bottom!_  
**Clank**: I DID THE STEP! And I didn’t fall!  
_He does a dance: forward, back, and a spin!_  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): moving our bodies: forward, back, spin  
> _(Do the Clank dance! Step forward. Step back. Spin round!)_  
> **Clank**: Announcement: you are a GREAT dancer!  
> Check: `edu:dance:clank:forward-back-spin`
> _For grown-ups: Did the Clank dance: a step forward, a step back and a spin._

_You copy it. Byte copies it too._  
_Later, Byte says he didn’t. His shoelace has come undone._  
**Pixel**: He did. He danced like that at our wedding.  
**Pixel**: His shoelace came undone that day too.  
**Pixel**: That’s… no bumps! I’ll take no bumps.  

#### `sprout:s21-evening` · [main](../../../side/S21.md#s21-evening)

_The afternoon turns gold. Clank sits by the ramp._  

> **Lesson** (science, 1-ESS1-1, notice): the sun is low in the evening  
> _In the evening, the sun sits low in the sky._  
> _The light goes gold, and shadows grow long._  
> _For grown-ups: Noticed that the evening sun is low, with golden light and long shadows._

_He doesn’t water anything. It’s very hard for him._  
**Clank**: Announcement: I am not helping. I am just… here.  
**Clank**: Is just here allowed?  
**You**: Just here is allowed.  
**Clank**: Announcement: just here is allowed.  

#### `sprout:s21-complete` · [main](../../../side/S21.md#s21-complete)

_At dusk the Nubbins come in from the long grass._  
_One by one, they climb onto Clank. He’s warm, like a kettle._  
_One sits on his wheel. One curls up inside his glass tummy._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 8  
> _(Count the Nubbins on Clank: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight!)_  
> **Clank**: Eight friends. Announcement: that is LOTS.  
> Check: `edu:count:nubbins:8`
> _For grown-ups: Counted eight Nubbins snuggled on Clank._

_One Nubbin licks Clank’s nose. Not supper!_  
**Clank**: Announcement.  
**Clank**: I am being very still.  
_He is. For a whole hour. You sit beside him._  
**Pixel**: Keep him. He’s yours. He’ll water things, slowly.  
_Next morning he waters the beans. One bean a minute._
