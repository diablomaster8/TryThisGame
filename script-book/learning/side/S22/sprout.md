# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Side story S22: The Climb

## S22. The Climb

- `s22-step1`: Climb the slidey stones with Nova. · _Three quick steps, then rest on a big rock. Keep going!_
- `s22-step2`: Climb up the Chimney. · _Push with your back, step with your feet. One at a time._
- `s22-step3`: Cross the Ridge. · _Stay low when the wind blows. Look at the next step, not down._
- `s22-step4`: Put the flag on the very top. · _It’s in Nova’s bag, under the oatcakes. Push the pole into the crack._

#### `sprout:s22-foot` · [main](../../../side/S22.md#s22-foot)

_The Tooth is a tall, pointy mountain. Snow on the very top._  
_Nova waits at the bottom with a rope and a bag of oatcakes._  
**Nova**: I’ve tried to climb it three times. I always come down.  
**Nova**: I asked you for help once. It went well. So here I am!  
_Next to her sits a little shadow, the size of a cat._  
_It looks just like Nova. Same hair. Same boots._  
_It has a tiny rope too, and it trips on it. Thump!_  
**Nova’s Worry**: What if you slip?  
**Nova**: …You can see it? Nobody ever sees it!  
**Nova**: It’s my Worry. It talks to me. It’s mostly right. Annoying!  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): naming a worry  
> **Nova**: Everybody has worries. Mine always says “what if”.  
> _Giving a worry a name makes it easier to carry._  
> _(Ask: do you have a “what if” worry? Wait for an answer.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Met Nova’s Worry and talked about naming their own “what if” worries._

**Nova**: Right. Up we go. I’ll sing. It helps me.  

#### `sprout:s22-scree` · [main](../../../side/S22.md#s22-scree)

_The little stones slide under your feet. Crunch, crunch!_  
**Nova**: ♪ Little stones, little stones, sliding under my toes! ♪  
**Nova**: ♪ I’m going up, but I’m sliding down, and that’s how it goes! ♪  
_She sings it again. And again. And again!_  

> **Lesson** (music, do): clapping a steady beat  
> _(Clap along as you sing: lit-tle stones, lit-tle stones!)_  
> **Nova**: Clap, clap! Now we’re a band.  
> Check: `edu:clap:beat:stones-song`
> _For grown-ups: Clapped a steady beat along with Nova’s climbing song._

_The Worry puts its hands over its ears._  
**Nova’s Worry**: Last time you slipped here. You hurt your knee.  
**Nova**: It’s right about that. It’s the next bit it gets wrong.  

**Choices**

- **“It is right. And you got up and kept going.”**
    - **Nova**: …I did, didn’t I? I kept going all the way up.
    - **Nova’s Worry**: That’s… also true.
    - **Nova**: Ha!
- **“That was last time. This time there are two of us.”**
    - **Nova**: Two of us. Huh.
    - **Nova’s Worry**: Three.
    - **Nova**: It can count! It always could.

#### `sprout:s22-chimney` · [main](../../../side/S22.md#s22-chimney)

_The Chimney is a narrow crack in the rock._  
_Back on one side. Boots on the other. Push and step!_  
**Nova**: ♪ I’m in a crack, I’m in a crack! This is my foot! ♪  
**Nova**: ♪ This is my other foot! ♪  
_Far below, a big bird called a buzzard flies round and round._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.G.A.1, ask): above and below  
> _Is the buzzard above you, or below you?_  
> - ✅ Below → Below! We’re higher than a bird. Wow.
> - Above → Above? Whee! We’d be up with the clouds. Look down… it’s below us! We’re higher than the bird.
> _For grown-ups: Used “above” and “below” to say where the bird was._


#### `sprout:s22-chimney-top` · [main](../../../side/S22.md#s22-chimney-top)

_Halfway up, Nova stops. The Worry sits just above her._  
**Nova’s Worry**: When you were little, somebody went away. What if somebody goes again?  
_Nova holds the rock tight. She doesn’t move._  
**Nova**: That’s true. Somebody went away when I was nine. I was very sad.  
**Nova**: So now I always go first. Then nobody can go away from me.  

> **Lesson** (health, CASEL: Self-management, do): slow breathing to feel calm  
> _(Help Nova feel calm. Smell a flower: breathe in…)_  
> _(Blow out a candle: breathe out… Once more!)_  
> **Nova**: That helps. A bit. Thanks.  
> Check: `edu:breathe:slow:flower-candle`
> _For grown-ups: Practised slow “smell the flower, blow the candle” breathing to calm a scared feeling._


**Choices**

- **“It’s true. But it isn’t a reason to let go.”**
    - **Nova**: …No. It isn’t.
    - She moves one hand. Then one foot. Slowly.
    - **Nova’s Worry**: I didn’t say let go. I said it’s a long way down.
    - **Nova**: You’re very bad at saying what you mean.
- **“Not now. Look at me, not down.”**
    - She looks at you. Not down. At you.
    - **Nova**: …Right. Hands.
    - She climbs. The Worry climbs too, muttering about loose rocks.

#### `sprout:s22-ridge` · [main](../../../side/S22.md#s22-ridge)

_The Ridge is thin, with sky on both sides. Whoosh goes the wind!_  
_This is where Nova always turns back._  
_There’s a little pile of stones. One stone for every try._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.OA.A.1, do): count to 3, and one more  
> _(Count the stones: one, two, three.)_  
> **Nova**: Three tries. If I get across, this try is number four!  
> Check: `edu:count:stones:3`
> _For grown-ups: Counted three stones and worked out that one more try makes four._

**Nova’s Worry**: Go back. It’s windy. It’s high. Come another day.  
**Nova**: When my Worry talks, I feel small.  
**Nova**: So I go home. Three times. Three stones.  

#### `sprout:s22-ridge-cross` · [main](../../../side/S22.md#s22-ridge-cross)

_In the middle of the Ridge, Nova stops. The Worry is beside her._  
_She looks at it. Then she holds out her hand._  
**Nova**: Come on, then. You always come. So come with me.  
**Nova’s Worry**: …What?  
_The Worry takes her hand. It gets smaller. Not gone. Smaller._  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, notice): worries feel smaller when we share them  
> _When Nova holds her Worry gently, it gets smaller._  
> _Worries feel smaller when we share them with someone._  
> _(Ask: who could you tell a worry to? Wait for an answer.)_  
> _For grown-ups: Saw a worry shrink when it was held gently, and named someone they could tell a worry to._

_They cross together. Nova sings. A second voice joins in, very flat. Hee, hee!_  
**Nova**: You can SING?  
**Nova’s Worry**: I always could. You never asked.  

#### `sprout:s22-scramble` · [main](../../../side/S22.md#s22-scramble)

_Thump, thump! The last bit is a climb on hands and knees._  
_Up here the air is very cold. The snow never melts._  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): high up on a mountain it is colder  
> **Nova**: High up on a mountain, it’s colder than down below.  
> **Nova**: That’s why the very top has snow, when the valley doesn’t.  
> _For grown-ups: Learned that mountain tops are colder than valleys, which is why snow stays up there._

_At the top is a point of rock, as big as a kitchen table._  
**Nova**: Give me the flag. No, you do it. No. Together!  

#### `sprout:s22-summit` · [main](../../../side/S22.md#s22-summit)

_Whee! From the top of the Tooth, you can see everything!_  
_The waterfall, like a white thread. Marina’s pools, like coins._  
_And the Grove: the pond, the cottage, four tiny lantern posts._  

> **Lesson** (social-studies, D2.Geo.2.K-2, do): a map is a picture of a place from above  
> _From up high, the valley looks like a map._  
> _(Point: where is the pond? Where is the cottage?)_  
> **Nova**: A map is a picture of a place, seen from above.  
> Check: `edu:point:map:valley`
> _For grown-ups: Looked down on the valley like a map and pointed out places in it._

_Nova sits down. The Worry sits next to her, holding her hand._  
**Nova**: I’d like to stay for a while.  
**Nova**: Not up here. Down there, in the valley. With you all.  
**Nova’s Worry**: It might be scary.  
**Nova**: Yes. You can come too.  

#### `sprout:s22-complete` · [main](../../../side/S22.md#s22-complete)

_Whee! Going down is quicker, and much more slippy._  
_Nova and the Worry sing all the way._  
_At the bottom, Nova opens the bag of oatcakes._  
_Squish! They are all flat._  
_Somebody sat on them. Nova looks at the Worry._  
_The Worry looks at the sky._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.4, ask): one each: counting to share fairly  
> **Nova**: Three of us: me, you, and my Worry. How many oatcakes do we need?  
> - ✅ 3 → Three! One each. Fair and square.
> - 2 → Two oatcakes? Crunch! One for me, one for you, one for the Worry. Let’s count: one for me, one for you, one for the Worry. Three!
> - 5 → Five oatcakes? Munch, munch! That’s too many. That’s extra! One each makes three.
> _For grown-ups: Matched one oatcake to each of three friends to share fairly._

**Nova**: Keep the flag. And my spare boots. I won’t need spares.  
**Nova**: I’m not going anywhere. I’m staying here, in the valley.  
_The Worry waves. Small. Only you can see it._  

**Notes:** S22 sprout. Nova’s Worry is small, a little funny and never frightening: it says “What if…?”, which gives the child the words for their own worries. The quest’s SEL thread is naming a worry (s22-foot), calming it (s22-chimney-top) and sharing it (s22-ridge-cross). Both choices at each fork are kept.
