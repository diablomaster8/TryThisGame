# Learning Grove · Little Sprouts (ages 3–6, read aloud): Ori's Alef-Bet, Hebrew (LH1–LH6)

## LH1. Shalom, Ori!

- `lh1-step1`: Say shalom to Ori at the gate. · _Go to the gate and tap Ori. Wave and say shalom!_
- `lh1-step2`: Teach three Nubbins to wave at shalom. · _Stand by a Nubbin. Say shalom and wave. When it waves back, say toda!_
- `lh1-step3`: Say laila tov to everyone. · _Find Fern, Clover and Nova. Tap each one and say laila tov._

#### `sprout:lh1-s1` · [main](main.md#lh1-s1)

_Rattle, rattle! Someone is stuck at the gate._  
**Nova**: Lift and push, Ori! Lift and push!  
_Creak! The gate opens. In tumbles a boy with a big tin._  
**Ori**: שָׁלוֹם (shalom, “hello”)! Your gate is very strong.  
_(Say it: sha-LOME. Wave as you say it!)_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, do): say hello in Hebrew: call and response  
> **Ori**: I say shalom. You say shalom!  
> **Ori**: Shalom!  
> _(Your turn. Wave and say: sha-LOME!)_  
> **Ori**: Perfect! A big wave and a big shalom.  
> Check: `edu:say:he:shalom`
> _For grown-ups: Said “shalom” (hello) back to Ori, with a wave, in a call-and-response game._

**Nova**: This is Ori. He lives in Israel. He is ten.  
**Ori**: Shalom means hello. It means goodbye too!  
_A wild round Nubbin waves at Ori. Wave… bump! It falls over._  
**Ori**: I can ask “How are you?” two ways. Which way?  

**Choices**

- **Ask me “ma shlomkha?” (the boy way).**
    - **Ori**: מָה שְׁלוֹמְךָ? (ma shlomkha?, “how are you?”)
    - (Say it: mah shlom-KHA.)
    - **You**: בְּסֵדֶר (beseder, “fine”)!
    - (Say it: beh-SEH-der.)
    - **Ori**: Beseder! That means OK. Fine!
- **Ask me “ma shlomekh?” (the girl way).**
    - **Ori**: מָה שְׁלוֹמֵךְ? (ma shlomekh?, “how are you?”)
    - (Say it: mah shlo-MEKH.)
    - **You**: בְּסֵדֶר (beseder, “fine”)!
    - (Say it: beh-SEH-der.)
    - **Ori**: Beseder! That means OK. Fine!
- **Either way. Just say shalom!**
    - **Ori**: Shalom, then! When I ask how you are, say בְּסֵדֶר (beseder, “fine”).
    - (Say it: beh-SEH-der.)
    - **You**: Beseder!
    - **Ori**: Perfect! You sound like my uncle.

#### `sprout:lh1-s1-done` · [main](main.md#lh1-s1-done)

**Ori**: You said it! A big shalom!  
_The round Nubbin waves again. Bump! Down it goes._  
_It lies on the grass. It looks very pleased._  
**Ori**: I will call it the Wobbler. It is my favourite.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Social awareness, ask): helping someone new feel welcome  
> **Ori**: I’m new here. Everything is new!  
> _What helps a new friend feel welcome?_  
> - ✅ Smile and say shalom → Shalom! That feels nice. Thank you.
> - Run away and hide → Feeling shy is OK. A little wave helps too!
> _For grown-ups: Talked about how to help someone new feel welcome: a smile and a hello._

**Ori**: Shh. Don’t tell the other Nubbins.  

#### `sprout:lh1-s2` · [main](main.md#lh1-s2)

**Ori**: Nubbin school! I’m the coach. You help!  
_Three Nubbins: the Wobbler, a shy one, a sleepy one._  
**Ori**: Rule one: say thank you. תּוֹדָה (toda, “thank you”)!  
_(Say it: toe-DAH.)_  
**Ori**: Rule two: say please. בְּבַקָּשָׁה (bevakasha, “please”)!  
_(Say it: beh-vah-kah-SHAH. It’s a long one!)_  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, do): saying please and thank you  
> **Ori**: I say toda. You say toda!  
> _(Your turn: toe-DAH!)_  
> **Ori**: Now bevakasha. Please!  
> _(Your turn: beh-vah-kah-SHAH!)_  
> **Ori**: Toda! You have very good manners.  
> Check: `edu:say:he:toda`
> _For grown-ups: Said “toda” (thank you) and “bevakasha” (please) back to Ori, and talked about good manners._

**Ori**: When they wave, we cheer טוֹב (tov, “good”)!  
_(Say it: TOVE. Cheer it loud!)_  
_The shy Nubbin hides behind a dandelion. Peek… gone._  
_The Wobbler waves at nobody. Bump! Up again._  

**Choices**

- **Sit by the shy one and wait.**
    - You sit. You wait. Very quietly.
    - A tiny paw lifts. A little wave!
    - **Ori**: כֵּן (ken, “yes”)! She waved! Toda!
    - (Say it: KEN. A happy yes!)
- **Whisper shalom very softly.**
    - You whisper: shalom.
    - The shy one tilts her head. A tiny paw lifts!
    - **Ori**: כֵּן (ken, “yes”)! Quiet shalom for a quiet Nubbin.
    - (Say it: KEN. A happy yes!)

#### `sprout:lh1-s2-done` · [main](main.md#lh1-s2-done)

_The shy one waves. The sleepy one waves in its sleep._  
_The Wobbler waves. Bump! It waves from the ground._  
**Ori**: Three Nubbins! Toda, toda, toda!  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 3  
> _(Count the waving Nubbins together. Touch each one.)_  
> **Ori**: One, two, three! Three Nubbins waving. Tov!  
> Check: `edu:count:nubbins:3`
> _For grown-ups: Counted three Nubbins, touching each one once._

**You**: Bevakasha!  
**Ori**: Bevakasha means please. And you’re welcome! A busy word.  
**Ori**: Can the Wobbler stand up yet? לֹא (lo, “no”)!  
_(Say it: LOW. Shake your head as you say it.)_  

#### `sprout:lh1-s3` · [main](main.md#lh1-s3)

_The sun goes down. The lanterns blink on._  
**Ori**: Hebrew has a “good” for each time of day.  
**Ori**: Morning is בֹּקֶר טוֹב (boker tov, “good morning”).  
_(Say it: BO-ker TOVE.)_  
**Ori**: Evening is עֶרֶב טוֹב (erev tov, “good evening”). Like now!  
_(Say it: EH-rev TOVE.)_  
**Ori**: Bedtime is לַיְלָה טוֹב (laila tov, “good night”).  
_(Say it: LIE-la TOVE. Give a big yawn!)_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): the order of the day: morning, evening, night  
> **Ori**: First morning. Then evening. What comes next?  
> - ✅ Night! Laila tov! → Yes! Night comes after evening. Laila tov!
> - Morning again → Morning comes back after the night. Evening, then night, then morning!
> _For grown-ups: Put the parts of the day in order: morning, evening, night._

**Nova**: Say laila tov to everyone. Fern is in the orchard.  
_The Wobbler yawns, waves, and falls asleep where it lands._  

#### `sprout:lh1-s3-done` · [main](main.md#lh1-s3-done)

**Fern**: Laila tov! Did I say it right?  
**Ori**: Perfect, Fern! Toda!  
_Clover and Nova say laila tov too._  
_Three Nubbins sleep in a heap by Ori’s tent._  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, notice): why we sleep  
> **Ori**: Look. The Nubbins are fast asleep.  
> **Ori**: Sleep helps our bodies rest and grow. Nubbins too!  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that sleep helps our bodies rest and grow._

**Ori**: Laila tov, everybody. Tomorrow: shapes!  

#### `sprout:lh1-complete` · [main](main.md#lh1-complete)

_Morning! A new sign hangs on the gate._  
**Ori**: Boker tov! I made you a sign. It says shalom!  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Literacy, do): trace the shape ש (shin)  
> **Ori**: Look at this shape: ש. It’s called shin.  
> **Ori**: This shape says “sh”, like shalom.  
> **Ori**: Three arms up, like a goalie making a save!  
> _(Trace it with a finger. Start on the right side.)_  
> Check: `edu:trace:he:shin`
> _For grown-ups: Traced the Hebrew letter shape ש (shin) and heard that it says “sh”, as in shalom. Tracing only, not reading._

**Ori**: I made up a chant too. Say it after me!  
**Ori**: “Boker tov, the sun is out!  
**Ori**: Shalom, shalom, wave and shout!  
**Ori**: Erev tov, the lanterns glow,  
**Ori**: laila tov, and off we go!”  

> **Lesson** (music, ELOF: Language and Communication, do): a call-and-response chant with actions  
> _(Say the chant line by line. Your child says each line back.)_  
> _(Stretch up on “boker tov”. Wave on “shalom”. Yawn on “laila tov”.)_  
> **Ori**: Again! Louder! The Nubbins are joining in!  
> Check: `edu:chant:he:lh1`
> _For grown-ups: Joined in an original Hebrew greetings chant, line by line, with actions._

_The Wobbler waves with both arms. Wobble, wobble… it stays up!_  
**Ori**: It stayed up! Ken! Toda, Wobbler!  

**Notes:** Words in sprout LH1: step 1 shalom, beseder, ma shlomkha? / ma shlomekh? (in the choice only); step 2 toda, bevakasha, tov, ken, lo; step 3 boker tov, erev tov, laila tov. lh1-s1 keeps all three choices; the player picks how Ori asks, and all three play alike. The game never assumes the player’s gender. The chant in lh1-complete is the base file’s original chant, spoken in rhythm, not sung to any known tune. The Wobbler falls over whenever it waves, and stays up for the first time in lh1-complete, as in the base. Sprout band: a grown-up reads every line aloud. Narrator lines in brackets are prompts for the grown-up, not text to perform. Fonts: Noto Sans Hebrew (or similar) for every Hebrew string, with niqqud rendered correctly. Bidi: wrap each Hebrew run in an isolate (U+2067 RLI … U+2069 PDI, or <bdi dir="rtl">) so brackets and punctuation stay on the correct side. Audio: every Hebrew word needs a native-speaker recording (the VocabItem.audio clips in HEBREW_COURSE), never TTS. Tapping a Hebrew word plays its clip; this matters most in sprout, where the grown-up may not know the sounds. Tracing: one letter shape per quest, shown large, as a shape to trace, never to read. Hebrew letters are written right to left: the tracing guide starts each stroke from the right side; do not invent a stroke order beyond that. Call-and-response beats wait for the child: show a big “your turn” bubble and a replay button, with no timer.

## LH2. Right to Left

- `lh2-step1`: Find four painted pebbles round the garden. Trace each one. · _Look by the pond, the gate, the orchard and Ori’s tent. Start on the right side._
- `lh2-step2`: Trace some shapes in the sand with Ori. · _Ori draws it first. Go over it with a stick. Start on the right side._
- `lh2-step3`: Help Ori paint his name on his tent. · _Tap the paintbrush on the tent. Ori helps. Start on the right side._

#### `sprout:lh2-s1` · [main](main.md#lh2-s1)

**Ori**: שָׁלוֹם (shalom, “hello”)! Today: Hebrew letters!  
_(Say it: sha-LOME. Wave!)_  
**Ori**: Hebrew goes the other way. Right to left!  
_(Hold up your right hand. Hebrew starts on that side.)_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Literacy, do): Hebrew goes right to left  
> _(Run a finger across the page, from right to left.)_  
> **Ori**: Whoosh! That’s the Hebrew way. Right to left.  
> _(Now left to right. That’s the English way.)_  
> Check: `edu:point:he:right-to-left`
> _For grown-ups: Showed with a finger that Hebrew is written right to left, and English left to right._

_The Wobbler hears “right to left”. It walks backwards!_  
**Ori**: I painted shapes on four pebbles. Together they make shalom.  
**Ori**: I hid them. By the pond, the gate, the orchard and my tent.  
_The Wobbler sets off to help. Backwards. Bump!_  

#### `sprout:lh2-s1-done` · [main](main.md#lh2-s1-done)

_Four pebbles sit on the path. Clink, clink, clink, clink._  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 4  
> _(Count the pebbles together on your fingers.)_  
> **Ori**: One, two, three, four! Four shapes make shalom.  
> Check: `edu:count:pebbles:4`
> _For grown-ups: Counted four pebbles, one finger for each._

**Ori**: You found them all! תּוֹדָה (toda, “thank you”)!  
_(Say it: toe-DAH.)_  
**Ori**: Now line them up. Start on the right!  
_The Wobbler arrives, backwards. It sits on a pebble._  
**Ori**: Off, Wobbler! That pebble is part of shalom.  

#### `sprout:lh2-s2` · [main](main.md#lh2-s2)

_By the pond, the mud is dry and smooth. Like sand._  
**Ori**: Hebrew letters are called the alef-bet.  
**Ori**: The first shape is alef. Here it is: א.  
**Ori**: Alef is the quiet shape. It makes no sound!  
**Ori**: It holds a sound for its friends. Like a cup holds tea.  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Literacy, do): trace the shape א (alef)  
> _(Trace א with a finger. Start on the right side.)_  
> **Ori**: A quiet shape. Shh!  
> _(Put a finger on your lips. Alef is quiet.)_  
> Check: `edu:trace:he:alef`
> _For grown-ups: Traced the Hebrew letter shape א (alef), the first letter, which makes no sound of its own. Tracing only, not reading._

**Ori**: The next letter is called bet. I’ll draw that one.  
_The Wobbler walks backwards. Plop! Into the pond._  
_It climbs out, dripping and delighted._  
**Ori**: Your stick is ready. Alef first, or bet first?  

**Choices**

- **Alef first. The quiet one.**
    - **Ori**: Alef first. The quiet ones go first today.
- **Bet first!**
    - **Ori**: Bet first! I’ll draw bet. Then you trace alef.

#### `sprout:lh2-s2-done` · [main](main.md#lh2-s2-done)

_In the sand: a wobbly alef. It looks very proud._  
**Ori**: Alef! The first letter. And the quiet one.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-awareness, notice): first tries can be wobbly, and that is fine  
> **Ori**: Your alef is a bit wobbly. That’s fine!  
> **Ori**: First tries are wobbly. Mine were too. You keep going.  
> _For grown-ups: Heard that first tries are often wobbly, and that practice helps._

**Ori**: Next, the best word in the world. My name!  
_The Wobbler shakes itself dry. All over Ori._  
**Ori**: Thank you, Wobbler. Very refreshing.  

#### `sprout:lh2-s3` · [main](main.md#lh2-s3)

_Ori’s tent is green and a bit crooked._  
_A football scarf hangs on the pole._  
**Ori**: My name in Hebrew is אוֹרִי (Ori, “my light”).  
_(Say it: OH-ree.)_  
**Ori**: אוֹר (or) means light. Ori means “my light”.  
_(Say it: ORE. Like the oar on a rowing boat.)_  
**Ori**: I love light. Stars are my favourite!  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, ask): things that give light  
> **Ori**: Or means light. What gives us light?  
> - ✅ A star → Yes! Stars shine. So do the sun and lanterns.
> - A stone → A stone doesn’t shine. Stars, the sun and lanterns do!
> _For grown-ups: Talked about things that give light, like stars, the sun and lanterns._

_The Wobbler watches closely. It is facing the wrong way._  
**Ori**: Help me paint my name. Start on the right!  

**Choices**

- **Paint it big!**
    - **Ori**: Big! You can see it from the hill!
- **Paint it small and neat.**
    - **Ori**: Small and neat. Like a famous person’s name.
- **Paint it, then add a star.**
    - **Ori**: A star! A light next to “my light”!

#### `sprout:lh2-s3-done` · [main](main.md#lh2-s3-done)

_On the tent, in fresh paint: אוֹרִי._  
**Ori**: That’s me! You helped paint my name.  
**Ori**: Nobody here has done that before.  

> **Lesson** (sel, ELOF: Social and Emotional Development, ask): noticing how someone feels  
> _Look at Ori’s face. How does he feel?_  
> - ✅ Happy and proud → Very happy. My name, on my tent, by my friend!
> - Cross → Look at his big smile. Ori feels happy!
> _For grown-ups: Looked at Ori’s face and named his feeling: happy and proud._

_The Wobbler turns round at last. It waves at the name._  

#### `sprout:lh2-complete` · [main](main.md#lh2-complete)

_At dusk, Ori lays flat stones on the path._  
**Ori**: Stepping stones! Each one has a letter.  
**Ori**: Hebrew has twenty-two letters. You met alef!  
**Ori**: Hop along them, right to left!  

> **Lesson** (health, ELOF: Perceptual, Motor, and Physical Development, do): hopping in one direction

> **Ori**: Right to left. The Hebrew way!

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Literacy, ask): review: which way Hebrew goes

> - Left to right → That’s English! Hebrew goes right to left. Whoosh!
> _For grown-ups: Remembered that Hebrew goes right to left._

## LH3. Counting Plums

- `lh3-step1`: Help Fern count plums in Hebrew. · _Tap each plum as it goes in the basket. Say its number with Ori._
- `lh3-step2`: Play Ori’s “How Many?” football game. · _Watch each goal. Then tap the number. Ori helps if you get stuck._
- `lh3-step3`: Count ten stars with Ori. · _Go up the hill after dark. Tap a star to count it._

#### `sprout:lh3-s1` · [main](main.md#lh3-s1)

_Fern’s plum trees are full. Plop! Plop!_  
**Fern**: I need ten plums in each basket. I keep losing count!  
**Ori**: I’ll help. I count in Hebrew!  
**Ori**: אַחַת (achat, “one”). שְׁתַּיִם (shtayim, “two”). שָׁלוֹשׁ (shalosh, “three”).  
_(Say them: ah-KHAT, SHTAI-yim, sha-LOSH.)_  
**Ori**: אַרְבַּע (arba, “four”). חָמֵשׁ (chamesh, “five”).  
_(Say them: ar-BAH, kha-MESH. The “kh” is throaty, like “loch”.)_  

> **Lesson** (maths, K.CC.B.5, do): count to 5 in Hebrew  
> _(Hold up one finger for each plum.)_  
> **Ori**: Achat, shtayim, shalosh, arba, chamesh!  
> _(Say them again together, with your fingers.)_  
> **Fern**: Five plums! Halfway there!  
> Check: `edu:count:he:plums:5`
> _For grown-ups: Counted five plums in Hebrew (achat to chamesh), one finger for each._

**Fern**: Shtayim… it sounds like a sneeze!  
_The Wobbler catches the falling plums. Mostly with its face._  

**Choices**

- **I’ll count. You check.**
    - **Ori**: Deal! I’ll cheer every number.
- **Let Fern count. We’ll cheer.**
    - **Fern**: No pressure, then!
    - **Ori**: Big cheers! Achat!

#### `sprout:lh3-s1-done` · [main](main.md#lh3-s1-done)

_Plop, plop, plop. The basket fills up._  
**Fern**: Ten plums! What is ten in Hebrew?  
**Ori**: עֶשֶׂר (eser, “ten”)! Ten is my favourite number.  
_(Say it: EH-ser. Hold up ten fingers!)_  
**Ori**: It’s my football shirt number.  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Relationship skills, ask): asking for help  
> **Fern**: I lost count, so I asked Ori for help.  
> **Fern**: What can you do when something is tricky?  
> - ✅ Ask a friend for help → Yes! Asking for help is brave and clever.
> - Give up → Let’s not give up. Asking a friend helps!
> _For grown-ups: Talked about asking a friend for help when something is tricky._

_The Wobbler has a plum on its head. Nobody mentions it._  

#### `sprout:lh3-s2` · [main](main.md#lh3-s2)

_Ori puts four jumpers on the grass. Two goals!_  
**Ori**: The game is called “How Many?” I shoot. You count!  
**Ori**: Big numbers now. שֵׁשׁ (shesh, “six”). שֶׁבַע (sheva, “seven”).  
_(Say them: SHESH, SHEH-vah.)_  
**Ori**: שְׁמוֹנֶה (shmone, “eight”). תֵּשַׁע (tesha, “nine”). And eser, ten!  
_(Say them: shmo-NEH, TEH-sha, EH-ser.)_  

> **Lesson** (literacy, ELOF: Language and Communication, do): say the Hebrew numbers 6 to 10: call and response  
> **Ori**: I say shesh. You say shesh!  
> _(Ori says each number. Your child says it back.)_  
> **Ori**: Shesh! Sheva! Shmone! Tesha! Eser!  
> **Ori**: Perfect! You count like a referee.  
> Check: `edu:say:he:numbers:6-10`
> _For grown-ups: Repeated the Hebrew numbers six to ten (shesh to eser) after Ori._

_The Wobbler is in goal. It waves at the ball._  
**Ori**: I am very good at football. And very bad at losing.  
**Ori**: Ready? Achat, shtayim, shalosh, GO!  

**Choices**

- **Go easy on me!**
    - **Ori**: Never! …OK, a little bit. For one shot.
- **Bring it on!**
    - **Ori**: Now you sound like a footballer!

#### `sprout:lh3-s2-done` · [main](main.md#lh3-s2-done)

_Ori scores ten goals. Eser!_  
_Then the ball bounces off the Wobbler’s tummy._  
_It rolls all the way down the grass. Into Ori’s goal!_  
**Ori**: The Wobbler scored one? Achat? ACHAT?  
**Ori**: Best of ten! Best of twenty!  
**Nova**: Ori.  
**Ori**: …בְּסֵדֶר (beseder, “OK”). Well played, Wobbler.  
_(Say it: beh-SEH-der.)_  

> **Lesson** (sel, CASEL: Self-management, ask): being a good sport  
> _Ori let in one goal. He feels cross._  
> _What can we say when a game ends?_  
> - ✅ Well played! → Well played. You’re right. That feels better.
> - I’m the best! → Hmm. That’s what I nearly said. “Well played” is kinder.
> _For grown-ups: Talked about being a good sport: saying “well played” after a game, even when cross._


#### `sprout:lh3-s3` · [main](main.md#lh3-s3)

_Night. You and Ori climb the hill with a blanket._  
**Ori**: Look up! So many stars!  
**Ori**: A star is a כּוֹכָב (kochav, “star”).  
_(Say it: ko-KHAV. Point up at the sky!)_  

> **Lesson** (science, ELOF: Scientific Reasoning, notice): we see stars at night
