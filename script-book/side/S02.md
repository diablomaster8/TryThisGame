# Side story S2: Wisp

## S2. Wisp

*The Lord of the Rings: the wretched creature spared out of pity* · audience: all · where: The edge of the long grass, where you leave fruit for Sable · when: Chapter 3, the morning after you first leave fruit out for Sable (after c3-open). The decision falls in chapter 7.

**Cast:** Wisp (Gollum (The Lord of the Rings))

### Step 1: Sit up after dark by Sable’s fruit, and keep still.

Hint: Leave fruit at the edge of the long grass, then wait nearby at night without moving. · check: `watch:night:sable-fruit`

#### <a id="s2-fruit"></a>Scene `s2-fruit`

_Morning. The fruit you left for Sable is gone._  
_That would be good news, but Sable is curled in the long grass looking thinner, not fatter._  
_There is frost on the grass round her, though it is nearly midsummer._  
**Luna** _(think)_: You noticed. Good.  
**Luna**: I saw her eat one piece last night. Then something else came out of the dark and took the rest.  
**Luna**: Not shared. Took.  
**You**: Another Nubbin?  
**Luna** _(sad)_: A grey one. Thinner than her. He moves like he’s always expecting a boot.  
**Luna**: Sit up tonight. Leave the fruit where you always do, and keep still. Still as a post.  
**Luna**: He wants something here. I’d like to know what, before he takes it.  

#### <a id="s2-thief"></a>Done `s2-thief`

_Past midnight. Sable creeps out of the grass to the fruit and takes one bite._  
_Then the grass parts again, and something comes through: grey, thin as a stick, one ear torn to a fringe._  
_He shoulders her aside, not hard, and drags the fruit towards the dark. Sable lets him._  
_Then he sees your lantern. He creeps to it and presses his cheek against the warm glass._  
_He is shaking. He keeps shaking, even there._  
_Then he sees you._  
**Wisp** _(angry)_: Hsss. Hers. Not yours. Not hands.  
_And under the hiss, much smaller, another voice._  
**Wisp** _(soft)_: …she ate. she ate today. good.  
_He is gone before you can breathe out. Sable looks at the place where he went for a long time._  
**Luna** _(shock)_: Greys don’t talk. They don’t. I’ve known a dozen.  
**Luna** _(think)_: Something’s talking through the cracks in that one. Two somethings.  
**Luna** _(soft)_: Call him Wisp. He’ll hate it. Everyone should have a name they hate a little.  

### Step 2: Keep an eye on Wisp. When the weather turns, find where he sleeps.

Hint: This picks up in the chapter 6 rain. The warm things went somewhere warm. · check: `find:wisp:rain`

#### <a id="s2-missing"></a>Scene `s2-missing`

_Over the next days, things go missing from the Grove. Not only fruit now. Warm things._  
**Fern** _(think)_: Someone’s had my left glove. Left me the right. I can’t decide if that’s cruel or considerate.  
_A sock off the line. A candle stub from the cottage window. The tea cosy, which nobody will admit to missing._  
_And the round stone Hazel kept by the hearth for warming eggs. Its dish is empty._  
**Clover** _(sad)_: He’s cold. That’s all it is. Can’t we feed him too? I can see the light through his ears.  
**Luna** _(angry)_: Shoo him off, Clover. I mean it.  
**Luna**: Every time Sable gets near a hand, he hisses at it. And she listens. She was taught to listen to that voice.  
**Clover** _(soft)_: He was taught too. Somebody taught him.  
**Luna** _(sad)_: …I know. It doesn’t make him any quieter.  
_In the fork of the fruit tree, Wisp licks his torn ear and watches you, with the patience of something that has been right before._  

**Choices**

- **Put down two piles of fruit: one each.** — sets `{"s2-early":"fed"}`
    - Wisp waits until you are far away and eats his pile. Then he eats most of hers.
    - **Clover**: That’s not quite how I pictured it. But he did eat.
- **Clap your hands and shoo him off.** — sets `{"s2-early":"shooed"}`
    - Wisp bolts to the edge of the grass and no further. Sable flinches at the clap too.
    - **Luna**: Not the hands. Never the hands. Use your voice next time.
- **Sit down where you are, and wait.** — sets `{"s2-early":"waited"}`
    - You sit. Wisp hisses until he runs out of hiss, and Sable eats, watching you both.
    - **Luna**: That works on her. I didn’t know it worked on him.

#### <a id="s2-rain"></a>Done `s2-rain`

_It has rained for a day and a night. The stream is over its stones, brown and loud._  
_Behind the cottage, where the chimney bricks hold the day’s heat, the woodpile has slumped into a pool._  
_Under it is a nest, or what is left of one: Fern’s glove, the sock, the tea cosy, all sodden through._  
_And Wisp, on the last dry log, shaking so hard the water ripples round him. Hazel’s egg stone is in his paws._  
**Wisp** _(soft)_: mine. warm. mine.  
**Wisp** _(soft)_: …was warm. was.  
_He tries to stand, and his legs fold. He is too cold to hiss._  
**Clover** _(sad)_: The bathhouse is warm. Hazel kept it warm for exactly this. Please.  
**Luna** _(soft)_: If you carry him, he’ll fight. Hold him anyway. Hold him like you expected it.  
_Across the yard, in the long grass, Sable is watching. She has seen hands pick up a Nubbin before._  

**Choices**

- **Pick him up. Hold on.** — sets `{"s2-carried":true}`
    - He fights, then stops fighting. He weighs less than the stone he won’t let go of.
    - In the steam of the bathhouse his fur lifts and his eyes go sharp. He looks at your hands, and bites one, hard.
    - **Wisp**: Hsss. Hands. Hands, hands.
    - He scrambles to the warmest corner with the stone, and glares until his tail stops dripping.
    - **Clover**: He’s warm now. Look at him. Warm right through.
    - He is. And he is still shaking.
- **Fetch a blanket, and let Clover lift him.** — sets `{"s2-carried":false}`
    - Clover wraps him up like a loaf and carries him in at a run, talking to him the whole way.
    - In the steam of the bathhouse his fur lifts and his eyes go sharp. He bites her, hard, through the blanket.
    - **Clover**: Ow. Yes. All right. That’s fair.
    - He scrambles to the warmest corner with the stone. He is warm now, right through.
    - And he is still shaking.

### Step 3: Keep winning Sable’s trust. Wisp is never far from her.

Hint: This picks up in chapter 7. Once Sable leans on you, visit Luna’s night garden after dark. · check: `night:luna-garden:chapter-7`

#### <a id="s2-number"></a>Scene `s2-number`

**Clover** _(sad)_: Warm. Dry. Fed. And look at him.  
**Luna** _(sad)_: …Then it was never the cold.  
_Nobody says anything for a while. The rain drums on the bathhouse roof._  
_High on Wisp’s flank, half washed off, is a number someone once painted on in grey._  
**Luna** _(think)_: That paint. I’ve seen that paint before. Square numbers, very neat.  
**Luna**: I’m going to ask around. Don’t tell Sable. I know that’s silly.  
_When you come out, Sable is by the bathhouse door. She sniffs your hands very carefully, and doesn’t run._  

#### <a id="s2-night"></a>Done `s2-night`

_Night, from Luna’s garden, with the moonflowers open. Two grey shapes sit at the edge of the Grove._  
**Luna** _(soft)_: Get down. Listen.  
_Wisp is close to Sable, closer than he lets anyone get. Tonight his voice is all whisper._  
**Wisp** _(soft)_: hands are for throwing. they all throw. in the end.  
**Wisp** _(soft)_: come back to the dark. no hands there. the dark never threw anyone.  
_Sable doesn’t answer. She looks at Wisp. Then she looks at the cottage, at your window._  
_She doesn’t move towards either._  
**Luna** _(think)_: Moss says the cold leaves them half a voice. That’s the only reason he can talk at all.  
**Luna** _(angry)_: And he’s spending every bit of it talking her out of the Grove.  
_Clover has crept up behind you in a dressing gown, holding a bag of fruit as if it might go off._  
**Clover** _(soft)_: You don’t feed him because he’s earned it. You feed him because you’re you.  
**Luna** _(angry)_: He bit the hand that carried him, Clover.  
**Clover** _(soft)_: He was cold. I’d have bitten too.  
**Luna** _(think)_: She leaned on a shin this week. A shin. Do you know how far that is, for her?  
**Luna**: One more month of that voice in her ear and she’ll be back under a hedge. She won’t come out twice.  
**Luna** _(sad)_: I’m not choosing against him. I’m choosing her. Somebody has to.  
**Clover** _(sad)_: And where does he go? Back to the dark he keeps talking about. On his own. Talking to nobody.  
_Neither of them looks at you. They both very carefully don’t._  

**Choices**

- **Keep feeding him. Even after the bite.** — sets `{"wisp":"spared"}`
    - In the morning you put down two piles of fruit, not one. And the next morning, and the one after.
    - Wisp eats his with his back to you. On the fourth morning, he eats it facing you.
    - **Wisp**: hands are for throwing. hands are for… not hers. maybe not hers.
    - Sable sees. Sable sees everything. She comes one step closer than she did yesterday.
    - It isn’t free. Your other sock goes missing on the fifth morning, and the hiss is still there, most nights.
    - **Luna**: He’s still talking to her. I can hear him. I hope you’re right.
- **Drive him off. Sable needs the quiet.** — sets `{"wisp":"driven"}`
    - You walk at him, steady, not fast, until he backs into the long grass. Then further. Then gone.
    - **Wisp**: Hsss. See? See? Hands.
    - Sable watches him go and doesn’t follow. She doesn’t look at you for the rest of the night, either.
    - But near dawn she eats her whole pile, every scrap, and sleeps in the open where you can see her.
    - **Luna**: It was the sensible thing. I keep telling myself so.
    - **Clover**: I know it was. I’m still not speaking to you till Thursday.
- **Let Sable decide.** — sets `{"wisp":"sable"}`
    - You put down one pile of fruit between them, and step back.
    - Sable looks at it, then at you. Then she pushes half the pile across the grass with her nose, to Wisp.
    - **Wisp**: …hers. she gave. hers.
    - He eats it at the very edge of the Grove. When he has finished, he doesn’t leave. He just doesn’t come in.
    - **Luna**: Well. That’s a better answer than either of ours.
    - **Clover**: Or we’ve just handed the smallest one here the biggest choice, because we couldn’t make it.
    - **Luna**: …Yes. That too.

### Complete

#### <a id="s2-complete"></a>Complete `s2-complete`

_Morning. Frost on the grass at the edge of the Grove, in two small circles where two grey things slept._  
_Wisp is curled in one of them, the egg stone between his paws. It has been cold for weeks. He keeps it anyway._  
**Fern** _(happy)_: He’s given me back my left glove. Now the right one’s gone. I think it’s a rotation.  
_Under the cottage step, something hisses. It sounds pleased with itself._  
**Moss** _(think)_: He’ll never give that back, you know. Not if he lives to be as old as me.  
**Moss** _(soft)_: Feed them before you feed yourself. She never said only the good ones. I checked. I was there.  
_…_  
**Moss** _(soft)_: Grey things remember who fed them. That’s all I’ll say. I’m old, and I’ve seen it go both ways.

**Notes:** WISP. A thin grey (Shadow) Nubbin, one ear torn to a fringe, a half-washed grey number on his flank (Corvin’s row paint; hint only, Luna learns where Sable came from in chapter 7 proper). He covets warm things. Greys do not talk: Wisp speaks only with the shard’s echo (bible, The Stillness), in two voices, never more than a few broken words. The hiss: mood angry, capitalised, harsh blip, jagged text box. The whisper: mood soft, lowercase, faint breathy blip. He is warm after the bathhouse and still trembles: the trembling is fear, not cold. Do not stop it anywhere; the first time he is not trembling is the chapter 10 dawn (bible), and that is the payoff. TIMING. Step 1 opens in chapter 3 the morning after the player first leaves fruit for Sable. s2-missing plays straight after s2-thief (it narrates the days that follow). The find:wisp:rain check becomes possible only in the chapter 6 rain, behind the cottage at the woodpile; s2-rain plays there. s2-number plays straight after the carry. The night:luna-garden:chapter-7 check is met at the first night after c7-close (chapter 7’s goal is Sable trust 45, and Moss has explained the Stillness in that chapter, which Luna refers to). s2-night holds the decision; the complete scene plays the next morning. If Sable has left the Grove before the chapter 7 night, the story stops there and wisp stays unset (epilogues read it as never met). No trust penalties: S2 does not slow the campaign. VARIANTS. The complete scene s2-complete is for wisp = spared. For wisp = sable play s2-complete-sable instead; for wisp = driven play s2-complete-driven instead (both in SIDE_STORIES_MAIN_VARIANTS). WHAT IT CHANGES. Nothing in chapters 8 or 9 (no back way, no third-row cameo; chapter 9 has no side-story options). At the Long Night (chapter 10), if wisp is spared or sable, Wisp, who has wanted the warm stone all along, snatches the shard from the bearer at the water’s edge (“mine — warm — mine”) and tumbles into the Wellspring; it unmakes (stoneUnmadeBy = wisp). Otherwise Moss takes it in (stoneUnmadeBy = moss). His hoarding of Hazel’s cold egg stone here is the setup; he never gives it back. Epilogue lines: spared, Sable’s choice, driven off, or never met. Small flags: s2-early (fed | shooed | waited) and s2-carried (bool) are flavour only. s2-carried = false means Clover carried him; nothing reads it except optional epilogue colour. HOME. If spared (or Sable’s choice), Wisp sleeps at the edge of the long grass until the story ends, then settles under the cottage step (bible). The bathhouse in s2-rain and s2-number is optional decor; it is never his home.
