# Side story S3: The Cycle

## S3. The Cycle

*Hazel’s Grove original: the Sun and Moon feud (story bible, side-story table)* · audience: all · where: The pond at Lantern Night; then the Circuit stands · when: Opens in chapter 4 at Lantern Night (c4-close), where the Keepers stand in two knots by the pond. Its first scene, s3-stands, waits for the Circuit: chapter 5, when you arrive for the race after c5-open.

### Step 1: Ask one Sun Keeper and one Moon Keeper what really happened at Midsummer.

Hint: Fern, Marina, Sparks, Clover and Pixel are Sun. Luna, Ember, Byte, Dusty and Nova are Moon. Ask one of each. · check: `talk:midsummer:sun-and-moon`

#### <a id="s3-stands"></a>Scene `s3-stands`

_The Circuit stands are split down the middle: Sun Keepers on the left, Moon Keepers on the right, an empty bench between._  
**Fern** _(think)_: Don’t sit there. That’s the gap. Nobody sits in the gap.  
**You**: Why not?  
**Fern** _(think)_: Because it’s the gap. Hazel used to sit in it every race, on purpose, to annoy everyone.  
_Down at the front stand two tea urns, one with a sun painted on it and one with a moon. Each queue is certain the other urn’s tea is worse._  
**Sparks** _(shock)_: Midsummer, years back, Fern’s Bramble went over the cliff rail, Sun says Ash barged her, Moon says rigged, nobody’s raced together since, OK bye.  
_Sparks sits down very fast and studies the race card as if it holds the meaning of life._  
**Sparks**: I didn’t say any of that. That was the wind.  
_Across the gap, the Moon side is pretending not to listen, and so is the Sun side. Both are bad at it._  
_Up in the owners’ box, Corvin isn’t watching the start line. He’s watching the stands._  

**Choices**

- **Sit in the gap, like Hazel did.** — sets `{"s3-seat":"gap"}`
    - You sit in the gap. Five heads turn from the left, and five from the right.
    - **Fern**: Oh, you’re hers all right.
    - **Byte**: That is statistically the worst seat. You can’t see the cliff bend. I’ve checked.
- **Sit with the Sun side.** — sets `{"s3-seat":"sun"}`
    - **Sparks**: YES. Sun gets the new Keeper! Sun gets the new Keeper!
    - Across the gap, Luna writes something in a very small notebook.
- **Sit with the Moon side.** — sets `{"s3-seat":"moon"}`
    - **Byte**: Welcome. Our biscuits are better. I ran a blind taste test. It was mostly blind.
    - **Fern**: Traitor. Lovely to see you. Traitor.

#### <a id="s3-two-stories"></a>Done `s3-two-stories`

_You get two stories. They agree about the weather and nothing else._  
_Sun: Ember’s Ash came round the cliff bend like a cannonball, and Fern’s Bramble went over the rail._  
_Moon: the cliff-bend flag was in the wrong place, so Ash went wide where the flag told her to._  
**Byte** _(think)_: I have a chart. Both stories, colour-coded. The colours are also disputed.  
**Byte**: Two hundred and fourteen grievances in all. Three are about the race. The rest are about the urns.  
**Byte** _(think)_: I have never tasted the Sun tea. On principle. The principle is also disputed.  
**Ember** _(angry)_: After Midsummer, nobody would race Ash. Not Sun. Not Moon. Do you know who would?  
**Ember** _(sad)_: Him. He said he’d make her a champion.  
_Nobody, on either side, talks about Bramble after the rail. The story just stops there, the way a path stops at a cliff._  

### Step 2: Help Sparks and Byte develop the old Midsummer photo-finish plates.

Hint: From chapter 6, go to Sparks’s workshop. Sparks works the trays, Byte works the timer, and you stop them arguing. · check: `develop:plates:sparks-workshop`

#### <a id="s3-plates"></a>Scene `s3-plates`

_Sparks turns up at your gate looking like someone who has swallowed a secret and it’s fizzing._  
**Sparks** _(shock)_: OK. OK OK OK. Confession. I’ve got the photo-finish plates from Midsummer. The camera on the cliff bend. Mine.  
**Sparks** _(sad)_: I never developed them. If it was Ash, Fern would never forgive Ember. If it wasn’t, I’d never forgive me.  
**Sparks**: Trouble is, my darkroom timer’s broken. And the only person in the valley with a timer that works is…  
_She can’t say it. She points up the lane towards the Moon end, at a cottage with labels on its windows._  
**Sparks** _(angry)_: Him.  

**Choices**

- **Fetch Byte. Tell him it’s for science.** — sets `{"s3-byte":"science"}`
    - **Byte**: Science? You should have opened with that. I’ll bring three timers. One to check the other two.
- **Fetch Byte. Tell him Sparks asked nicely.** — sets `{"s3-byte":"nicely"}`
    - **Byte**: She did not.
    - You admit she did not.
    - **Byte**: I’ll come anyway. Someone has to see it done properly.

#### <a id="s3-flag"></a>Done `s3-flag`

_Sparks’s darkroom is red and cramped and smells of vinegar. Byte times, Sparks rocks the trays, and they argue in whispers._  
**Byte** _(think)_: Rock on the count. One, and two, and— that was not two. That was one and a half.  
**Sparks** _(angry)_: I rock on FEELING, Byte.  
_The first plate swims up out of the dark: an enormous, blurred, perfectly exposed thumb._  
**Byte**: Your thumb.  
**Sparks** _(angry)_: It’s a GOOD thumb.  
_The second plate swims up slower, on the count: the cliff bend at dawn, before the race. An empty course, and a flag._  
_And a man in a grey Circuit coat, pulling the flag up and moving it._  
**Byte** _(think)_: Two posts nearer the edge. Look at the turf. Old hole there. New hole here.  
**Sparks** _(shock)_: Nobody on either side moved that flag. Nobody. It was a steward.  
_The last plate is the finish, with the owners’ box behind it. Corvin isn’t watching the race; he’s watching the stands._  
**Byte** _(sad)_: The flag sent them wide. Ash went wide because the course told her to. Bramble went wide because Ash did.  
**Sparks** _(shock)_: We’ve been fighting about the wrong thing for years. Byte. We have two URNS.  
**Byte**: The urns were your idea.  
**Sparks** _(angry)_: They were a GOOD idea at the TIME.  
**Byte**: There’s a thumb on the finish plate too. In the corner.  
**Sparks** _(happy)_: It’s my signature.  

### Step 3: Show the plates to Fern and to Ember.

Hint: Fern is usually in her orchard. Ember is at the Circuit fence most evenings. · check: `show:item:midsummer-plates:fern-ember`

#### <a id="s3-courier"></a>Scene `s3-courier`

_Sparks wraps the plates in a tea towel with a sun on it. Byte wraps a second tea towel, with a moon on it, round that._  
**Sparks**: You show them. They both like you. It’s very annoying.  
**Byte** _(think)_: A neutral courier. I approve. I’d like that noted somewhere.  

**Choices**

- **Show them together, at the same time.** — sets `{"s3-shown":"together"}`
    - Getting Fern and Ember into one place takes a week. You manage it by telling each that the other won’t come.
    - **Fern**: You absolute snake. Hazel did that to me once.
- **Show them one at a time.** — sets `{"s3-shown":"apart"}`
    - You go to Fern first, then to Ember. Each asks whether the other has seen it yet.
    - By evening both are at the same fence, looking at the same plates. Both swear it’s a coincidence.

#### <a id="s3-truce"></a>Done `s3-truce`

_Fern looks at the plates for a long time, and so does Ember. Neither looks at the other._  
**Ember** _(angry)_: I don’t need to be forgiven. I need to know who to be angry at.  
**Ember** _(sad)_: Him. It’s always him. He had Ash by the autumn.  
**Fern** _(sad)_: All those years. I hated you because of a flag. That’s… honestly efficient of him.  
**Ember** _(think)_: There should be a Moot. Everyone. Both sides. Out in the open.  
**Fern** _(think)_: After midwinter. When there’s time to do it properly. And one urn.  
**Ember** _(soft)_: One urn.  
_It’s the first thing they’ve agreed on in years, and they both look faintly ill about it._  

### Step 4: Host the Lantern Moot at the pond, after the Long Night.

Hint: In chapter 11, tap the pond and choose Call a Moot. All ten Keepers come. · check: `host:moot:pond`

#### <a id="s3-invitations"></a>Scene `s3-invitations`

_Pixel has been put in charge of the invitations, which means there is a planning meeting about the invitations._  
**Pixel** _(think)_: Gold ink or silver? Gold is Sun. Silver is Moon. Black is a funeral. I’ve tested twelve inks. Everything means something.  
**Clover** _(soft)_: Green? Nobody’s green.  
**Pixel** _(shock)_: …Clover, that’s genius. Green. Nobody’s green.  
_Dusty has offered to dig a hole for the one urn. Nobody asked for a hole, and nobody has the heart to say so._  
**Nova** _(think)_: I’ve been to this valley four times and I still don’t know if I’m Sun or Moon. Somebody said it’s my scarf.  
**Nova** _(happy)_: I’ve decided I’m weather.  
**Luna** _(soft)_: Ember says she’s coming “to make sure it’s done right”. That’s Ember for yes.  

### Complete

#### <a id="s3-moot"></a>Complete `s3-moot`

_Evening at the pond. Ten Keepers, one urn, green invitations, and nobody sure where to stand._  
_Dusty has dug the hole. The one urn stands in it, so that it is exactly level with nobody. Dusty is very proud._  
**Gran Hazel** _(happy)_: A Moot! I haven’t been to a Moot since my hair was brown. Isn’t this awkward. I love it.  
_You set the plates on the bench by the pond, between the two groups._  
_Gran Hazel sits down on the bench first, right in the middle, and beams at both sides._  
_Both sides shuffle in to see the plates until there is no gap left. Nobody can say who started it._  
**Fern** _(happy)_: She’s done it again. Forty years, and still annoying everyone on purpose.  
**Sparks** _(sad)_: It was the flag. I had it in a box the whole time. I’m loud about everything except the one thing.  
**Fern**: Bramble went over that rail because a man moved a flag. Not because of Ash. Not because of anyone here.  
_Pixel pours the last of the sun urn and the last of the moon urn into the one urn. Byte tastes it, gravely._  
**Byte** _(think)_: Same tea. It was always the same tea. I’d like that read into the record.  
**Sparks** _(angry)_: Byte, I swear by every lantern in this valley—  
**Byte**: Into the record.  
**Ember** _(think)_: So. Midsummer. A Cup. Everybody runs. Same course, new flags, and I check them.  
**Fern**: We check them.  
**Byte** _(happy)_: I’ll check the checking.  
**Gran Hazel** _(happy)_: And who’s keeping the Moot, love? Somebody has to bang the spoon.  

**Choices**

- **Bang the spoon on the urn.** — sets `{"s3-moot":"spoon"}`
    - You bang the spoon on the urn, and ten Keepers stop talking at once. It is the most powerful you have ever felt.
    - **Byte**: Procedurally, that was a gavel. I’m noting that the gavel is a spoon.
    - **Pixel**: Ribbons! Gold and silver, stitched down the middle in green. One for every Nubbin who runs the Cup.
    - **Gran Hazel**: Moot-Keeper. Told you it’d suit.
- **Give the spoon to Fern and Ember to share.** — sets `{"s3-moot":"shared"}`
    - Fern and Ember hold the spoon between them and bang the urn together. Both miss, and the whole pond cheers.
    - **Pixel**: Ribbons! Gold and silver, stitched down the middle in green. I did a test run. The stitching is also disputed.
    - **Gran Hazel**: You’re still the Moot-Keeper, love. Somebody had to bring the spoon.

**Rewards**

- Midsummer Cup race (feature): A new yearly race on the cliff course that Sun and Moon Keepers run together, with new flags that everybody checks.
- Sun and Moon ribbons (cosmetic): A gold Sun ribbon and a silver Moon ribbon, stitched together down the middle in green. Any Nubbin can wear them.
- “Moot-Keeper” (title): A title for the Keeper who brought the whole valley to one urn. Shown on your Keeper card.
