# Side story S4: The Wellspring

## S4. The Wellspring

*Hazel’s Grove original: Marina, the falls and the ledge path (story bible, Places)* · audience: all · where: The Grove gate · when: After c5-close (the race won), once Fossil’s farewell in c6-open has played: Marina is waiting at the gate with a bucket. Never on the same morning as S5’s opening scene.

### Step 1: Check the water line Hazel painted on the rock by the falls.

Hint: The falls are past the pond, on the Hollow side. Tap the white mark on the rock beside the pool. · check: `inspect:falls:water-line`

#### <a id="s4-pools"></a>Scene `s4-pools`

**Marina** _(think)_: You won. Good. Come and see something that isn’t winning.  
_Past the ridge, Marina’s tide pools step down the rock like a row of bowls. Half of them are dry._  
_The anemones in the rest have shut up tight, like fists._  
**Marina** _(sad)_: They get what the stream gives them. This summer it’s giving them less. And what does come down is cold.  
**Marina**: Cold. In July.  
**Marina**: Hazel painted a line on the rock by the falls, years back. Where the water ought to come to. Go and look.  
**You**: Why not look yourself?  
**Marina** _(soft)_: I’ve looked forty times. I want someone else to tell me I’m wrong.  
**Marina** _(happy)_: Take a Nubbin. They like the falls. Mind the moss on the rocks. Not that Moss. The slippery kind.  

#### <a id="s4-line"></a>Done `s4-line`

_The falls are loud and white. On the dark rock beside the pool, someone has painted a thick white line._  
_The water sits a hand’s width below it. Above the water the rock is pale and dry where it should be dark._  
_Your Nubbin dips a paw in, pulls it out, and looks at you as if you did this._  
**Marina** _(sad)_: A hand. A whole hand.  
_Marina has followed you after all. She is standing at the edge of the pool with her shoes in her hand._  
**Marina** _(think)_: Something upstream is taking it. Something big enough to take a hand of water off the falls.  

### Step 2: Send a swimmer up the outflow to see what’s above the falls.

Hint: Choose Swim at the top of the pool. If none of your Nubbins can swim yet, Marina gives one a lesson first. · check: `swim:inspect:falls-dam`

#### <a id="s4-lesson"></a>Scene `s4-lesson`

**Marina** _(happy)_: Hazel traded me fruit for swimming lessons. For her Nubbins, not her. She swam like a dropped teapot.  
**Marina** _(soft)_: Fossil swam sideways, on purpose. Never once the way I taught her. I still check that corner of the pool.  
**Marina**: Lesson one: they float better than you’d think. Lesson two: they don’t think, so let them.  
_She wades in to her knees and holds your Nubbin under the chin, the way you’d steady a cup._  
_It kicks once, astonished, and doesn’t sink._  
**Marina**: There. Now up the outflow. Not far. Just far enough to see what’s up there.  

**Choices**

- **Wade in after them.** — sets `{"s4-swim":"with"}`
    - You get in up to your waist and stop, gasping. It is very, very cold.
    - **Marina**: Lesson three: Keepers sink. Out you get.
- **Stay on the bank and trust them.** — sets `{"s4-swim":"trust"}`
    - **Marina**: Good. That’s the hardest stroke there is: staying on the bank.

#### <a id="s4-dam"></a>Done `s4-dam`

_Your swimmer comes back down the outflow and shakes itself all over you, the way Nubbins do when water has been rude._  
_Caught in its fur: a grey thread, and a flake of fresh mortar._  
_Round the bend upstream you find it. A wall of new grey stone across the stream, with a sluice gate in it, shut._  
_Behind the wall the water lies flat and still and very blue, all the way up to the Circuit’s new swimming lanes._  
_Each lane has a rope, a number, and a neat sign: NO SPLASHING._  
**Marina** _(angry)_: No splashing. It’s water. Splashing is what it’s for.  
**Marina** _(angry)_: A swimming course. My pools are dying for a swimming course.  
**Marina** _(think)_: I’ve written to the Circuit three times. I got three forms back. Received. Filed. Very tidy.  

### Step 3: Walk the ledge path behind the falls with Marina.

Hint: Meet Marina at the pool in the evening. Keep close behind her on the ledge, and don’t stop to look down. · check: `walk:ledge:falls-with-marina`

#### <a id="s4-ledge-ask"></a>Scene `s4-ledge-ask`

**Marina** _(soft)_: Before we do anything about that wall, I want to show you something. Not because it helps.  
**Marina**: Because somebody besides me ought to know the way. Hazel knew it. Hazel’s away.  
_Beside the falls, half hidden by ferns, a narrow ledge runs straight into the roar._  
**Marina** _(think)_: The ledge path. Back to the rock. Eyes on my heels. They’re very good heels. Twenty years’ wear.  
**Marina**: Don’t look at the water. Don’t look up either. Up is worse. It doesn’t like being stared at.  
**Marina** _(happy)_: Rule five. Don’t be me at your age. Rule four is “Don’t be Sparks”, and at your age I was Sparks, with longer hair.  

#### <a id="s4-ledge"></a>Done `s4-ledge`

_Behind the falls the world turns to noise and spray. The rock under your hand is wet and soft with moss._  
_Halfway along, the ledge bends into the dark. From round the bend comes a breath of air, warm, smelling of stone._  
**Marina** _(soft)_: That’s the spring breathing. Where the first ones came up, if you believe Moss. I do, mostly.  
_Marina stops at the bend. She doesn’t go on._  
**Marina** _(soft)_: Further is for when it’s needed. Hazel’s rule. I’ve kept it twenty years.  
**Marina**: But now you know the way this far. That’s all a path is, really. Somebody else knowing it.  

### Step 4: Open the sluice gate on the Circuit’s dam, after dark.

Hint: Go to the dam at night with Marina and a strong Nubbin. The sluice sticks. · check: `open:sluice:falls-dam`

#### <a id="s4-sluice"></a>Scene `s4-sluice`

**Marina** _(angry)_: Three letters. Three forms. I’m done writing.  
**Marina**: We’re not breaking anything. Breaking is loud, and it gets you talked about. We’re opening a gate that ought to be open.  
_At night the dam is just a dark wall and the sound of held water. The sluice is a great wooden door in new stone._  
_Beside it, in a tidy hut, the Circuit’s night steward sits bolt upright in his chair, fast asleep, clipboard on his chest._  
_He snores in a perfect rhythm. In, out, and a thin little whistle. The clipboard rises and falls._  
**Marina** _(think)_: I’ve watched him three nights. Move on the snore. Freeze on the whistle. The whistle’s when they wake.  
**You**: “They”?  
**Marina**: Stewards. They’re all the same steward, as far as I can tell.  
_You creep to the sluice on a snore. Your Nubbin creeps beside you, one foot raised, and freezes on the whistle like a garden ornament._  
_It holds the pose through three more whistles. It is still holding it when the snore comes back._  
**Marina** _(soft)_: You can put your foot down now, pet.  
_It puts its foot down. It looks as if it would have preferred to win._  
_You try the sluice on the next snore. It doesn’t move. It is, unmistakably, stuck._  

**Choices**

- **Lift, then push.** — sets `{"s4-gate":"lift-push"}`
    - You lift, and push, the way the Grove gate taught you. The sluice groans, gives, and swings.
    - **Marina**: Where did you learn that?
    - **You**: Home.
- **Let your strongest Nubbin have a go.** — sets `{"s4-gate":"nubbin"}`
    - Your Nubbin puts its back into it and shoves, and nothing happens. Then it slips, lifts by accident, and the whole thing swings.
    - **Marina**: Hazel’s trick. Even the Nubbins know it.
- **Pin a polite note to the gate first.** — sets `{"s4-gate":"note"}`
    - You write: “Sluice opened for the tide pools, with thanks, from Hazel’s Grove.” Then you lift, and push, and it swings.
    - **Marina**: Polite. He’ll hate that more than anything.
    - In the hut, the steward’s hand rises in his sleep, pats the air where a note might be, and puts it in a tray that isn’t there.

#### <a id="s4-water"></a>Done `s4-water`

_Water pours through the gap, dark and loud, and goes tumbling off towards the falls as if it’s late for something._  
**Marina** _(soft)_: Listen.  
_Below you, the falls change their note: deeper and fuller, the way the rock remembers them._  
_In the hut, the steward stirs, says “Received,” quite clearly, and goes back to sleep._  
**Marina** _(happy)_: Filed, I expect.  
**Marina** _(think)_: Somebody will shut it again, probably. Then somebody will open it again. That’s most of keeping.  

### Complete

#### <a id="s4-complete"></a>Complete `s4-complete`

_A week later the tide pools are full to the brim. The anemones have opened, as if surprised to be asked._  
**Marina** _(happy)_: Look at them. Showing off.  
_In the deepest pool, wedged between two stones, something round and pearl-white is catching the light._  
_Your swimmer has seen it too. It dives before anyone can stop it: one kick, two, and a long silver string of bubbles._  
_It comes up with the thing balanced on its head, extremely pleased with itself, and sneezes. It is an egg._  
**Marina** _(shock)_: …Oh. Oh, you beauty.  
**Marina**: Once in a long while one comes down from behind the falls. Hazel saw one in forty years. It’s yours. You opened the gate.  
**Marina** _(soft)_: And your swimmers can dive here whenever they like. As deep as they want. I’ll teach them the rest.  
**Marina** _(happy)_: Take some shells for the Grove. Take the pool weed too; it’s lucky. It isn’t lucky. It smells. Take it anyway.  
_Up at the dam, someone has hung a neat painted sign on the open sluice: OPEN BY ORDER. It doesn’t say whose._  

**Rewards**

- Deep-swim diving (feature): Marina’s tide pools open for diving. Nubbins dive for shells and trinkets on the bottom, and it trains Swim.
- Pearl-white Spring egg (item): A rare egg that came down from behind the falls. It hatches a Nubbin in the rare pearl-white colour.
- Tide-pool decorations (decor): Shells, a little rock-pool basin, and a tangle of pool weed for the Grove. The weed smells a bit. It’s worth it.
