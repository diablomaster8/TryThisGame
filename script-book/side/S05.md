# Side story S5: Fossil’s Last Walk

## S5. Fossil’s Last Walk

*Hazel’s Grove original: Dusty and Fossil (story bible, chapter 6)* · audience: all · where: The Grove gate · when: Chapter 5, the first morning after c5-open: Dusty visits with Fossil. Must finish before c5-close.

**Cast:** Fossil

### Step 1: Carry Fossil to the cliff where Dusty found her egg.

Hint: The cliff is east, past the stream. Walk, don’t run. She’s very old and she likes to look at things. · check: `carry:fossil:cliff`

#### <a id="s5-list"></a>Scene `s5-list`

_Dusty is at the gate with a very old Nubbin in the crook of one arm and a much-folded bit of paper in the other hand._  
_The Nubbin is the colour of sand. When she blinks, she does it slowly, as if each blink costs something._  
**Dusty** _(happy)_: This is Fossil. I dug her egg out of the cliff when I was eleven. Don’t say the name isn’t original. I was eleven.  
**Dusty** _(sad)_: She’s fading. Not her glow. Her. You’ll have seen it, if you’ve looked.  
**Dusty**: Twenty years. You’d think I’d have run out of things to say to her. I’ve got a list.  
_Dusty unfolds the paper. It has been folded and unfolded so often it has gone soft as cloth._  
**Dusty** _(soft)_: Three things. My back’s gone; I can’t carry her and do the things. Would you?  
**Dusty** _(happy)_: One, the cliff. Two, Hazel’s first tree. Three, the water. She used to swim like she was late for something.  
_Fossil looks up at you, unhurried, and holds out her arms the way very small children do._  
_Fossil says nothing. Her slow blink lands on you, and stays._  

**Choices**

- **Lift her with both hands, like an egg.** — sets `{"s5-carry":"egg"}`
    - You lift her the way you’d lift a full cup. She settles against you, approving.
    - **Dusty**: That’s it. That’s exactly it.
- **Let her ride on your shoulder.** — sets `{"s5-carry":"shoulder"}`
    - She climbs up, slowly, and sits on your shoulder like a very old queen inspecting something ridiculous.
    - **Dusty**: Oh, she likes you. The last person who tried that got their ear nibbled.

#### <a id="s5-cliff"></a>Done `s5-cliff`

_At the foot of the cliff the rock is striped like a cake: brown, red, grey, brown. Dusty puts a hand flat on one stripe._  
**Dusty** _(think)_: Here. I was twelve. It was raining. I used a trowel.  
_Fossil leans out and taps the rock, twice, very firmly._  
**Dusty**: …Eleven. Fine. Eleven. And it was sunny.  
_Tap._  
**Dusty** _(happy)_: A spoon. It was a spoon. A big spoon. You try digging out a whole egg with a spoon. It took two days.  
_Tap._  
**Dusty** _(think)_: …Four days. And a teaspoon. I’d like it noted that I was extremely thorough.  
_Fossil lays her head against the rock and leaves it there a while. Dusty doesn’t say anything at all._  
**Dusty** _(soft)_: I tell it wrong on purpose. Every time. Twenty years, and she’s never once let me get away with it.  
_By her head a little fern grows out of a crack, curled tight. Pressed into the stone beside it is a much older one._  

### Step 2: Take Fossil to Hazel’s first tree and shake it once.

Hint: Hazel’s first tree is the low, crooked one across the stream. One shake only. · check: `shake:tree:hazels-first`

#### <a id="s5-tree-ask"></a>Scene `s5-tree-ask`

**Dusty** _(think)_: Next, Hazel’s first tree. The crooked one over the stream. Shake it once. She was very specific.  
**Dusty**: Well. I was. On her behalf.  
_Fossil falls asleep on the way. She wakes exactly as you reach the tree, as if she set an alarm._  

#### <a id="s5-tree"></a>Done `s5-tree`

_Every Nubbin you own has followed you here. They sit in a row under the branches with their mouths open._  
**Dusty** _(think)_: Once. Not twice. Twice is just weather.  
_You shake it, once. One fruit drops: small, wrinkled, an odd yellow-brown, like an apple that has heard bad news._  
_It misses every open mouth in the row and lands in Fossil’s lap, as if it knew the way._  
**Dusty** _(happy)_: That’s the one. Hazel gave her one the day she hatched. She ate it in one go and was sick on my shoe.  
_Fossil takes the fruit in both hands but doesn’t eat it yet. She holds it, and smells it, and closes her eyes._  
**Dusty** _(soft)_: Hazel stood where you are and said, “Well, she’s yours now. Good luck to you both.” I think she meant me.  
_Fossil eats a third of it, very slowly. Then she hands the rest to Dusty, who eats it, though it is horrible._  
**Dusty** _(soft)_: Tastes like being eleven.  

### Step 3: Hold Fossil up in the shallows of the pond.

Hint: Bring Fossil to the pond’s edge and choose Swim. One of your swimmers can hold her, or Marina will. · check: `swim:hold:fossil:pond`

#### <a id="s5-water-ask"></a>Scene `s5-water-ask`

**Dusty** _(sad)_: Last one. The water. She can’t swim on her own any more.  
**Dusty** _(happy)_: Marina taught her, years back, with Hazel’s lot. Says she was the only one who swam sideways on purpose.  
_Marina is already at the pond, sleeves rolled up, as though somebody sent for her. Dusty is very bad at secrets._  
**Marina** _(soft)_: I was passing. With a towel. As you do.  

**Choices**

- **Let one of your swimmers hold her.** — sets `{"s5-holder":"yours"}`
    - Your swimmer wades out and waits, very serious, as if it has been given the most important job in the valley.
    - **Marina**: Paw under the chin. Like a cup. There you go.
    - **Dusty**: It’s holding her like she’s the last egg in the valley. …Don’t tell it I said that.
- **Ask Marina to hold her.** — sets `{"s5-holder":"marina"}`
    - Marina wades in to her waist and holds out her hands.
    - **Marina**: Come on then, old girl. Same as always.

#### <a id="s5-water"></a>Done `s5-water`

_You lower Fossil into the water, and it takes her weight. For a moment she is stiff and heavy, and then she is light._  
_Then, very slowly, she kicks._  
**Dusty** _(happy)_: Look at her. That’s the stroke. The little sideways one. Like she’s late for something.  
**Dusty**: To the reeds and back, Fos. Then you can be smug about it.  
_She swims to the reeds and back. It takes a long time, and nobody hurries her._  
_When you lift Fossil out she is shivering, and her eyes are bright. She isn’t looking at Dusty; she’s looking at the Grove._  
**Dusty** _(think)_: …That’s not the list. She’s got a list of her own.  

### Step 4: Sit with them until dark.

Hint: Stay still near Fossil and your youngest Nubbin until night falls. If you wander off, she’ll wait. · check: `wait:dusk:fossil-youngest`

#### <a id="s5-secret"></a>Scene `s5-secret`

_You set Fossil down on the bank. She walks across the Grove on her own, one slow step at a time._  
_Not to Dusty, and not to you. She goes to your youngest Nubbin, busy falling over a fruit, and sits down beside it._  
**Dusty** _(shock)_: …Oh.  
_Your youngest stops falling over and looks at Fossil, and Fossil looks back. Then they just sit._  
**Dusty** _(soft)_: She did this for me once. I was small and I’d had a bad day. She just sat. Didn’t fix anything. Didn’t try.  
**Dusty** _(soft)_: Nobody taught her that. She worked it out years before I did.  

#### <a id="s5-gift"></a>Done `s5-gift`

_The sun goes down behind the western hill. Fossil doesn’t move, nor does your youngest, and after a while nor do you._  
_Behind you, the rest of your Nubbins have given up waiting and fallen asleep in a heap. One of them snores in a very small voice._  
_Dusty looks at the heap, and then at Fossil, and doesn’t say anything. Dusty is smiling._  
_When it’s dark, Fossil gets up, touches her nose to your youngest Nubbin’s nose, and walks back to Dusty._  
_Your youngest sits up straighter than you’ve ever seen it sit. On its stats sheet, under Stamina: “A gift from Fossil.”_  

### Complete

#### <a id="s5-home"></a>Complete `s5-home`

_Dusty picks Fossil up to carry her home, bad back or not. The ball above her head is very pale now._  
_It doesn’t glow. It only gets paler, the way a sky does._  
**Dusty** _(soft)_: Thank you. I mean it. I don’t say that much. Ask anyone. They’ll tell you I never say it.  
**Dusty** _(soft)_: I think it thins slower with someone walking beside it. I’d like to think so.  
_Fossil reaches out to you. In her fist is a curled seed from the fern at the foot of the cliff._  
**Dusty** _(soft)_: She wants you to plant it. Fossil-fern. Grows out of rock, if you’re patient. Course it does.  
**Dusty** _(happy)_: And have this. Don’t argue. I bought it the week after I dug her out. Never needed it like I needed the spoon.  
_Dusty’s trowel. The handle is worn pale where a hand has held it for twenty years._  
_They go out through the gate together. Dusty lifts and pushes without thinking._  
_On the western hill, Moss watches the gate long after it has closed._  

**Rewards**

- Fossil’s seed (item): A curled seed from the fern at the foot of Fossil’s cliff. Planted in the Grove, it grows into a fossil-fern: slow, grey-green, and very hard to kill.
- Dusty’s trowel (item): Dusty’s old trowel, the handle worn pale. Digs up buried things a little faster, or hangs on the cottage wall.
- A gift from Fossil (feature): Your youngest Nubbin’s Stamina grade rises by one, for good. Its stats sheet says who gave it.
