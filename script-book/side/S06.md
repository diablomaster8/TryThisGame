# Side story S6: Ash’s True Name

## S6. Ash’s True Name

*Original (Hazel’s Grove story bible v2): a first sound kept in a ledger, and a name given back* · audience: all · where: Ember’s house; then the cottage bookshelf and the pond; ends in Ember’s kitchen · when: The morning after chapter 9 closes (c9-close), only if Ash left Corvin’s garden in chapter 9 (corvin is reconciled, bargained or forced). Never offered on the collector route.

**Cast:** Ash

### Step 1: Ember has asked you over. Go and see Ash.

Hint: Ember’s house is past the dojo on the Moon side. Knock; she’ll pretend she wasn’t waiting. · check: `talk:ember:s6-kitchen`

#### <a id="s6-kitchen"></a>Scene `s6-kitchen`

_A note under your door at dawn: COME OVER. Then PLEASE, crossed out, and written again._  
_Ember’s house is tidy the way a house gets when someone cleans because they’re angry. It is very tidy._  
_Ember opens the door before you knock. She says she happened to be passing it._  
**Ember**: Ash is out of Corvin’s garden. Don’t ask how. I don’t care how, and I’m never going to care how.  
_A grey Nubbin sits in the corner of the kitchen, facing the wall. Beside her is a full bowl._  
**Ember**: Ash.  
**Ember** _(soft)_: Ash, love. It’s me. Ash.  
_The grey Nubbin does not turn. A drip falls from the tap, and she does not turn for that either._  
**Ember**: Four hundred and twelve times since yesterday. I counted. Tell Byte I counted and I’ll deny it.  
**Ember** _(sad)_: She eats when I leave the room. She sleeps standing up. It isn’t her name any more. It’s just a noise.  

**Choices**

- **Sit on the floor near Ash, and wait.**
    - You sit, for a long time. Your Nubbin sits too, facing Ash’s wall, to see what’s so interesting about it.
    - Ash doesn’t look at you, but she stops pressing quite so hard into the wall.
    - **Ember**: …Hazel used to do that. Sit on the floor like it was a chair. It drove me mad.
- **Say “Ash” yourself, gently.**
    - Ash doesn’t turn. Ember lets out a breath that might be relief.
    - **Ember**: See? It’s not me. It isn’t just me.
- **Ask what Corvin called her.**
    - **Ember**: Nothing. He didn’t call them anything. He just stood where they could see him.
    - **Ember**: Years of nobody saying her name. You’d forget too.

#### <a id="s6-egg"></a>Done `s6-egg`

_Ember puts the kettle on and doesn’t make any tea._  
**Ember**: When she was tiny she answered to everything. Her name, my name, the kettle.  
**Ember** _(think)_: I named her Ash a week after I got her. Before that she was just the egg from Hazel’s.  
**Ember**: Go home. Thanks for coming. Don’t make it a thing.  

### Step 2: Look for anything Hazel wrote about the egg she gave Ember.

Hint: Inside the cottage, tap the bookshelf by the stove. Hazel kept her ledgers on the bottom shelf. · check: `find:item:hatch-register:cottage-bookshelf`

#### <a id="s6-fern"></a>Scene `s6-fern`

_Fern is leaning on the fence between your gardens, eating an apple she has clearly taken from your tree._  
**Fern** _(think)_: You’ve been at Ember’s. You’ve got the look. It’s the look of someone told not to make it a thing.  
**You**: Ash won’t answer to her name.  
**Fern** _(shock)_: Ember asked you over? Ember doesn’t like anyone. She doesn’t like me, and I’m delightful.  
**Fern** _(sad)_: …Poor Ash. Poor Ember. Don’t tell her I said “poor”.  
**You**: Ash came from one of Hazel’s eggs.  
**Fern** _(think)_: Then she’s in the book. Hazel wrote down every egg she ever gave away. And the first noise each one made.  
**Fern**: “For the record,” she said. Whose record, I said. “Theirs,” she said.  
**Fern**: Bottom shelf, behind the jam. Don’t eat the jam. Nobody knows what the jam was.  

#### <a id="s6-register"></a>Done `s6-register`

_Behind the jam: a fat ledger, soft at the corners. On the cover, in Hazel’s hand: “EGGS.”_  
_Underneath, smaller: “Do not lend this to Byte.”_  
_Halfway through, a section starts under a heading underlined twice: “Given away.”_  
_Forty years of eggs, in pencil, with the weather. You can’t help reading the others on the way._  
_You read it in her voice. It’s easy. She wrote exactly the way she talked._  
**Gran Hazel** _(happy)_: “Egg 7. Given to Sparks, aged six. First sound: a bang. Not from the egg. From Sparks.”  
**Gran Hazel** _(think)_: “Egg 23. Given to Byte, who asked for a receipt. Gave him one. First sound: a hiccup. He wrote that down too.”  
**Gran Hazel** _(soft)_: “Egg 41. Given to Ember, aged fifteen, very serious. Promised to feed it before herself. Meant it.”  
**Gran Hazel**: “First sound: three notes. Low, high, and a little one at the end, like a question.”  
_Hazel has drawn the notes as three dots: low, high, and a tiny one trailing upwards._  

### Step 3: Take the register to Ember, and practise the three notes by the pond.

Hint: Pick the pattern Hazel wrote down. There’s no timer, and a wrong try costs nothing. · check: `choose:s6-notes:low-high-question`

#### <a id="s6-practise"></a>Scene `s6-practise`

_Ember reads the page twice. Then she shuts the book on her own thumb, on purpose._  
**Ember** _(sad)_: And if I do it and she doesn’t turn round?  
**Ember**: Right now she’s lost. If I call her real name and she still doesn’t turn, she’s gone. I’d rather have lost.  
_She follows you to the pond anyway, with the register under her arm, scowling at the water._  
**Ember**: I can’t sing. Don’t ask me to sing. I can whistle.  
**Ember**: You first. I’m not doing it till I know it doesn’t sound stupid.  
_Your Nubbins gather on the bank to watch. It is the most interesting thing to happen all week._  
**Ember**: Low, high, little one. Like a question. If a duck answers, we’re stopping.  
_There are no ducks on the pond. Ember checks anyway._  

**Choices**

- **Whistle: high, low, a little one.**
    - One of your Nubbins sneezes. Ember laughs once, before she can stop it.
    - **Ember**: That was a duck. Again.
- **Whistle: low, a little one, high.**
    - The last note goes up and keeps going, like a kettle.
    - **Ember**: That’s not a question. That’s a complaint. Again.
- **Whistle: low, high, and a little one at the end.** — sets `{"s6-notes":true}`
    - Low, high, and a small one tipped up at the end, like a question.
    - Every Nubbin on the bank turns its head at once. Your first one trots over to see who called.
    - **Ember**: …Oh.
    - **Ember**: Again. Slower. I want to hear it.

#### <a id="s6-ember-tries"></a>Done `s6-ember-tries`

_Ember puts two fingers in her mouth and whistles the three notes, quietly, to nobody._  
_Your Nubbins don’t come to her; they don’t know her._  
_From the far side of the pond, a duck nobody has ever seen before answers, loudly._  
**Ember** _(angry)_: No.  
_Ember glares at the duck until it leaves. It takes a while. It is a very confident duck._  
_It doesn’t matter. She heard herself do it._  
**Ember**: Tonight. Before I can think about it. You’re coming, and you’re not saying anything.  

### Step 4: Be in Ember’s kitchen when she whistles to Ash.

Hint: Go to Ember’s house after dark. Stand by the door and let her do it. · check: `watch:ember:whistle:ash`

#### <a id="s6-whistle"></a>Scene `s6-whistle`

_Ember’s kitchen after dark, with one lamp lit. Ash is in the corner, facing the wall._  
_Ember kneels a little way off, not too close. She has thought about the distance._  
**Ember**: If this doesn’t work, you walk me to the pond and we don’t talk. Agreed?  
**You**: Agreed.  
_She puts two fingers to her mouth. For a moment nothing comes out at all._  
_Then three notes. Low, high, and a small one at the end, like a question._  
_Ash’s ear moves. One ear, a very little._  
_Ember whistles it again, slower. Her hands are shaking, so it wobbles, but it is still the right shape._  
_Ash turns round._  
_Then a sound, small and cracked: pip. wirr. a? The first sound in the register, half remembered._  
_She crosses the kitchen one careful step at a time and puts her grey head against Ember’s knee._  
**Ember** _(soft)_: Hello, you. It’s me. It was always me.  

**Choices**

- **Stay by the door, very quietly.** — sets `{"s6-stayed":true}`
    - You stay. Ember cries without making any noise at all, which you suspect she has practised.
    - Ash lets her.
    - **Ember**: You stayed. You didn’t make a sound. I’ll never forgive you for that, or forget it.
- **Let yourself out. This part is theirs.**
    - You lift the latch slowly so it doesn’t click.
    - **Ember**: You let yourself out without clicking the latch. Nobody manages that latch. I noticed.
    - Behind you Ember whistles the three notes again, and again, and every time, Ash answers.

### Complete

#### <a id="s6-complete"></a>Complete `s6-complete`

_Next morning Ember is at your gate. She lifts and pushes without thinking; she’s known the trick since she was fifteen._  
_Ash walks so close to Ember’s boots that Ember has to walk carefully. She is still grey._  
**Ember** _(think)_: Clover says the grey stays. Clover says you can only make it lighter.  
**Ember**: She ate breakfast with me in the room. That’s lighter.  
**Ember** _(soft)_: She answers to both now. The whistle’s hers. Ash is the one I gave her. She can keep both.  
**Ember**: This was her first ribbon. I kept it in a drawer for years like an idiot. Give it to one of yours.  
**Ember**: And this. Two fingers, like that. Now blow. No. Less spit.  
_It takes four tries. On the fifth your whistle carries across the Grove, and three of your Nubbins come running._  
**Ember** _(happy)_: Don’t tell anyone I like you. I’ve got a reputation.  
_On your gatepost sits a duck. It has the look of a duck that has heard there might be whistling._  
**Ember**: No. We’re done. You were wonderful. Go home.  
_From behind Ember’s boots, Ash glares at the duck. It is the first thing Ash has glared at in years._  

**Rewards**

- Ember’s Whistle (emote) (cosmetic): The two-fingered whistle Ember taught you. Use it from the emote wheel: nearby Nubbins look up and come to you.
- Ash’s Ribbon (cosmetic): Ash’s first ribbon, rust-red and frayed at one end, kept in Ember’s drawer for years. Any of your Nubbins can wear it.
- Ember’s Friendship (feature): +5 friendship with Ember on top of any from choices. She says hello first now, which for Ember is a great deal.
