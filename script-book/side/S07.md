# Side story S7: Unverified

## S7. Unverified

*Original (Hazel’s Grove story bible v2): a clever man reinvents the Stillness with a clock* · audience: all · where: The wall by the long grass; then Pixel’s workbench behind the lantern shed, and Byte’s house · when: Chapter 7, after Moss explains the Stillness (c7-close). Meant to finish before chapter 10 opens (c10-open).

**Cast:** Null

### Step 1: Find Byte after Moss’s talk and hear what he’s writing.

Hint: Byte stayed behind on the low wall by the long grass, with his notebook. · check: `talk:byte:s7-unverified`

#### <a id="s7-unverified"></a>Scene `s7-unverified`

_After Moss’s talk the Keepers drift home in twos and threes. Byte stays on the wall, writing very small and very fast._  
_Beside him sits a pale blue Nubbin with a flat, tidy ball above its head. It is watching the notebook._  
_At the far end of the wall sits Moss, eyes shut. He is not asleep. He is listening very hard._  
**Byte**: This is Null. Adult. Four years and some weeks. I know the weeks. Pixel says saying the weeks is unsettling.  
**Byte** _(think)_: Moss says a Nubbin loved enough becomes an Eternal. “Enough.” That isn’t a unit.  
_He shows you the page. Under everything Moss said tonight, in neat capitals: UNVERIFIED._  
**Byte** _(sad)_: Fossil thinned and everybody just sat there. Sitting isn’t a method.  
**Byte**: Enough is a quantity. Quantities can be scheduled. Pixel’s timer runs four lanterns to the second.  
**Byte**: Before you say it: no stone. I’m not Cinder. I’m using a clock. Completely different.  
**Moss**: Your shoelace is untied.  
_Byte looks down. It is; it always is._  
_He goes very quiet._  
**Moss** _(think)_: Things on a timer learn to wait for the bell, Byte. Safe is not the same as well.  
_Byte writes it down carefully. Then, next to it: UNVERIFIED._  

**Choices**

- **I’ll help. What do you need?**
    - **Byte**: Fruit, a bed and a witness. You can be the witness. Witnesses don’t touch anything.
- **Moss is usually right, Byte.**
    - **Byte**: “Usually” is a percentage. I’d like to know which percentage.
    - On the wall, Moss appears to be asleep. He is very bad at appearing to be asleep.
- **Pet Null.**
    - Null leans into your hand, surprised, and then leans harder.
    - **Byte**: Don’t, you’ll skew the… fine. Fine. Once.

### Step 2: See the timer Pixel has built for Null.

Hint: Pixel’s workbench is behind the lantern shed. Follow the ticking. · check: `inspect:device:s7-minder`

#### <a id="s7-minder"></a>Scene `s7-minder`

_Pixel’s workbench: a clock face, three little brass bells, a feeding chute, a very small bed and, on the left, a red lever._  
**Pixel** _(happy)_: Hi. So. It’s my lantern timer, basically. Four lanterns or one Nubbin, same maths, Byte says.  
**Pixel**: Bell one, fruit drops. Bell two, bed warms. Bell three, ninety seconds of petting. Byte does the petting.  
**Byte** _(think)_: Ninety-one seconds was too many. I tested it.  
**Pixel**: He wanted to call it the Accelerator. I wanted the Nap Box. We settled on the Minder, which neither of us likes.  
_The clock ticks. The chute fires a plum across the shed and hits Byte squarely in the clipboard._  
**Pixel** _(think)_: That was calibration.  
**Byte**: That was a plum.  
_Null climbs onto the clipboard and eats the plum. Byte writes down the time. Nobody rang a bell._  
**Pixel** _(happy)_: Twenty years married, and he still argues with my clocks.  
_On the next tick a piece of fruit drops into the bowl, exactly on the beat._  
**Pixel** _(sad)_: I built the clock. Clocks don’t care what they count down to. I’m trying to care on its behalf.  
**Byte**: Null eats on the bell, sleeps on the bell, is loved on the bell. If enough is a quantity, this is enough.  

**Choices**

- **What if Null’s hungry before the bell?**
    - **Byte**: Null won’t be. The bell’s calibrated.
    - **Pixel**: …I asked him that too. It sounded better when he said it to me.
- **Does it have an off switch?** — sets `{"s7-lever":true}`
    - **Pixel**: Lever. Left side. I always put a lever on.
    - **Pixel**: Nobody ever asks about the lever. Thank you for asking about the lever.

#### <a id="s7-first-bell"></a>Done `s7-first-bell`

_Byte sets Null in the little bed, and the first bell rings._  
_Null looks at Byte, then at the bell, then eats._  
**Byte**: Day one. Logging begins. Come back in a week.  
**Pixel** _(think)_: It’s going to be fine. It’s a clock. Clocks are fine.  

### Step 3: After a week of bells, go and see how Null is doing.

Hint: Visit Byte’s house once the journal says a week has passed. · check: `talk:byte:s7-week`

#### <a id="s7-week"></a>Scene `s7-week`

_A week later, Byte’s front room is all clipboards. Null sits in the little bed, perfectly upright, waiting._  
**Byte** _(happy)_: Best week of data I’ve ever had. Run, Swim, Power, all up. It’s never eaten so tidily.  
**Pixel** _(sad)_: Watch this.  
_Pixel rolls a ball across the floor, the way you would for any Nubbin. Null watches it go by and doesn’t move._  
_The bell rings, and Null eats. It doesn’t look at the fruit, only at the bell._  
_Its blue has gone thin at the edges, like a shirt washed too often. Not grey: paler._  
**Byte** _(shock)_: It used to chase that ball into the stove. Why doesn’t it play?  
**Pixel** _(sad)_: Byte. I think it’s waiting to be told.  
_Byte turns his notebook back a week. There is Moss’s line, in his own neat capitals, and beside it: UNVERIFIED._  
**Byte** _(sad)_: He told me. In plain words. And I wrote “unverified” next to it.  
_He crosses it out and writes VERIFIED. Then he crosses that out too._  
_It isn’t something he wanted to be right about._  

**Choices**

- **Pull the lever.** — sets `{"s7-stopped":true}`
    - Pixel pulls it before you’ve finished saying it. The ticking stops, and the quiet is enormous.
    - **Pixel**: I always put a lever on.
- **Sit down next to Null, Byte. And wait.** — sets `{"s7-stopped":true}`
    - **Byte**: For how long?
    - **You**: Until it isn’t waiting for the bell.
    - **Byte**: That isn’t a duration.
    - But he reaches over and pulls the lever himself.

#### <a id="s7-same-graph"></a>Done `s7-same-graph`

**Byte** _(sad)_: I wasn’t trying to own it. I was trying to keep it. Apparently those are the same graph.  
_Null sits in the bed and waits for a bell that isn’t coming. For the first time it looks a little lost._  
**Pixel** _(soft)_: Tomorrow evening. No clock. We’ll all just sit there. We’re very good at sitting there.  

### Step 4: Spend an evening with Byte and Null, timer off. Pet Null when it comes to you, and don’t count.

Hint: Sit near Null in Byte’s garden and wait. Only pet Null once it comes close on its own. · check: `pet:nubbin:null:timer-off`

#### <a id="s7-no-clock"></a>Scene `s7-no-clock`

_Evening in Byte’s garden, and no bells. The Minder sits under a cloth on the step with its lever down._  
_Byte has brought his stopwatch. He holds it the way some people hold a habit they’ve given up._  
**Byte** _(think)_: I’m not timing. I’m just holding it. In case.  
**You**: In case of what?  
**Byte**: Unverified.  
_For a long time, nothing. Null sits in the grass, looking at the place where the bell used to be._  
_Then a moth goes past, and Null’s head turns. It watches the moth all the way to the hedge._  
**Byte** _(shock)_: Did you see that? That isn’t on any schedule.  
_Null takes one step after the moth, then looks back at you, as if checking that’s allowed._  

**Choices**

- **Nod. “Go on.”**
    - Null goes. It doesn’t catch the moth, but it falls into the hedge and comes out with a leaf on its head.
    - Null, with no clock on it at all, says prrp, which Byte does not write down.
- **Put the stopwatch down, Byte.** — sets `{"s7-watch-down":true}`
    - Byte looks at it for a long moment. Then he lays it face down in the grass.
    - **Byte**: It’s still running. I just can’t see it. That’s a start.

#### <a id="s7-lap"></a>Done `s7-lap`

_Near dark, Null comes back, climbs into Byte’s lap without being asked, and falls asleep at once._  
_Byte doesn’t move for an hour. You know it’s an hour because he doesn’t check._  
**Byte** _(soft)_: Its edges are bluer. Don’t tell me if I’m imagining it.  

### Complete

#### <a id="s7-complete"></a>Complete `s7-complete`

_A few evenings later Pixel knocks on your door and says nothing, which is how you know._  
_Null has curled up under Byte’s hedge, and the air around it has begun to glow._  
**Byte** _(sad)_: End-of-life check. Four years, eleven weeks, two days. I said I wouldn’t say the days.  
_Byte sits by the cocoon with his hand on it, and doesn’t count. You can see how hard that is._  
_The cocoon doesn’t thin. It glows warm and gold, and opens on an egg: small, blue, humming._  
**Byte** _(soft)_: Not Eternal. Reborn. It gets to start again, with someone who remembers it.  
**Byte**: Here. My stopwatch. You keep it, please. If I keep it, I’ll only look at it.  
**Pixel** _(soft)_: And the timer, with the bells off. It only counts lanterns now.  
_On your way home you notice Byte’s shoelace is tied. Nobody had to tell him. It won’t last._
