# Side story S8: The Warren in the Hollow

## S8. The Warren in the Hollow

*Original (Hazel’s Grove story bible v2): the strays’ own home in the wild, and a Keeper who finds what she lost* · audience: all · where: The Hollow, past Hazel’s camp; Fern’s gate; Hazel’s bench by the pond · when: Chapter 8, after you find Hazel’s notebook (c8-close). Closes in chapter 11, after Hazel comes home.

**Cast:** Bramble

### Step 1: Follow Nova and the fireflies past Hazel’s camp.

Hint: Walk when the fireflies drift; stop when they settle. They lead on past the flat stone. · check: `follow:fireflies:hollow:ring-of-roots`

#### <a id="s8-further"></a>Scene `s8-further`

_You are still by the flat stone with the notebook when Nova steps out of the trees. She doesn’t say hello._  
**Nova** _(think)_: That’s the camp. It isn’t the whole of it.  
**Nova**: “Fed nineteen today.” Did you count her bowls? Twelve.  
**Nova**: Twelve bowls, nineteen mouths. She wasn’t feeding them here. She was carrying it further in.  
_A firefly lands on the notebook, sits a moment, and lifts off into the dark beyond the camp. Then another._  
**Nova**: They go the same way every night. I followed them once, years back. I look in whenever I pass.  
**Nova**: I’ve never brought anyone. Bring fruit. Walk slowly. Slower than that.  

**Choices**

- **Lead on.**
    - **Nova**: I don’t lead. I follow fireflies. You can follow me following them.
- **Should my Nubbin wait here?**
    - **Nova**: No. They need to see you came with one that trusts you. It’s the only introduction that works.

#### <a id="s8-ring"></a>Done `s8-ring`

_The fireflies stop above a ring of old roots, knotted out of the ground like a fence nobody built._  
_Inside sit dozens of grey Nubbins: not in rows, in a ring, facing in. Some of them are touching._  
_In the middle sits an old grey Nubbin whose left ear folds over at the tip. A scrap of faded ribbon is tied round it._  
**Nova** _(soft)_: They aren’t lost. They know exactly where they are. It’s everyone else they’ve lost track of.  
**Nova**: That one’s in charge. I call her the Old Girl. She’s never told me what she calls herself.  

### Step 2: Leave fruit at the edge of the ring, a little at a time, until the Old Girl lets you in.

Hint: Bring twelve pieces of fruit over three evenings. Set them down at the roots and step back. · check: `deliver:fruit:hollow:warren:12`

#### <a id="s8-edge"></a>Scene `s8-edge`

_You take one step towards the roots. Every grey head in the ring turns at once, and you stop._  
**Nova**: Nobody crosses. Not me. You don’t walk into someone’s house just because the door is made of roots.  
**You**: Then how did Hazel feed them?  
**Nova**: She left it at the edge and stepped back. Then she came again the next night. That’s the whole trick.  
_The Old Girl watches you. Her dark ball hangs very still above her head._  
_Close at her side sits a grey Nubbin thinner than all the rest. Its ear folds over at the tip, like hers._  
**Nova** _(soft)_: Her youngest, I think. Doesn’t eat much. The others leave it the soft fruit.  

**Choices**

- **Leave the softest fruit nearest the thin one.** — sets `{"s8-soft-fruit":true}`
    - **Nova**: …That’s what Hazel did. Exactly that.
- **Put the fruit down, and sit a little way off.**
    - You sit. After a long time a grey Nubbin creeps out, takes one piece, and carries it to the middle.
    - **Nova**: Good. Now do that for three nights.

#### <a id="s8-gap"></a>Done `s8-gap`

_On the third evening the Old Girl gets up, slowly, and steps aside. There is a gap in the ring one Keeper wide._  
**Nova** _(shock)_: Every time I’ve passed through, I’ve come and sat at these roots. She never did that for me.  
_Up close, her ribbon was brown once, with a leaf stitched on it. Five points, one curled stem._  
_You have seen that leaf before. It is on Fern’s gloves, Fern’s gate, and Fern’s tea cosy._  
**Nova** _(think)_: Hazel’s notebook. “The brown one let me touch her ear.” She isn’t brown. Her ribbon was.  

### Step 3: Bring Fern to the ring of roots in the Hollow.

Hint: Fern is at her gate next door. Walk her past the falls to the Hollow; she’ll keep up. · check: `escort:fern:hollow:ring-of-roots`

#### <a id="s8-fern-gate"></a>Scene `s8-fern-gate`

_Fern is leaning on her gate, stitching a leaf onto a glove that already has three._  
**Fern**: You’re staring at my glove. It’s a leaf. I put them on everything.  
**Fern** _(think)_: I put one on a ribbon once. Long time ago.  
_She pulls the thread tight, and keeps pulling after it’s tight._  
**Fern** _(sad)_: Six years. Midsummer race. She went over the rail on the cliff bend and I stood at the bottom till dark.  
**Fern**: Everyone said she couldn’t have lived. Everyone was very kind about it. I hated it.  

**Choices**

- **Fern. I think I’ve found her.** — sets `{"s8-told":true}`
    - **Fern**: Don’t.
    - **Fern**: Don’t say that unless…
    - **You**: Bring fruit. Soft fruit.
    - She is through her own gate before you’ve finished, still holding the needle.
- **Will you help me carry fruit to the Hollow tonight?** — sets `{"s8-told":false}`
    - **Fern**: The Hollow? At night? With you?
    - **Fern**: …Fine. Someone has to carry things badly.
    - She doesn’t ask why. You don’t tell her, not yet.

#### <a id="s8-arrive"></a>Done `s8-arrive`

_The fireflies go ahead of you both, low over the path, as if they know who you’ve brought._  
**Fern** _(think)_: Is it always this dark? How did Hazel do this every night? She couldn’t see her own feet.  
_Fern walks into a tree, apologises to it, and walks into the same tree again._  
**Fern** _(sad)_: I’m carrying the soft fruit. I insisted. I have sat on two of them.  
**Fern** _(think)_: They’re still soft. Softer, if anything. That has to count.  
_At the stream she takes her boots off, wades across, and puts them back on the wrong feet. She doesn’t notice for a mile._  
_Then the path dips, the fireflies settle low, and for the first time all night Fern stops talking._  

### Step 4: Sit at the edge of the ring with Fern, and let the Old Girl come to her.

Hint: Sit down by the roots with Fern and wait. Don’t reach in. · check: `wait:fern:bramble:ring-edge`

#### <a id="s8-reunion"></a>Scene `s8-reunion`

_Fern stops at the roots, and every grey head turns._  
_She doesn’t notice them. She is looking at the middle._  
_The Old Girl is already standing. Her folded ear is up, and the scrap of brown ribbon hangs from it, leaf and all._  
**Fern** _(shock)_: Oh.  
**Fern** _(soft)_: Bramble? …Oh, you’ve gone grey. That’s all right, love. So have I.  
_For a long moment nobody in the ring moves._  
_Then Bramble walks out through the gap, one careful step at a time, all the way to Fern’s boots._  
_Bramble says nothing. She puts her grey head against Fern’s shin and keeps it there._  
**Fern** _(sad)_: I stood at the bottom of that cliff till dark. I thought I’d left you there.  
**Fern** _(sad)_: I’m sorry I stopped looking. I’m so sorry I stopped looking.  
_Bramble sits down on Fern’s boots, both of them, and stays there._  
**Fern** _(happy)_: Right. Good. I’m going to cry now. Somebody hold my gloves.  

**Choices**

- **Hold her gloves.**
    - You hold them. Fern sits down in the mud with Bramble in her lap and cries for a long time.
    - Bramble lets her.
- **Hand Fern a ribbon from your pocket.** — sets `{"s8-new-ribbon":true}`
    - **Fern**: You carry ribbons? Course you do. You’re Hazel’s.
    - Fern stitches a leaf onto it there and then, with shaking hands, and ties it under the old one.
    - **Fern**: She keeps both. She always was greedy.

#### <a id="s8-one-short"></a>Done `s8-one-short`

_Later, Bramble walks back to the middle of the ring. She has a job, and she goes back to it._  
**Fern** _(soft)_: She always did like being in charge.  
_Bramble sits down and looks past you all, up the dark path, the way she looked the first night._  
**Nova** _(think)_: The thin one. Her youngest. It isn’t at the ring tonight.  
**Fern** _(soft)_: Then we leave the soft fruit at the edge till it is.  

### Complete

#### <a id="s8-complete"></a>Complete `s8-complete`

_Hazel sits on the bench by the pond, the thin grey Nubbin asleep in the crook of her arm._  
**Gran Hazel** _(think)_: This one? Found her at the edge of a ring of roots, too tired to stand. So I carried her.  
**Gran Hazel**: Her mother runs the place. Ear folds over at the tip. Opinions. I gather you’ve met.  
**You**: Fern found her. Her name’s Bramble.  
**Gran Hazel** _(soft)_: Did she. Oh, Fern. Did she.  
**Gran Hazel**: Take this one home, then, love. Nothing’s mine. I only carry things for a bit.  
_At the ring the thin one wakes in your arms, sees her mother, and goes stiff all over with joy._  
_Bramble washes her, ear to ear. Then she noses the old brown ribbon off her folded ear and drops it at your feet._  
_Behind her, three strays stand up and look at you, as if waiting to see where you go next._  
**Nova** _(soft)_: That’s you invited. Come whenever you like. Bring soft fruit.
