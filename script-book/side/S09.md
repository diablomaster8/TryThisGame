# Side story S9: The Gate That Sticks

## S9. The Gate That Sticks

*The Lord of the Rings: the fallen apprentice (Saruman) and the one who stole and must come home (Boromir)* · audience: all · where: Your gate, at dusk · when: Chapter 11, Hazel’s second evening home, after the stone has been unmade at the Long Night. Ends on chapter 12’s morning, before c12-open.

### Step 1: Take the old trowel to Hazel.

Hint: She’s on the cottage step. Give it to her from your bag. · check: `give:item:old-trowel:hazel`

#### <a id="s9-dusk"></a>Scene `s9-dusk`

_Dusk, on Hazel’s second evening home. The gate rattles._  
_Not the clean clack of someone who knows the trick. A long, uncertain rattle. Then another._  
**Gran Hazel** _(soft)_: That’ll be him.  
_Hazel doesn’t get up from the step. She doesn’t look round. Her eyes are shut._  
**Gran Hazel**: Go on. I jammed it so I’d hear him. I’ve heard him. That’s my part done.  
_Corvin Vale is at the gate, hatless, in a coat that has been slept in. No runners behind him. No rows._  
_His hands are on the top bar, red and raw, like hands brought in out of the snow._  
**Corvin Vale** _(sad)_: I can feel them. My hands. I’d forgotten it hurts, coming back. Nobody mentions that it hurts.  
_His hand goes to his breast pocket, finds it flat, and comes away. He does it twice while you watch._  
_He lifts the latch and pushes, as anyone would. The gate shifts in its frame, and sticks._  
**Corvin Vale** _(think)_: It used to open for me. I’d wait, and it would just swing. I thought that meant something.  
**Corvin Vale**: Wood shrinks in a frost. It was the cold in my pocket. I took it for welcome.  
_He takes something from inside his coat and passes it through the bars, handle first: a trowel, the blade worn thin._  
**Corvin Vale** _(soft)_: Hers. I dug with it, under the long grass, twenty-two years ago. I’ve kept it oiled. Don’t say it’s from me.  

**Choices**

- **Why now?**
    - **Corvin Vale**: Because I can feel my hands. I didn’t have a reason before. I had a stone.
    - He goes down the lane. He looks back once, at the cottage, where Hazel sits with her eyes shut.
- **Do you want to come in?**
    - **Corvin Vale**: Want. Yes.
    - **Corvin Vale**: Not tonight. I haven’t asked. If I ever come in, it’ll be because I asked.
    - He goes down the lane, and doesn’t look back. You think it costs him something not to.
- **Say nothing. Take the trowel.**
    - You take it. The handle is warm from his coat.
    - **Corvin Vale**: Thank you.
    - He goes down the lane. He looks back once, at the cottage, where Hazel sits with her eyes shut.

#### <a id="s9-trowel"></a>Done `s9-trowel`

_Hazel turns the trowel over in her hands, and runs her thumb along the worn edge._  
**Gran Hazel** _(think)_: I wondered where that went. I bought another. It was never as good.  
**Gran Hazel** _(happy)_: You’ll want the story. You’ve been very good about not asking. It’s been unbearable to watch.  
**Gran Hazel**: He came to me at twenty-two, with nothing but months. Clever. Quick. Hungry, in a way I took for keen.  
**Gran Hazel** _(think)_: He found the old clay tablets in this cottage. Older than me. Older than the wall. They told him where the fourth one lay.  
**Gran Hazel** _(sad)_: One winter he tried their method on mine. My Nubbins, in my Grove. Quiet as stones, by morning.  
**Gran Hazel**: I stopped him. I said things. He said things. I was right, which as you know is very tiring for everyone.  
**Gran Hazel** _(sad)_: That night he opened the gate, let the buyers in, and went with them. Nubbins, and the stone.  
**Gran Hazel**: In the morning I wedged the gate.  
**You**: So he couldn’t get back in.  
**Gran Hazel** _(soft)_: No, love. So I’d hear him if he ever came back.  

**Choices**

- **How can you not hate him?**
    - **Gran Hazel**: I did, for a good while. It’s heavy, hating someone. I had Nubbins to look for, so I put it down.
    - **Gran Hazel**: I didn’t forgive him. I just needed both hands.
- **Did you find them? The ones he took?**
    - **Gran Hazel**: Most. It’s in the register. The rest I stopped looking for, one winter. I’ve never forgiven myself for stopping.
- **Sit down next to her. Say nothing.**
    - You sit. She leans, just a little, so that your shoulders touch.
    - **Gran Hazel**: There. Better than anything you could have said. Don’t let it go to your head.

### Step 2: Go down to the gate at dusk tomorrow.

Hint: Corvin comes at sundown. Stand by the gate. You don’t need to open it. · check: `talk:corvin:gate:dusk-2`

#### <a id="s9-yours"></a>Scene `s9-yours`

_She sets the trowel on the step beside her, blade down, as if it might get away._  
**Gran Hazel**: He’ll be back tomorrow at dusk. Same time. He was always a creature of habit. That was half the trouble.  
**Gran Hazel** _(soft)_: I’m not going down there. I’ve done my part. I listened for twenty-two years.  
**Gran Hazel** _(happy)_: It’s your gate now. Don’t look at me like that. I only wedged it.  
**You**: What do I say to him?  
**Gran Hazel** _(think)_: Nothing, to start with. Rule three works on people too. It works on people worst of all.  

#### <a id="s9-ask"></a>Done `s9-ask`

_Sundown. He is there before you, as if he never left. Perhaps he didn’t._  
**Corvin Vale** _(think)_: I’ve been practising this. In the lane. Out loud. A sheep heard most of it.  
_He puts both hands on the top bar. He doesn’t try the latch._  
**Corvin Vale**: Twenty-two years ago I stood here and opened this gate for men with carts. I didn’t ask anyone.  
**Corvin Vale**: I’ve opened every gate since. Mine. The Circuit’s. Other people’s. I never once had to ask.  
**Corvin Vale**: So.  
_…_  
**Corvin Vale** _(soft)_: I would like to come in.  
_He says it to the gate, not to you. Then he looks up, and says it again, to you._  
**Corvin Vale** _(soft)_: I would like to come in. I don’t expect it. I’m only asking.  
**Corvin Vale**: I’ll stand here till first light. If it’s still shut then, I’ll understand. I’ll understand better than anyone.  
_He lets go of the bar and steps back into the lane, out of the lamplight. He doesn’t go far._  

### Step 3: Be at the gate at first light, before the others come.

Hint: Chapter 12 begins at dawn. Go to the gate before anything else. · check: `reach:gate:dawn:chapter-12`

#### <a id="s9-word"></a>Scene `s9-word`

_Word gets round, as it does in a valley this size. By supper, everyone knows Corvin Vale has asked to come in._  
**Ember** _(angry)_: He took Ash. I’m not telling you what to do. I’m telling you what he did. That’s different.  
**Ember** _(think)_: And some of what sleeps in your long grass came out of his rows. They live on your side of that gate too.  
**Luna** _(think)_: Sable came out of his garden. I keep thinking that. And then I keep thinking: she came out.  
**Fern** _(think)_: I’d let him in. Then I’d lie awake all night. Both of those are true, so I’m no use to you whatsoever.  
**Clover** _(soft)_: The grey stays, you know. On him too, I expect. You can only make it lighter.  
**Byte** _(think)_: For the record, I’ve modelled it. Redemption has a terrible base rate.  
**Byte**: I’d still open the gate. I don’t know why. Don’t put that in the record.  
_Hazel says nothing at all. On her way out to the pond, she leaves the cottage door on the latch, which she never does._  

#### <a id="s9-gate"></a>Done `s9-gate`

_First light. The pond is grey and still. Hazel sits beside it with a cup, her back to the gate._  
_Corvin is at the gate. He has been there all night._  
_There is frost on his shoulders, the ordinary kind that melts._  
**Corvin Vale** _(soft)_: Hazel’s grandchild.  
_His hands rest on the top bar. They aren’t blue any more._  
_They shake a little, the way warm hands do in the cold._  
_At the edge of the long grass a grey ear turns towards his voice, and goes flat._  
**Corvin Vale**: I know who’s sitting by that pond. I know what I took from her. I’m asking anyway.  
_Behind you, Hazel drinks her tea and does not turn round. She is listening to every word._  

**Choices**

- **Open it for him. Don’t show him how.** — sets `{"corvinGate":"guest"}`

    - **Corvin Vale**: Thank you.
    - **Corvin Vale**: I’ll have to knock, every time. That’s right. That’s more than right.
    - By the pond, Hazel lifts one hand a few inches and lets it fall. It might be a wave.
- **Leave it shut.** — sets `{"corvinGate":"shut"}`

    - **Corvin Vale**: Yes. All right. Yes.
    - **Corvin Vale**: It ought to cost something. I’d think less of the place if it didn’t.
    - **Corvin Vale**: Thank you for taking the trowel. I mean that.
    - He takes his hands off the gate one at a time. He goes down the lane, and is gone before Sparks arrives.
    - By the pond, Hazel finishes her tea. She sits a while longer than she needs to.

### Complete

#### <a id="s9-complete"></a>Complete `s9-complete`

_Sparks arrives first, at a run, as always. Then Fern, then everyone: a whole valley coming up the lane._  
_One by one they lift and push. The gate opens for each of them, and sticks again behind them._  
**Gran Hazel** _(soft)_: Good gate, that. I never did unwedge it.  
_She doesn’t say anything else about it, then or ever. You understand that this is how she says it’s finished._
