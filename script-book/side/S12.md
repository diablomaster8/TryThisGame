# Side story S12: The Hum

## S12. The Hum

*Inspired by StarCraft: a swarm that thinks as one, a shared mind, and the ones who cut themselves off to think alone (themes only)* · audience: all · where: Luna’s night garden, at dusk · when: Chapter 6, any evening after c6-open. Closes before chapter 10 begins.

**Cast:** The Thrums, Solo, The Queen-Mother

### Step 1: Carry Solo home to the Thrums in the old wall.

Hint: Hush’s old wall is the tumbled stone one on the north side of the Grove. Carry the teacup level; Solo gets seasick. · check: `carry:nubbin:s12-solo:old-wall`

#### <a id="s12-solo"></a>Scene `s12-solo`

_Dusk in Luna’s night garden. The moonflowers open one by one, with a sound like someone unwrapping a sweet._  
**Luna** _(think)_: Keeper. Over here. Quietly. I’ve got something in a teacup and I don’t know what to do with it.  
_In the teacup is a beetle the size of your thumb: round, bronze, fuzzed on the back like a peach._  
_It is, just about, a Nubbin. Its wings buzz whenever anyone speaks._  
**Luna**: It’s a Thrum. They live in Hush’s old wall, hundreds of them. They hum. They never, ever come out alone.  
_The beetle looks up at you. It clears its throat, which for a beetle is mostly wings._  
**Solo** _(shock)_: We are— no.  
**Solo** _(sad)_: I. I am. Good evening. I am sorry. I am not sure who is sorry. I think it is me.  
**Luna** _(soft)_: It’s been doing that for an hour. It finds it very upsetting.  
**Luna**: It needs a name, I think. Things that say “I” usually do.  

**Choices**

- **Call it Solo.** — sets `{"s12-name":"solo"}`
    - **Solo**: Solo. Solo is… one of something. Yes. That is what I am. Currently.
    - **Luna**: Currently. Good. Always leave yourself a way back.
- **Give it a name of your own.** — sets `{"s12-name":"own"}`
    - You say a name. The beetle repeats it three times under its breath, as if testing a step.
    - **Solo**: Nobody has ever said a thing just to me before. It is very loud.
- **Ask what it would like to be called.** — sets `{"s12-name":"asked"}`
    - **Solo**: Like? I have never liked anything on my own. We like things. Together. By agreement.
    - **Solo**: …I would like you to choose. Is that allowed? That is my first like.
    - You call it Solo. It seems to fit, the way a borrowed coat fits.

#### <a id="s12-wall"></a>Done `s12-wall`

_The old wall hums. You feel it before you hear it: lay a palm on the stone and it buzzes like a cat on a lap._  
_The mortar smells faintly of honey. Every crack has a tiny round door, and every door has a face in it._  
**The Thrums**: Good evening, Keeper.  
**The Thrums**: We would like to say: thank you for returning the one.  
**The Thrums** _(think)_: We would also like to say: the one is welcome. One moment. We are checking we all agree about “welcome”.  
_There is a long pause, full of buzzing._  
**The Thrums** _(happy)_: We do. Welcome. We apologise for the delay. We are very many.  
_Solo does not get out of the teacup. Solo goes up your sleeve instead, and stays there, humming very quietly, alone._  
**Solo** _(sad)_: In a minute. I will go in in a minute.  
_It does not go in._  

### Step 2: Put your ear to the old wall at dusk and listen for the Queen-Mother.

Hint: She lives at the heart of the wall, low down by Hush’s lantern post. Go when the hum is quietest, and keep still. · check: `listen:old-wall:s12-queen-mother`

#### <a id="s12-flat"></a>Scene `s12-flat`

_Next morning Pixel is at the old wall with a tuning fork, a notebook and an expression._  
**Pixel** _(think)_: Hi. Don’t move. Sorry. You can move. Just don’t buzz.  
**Pixel**: I tune my lantern timer to this wall. Have done for years. It hums a perfect B-flat. Ish. Always has.  
_She taps the fork on her knee and holds it to the stone. The fork sings. The wall sings back, a hair lower._  
**Pixel** _(shock)_: It’s gone flat. Walls don’t go flat. Walls don’t do anything. That’s their whole thing.  
**The Thrums** _(sad)_: Good morning. We are sorry about the flat.  
**The Thrums**: It is our Queen-Mother. She is the middle of the hum. She is getting quieter.  
**The Thrums**: When the middle goes quiet, the hum splits. Half of us leave with a new queen. Half of us stay.  
**The Thrums**: We have done it before. We do not remember it. The ones who remember always leave.  
**Pixel** _(sad)_: That is the saddest thing a wall has ever said to me.  
_In your sleeve, Solo has gone completely still._  

#### <a id="s12-queen"></a>Done `s12-queen`

_At dusk you lie in the grass with your ear to the bottom stone, where the moss is warmest._  
_Deep in the wall, under all the others, there is one slow note, like a big bee in a shut room._  
**The Queen-Mother** _(soft)_: Ah. The Keeper. And the one in your sleeve. Yes, I can hear it. I can hear all of them.  
**The Queen-Mother**: I am going to tell you something, and you are not to tell the we.  
**The Queen-Mother** _(think)_: I have said “I” for a very long time. Somebody has to, in the middle of a we. Somebody has to decide when.  
**The Queen-Mother** _(sad)_: It is quite lonely. Nobody tells you that about the middle.  
_Solo makes a small sound in your sleeve, like a question with no words in it._  
**The Queen-Mother** _(soft)_: On the first warm, still morning, half will go. The young one must choose. Go, stay, or something else.  
**The Queen-Mother** _(happy)_: Don’t choose for it. That would be the we all over again, only taller.  

### Step 3: Sit with Solo in Luna’s garden until the first warm, still morning.

Hint: Sit on the bench by the moonflowers after dark and don’t get up. Solo doesn’t need answers. It needs company. · check: `sit:s12-solo:luna-garden:dawn`

#### <a id="s12-dark"></a>Scene `s12-dark`

_Solo won’t go into the wall and won’t come out of your sleeve. So that evening you take it to Luna’s garden._  
_Luna has raked the gravel into slow spirals. In the dark they shine a little, like the inside of a shell._  
**Solo** _(soft)_: In the hum I am warm on every side. Nothing is my fault. Nothing is mine. It is very restful.  
**Solo** _(sad)_: Out here everything is mine. The dark is mine. The cold is mine. This leaf is mine, and I did not ask for it.  
**Solo**: If I go back, I stop. Not die. Stop being the one who is frightened. I would quite like to stop.  
**Solo** _(think)_: But I would also like to see what happens next. To me. Is that greedy?  
**Luna** _(think)_: Greedy is the slugs in my moonflowers. They eat a whole one, then sit there looking pleased.  
**Luna** _(soft)_: Wanting to see what happens next isn’t greedy. It’s just wanting.  
**Luna** _(soft)_: I garden at night because nobody else does. I liked being one on my own before I liked anything else.  
**Luna**: No. That’s not true. It took years. Some nights I still don’t like it. I come out anyway.  
**Luna** _(think)_: That isn’t an answer. I haven’t got one. I’ve just got a garden.  

#### <a id="s12-swarm-goes"></a>Done `s12-swarm-goes`

_You wake on the bench with dew in your hair. The air is warm and doesn’t move. It is the morning the Queen-Mother meant._  
_Across the Grove, the old wall roars._  
_Bronze pours out of every crack at once and turns in the air, glittering, like a handful of pennies thrown at the sun._  
**The Thrums** _(shock)_: Goodbye. Goodbye. We are leaving. Some of us are leaving. We are deciding which.  
**The Thrums**: It is going very slowly. We apologise. We are trying to be quick and also polite.  
**Solo** _(think)_: I want to see where they go. Not to go. To see. Is there a difference?  
**Pixel** _(happy)_: There’s a difference. I’ve got a tuning fork. Come on.  

### Step 4: Follow the swarm by sound with Pixel’s tuning fork.

Hint: Strike the fork and turn slowly: it buzzes hardest when you face the swarm. If you lose them, stand still and strike it again. · check: `minigame:tuning-fork:s12-swarm`

#### <a id="s12-follow"></a>Scene `s12-follow`

_Pixel presses the fork into your hand. It is warm from her pocket and has PROPERTY OF PIXEL scratched on the handle._  
**Pixel**: Strike it on your knee. Hold it up. Turn slowly. When the fork and the swarm match, it buzzes in your fingers.  
**Pixel**: That’s them. Walk that way. Strike it again when it goes quiet. Don’t walk into the stream. People do.  
**Pixel** _(think)_: Not me. Other people. Specific other people.  
**The Thrums** _(think)_: Left. No. Some of us say left. We are voting.  

#### <a id="s12-settled"></a>Done `s12-settled`

_The fork leads you over the stream to Hazel’s crooked tree. The swarm hangs from a branch in a great buzzing pear._  
_Pixel holds her fork to it, then towards the wall, then to it again. Then she sits down hard on the bank._  
**Pixel** _(shock)_: It was never one note.  
**Pixel**: It’s hundreds. All a hair apart. They lean towards each other till it sounds like one. That’s all a hum is.  
**Pixel** _(happy)_: I’ve been tuning a clock to a crowd. For years.  
**The Thrums** _(think)_: We are here. We are a new we. We are not yet sure what we are like.  
**The Thrums** _(soft)_: One. Solo. Will you come into a we? This one or the old one. Either is warm.  
**Solo** _(sad)_: Keeper. I don’t know. Sit with me. No. Tell me. No. Both. Is that allowed?  
_You don’t tell it. You sit down on the bank beside the teacup, and wait for it to choose._  

**Choices**

- **Solo goes home to the hum. You nod.** — sets `{"s12-solo":"hum"}`
    - You carry the teacup back to the old wall. Solo looks at you for a long moment, then climbs out.
    - **Solo**: I will forget being frightened. I think I will forget this, too. Will you remember it for me?
    - It walks in through a round door in the stone. The hum goes on, a hair fuller than before.
    - **Pixel**: Listen to that. B-flat. Ish. It’s back in tune.
- **Solo stays out on its own. You nod.** — sets `{"s12-solo":"alone"}`
    - You find it a knot-hole in the gatepost, out of the wind, with a view of the pond. It inspects it for a long time.
    - **Solo**: Mine. The knot-hole is mine. I chose it. Nobody agreed.
    - **Solo**: It is very quiet in here. I will get used to it. Or I will get used to not being used to it.
    - Every dusk after that, a tiny hum comes from the gatepost. One note. Slightly flat. Entirely its own.
- **Solo asks the hum to leave a little room. You nod.** — sets `{"s12-solo":"gaps"}`
    - **Solo**: Lean. But leave a gap. For one. Is that allowed?
    - **The Thrums**: We will discuss it.
    - They discuss it. It takes until lunch. Pixel eats a sandwich. Then a second sandwich. Then yours.
    - **The Thrums**: We have agreed. We will be a hum with gaps in it. Ones may go out, and come in, and go out.
    - **The Thrums**: We have also agreed this was the longest meeting in the history of the wall. We are very proud.
    - **Solo**: In for supper. Out for the stars. I can do that. I can be both, slowly.

### Complete

#### <a id="s12-complete"></a>Complete `s12-complete`

_A week on, the Queen-Mother’s note goes quiet for good, in the night, with the whole wall humming round her._  
_In the morning there is a gift on your doorstep: a little hive of clay and wax, no bigger than a teapot._  
**The Thrums** _(soft)_: For the Keeper. It hums. We taught it. We hope that is not rude.  
_At dusk it hums a tune you know: the bit of the Lantern Song that comes round twice._  
_Beside it stands a thimble of pale gold nectar that smells of the wall in summer._  
**The Thrums**: Hum-nectar. A Nubbin who sips it is calm for a day. Only a day. We did not want to overdo it.  
_You find you can now say things in chorus with whoever is standing nearby. It is a skill you didn’t know you lacked._  
**Pixel** _(happy)_: I’ve retuned the lantern timer. Not to B-flat. To the whole crowd. It runs a bit early now. I like it.
