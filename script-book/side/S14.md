# Side story S14: Tilly’s Tabletop

## S14. Tilly’s Tabletop

*Borderlands 2: Tiny Tina’s Assault on Dragon Keep (a child running a chaotic tabletop game to work through grief); theme only, all words original* · audience: all · where: Hazel’s cottage, the kitchen table · when: Chapter 8, after c8-close: the first wet afternoon of autumn half-term.

**Cast:** Tilly (Tiny Tina as Bunker Master (Borderlands 2: Tiny Tina’s Assault on Dragon Keep), reference only)

### Step 1: Play the first session of Keeps & Critters: the Bog of Mild Peril.

Hint: Roll the cardboard dice when Tilly says. Arguing with her rulings makes them worse, never better. · check: `minigame:clear:s14-bog-of-mild-peril`

#### <a id="s14-table"></a>Scene `s14-table`

_Rain on the kitchen window. Sparks comes in without knocking, which is how Sparks comes in, towing a small girl in big wellies._  
**Sparks** _(happy)_: This is Tilly. My cousin. Half-term. She’s nine. She’s in charge now. Sorry.  
**Tilly**: I’m not in charge. I’m the Game Mistress. It’s different. It’s worse.  
_She unrolls a map across Hazel’s table, over forty years of teapot rings. It is drawn in felt tip and smells strongly of it._  
_Then the dice: cardboard, hand-cut, sellotaped at the corners. On every die, the six side just says “LOTS”._  
**Tilly**: Welcome to Keeps & Critters. Rule one: the Game Mistress is always right. Rule two: see rule one.  
**Tilly**: Rule three: there are snacks, but only at the end. If you ask about the snacks, the end gets further away.  
**Byte** _(think)_: Is there a rulebook? I’d like to read the rulebook first.  
**Tilly**: I’m the rulebook.  
_Pixel is already building a dice tower out of a cereal box. Tilly watches it the way a hawk watches a vole._  
**Tilly**: Pick your hero. Ten seconds. Nine. Four.  

**Choices**

- **A knight with a slightly bent sword.** — sets `{"s14-hero":"knight"}`
    - **Tilly**: Sir You of the Bent Sword. Good. Bent swords can hit round corners.
- **A wizard who mostly does soup.** — sets `{"s14-hero":"wizard"}`
    - **Tilly**: Soup Wizard. Your spells are hot but slow, and you have to blow on them. Don’t argue. It’s canon.
- **A rogue disguised as a hat stand.** — sets `{"s14-hero":"rogue"}`
    - **Tilly**: Excellent. Nobody suspects a hat stand. Not even other hat stands.

#### <a id="s14-bog"></a>Done `s14-bog`

_The Bog of Mild Peril is the pond, coloured green and labelled “BOG (MILD)”. A felt-tip frog sits in it, looking unimpressed._  
**Sparks** _(happy)_: I ATTACK THE BOG.  
**Tilly**: You can’t attack a bog. Roll anyway.  
**Tilly**: …Natural one. Your sword is now a sandwich.  
**Sparks** _(shock)_: What KIND of sandwich?  
**Tilly**: Cheese. A sad one. You ate the good half on the way here.  
**Byte**: Actually, a one on a six-sided die isn’t technically a natural—  
**Tilly**: Rule four. Anybody who says “actually” loses a hit point.  
**Byte** _(shock)_: That isn’t—  
_Byte writes “HP: 8” on his character sheet, crosses it out, and writes “HP: 7 (DISPUTED)”._  
_You roll a LOTS. Tilly decides the bog likes you and lets the whole party across._  
_Nobody is sure why, including her._  
**Tilly** _(happy)_: Session over. Nobody died. Somebody’s a sandwich. Same time tomorrow.  

### Step 2: Get the party up the Tower of the Tax Toad without going broke.

Hint: Every Shell you pay is taxed again. Pay in things the Toad hasn’t thought to tax yet: Tilly makes it up as she goes, so watch her face. · check: `minigame:clear:s14-tower-of-the-tax-toad`

#### <a id="s14-tower"></a>Scene `s14-tower`

_Next afternoon there is a new tower on the map, drawn exactly where Sparks’s workshop chimney stands._  
**Tilly**: The Tower of the Tax Toad. He taxes everything. He’s very rich and very damp.  
**Pixel** _(happy)_: Can I build a siege engine?  
**Tilly**: You can build a siege engine. Roll. …Three. Your siege engine is a toad. It works for him now.  
_Pixel looks at the cereal-box dice tower, then at Tilly, and very slowly moves it out of her reach._  
**Tilly** _(angry)_: RIBBIT. That’ll be two Shells for the ribbit.  
**Tilly**: There’s a tax on hats. A tax on stairs. A tax on being taller than a toad. That’s all of you. Sorry.  
**Byte** _(think)_: And is there a tax on paying the tax?  
_Tilly’s eyes go very wide and very bright._  
**Tilly** _(happy)_: …Yes. Thanks, Byte. Thanks SO much.  

#### <a id="s14-toad"></a>Done `s14-toad`

**Byte**: It’s a series. Each tax is a tenth of the one before. It converges. We owe him two Shells, two, two, two… forever.  
**Tilly**: The Toad doesn’t do maths. The Toad does vibes.  
**Byte**: Actually—  
**Tilly**: Six.  
_Then Pixel’s Nubbin, asleep on the bread bin all afternoon, wakes, climbs down onto the map, and eats the Tax Toad._  
_The Toad is cardboard. The Nubbin doesn’t seem to mind._  
_The Toad may have opinions, but nobody will hear them now._  
**Tilly** _(shock)_: …  
**Tilly** _(happy)_: The Tax Toad has been eaten by a god. That’s canon now. Everyone’s taxes are cancelled.  
**Sparks** _(happy)_: BEST GAME. BEST GAME EVER MADE.  
_Tilly laughs so hard she has to lie on the floor. Then she stops, quite suddenly, and looks at the top corner of the map._  
_That corner is coloured grey. The felt tip was pressed so hard it went through the paper, into Hazel’s table._  

### Step 3: Find Tilly before the last session.

Hint: Listen. When she’s on her own she whistles the two-finger supper call Hazel taught her. · check: `find:tilly:s14-whistle`

#### <a id="s14-sparks"></a>Scene `s14-sparks`

_The morning of the last session, Sparks is waiting at your gate, quietly. You didn’t know Sparks had a quiet setting._  
**Sparks** _(sad)_: The dragon in the corner’s got bigger. She redraws it every night. Last night it got a cardigan.  
**Sparks**: It’s Hazel. Obviously it’s Hazel. Hazel taught her to whistle. Two fingers. The supper call.  
**Sparks**: Hazel went off at the end of the winter. She left Tilly a note. Just Tilly. She won’t open it. It’s in her welly.  
**Sparks**: She won’t say the name. Just “the dragon”.  
**Sparks** _(soft)_: She’s gone off somewhere. She does that. You go. She likes you. You’re not a sandwich.  

#### <a id="s14-gate"></a>Done `s14-gate`

_You hear her before you see her: a whistle through two fingers, rising at the end. Every Nubbin in the Grove looks up._  
_Tilly is sitting on the gate that sticks, kicking it. It doesn’t swing. It hardly ever does._  
**Tilly**: They think it’s supper. It isn’t supper. I just do it.  
**Tilly**: She said if you whistle it properly, anyone who loves you comes running. She said that.  
_The Nubbins arrive, hopeful, and sit in a row in front of her wellies._  
**Tilly** _(sad)_: See. It works on them.  

**Choices**

- **Ask her about Hazel.** — sets `{"s14-approach":"asked"}`
    - **Tilly**: I’m not talking about her.
    - A long pause. A Nubbin licks her welly.
    - **Tilly**: She went without saying goodbye. You’re supposed to say goodbye. That’s the RULE.
    - She takes the note out of her welly, still folded, damp at one corner. She looks at it. She puts it back.
    - **Tilly**: Come and play. I’ve got a dragon to do.
- **Ask her about the dragon instead.** — sets `{"s14-approach":"played"}`
    - **Tilly**: She’s the Grey Grandmother. She’s very old and very rude.
    - **Tilly**: She flew away without saying goodbye. Her hoard is mostly biscuit tins.
    - **Tilly**: She’s the final boss. Final means you can’t have another one after. Come on.

### Step 4: Play the last session: get past the Grey Grandmother’s geese and into the Lair.

Hint: Three rounds of dice. You can’t lose to the geese. Worst case, you get a goose. · check: `minigame:clear:s14-lair-geese`

#### <a id="s14-lair"></a>Scene `s14-lair`

_The last session. Tilly has drawn the curtains and put a torch under a mixing bowl for “atmosphere”._  
_It mostly lights the bowl._  
**Tilly**: The Lair. Beyond this door is the Grey Grandmother. First you have to get past her geese.  
**Tilly**: Her geese are very rude. Rude geese, rude dragon. It runs in the family.  
**Sparks** _(angry)_: I HIT THE GOOSE WITH MY SANDWICH.  
**Tilly**: Roll. …LOTS. The goose eats the sandwich and is now your friend. You have a goose.  
**Pixel**: My toad and I would like to help.  
**Tilly**: Your toad worked for the Tax Toad. The Tax Toad got eaten. Your toad is having a hard time. Roll anyway.  
**Byte**: Do I still have seven hit—  
**Tilly**: Four. You said “do” in an “actually” voice.  

#### <a id="s14-dragon"></a>Done `s14-dragon`

_Tilly lifts an upside-down cereal box off the top corner of the map. Under it is the dragon._  
_She is cut from a biscuit-tin lid and coloured grey, with a cardigan in felt tip and a tiny whistle on a thread._  
**Tilly**: The Grey Grandmother. She flew away without saying goodbye. She’s the boss.  
**Sparks**: Right. I roll to hit the—  
_Tilly snatches the dice off the table so fast that one of them tears._  
**Tilly** _(angry)_: No.  
**Tilly**: Nobody hits her. She’s immune. To swords and sandwiches and toads and geese. To everything.  
_It is suddenly very quiet in the kitchen. The rain has stopped. Nobody noticed when._  
**Tilly** _(sad)_: I don’t know how it ends. I don’t know how you end it if nobody’s allowed to hit her.  

**Choices**

- **“My hero puts the sword down and sits next to the dragon.”** — sets `{"s14-lair":"sat"}`
    - **Tilly**: That’s not a move.
    - **Tilly**: …What for?
    - **You**: Hazel’s third rule. When you don’t know what to do, sit down next to them and wait.
- **Roll to see if your hero is allowed to sit down.** — sets `{"s14-lair":"rolled"}`
    - You roll the torn die. It lands on the torn side, which has no number on it at all.
    - **Tilly**: That’s… not a number.
    - **You**: Then the Game Mistress decides. My hero would like to sit down next to the dragon.
    - Tilly looks at the torn die for a long time.
    - **Tilly**: The dice says… the dragon allows it.
- **Ask the Game Mistress what the dragon wants.** — sets `{"s14-lair":"asked"}`
    - **Tilly**: She doesn’t WANT anything. She just WENT.
    - You don’t say anything. Tilly glares at the dragon. The dragon, being cardboard, glares back.
    - **Tilly**: …She wants someone to sit with her. Fine. FINE.

### Step 5: Sit next to the dragon. Wait.

Hint: Don’t roll. Don’t move. Put the dice down. It takes as long as it takes. · check: `wait:s14-table:dragon`

#### <a id="s14-wait"></a>Scene `s14-wait`

_You move your counter to the dragon’s square, and leave it there. Your hero is a button._  
**Tilly**: She doesn’t do anything. She just sits there being a dragon.  
**You**: That’s all right. We’re waiting.  
_Sparks moves a button next to yours. Then Pixel. Then Byte, after looking round for a rule against it._  
**Byte**: Is there a rule against it?  
**Tilly** _(soft)_: …No.  
_Four buttons round a biscuit-tin dragon. At the window, a Nubbin taps on the glass, hoping it might be supper._  

#### <a id="s14-tears"></a>Done `s14-tears`

**Tilly**: The dragon says—  
_She stops. She picks up the dragon and holds it in both hands._  
**Tilly**: The dragon says she had something important to do. Grown-ups always say that.  
**Tilly**: She says she didn’t say goodbye because she’s rubbish at it. She’d have cried and made it everyone’s problem.  
**Tilly** _(sad)_: She says—  
**Tilly** _(sad)_: I don’t know what she says. I don’t KNOW. She didn’t SAY.  
_Tilly cries the way nine-year-olds cry: all at once, with her whole face._  
_Sparks is round the table before anyone else can move, and says nothing at all. It is the loudest thing Sparks has ever done._  
_Pixel quietly passes a tea towel. Byte quietly passes a better tea towel._  
_You keep your button where it is._  

### Complete

#### <a id="s14-complete"></a>Complete `s14-complete`

**Tilly** _(soft)_: Session over. Everyone gets snacks. That’s a rule. It was always a rule.  
_The snacks are crisps, Fern’s slightly burnt biscuits, and a bowl of grapes the Nubbins have already found._  
_Tilly eats with the dragon in her lap. She doesn’t let go of it once._  
**Tilly**: The Grey Grandmother isn’t a boss any more. She’s a character. She lives in the game and you can visit her.  
**Tilly**: And Byte gets all his hit points back. For the tea towel.  
**Byte** _(happy)_: Thank you. I’d like that in writing. Actually—  
**Tilly**: Don’t push it.  
_She puts her cardboard crown on your head._  
_The front says GAME MISTRESS. The back, smaller, says “(OR KEEPER)”._  
**Tilly**: You keep the map. And the dice. Not the torn one. I’m keeping the torn one.  
_When they’ve gone, you find the map spread on Hazel’s table. Someone has coloured the grey corner yellow._  
_“HERE BE HAZEL. SHE IS ALOWED.”_  
_Out by the gate, faintly, someone is practising the supper call. Every Nubbin in the Grove goes to look._
