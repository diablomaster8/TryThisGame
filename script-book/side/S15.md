# Side story S15: Lucky Eggs

## S15. Lucky Eggs

*Borderlands: the grinning villain who is sure he’s the hero, and the love of loot; Diablo: the gambling vendor (themes only)* · audience: all · where: The lane below the Grove gate, by the stream bridge · when: Chapter 5, a market morning after c5-open, but not the morning S5 or S11 opens. Closes before chapter 8 begins.

**Cast:** Dashwood Prance (Borderlands’ grinning villain who is sure he’s the hero; Diablo’s gambling vendor (themes only)), Jackpot, Pip, The children

### Step 1: Help Byte keep a tally of what comes out of thirty Mystery Eggs.

Hint: Stand by the wagon. Each time a child opens an egg, mark it plain or sparkly on Byte’s card. · check: `tally:eggs:s15-wagon:30`

#### <a id="s15-wagon"></a>Scene `s15-wagon`

_A wagon comes down the lane, striped red and cream, with a hundred little bells on the roof that ring at every pothole._  
_On the box sits a man in a mustard waistcoat. On his shoulder sits a brass parrot that ticks._  
**Dashwood Prance** _(happy)_: Children of the valley! Gather! Dashwood Prance has come, and Dashwood Prance has brought you luck!  
**Dashwood Prance** _(happy)_: Mystery Eggs! Ten Shells each! Every egg a winner! Some eggs more winner than others!  
**Jackpot**: MOST EGGS ARE PLAIN.  
**Dashwood Prance** _(happy)_: Thank you, Jackpot.  
_A poster unrolls down the side of the wagon in gold letters: 1 IN 3 SPARKLY! Underneath, much smaller: (approx.)_  
_In the window, on a velvet cushion, an egg glitters like frost on a web._  
_Its card says LEGENDARY. NOT FOR SALE (YET)._  
**The children** _(happy)_: Sparkly! Sparkly! Sparkly!  
**Pip** _(happy)_: I’ve got forty Shells. I’ve saved since my birthday. That’s four eggs. That’s one and a third sparkly!  
_Byte appears at your elbow with a clipboard and the face of someone who has just heard maths done wrong._  
**Byte** _(think)_: One in three. Says who? I want to see it happen. Thirty eggs. We count every single one.  

**Choices**

- **Buy an egg yourself. For science.** — sets `{"s15-bought":true}`
    - **Dashwood Prance**: A Keeper! A person of taste! Choose with your heart!
    - You choose with your heart. It’s plain. Jackpot looks at it and says nothing, very loudly.
    - **Byte**: That’s one. Twenty-nine to go. Don’t look like that. It’s data.
- **Ask Pip to wait before she spends it all.** — sets `{"s15-pip":"asked"}`
    - **Pip**: Wait? The sparkly ones might run out!
    - **Dashwood Prance**: They might! They very well might! That’s the magic of a limited time!
    - **Jackpot**: THEY WILL NOT RUN OUT.
- **Ask Dashwood where his odds come from.** — sets `{"s15-asked":true}`
    - **Dashwood Prance**: From the heart, friend! From the very bottom of the heart, where the best numbers live!
    - **Byte**: That is not where numbers live.

#### <a id="s15-tally"></a>Done `s15-tally`

_By noon Byte’s card is full. Thirty eggs. Thirty faces, mostly falling._  
**Byte** _(think)_: Twenty-seven plain. Three sparkly. That isn’t one in three. That’s one in ten.  
**Byte** _(angry)_: The poster is out by a factor of three point three recurring. I’ve underlined “recurring”. Twice.  
_Pip sits on the bridge with four plain eggs in her lap._  
_She isn’t crying. She is being extremely brave at the water._  
**Pip** _(sad)_: The plain ones are nice too. They’re warm.  

### Step 2: Get Jackpot on his own and ask him some questions.

Hint: Dashwood naps after lunch. Creep along the stream side of the wagon, where a bell has fallen off, and don’t touch the wheels. · check: `sneak:wagon:s15-jackpot`

#### <a id="s15-sneak"></a>Scene `s15-sneak`

**Byte** _(think)_: We need a witness. Someone who’s seen the books.  
**Byte**: That parrot. Every time Dashwood says something, it corrects him. I don’t think it can help it.  
_After lunch the wagon goes quiet. The mustard waistcoat hangs over the step. Inside, a snore like a slow saw._  
_Jackpot sits on a perch at the open window, ticking. His eyes are brass buttons; one is sewn back on with red thread._  
**Byte** _(think)_: The bells. Touch the wagon and they all go. Come round the stream side. There’s a gap where one fell off.  
_Stepping stones lead along the stream. One wobbles. One is a frog._  

#### <a id="s15-jackpot"></a>Done `s15-jackpot`

**Jackpot**: GOOD AFTERNOON. I CANNOT LIE. IT IS A FAULT IN MY SPRINGS.  
**You**: How many eggs are sparkly, Jackpot?  
**Jackpot**: ONE IN TEN. HE KNOWS. HE WROTE IT IN THE LEDGER IN VERY SMALL WRITING.  
**Byte** _(think)_: And the poster?  
**Jackpot**: HE SAYS THE POSTER IS A POEM. I CANNOT LIE. A POEM IS NOT A NUMBER.  
**You**: What happens to the eggs nobody buys?  
**Jackpot**: THEY GO UP THE VALLEY. TO A MAN WHO BUYS IN BULK. HE NEVER HAGGLES. HE NEVER SMILES.  
_Among the stickers on the tailboard you notice one small, neat silver square: the Circuit’s mark._  
**Jackpot**: ALSO THE LEGENDARY EGG IS NOT LEGENDARY. I HAVE TRIED NOT TO SAY THAT. IT IS VERY UNCOMFORTABLE.  
_The snoring stops. You both freeze. It starts again._  

### Step 3: Hold the Legendary egg up to a lantern and see what’s really inside.

Hint: Candling: in a dim place, a light behind an egg shows what’s growing. Try under the bridge. · check: `candle:egg:s15-legendary`

#### <a id="s15-legendary"></a>Scene `s15-legendary`

_Dashwood wakes up exactly as charming as he went to sleep. He polishes the Legendary egg on his sleeve._  
**Dashwood Prance** _(happy)_: Admiring her? Course you are. Hold her if you like. One Shell a minute. Feel the luck soak in.  
**Byte** _(think)_: Here’s three Shells. We’ll need exactly three minutes.  
_Under the bridge it is dim and green and smells of watercress. Byte lights the stub of a lantern._  
**Byte** _(think)_: Candling. Hazel showed everybody. Light behind the egg, and you can see what’s growing.  
**Byte**: A sparkly egg glows all through, like a pearl. A plain one shows a little curled shadow. This one… we’ll see.  

#### <a id="s15-painted"></a>Done `s15-painted`

_The light comes through the shell, and the glitter on it throws stars all over the underside of the bridge._  
_Under the glitter, the egg glows plain pink. Inside is a small curled shadow, and it’s moving._  
**Byte** _(shock)_: Plain. It’s a plain egg, painted. There’s a thumbprint in the varnish. A big one.  
**Byte** _(soft)_: A healthy plain egg, mind. It’ll hatch in about a week. It just won’t be legendary. It’ll be a Nubbin.  
_Glitter comes off on your fingers. It doesn’t come off for days._  

### Step 4: Set up an honest egg stall with Byte, and chalk up the real odds.

Hint: Byte has a slate and chalk. Put the stall by the bridge (or at your gate) and write the true numbers on the board. · check: `build:decor:s15-odds-board`

#### <a id="s15-verdict"></a>Scene `s15-verdict`

_You come back up the bank with glitter on your hands. Dashwood sees it, and his smile stays exactly where it is._  
_He steers you round the back of the wagon, where the children can’t see, and sits down on the step._  
**Dashwood Prance** _(soft)_: When I was eight, a man came through my village with a wagon. Not as nice as mine.  
**Dashwood Prance** _(soft)_: He sold me a golden egg for every Shell I had. I carried it home in my hat. I slept curled round it like a cat.  
**Dashwood Prance** _(think)_: It hatched brown. Brown as a boot. It had been painted.  
**Dashwood Prance** _(happy)_: And I loved that brown thing more than anything since. Best week of my life was the week before it hatched.  
**Dashwood Prance** _(soft)_: That’s what I sell, friend. Not eggs. The week before.  
**Jackpot**: HE BELIEVES THIS. I CANNOT LIE. HE DOES.  
_Round the front of the wagon, the children are waiting. Pip holds her four plain eggs. Byte holds the tally._  

**Choices**

- **Show everyone the tally and the painted egg.** — sets `{"s15-verdict":"exposed"}`
    - You climb on the wagon step and hold up the painted egg. Byte reads the tally aloud, slowly, including the recurring.
    - **The children**: ONE IN TEN?
    - The roof bells ring as children kick the wheels. Dashwood’s smile finally goes, and something small looks out from behind it.
    - **Dashwood Prance**: It was a poem. It was only ever a poem.
    - He drives off before dark. He leaves his spare trestle, a crate of eggs and the parrot, who refuses to get on.
    - **Jackpot**: I CANNOT LIE. I DID NOT WANT TO GO.
- **Make him post the true odds and give the children their Shells back.** — sets `{"s15-verdict":"refunded"}`
    - **Dashwood Prance**: Refunds? Refunds! Do you know what that does to a man’s margins?
    - **Jackpot**: HIS MARGINS ARE ENORMOUS.
    - Dashwood counts Shells back into small hands, one by one. He does it with a flourish, because he can’t help that either.
    - He paints out the 3 on his poster and paints in a 10. Then, after a long look, he adds: STILL WORTH IT.
    - **Pip**: I’m keeping one egg. I want to see what comes out. I don’t mind if it’s plain.
    - He leaves the valley the next morning with a lighter crate. The parrot stays, by mutual and very honest agreement.
- **Buy his wagon, and run an honest stall yourself.** — sets `{"s15-verdict":"bought"}`
    - **Dashwood Prance**: Buy her? Buy Marigold? You don’t buy a wagon, friend. You marry it.
    - **Dashwood Prance**: …How much were you thinking?
    - It takes most of an afternoon and most of your Shells. Byte negotiates. Byte enjoys it far too much.
    - **Dashwood Prance**: Paint the stripes once a year. She likes a polish. And never, ever let her near a goat.
    - He walks off down the lane with his hands in his pockets, whistling, lighter than he came.

#### <a id="s15-odds-board"></a>Done `s15-odds-board`

_Byte nails up a slate and chalks it in big, honest numbers: PLAIN 9 IN 10. SPARKLY 1 IN 10. LEGENDARY: NO._  
_Underneath, smaller: ALL OF THEM ARE WARM._  
**Pip** _(think)_: Those are worse odds. Why do I like it better?  
**Byte** _(happy)_: Because you know where you are. Knowing where you are is the second-best feeling there is. After winning.  
_The children queue anyway. Some buy. Some just come to read the board._  

### Complete

#### <a id="s15-complete"></a>Complete `s15-complete`

_The last egg in the crate is wrapped in a red spotted handkerchief._  
_You unwrap it. It glows all through, like a pearl in a lamp._  
**Jackpot**: THAT ONE IS REAL. I CANNOT LIE. I CHECKED. I CHECKED TWICE.  
_Tucked in the knot is a card in loopy gold handwriting._  
_“For the Keeper. Every egg a winner. This one actually. —D.P.”_  
**Byte** _(soft)_: Statistically, that’s the only certain thing he ever sold.  
_Jackpot hops onto your shoulder. He ticks. He weighs about as much as a teapot._  
**Jackpot**: I WOULD LIKE TO LIVE WITH YOU. I CANNOT LIE. I HAVE WANTED TO SAY THAT FOR A WHILE.  
_Dashwood pressed the painted egg into Pip’s hands on his way out._  
_Pip carries the painted egg home in her hat. A week later it hatches, brown as a boot._  
_She loves it beyond all reason._  

**Rewards**

- Honest egg stall with an Odds Board (feature): An egg stall at your gate that sells at fair prices. Its Odds Board shows the real chance of every kind of egg, worked out by Byte and updated as the stock changes.
- A real sparkly egg (item): The last egg in Dashwood’s crate, wrapped in his red spotted handkerchief. It glows all through. Jackpot checked twice.
- Jackpot (cosmetic): A ticking brass parrot petpet who rides on your shoulder and cannot lie. Now and then he corrects your Keeper.
