# Side story S16: The Workyard

## S16. The Workyard

*Palworld: cheerful creature labour, base-building and the uncomfortable question underneath (themes only)* · audience: all · where: The Grove gate, then Brisk Hollins’s sawmill upriver · when: Chapter 6, any day after c6-close, once the rain has passed. Closes before chapter 9 begins.

**Cast:** Brisk Hollins (Palworld’s cheerful base-owner (theme only; an original character))

### Step 1: Go upriver with Sparks and Clover and see the Workyard.

Hint: Follow the river path north past the stepping stones. You’ll hear the saws long before you see the sheds. · check: `visit:place:s16-workyard`

#### <a id="s16-sparks"></a>Scene `s16-sparks`

_Sparks turns up with a coil of wire over each shoulder, like a very small, very loud pirate electrician._  
**Sparks** _(happy)_: Job upriver! A big one! A sawmill wants lights in every shed, and I need someone to hold the ladder and say “wow”.  
**Sparks**: Owner’s called Brisk Hollins. Nice man. Very nice. Suspiciously cheerful. I’ve brought Clover. She’s good with ladders.  
**Clover** _(think)_: I’m not good with ladders. I’m good at not falling off them. It’s different.  
_The river path is soft after the rain. Somewhere ahead the saws are going: a bright, busy whine, like wasps in a jar._  
**Sparks**: He says his Nubbins love it there. Love it! He wrote it in his letter twice and underlined it both times.  
**Clover** _(think)_: Twice is a lot of underlining.  

#### <a id="s16-yard"></a>Done `s16-yard`

_The Workyard opens out at a bend in the river: sheds, log piles, a waterwheel that isn’t turning, and Nubbins. Dozens._  
_Each one wears a tiny hard hat made from half a walnut shell, painted yellow. They chop, they stack, they haul._  
_Over the gate a banner says WORK IS PLAY! In the middle of the yard, three Nubbins run inside a big wooden wheel._  
_Wires run from the wheel to every shed. Every bulb in the yard flickers in time with their feet._  
**Brisk Hollins** _(happy)_: Welcome! Brisk Hollins. Pleased as punch. Output’s up nine per cent this month. I’m no Circuit man, mind. Mine get Sundays.  
_A big Power Nubbin trundles past with a log three times his size on his back, humming. His hat says LUG in pencil._  
**Brisk Hollins** _(happy)_: That’s Lug. Best worker I’ve got. Can’t stop him. Tried once. He sulked for a week.  
_Behind the log pile, out of the sun, sit five Nubbins whose colours have worn grey at the edges, like a flag left out all summer._  
**Clover** _(soft)_: Those ones are tired. Not lazy. Tired.  

### Step 2: Shadow the Workyard’s Nubbins for a day. Write down what each one does when nobody’s asking.

Hint: Stay out of sight behind the log piles and sheds. If a Nubbin sees you, it starts working to be polite, and that note doesn’t count. · check: `minigame:clear:s16-shadow-day`

#### <a id="s16-shadow"></a>Scene `s16-shadow`

**Brisk Hollins** _(happy)_: Tired? Nonsense! Look at the chart!  
_He unrolls a chart. A red line climbs so steeply it runs off the paper and carries on, in pencil, across the table._  
**Clover** _(think)_: That chart is about logs. I’m asking about them.  
**Brisk Hollins** _(happy)_: Same thing! Happy Nubbins, happy logs. The logs are very happy.  
**Sparks** _(think)_: Brisk. When nobody’s telling them what to do, what do they do?  
_Brisk opens his mouth to answer. He shuts it again. He has clearly never once wondered._  
**Brisk Hollins** _(think)_: …Well. Good question. Splendid question. Find out! Have a clipboard. I’ve got forty.  
**Clover** _(soft)_: Watch from where they can’t see you. If they know they’re watched, they’ll work to be polite. Nubbins are like that.  

**Choices**

- **Start with Lug.** — sets `{"s16-first":"lug"}`
    - Lug puts down a log, sees you, and at once picks up a bigger one, to show you. You will need to be sneakier.
- **Start with the tired ones behind the log pile.** — sets `{"s16-first":"tired"}`
    - **Clover**: Good. Start with the ones nobody’s counting.
- **Start with the wheel.** — sets `{"s16-first":"wheel"}`
    - **Sparks**: The wheel. Yeah. That thing’s bothered me since we walked in. It’s wired backwards, for a start.

#### <a id="s16-notes"></a>Done `s16-notes`

_By sundown your clipboard is full of small handwriting and one large sawdust thumbprint._  
_The choppers chop on their breaks too. They like the thunk._  
_One chops the same stump all day, very happily, for no reason._  
_The wheel runners step out the moment Brisk turns his back, and sit under the bench with their eyes shut._  
_The five tired ones never once choose work. They lie in a patch of sun and watch the river go by._  
_And Lug, on his breaks, carries offcuts. Not to the stack. Out to the bank, behind the reeds, one at a time._  
_You follow him. In the reeds stands a little lean-to of planks and bark, roofed with moss._  
_The five tired ones are asleep inside._  
**Clover** _(shock)_: He built them a house.  
**Sparks** _(soft)_: Nobody asked him to. He just… did.  

### Step 3: Build a Fair Roster with Brisk: jobs by liking, rests, fruit breaks, a bell, and no more wheel.

Hint: Put each Nubbin’s card under the job it chose when nobody was asking. Hold Sparks’s ladder while she moves the lights to the waterwheel. · check: `build:decor:s16-fair-roster`

#### <a id="s16-roster"></a>Scene `s16-roster`

_You show Brisk the clipboard, and then the lean-to in the reeds. He stands in the wet grass a long time._  
**Brisk Hollins** _(sad)_: I thought they loved it. They looked like they loved it. They were always so busy.  
**Brisk Hollins** _(think)_: …Busy isn’t the same thing, is it.  
_He takes down WORK IS PLAY! and puts up a new banner: WORK IS… OFTEN PLAY!_  
**Sparks** _(think)_: That’s worse.  
**Brisk Hollins** _(sad)_: I know. I’m working on it.  
**Sparks** _(happy)_: Right. That wheel’s coming off the wires. Your lights go on the waterwheel. Like a normal person’s.  
**Clover** _(soft)_: And every Nubbin gets a card, under the job it picked. And breaks. With fruit.  
**Brisk Hollins** _(shock)_: And the ones who don’t want a job at all? What then? What’s a yard for?  
_Nobody answers. Brisk puts up another banner. REST IS A KIND OF WORK?? It has two question marks._  

#### <a id="s16-bell"></a>Done `s16-bell`

_By evening the Fair Roster hangs by the gate: a board of little painted cards, one for every Nubbin, under the job it chose._  
_Beside it hangs a small brass bell, low enough for the smallest Nubbin to reach. Clover has painted on it: NO THANK YOU._  
_The big wheel stands still. Sparks has hung a sign on it: RETIRED. A Nubbin is already asleep inside it._  
_A wheel runner waddles to the bell, looks round at everyone, and rings it. Once. Very softly._  
_Then it goes and sits in the sun. Nobody stops it. It looks astonished._  
**Brisk Hollins** _(happy)_: Well, I’ll be. I’ll be. Output’s going to be awful.  

### Step 4: Go back to the Workyard in a week and see how the Fair Roster is holding up.

Hint: Any day from a week on. Follow the river path north; Brisk will have the kettle on. · check: `visit:place:s16-workyard-week`

#### <a id="s16-question"></a>Scene `s16-question`

_Brisk walks you to the river path._  
_His hands are in his pockets, and he has a new slogan in his head. You can tell._  
**Brisk Hollins** _(think)_: Can I ask you something, Keeper? Honestly? Your lot at the Grove. They fetch you things, don’t they?  
**Brisk Hollins** _(think)_: Apples. Sticks. Do you ask them? Or do they just do it, because you’re the one who feeds them?  
_You think of your Nubbins dragging fallen apples to your door, and how pleased they look, and how pleased you look back._  
**You**: I don’t know.  
**Brisk Hollins** _(sad)_: No. Me neither. That’s the trouble with being liked. You never find out what they’d do if they didn’t.  
**Brisk Hollins** _(think)_: Now. The five in the reeds. They don’t want to work. Not a bit. What do I do with them?  

**Choices**

- **Send them to the Grove. They can rest with you.** — sets `{"s16-tired":"grove"}`
    - **Brisk Hollins**: …Yes. Yes, that’s kind. Lug will want to visit. Lug will want to carry them there.
    - Lug, who has been listening, is already lifting the whole lean-to off the ground, with them inside it.
- **Keep them on, as “morale officers”.** — sets `{"s16-tired":"morale"}`
    - **Brisk Hollins**: Morale officers! Officially in charge of sitting in the sun and being lovely. Yes!
    - **Clover**: They’ll be the best in the yard at it. Leave them where Lug can see them.
    - **Sparks**: Are we sure that isn’t just a job with a nicer hat?
- **Let them go wild, up the river.** — sets `{"s16-tired":"wild"}`
    - **Brisk Hollins**: Wild? Out there? Who’ll feed them? Who’ll—
    - **Clover**: Themselves, maybe. Some Nubbins are happiest belonging to nobody.
    - Brisk looks up the river a long while. He doesn’t like it. He doesn’t say no.

#### <a id="s16-week"></a>Done `s16-week`

_A week later the Workyard is louder, and slower._  
_The saws stop for fruit at eleven and at three. Somebody has hung a hammock._  
_Brisk’s banner has been through several drafts. You can read them all, crossed out, one under the other:_  
_WORK IS PLAY! · WORK IS… OFTEN PLAY! · REST IS A KIND OF WORK?? · ASK FIRST._  
**Brisk Hollins** _(happy)_: Output’s down eleven per cent. Lights stay on, mind. The waterwheel’s steadier than three tired Nubbins. Who knew?  
**Sparks** _(happy)_: I knew. I said. I said it loudly.  
_Lug trundles past with a log on his back, humming. Nobody asked him. He just likes it._  

### Complete

#### <a id="s16-complete"></a>Complete `s16-complete`

**Brisk Hollins** _(happy)_: Here. Can’t have a Keeper on my yard without a hat. Regulations. My regulations. New ones.  
_A yellow hard hat in your size, painted to match the walnut-shell ones. Lug’s pencil writing on the brim says KEEPER._  
**Brisk Hollins** _(soft)_: And take a copy of the roster. Try it at the Grove. Let them pick their jobs, and see what they pick.  
**Clover** _(soft)_: And see what they don’t. That’s just as worth knowing.  
_Back home you nail the Fair Roster by the cottage door. By morning, three Nubbins have pushed their cards under jobs._  
_One has chosen watering. One has chosen fetching apples._  
_One has pushed its card under “sitting with Moss”, and won’t be moved._  
**Sparks** _(think)_: That last one isn’t a job.  
**Clover** _(happy)_: It is to Moss.  

**Rewards**

- Nubbin Jobs (feature): Your Nubbins volunteer for the garden jobs they enjoy (watering, fetching, digging, keeping someone company) and only those. A Nubbin never does a job it didn’t pick.
- Workyard hard hat (cosmetic): A yellow hard hat with KEEPER on the brim in Lug’s pencil. Your Nubbins can wear walnut-shell ones to match.
- The Fair Roster board (decor): A board of painted job cards with a little brass NO THANK YOU bell. Your Nubbins put their own cards under the jobs they like.
