# Side story S17: Nightjar

## S17. Nightjar

*Inspired by Ori and the Blind Forest: the great owl whose grief made her a terror to the forest (themes only)* · audience: all · where: The Grove nursery, at dawn · when: Chapter 7 (autumn), the first morning after c7-open, if a nursery is placed. Closes before chapter 10 begins.

**Cast:** Nightjar

### Step 1: Search the nursery and the fence for clues.

Hint: Look closely at the straw, the nursery roof and the top rail of the fence. Show Luna anything you find. · check: `inspect:nursery:s17-clues`

#### <a id="s17-missing"></a>Scene `s17-missing`

_Autumn. The first conkers are down, and the nursery straw is damp with dew._  
_In the straw there is a round, warm dent where an egg was last night. It is the fourth dent this fortnight._  
**Luna** _(think)_: One a night. Always the warmest one. Never a crack, never a crumb of shell. Whatever it is, it’s careful.  
**Nova**: Morning. I’m not stopping. I was passing, and I heard there was a mystery, and I’m not stopping.  
**Nova** _(think)_: Badger.  
**Luna**: It’s not a badger.  
**Nova**: A tall badger.  
**Luna** _(soft)_: Look at the straw before you trample it, both of you. Then we can talk about tall badgers.  

#### <a id="s17-clues"></a>Done `s17-clues`

_On the top rail of the fence: a grey feather as long as your forearm, soft as smoke. Its edge is torn, as if on rock._  
_On the roof, faint scratches. Something landed very gently here, and left very gently._  
_And the dent in the straw is still warm, hours later. Something kept that egg warm until the last second._  
**Luna** _(shock)_: That’s an owl’s. A big owl. Bigger than an owl has any business being.  
**Nova** _(happy)_: Two badgers in a coat, holding an owl feather to throw us off.  
**Luna**: Nova.  
**Nova** _(think)_: It stays on the list. Lists should be long. Tonight, then? I’m not stopping. I’m just staying up.  

### Step 2: Creep through Luna’s garden to the hide by the nursery without being heard.

Hint: Move when the wind moves the leaves. Keep to moss and thyme, off the gravel, in the shadows of the big flowers. · check: `minigame:sneak:s17-luna-garden`

#### <a id="s17-night"></a>Scene `s17-night`

_After dark Luna’s garden is another country. The white flowers are wide open and the raked paths glow faintly._  
**Luna** _(think)_: We watch the nursery from in here. The flower beds give shadow. The gravel doesn’t. Gravel tells everyone everything.  
**Luna**: Stay on the moss. Stay low. If something lands, don’t stare at it. Owls hate being stared at. So do I.  
**Nova**: Can I bring the list?  
**Luna** _(soft)_: You can bring the list in your head. Quietly.  

**Choices**

- **Follow Luna’s path exactly.** — sets `{"s17-path":"luna"}`
    - **Luna**: Good. Left foot on the thyme. It doesn’t mind. It smells nicer for it.
- **Find your own way through.** — sets `{"s17-path":"own"}`
    - You step on a snail shell. It cracks like a pistol. The whole garden holds its breath. So does Luna.
    - **Luna**: …Your own way is very loud.

#### <a id="s17-reveal"></a>Done `s17-reveal`

_Midnight. The wind drops. Something crosses the moon without a sound, and the garden goes still beneath it._  
_An owl lands on the nursery roof. She is enormous, grey as ash, and one long feather on her left wing is torn._  
_She lifts an egg out of the straw in her feet, slowly, the way you would carry a held breath._  
_Then she turns her head right round and looks at your hiding place. For a long moment she only looks._  
**Nightjar** _(soft)_: Hoo-oo.  
_And she is gone towards the cliffs, low over the hedges, carrying the egg as if it were the only thing in the world._  
**Nova** _(shock)_: …I’m crossing off the badgers.  
**Luna** _(soft)_: Nightjar. That’s what Hazel called her. I thought she was a story. The biggest owl in the valley, Hazel said.  
**Luna**: And the saddest.  

### Step 3: Climb to Nightjar’s nest on the cliff, slowly.

Hint: Use the cracks with ferns growing in them; those are the solid ones. Stop whenever she turns her head. · check: `climb:cliff:s17-nest`

#### <a id="s17-cliffs"></a>Scene `s17-cliffs`

_Next night you follow the way she flew, past the ridge to the high cliffs, where the wind smells of salt and cold stone._  
_Halfway up, wedged in a crack, is a ledge with a nest on it the size of a cartwheel._  
**Nova** _(think)_: I’ve slept up here. Years back. An owl called all night, the same two notes. I thought it was the wind.  
**Luna** _(think)_: She hunts at the turn of the tide. That’s soon. We could go up while she’s out.  
**Luna**: Or we could wait for her, and let her see us coming. Owls remember faces.  

**Choices**

- **Climb while she’s out hunting.** — sets `{"s17-approach":"sneak"}`
    - You climb fast, hand over hand. You are nearly at the ledge when a shadow drops past you, silent, and settles.
    - She is home. She sits on the nest and looks at you, and does not move.
    - **Nova**: Well. She was always going to be home, wasn’t she.
- **Wait for her, where she can see you.** — sets `{"s17-approach":"wait"}`
    - You sit on a rock below the ledge in plain view. She comes home at the turn of the tide and looks at you a long time.
    - Then she shuffles over, just a little, as if making room.
    - **Luna**: That’s an invitation. Or a warning. With owls it’s mostly both.

#### <a id="s17-nest"></a>Done `s17-nest`

_The nest is woven of heather, sheep’s wool and grey feathers. Under her, packed snug in a ring, are eggs._  
_Five eggs. Four glow faintly, the way the Grove’s eggs do. Every one is whole. Every one is warm._  
_She has turned them so that each one gets its time in the middle._  
_Woven deep into the lining, where they can never be dropped, are two very old eggshells, grey and cracked across._  
**Luna** _(soft)_: Hazel told me about a night before my time. Spring, blossom out. Then a cold came down the valley that had no business there.  
**Luna** _(sad)_: Frost on the blossom. Ice in the stream. In the morning the owl on the cliff was calling and calling.  
**Luna**: Hazel said she never really stopped. That was twenty-two years ago.  
_…_  
**Nova** _(think)_: That number keeps turning up.  
**Nightjar** _(sad)_: Hoo-oo.  
_She settles lower over the eggs, the way you would pull a blanket up over someone already asleep._  

### Step 4: Carry the Grove’s eggs back to the nursery, one at a time, while Nightjar watches.

Hint: Carry each egg in both hands and walk, don’t run. If you hurry, she follows you down and you start that egg again. · check: `carry:egg:s17-nest-to-nursery:4`

#### <a id="s17-return"></a>Scene `s17-return`

_She will not let you take them all at once. You lift two and she clacks her beak, once, like a door shutting._  
**Luna** _(soft)_: One at a time, then. Down and back. Let her watch each one go, and see you come back for the next.  
**Nova** _(shock)_: That’s four climbs. In the dark.  
**Luna** _(happy)_: That’s four climbs. In the dark.  
_Nightjar watches you lift the first egg. Her eyes follow it all the way down, and all the way back up to you._  

#### <a id="s17-wild"></a>Done `s17-wild`

_Dawn. Four eggs back in the nursery straw. You climb up one last time for the lantern you left behind._  
_There is still one egg under her. Small, speckled like lichen, and it doesn’t glow. It isn’t the Grove’s._  
**Nova** _(think)_: That’s nobody’s. Wild. Its mother never came back, I’d guess. It happens.  
_Nightjar looks at the egg, then at you. She does not hide it. She does not move off it, either._  
**Luna** _(soft)_: Leave it with her. Let somebody keep something warm and have it work.  
_You leave it. On the way down, Luna stops on the path._  
**Luna** _(think)_: Next spring she’ll come back to the nursery. Grief keeps a better calendar than I do. What do we do?  

**Choices**

- **Leave the nursery open, with a lantern lit for her.** — sets `{"s17-nursery":"open"}`
    - **Nova**: A night nurse. With a beak. That’s either brilliant or a disaster.
    - **Luna**: If she wants eggs to sit on, she can sit on ours, where we can see. Hazel would have done that.
    - **Luna**: Then worried all night. Then done it again.
- **Move the nursery in by the stove, with a latch.** — sets `{"s17-nursery":"shut"}`
    - **Luna**: That’s fair. The eggs come first. Feed them before you feed yourself, nearly.
    - **Nova**: She won’t take it personally. She’ll just sit on the roof and look at the chimney. Sadly. For ever.
    - **Luna**: Then I’ll leave her a mouse on the step now and then. Grief gets hungry.

### Step 5: Visit Nightjar’s nest on the night the wild egg hatches.

Hint: Take the cliff path after dark. Sit below the ledge and wait; she shuffles aside when she’s ready. · check: `visit:cliff:s17-hatching:night`

#### <a id="s17-feathers"></a>Scene `s17-feathers`

_Some weeks later there is a feather on your windowsill._  
_Grey, soft as smoke, laid down carefully. Then another, next night._  
**Luna** _(soft)_: She’s calling you. The wild one must be hatching. Go after dark. Take nothing. Just go.  
**Nova**: I’m not coming. I’m not stopping. I’ll be on the cliff path by accident, about nine.  

#### <a id="s17-hatching"></a>Done `s17-hatching`

_The wind has dropped. The egg under her is rocking. Nightjar shifts off it, just enough, and you both watch._  
_A crack. A beak like a fingernail. Then a damp grey thing the size of a dumpling, all eyes, very offended._  
**Nightjar** _(happy)_: Hoo-oo. Hoo-oo.  
_The same two notes. They don’t sound the same at all._  
_Later the fledgling tumbles across the rock and into your lap. Nightjar watches. She does not call it back._  
**Luna** _(soft)_: She’s sharing. Owls don’t share. Look at that.  

### Complete

#### <a id="s17-complete"></a>Complete `s17-complete`

_At the end of autumn Nightjar moults, and the wind brings her old grey feathers down into the Grove._  
**Luna** _(happy)_: I’ve sewn them into a cloak. Don’t ask how long it took. I’ll tell you, and then you’ll feel bad.  
_It is as light as breath and as warm as a bird._  
_That night, on the cliff top in the cloak, you hear a hush of wings behind you. Two careful feet take hold of your collar._  
_The valley turns under you, silver and black. The Grove comes up to meet you as gently as an egg set down in straw._  
**Nova** _(happy)_: Show-off. I walked for years to see this valley properly, and you did it in a minute.  
**Nova** _(soft)_: …Take me next time.  

**Rewards**

- Nightjar’s fledgling (petpet) (item): A grey, round, permanently offended owlet. It comes to you at dusk and goes home to its mother on the cliff at dawn. Only if you visited the hatching.
- Night glide (feature): Fast travel from the cliff top by night: wear the feather cloak, stand at the edge, and Nightjar glides you down to the Grove.
- Feather cloak (cosmetic): Nightjar’s moulted grey feathers, sewn into a cloak by Luna. Light as breath, warm as a bird.
