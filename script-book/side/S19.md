# Side story S19: Cinder’s Last Light

## S19. Cinder’s Last Light

*Warcraft: the last guardian who fell and spent his long end trying to make amends, and the fallen hero exiled for an age (themes only)* · audience: all · where: The Grove, then the snowfield and the hollow on the high pass above it · when: Chapter 11 (midwinter), the morning after Hazel’s first evening home, only if the stone was unmade (keptStone false). Closes before the final choice in chapter 12.

**Cast:** Cinder (Warcraft’s fallen guardian who spent his long end making amends (theme only))

### Step 1: Ask Marina about the path up to the high pass.

Hint: She’s at the tide pools, breaking the ice so the anemones can breathe. · check: `talk:marina:high-pass`

#### <a id="s19-warmth"></a>Scene `s19-warmth`

_Two days after the Long Night. The valley is white to the doorsteps, and very quiet._  
_Moss sits on the cottage step, facing north, where the snowfield climbs to the high pass. He has sat like that since dawn._  
_He is thinner than he was. On a bright morning you can see the step through him._  
**Moss** _(think)_: There is something warm up there.  
**Moss**: There shouldn’t be. Nothing has been warm on the high pass since the White Year. Nothing.  
_Up there the snow is the blue of skimmed milk, and quite empty._  
**Moss** _(soft)_: I felt it on the night we unmade the stone. Like a candle someone forgot, in a room you thought was dark.  
**Moss** _(soft)_: I am too thin to climb. I am going anyway. You may carry me, if you would. In your coat. I won’t talk much.  
**Moss**: That last part was a lie. Marina knows the path. Ask her.

### Step 3: Sit beside the red light, and wait.

Hint: Don’t move and don’t speak. It can take a long time. That’s the point. · check: `sit:wait:cinder-hollow`

#### <a id="s19-sit"></a>Scene `s19-sit`

_You sit on the dry rock. The wind goes over the overhang with a sound like a long breath out._  
_Below you, the whole valley: the falls frozen white, smoke from the cottage, four lantern posts too small to see._  
_You wait. Your feet go cold, then numb, and then you stop thinking about them._

**Moss** _(soft)_: I looked for you. After. For a long time, I looked.  
**Cinder** _(sad)_: I know. I hid. You were looking with such a kind face. I couldn’t bear it.

**Cinder** _(sad)_: I was so frightened. I held them so tight.

### Complete

#### <a id="s19-complete"></a>Complete `s19-complete`

_Where the light was, the flat stone is still warm. Not hot._  
_Warm, like a hand that has only just let go of yours._  
_There is a small red pebble on it that wasn’t there before._  
_It is warm too. It stays warm all the way down the mountain._  
_Marina is on the lip with the rope, looking at the view very hard. Her eyes are red. She says it’s the wind._  
**Marina** _(sad)_: Lovely up here.  
**Marina** _(soft)_: Come on. Feet in my prints. Home before the light goes.  
_That night you go and stand by the Long-Grass lantern. It burns as it has every night since the Long Night._  
_But it’s warm now, standing beside it. The other three never are._  
_You put the red pebble on the windowsill._  
_By morning the Nubbins are asleep in a heap under it. None of them will say why._

#### <a id="end-epi-cinder-remembered"></a>Variant `end-epi-cinder-remembered`

_In the spring there is a new flower on the hill. It is red, and it comes up a little apart from the others, as if it isn’t sure it’s allowed._  
_You never said she was forgiven. It wasn’t yours to say._  
_But you tell her story, all of it, to anyone who asks, and some who don’t._  
_The Long-Grass lantern burns warm now. You stand beside it on cold nights, and don’t explain why._  
_The children still skip to her rhyme. Now, at the end, one of them always shouts “She loved them!” and the rest shout “Too tight!”_  
_Then they all run off, laughing. It’s the whole story. They’ve got it right._
