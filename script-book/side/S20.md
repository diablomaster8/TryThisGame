# Side story S20: Fort Night

## S20. Fort Night

*StarCraft and Warcraft as games: real-time strategy, gathering, building, scouting and units that counter each other; theme only, all words original* · audience: all · where: The Common below Fern’s orchard, deep in snow · when: Chapter 11, after c11-open: the first clear morning after the Long Night. Closes before c12’s final choice.

**Cast:** Tilly (Tiny Tina as Bunker Master (Borderlands 2: Tiny Tina’s Assault on Dragon Keep), reference only), Bodge, Gordon, Wren, Granny Brock

### Step 1: Round one: gather snow and sticks, and build your walls before the other fort does.

Hint: Put most of your children on gathering early. Walls first, catapult second: a catapult with no wall is a target with a spoon. · check: `minigame:clear:s20-round1-economy`

#### <a id="s20-declaration"></a>Scene `s20-declaration`

_The snow after the Long Night is the deep, squeaky kind. Every child in the valley is out in it, and none of them is cold, they say._  
_A sheet of cereal-box card is tied to your gate. The capitals are very angry and the spelling is very brave._  
_“DECLARASHUN OF WAR. FORT NIGHT. THE COMMON. BRING MITTENS. — THE OTHER FORT”_  
_Sparks’s cousin is back for the winter holidays, and has found out about snow._  
**Tilly** _(happy)_: You’re commanding one fort. I’m commanding the other. One of us is going to win, and it’s me.  
_On the Common, two forts are marked out in sticks: one by the hedge, one by the frozen ditch._  
_Neither has a wall. Both have flags._  
**Pixel**: Morning. Referee. I’ve written the rules. Forty-one pages. Nobody will read them. That’s fine. I’ll enforce them.  
**Pixel**: Three rounds. Each round, the referee teaches one idea. This is educational. Nobody tell the children.  
**Pixel**: Round one: economy. Snow makes walls. Sticks make the catapult. More gatherers early means more of everything later.  
**Pixel**: Units: Speedy beats Splashy. Splashy beats Sturdy. Sturdy beats Speedy. It goes round, like the seasons. Or a grudge.  
_Behind Pixel, a small boy in a bobble hat is hit in the ear by a snowball. Nobody has started yet._  
**Gordon** _(happy)_: I’m all right!  
**Pixel**: That’s Gordon. Gordon gets hit. It isn’t a rule. It’s more like weather.  
_At a cottage door at the edge of the Common, Granny Brock stands with her arms folded, refereeing nobody._  
**Granny Brock**: Nobody hit my bell.  
_Gordon hits her bell._  
**Wren** _(happy)_: Commander! I’m Wren! What’s our battle cry, commander?  

**Choices**

- **“FOR THE FORT!”** — sets `{"s20-cry":"fort"}`
    - **Wren**: FOR THE FORT!
    - **Pixel**: Classic. Solid. Ruling: approved.
- **“FOR WREN’S NAN’S GOOD MITTENS!”** — sets `{"s20-cry":"mittens"}`
    - **Wren**: FOR MY NAN’S GOOD MITTENS!
    - **Wren**: She knitted them. They’ve got thumbs and everything.
- **“REMEMBER GORDON!”** — sets `{"s20-cry":"gordon"}`
    - **Gordon**: I’m right here.
    - Your whole fort roars it anyway. Gordon looks touched. Then he is hit by a snowball.

#### <a id="s20-round1"></a>Done `s20-round1`

**Wren**: Yes, commander! On it, commander!  
_Every time you point at Wren, she salutes and says something. You point at her rather a lot._  
**Wren** _(angry)_: Yes, commander. Yes, commander. Commander, that’s nine times. I’m only little.  
**Pixel**: Notice: your sticks are low. Notice: you can’t recruit more children. You’ve run out of mittens.  
**Pixel**: Ruling: a snowball that hits a snowman is friendly fire. The snowman has feelings. He’s called Derek.  
_At the whistle, your wall is knee-high, fat and lumpy. Theirs is shoulder-high, thin, and has battlements._  
**Tilly** _(happy)_: Economy! I put everyone on snow from the very start. I learned it off the Tax Toad.  
**Pixel**: Round one: draw. Theirs is taller. Yours is fatter. Ruling: both of them are walls.  
_Between rounds, two children skip to a rhyme you almost know. Both fall over at the same word._  

### Step 2: Round two: send Zip to scout the other fort and get back unseen.

Hint: Drifts block their lookout’s view. Sprint between them when the lookout turns away. Zip’s ears go flat when he’s spotted. · check: `minigame:clear:s20-round2-scouting`

#### <a id="s20-scout"></a>Scene `s20-scout`

**Pixel**: Round two: scouting. You can’t beat what you can’t see.  
_Sparks lends you a Swift Nubbin called Zip: thin as a whisker, faster than gossip, and unable to sit still for any reason._  
_Zip vibrates in your mittens like a sneeze that hasn’t happened yet._  
**Pixel**: Send the scout round their fort. Count what they’ve got. Don’t let him get caught.  
**Pixel**: Being caught means a snowball to the face. For Zip, that’s most of his body.  
**Gordon** _(happy)_: Can I scout?  
**Pixel**: No, Gordon. You’d get hit.  
**Gordon**: That’s fair.  

#### <a id="s20-intel"></a>Done `s20-intel`

_Zip comes back with snow on his eyebrows and something urgent to say, which he says by running round you nine times._  
_You read it in his tracks. Their wall is magnificent. Behind it, there is almost nobody._  
_Three of their children have gone home: one lost a mitten, one lost a boot, and one just wanted his tea._  
**Tilly** _(angry)_: We are FINE. We have LOADS of people. Some of them are in the wall. As bricks.  
**Pixel**: Ruling: children are not bricks.  
**Pixel**: …Ruling: nearly.  

**Choices**

- **Attack now, while they’re thin. It’s a war. They’d do the same.** — sets `{"s20-round2":"pressed"}`
    - **Wren**: CHARGE! Yes, commander! Charging, commander!
    - Your fort pours across the Common. Their wall is splendid, and almost nobody is behind it.
    - **Tilly**: That’s not FAIR!
    - **Tilly**: …That’s exactly what I’d have done. I hate it.
    - **Pixel**: Round two to your fort. Scouting works. It’s horrible. That’s the lesson.
- **Wait till their three come back. Beating half a fort isn’t winning.** — sets `{"s20-round2":"waited"}`
    - You send Zip back carrying a snowball with a smiley face pressed into it. It means “we’ll wait”.
    - Ten minutes later one mitten, one boot and one boy full of tea come trudging back up the lane.
    - **Tilly**: You waited for us? That’s either very nice or very sneaky.
    - **Pixel**: Round two: draw. Ruling: honour is worth one point. I’ve just added it. Page forty-two.

### Step 3: Round three: send the units that beat theirs, and take their flag.

Hint: Splashy beats Sturdy: wet slush soaks a roller and it slumps. Watch what they switch to, and switch too. · check: `minigame:clear:s20-round3-counters`

#### <a id="s20-counters"></a>Scene `s20-counters`

**Pixel**: Round three. The last one. Counters.  
**Pixel**: Look at what they’re sending. Send what beats it. That’s all strategy is. Everything else is shouting.  
_Over the ditch, the other fort is massing Sturdy: big children rolling big snowballs, slow as weather._  
**Tilly** _(angry)_: SURRENDER OR BE ROLLED!  
_Your fort answers with its battle cries. “I AM NOT COLD, I AM BRAVE!” “FOR DEREK!” “MUM SAID BE BACK BY FOUR!”_  
_Above the hill the sky has gone the colour of a pigeon. Nobody is looking at it except Pixel._  
**Pixel** _(think)_: Hm.  

#### <a id="s20-blizzard"></a>Done `s20-blizzard`

_You are one dash from their flag when the snow stops falling down and starts falling sideways._  
_Then it is everywhere. You can’t see their fort._  
_You can’t see your own. You can only hear Gordon, being hit by weather._  
**Pixel** _(shock)_: STOP. Whistle. Full stop. That’s a real one. That’s a real blizzard.  
_Pixel’s whistle is lost in it. Somebody small is crying, and you can’t tell which fort they belong to._  
**Tilly** _(shock)_: Our wall’s fallen in! It all fell in! I can’t find everyone!  
_Your own wall is still standing: knee-high, fat and lumpy. It is nowhere near big enough for everybody._  

### Step 4: Build one shelter out of both forts, and get every child inside.

Hint: Speedy children fetch, Sturdy children roll, Splashy children pack the gaps. Count heads: Wren is small and easy to miss. · check: `build:shelter:s20-one-fort`

#### <a id="s20-merge"></a>Scene `s20-merge`

_Through the white you can just make out the hedge, the ditch, and children from both forts, standing about like dropped gloves._  
**Pixel**: Referee’s ruling.  
**Pixel**: …I don’t have a ruling. Commander? You’ve got a wall. They’ve got the snow. Nobody’s got enough.  
**Tilly** _(think)_: Is this a trick? Is this a strategy?  
**You**: Yes.  

**Choices**

- **Kick open your wall and shout them all in.** — sets `{"s20-merge":"gate"}`
    - You kick a gap in your own wall. “IN! EVERYONE IN!”
    - Your fort, which has been taught all morning to do what you say, does it, and starts pulling the others in by their scarves.
- **Carry your wall to them, snowball by snowball.** — sets `{"s20-merge":"carried"}`
    - Your gatherers know how to move snow fast. You taught them that this morning.
    - It turns out that was what it was for.
- **Meet in the middle. Both walls, one shelter.** — sets `{"s20-merge":"middle"}`
    - You meet in the ditch between the forts. Both sides bring snow. Both sides bring sticks.
    - The catapult becomes a roof beam. Derek the snowman becomes a door.

#### <a id="s20-count"></a>Done `s20-count`

**Pixel**: Head count.  
**Pixel** _(soft)_: …Everyone. Even Gordon.  
**Gordon** _(happy)_: I got hit by a whole sky.  
_Inside it is close and blue and smells of wet wool. Forty mittens steam._  
_Somebody is eating snow. Pixel rules against it, then allows it._  
**Tilly**: That’s my wall, in our roof. That bit’s mine.  
**Wren**: Commander? Which fort won?  
_Outside, the wind drops as suddenly as it came. Somebody pokes a hole in the roof to look._  

### Complete

#### <a id="s20-complete"></a>Complete `s20-complete`

_The sky is clear and black and enormous. Every star the valley owns is out, and a few it has borrowed._  
_Nobody goes home. Both forts lie on their backs in one fort and look up._  
**Wren** _(happy)_: That one’s a mitten. That one’s Gordon. That one’s Derek.  
**Pixel**: Final ruling. Both forts win. I’ve written it in pen. Pen is final.  
**Tilly** _(think)_: So what was it for, then? The economy and the scouting and the counters?  
**You**: So we could build one fort fast, when it mattered.  
**Tilly**: …That’s annoying. That’s a really good answer.  
_Up at the cottage a lamp is lit in the window, and somebody in a blanket is watching the Common._  
_Tilly puts two fingers in her mouth and whistles the supper call, rising at the end. From the window, faintly, it comes back._  
**Tilly** _(soft)_: She can still do it.  
_Tilly pulls a damp folded note out of her welly. She doesn’t open it._  
**Tilly** _(soft)_: I’ll read it tomorrow. Out loud. At her.  
**Pixel**: Prizes. Commander’s hat: it was a tea cosy. Snowball launcher: prototype. Ruling: point it away from Gordon.  
**Pixel**: And the fort’s yours. I’ll build you one for the Grove that only comes out when it snows.  

**Rewards**

- Snow fort (decor): A low, lumpy, unbeatable snow fort for the Grove, with a catapult roof beam and a door shaped like a snowman called Derek. Appears whenever it snows.
- Commander’s hat (cosmetic): A tea cosy with a feather in it, issued by the referee. Nubbins near you salute. Most of them get it wrong.
- Snowball-launcher toy (item): Pixel’s prototype: a spring, a spoon and a lot of string. Lobs a soft snowball in winter. Point it away from Gordon.
