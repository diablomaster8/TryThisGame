# Side story S21: CL-4NK

## S21. CL-4NK

*Borderlands: the tiny, over-enthusiastic robot who desperately wants friends and can’t do stairs; theme only, all words original* · audience: all · where: Hazel’s cottage, the front step · when: Chapter 3, after c3-open, on a sunny early-summer morning. Closes before chapter 6.

**Cast:** Clank (Claptrap, the CL4P-TP unit (Borderlands), reference only)

### Step 1: Show Clank round the Grove.

Hint: He follows you on one wheel. Go round things, not over them: he can’t do steps, kerbs or roots, though he believes he can. · check: `walk:clank:grove-tour`

#### <a id="s21-unveil"></a>Scene `s21-unveil`

_Something is coming up the lane, squeaking. It’s Pixel, pushing a wheelbarrow with a tea towel over it._  
**Pixel**: Morning. Don’t be alarmed. Be a bit alarmed. A normal amount.  
**Pixel**: After Sparks fixed the lantern posts there was a whole box of parts left over. I couldn’t just leave them in a box.  
_Off comes the tea towel. Underneath: a lantern hood, a glass chimney, a brass wick-winder for a nose, and one wheel._  
**Pixel**: Garden helper. Watering, weeding, carrying. In theory.  
_Pixel turns the wick-winder. Something inside begins to warm, then whistle, rising, like a kettle that has just heard good news._  
**Clank** _(happy)_: HELLO! Hello hello HELLO. I am CL-4NK! The four is silent! So is the dash!  
**Clank** _(happy)_: Call me Clank! You are my BEST FRIEND!  
**You**: We’ve only just—  
**Clank** _(happy)_: Best friend. Decided. Announcement: I HAVE A BEST FRIEND!  
_He rolls forward to shake your hand, rolls straight off the cottage step, and lands on his hood in the thyme._  
**Clank** _(happy)_: Announcement! I have found the step!  
**Clank**: The step is very good. The step is also my best friend.  
**Pixel** _(think)_: That’s one. I gave it a week before he met the step. I was being optimistic.  

#### <a id="s21-tour"></a>Done `s21-tour`

**Clank** _(happy)_: Announcement: this is a POND! Announcement: this is a GATE! It sticks! Best friend!  
**Clank**: Announcement: I am announcing!  
_By the time you reach the foot of the hill, every Nubbin in the Grove is following him in a line, like ducklings behind a teapot._  
_One climbs onto his hood. Then three. He turns his chimney-light down low for them, and they purr._  
**Clank** _(shock)_: They like me. Announcement: they LIKE me.  
**Clank** _(soft)_: I didn’t know that was available.  

### Step 2: Undo Clank’s helping before he helps anything else.

Hint: Dry the cat, stake the tree, and catch Clank before he reaches the step. In that order, if you can. You won’t. · check: `minigame:clear:s21-undo-the-help`

#### <a id="s21-help"></a>Scene `s21-help`

_You are woken at dawn by a whistle going up and up, and an announcement right outside your bedroom window._  
**Clank** _(happy)_: GOOD MORNING, BEST FRIEND! I have done SO MUCH HELPING!  
_Fern is in her garden in her dressing gown, holding her cat at arm’s length. Mustard is soaked, and furious about it._  
**Fern** _(think)_: He watered Mustard. He watered my cat. In fairness, Mustard was in the beans. And he is a bit bean-coloured.  
**Clank**: Cats are plants that run! I looked it up! In my head!  
_Behind her, Fern’s one good apple tree has been pruned. Carefully. Thoroughly. Into the shape of a duck._  
**Fern** _(sad)_: That was my good tree. The three I’m keeping alive, badly, he never touched. He has a gift.  
**Clank** _(happy)_: Announcement: it is a DUCK!  
_Then you notice the western hill. The small old shape that is always on top of it isn’t there._  
**Clank** _(happy)_: Also! I rescued a little old one! He was stranded up the hill! For HOURS! He wasn’t even MOVING!  
_Speeding round the pond at what can only be called full kettle is Clank._  
_He is holding the little grey watcher from the hill out in front of him, like a pie._  

#### <a id="s21-moss"></a>Done `s21-moss`

_You catch up with Clank at the cottage. So does the step._  
_Clank goes over the edge._  
_The little grey one leaves his hands, turns once in the air, and lands on his feet on the path, as if he’d planned the morning._  
**Clank** _(sad)_: Announcement… I have found the step again.  
_The little grey one looks at you for a long time. It is the look of someone very old who has been carried round a pond like a pie._  
_Then he walks back up the hill, and Clank watches him go. It takes him the rest of the morning, and he doesn’t look back once._  
**Pixel**: That’s two.  

### Step 3: Find Clank.

Hint: Follow the wheel-track through the thyme. He’s hiding somewhere he thinks he fits in. · check: `find:clank:s21-hiding`

#### <a id="s21-log"></a>Scene `s21-log`

_That afternoon Byte is at the gate with a clipboard, a pencil behind each ear, and the face of someone who has made a table._  
**Byte**: Incident log. Since breakfast: one wet cat. One duck-shaped tree. One elderly Nubbin, moved at roughly eleven miles an hour.  
**Byte**: Two falls down the step. Fourteen declarations of best friendship. One of them to a bucket.  
**Byte** _(think)_: He’s a kettle on a wheel with a stated intention to help. That is exactly how accidents introduce themselves.  
**Byte**: Next time it’s a Nubbin he waters. Or drops. Pixel should switch Clank off. Tonight. Before he hurts someone.  
**Pixel**: Pixel is standing right here, Byte.  
**Byte** _(think)_: Noted. You’re in the log too. Line six. “Wife: encouraging him.”  
**Pixel**: I was encouraging him to stay off the step.  
**Byte**: Line seven. “Unsuccessfully.”  
_Behind you, a small whistle starts to rise, and stops halfway._  
_Clank is at the top of the cottage step. He has heard all of it. His chimney-light has gone very dim._  
_He reverses. He goes off the step backwards, lands in the thyme, and doesn’t announce anything at all._  
**Pixel** _(sad)_: …That’s three.  
_By the time you reach the step he’s gone. There is just a wheel-track through the herbs, heading for the lantern shed._  
**Byte** _(sad)_: I didn’t know he could hear. I didn’t know he could… mind.  

**Choices**

- **Tell Byte he had a point. Clank does need to learn.** — sets `{"s21-byte":"fair"}`
    - **Byte**: I did have a point. I hate that I had a point. It’s much worse having one when it hurts.
    - **Pixel**: Something between “on” and “kettle”. That’s all he needs. I can build that.
- **Tell Byte that wasn’t fair. He’s days old.** — sets `{"s21-byte":"unfair"}`
    - **Byte**: Days old. I didn’t factor that in.
    - **Byte**: I was days old once. I’m told I bit a vicar.

#### <a id="s21-shed"></a>Done `s21-shed`

_The lantern shed smells of paraffin and old string. Clank is on the back shelf among the spare chimneys, pretending to be a lantern._  
_He is very bad at it. Lanterns don’t shake._  
**Clank** _(sad)_: Announcement. I’m a lantern now. Lanterns are useful. Nobody switches off a lantern.  
**Clank**: Except in the morning. They do switch them off in the morning.  
**Clank**: Byte’s right. I watered a cat. I made a tree into a duck. I carried the old one too fast and he didn’t say one single thing.  
**Clank** _(think)_: Am I a friend? Or am I a thing that does jobs?  
**Clank**: Because if it’s jobs, I can do jobs better. I can learn. But if I stop being useful…  
**Clank**: If I stop being useful, will you still…  
_He stops. Something inside him clicks, and clicks again, looking for a word it hasn’t got._  
**Clank** _(sad)_: I don’t have the word. Pixel didn’t put it in.  

**Choices**

- **Sit down below the shelf, and wait.** — sets `{"s21-lesson":"sit"}`
    - You sit on the shed floor under the shelf. It isn’t comfortable. You stay.
    - **Clank**: What are you doing? Is it a job?
    - **You**: No. I’m just here.
    - **Clank**: Here for what?
    - **You**: Nothing. That’s the whole thing.
    - After a long time Clank comes down off the shelf, very carefully, and parks beside you. He doesn’t announce it.
- **Teach him the step: it’s all right to ask for help.** — sets `{"s21-lesson":"step"}`
    - **You**: Do you know why you keep falling down the step?
    - **Clank**: Because I am brave?
    - **You**: Because you never ask anyone to help you down it.
    - **Clank**: But then I’d be needing. Helpers don’t need. Needing is the opposite of a job.
    - **You**: Everybody needs a hand with a step sometimes. Fern does. Hazel did. I do, on bad days.
    - Clank thinks about this for so long that his whistle goes off by itself, very softly.

### Step 4: Help Byte build a ramp for the cottage step.

Hint: Byte measures; you saw. Measure twice. Byte will measure eleven times. Let him. · check: `build:ramp:s21-cottage-step`

#### <a id="s21-ramp"></a>Scene `s21-ramp`

_Next afternoon Byte comes up the lane with a plank, a saw, and the face of a sum that won’t come out._  
**Byte**: I measured the step. Nineteen centimetres. The safe height for one wheel is, as far as I can find, zero.  
**Byte**: So it isn’t him. Statistically, it’s the step.  
**Byte** _(think)_: I said he should be switched off. That was a conclusion. I hadn’t finished collecting the data.  
**Byte** _(soft)_: The data now includes eleven Nubbins who follow him everywhere. That is a significant result.  
**Clank** _(happy)_: Byte! Byte! Does that mean you’re my best—  
**Byte** _(happy)_: Provisionally.  

#### <a id="s21-down"></a>Done `s21-down`

_The ramp is finished: small, sanded, very well made. Clank rolls to the top of the step and stops._  
**Clank** _(soft)_: Announcement. I am going down the step.  
**Clank**: Announcement. Would somebody… come with me? The first time?  
_Byte puts one hand on his hood. You put one on the other side. Pixel holds the wheelbarrow, for reasons._  
_He goes down the ramp at the speed of a snail with doubts. He reaches the bottom. Nothing falls._  
**Clank** _(happy)_: I DID THE STEP! I did the step and I didn’t find it!  
_He does a little dance: forward, back, a spin on his one wheel._  
_You copy it. Byte copies it, and later denies it._  
**Pixel** _(happy)_: That’s… none. I’ll take none.  

### Step 5: Stay in the Grove with Clank until dusk.

Hint: Feed the Nubbins first. Then sit by the cottage step and do nothing at all. It’s harder than it sounds. · check: `wait:dusk:s21-step`

#### <a id="s21-evening"></a>Scene `s21-evening`

_The afternoon goes gold. Clank parks at the bottom of the new ramp and doesn’t water anything, which costs him a great deal._  
**Clank** _(think)_: Announcement: I am not helping. I am just… here.  
**Clank**: Is that allowed? Is just here allowed?  
**You**: Just here is allowed.  
**Clank** _(soft)_: Announcement: just here is allowed.  

### Complete

#### <a id="s21-complete"></a>Complete `s21-complete`

_At dusk the Nubbins come in from the long grass one by one, and climb onto Clank, because he’s warm, like a kettle that’s just been on._  
_Seven of them. Eight. One on his wheel. One perched on his lantern hood, like a hat._  
**Clank** _(soft)_: Announcement.  
**Clank** _(soft)_: I am being very still.  
_He is. For an hour, the chattiest thing in the valley doesn’t move a bolt._  
_Not even to announce that he isn’t moving._  
_You sit beside him. That’s all. It turns out that’s plenty._  
**Pixel**: Keep him. He’s yours now. He’ll water things, slowly. Mostly plants.  
_Pixel drops a spare lantern hood on your head. It fits, as if it was waiting to._  
_Next morning he is out among the beans with a watering can, one bean a minute. Mustard watches from Fern’s wall, from very far away._
