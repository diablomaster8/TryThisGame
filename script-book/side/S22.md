# Side story S22: The Climb

## S22. The Climb

*Celeste: climbing a mountain with your own anxiety as a companion, and learning to climb with it, not against it (themes only)* · audience: all · where: The foot of the Tooth, the sharp peak above Dusty’s cliff · when: After chapter 8’s opening (once Nova has asked for help for the first time). Closes in chapter 11.

**Cast:** Nova’s Worry (Celeste: climbing with your own anxiety as a companion (theme only; an original character))

### Step 1: Climb the Scree with Nova.

Hint: On loose stones keep moving: three quick steps, then rest on a big rock. Never stop on the small ones. · check: `climb:tooth:scree`

#### <a id="s22-foot"></a>Scene `s22-foot`

_The Tooth stands over the valley like something the valley bit and didn’t finish. There’s already snow on its tip._  
_Nova is at the foot with a coil of rope, a bag of oatcakes, and the look of someone who has been here before._  
**Nova** _(think)_: Three times. I’ve tried it three times. I got as far as the Ridge every time. Then I came down. I always come down.  
**Nova**: That’s me. I come, I look, and I leave before anywhere can start to matter.  
**Nova** _(soft)_: I asked you for help once, in the Hollow. It went well. I thought I’d try it again before I lose my nerve.  
_On the rock beside her sits something the size of a cat, made of shadow. It has Nova’s shape exactly._  
**Nova’s Worry** _(think)_: You’re going to fall.  
_Nova goes very still. She looks at you._  
**Nova** _(shock)_: …You can see it.  
**Nova** _(shock)_: Nobody can see it. I’ve carried that about my whole life and nobody has ever once—  
**You**: Is it dangerous?  
**Nova** _(think)_: It’s mine. It’s my Worry. It’s talked to me since I was small. It’s mostly right, which is the annoying part.  
**Nova’s Worry** _(happy)_: Mostly.  
**Nova**: Right. Scree first. I’ll sing. It helps. It doesn’t help anyone else.  

#### <a id="s22-scree"></a>Done `s22-scree`

_The scree slides under every step, with a sound like someone pouring out a sack of teacups._  
**Nova** _(happy)_: ♪ Little stones, little stones, sliding out from under my bones, ♪  
**Nova** _(happy)_: ♪ I would like to go up but I’m going down, which is the opposite of up, little stones. ♪  
_It doesn’t rhyme. It doesn’t scan. She sings it four times._  
**Nova’s Worry**: Last time you slipped here and cut your knee. You’ve still got the scar.  
**Nova** _(think)_: It’s right. It’s always right about the facts. It’s the next bit it gets wrong.

### Step 2: Climb the Chimney: brace and push, one side to the other.

Hint: Push with your back, step with your feet. Move one limb at a time and you won’t slip. · check: `climb:tooth:chimney`

#### <a id="s22-chimney"></a>Scene `s22-chimney`

_The Chimney is a crack in the rock just wide enough for a person and a doubt. You go up it back on one side, boots on the other._  
**Nova** _(happy)_: ♪ I’m in a chimney, I am not a soot, I am a Nova, and this is my foot. ♪  
**Nova** _(happy)_: ♪ This is my other foot. ♪  
_Far below, a buzzard turns and turns, watching you with what you feel is professional interest._  

#### <a id="s22-chimney-top"></a>Done `s22-chimney-top`

_Halfway up the Chimney, Nova stops. The Worry is wedged in above her, looking down._  
**Nova’s Worry** _(soft)_: Everywhere you stay, you lose something. The last place you stayed, you were nine. Remember?  
_Nova doesn’t move. Her knuckles are white on the rock._  
**Nova** _(sad)_: It’s true. That’s the thing. That one’s true.  
**Nova** _(sad)_: Somebody left. So I learned to be the one who leaves first. It’s quicker. It hurts less. It’s quicker.  

**Choices**

- **“It’s true. It still isn’t a reason to let go.”** — sets `{"s22-chimney":"true"}`
    - **Nova**: …No. It isn’t.
    - She moves one hand. Then one foot. Then the other hand. Very slowly, like someone learning how.
    - **Nova’s Worry**: I wasn’t telling you to let go. I was telling you it was a long way down.
    - **Nova**: I know. You’re very bad at saying what you mean. I wonder where you get it.
- **“Not here. Not now. Look at me, not down.”** — sets `{"s22-chimney":"look"}`
    - She looks at you. Not at the drop. Not at the Worry. At you.
    - **Nova**: …Right. Right. Hands.
    - She climbs. The Worry climbs too, right behind her, muttering about loose rock.
    - It’s right about the loose rock.
    - Far below, the buzzard gives up and goes to watch somebody else.

### Step 3: Cross the Ridge.

Hint: Keep low in the gusts. Hand on the rock, eyes on the next step, not the drop. · check: `climb:tooth:ridge`

#### <a id="s22-ridge"></a>Scene `s22-ridge`

_The Ridge is a knife of rock with the whole sky on both sides. The wind comes over it in long, cold shoves._  
_This is where Nova turned back. You can see the place: a little cairn of three stones, one for each try._  
**Nova** _(think)_: Here. This is where I always… yes.  
**Nova’s Worry** _(soft)_: Go back. It’s windy. It’s high. They’ll wonder where you are. You can come another day.  
**Nova** _(sad)_: Every true thing it says makes me smaller. And I fight it, and fight it, and up here I lose. Every time.  

#### <a id="s22-ridge-cross"></a>Done `s22-ridge-cross`

_In the middle of the Ridge Nova stops. The Worry is beside her. It has always been beside her._  
_She looks at it for a long time. Then she holds out her hand._  
**Nova** _(soft)_: Come on, then.  
**Nova’s Worry** _(shock)_: …What?  
**Nova** _(soft)_: You’re coming anyway. You always come. So come with me. Not at me.  
_The Worry looks at her hand. Then it takes it._  
_It’s smaller, with its hand in hers. Not gone. Smaller._  
_They cross the Ridge together. Nova starts the stones song again, and a second voice joins in, a third below hers, horribly flat._  
**Nova’s Worry** _(happy)_: ♪ …little stones, which are very loose, and we should mind them… ♪  
**Nova** _(shock)_: You can SING?  
**Nova’s Worry** _(soft)_: I’ve always been able to sing. You never asked.  

### Step 4: Plant the summit flag on top of the Tooth.

Hint: It’s in Nova’s bag, under the oatcakes. Wedge the pole into the crack on the very top. · check: `place:decor:summit-flag`

#### <a id="s22-scramble"></a>Scene `s22-scramble`

_The last bit is a scramble on hands and knees, up to a point of rock the size of a kitchen table._  
**Nova** _(happy)_: Give me the flag. No, you do it. No. Together. Fine. Together.  

#### <a id="s22-summit"></a>Done `s22-summit`

_From the top of the Tooth you can see everything at once._  
_The falls, a long white thread. Dusty’s cliff._  
_Marina’s pools like spilled coins. The Circuit, square and small._  
_The Hollow, dark in its fold of trees. And the Grove: the pond, the cottage roof, four lantern posts no bigger than pins._  
_Nova sits down. The Worry sits beside her, holding her hand. Neither of them says anything for a while._  
**Nova** _(soft)_: I’d like to stay for a while.  
_She says it to the valley first. Then, more quietly, to the Worry. Then to you._  
**Nova** _(soft)_: Not up here. Down there. I’d like to stay for a while.  
**Nova’s Worry** _(soft)_: It’ll be frightening.  
**Nova** _(soft)_: Yes. You’ll come too.  

### Complete

#### <a id="s22-complete"></a>Complete `s22-complete`

_Going down is quicker and much worse, as it always is. Nova sings the whole way. So does the Worry._  
_At the bottom she unpacks the oatcakes, which have been sat on, and shares them out in three._  
_The Worry checks its piece is exactly as big as yours. It is. It checks again._  
**Nova** _(happy)_: It’s always done that. Since I was small.  
**Nova** _(soft)_: Keep the flag. Put it somewhere you’ll see it. I’ll know where it is. I’ll come and look at it.  
**Nova** _(happy)_: And have my boots. They’re my spare pair. I won’t need spares. I’m not going anywhere.  
**Nova** _(soft)_: You can see the whole valley from up there now, whenever you like. In case you ever need to find me. I’ll be in it.  
_The Worry waves. Small. Only you can see it._
