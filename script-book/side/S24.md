## S24. The Adventure Book

Two quoted lines; all other words original* · audience: all · where: The western hill by Hazel’s flower; then the carrier’s cart, the scree path beside the falls, the far ridge and the Sunrise Shelf · when: Post-game: the first spring after chapter 12, once the memorial flowers open (after the ending has played). Any ending.
