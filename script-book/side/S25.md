# Side story S25: Real

## S25. Real

*The Velveteen Rabbit, or How Toys Become Real (Margery Williams, 1922; public domain; Project Gutenberg #11757): the toy, the Boy, the Skin Horse and the nursery magic, retold in the valley* · audience: all · where: Hazel’s cottage: the loft, the window seat, the lawn and the long grass, and the shed · when: From chapter 3 (after c3-open) to chapter 8. Step 3 waits for c4-open, step 10 for c6-close, step 13 for c8-nova. Closes when chapter 9 opens.

### Step 1: Open the parcel in Hazel’s hand under the loft eaves.

Hint: It’s on the trunk under the little window. Pull the right end of the string; there is only one right end. · check: `inspect:s25-parcel`

#### <a id="s25-loft"></a>Scene `s25-loft`

_Early-summer rain, steady and warm. The loft lamp has died, and Sparks says it’s the small, fiddly kind of broken._  
_Small and fiddly is Pixel’s department. Pixel is coming after lunch._  
_You go up the ladder first, to clear a space, and because you were never once allowed up there._  
_The loft smells of apples, dust and mothballs. Rain ticks on the slates a hand’s breadth above your head._  
_Hazel’s things are up here, in the order of someone who knew exactly where everything was and told nobody._  
_Seed trays, a broken umbrella labelled STILL GOOD, and forty years of Lantern Night programmes tied up with string._  
_Each one is marked out of ten in pencil. There is one ten. Under it: “Rained. Best yet.”_  
_Your Nubbin climbs up behind you, sneezes at the dust, and sets off to investigate a hatbox._  
_Under the eaves, on a trunk, is a parcel in brown paper, soft and gone pale where the sun from the little window has reached it for years._  
_On the front, in her hand: “For when they come back. (They’ll have grown. It won’t mind.)”_  
_The writing doesn’t lean. She wrote this a long time ago, before her words began to walk uphill._  
_The string is tied in her bow: the one that falls open if you pull the right end, and never if you pull the wrong one._  

#### <a id="s25-name"></a>Done `s25-name`

_Inside, in tissue: a knitted Nubbin, lumpy, in grey-brown wool. The bobble on top is a little crooked._  
_One button eye is sewn on tight. The other hangs on a long loose thread, as if it were looking at something far off._  
_Under it is a thin book with a rabbit on the cover, its spine mended twice with brown tape._  
_You remember, though not all at once. The smell comes first, wool and peppermint, then her kitchen, and being three, and holding it by the bobble._  
_She made it for you, and you took it everywhere for one summer. Then you went home and grew, and it stayed._  
_You called it something. You’re nearly sure you did._  

**Choices**

- **“Button.” For the eye.** — sets `{"s25-name":"button"}`

    - Your Nubbin looks at the toy’s loose eye, then checks its own two.
- **“Bobble.” For the bobble.** — sets `{"s25-name":"bobble"}`
    - Bobble. You carried it by the bobble everywhere, which is why the bobble is crooked.

### Step 3: Stay on the window seat until Moss has finished.

Hint: Don’t get up, and don’t tap anything else. He’s slow, and he’s nearly done. · check: `sit:s25-window-seat:moss`

#### <a id="s25-real"></a>Scene `s25-real`

_One morning the top of the western hill is empty._  
_Moss is on the outside ledge of the window seat, in the sun, by the open window. The toy is on the inside sill._  
_It is a long way down from the hill for him. He must have set out before it was light._  
**Moss**: Don’t mention it. I’m not here.  
_You sit on the window seat, because that is the rule, and you wait. A bee comes in, considers them both, and leaves._  
**Moss** _(happy)_: She knitted that in a week, with her feet on the fender. She dropped a stitch every night and said words I won’t repeat.  
**Moss** _(soft)_: You were three. You carried it by the bobble and you wouldn’t let anyone else hold it. Not even her.  
**Moss**: She read you that book on this seat, every night of one wet August. I sat on this ledge. I heard every word.  
**Moss**: The horse in it talks a great deal of sense, for a horse.  
_He turns, not to you, but to the toy._  
**Moss** _(soft)_: Real isn’t how you are made. It’s a thing that happens to you.  
**Moss** _(soft)_: When someone loves you for a long, long time, not just to play with, but REALLY loves you, then you become Real.  
_The toy, being knitted, takes this well._  

**Choices**

- **Ask him: does it hurt?** — sets `{"s25-ask":"hurt"}`
    - **You**: Does it hurt?
    - Moss doesn’t answer you. He answers the toy.
- **Ask him: does it happen all at once?** — sets `{"s25-ask":"when"}`
    - **You**: Does it happen all at once?
    - Moss doesn’t answer you. He answers the toy.
- **Say nothing. Sit with them both.** — sets `{"s25-ask":"sit"}`
    - You say nothing. After a while Moss nods, very slightly, and goes on as if somebody had asked.

#### <a id="s25-hurt"></a>Done `s25-hurt`

**Moss** _(soft)_: Sometimes. When you are Real you don’t mind being hurt.  
**Moss**: It doesn’t happen all at once. You become. It takes a long time.  
**Moss** _(think)_: That’s why it doesn’t happen often to people who break easily, or have sharp edges, or who have to be carefully kept.  
_Then he climbs down off the ledge, one careful foot at a time, and sets off back to the hill. It will take him until supper._  
**Moss**: He has a good face, your toy. Don’t let him be carefully kept.

### Step 6: Go back to Fern’s for tea, with the toy, so she can apologise properly.

Hint: Let your Nubbin carry him in. It will insist. · check: `visit:fern:s25-apology`

#### <a id="s25-fern"></a>Scene `s25-fern`

_You take your Nubbin round to Fern’s for tea, and the toy comes too. The toy always comes too._

**Fern**: Oh, not on the cushion, love. It’s only a toy. It can go on the floor with the shoes.

**You**: He isn’t a toy. He’s REAL!  
_There is a short silence. In it, Fern looks at the toy properly for the first time._  
**Fern** _(think)_: …Right. Yes. I see. Then I owe him an apology. Possibly several.  
**Fern**: And I’m going to do it properly, because Hazel would have, and because your Nubbin is looking at me like that.  
**Fern**: Come round for tea, and bring him. I’m getting the good cups out.  

#### <a id="s25-apology"></a>Done `s25-apology`

_Fern has the good cups out, a cushion on the good chair, and a very small cup on the cushion._  
**Fern**: Sir. I said you were only a toy. That was wrong, and rude, and in front of your friend. I apologise unreservedly.  
**Fern**: I am, as everybody knows, only a neighbour. You are clearly a great deal more than that.  
_Your Nubbin considers this. Then it sets him on the cushion, and accepts a biscuit on his behalf._
