# Side story S30: What Sad Is For

## S30. What Sad Is For

*Inside Out (Pixar, 2015) and Inside Out 2 (Pixar, 2024): a Joy that keeps a girl so happy she can’t ask for help, the discovery that sadness tells the people who love you where to find you, and a worry that keeps everyone too safe. A light nod; two short quotes, everything else original* · audience: all · where: Your gate, then Clover’s cottage down the lane (the deep kitchen windowsill, the shed, the rain butt) · when: Chapter 6, the morning after c6-close (Fossil’s farewell). Steps 1–6 in chapter 6; step 7 after c7-stillness; steps 8–9 in chapter 7’s first frost week; steps 10–14 in chapter 8. Closes when chapter 9 opens.

**Cast:** Dimple (Joy (Inside Out, Pixar 2015), a light nod only; an original Nubbin of Clover’s), Drizzle (Sadness (Inside Out, Pixar 2015), a light nod only; an original Nubbin of Clover’s), Fidget (Anxiety (Inside Out 2, Pixar 2024), a light nod only; an original Nubbin of Clover’s)

### Step 1: Go down the lane to Clover’s cottage for a cup of tea, and meet her Nubbins.

Hint: Clover’s is the cottage past Fern’s with the deep kitchen windowsill and the rain butt by the door. · check: `visit:s30-clover-cottage`

#### <a id="s30-smile"></a>Scene `s30-smile`

_The morning after the rain, the whole valley drips. Somebody is rattling your gate, trying to push it without lifting._  
_It’s Clover. A small gold Nubbin is bouncing on her shoulder like a kettle just before it boils._  
**Clover** _(happy)_: I know, I know. Lift and push. I never remember till I’m standing here pushing.  
**Clover**: This is Dimple. One of mine. She’s smiled since the hill. Since Dusty’s Fossil. Smiled all night. Smiled in her sleep.  
_Dimple beams at you. She beams at the gatepost, and at a slug on the gatepost, with exactly the same beam._  
**Clover** _(happy)_: At least somebody’s cheerful.  
_Behind Clover, on the wet path, four more Nubbins sit in a damp heap, as if somebody had dropped them there and walked on._  
**Clover** _(sad)_: That’s the rest. Drizzle, Bristle, Flinch, and her ladyship, who only eats red things. They’ve taken it hard.  
**Clover**: Fossil sat on my kitchen sill every digging day, you know. Years. Dusty dropped her off on the way to the cliff.  
**Clover** _(think)_: Come down for tea. I want somebody to look at Dimple who isn’t me. I keep looking at her and thinking: good. Good.  
_Your Nubbin has come to the gate. It studies Dimple’s smile for a long moment, and then, carefully, puts one on._  

#### <a id="s30-sill"></a>Done `s30-sill`

_Clover’s kitchen is warm and loud and full of steam. Bristle is stamping on a saucepan lid; Flinch has jumped into the bread bin._  
**Clover** _(happy)_: Hazel said my kitchen was like the inside of somebody’s head. Everybody shouting, nobody in charge.  
_The windowsill is deep enough to sit in. On it is a flat old cushion with a dent in the middle, the shape of somebody small and round._  
**Clover** _(soft)_: Fossil’s. Dusty won’t take it back, and I can’t bring myself to plump it.  
_Dimple has filled the dent. Every toy she owns is in it: a cotton reel, a pine cone, a sock and half a wooden peg, piled up high._  
_Drizzle climbs the curtain, very slowly, and lays one pale-blue hand flat in the last gap in the dent._  
_Dimple is there at once. She takes Drizzle by the wrist and pulls her off the sill, still smiling._  
_Dimple beams at her, and goes back to patting the pile flat._  
_Then you see the ball above Dimple’s head. It has gone pale and thin, like a soap bubble that has been out too long._  
**Clover** _(happy)_: See? Not a cloud on her. She’s my good one, this week.  
_Clover is looking at the smile. She isn’t looking up._  

**Choices**

- **Point out Dimple’s ball to Clover.**
    - **Clover**: Balls flicker. It’s been a big week for everybody. Look at that face, though.
    - She means it kindly. She goes on looking at the face.
- **Say nothing yet. Keep watching.**
    - You drink your tea and watch the ball, and the ball doesn’t change.
    - Dimple sees you looking, and smiles harder.

### Step 2: Go out to the rain butt and see what Dimple is doing to Drizzle.

Hint: The yard is round the back of Clover’s. Go quietly; Flinch is asleep in the bucket by the door. · check: `watch:s30-acorn-ring`

#### <a id="s30-ring"></a>Done `s30-ring`

_Drizzle sits in the corner under the drip from the rain butt. Round her, Dimple has laid a ring of acorns, nose to tail, very neat._  
_Drizzle puts one foot over the acorns. Dimple bounces over and nudges her back in, smiling, quick and kind and firm._  
_Drizzle tries again, and again: nudge, nudge. Each time, Dimple adds another acorn to the ring._  
_Your Nubbin, who has followed you out, starts fetching acorns too, to help. It doesn’t know what for._  
_Drizzle looks at you over the ring. She doesn’t look upset; she looks like someone waiting to be let out of a room._  
_Drizzle says nothing. She lifts one foot over the acorns again, and waits to see what you do._

**Sparks** _(happy)_: LOOK AT HER GO. That’s a happy Nubbin. That’s a party working.  
**Ember** _(angry)_: That’s not happy. That’s busy.

**Byte**: Out of ten. On my scale. I made a scale.

### Step 4: Watch Dimple with each of her five favourite things.

Hint: The sill and the bedtime hum are at Clover’s, the puddles in her yard, Sparks’s racers at the pond, and Byte wherever Byte is. · check: `watch:s30-dimple:five-things`

**Luna** _(soft)_: Did you see her face? Before?

### Step 7: Look closely at Hush’s flat stone in the old wall.

**Moss** _(soft)_: People thought that made her a sad thing. They were wrong about which part was sad.

### Step 8: Come back to Clover’s at dusk and see how Fidget is settling in.

#### <a id="s30-shed"></a>Done `s30-shed`

_At dusk the frost comes down over the yard like a cloth over a cage. Fidget goes very still for one second, and then she gets to work._  
_She herds the whole household into the shed: Bristle, Flinch, her ladyship, Drizzle, Dimple. She pushes from behind with her head._

**Clover** _(sad)_: Every night since the frost. In they go, and the bucket.

**Clover** _(soft)_: She just wants them safe. I know how she feels. I’ve felt it every night of my life.  
**Clover** _(think)_: Safe is not the same as well.

**Clover** _(sad)_: That’s Moss’s. And I said it like it was mine. Well. It is now.
