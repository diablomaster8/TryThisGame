# Side story S31: The Great Gourd

## S31. The Great Gourd

*It’s the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown (Peanuts, CBS 1966; Peanuts Worldwide LLC): a small boy who believes what nobody else does, and waits all night in a cold field. Theme and shape, plus three listed one-sentence quotes.* · audience: all · where: Hazel’s Grove: Tam on the Grove wall by the gate · when: Chapter 7, after c7-stillness (early autumn). Gourd Night is the first dusk after step 6. Closes when chapter 8 opens; unfinished if Gourd Night has not come.

**Cast:** Tam (Linus van Pelt (Peanuts: It’s the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown)), Ivy (Lucy van Pelt (Peanuts: It’s the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown)), Ned (Charlie Brown (Peanuts: It’s the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown)), Nettie (Sally Brown (Peanuts: It’s the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown)), Granny Brock

### Step 1: Help Tam spell his letter to the Great Gourd.

Hint: Tam is on the Grove wall by the gate. Fix the words he asks about; he will argue about some of them. · check: `puzzle:s31-letter:spelling`

#### <a id="s31-wall"></a>Scene `s31-wall`

_The plums have gone soft, and the wind smells of bonfires two gardens away. Autumn is coming in at the edges._  
_A small boy is sitting on your Grove wall, wrapped in a patchwork quilt, writing very hard with his tongue out._  
_The quilt goes right down to his boots. It has been patched so often that some of the patches have patches._  
**Tam**: Don’t look. It’s private. It’s a letter.  
**Tam** _(think)_: …You can look a bit. I need a speller.  
**Tam**: I’m Tam. I live in the lane. I’m seven, and I’m writing to the Great Gourd.  
**You**: The what?  
**Tam** _(happy)_: The Great Gourd. Every Gourd Night he rises out of the most sincere gourd patch in the valley and flies over, bringing toys.  
**Tam**: Not the biggest patch. Not the tidiest. The most sincere one. He can tell.  
**Tam**: Nobody else believes in him. That’s all right. He doesn’t need everybody. He only needs one patch.  
_He holds out the letter. It begins DEAR GRAET GORD, and it has been rubbed out so hard in three places that there are holes._  

#### <a id="s31-letter"></a>Done `s31-letter`

_Between you, GRAET becomes GREAT. Tam keeps GORD for a while on principle, then gives it up._  
**Tam** _(think)_: Now the important bit. What to ask for. You choose. You’re a grown-up; he might take you more seriously.  

**Choices**

- **Toys. It’s what he brings. Ask for toys.** — sets `{"s31-letter":"toys"}`
    - **You**: Ask for toys. He’s bringing them anyway.
    - **Tam**: Toys. Yes. Not greedy ones. Medium toys.
    - He writes A MEDIUM AMOUNT OF TOYS PLEASE, and underlines PLEASE twice.
- **Something for the strays in the Hollow.** — sets `{"s31-letter":"strays"}`
    - **Tam**: The strays?
    - **You**: The ones past the falls. Nobody brings them anything.
    - Tam thinks about this with his whole face.
    - **Tam**: He’d like that. He’s flying over anyway.
    - He writes SOMETHING FOR THE STRAYS IN THE HOLLOW. Then, smaller: THEY ARE NOT GREEDY.
- **Tell him, very politely, that he needn’t come if he’s busy.** — sets `{"s31-letter":"busy"}`
    - **Tam**: Not come?
    - **You**: Very politely. In case he’s busy. It’s a long night.
    - **Tam**: …That’s very thoughtful. Nobody ever thinks he might be tired.
    - He writes YOU NEEDN’T COME IF YOU ARE BUSY, WE WILL UNDERSTAND. Then, after a long think: MOSTLY.

### Step 2: Go with Tam and the lane children to Fern’s gourd patch.

Hint: Fern’s patch is over the wall next door, past the hens. Follow Ivy; she walks fast on purpose. · check: `visit:s31-gourd-patch`

#### <a id="s31-creed"></a>Scene `s31-creed`

_Tam folds the letter into four and posts it down the front of his quilt, for safekeeping._  
_Two more children come down the lane: a girl with a straight fringe and a clipboard, and a boy with a round, worried face._  
**Ivy** _(angry)_: Tam. Are you writing to that pumpkin again?  
**Tam**: Gourd. He’s a gourd. A pumpkin is only one kind of gourd. Fern told me.  
**Ivy**: This is my brother. I’m Ivy. I’m ten, and I’m sorry about him in advance.  
**Ned** _(think)_: I’m Ned. Hello. Is it true he flies? Ivy says it isn’t. I’m only asking.  
_Ivy laughs. Ned laughs too, a beat late because Ivy is, and then looks as if he wishes he hadn’t._  
_Tam gets down off the wall. He draws himself up, quilt and all, to very nearly the height of the gatepost._  
**Tam**: There are three things that I’ve learned never to discuss with people: religion, politics, and the Great Gourd.  
_A very small girl has come down the lane behind them, and is listening hard._  
**Nettie** _(happy)_: I believe him. I’m Nettie. I’m six. I believe everything Tam says.  
**Ivy**: You believed the hens could read. Come on. Mum wants the biggest gourd in Fern’s patch, and Tam’s carrying it.  
_Ivy marches Nettie off by the hand. Tam follows with great dignity, holding his quilt up off the mud like a train._  

#### <a id="s31-patch"></a>Done `s31-patch`

_Fern’s gourd patch is vines going in every direction at once. The gourds are lumpy and leaning, and several are warty._  
**Fern**: Sorry. Sorry about the patch. I grow them badly on purpose. It’s a style.  
_Ivy walks straight to the biggest gourd there is: orange, enormous, and the shape of a sat-on cushion._  
**Ivy**: That one. Tam, lift.  
**Tam**: It’s bigger than me.  
**Ivy**: Then you’ll have to be bigger than it. Go on.  

### Step 3: Carve a gourd lantern with Tam: a smaller, kinder one.

Hint: Pick a small gourd from Fern’s patch and carve it on the cottage step. A kind face takes longer than a fierce one. · check: `craft:s31-gourd-lantern`

#### <a id="s31-carve"></a>Scene `s31-carve`

_Tam carries the gourd all the way up the lane, very slowly, like a man carrying a sleeping relative._  
_At the cottage step, Ivy takes it off him, sets it down, and fetches a big knife from the kitchen._  
**Tam**: What’s that for?  
**Ivy**: It’s nearly Gourd Night. You carve them. Everyone carves them. You put a candle in.  
_She cuts the top off in one go. Tam makes a noise like a kettle._  
**Tam** _(shock)_: Nobody told me. Nobody TOLD me you cut them.  
**Tam** _(shock)_: What if it was a relative of his?  
**Ivy**: It’s a gourd, Tam. It hasn’t got relatives. It’s got seeds.  
_She carves a face with jagged teeth and one eyebrow higher than the other. It looks a lot like Ivy._  
_Tam sits on the step with his quilt over his head. Nettie sits beside him with a tea towel over hers, to be fair._  

#### <a id="s31-kinder"></a>Done `s31-kinder`

_The gourd you chose is the most lopsided one in Fern’s patch. You carve it slowly, on the step beside the quilt._  
_Round eyes, no teeth, and a mouth that is mostly thinking about smiling._  
_After a while, one corner of the quilt lifts._  
**Tam** _(soft)_: …That one’s kind. He won’t mind that one.  
**Tam**: You keep it. Put it somewhere he’ll see it from the air.  

### Step 4: Witness Ivy’s promise, and watch Ned take his kick.

Hint: Ned is on the green at the end of the lane with a ball and a great deal of hope. · check: `watch:s31-ned:kick`

#### <a id="s31-ball"></a>Scene `s31-ball`

_Next morning, on the green at the end of the lane, Ivy is holding a ball upright on the grass with one finger._  
**Ivy**: Ned. I’ll hold it. You run up and kick it. I won’t pull it away.  
**Ned** _(think)_: You pulled it away last time.  
**Ivy**: That was last time. This time I’ve written it down.  
_She holds out the clipboard: I PROMISE NOT TO PULL THE BALL AWAY, SIGNED IVY. Underneath is a line marked WITNESS._  
**Ned** _(soft)_: Will you witness it? Please. A grown-up. Then it counts.  

**Choices**

- **Sign as witness.**
    - You sign. Ned looks at your name on the line as if it were a bridge over a river.
    - **Ned**: Right. Right. This is it.
- **Sign, and tell Ned quietly to watch her finger.**
    - You sign, and tell Ned quietly to watch her finger.
    - **Ned**: I will. I’ll watch it the whole way.
    - **Ivy**: Witnesses aren’t allowed to coach.
- **Don’t sign. Tell Ned not to do it.**
    - **You**: Don’t do it, Ned.
    - **Ned**: But she’s written it down.
    - He signs the witness line himself, in very small letters, and backs off for his run-up.

#### <a id="s31-kick"></a>Done `s31-kick`

_Ned runs with everything he has. At the very last moment Ivy lifts her finger, and the ball rolls gently away._  
_Ned kicks the air, goes up, and comes down flat on his back with a sound like a dropped sack._  
**Ned** _(sad)_: …You promised. It was witnessed.  
**Ivy**: By a biased party. Look at them. They’re obviously on your side. It doesn’t count.  
_She tucks the clipboard under her arm and walks off. Ned lies on the grass and looks at the sky, which at least stays put._  

### Step 5: Find out whether Ned is really invited to Sparks’s party.

Hint: Sparks is up a ladder outside her workshop at the top of the lane. You will hear her before you see her. · check: `talk:sparks:s31-lists`

#### <a id="s31-lists"></a>Scene `s31-lists`

_Ned is still on the green when you come back past. He is holding a card cut in the shape of a cog._  
**Ned** _(happy)_: Sparks asked me to her Gourd Night party. Me. On a card.  
**Ivy**: Sparks keeps two lists. Everybody knows. One of who to ask, and one of who not to ask.  
**Ivy**: She’ll have muddled them up. You’re on the wrong one. I’m only saying it to help.  
_Ned looks at the card. The card looks back, cheerfully, being a cog._  
**Ned** _(sad)_: I didn’t think it was a mistake. I thought it was a card.  
**Tam**: Ask her. That’s what you do. You ask.  
**Ned**: I can’t ask. What if she says?  
_He holds the card out to you very carefully, as if it might go off._  

#### <a id="s31-everybody"></a>Done `s31-everybody`

_Sparks is up a ladder with a string of bulbs in her teeth. She takes them out to hear the question, and goes red._  
**Sparks** _(shock)_: TWO LISTS? Who said two lists? There’s one list. ONE.  
_She points at her workshop door, where it is chalked in letters a foot high: GOURD NIGHT, EVERYBODY._  
**Sparks** _(happy)_: I wrote it on the door so I couldn’t lose it. Tell Ned. No, I’ll tell Ned. NED!  
_Down the lane, a small figure on the green sits up very fast._  

### Step 6: Help Tam put up his sign in the patch he has chosen.

Hint: Tam has chosen the long grass by your fourth lantern post. The hammer is in the cottage shed. · check: `build:s31-welcome-sign`

#### <a id="s31-sincere"></a>Scene `s31-sincere`

_Tam has been all round the valley looking at gourd patches. He comes back with his quilt full of burrs and his mind made up._  
_He walks past three lantern posts, lit and neat, and stops at the fourth._  
_The fourth post stands dark at the edge of the long grass. There isn’t a gourd anywhere near it._  
**Tam**: This one.  
**You**: There aren’t any gourds here.  
**Tam** _(think)_: No. But it isn’t pretending. Everywhere else is tidy, for people. This one’s just itself.  
**Tam**: He’ll like that. The most sincere patch. I’ve got a feeling for sincere.  
_His sign is painted on the back of a tea tray: WELCOME GREAT GOURD. The R is backwards, on purpose, for luck._  
_He also has a toffee apple in a hanky. It’s for the Great Gourd, in case he’s hungry from flying._  

#### <a id="s31-moss"></a>Done `s31-moss`

_You knock the sign in by the dark post. Tam sets the toffee apple on top and looks at it all the way people look at cathedrals._  
_Moss comes past along the hill path, slowly, the grass hardly bending under him. He stops to read the sign._  
**Tam**: Moss. You’re very old. Is the Great Gourd real?  
**Moss** _(think)_: I have never seen it. I have not seen a great many things that are real.  
_Tam nods slowly, as if that settles it in his favour. Moss goes on up the hill, and you think he might be smiling._  
_Then Clover comes over the stile with three folded blankets, puts them down by the sign, and turns straight round._  
**Clover**: I’m not discussing it. Three. One each and a spare. Don’t look at me like that.  

### Step 7: Get your Nubbin ready for Gourd Night in Sparks’s goggles and scarf.

**Ned** _(happy)_: I can see really well, though.

**Sparks** _(happy)_: For your Nubbin! Every Gourd Night needs a flying ace. I wore those for twelve years. They’ve seen things.

### Step 8: Decide where you’ll spend Gourd Night.

Hint: Ivy is waiting in the lane, Sparks at her workshop, and Tam in the long grass. Choose one; the others will manage. · check: `choose:s31-gourd-night`

_The guisers stop at the edge of the long grass on their way out: a witch, a ghost mostly made of holes, and three small bats._  
**Ivy**: Tam. You’re going to sit in a field all night, in the cold, for a pumpkin.  
**Tam**: Gourd. And it’s a patch, not a field.  
**Ned** _(soft)_: You could come guising. There are toffee apples. You could come back after.  
**Tam**: If I’m not here when he rises, he’ll think nobody meant it. I mean it.  
_Nettie looks at the guisers, and at the bag in Ivy’s hand. Then she sits back down, firmly, next to Tam._  
**Nettie**: I’m staying. I mean it too. I think.  
**Ivy**: Fine. I’m fetching you both at four. I’ve told Mum. I’ve told Clover. Clover’s told everyone.

_Granny Brock comes down the lane with a basket, and stops at the gate to look at the sign._  
**Granny Brock**: Waiting for a gourd. In the cold. Hm.

**Sparks** _(happy)_: KEEPER! PARTY! You’re on the list! Everyone is!

### Step 10: Your Nubbin is having its own night. Guide it home from the war.

Hint: Keep low along the hedges and out of the frosty grass. The lit windows of Sparks’s workshop are friendly lines. · check: `minigame:s31-flying-ace`

#### <a id="s31-ace-roof"></a>Scene `s31-ace-roof`

_Meanwhile, on the roof of the end cottage, a small figure in goggles and a long red scarf is at war._  
_Your Nubbin is the flying ace. It stands on the ridge tiles and stares into the dark at an enemy only it can see._  
_It ducks and weaves and shakes one paw at the sky. Then something only it can hear goes wrong with its engine._  
_It spins three times on the tiles, slides down the slates, and drops into the water butt with a small, heroic splash._  
_Shot down, behind the lines. It climbs out, wrings its scarf out with great dignity, and looks about for cover._  
_Far off across the hedges, Sparks’s workshop glows like a field hospital._  

#### <a id="s31-ace-party"></a>Done `s31-ace-party`

_Your Nubbin crawls the last hedge on its belly, slips into the party between everyone’s boots, and hides in the apple-bobbing tub._  
_Ivy, back from guising, puts her hands behind her back and plunges her face in for an apple. She comes up with a lick instead._  
**Ivy** _(shock)_: Something LICKED me. Something in the TUB licked me.  
_Pixel plays old tunes on the pianola. Your Nubbin, dripping in its goggles, is merry for a jig, tragic for a slow one, and merry again._  
_It sits by the stove gazing into the middle distance, like a veteran. Then it howls, once, by accident, and looks shocked at itself._  
_Before anyone can say anything, it slinks out into the dark, scarf trailing._

### Step 12: Before dawn, the frost comes in over the long grass.

Hint: Nothing to do but wait. Keep your Nubbins at the edge, out of the white grass. · check: `wait:s31-long-grass:frost`

#### <a id="s31-frost"></a>Scene `s31-frost`

_In the small hours the frost comes, the way it has every dawn this autumn._  
_It creeps out of the long grass in a long white tongue, straight for the dark post. The grass goes stiff and silver as it passes._  
_Tam is awake. He has been awake all night, out of principle._  
_Nettie is asleep against him with her mouth open and one boot off._  
**Tam** _(shock)_: Here it comes. Here he comes. That’s what the cold is. It’s him, getting near.  
_Out in the white grass, by the post, something moves._  

#### <a id="s31-shape"></a>Done `s31-shape`
